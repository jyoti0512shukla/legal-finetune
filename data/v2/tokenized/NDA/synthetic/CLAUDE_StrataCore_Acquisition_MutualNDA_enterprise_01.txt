MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date"), by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} with its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} ("Party A"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} with its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} ("Party B"). Party A and Party B are each referred to herein as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties." For any particular disclosure under this Agreement, the Party disclosing Confidential Information is referred to as the "Disclosing Party" and the Party receiving such Confidential Information is referred to as the "Receiving Party."

WHEREAS, Party A is contemplating a possible negotiated acquisition of all or substantially all of the equity interests or assets of Party B (the "Potential Transaction"), and the Parties desire to evaluate the Potential Transaction in a mutually cooperative manner;

WHEREAS, in connection with such evaluation, each Party may disclose to the other certain non-public information that is proprietary, commercially sensitive, or otherwise confidential in nature;

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to establish the terms and conditions upon which such Confidential Information may be disclosed and received, and to restrict the use and further disclosure of such information to the narrowly defined purposes set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, hereby agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that, directly or indirectly through one or more intermediaries, Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with such Person. For purposes of this definition, "Control" means the possession, directly or indirectly, of the power to direct or cause the direction of the management and policies of a Person, whether through the ownership of voting securities, by contract, or otherwise.

1.2 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information, in whatever form or medium (whether written, oral, electronic, visual, or otherwise), that is disclosed by or on behalf of a Disclosing Party to a Receiving Party or any of its Representatives in connection with the Purpose, together with all analyses, compilations, studies, forecasts, summaries, memoranda, notes and other documents or materials (in any medium) prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives that contain, are based upon, reflect, or are derived from any such information. Confidential Information includes, without limitation, business plans, financial statements and projections, customer and supplier information, trade secrets, formulations, manufacturing processes, product designs, research and development data, regulatory filings, personnel information, the existence and status of the discussions between the Parties concerning the Potential Transaction, and the terms of this Agreement. Information shall be treated as Confidential Information regardless of whether it is marked as such, provided that a reasonable person in the position of the Receiving Party would understand from the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure that it is confidential or proprietary.

1.3 "Evaluation Material" means any Confidential Information disclosed by a Disclosing Party in connection with the Purpose, and is a subset of Confidential Information.

1.4 "Person" means any natural person, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, governmental authority, or other entity.

1.5 "Purpose" means the sole purpose of evaluating, negotiating, and (if and only if the Parties mutually so elect) consummating the Potential Transaction. The Purpose does not include, and no Confidential Information may be used for, any operational, commercial, competitive, investment, trading, or strategic purpose unrelated to the Potential Transaction.

1.6 "Representatives" means, with respect to a Receiving Party, such Receiving Party's Affiliates, and its and their respective directors, officers, employees, attorneys, accountants, financial advisors, consultants, and lenders (and counsel to such lenders), in each case to the extent that such Persons have a bona fide need to know Confidential Information for the Purpose and have been informed of the confidential nature of such information and the terms of this Agreement.

1.7 "Trade Secret" means any item of Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under the Delaware Uniform Trade Secrets Act or any other applicable law.

ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

2.1 Narrow Purpose. The Receiving Party acknowledges and agrees that any Confidential Information furnished to it by or on behalf of the Disclosing Party is being furnished solely for the Purpose. The Receiving Party shall use such Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever.

2.2 No Competitive or Commercial Use. Without limiting Section 2.1, the Receiving Party shall not use any Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party, directly or indirectly: (a) to obtain a competitive advantage against the Disclosing Party or its Affiliates in any line of business; (b) to solicit, bid for, price against, or attempt to acquire any customer, supplier, employee, investment opportunity, or asset of the Disclosing Party; or (c) in connection with the purchase, sale, or other trading of any securities of the Disclosing Party in violation of applicable securities laws.

2.3 No Reverse Engineering. The Receiving Party shall not, and shall cause its Representatives not to, reverse engineer, disassemble, decompile, or otherwise attempt to derive the composition, structure, design, or methods of manufacture of any tangible item of Confidential Information disclosed by the Disclosing Party, including without limitation any chemical sample, formulation, or product prototype made available in connection with the Purpose.

2.4 No Residuals. The Receiving Party expressly acknowledges that this Agreement does not contain a "residuals" clause. The retention in the unaided memory of the Receiving Party's Representatives shall not authorize any use or disclosure of Confidential Information that would otherwise be prohibited by this Agreement.

ARTICLE 3 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

3.1 Obligations of the Receiving Party. The Receiving Party shall (a) hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence, (b) use such Confidential Information solely for the Purpose in accordance with Article 2, (c) disclose such Confidential Information only to those Representatives who have a bona fide need to know such Confidential Information for the Purpose and who are bound by written or professional obligations of confidentiality and non-use no less protective than the obligations set forth in this Agreement, and (d) protect such Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care the Receiving Party uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

3.2 Responsibility for Representatives. The Receiving Party shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives and shall take all reasonable measures (including without limitation, where appropriate, the institution of injunctive or other equitable proceedings) to restrain its Representatives from any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

3.3 Notification of Unauthorized Disclosure. The Receiving Party shall promptly, and in any event within five (5) business days after discovery, notify the Disclosing Party in writing of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information of which the Receiving Party becomes aware, and shall cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party in any effort by the Disclosing Party to mitigate or remedy such unauthorized use or disclosure.

3.4 Data Room Protocol. To the extent that either Disclosing Party makes Confidential Information available to the Receiving Party through a virtual data room, the Receiving Party shall comply with any data room access protocols designated by the Disclosing Party, including without limitation: (a) restrictions on printing, downloading, or copying of watermarked documents; (b) limitations on the number and identity of Representatives granted access; (c) maintenance of an access log upon request of the Disclosing Party; and (d) return or deletion of any locally retained copies upon conclusion of the review.

ARTICLE 4 — EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Exclusions. The obligations set forth in Article 3 shall not apply to any information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by competent written evidence:

(a) was already in the public domain at the time of disclosure to the Receiving Party, or subsequently enters the public domain through no act or omission of the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was already known to the Receiving Party, free of any obligation of confidence, prior to the time of disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

(c) was independently developed by the Receiving Party or its Representatives without use of or reference to any Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party; or

(d) was rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party who was not, to the Receiving Party's knowledge after reasonable inquiry, under any obligation of confidence to the Disclosing Party with respect to such information.

4.2 No Aggregate Knowledge Defense. Confidential Information shall not be deemed to fall within any of the foregoing exclusions merely because it is embraced by more general information in the public domain or in the Receiving Party's possession, or because individual elements of such Confidential Information are in the public domain or in the Receiving Party's possession, unless the combination itself and its principle of operation are in the public domain or in the Receiving Party's prior possession.

ARTICLE 5 — COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

5.1 Compelled Disclosure by Legal Process. In the event that the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required (by oral questions, interrogatories, requests for information or documents in a legal proceeding, subpoena, civil investigative demand, or other similar process) to disclose any Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permissible, provide the Disclosing Party with prompt written notice of any such request or requirement so that the Disclosing Party may, at its sole cost, seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

5.2 Cooperation and Limitation. If, in the absence of a protective order or the receipt of a waiver from the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is nonetheless, on the advice of counsel, legally compelled to disclose Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party or such Representative may disclose only that portion of such Confidential Information that such counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed, and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain assurances that confidential treatment will be accorded to such Confidential Information. The Receiving Party shall continue to treat all non-disclosed portions of the Confidential Information as confidential in accordance with this Agreement.

5.3 Regulation FD Carve-Out. The Parties acknowledge that Party A is a publicly traded company subject to Regulation FD under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934. Nothing in this Agreement shall prohibit Party A from making any disclosure that its outside securities counsel advises is necessary to comply with Regulation FD or other applicable securities disclosure laws, provided that Party A shall, to the extent practicable, notify Party B in advance of any such disclosure and shall limit such disclosure to the minimum extent required.

ARTICLE 6 — RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

6.1 Return or Destruction. Upon the earlier of (a) the written request of the Disclosing Party or (b) the termination of discussions between the Parties regarding the Potential Transaction, the Receiving Party shall, at the Disclosing Party's election, promptly return to the Disclosing Party or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in the Receiving Party's or its Representatives' possession, custody or control, together with all copies, extracts, and derivative materials thereof, whether in tangible or electronic form.

6.2 Certification. If requested by the Disclosing Party, an executive officer of the Receiving Party shall certify in writing to the Disclosing Party that the Receiving Party has complied with the requirements of Section 6.1.

6.3 Archival Exception. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Receiving Party may retain (a) one (1) archival copy of Confidential Information in the custody of its outside legal counsel solely for purposes of establishing compliance with this Agreement, and (b) any Confidential Information contained in automatic system backups made in the ordinary course of business, provided that such retained materials shall remain subject to the confidentiality and non-use obligations of this Agreement for so long as they are retained, notwithstanding any termination of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 7 — NO LICENSE OR OWNERSHIP TRANSFER

7.1 Ownership Reserved. All Confidential Information disclosed by a Disclosing Party shall remain the exclusive property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting to the Receiving Party, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, any license or other right under any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of the Disclosing Party.

7.2 All Rights Reserved. The Disclosing Party reserves all rights in and to its Confidential Information, including without limitation all patent, copyright, trademark, and trade secret rights therein. No obligation to enter into any further agreement, including without limitation any definitive agreement for the Potential Transaction, is created by this Agreement.

ARTICLE 8 — STANDSTILL

8.1 Standstill Period. For a period of two (2) years following the Effective Date (the "Standstill Period"), Party A shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates and its and their Representatives acting on its behalf not to, without the prior written consent of Party B's board of managers: (a) acquire or propose to acquire, directly or indirectly, any equity interests or material assets of Party B; (b) make, or in any way participate in, any "solicitation" of "proxies" (as such terms are used in the proxy rules of the Securities and Exchange Commission) to vote any equity interests of Party B; (c) form, join, or participate in a "group" (as defined in Section 13(d)(3) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934) with respect to any equity interests of Party B; or (d) otherwise seek to control or influence the management, board, or policies of Party B.

8.2 Permitted Activities. Nothing in Section 8.1 shall prohibit Party A from (a) privately submitting a confidential written proposal to Party B's board of managers regarding the Potential Transaction or (b) taking any action expressly approved in writing by Party B's board of managers.

ARTICLE 9 — NON-SOLICITATION

9.1 Employee Non-Solicitation. For a period of eighteen (18) months following the Effective Date, neither Party shall, and each Party shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party with whom such Party first came into contact in connection with the Purpose or whose existence and role first became known to such Party as a result of Confidential Information disclosed hereunder.

9.2 General Solicitation Carve-Out. The restrictions set forth in Section 9.1 shall not prohibit (a) general solicitations of employment not specifically directed at employees of the other Party (including general advertisements, job postings, and searches conducted by executive search firms that have not been instructed to target employees of the other Party) or (b) the hiring of any Person who responds to such a general solicitation.

ARTICLE 10 — TERM AND SURVIVAL

10.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force and effect for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years thereafter, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Section 10.3.

10.2 Survival of Confidentiality Obligations. Notwithstanding any termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality and non-use with respect to Confidential Information shall survive for a period of three (3) years after the date of expiration or termination of this Agreement. With respect to any item of Confidential Information that constitutes a Trade Secret, however, the Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality and non-use shall survive for so long as such item continues to qualify as a Trade Secret under applicable law.

10.3 Termination for Material Breach. Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' prior written notice to the other Party in the event of a material breach of this Agreement by such other Party that has not been cured within such notice period; provided that no such termination shall relieve the breaching Party of any liability for such breach or of any of its surviving obligations under Section 10.2.

ARTICLE 11 — EQUITABLE REMEDIES

11.1 Irreparable Harm. Each Party acknowledges and agrees that the Confidential Information of the other Party is of a special, unique, and extraordinary character, and that any breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy.

11.2 Injunctive Relief. Accordingly, each Party agrees that, in the event of any actual or threatened breach of this Agreement by the other Party or any of its Representatives, the non-breaching Party shall be entitled to seek, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, specific performance, injunctive relief, and other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction, without the necessity of posting a bond or other security and without the necessity of proving actual damages.

11.3 Cumulative Remedies. The rights and remedies of the Parties under this Agreement are cumulative and in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other rights and remedies available to the Parties at law or in equity.

ARTICLE 12 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

12.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

12.2 Venue. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to such venue on the basis of forum non conveniens.

12.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous understandings, agreements, negotiations, representations, and warranties, whether oral or written, relating to such subject matter.

12.4 Amendments. No amendment, modification, or waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by a duly authorized representative of each Party.

12.5 Assignment. Neither Party may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Party A may assign this Agreement in connection with a merger, consolidation, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section shall be void.

12.6 No Waiver. No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any right, power, or remedy under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver thereof, nor shall any single or partial exercise of any such right, power, or remedy preclude any other or further exercise thereof.

12.7 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be deemed modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable while preserving the Parties' original intent.

12.8 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Delivery of an executed counterpart by electronic means (including PDF transmitted via email) shall be as effective as delivery of an originally executed counterpart.
