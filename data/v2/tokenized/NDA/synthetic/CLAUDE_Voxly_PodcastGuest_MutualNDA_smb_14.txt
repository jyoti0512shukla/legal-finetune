SHORT-FORM MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT FOR A PRE-INTERVIEW CALL

Effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This is a short mutual confidentiality agreement between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} ("Producer"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} ("Guest"). We're signing it because we're going to have a pre-interview conversation and we might both share things that aren't public yet — stories, research, opinions, unreleased work. We want to keep that conversation safe so we can actually have a real one. "We" and "you" in this agreement refer to whichever of us is sharing or receiving at a given moment.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" is anything either of us shares with the other during the pre-interview call or any related preparation conversations that isn't already public and that we've marked as confidential, or that a reasonable person in the room would understand to be confidential. That includes unreleased research, unannounced projects, personal history the Guest hasn't shared publicly, show editorial direction, planned episode themes, and any off-the-record comments either of us makes.

1.2 Purpose. "Purpose" means preparing for a potential podcast episode of the Producer's show featuring the Guest — including the pre-interview call, any follow-up conversations, any research the Producer does based on what the Guest shares, and (if the Guest agrees) the recorded interview itself.

1.3 Off the Record. "Off the record" means the Guest has said something to help the Producer understand the context but doesn't want it quoted, attributed, or referenced in the recorded interview. Off-the-record statements are Confidential Information.

ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

2.1 Use it only for the Purpose. We'll each use the other's Confidential Information only for the Purpose. Neither of us will use it to pitch competing shows, write unrelated articles, or share with third parties outside the Purpose.

2.2 Off-the-record commitments. Anything the Guest tells the Producer "off the record" stays off the record unless the Guest later agrees in writing that a specific item can be used on the show. The Producer will not quote, paraphrase, or reference off-the-record information in the recorded interview, in any show notes, in any promotional material, or in any social media post.

2.3 Recording consent. The pre-interview call is not recorded unless both of us agree in writing beforehand. The recorded interview is recorded, and that recording is not Confidential Information with respect to anything the Guest says on the record during the recording.

ARTICLE 3 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

3.1 The basics. We'll each: (a) keep the other's Confidential Information private; (b) use it only for the Purpose; (c) share it only with members of our team who need to know for the Purpose and who understand it's confidential (for the Producer, this is the show's producers, researchers, and legal reviewers; for the Guest, this is the Guest's publicist, assistant, or legal counsel, if any); and (d) take reasonable care to protect it.

3.2 Responsibility for our people. Each of us is responsible for our own team members. If one of our team leaks something, that's on us.

3.3 Memory is a thing. We recognize that this is a conversation, and neither of us can perfectly "forget" something we heard. But "remembering" something doesn't give either of us the right to use it or share it in a way this agreement prohibits. If something comes up in memory that was off the record, we won't act on it without checking with the other party.

3.4 Tell us if something leaks. If one of us finds out Confidential Information has been leaked — for example, if an off-the-record story gets picked up by a news outlet or shows up on social media — we'll tell the other party within three (3) business days and do what we can to help contain the problem.

ARTICLE 4 — EXCLUSIONS

4.1 What's not covered. The obligations in Article 3 don't apply to information that:
  (a) is already public or becomes public without either of us breaking this agreement;
  (b) we already knew, without any confidentiality obligation, before the pre-interview call;
  (c) we independently developed without using the other party's Confidential Information; or
  (d) we rightfully got from a third party who had no confidentiality duty to the other side.

4.2 Already public. For the Guest, any story, quote, or opinion the Guest has already shared publicly — in prior interviews, on social media, in published writing, or in any widely available forum — is not Confidential Information. The Producer is free to reference and quote such publicly available material on the show. For the Producer, anything the show has publicly released, including prior episodes, show notes, and promotional material, is not Confidential Information.

ARTICLE 5 — COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

5.1 Court orders and journalist privilege. If either of us is legally required to disclose the other's Confidential Information, we'll let the other party know right away, so they can seek a protective order or assert any applicable privilege. If we can't get a protective order and the other party doesn't waive the confidentiality obligation, we'll disclose only the minimum legally required. The Producer may also rely on any applicable reporter's shield laws that protect journalists' sources.

ARTICLE 6 — RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

6.1 When the pre-interview is done. If the recorded interview does not happen, or after the recorded interview has aired, either of us can ask the other to destroy any notes or files containing the other's Confidential Information. We'll do so within ten (10) business days. Routine email and cloud-storage backups are fine to keep, but they stay subject to this agreement.

ARTICLE 7 — NO LICENSE OR OWNERSHIP TRANSFER

7.1 Sharing information during the pre-interview call doesn't transfer any ownership and doesn't grant either of us any license to the other's stories, research, trademarks, or other intellectual property. Whatever either of us owns before the call, we still own after.

ARTICLE 8 — TERM AND SURVIVAL

8.2 After the agreement ends. Our confidentiality and non-use obligations continue for two (2) years after termination. Off-the-record commitments survive indefinitely — whatever was off the record during the pre-interview call stays off the record forever.

ARTICLE 9 — EQUITABLE REMEDIES

9.1 If either of us leaks the other's Confidential Information, money damages alone probably won't fix it — an unreleased story could get scooped, a personal disclosure could be broadcast without consent. So either of us can ask a court for an injunction or specific performance, without having to post a bond or prove actual damages, in addition to any other remedies at law or in equity.
