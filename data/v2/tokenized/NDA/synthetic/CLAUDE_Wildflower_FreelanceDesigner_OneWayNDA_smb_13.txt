DESIGNER CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

Effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This is a plain-language confidentiality agreement between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} ("we" or "Client"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} located at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} ("you" or "Designer"). We're using it because we're going to share some private information with you as we work together on a brand design project, and we want to be clear about what you can and can't do with that information. This agreement covers confidentiality only. The scope of the design work, fees, deliverables, and ownership of the final artwork are in a separate engagement contract.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means anything we share with you that isn't public and that a reasonable person in your position would understand to be confidential. It includes brand strategy, unreleased product names and images, pricing plans, supplier information, sales data, customer insights, and our creative briefs. It also includes anything we tell you in a meeting or a call that would clearly be embarrassing or harmful if it got out.

1.2 Purpose. "Purpose" means your work as a freelance designer on our brand project — the thing we hired you for. That's it.

1.3 Representatives. "Representatives" means the people you work with on this project who need to see our information to help you get the work done — like a subcontracting illustrator or a production coordinator — and who have agreed to keep things confidential on the same terms as this agreement. If you want to bring anyone in, let us know first.

ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

2.1 Use it only for the Purpose. You'll use our Confidential Information only to do the design work we hired you for. You won't use it to pitch other clients, to start a side project, or to get an advantage in any other engagement.

2.2 No reverse engineering. If we share physical product samples or prototypes, you won't take them apart or try to figure out how they were made.

ARTICLE 3 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

3.1 The basics. You'll: (a) keep our Confidential Information private; (b) use it only for the Purpose; (c) share it only with your Representatives who need to see it and who have agreed to the same confidentiality obligations; and (d) take reasonable care to protect it — at least as much care as you take with your own private information. That means no posting our unreleased stuff on Instagram, Dribbble, Behance, or anywhere else until we say so.

3.2 Your people, your responsibility. If one of your Representatives leaks something or uses it in a way this agreement prohibits, we'll treat that as if you did it yourself.

3.3 Tell us if something leaks. If you find out our Confidential Information has been leaked, lost, or misused, tell us within three (3) business days and do what you can to help contain the problem.

ARTICLE 4 — EXCLUSIONS

4.1 What's not covered. The obligations above don't apply to information that:
  (a) is already public or becomes public without you breaking this agreement;
  (b) you already knew, without any confidentiality obligation, before we shared it;
  (c) you independently developed without using any of our Confidential Information; or
  (d) you rightfully got from a third party who had no confidentiality duty to us.

4.2 Your own style and experience. Your general design skills, your professional experience, and your stylistic approach are yours. Nothing in this agreement limits your ability to keep doing the kind of work you do for other clients — as long as you don't use any of our Confidential Information to do it.

ARTICLE 5 — COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

5.1 Court orders and subpoenas. If you're legally required to disclose our Confidential Information (like a subpoena or a court order), let us know right away — as soon as you legally can — so we have a chance to ask a court to protect the information. If we can't get a protective order and we don't waive the confidentiality obligation, disclose only the minimum you're legally required to disclose. Everything else stays confidential.

ARTICLE 6 — RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

6.1 When the project's done. When the project wraps up, or whenever we ask you to, you'll return or destroy the Confidential Information we gave you, including any working files and any copies. You can send us a short email confirming you've done it — no need for a formal affidavit. You can keep one set of routine email and cloud-storage backups, but those stay subject to this agreement.

6.2 Portfolio. You can include the final deliverables in your portfolio after the project has publicly launched, but not before. Don't share work in progress, unused concepts, or any material we haven't released.

ARTICLE 7 — NO LICENSE OR OWNERSHIP TRANSFER

7.1 This agreement doesn't transfer any ownership or grant you any license to our Confidential Information beyond using it to do the design work. Our brands, trademarks, photography, and product information stay ours. The ownership of the design deliverables is handled in our separate engagement contract.

ARTICLE 8 — TERM AND SURVIVAL

8.1 How long this lasts. This agreement runs for {{TERM_YEARS}} years from the Effective Date. Either of us can end it earlier with {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' written notice.

8.2 After the agreement ends. Even after the agreement ends, your confidentiality and non-use obligations continue for another two (2) years. Anything that qualifies as a trade secret is protected for as long as it stays a trade secret under applicable law.

ARTICLE 9 — EQUITABLE REMEDIES

9.1 If you leak our Confidential Information or use it in a way this agreement prohibits, money damages alone probably won't make it right — an unreleased product could end up on a competitor's shelves, or our brand launch could be spoiled. So we can ask a court for an injunction or specific performance, without having to post a bond or prove actual damages, in addition to any other remedies we might have at law or in equity.
