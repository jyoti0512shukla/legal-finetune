MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} with its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} ("Party A"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} with its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} ("Party B"). Party A and Party B are each referred to herein as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties." Party A is a portfolio company of Summit Flagship Fund V, L.P. (the "Sponsor"), a private equity fund advised by Summit Capital Management, LLC.

WHEREAS, the Parties are exploring a potential bolt-on acquisition of Party B by Party A, which would be financed in part by additional equity capital contributed by the Sponsor (the "Potential Acquisition");

WHEREAS, in connection with evaluating the Potential Acquisition, each Party may disclose to the other certain non-public information, and Party A may also share certain Confidential Information with the Sponsor and, subject to the restrictions set forth herein, with the Sponsor's limited partners and advisors;

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to establish the terms on which such Confidential Information may be exchanged and to address the unique disclosures required where a private equity sponsor is actively involved in the evaluation;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, hereby agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly, through one or more intermediaries, Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with such Person. For the avoidance of doubt, the Sponsor and other funds advised by Summit Capital Management, LLC are Affiliates of Party A for purposes of this Agreement.

1.2 "Confidential Information" has the meaning set forth in Section 1.3, and "Disclosing Party" and "Receiving Party" have their customary meanings under mutual non-disclosure agreements.

1.3 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Disclosing Party to a Receiving Party or any of its Representatives in connection with the Purpose, in whatever form or medium, whether or not marked as confidential. Confidential Information includes, without limitation, financial statements and projections, management reports, operational data, customer and supplier information, product roadmaps, intellectual property, personnel information, and the existence and status of the discussions between the Parties regarding the Potential Acquisition.

1.4 "Purpose" means the Parties' good-faith evaluation of the Potential Acquisition, and (if and only if the Parties mutually so elect in their respective sole discretion) the negotiation and consummation of the Potential Acquisition.

1.5 "Representatives" means, with respect to a Receiving Party, such Receiving Party's Affiliates (including the Sponsor in the case of Party A), and its and their respective directors, officers, employees, attorneys, accountants, financial advisors, consultants, and (in the case of Party A only) the limited partners and investment committee members of the Sponsor, in each case to the extent such Persons have a bona fide need to know Confidential Information for the Purpose and are bound by written or professional obligations of confidentiality no less protective than the obligations set forth herein.

1.6 "Trade Secret" means any Confidential Information that qualifies as a trade secret under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016 or the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}} or any other applicable law.

ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

2.1 Narrow Purpose. The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose. The Sponsor's continued pursuit of other acquisitions in the same industry sector, whether through Party A or any other portfolio company of the Sponsor, is not a use prohibited by this Agreement, provided that no Confidential Information of Party B is used in any such unrelated pursuit.

2.2 No Reverse Engineering. The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, disassemble, or otherwise attempt to derive the composition, structure, or methods of manufacture of any tangible item of Confidential Information.

2.3 No Aggregate Use. Confidential Information shall not be aggregated with the Receiving Party's other information or used for any commercial analysis or research purpose other than the Purpose.

ARTICLE 3 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

3.1 Obligations. The Receiving Party shall: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (c) disclose Confidential Information only to its Representatives who have a bona fide need to know in connection with the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those set forth herein; and (d) protect Confidential Information using at least the degree of care the Receiving Party uses for its own most sensitive confidential information, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

3.2 Sponsor Flow-Down. Party A may disclose Confidential Information of Party B to the Sponsor and to the Sponsor's investment committee, limited partners, and fund advisors, provided that: (a) each such recipient has a bona fide need to know in connection with the Sponsor's evaluation of the capital contribution supporting the Potential Acquisition; (b) each such recipient is bound by confidentiality obligations (either under the Sponsor's limited partnership agreement, its side letters, or a separate written undertaking) no less protective than those set forth herein; and (c) Party A shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by the Sponsor or any of the Sponsor's limited partners, investment committee members, or fund advisors to whom Party A has disclosed Confidential Information.

3.3 Responsibility for Representatives. The Receiving Party shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives.

3.4 Incident Reporting. The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party in writing within three (3) business days after becoming aware of any actual or suspected unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information.

ARTICLE 4 — EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Exclusions. The obligations in Article 3 shall not apply to any information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by competent written evidence: (a) was in the public domain at the time of disclosure or subsequently entered the public domain through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was in the Receiving Party's possession free of any obligation of confidence prior to disclosure; (c) was independently developed by employees or contractors of the Receiving Party who had no access to Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party; or (d) was rightfully received from a third party without any obligation of confidence to the Disclosing Party.

4.2 Sponsor's Sector Intelligence. General information about the industry sector that the Sponsor has obtained through its ordinary course investment research, separate from and independent of Confidential Information disclosed by Party B, is not Confidential Information.

ARTICLE 5 — COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

5.1 Legal Process. In the event that the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required to disclose any Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party by legal process, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permissible, provide the Disclosing Party with prompt prior written notice so the Disclosing Party may, at its sole cost, seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

5.2 Limitation. If a protective order is not obtained and the Disclosing Party does not waive compliance, the Receiving Party or such Representative may disclose only the minimum portion of Confidential Information that its outside legal counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain assurances that confidential treatment will be accorded to such information. Non-disclosed portions shall continue to be treated as confidential.

ARTICLE 6 — RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

6.1 Return on Request. Upon the earlier of (a) the Disclosing Party's written request or (b) the termination of discussions between the Parties regarding the Potential Acquisition, the Receiving Party shall, at the Disclosing Party's election, return or destroy all Confidential Information together with all copies, extracts, and derivative materials, and shall certify such return or destruction in writing within ten (10) business days.

6.2 Retention Exceptions. The Receiving Party may retain (a) one archival copy of Confidential Information in the custody of its outside legal counsel, and (b) Confidential Information contained in automatic system backups, in each case subject to the continuing obligations of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 7 — NO LICENSE OR OWNERSHIP TRANSFER

7.1 Ownership Reserved. All Confidential Information shall remain the sole and exclusive property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting the Receiving Party, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, any license or other right in any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of the Disclosing Party.

7.2 No Obligation to Close. No obligation to enter into any further agreement with respect to the Potential Acquisition is created by this Agreement.

ARTICLE 8 — STANDSTILL

8.1 Twelve-Month Standstill. For a period of twelve (12) months following the Effective Date, neither Party shall, and each Party shall cause its Affiliates (including, in the case of Party A, the Sponsor) and their respective Representatives not to, without the prior written consent of the other Party's board of directors or board of managers: (a) acquire or propose to acquire any equity interests or material assets of the other Party outside the scope of the Potential Acquisition; (b) make, or participate in, any proxy solicitation or control contest; or (c) seek to influence the management, board, or policies of the other Party.

8.2 Board-Level Exception. Nothing in Section 8.1 shall prevent either Party from privately submitting a confidential written proposal to the other Party's board of directors or board of managers regarding the Potential Acquisition.

ARTICLE 9 — NON-SOLICITATION

9.1 Senior Management Non-Solicit. For a period of eighteen (18) months following the Effective Date, neither Party shall, and each Party shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly solicit for employment any member of the other Party's senior management team whose identity first became known to such Party in connection with the Purpose.

9.2 General Solicitation Carve-Out. General advertising, job postings, and unsolicited approaches by prospective candidates are not restricted by Section 9.1.

ARTICLE 10 — TERM AND SURVIVAL

10.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force and effect for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years thereafter.

10.2 Survival. Notwithstanding any termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality and non-use with respect to Confidential Information shall survive for a period of three (3) years after such termination or expiration. With respect to any Trade Secret, such obligations shall survive for so long as the information remains a Trade Secret under applicable law.

10.3 Termination for Breach. Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' prior written notice in the event of a material uncured breach by the other Party.

ARTICLE 11 — EQUITABLE REMEDIES

11.1 Irreparable Harm. Each Party acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate.

11.2 Injunctive Relief. Each Party is entitled to seek specific performance, injunctive relief, and other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction, without posting bond and without proving actual damages, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity. Remedies are cumulative.

ARTICLE 12 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

12.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to conflict of laws principles.

12.2 Venue. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

12.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.

12.4 Amendments. No amendment of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

12.5 Assignment. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Party A may assign this Agreement to any entity Controlled by the Sponsor in connection with the Potential Acquisition.

12.6 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

12.7 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Agreement to be executed as of the Effective Date.
