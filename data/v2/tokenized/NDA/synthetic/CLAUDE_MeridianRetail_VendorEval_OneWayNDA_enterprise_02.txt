CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} ("Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} ("Vendor"). The Company and Vendor are each referred to herein as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

This is a one-way confidentiality agreement. The Company is the "Disclosing Party" and Vendor is the "Receiving Party." Vendor acknowledges that the sensitive nature of the Company's operations, its status as a publicly traded retail enterprise, and the competitive landscape in which the Company operates require rigorous protection of the information disclosed hereunder.

WHEREAS, the Company is considering whether to engage Vendor to provide an enterprise commerce and fulfillment orchestration platform for use across the Company's retail operations (the "Platform");

WHEREAS, for Vendor to meaningfully propose, scope, and demonstrate the Platform's fit for the Company's operations, the Company is prepared to disclose to Vendor certain non-public information regarding the Company's business, systems, data flows, store operations, vendor relationships, customer experience, and technology environment; and

WHEREAS, the Company is willing to disclose such information only on the express terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement, which Vendor has reviewed and accepted;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing recitals and the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, hereby agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly, through one or more intermediaries, Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with such Person.

1.2 "Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information of or relating to the Company or its Affiliates, whether disclosed in writing, orally, electronically, visually, by inspection of tangible items, or in any other form or medium, whether before or after the Effective Date, whether or not marked or designated as "confidential," "proprietary," or with any similar legend, that a reasonable person in the position of Vendor would recognize as confidential or proprietary based on the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes, without limitation: (a) financial information, including sales data, margins, unit economics, forecasts, and store-level performance metrics; (b) customer information, including customer lists, transaction histories, loyalty program data, and personally identifiable information of the Company's customers; (c) supplier and merchandising information, including vendor terms, category plans, buy sheets, and assortment strategies; (d) technology information, including systems architecture, data models, source code (if any), integration diagrams, and security configurations; (e) operational information, including store layouts, workforce planning, fulfillment flows, and loss-prevention methods; (f) strategic information, including M&A plans, real estate strategy, and marketing calendars; and (g) the existence, subject matter, and content of the discussions between the Parties regarding the Platform.

1.3 "Evaluation Period" means the period during which Vendor is engaged in the evaluation of the Purpose, commencing on the Effective Date and ending on the earlier of (a) the Company's written notice that the evaluation is concluded, (b) the execution of a definitive master agreement between the Parties for the Platform, or (c) the termination of this Agreement.

1.4 "Person" means any natural person, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, governmental authority, or other entity.

1.5 "Purpose" means the sole purpose of Vendor preparing and presenting a proposal to the Company for the Company's potential licensing and implementation of the Platform, and associated discussions, demonstrations, technical diligence sessions, and negotiations between the Parties concerning such proposal. The Purpose does not include any use of Confidential Information to serve any other customer of Vendor, to inform any Vendor product roadmap decision, or to support any unrelated business activity of Vendor or its Affiliates.

1.6 "Representatives" means, with respect to Vendor, Vendor's employees, and (to the extent expressly approved in advance in writing by the Company) its outside legal counsel and independent financial advisors, in each case who have a bona fide need to know Confidential Information for the Purpose and who are bound by written obligations of confidentiality and non-use no less protective than those set forth in this Agreement. For the avoidance of doubt, Vendor's subcontractors, offshore resources, and third-party consultants are not "Representatives" without the Company's specific prior written approval.

1.7 "Trade Secret" means any item of Confidential Information that qualifies as a trade secret under the Uniform Trade Secrets Act as enacted in {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}} or any other applicable law.

ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

2.1 Narrow Purpose. Vendor shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever. Vendor shall not use Confidential Information for Vendor's own business operations, for the benefit of any other customer, for product roadmap planning, for marketing or sales to any Person other than the Company, or for any other purpose outside the scope of the Purpose.

2.2 No Reverse Engineering. Vendor shall not reverse engineer, disassemble, decompile, or otherwise attempt to derive the composition, structure, or methods of operation of any tangible item of Confidential Information, including any source code samples, configuration files, or system diagrams made available for inspection during the Purpose.

2.3 No Competitive Advantage. Vendor shall not use Confidential Information to obtain a competitive advantage against the Company, its Affiliates, or any other customer of Vendor. Vendor shall not use any Confidential Information to target, solicit, bid for, or otherwise pursue any customer, supplier, real estate asset, or employee of the Company.

2.4 No Residuals. This Agreement contains no "residuals" clause. Vendor expressly acknowledges that the retention of Confidential Information in the unaided memory of any Vendor Representative shall not authorize any use or disclosure that would otherwise be prohibited by this Agreement.

ARTICLE 3 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

3.1 Vendor's Obligations. Vendor shall: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose as set forth in Article 2; (c) disclose Confidential Information only to those of Vendor's Representatives who have a bona fide need to know such Confidential Information for the Purpose and who are bound by written obligations of confidentiality and non-use no less protective than those set forth herein; and (d) protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care that Vendor uses to protect its own most sensitive confidential information, but in no event less than a commercially reasonable degree of care under the circumstances.

3.2 Responsibility for Representatives. Vendor shall be fully responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives. Any act or omission by any Representative of Vendor that would constitute a breach of this Agreement if taken or omitted by Vendor itself shall be deemed a breach by Vendor.

3.3 Notification of Unauthorized Disclosure. Vendor shall notify the Company in writing promptly, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours, after Vendor becomes aware of any actual or suspected unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information, whether by Vendor, any of its Representatives, or any third party. Such notification shall include a reasonable description of the circumstances of the unauthorized use or disclosure, the Confidential Information affected, and the remedial steps taken and planned by Vendor. Vendor shall cooperate fully with the Company in investigating, mitigating, and remedying any unauthorized use or disclosure.

3.4 Information Security Audit. Upon not less than ten (10) business days' prior written notice, and no more frequently than once per calendar year during the Evaluation Period and for a period of twelve (12) months following its expiration, the Company (or a third-party auditor engaged by the Company and bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those set forth herein) may audit Vendor's information security controls, policies, and practices as applied to Confidential Information of the Company. Vendor shall cooperate in good faith with any such audit and shall promptly remediate, at Vendor's sole cost and expense, any material deficiencies in Vendor's handling of Confidential Information identified during such audit.

ARTICLE 4 — EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Exclusions. The obligations of Vendor under Article 3 shall not apply to any information that Vendor can demonstrate by competent written evidence:

(a) was in the public domain at the time of disclosure to Vendor or subsequently entered the public domain through no act or omission of Vendor or any of its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was in Vendor's possession, free of any obligation of confidence, prior to the time of its disclosure to Vendor by the Company;

(c) was independently developed by Vendor employees who had no access to Confidential Information of the Company and without any use of or reference to Confidential Information of the Company; or

(d) was rightfully received by Vendor from a third party who was not, to Vendor's knowledge after reasonable inquiry, under any obligation of confidence to the Company with respect to such information.

4.2 Combination Principle. No combination of individually public features or features known to Vendor shall be deemed to fall within any of the foregoing exclusions unless the combination itself and its principle of operation were in the public domain or in Vendor's lawful possession prior to disclosure hereunder.

ARTICLE 5 — COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

5.1 Compelled Disclosure by Legal Process. In the event that Vendor or any of its Representatives is requested or required by any court, governmental authority, or other legal process (including by subpoena, interrogatory, civil investigative demand, or similar process) to disclose any Confidential Information of the Company, Vendor shall, to the extent legally permissible, provide the Company with prompt prior written notice of such request or requirement so that the Company may, at its sole cost, seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy or (at the Company's sole election) waive compliance with this Agreement with respect to such disclosure.

5.2 Limitation on Compelled Disclosure. If a protective order is not obtained and the Company does not waive compliance, Vendor or such Representative may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that Vendor's outside legal counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed. Vendor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain assurances that confidential treatment will be accorded to the Confidential Information so disclosed. Vendor shall continue to treat all non-disclosed portions of the Confidential Information as confidential in accordance with this Agreement.

ARTICLE 6 — RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

6.1 Return or Destruction on Request. Promptly, and in any event within ten (10) business days, after the Company's written request (which the Company may make at any time in its sole discretion), Vendor shall, at the Company's election, either (a) return to the Company all Confidential Information in Vendor's or its Representatives' possession, custody or control, together with all copies, extracts, and derivative materials thereof, whether in tangible or electronic form, or (b) destroy all such Confidential Information, copies, extracts, and derivative materials.

6.2 Sworn Certification. Within fifteen (15) business days after the return or destruction required under Section 6.1, an executive officer of Vendor shall deliver to the Company a sworn certification (in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company) stating that Vendor has fully complied with Section 6.1 and that no Confidential Information remains in Vendor's or its Representatives' possession except for the limited exceptions expressly permitted by Section 6.3.

6.3 Limited Retention Exceptions. Notwithstanding Section 6.1, Vendor may retain: (a) one (1) archival copy of Confidential Information in the custody of its outside legal counsel solely for the purpose of establishing the scope of Vendor's obligations and Vendor's compliance with this Agreement; and (b) Confidential Information contained in automatic, tamper-resistant system backups made in the ordinary course of Vendor's business, provided that such retained backups are not accessed in the ordinary course of Vendor's operations. Any Confidential Information so retained shall remain subject to the confidentiality and non-use obligations of this Agreement for so long as it is retained, notwithstanding any expiration or termination of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 7 — NO LICENSE OR OWNERSHIP TRANSFER

7.1 Ownership Reserved. All Confidential Information shall remain the sole and exclusive property of the Company. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as transferring any ownership interest in Confidential Information to Vendor, or as granting Vendor any license or other right, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, in or to any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of the Company or any of its Affiliates.

7.2 Rights Reserved. the Company reserves all rights in and to its Confidential Information, including without limitation all patent, copyright, trademark, and trade secret rights. No obligation to enter into any further agreement between the Parties, including without limitation any definitive master agreement for the Platform, is created by this Agreement.

ARTICLE 8 — TERM AND SURVIVAL

8.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force and effect until the expiration of the Evaluation Period, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Section 8.3.

8.2 Survival of Confidentiality Obligations. Notwithstanding any expiration or termination of this Agreement, Vendor's obligations of confidentiality and non-use with respect to Confidential Information shall survive for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years after the date of expiration or termination of this Agreement. With respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a Trade Secret, Vendor's obligations of confidentiality and non-use shall survive for so long as such information continues to qualify as a Trade Secret under applicable law.

8.3 Termination for Material Breach. the Company may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice to Vendor in the event of any material breach of this Agreement by Vendor. Vendor may terminate this Agreement upon {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' prior written notice to the Company in the event of a material breach of this Agreement by the Company that has not been cured within such notice period. No termination of this Agreement shall relieve either Party of any liability for any breach occurring prior to such termination or of any of Vendor's surviving obligations under Section 8.2.

ARTICLE 9 — EQUITABLE REMEDIES

9.1 Irreparable Harm. Vendor acknowledges and agrees that the Company's Confidential Information is of a special, unique, and extraordinary character, the loss or unauthorized disclosure of which would cause the Company irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be an inadequate remedy. Vendor further acknowledges that money damages alone would not provide the Company with an adequate remedy in the event of any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement by Vendor or any of its Representatives.

9.2 Injunctive Relief. Accordingly, the Company shall be entitled, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, to seek specific performance, injunctive relief, and other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to prevent or restrain any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement, without the necessity of posting a bond or other security and without the necessity of proving actual damages.

9.3 Cumulative Remedies. The rights and remedies of the Company under this Agreement are cumulative and in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other rights and remedies available to the Company at law, in equity, or under any other agreement between the Parties.
