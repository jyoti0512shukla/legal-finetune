CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT FOR CLOUD OUTSOURCING DUE DILIGENCE

THIS CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date"), between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} with its registered office at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Bank"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} with its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} ("Vendor"). The Bank and Vendor are each referred to herein as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties." For the avoidance of doubt, this Agreement is provided on the Bank's standard paper, reflects the Bank's non-negotiable baseline terms for outsourcing due diligence, and does not constitute any commitment by the Bank to engage Vendor or to consider Vendor further.

WHEREAS, the Bank is a federally regulated financial institution subject to the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions ("OSFI") Guideline B-10 (Third-Party Risk Management) and Guideline B-13 (Technology and Cyber Risk Management), and the Bank's handling of any information disclosed by Vendor in the context of outsourcing due diligence, and Vendor's handling of any information disclosed by the Bank, must comply with the Bank's regulatory obligations;

WHEREAS, the Bank is evaluating whether to enter into a potential outsourcing arrangement with Vendor for the provision of certain technology services (the "Potential Engagement"), and in connection with such evaluation the Bank will disclose to Vendor certain non-public information regarding the Bank's business, technology environment, and customer base;

WHEREAS, the Bank requires that Vendor execute this Agreement, in the form presented and without material modification, as a pre-condition to the Bank making any non-public information available to Vendor;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises, the mutual covenants set forth herein, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, hereby agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly, through one or more intermediaries, Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with such Person.

1.2 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of the Bank to Vendor or any of its Representatives in connection with the Purpose, in whatever form or medium (whether written, oral, electronic, visual, or otherwise), whether or not marked as confidential, together with all analyses, compilations, studies, forecasts, summaries, memoranda, notes, and other documents or materials (in any medium) prepared by Vendor or its Representatives that contain, are based upon, reflect, or are derived from any such information. Confidential Information includes, without limitation: (a) customer names, account information, transaction histories, and personal information protected by the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act ("PIPEDA"); (b) the Bank's current and projected IT architecture, security controls, incident history, and vendor landscape; (c) the Bank's internal risk assessments, audit findings, OSFI correspondence, and regulatory communications; (d) any information relating to the Bank's outsourcing due diligence process, including scoring criteria, competing vendor evaluations, and commercial terms; and (e) the existence and subject matter of the discussions between the Bank and Vendor regarding the Potential Engagement.

1.3 "Personal Information" means any information about an identifiable individual, as defined under PIPEDA, that is contained within Confidential Information.

1.4 "Purpose" means Vendor's good-faith participation in the Bank's outsourcing due diligence process with respect to the Potential Engagement, and nothing else.

1.5 "Regulator" means OSFI, the Financial Consumer Agency of Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, and any other Canadian federal or provincial regulator with jurisdiction over the Bank.

1.6 "Representatives" means Vendor's employees and (to the extent expressly approved in advance in writing by the Bank) outside legal counsel, in each case who have a bona fide need to know Confidential Information for the Purpose and who are individually bound by written obligations of confidentiality and non-use no less protective than the obligations set forth herein. For the avoidance of doubt, Vendor's subcontractors, offshore resources, and third-party consultants are not "Representatives" without the Bank's specific prior written approval.

1.7 "Trade Secret" means any Confidential Information that qualifies as a trade secret under the laws of the Province of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, under federal Canadian law, or under any other applicable law.

ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

2.1 Vendor shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever. Vendor shall not use Confidential Information for Vendor's own business operations, for the benefit of any other customer of Vendor, for product roadmap or commercial planning, or in any manner not strictly necessary for Vendor's participation in the Bank's outsourcing due diligence process.

2.2 Vendor shall not use Confidential Information to obtain a competitive advantage against the Bank, its Affiliates, or any other customer of Vendor, and shall not use Confidential Information to inform Vendor's pricing, bidding, or positioning for any other procurement or outsourcing opportunity involving any other Canadian financial institution.

2.3 Vendor shall not reverse engineer, disassemble, decompile, or otherwise attempt to derive the composition, structure, or methods of operation of any tangible item of Confidential Information, including any architecture diagram, source code sample, configuration file, or system walk-through made available for inspection during the Purpose.

2.4 No Residuals. This Agreement contains no "residuals" clause. Vendor expressly acknowledges that the retention of Confidential Information in the unaided memory of any Vendor Representative shall not authorize any use or disclosure that would otherwise be prohibited by this Agreement.

ARTICLE 3 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

3.1 Obligations of Vendor. Vendor shall: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose in accordance with Article 2; (c) disclose Confidential Information only to those of Vendor's Representatives who have a bona fide need to know such Confidential Information for the Purpose and who are bound by written obligations of confidentiality and non-use no less protective than those set forth herein; and (d) protect Confidential Information using at least the degree of care Vendor uses to protect its own most sensitive confidential information, but in no event less than a commercially reasonable degree of care under the circumstances.

3.2 PIPEDA Compliance. To the extent any Confidential Information contains Personal Information, Vendor shall (a) limit its collection, use, and disclosure of such Personal Information to what is strictly necessary for the Purpose, (b) maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of such Personal Information, and (c) not transfer such Personal Information outside Canada without the Bank's prior written consent. Vendor acknowledges that the Bank is accountable for the Personal Information of its customers under PIPEDA and that any failure by Vendor to comply with this Section 3.2 may expose the Bank to regulatory liability.

3.3 Responsibility for Representatives. Vendor shall be fully responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives. Any act or omission by any Representative of Vendor that would constitute a breach of this Agreement if taken or omitted by Vendor itself shall be deemed a breach by Vendor.

3.4 Incident Reporting. Vendor shall notify the Bank (at the Bank's Chief Privacy Officer and Chief Information Security Officer) in writing immediately, and in any event within twelve (12) hours, after Vendor becomes aware of any actual or suspected unauthorized use, disclosure, loss, or exposure of Confidential Information. Such notification shall include a reasonable description of the circumstances, the Confidential Information affected, whether any Personal Information may have been affected, and any remedial steps taken. Vendor shall cooperate fully with the Bank in investigating, mitigating, and remedying any such incident, including by providing access to Vendor's incident response team and relevant records on the Bank's reasonable request.

3.5 Regulator Pass-Through. Vendor acknowledges that OSFI and other Regulators may exercise their statutory powers to access books, records, information, and premises of the Bank and its third-party service providers, including Vendor, in connection with the Bank's outsourcing arrangements. Vendor agrees to cooperate with any such Regulator access request involving Confidential Information and shall not assert confidentiality obligations in this Agreement as a basis to refuse or delay such Regulator access.

ARTICLE 4 — EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Exclusions. The obligations set forth in Article 3 shall not apply to any information that Vendor can demonstrate by competent written evidence:

(a) was in the public domain at the time of disclosure to Vendor, or subsequently entered the public domain through no act or omission of Vendor or any of its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was in Vendor's possession, free of any obligation of confidence, prior to the time of its disclosure to Vendor by the Bank;

(c) was independently developed by employees or contractors of Vendor who had no access to Confidential Information of the Bank and without any use of or reference to any Confidential Information of the Bank; or

(d) was rightfully received by Vendor from a third party who was not, to Vendor's knowledge after reasonable inquiry, under any obligation of confidence to the Bank with respect to such information.

4.2 Personal Information Never Excluded. For the avoidance of doubt, Personal Information of the Bank's customers shall not fall within any of the exclusions in Section 4.1 unless Vendor can demonstrate that the Personal Information is lawfully held by Vendor from a source independent of the Bank and the Bank's customers.

ARTICLE 5 — COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

5.1 Legal Process. In the event that Vendor or any of its Representatives is requested or required by any court, governmental authority, or other legal process to disclose any Confidential Information of the Bank, Vendor shall, to the extent legally permissible, provide the Bank with prompt prior written notice of such request or requirement so that the Bank may, at its sole cost, seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy or (at the Bank's sole election) waive compliance with this Agreement with respect to such disclosure.

5.2 Limitation on Compelled Disclosure. If a protective order is not obtained and the Bank does not waive compliance, Vendor may disclose only the minimum portion of Confidential Information that Vendor's outside legal counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed, and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain assurances that confidential treatment will be accorded to such Confidential Information. Vendor shall continue to treat all non-disclosed portions as confidential.

5.3 Regulator Inquiries. For the avoidance of doubt, any inquiry or request from a Regulator directed at Vendor that relates to Confidential Information of the Bank is a "request or requirement" subject to this Article 5, and Vendor shall route any such inquiry through the Bank's compliance function before responding, to the extent legally permissible.

ARTICLE 6 — RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

6.1 Return or Destruction on Request. Promptly, and in any event within five (5) business days, after the Bank's written request (which the Bank may make at any time, at its sole discretion, for any reason or no reason), Vendor shall, at the Bank's election, either (a) return to the Bank all Confidential Information in Vendor's or its Representatives' possession, custody or control, together with all copies, extracts, and derivative materials, or (b) destroy all such Confidential Information, copies, extracts, and derivative materials, including by secure deletion in the case of electronic records.

6.2 Sworn Destruction Certificate. Within ten (10) business days after the return or destruction required under Section 6.1, an officer of Vendor shall deliver to the Bank a sworn destruction certificate, in a form satisfactory to the Bank, stating that Vendor has fully complied with Section 6.1 and identifying by category the Confidential Information destroyed and the method of destruction.

6.3 Retention Exception. Notwithstanding Section 6.1, Vendor may retain: (a) one (1) archival copy of Confidential Information in the custody of its outside legal counsel, solely for the purpose of establishing Vendor's compliance with this Agreement; and (b) Confidential Information contained in automatic, tamper-resistant system backups made in the ordinary course of Vendor's business. Any Confidential Information so retained shall remain subject to the obligations of this Agreement for so long as it is retained, notwithstanding any termination of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 7 — NO LICENSE OR OWNERSHIP TRANSFER

7.1 Ownership Reserved. All Confidential Information shall remain the sole and exclusive property of the Bank. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as transferring any ownership interest to Vendor, or as granting Vendor any license or other right, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, in or to any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of the Bank.

7.2 All Rights Reserved. The Bank reserves all rights in and to its Confidential Information and makes no commitment to enter into any further agreement with Vendor, including the Potential Engagement.

ARTICLE 8 — TERM AND SURVIVAL

8.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force and effect for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years thereafter, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Section 8.3.

8.2 Survival. Notwithstanding any expiration or termination of this Agreement, Vendor's obligations of confidentiality and non-use shall survive for a period of seven (7) years after the date of expiration or termination. With respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a Trade Secret or that contains Personal Information, Vendor's obligations shall survive for so long as such information remains a Trade Secret or Personal Information under applicable law.

8.3 Bank Termination Right. The Bank may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or for no reason, upon {{NOTICE_DAYS}} business days' prior written notice to Vendor (or immediately in the event of a material breach or a Regulator directive). Termination by the Bank shall not relieve Vendor of its surviving obligations.

ARTICLE 9 — EQUITABLE REMEDIES

9.1 Irreparable Harm. Vendor acknowledges and agrees that the Bank's Confidential Information is of a special, unique, and extraordinary character, the loss or unauthorized disclosure of which may cause the Bank irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be an inadequate remedy.

9.2 Injunctive Relief. Accordingly, the Bank shall be entitled, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, to seek specific performance, injunctive relief, and other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to prevent or restrain any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement, without the necessity of posting a bond or other security and without the necessity of proving actual damages.

9.3 Cumulative Remedies. The rights and remedies of the Bank under this Agreement are cumulative and in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other rights and remedies available to the Bank at law, in equity, or under any other agreement between the Parties.

Vendor may not assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, or delegate any of its obligations hereunder, without the prior written consent of the Bank, which consent may be withheld in the Bank's sole and absolute discretion. Any purported assignment or delegation in violation of this Section shall be void.

10.6 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be deemed modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable while preserving the Bank's original intent that Vendor's confidentiality obligations be enforced to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law.

10.7 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Delivery of an executed counterpart by electronic means shall be as effective as delivery of an originally executed counterpart.
