NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT FOR UNRELEASED CONTENT SCREENING

This NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date"), by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} with its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} ("Studio"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} with its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} ("Distributor"). Studio and Distributor are each referred to herein as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

WHEREAS, Studio is producing an unreleased feature-length motion picture and related marketing materials (collectively, the "Unreleased Content");

WHEREAS, in connection with a potential distribution arrangement, Studio is willing to make the Unreleased Content available for confidential screening by Distributor and certain authorized Representatives;

WHEREAS, the unauthorized disclosure or leak of any part of the Unreleased Content prior to Studio's general release would cause substantial, difficult-to-quantify harm to Studio, including diminution of box office revenue, loss of promotional value, and disclosure of creative decisions competitors could exploit;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Confidential Information" means the Unreleased Content, all copies and excerpts of the Unreleased Content, and any non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of Studio to Distributor in connection with the Purpose, in whatever form or medium. Confidential Information includes, without limitation, (a) any video, audio, still images, or text from the Unreleased Content; (b) the identity of cast members, crew, creative talent, and creative collaborators not yet publicly announced; (c) plot details, spoilers, twist endings, and narrative structure; (d) unreleased marketing materials, key art, trailers, and promotional strategy; (e) theatrical release plans, territorial strategies, and release date information; and (f) budget, production cost, and projected box office information.

1.2 "Screener" means any copy of the Unreleased Content delivered to Distributor in any form (including digital streaming, download, or physical media) for screening under this Agreement.

1.3 "Forensic Watermark" means a unique, individualized watermark or identifier embedded in each Screener that permits Studio to trace the source of any copy that is leaked or distributed in violation of this Agreement.

1.4 "Purpose" means Distributor's internal evaluation of the Unreleased Content for a potential distribution arrangement with Studio, conducted through controlled screenings by designated Representatives, and nothing else.

1.5 "Representatives" means Distributor's directors, officers, employees, and outside advisors who have been specifically identified to Studio in writing prior to any access to a Screener, who have a bona fide need to view the Screener in connection with the Purpose, and who have agreed in writing to be bound by the terms of this Agreement.

1.6 "Trade Secret" means any Confidential Information that qualifies as a trade secret under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016 or the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}} or any other applicable law.

ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

2.1 Narrow Purpose. Distributor shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever.

2.2 No Public Commentary. Distributor shall not, and shall cause its Representatives not to: (a) publish any review, critique, commentary, or reaction to the Unreleased Content in any form; (b) post about the Unreleased Content on any social media platform, blog, newsletter, podcast, or livestream; (c) share any spoilers, plot details, or promotional material with any third party; or (d) confirm or deny the existence of any particular scene, cast member, or plot element from the Unreleased Content. These restrictions apply during the term of this Agreement and continue until Studio's general theatrical release of the Unreleased Content, after which the restrictions shall be released only with respect to information made publicly available by Studio.

2.3 Screening Restrictions. Distributor shall view each Screener only under the following controls: (a) on a single designated viewing device at a time, (b) in a private location not accessible to the general public, (c) without recording, photographing, or capturing any portion of the Screener through any means, including cameras, screen capture software, or audio recorders, and (d) with any forensic watermark intact and unmodified.

2.4 No Copying or Distribution. Distributor shall not copy, duplicate, transcode, redistribute, forward, upload, screen-share, or otherwise reproduce or transmit any Screener or any portion thereof.

ARTICLE 3 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND CONTENT PROTECTION

3.1 Obligations. Distributor shall: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (c) disclose Confidential Information only to Representatives who have been identified to Studio in writing and who are bound by written obligations of confidentiality and non-use no less protective than those set forth herein; and (d) protect Confidential Information using at least the degree of care Distributor uses for its own unreleased content, but no less than a high degree of care given the significant risk of harm from unauthorized disclosure.

3.2 Content Protection Compliance. Distributor shall maintain content protection practices consistent with the Motion Picture Association's Content Security Best Practices and shall implement any additional security controls reasonably requested by Studio, including forensic watermarking, access logging, secure storage, and tamper detection.

3.3 Forensic Watermarking. Distributor acknowledges that each Screener contains a unique Forensic Watermark traceable to Distributor, and that Studio may use digital fingerprinting, watermark extraction, and forensic analysis to trace the source of any leaked Screener. In the event Studio identifies a leak traceable to Distributor or any of its Representatives, such identification shall be presumptive evidence of a breach of this Agreement.

3.4 Responsibility for Representatives. Distributor shall be fully responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives.

3.5 Incident Reporting. Distributor shall notify Studio in writing immediately, and in any event within two (2) hours, after becoming aware of any actual or suspected unauthorized disclosure, copy, leak, publication, or loss of a Screener or any portion of Confidential Information. Distributor shall cooperate fully with Studio's investigation and remediation efforts, including providing access to Distributor's systems, access logs, and personnel as reasonably requested.

ARTICLE 4 — EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Exclusions. The obligations in Article 3 shall not apply to any information that Distributor can demonstrate by competent written evidence: (a) was in the public domain at the time of disclosure or subsequently entered the public domain through no breach of this Agreement or any other nondisclosure obligation; (b) was in Distributor's possession free of any obligation of confidence prior to disclosure; (c) was independently developed by Distributor without use of or reference to any Confidential Information; or (d) was rightfully received from a third party without any obligation of confidence to Studio.

4.2 No Aggregation of Public Material. The fact that individual items of publicly available marketing material (such as the official public trailer, cast announcements, or press releases) are in the public domain does not excuse any disclosure of Confidential Information that goes beyond such public material.

ARTICLE 5 — COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

5.1 Legal Process. If Distributor or any of its Representatives is requested or required by any court, governmental authority, or other legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, Distributor shall, to the extent legally permissible, provide Studio with prompt prior written notice so Studio may, at its sole cost, seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

5.2 Limitation. If a protective order is not obtained and Studio does not waive compliance, Distributor may disclose only the minimum portion of Confidential Information legally required to be disclosed, shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain assurances of confidential treatment, and shall continue to treat non-disclosed portions as confidential.

ARTICLE 6 — RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

6.1 Return on Request. Upon the earlier of (a) Studio's written request, (b) Distributor's determination not to proceed with a distribution arrangement, or (c) the conclusion of the Purpose, Distributor shall, at Studio's election, return or destroy all Screeners and all other Confidential Information, together with all copies, and shall certify such return or destruction in writing within five (5) business days.

6.2 No Retained Copies. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, Distributor shall not retain any copy of any Screener or any copy of the Unreleased Content after the return or destruction required under Section 6.1, whether in backups or otherwise. Distributor shall take affirmative steps to ensure that no Screener or Unreleased Content remains in any system backup, email archive, or other storage system.

ARTICLE 7 — NO LICENSE OR OWNERSHIP TRANSFER

7.1 Ownership Reserved. All Confidential Information and all Screeners shall remain the sole and exclusive property of Studio. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting Distributor, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, any license or other right in any copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of Studio, except for the limited right to view the Screeners during a controlled screening for the Purpose.

ARTICLE 8 — TERM AND SURVIVAL

8.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force and effect until the earlier of (a) {{TERM_YEARS}} years after the Effective Date, or (b) Studio's general theatrical release of the Unreleased Content.

8.2 Survival. Distributor's obligations of confidentiality and non-use with respect to Confidential Information shall survive any termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years, and with respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a Trade Secret, for so long as such information remains a Trade Secret under applicable law. Obligations relating to any Screener shall survive indefinitely.

ARTICLE 9 — EQUITABLE REMEDIES

9.1 Irreparable Harm. Distributor acknowledges that any unauthorized disclosure, leak, or distribution of any Screener or Unreleased Content would cause Studio irreparable harm that cannot be adequately compensated by monetary damages alone, including but not limited to diminution of box office revenue, loss of promotional momentum, and disclosure of creative decisions to competitors.

9.2 Injunctive Relief. Studio shall be entitled to seek immediate specific performance, injunctive relief, and other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to prevent or restrain any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement, without the necessity of posting a bond or other security and without the necessity of proving actual damages. Remedies are cumulative and in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

9.3 Liquidated-Damages Acknowledgment. Distributor acknowledges that actual damages from a leak of the Unreleased Content may be difficult to quantify with precision, and nothing in this Agreement limits Studio's right to prove such damages by any available means, including industry-standard measures of lost box office revenue, diminished viewership, and brand injury. For the avoidance of doubt, the Parties have not agreed to a liquidated damages figure in this Agreement; actual damages shall be provable by Studio using all evidence reasonably available.

ARTICLE 10 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to conflict of laws principles.

10.2 Venue. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

10.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.

10.4 Amendments. No amendment of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

10.5 Assignment. Distributor may not assign this Agreement without Studio's prior written consent, which may be withheld in Studio's sole and absolute discretion. Studio may assign this Agreement freely in connection with a corporate transaction.

10.6 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

10.7 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
