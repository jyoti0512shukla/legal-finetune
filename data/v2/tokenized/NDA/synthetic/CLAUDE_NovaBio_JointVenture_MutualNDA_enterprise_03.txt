MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} ("Party A"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} ("Party B"). Party A and Party B are each referred to herein as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Parties are engaged in preliminary, non-binding discussions concerning the potential formation of a joint venture or other collaborative commercial arrangement for the co-development and commercialization of a particular portfolio of early-stage therapeutic candidates (the "Potential JV");

WHEREAS, for the Parties to meaningfully evaluate the Potential JV, each Party may, from time to time, disclose to the other Party certain non-public information regarding such Party's research pipeline, scientific data, regulatory interactions, commercial plans, and financial position;

WHEREAS, Party A and Party B are each publicly traded companies subject to the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, Regulation FD promulgated thereunder, and the pre-closing conduct limitations of the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, and each Party desires to structure its information exchange with the other Party in a manner that is consistent with those requirements;

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to establish the terms and conditions upon which such Confidential Information may be disclosed and received, and to segregate competitively sensitive information from general Confidential Information through the adoption of a clean-team protocol;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing recitals and the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, hereby agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly, through one or more intermediaries, Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with such Person.

1.2 "Clean Team" means, with respect to each Party, a group of individuals designated in writing by such Party in accordance with Article 4 who are authorized to review Ring-Fenced Competitive Information of the other Party.

1.3 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information, in whatever form or medium (whether written, oral, electronic, visual, or otherwise), that is disclosed by or on behalf of a Disclosing Party to a Receiving Party or any of its Representatives in connection with the Purpose, together with all analyses, compilations, studies, forecasts, summaries, memoranda, notes, and other documents or materials (in any medium) prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives that contain, are based upon, reflect, or are derived from any such information. Confidential Information includes, without limitation, pre-clinical and clinical data, manufacturing information, chemistry-manufacturing-controls (CMC) data, regulatory submissions and correspondence with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and foreign regulators, intellectual property strategy, research plans, financial projections, commercial plans, and the existence and status of the Parties' discussions regarding the Potential JV. Information shall be treated as Confidential Information whether or not marked as such, provided that a reasonable person in the position of the Receiving Party would understand from the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure that it is confidential or proprietary.

1.4 "Disclosing Party" means, with respect to any item of Confidential Information, the Party (or an Affiliate of such Party acting on its behalf) that disclosed such item. "Receiving Party" means the Party to which such item was disclosed.

1.5 "Person" means any natural person, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, governmental authority, or other entity.

1.6 "Purpose" means the sole purpose of evaluating, negotiating, and (if and only if the Parties mutually so elect in their respective sole discretion) consummating a definitive agreement for the Potential JV. The Purpose does not include any use of Confidential Information in any operational, commercial, competitive, product-development, or regulatory activity unrelated to the Potential JV, and does not include any use of Confidential Information to inform either Party's independent decisions regarding its own business pending the execution of any definitive agreement.

1.7 "Representatives" means, with respect to a Receiving Party, such Receiving Party's Affiliates, and its and their respective directors, officers, employees, attorneys, accountants, financial advisors, and consultants, in each case to the extent that such Persons have a bona fide need to know Confidential Information for the Purpose and have been informed of the confidential nature of such information and the terms of this Agreement.

1.8 "Ring-Fenced Competitive Information" means any Confidential Information that the Disclosing Party designates in writing as "Ring-Fenced," including without limitation: (a) current or forward-looking pricing, discounting, or rebate terms; (b) current or forward-looking customer-level commercial terms; (c) marketing plans for any product that is on the market; (d) detailed manufacturing cost structures; and (e) any other information that, if disclosed to commercial or research personnel of the Receiving Party outside the Clean Team, would pose a material risk of competitive harm to the Disclosing Party.

1.9 "Trade Secret" means any Confidential Information that qualifies as a trade secret under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016 or the Uniform Trade Secrets Act as enacted in {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}} or any other applicable law.

ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

2.1 Narrow Purpose. Each Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purpose does not include the advancement of either Party's independent research, clinical, regulatory, manufacturing, or commercial activities, and no Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party may be used for any such purpose.

2.2 No Competitive Advantage. Each Receiving Party shall not use Confidential Information of the other Party, directly or indirectly, to obtain a competitive advantage against the Disclosing Party in any line of business, including without limitation in any clinical or commercial program of the Disclosing Party.

2.3 No Reverse Engineering. Neither Party shall reverse engineer, disassemble, or otherwise attempt to derive the composition, structure, formulation, or methods of manufacture of any physical sample, compound, or tangible research material provided by the other Party in connection with the Purpose.

2.4 HSR Gun-Jumping Acknowledgment. Each Party acknowledges and agrees that, pending the execution of any definitive agreement for the Potential JV and the expiration or early termination of any applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, (a) the Parties remain independent, arms-length, and potentially competitive entities, (b) no Party shall take any action that would impermissibly coordinate competitive conduct between the Parties, and (c) nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as authorizing any such coordination.

ARTICLE 3 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

3.1 Obligations of the Receiving Party. The Receiving Party shall (a) hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use such Confidential Information solely for the Purpose as set forth in Article 2; (c) disclose such Confidential Information only to those of its Representatives who have a bona fide need to know such Confidential Information for the Purpose, who have been informed of the confidential nature of such information, and who are bound by written or professional obligations of confidentiality and non-use no less protective than the obligations set forth in this Agreement; and (d) protect such Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care the Receiving Party uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

3.2 Responsibility for Representatives. The Receiving Party shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives. Any act or omission of a Representative that would constitute a breach of this Agreement if taken or omitted by the Receiving Party itself shall be deemed a breach by the Receiving Party.

3.3 Notification of Unauthorized Disclosure. The Receiving Party shall promptly, and in any event within three (3) business days after discovery, notify the Disclosing Party in writing of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information of which the Receiving Party becomes aware and shall cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party in any effort to mitigate or remedy such unauthorized use or disclosure.

ARTICLE 4 — CLEAN-TEAM PROTOCOL FOR RING-FENCED COMPETITIVE INFORMATION

4.1 Clean-Team Designation. Prior to any disclosure of Ring-Fenced Competitive Information, the Disclosing Party shall deliver to the Receiving Party a written designation identifying the information to be so disclosed and the specific fields or categories that qualify as Ring-Fenced Competitive Information. The Receiving Party shall then designate in writing its Clean Team, identifying by name each individual authorized to receive such information.

4.2 Clean-Team Eligibility. Each member of a Receiving Party's Clean Team shall be: (a) an individual whose current and reasonably foreseeable job responsibilities do not include commercial, pricing, or customer-facing decision-making for any product or program that competes with the Disclosing Party, or an outside counsel or financial advisor of the Receiving Party; (b) specifically informed of the existence of the clean-team protocol and of the limitations imposed on the use of Ring-Fenced Competitive Information; and (c) bound by individual written confidentiality obligations no less protective than those set forth in this Agreement.

4.3 Clean-Team Use Restriction. Ring-Fenced Competitive Information shall not be disclosed, directly or indirectly, to any person who is not a member of the Clean Team, and shall not be used by any Clean Team member for any purpose other than the Purpose. No summary, extract, or derivative of Ring-Fenced Competitive Information shall be shared with any person outside the Clean Team without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party, and any such summary, extract, or derivative that is so shared shall be reviewed in advance to ensure that it does not include Ring-Fenced Competitive Information.

4.4 Clean-Team Records. Each Party shall maintain a log of the individuals who have received Ring-Fenced Competitive Information and shall provide such log to the Disclosing Party upon reasonable request.

ARTICLE 5 — EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Exclusions. The obligations set forth in Article 3 shall not apply to any information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by competent written evidence:

(a) was in the public domain at the time of disclosure, or subsequently entered the public domain through no act or omission of the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was in the Receiving Party's possession, free of any obligation of confidence, prior to the time of its disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

(c) was independently developed by employees or contractors of the Receiving Party who had no access to Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party and without any use of or reference to any Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party; or

(d) was rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party who was not, to the Receiving Party's knowledge after reasonable inquiry, under any obligation of confidence to the Disclosing Party with respect to such information.

5.2 No Aggregate Knowledge Defense. Confidential Information shall not be deemed to fall within any of the foregoing exclusions merely because it is embraced by more general information in the public domain, unless the specific combination and principle of operation of the Confidential Information is itself in the public domain or was independently developed or lawfully received.

ARTICLE 6 — COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

6.1 Compelled Disclosure by Legal Process. In the event that a Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required (by oral questions, interrogatories, requests for information or documents in a legal proceeding, subpoena, civil investigative demand, or other similar process) to disclose any Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permissible, provide the Disclosing Party with prompt prior written notice of any such request or requirement so that the Disclosing Party may, at its sole cost, seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

6.2 Cooperation and Limitation. If, in the absence of a protective order or the receipt of a waiver from the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is nonetheless, on the advice of outside counsel, legally compelled to disclose Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party or such Representative may disclose only that portion of such Confidential Information that such counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed, and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain assurances that confidential treatment will be accorded to such Confidential Information. The Receiving Party shall continue to treat all non-disclosed portions of the Confidential Information as confidential in accordance with this Agreement.

6.3 Regulation FD Carve-Out. The Parties acknowledge that each Party is a publicly traded company subject to Regulation FD under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934. Nothing in this Agreement shall prohibit either Party from making any disclosure that its outside securities counsel advises is necessary to comply with Regulation FD or other applicable securities disclosure laws (including any required current report on Form 8-K), provided that such disclosing Party shall, to the extent practicable, notify the other Party in advance and shall limit such disclosure to the minimum extent required by applicable law.

ARTICLE 7 — RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

7.1 Return or Destruction. Upon the earlier of (a) the written request of the Disclosing Party or (b) the termination of discussions between the Parties regarding the Potential JV, the Receiving Party shall, at the Disclosing Party's election, promptly return to the Disclosing Party or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in the Receiving Party's or its Representatives' possession, custody or control, together with all copies, extracts, and derivative materials thereof, whether in tangible or electronic form.

7.2 Certification. If requested by the Disclosing Party, an executive officer of the Receiving Party shall certify in writing to the Disclosing Party that the Receiving Party has complied with the requirements of Section 7.1.

7.3 Archival Exception. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Receiving Party may retain (a) one (1) archival copy of Confidential Information in the custody of its outside legal counsel solely for purposes of establishing compliance with this Agreement and (b) any Confidential Information contained in automatic system backups made in the ordinary course of business, provided that any such retained materials shall remain subject to the confidentiality and non-use obligations of this Agreement for so long as they are retained, notwithstanding any termination of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 8 — NO LICENSE OR OWNERSHIP TRANSFER

8.1 Ownership Reserved. All Confidential Information disclosed by a Disclosing Party shall remain the sole and exclusive property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting to the Receiving Party, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, any license or other right under any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of the Disclosing Party.

8.2 All Rights Reserved. The Disclosing Party reserves all rights in and to its Confidential Information, including without limitation all patent, copyright, trademark, and trade secret rights therein. No obligation to enter into any further agreement, including without limitation any definitive agreement for the Potential JV, is created by this Agreement.

ARTICLE 9 — NON-SOLICITATION

9.1 Employee Non-Solicitation. For a period of eighteen (18) months following the Effective Date, neither Party shall, and each Party shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party with whom such Party first came into contact in connection with the Purpose or whose existence and role first became known to such Party through Confidential Information disclosed hereunder.

9.2 General Solicitation Carve-Out. The restrictions set forth in Section 9.1 shall not prohibit (a) general solicitations of employment not specifically directed at employees of the other Party or (b) the hiring of any person who responds to such a general solicitation, nor shall such restrictions prohibit the hiring of any person who contacts the hiring Party on his or her own initiative without any direct or indirect solicitation by the hiring Party.

ARTICLE 10 — TERM AND SURVIVAL

10.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force and effect for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years thereafter, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Section 10.3.

10.2 Survival of Confidentiality Obligations. Notwithstanding any termination or expiration of this Agreement, each Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality and non-use with respect to Confidential Information shall survive for a period of five (5) years after the date of expiration or termination of this Agreement. With respect to any item of Confidential Information that constitutes a Trade Secret, such obligations shall survive for so long as such item continues to qualify as a Trade Secret under applicable law.

10.3 Termination for Material Breach. Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' prior written notice to the other Party in the event of a material breach of this Agreement by such other Party that has not been cured within such notice period; provided that no such termination shall relieve the breaching Party of any liability for such breach or of any of the Parties' surviving obligations under Section 10.2.

ARTICLE 11 — EQUITABLE REMEDIES

11.1 Irreparable Harm. Each Party acknowledges and agrees that the Confidential Information of the other Party is of a special, unique, and extraordinary character, and that any breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy.

11.2 Injunctive Relief. Accordingly, each Party agrees that, in the event of any actual or threatened breach of this Agreement by the other Party or any of its Representatives, the non-breaching Party shall be entitled to seek, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, specific performance, injunctive relief, and other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction, without the necessity of posting a bond or other security and without the necessity of proving actual damages.

11.3 Cumulative Remedies. The rights and remedies of the Parties under this Agreement are cumulative and in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other rights and remedies available to the Parties at law or in equity.

ARTICLE 12 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

12.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

12.2 Venue; Waiver of Jury Trial. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to such venue on the basis of forum non conveniens. EACH PARTY HEREBY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

12.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous understandings, agreements, negotiations, representations, and warranties, whether oral or written, relating to such subject matter.

12.4 No Binding Obligation. The Parties acknowledge and agree that neither the furnishing of any Confidential Information under this Agreement nor the discussions between the Parties regarding the Potential JV shall be construed as (a) creating any agreement or commitment to enter into any further agreement between the Parties, or (b) limiting the discretion of either Party to terminate discussions regarding the Potential JV at any time, for any reason or no reason, without liability of any kind.

12.5 Amendments. No amendment, modification, or waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by a duly authorized representative of each Party.

12.6 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be deemed modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable while preserving the Parties' original intent.

12.7 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Delivery of an executed counterpart by electronic means (including PDF transmitted via email) shall be as effective as delivery of an originally executed counterpart.
