FRANCHISE PROSPECT NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This FRANCHISE PROSPECT NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} with its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} ("Franchisor"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} with its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} ("Prospect"). Franchisor and Prospect are each referred to herein as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

WHEREAS, Franchisor operates a franchise system of coffee roasters and related retail outlets and has developed over many years a proprietary operating system, brand identity, recipes, training program, and supply chain (collectively, the "System");

WHEREAS, Prospect has expressed interest in becoming a franchisee of Franchisor and has requested access to certain confidential information about the System in order to evaluate whether to pursue a franchise opportunity;

WHEREAS, this Agreement is entered into separately from and prior to the delivery of Franchisor's Franchise Disclosure Document ("FDD") required by the Federal Trade Commission's Franchise Rule (16 CFR Part 436), and is neither a franchise agreement nor a franchise disclosure document;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, hereby agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of Franchisor to Prospect or any of its Representatives in connection with the Purpose, in whatever form or medium, whether or not marked as confidential. Confidential Information includes, without limitation: (a) Franchisor's Operations Manual, standard operating procedures, and training materials; (b) the specific recipes, coffee bean blends, roasting profiles, and supplier relationships that constitute Franchisor's products; (c) unit economics information, including sample P&L statements, average unit volumes, labor cost models, and margin targets for existing System units; (d) Franchisor's System-wide marketing materials, loyalty program data, and growth plans; (e) the identities of existing franchisees and supplier information; and (f) the existence and content of the discussions between the Parties regarding the potential franchise opportunity.

1.2 "Operations Manual" means Franchisor's complete manual of operating policies, procedures, and standards for the System, including all updates thereto, which contains the core operating know-how of the franchise System.

1.3 "Purpose" means Prospect's good-faith internal evaluation of whether to pursue becoming a franchisee of Franchisor and, if Prospect decides to pursue that opportunity, the Parties' negotiation toward a potential franchise agreement in accordance with the FTC Franchise Rule.

1.4 "Representatives" means Prospect's officers, directors, employees, outside legal counsel, accountants, and financial advisors who have a bona fide need to know Confidential Information for the Purpose and who are bound by written obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set forth herein.

1.5 "Trade Secret" means any Confidential Information that qualifies as a trade secret under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016 or the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}} or any other applicable law. For the avoidance of doubt, Franchisor's recipes, roasting profiles, and Operations Manual are Trade Secrets.

ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

2.1 Narrow Purpose. Prospect shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever.

2.2 No Use to Build Competing System. Prospect shall not use Confidential Information, directly or indirectly, to: (a) open, operate, or invest in any coffee roaster, coffee retail, or quick-service beverage outlet that competes with the System, whether independently or under any other franchise brand; (b) design, develop, or implement any operating system, training program, or marketing program for any such competing business; or (c) recruit or solicit any employee, franchisee, or supplier of Franchisor. These restrictions shall apply during the term of this Agreement and for a period of three (3) years after its expiration or termination.

2.3 No Shop to Competing Franchise. Prospect shall not use Confidential Information to evaluate, negotiate with, or enroll in any competing franchise system. If Prospect decides not to pursue a franchise opportunity with Franchisor, Prospect shall not share any Confidential Information with any other franchise system or competing business.

2.4 No Reverse Engineering. Prospect shall not reverse engineer any of Franchisor's products, recipes, or operating procedures disclosed in connection with the Purpose.

ARTICLE 3 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

3.1 Obligations. Prospect shall: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (c) disclose Confidential Information only to its Representatives who have a bona fide need to know in connection with the Purpose and who are bound by written obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set forth herein; and (d) protect Confidential Information using at least the degree of care Prospect uses for its own most sensitive confidential information, but no less than a reasonable degree of care.

3.2 Responsibility for Representatives. Prospect shall be fully responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives.

3.3 Incident Reporting. Prospect shall notify Franchisor in writing within five (5) business days after becoming aware of any actual or suspected unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information.

ARTICLE 4 — EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Exclusions. The obligations in Article 3 shall not apply to any information that Prospect can demonstrate by competent written evidence: (a) was in the public domain at the time of disclosure or subsequently entered the public domain through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was in Prospect's possession, free of any obligation of confidence, prior to disclosure; (c) was independently developed by Prospect without use of or reference to any Confidential Information; or (d) was rightfully received from a third party without any obligation of confidence to Franchisor.

4.2 FDD Disclosures. Information that Franchisor ultimately includes in any FDD delivered to Prospect or to other prospective franchisees shall cease to be Confidential Information as of the date of such delivery, to the extent of what is actually included in such FDD.

ARTICLE 5 — COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

5.1 Legal Process. If Prospect is requested or required by any court, governmental authority, or other legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, Prospect shall, to the extent legally permissible, provide Franchisor with prompt prior written notice so Franchisor may, at its sole cost, seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

5.2 Limitation. If a protective order is not obtained and Franchisor does not waive compliance, Prospect may disclose only the minimum portion of Confidential Information legally required to be disclosed and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain assurances of confidential treatment.

ARTICLE 6 — RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

6.1 Return on Request. Upon the earlier of (a) Franchisor's written request, (b) Prospect's written notice that it will not proceed with a potential franchise, or (c) the execution of a franchise agreement between the Parties (in which case the confidentiality provisions of the franchise agreement shall govern going forward), Prospect shall, at Franchisor's election, return or destroy all Confidential Information, together with all copies, extracts, and derivative materials, and shall certify such return or destruction in writing within ten (10) business days.

6.2 Retention Exceptions. Prospect may retain (a) one archival copy of Confidential Information in the custody of its outside legal counsel, and (b) Confidential Information contained in automatic system backups, in each case subject to the continuing obligations of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 7 — NO LICENSE OR OWNERSHIP TRANSFER

7.1 Ownership Reserved. All Confidential Information shall remain the sole and exclusive property of Franchisor. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting Prospect, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, any license or other right in any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of Franchisor. No right to use Franchisor's trademarks, trade dress, recipes, or Operations Manual is granted by this Agreement. Any such rights shall be granted, if at all, only under a separate executed franchise agreement.

7.2 FTC Franchise Rule Acknowledgment. This Agreement does not constitute an offer to sell a franchise, does not constitute any part of a Franchise Disclosure Document, and shall not be relied upon by Prospect as a substitute for the FDD. Prior to the execution of any franchise agreement, Franchisor shall deliver to Prospect an FDD in accordance with 16 CFR Part 436 and any applicable state franchise registration or disclosure laws.

ARTICLE 8 — TERM AND SURVIVAL

8.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force and effect for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years thereafter, unless earlier terminated upon {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' prior written notice by either Party.

8.2 Survival. Prospect's obligations of confidentiality and non-use with respect to Confidential Information shall survive for a period of seven (7) years after the termination or expiration of this Agreement, and with respect to any Trade Secret, for so long as such information remains a Trade Secret under applicable law. The restrictions set forth in Sections 2.2 and 2.3 shall survive for a period of three (3) years after the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 9 — EQUITABLE REMEDIES

9.1 Irreparable Harm. Prospect acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement may cause Franchisor irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate, particularly given the value of Franchisor's Trade Secrets and the investment Franchisor has made in building the System.

9.2 Injunctive Relief. Franchisor shall be entitled, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, to seek specific performance, injunctive relief, and other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction, without the necessity of posting a bond or other security and without the necessity of proving actual damages.

9.3 Cumulative Remedies. Remedies are cumulative and in addition to any other rights and remedies.

ARTICLE 10 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to conflict of laws principles.

10.2 Venue. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

10.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous understandings, representations, and agreements relating thereto.

10.4 Amendments. No amendment of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

10.5 Assignment. Prospect may not assign this Agreement without Franchisor's prior written consent. Franchisor may assign this Agreement in connection with a corporate transaction.

10.6 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

10.7 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
