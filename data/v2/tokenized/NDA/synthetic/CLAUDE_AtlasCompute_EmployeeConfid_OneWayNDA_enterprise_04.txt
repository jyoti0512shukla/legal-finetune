EMPLOYEE CONFIDENTIALITY AND INVENTION ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This EMPLOYEE CONFIDENTIALITY AND INVENTION ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} with its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} residing at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} ("Employee"). The Company and Employee are each referred to herein as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Company has offered to employ Employee in a senior technical role in which Employee will have access to, and be expected to contribute to, the Company's proprietary technology, products, research and development activities, and business operations;

WHEREAS, the Company has invested substantial resources in the development of its Confidential Information and intellectual property, and the protection of such Confidential Information and intellectual property is essential to the Company's business;

WHEREAS, as a material condition of Employee's employment with the Company, the Company requires Employee to enter into this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of Employee's employment by the Company, the compensation and benefits to be paid by the Company to Employee in connection with such employment, and the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, hereby agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Company Group" means the Company and each of its subsidiaries, parent entities, and affiliated entities.

1.2 "Company Information Systems" means all information systems, devices, networks, cloud accounts, communications platforms, source-code repositories, and storage media owned, leased, or controlled by or for the benefit of the Company Group.

1.3 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information of the Company Group, in whatever form or medium, whether disclosed to Employee or developed, accessed, or learned by Employee in the course of Employee's employment by the Company, that derives actual or potential value from not being generally known. Confidential Information includes, without limitation: (a) technical information, including source code, object code, software architecture, algorithms, data structures, machine-learning models, training data, design documents, and hardware designs; (b) research and development information, including experimental results, prototypes, research notebooks, and unreleased product plans; (c) business and commercial information, including customer and prospect lists, pricing, deal terms, revenue and margin data, sales pipeline, business strategies, and marketing plans; (d) personnel information, including compensation, performance data, and organizational plans; (e) third-party information that the Company Group holds under obligations of confidence; and (f) any other information that a reasonable person in Employee's position would understand, based on the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure or development, to be confidential or proprietary.

1.4 "Prior Inventions" means all inventions, original works of authorship, developments, improvements, and trade secrets that Employee made, conceived, developed, or reduced to practice prior to Employee's employment with the Company and that Employee wishes to exclude from this Agreement. Prior Inventions are listed in Schedule A hereto; if Schedule A is blank or not attached, Employee represents that there are no Prior Inventions.

1.5 "Purpose" means the performance of Employee's duties as an employee of the Company, as such duties are assigned to Employee by the Company from time to time.

1.6 "Trade Secret" means any item of Confidential Information that qualifies as a trade secret under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016 or the {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}} Uniform Trade Secrets Act or any other applicable law.

1.7 "Work Product" means all inventions, discoveries, improvements, developments, concepts, ideas, original works of authorship, software, source code, mask works, designs, processes, techniques, formulae, data, databases, trade secrets, and know-how, whether or not patentable or registrable under patent, copyright, or similar statutes, that Employee, solely or jointly with others, makes, conceives, develops, reduces to practice, authors, or creates during the term of Employee's employment with the Company, other than any invention that qualifies for the exemption described in Section 5.3.

ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

2.1 Narrow Purpose. Employee shall access and use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose. Employee shall not access, copy, transmit, disclose, or use Confidential Information for Employee's personal benefit, for the benefit of any person other than the Company Group, or for any business activity outside the scope of Employee's employment duties.

2.2 No External Use. Employee shall not retain Confidential Information on any personal device, personal cloud account, personal email account, or personal storage medium, and shall not transmit Confidential Information over any channel other than Company Information Systems. Employee shall not use Confidential Information for any purpose that is, or could reasonably be, competitive with or adverse to the interests of the Company Group.

2.3 No Competitive Use. Employee shall not use Confidential Information to obtain a competitive advantage against the Company Group, including by offering Confidential Information to any prospective or actual competitor of the Company Group, any investor considering an investment in a competitor, or any other third party whose access to such Confidential Information could harm the Company Group.

ARTICLE 3 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

3.1 Obligations of Employee. Employee shall: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence during and after Employee's employment with the Company; (b) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose in accordance with Article 2; (c) disclose Confidential Information only to other personnel of the Company Group who have a bona fide need to know such Confidential Information in connection with the performance of their duties for the Company Group; and (d) protect Confidential Information using at least the degree of care Employee uses to protect his or her own confidential information, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

3.2 Information Handling. Employee shall comply with all Company Group policies, procedures, and standards concerning the handling, access, transmission, and storage of Confidential Information, including without limitation any policies concerning access controls, password management, encryption, incident reporting, clean-desk practices, and remote-work security.

3.3 Notification of Unauthorized Disclosure. Employee shall notify the Company's General Counsel in writing promptly, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours, after Employee becomes aware of any actual or suspected unauthorized use, disclosure, loss, or exposure of Confidential Information, whether by Employee or any other person. Such notification shall include a reasonable description of the circumstances of the unauthorized use or disclosure, the Confidential Information affected, and any remedial steps Employee has taken or proposes to take.

3.4 Whistleblower Immunity Notice. Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b) of the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016, Employee is hereby notified that: (a) an individual shall not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret that is made (i) in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official, either directly or indirectly, or to an attorney, and (ii) solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or is made in a complaint or other document filed in a lawsuit or other proceeding, if such filing is made under seal; and (b) an individual who files a lawsuit for retaliation by an employer for reporting a suspected violation of law may disclose the trade secret to the attorney of the individual and use the trade secret information in the court proceeding, if the individual (i) files any document containing the trade secret under seal and (ii) does not disclose the trade secret, except pursuant to court order.

ARTICLE 4 — EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Exclusions. The obligations set forth in Article 3 shall not apply to any information that Employee can demonstrate by competent written evidence:

(a) was in the public domain at the time it was disclosed to or developed by Employee, or subsequently entered the public domain through no breach of this Agreement by Employee;

(b) was in Employee's possession, free of any obligation of confidence to the Company Group or any third party, prior to the commencement of Employee's employment with the Company;

(c) was independently developed by Employee outside the scope of Employee's employment, without the use of any Company Group facilities, equipment, supplies, information, or trade secrets, and otherwise satisfies the requirements of Section 5.3; or

(d) was rightfully received by Employee from a third party who was not, to Employee's knowledge, under any obligation of confidence to the Company Group with respect to such information.

4.2 No Aggregate Knowledge Defense. Information shall not be deemed to fall within any of the foregoing exclusions merely because it is embraced by more general information in the public domain or in Employee's pre-employment possession, unless the specific combination and principle of operation of the information is itself in the public domain or was rightfully in Employee's pre-employment possession.

ARTICLE 5 — INVENTION ASSIGNMENT

5.1 Assignment of Work Product. Employee hereby assigns, and agrees to assign, to the Company Employee's entire right, title, and interest in and to all Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein, effective as of the moment of the creation of each item of Work Product. Employee agrees to execute any further documents, and to take any further actions, reasonably requested by the Company to confirm, record, or perfect the Company's ownership of the Work Product, both during and after the term of Employee's employment.

5.2 Works Made for Hire. Employee acknowledges that all copyrightable Work Product, to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, shall constitute "work made for hire" within the meaning of the United States Copyright Act, with the Company deemed to be the author thereof. To the extent that any copyrightable Work Product does not qualify as a "work made for hire," Employee hereby assigns all right, title, and interest in such Work Product (including all copyrights therein and the right to register copyrights in the Company's name) to the Company in accordance with Section 5.1.

5.3 California Labor Code Section 2870 Notice. Notwithstanding anything in this Agreement to the contrary, this assignment does not apply to any invention that Employee developed entirely on Employee's own time without using the Company's equipment, supplies, facilities, or trade secret information, except for those inventions that either (a) relate at the time of conception or reduction to practice of the invention (i) to the business of the Company Group, or (ii) to the Company Group's actual or demonstrably anticipated research or development, or (b) result from any work performed by Employee for the Company. Employee is hereby notified that this Agreement does not require assignment of any invention that qualifies for the foregoing exemption under California Labor Code Section 2870.

5.4 Prior Inventions. Employee represents and warrants that the Prior Inventions listed on Schedule A (if any) constitute a complete list of all inventions that Employee wishes to exclude from the scope of this Agreement. The Company shall have no rights in any Prior Invention. If, in the course of Employee's employment, Employee incorporates any Prior Invention into any Work Product or Company product or service, Employee hereby grants the Company Group a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, sublicensable license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, and otherwise exploit such Prior Invention as part of such Work Product, Company product, or Company service.

5.5 Moral Rights. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Employee hereby waives any and all "moral rights" Employee may have in any Work Product.

ARTICLE 6 — COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

6.1 Compelled Disclosure by Legal Process. In the event that Employee is requested or required by any court, governmental authority, or other legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, Employee shall, to the extent legally permissible, provide the Company's General Counsel with prompt prior written notice of such request or requirement so that the Company may, at its sole cost, seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy or (at the Company's sole election) waive compliance with this Agreement with respect to such disclosure.

6.2 Limitation on Compelled Disclosure. If a protective order is not obtained and the Company does not waive compliance, Employee may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that Employee's counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain assurances that confidential treatment will be accorded to the Confidential Information so disclosed. Employee shall continue to treat all non-disclosed portions of the Confidential Information as confidential in accordance with this Agreement. For the avoidance of doubt, nothing in this Section 6 shall limit Employee's whistleblower rights under Section 3.4.

ARTICLE 7 — RETURN OF COMPANY PROPERTY AND CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

7.1 Return on Termination. Upon the termination of Employee's employment with the Company for any reason, or at any earlier time upon the Company's request, Employee shall promptly return to the Company all Confidential Information and all other Company Group property in Employee's possession, custody, or control, including without limitation all Company Information Systems and devices, identification badges, keys, documents, and any tangible or electronic materials that contain or reflect Confidential Information. Employee shall not retain any copies, extracts, or derivative materials of any such Confidential Information, whether in tangible or electronic form.

7.2 Destruction Certification. Upon the Company's request, Employee shall certify in writing that Employee has complied with the requirements of Section 7.1 and has not retained any Confidential Information, other than as expressly permitted by applicable law.

7.3 No Use on Company Information Systems after Termination. After the termination of Employee's employment, Employee shall not access any Company Information Systems and shall not attempt to retrieve any Confidential Information therefrom.

ARTICLE 8 — NO LICENSE OR OWNERSHIP TRANSFER

8.1 Ownership Reserved. All Confidential Information and all Work Product shall be and remain the sole and exclusive property of the Company Group. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting Employee, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, any license or other right under any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of the Company Group, except such limited right to use Confidential Information as is necessary for Employee to perform the Purpose during the term of Employee's employment.

8.2 All Rights Reserved. The Company Group reserves all rights in and to its Confidential Information and Work Product, including without limitation all patent, copyright, trademark, and trade secret rights therein.

ARTICLE 9 — NON-SOLICITATION

9.1 Employee Non-Solicitation. For a period of twelve (12) months following the termination of Employee's employment with the Company for any reason, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, recruit, or induce any employee or contractor of the Company Group to leave or terminate his or her employment or engagement with the Company Group.

9.2 No Non-Competition. Employee and the Company acknowledge and agree that, under California Business and Professions Code Section 16600, a non-competition covenant purporting to prevent Employee from engaging in a lawful profession, trade, or business of any kind is void and unenforceable. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as a covenant not to compete, and the restrictions in Section 9.1 are not intended to, and shall not be construed to, restrict Employee's right to engage in any lawful profession, trade, or business following the termination of Employee's employment.

ARTICLE 10 — TERM AND SURVIVAL

10.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force and effect throughout the term of Employee's employment with the Company.

10.2 Survival of Confidentiality Obligations. Notwithstanding any expiration or termination of this Agreement or the termination of Employee's employment with the Company, Employee's obligations of confidentiality and non-use with respect to Confidential Information shall survive for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years after the termination of Employee's employment. With respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a Trade Secret, Employee's obligations of confidentiality and non-use shall survive for so long as such information continues to qualify as a Trade Secret under applicable law. The invention assignment provisions of Article 5 and the return obligations of Article 7 shall survive the termination of Employee's employment indefinitely.

10.3 Termination for Material Breach. Without limiting any other right or remedy the Company may have, the Company may treat any material breach of this Agreement by Employee as a material breach of Employee's employment obligations. Employee may seek to terminate this Agreement upon {{NOTICE_DAYS}} business days' prior written notice to the Company in the event of a material breach by the Company that has not been cured within such notice period, subject to Employee's surviving obligations under Section 10.2.

ARTICLE 11 — EQUITABLE REMEDIES

11.1 Irreparable Harm. Employee acknowledges and agrees that the Company Group's Confidential Information and Work Product are of a special, unique, and extraordinary character, the loss or unauthorized disclosure of which would cause the Company Group irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be an inadequate remedy.

11.2 Injunctive Relief. Accordingly, the Company shall be entitled, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, to seek specific performance, injunctive relief, and other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to prevent or restrain any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement by Employee, without the necessity of posting a bond or other security and without the necessity of proving actual damages.

11.3 Cumulative Remedies. The rights and remedies of the Company under this Agreement are cumulative and in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other rights and remedies available to the Company at law, in equity, or under any other agreement between the Parties.

ARTICLE 12 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

12.1 At-Will Employment. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create any term of employment or to alter Employee's status as an at-will employee of the Company. Employee's employment may be terminated at any time, by either Employee or the Company, for any reason or no reason, with or without cause and with or without notice, subject only to the obligations set forth in this Agreement and any other written agreement between the Parties.

12.2 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

12.3 Venue. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

12.4 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous understandings, agreements, representations, and warranties (whether oral or written) relating to such subject matter, except for any separate written offer letter or employment agreement between the Parties that expressly references and incorporates this Agreement.

12.5 Amendments. No amendment, modification, or waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by Employee and a duly authorized officer of the Company.

12.6 Assignment. Employee may not assign this Agreement. The Company may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, without Employee's consent, in connection with any merger, consolidation, or sale of all or substantially all of the Company's assets or business.

12.7 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be deemed modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable while preserving the Parties' original intent.

12.8 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Delivery of an executed counterpart by electronic means (including PDF transmitted via email) shall be as effective as delivery of an originally executed counterpart.
