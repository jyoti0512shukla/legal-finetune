SHORT-FORM MUTUAL NDA

Effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This is a short mutual NDA between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} ("Vendor"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} ("Prospect"). It exists so we can have a real conversation about what Vendor does, and what Prospect needs, without either of us worrying about what happens to what gets said in the room. "We" and "you" in this agreement means whichever of us is receiving the other's information at the time.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 Confidential Information is anything one of us shares with the other that isn't already public and that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from context. That includes pricing, customer lists, product roadmap, pain points, internal systems, and financials. It does not include anything either of us has already published on our website, in a blog post, in a marketing email, in a product demo video, or in a publicly filed document.

1.2 Purpose means exchanging information for the limited purpose of exploring whether we might do business together — a discovery call, a demo, a pilot scoping conversation. That's it.

1.3 Representatives means each party's employees, advisors, and lawyers with a real need to know.

ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

2.1 We'll each use the other's Confidential Information only for the Purpose. Nothing else.

2.2 No using Confidential Information to build a competing product, to talk to a competing vendor, or to train any machine learning model on what was shared.

ARTICLE 3 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

3.1 We'll each: (a) keep the other's Confidential Information confidential; (b) only share it with our own Representatives on a need-to-know basis and only with people bound by at least equivalent confidentiality obligations; (c) use it only for the Purpose; and (d) take reasonable care to protect it.

3.2 Each of us is responsible for its own people. If a Representative slips up, it's treated as a slip-up by the party they work for.

3.3 If either of us finds out Confidential Information has been leaked, we'll tell the other party within five (5) business days and do what we reasonably can to help contain it.

ARTICLE 4 — EXCLUSIONS

4.1 The obligations in Article 3 don't apply to information that:
  (a) is public or becomes public without either of us breaking this agreement;
  (b) we already knew without any duty of confidence before receiving it;
  (c) we independently developed without using the other party's Confidential Information; or
  (d) we properly got from a third party without any confidentiality duty to the disclosing party.

4.2 Published marketing materials, product documentation, public pricing pages, and anything either of us tweets publicly aren't Confidential Information.

ARTICLE 5 — COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

5.1 If legally compelled to disclose the other party's Confidential Information, we'll give notice as soon as legally permitted, cooperate on any protective order, and only disclose the minimum legally required. Whatever isn't compelled stays confidential.

ARTICLE 6 — RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

6.1 Either of us can ask the other to return or destroy Confidential Information. The receiving party will do so within ten (10) business days and will confirm in writing. Backups are fine to keep, but they stay subject to this agreement.

ARTICLE 7 — NO LICENSE OR OWNERSHIP TRANSFER

7.1 Sharing information doesn't transfer any ownership and doesn't grant any license. Whatever either of us owns, we still own. This agreement doesn't obligate either of us to sign any further agreement or to continue the conversation.

ARTICLE 8 — TERM AND SURVIVAL

8.1 This agreement runs for {{TERM_YEARS}} year from the Effective Date. Either of us can terminate it at any time on {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' written notice.

8.2 After termination, our confidentiality and non-use obligations continue for two (2) years. Trade secret obligations continue as long as the information qualifies as a trade secret under applicable law.

ARTICLE 9 — EQUITABLE REMEDIES

9.1 Both of us agree that if the other's Confidential Information is leaked or misused, damages alone probably won't fix it. So either of us can seek an injunction or specific performance from a court, without having to post a bond or prove actual damages, in addition to any other remedies at law or in equity.

10.2 This is the whole agreement between us about confidentiality for the Purpose.

10.3 Any changes have to be in writing signed by both of us.

10.5 If a court strikes part of this agreement, the rest stays in force.
