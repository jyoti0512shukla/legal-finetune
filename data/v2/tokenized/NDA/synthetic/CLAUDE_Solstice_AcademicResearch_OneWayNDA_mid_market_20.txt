NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT FOR A UNIVERSITY RESEARCH COLLABORATION

This NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date"), by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} with its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} ("Sponsor"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}}, acting through its Office of Research and Innovation, at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} ("University"). Sponsor and University are each referred to herein as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

WHEREAS, Sponsor is considering sponsoring a research collaboration in the laboratory of a designated University principal investigator (the "PI") on a topic of mutual scientific interest (the "Research Topic");

WHEREAS, to evaluate the feasibility of the potential collaboration, Sponsor will disclose to University certain non-public information concerning Sponsor's proprietary research materials, biological reagents, and research plans (the "Purpose");

WHEREAS, University is a research institution committed to academic freedom, the open publication of research results, and the education of students, and University's ability to engage in any sponsored research is subject to its policies concerning intellectual property, conflicts of interest, and publication rights;

WHEREAS, certain University research, including research that may be related to the Research Topic, is federally funded, and may be subject to the Bayh-Dole Act (35 U.S.C. §§ 200-212) and the NIH Grants Policy Statement, which Sponsor acknowledges;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing recitals and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, hereby agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by Sponsor to University in connection with the Purpose, in whatever form or medium, marked or clearly identified as confidential at the time of disclosure (or, if disclosed orally or visually, subsequently summarized in writing, marked as confidential, and provided to University within thirty (30) days of such oral or visual disclosure). Confidential Information may include Sponsor's research plans, proprietary compounds, biological reagents, experimental methods, and unpublished data.

1.2 "PI" means the principal investigator at University designated by University to evaluate the potential collaboration under this Agreement, as identified in a separate writing.

1.3 "Purpose" means University's good-faith scientific evaluation, in the PI's laboratory, of the feasibility of a potential sponsored research collaboration with Sponsor, and nothing else.

1.4 "Research Results" means any data, observations, ideas, inventions, or findings generated by the PI or other University personnel as a result of activities performed under this Agreement, other than Confidential Information of Sponsor.

1.5 "Representatives" means the PI, the PI's supervised postdoctoral researchers, graduate students, and undergraduate students, and University's research administration personnel, in each case who have a bona fide need to know Confidential Information for the Purpose and who are subject to University's written confidentiality and intellectual property policies.

1.6 "Trade Secret" means any Confidential Information that qualifies as a trade secret under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016 or the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}} or any other applicable law.

ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

2.1 Narrow Purpose. University shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever. University shall not use Confidential Information to support any commercial activity or any third-party-sponsored research other than a future sponsored research agreement with Sponsor, if any.

2.2 Academic Freedom Acknowledgment. Sponsor acknowledges and agrees that University's primary mission is research and education, and that nothing in this Agreement restricts the academic freedom of the PI or other University personnel to pursue research on the Research Topic or any related subject, provided that no Confidential Information of Sponsor is used in such research.

2.3 No Restriction on University's Independent Research. University's continued research on the Research Topic or any related subject, conducted independently of Sponsor's Confidential Information, is not restricted by this Agreement, regardless of whether the research yields findings similar to those arising from the Purpose.

ARTICLE 3 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

3.1 Obligations. University shall: (a) hold Confidential Information in confidence; (b) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (c) disclose Confidential Information only to Representatives who have a bona fide need to know in connection with the Purpose and who are subject to University's written confidentiality policies; and (d) protect Confidential Information using the same degree of care University uses for its own comparable confidential information, but no less than a reasonable degree of care.

3.2 Student Researchers. University acknowledges that undergraduate and graduate students who serve as Representatives may not be individually bound by written confidentiality agreements beyond University's general research policies. University shall instruct any student Representatives regarding the confidential nature of the Confidential Information and the restrictions of this Agreement, and shall be responsible for any use or disclosure of Confidential Information by such students that would constitute a breach of this Agreement if taken or omitted by University itself.

3.3 Responsibility for Representatives. University shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives.

3.4 Incident Reporting. University shall notify Sponsor in writing promptly after becoming aware of any actual or suspected unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information.

ARTICLE 4 — EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Exclusions. The obligations in Article 3 shall not apply to any information that University can demonstrate by competent written evidence: (a) was in the public domain at the time of disclosure or subsequently entered the public domain through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was already in University's possession, free of any obligation of confidence, prior to disclosure (including any information previously generated by the PI or other University researchers); (c) was independently developed by University without use of or reference to any Confidential Information; or (d) was rightfully received from a third party without any obligation of confidence to Sponsor.

4.2 University Background IP Excluded. Information generated by the PI or other University researchers in the course of independent research, unfunded by Sponsor and not using Sponsor's Confidential Information, is not Confidential Information and is not subject to the obligations of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 5 — PUBLICATION AND COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

5.1 Publication Carve-Out. University is committed to the open publication of research results. Accordingly, if University develops Research Results in the course of activities under this Agreement, University shall have the right to publish and present such Research Results, subject only to a courtesy review by Sponsor for a period not to exceed sixty (60) days, during which Sponsor may request redactions of any Confidential Information of Sponsor that would otherwise be disclosed in the publication. Sponsor may also request a delay of publication of up to an additional thirty (30) days to permit Sponsor to file a patent application on any patentable subject matter in the Research Results that constitutes an invention of Sponsor. Beyond these two review periods, University shall not be required to delay or refrain from publication.

5.2 Legal Process. If University is requested or required by any court, governmental authority, or other legal process (including, without limitation, a Freedom of Information Act or state public-records-law request) to disclose any Confidential Information of Sponsor, University shall, to the extent legally permissible, provide Sponsor with prompt prior written notice so Sponsor may, at its sole cost, seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy. If a protective order is not obtained and Sponsor does not waive compliance, University may disclose only the minimum portion of Confidential Information legally required to be disclosed and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment.

5.3 Federal Funding Disclosure. University acknowledges that research conducted under federal funding may be subject to disclosure obligations to the federal funding agency, the Bayh-Dole Act's invention-disclosure requirements, and federal march-in rights under 35 U.S.C. § 203. Sponsor acknowledges these obligations and agrees that University's compliance with such federal obligations shall not constitute a breach of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 6 — RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

6.1 Return on Request. Upon the earlier of (a) Sponsor's written request, (b) the execution of a definitive sponsored research agreement between the Parties (in which case this Agreement shall continue in effect with respect to Confidential Information already disclosed hereunder), or (c) the conclusion of the Purpose without a sponsored research agreement, University shall, at Sponsor's election, return or destroy all Confidential Information, together with all copies, extracts, and derivative materials, and shall confirm such return or destruction in writing within thirty (30) days. University may retain (i) one archival copy in the University's Office of Research and Innovation, and (ii) Confidential Information contained in automatic system backups and in the PI's laboratory notebooks, in each case subject to the continuing obligations of this Agreement. Laboratory notebook entries reflecting experiments performed using Sponsor's Confidential Information shall not be destroyed, but shall be treated as confidential by University in accordance with University's research records retention policies.

ARTICLE 7 — NO LICENSE OR OWNERSHIP TRANSFER

7.1 Ownership Reserved. All Confidential Information shall remain the sole and exclusive property of Sponsor. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting University, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, any license or other right in any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of Sponsor. No license is granted to practice any patent or to use any Trade Secret. Any license rights shall be granted, if at all, under a separate written agreement between the Parties.

7.2 University Background IP. All intellectual property generated by University independently of the Purpose shall remain the exclusive property of University. Any intellectual property generated by University under this Agreement in connection with the Purpose shall be addressed in a separate definitive sponsored research agreement, if any, entered into between the Parties.

7.3 Bayh-Dole Acknowledgment. To the extent any invention is conceived or reduced to practice using federal funds, such invention is subject to the Bayh-Dole Act and the federal government retains certain march-in and royalty-free license rights therein.

ARTICLE 8 — TERM AND SURVIVAL

8.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force and effect for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years thereafter, unless earlier terminated upon {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' prior written notice by either Party.

8.2 Survival. University's obligations of confidentiality and non-use with respect to Confidential Information shall survive for a period of five (5) years after such termination or expiration, and with respect to any Trade Secret, for so long as such information remains a Trade Secret under applicable law.

ARTICLE 9 — EQUITABLE REMEDIES

9.1 Irreparable Harm. University acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement may cause Sponsor irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate.

9.2 Injunctive Relief. Sponsor shall be entitled to seek specific performance, injunctive relief, and other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction, without the necessity of posting a bond or other security and without the necessity of proving actual damages, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

9.3 Sovereign Immunity. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as a waiver of any sovereign or governmental immunity that University may enjoy under applicable law.

ARTICLE 10 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to conflict of laws principles.

10.2 Venue. Each Party submits to the jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, to the extent permitted by applicable law, including any sovereign immunity limitations.

10.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.

10.4 Amendments. No amendment of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by an authorized representative of each Party.

10.5 Assignment. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party.

10.6 Severability. If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

10.7 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
