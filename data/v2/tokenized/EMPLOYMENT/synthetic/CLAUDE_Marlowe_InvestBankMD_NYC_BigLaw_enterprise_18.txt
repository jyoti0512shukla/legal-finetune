EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Bank"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing in the State of {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} (the "Managing Director"). The Bank and the Managing Director are collectively the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Bank is a FINRA-registered broker-dealer engaged in equity capital markets, debt capital markets, and merger and acquisition advisory services;

WHEREAS, the Bank desires to employ the Managing Director to lead its equity capital markets origination and execution business, and the Managing Director desires to accept such employment on the terms set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Cause" means the Managing Director's: (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, any felony or any crime involving fraud, embezzlement, or moral turpitude; (b) failure to maintain FINRA registration or any other regulatory license required for the Managing Director's role; (c) any final disciplinary action by FINRA, the SEC, the Federal Reserve, or any other financial regulator that would prevent the Managing Director from continuing in the Managing Director's role; (d) willful misconduct or gross negligence in the performance of the Managing Director's duties; (e) material breach of this Agreement, the Bank's Compliance Manual, or the Bank's information barrier policies, after written notice and a thirty-day cure opportunity (where cure is reasonably possible); or (f) any act or omission that would, if it had occurred prior to the Effective Date, have been required to be disclosed on the Managing Director's Form U4.

1.2 "Garden Leave Period" means the ninety (90) calendar day period during which the Managing Director, following a notice of termination, remains employed by the Bank but is not assigned active client responsibilities, as set forth in Section 5.2.

1.3 "Good Reason" has the customary meaning given to that term in NYC bulge-bracket investment bank employment agreements, including without limitation a material adverse change in the Managing Director's title, duties, or reporting line, a material reduction in Base Salary, or a material relocation more than 50 miles from {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

ARTICLE 2 — POSITION AND DUTIES

2.1 Position. The Bank shall employ the Managing Director as {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}, reporting to the {{EMPLOYEE_REPORTS_TO}}.

2.2 FINRA Registration. The Managing Director's continued employment is contingent on the Managing Director maintaining all FINRA registrations and licenses required for the Managing Director's role, including Series 7, Series 24, and Series 79 (or such other registrations as required from time to time), and on the Managing Director's Form U4 remaining current and free of any disqualifying disclosures.

2.3 Information Barriers. The Managing Director shall comply at all times with the Bank's information barrier policies and the Bank's Compliance Manual, including without limitation any restrictions on cross-divisional information sharing between the public-side investment banking division and the private-side trading division.

2.4 Place of Employment. The Managing Director's principal place of employment shall be the Bank's offices in {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

ARTICLE 3 — TERM

3.1 Term. The Managing Director's employment shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated in accordance with Article 5.

ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

4.1 Base Salary. The Bank shall pay the Managing Director an annual base salary of {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}} (the "Base Salary"), payable in accordance with the Bank's regular payroll practices.

4.2 Discretionary Year-End Bonus and Bonus Pool Participation. The Managing Director shall be eligible to participate in the Bank's annual discretionary year-end bonus, with a target opportunity of {{EMPLOYEE_TARGET_BONUS_PCT}}% of Base Salary at on-target performance. The actual bonus amount, if any, shall be determined by the Bank's Compensation Committee in its sole discretion based on (a) overall Bank performance, (b) the performance of the Equity Capital Markets division, and (c) the Managing Director's individual contribution to revenue, deal credit, and team development. The Managing Director acknowledges that there is no individual contractual minimum bonus and that bonus amounts may vary materially from year to year based on Bank and divisional performance.

4.3 Sign-On Bonus. The Bank shall pay the Managing Director a sign-on bonus of Five Hundred Thousand United States Dollars (US$500,000), payable within 15 days after the Effective Date. The sign-on bonus is fully earned on payment.

4.4 Initial Equity Grant. The Bank shall grant the Managing Director an initial equity award (in the form of restricted stock units of the Bank's parent company) with a grant-date fair value of {{EMPLOYEE_INITIAL_EQUITY}}, vesting in equal annual installments over four years, subject to the Bank's standard executive equity vesting and clawback terms.

4.5 Deferred Compensation. To the extent any portion of the Managing Director's annual bonus is paid in deferred form (in cash or equity), such deferred compensation shall be subject to the Bank's standard deferral terms, including a two-year clawback if the Managing Director resigns to join a competitor before the deferred amount vests.

4.6 Welfare and Retirement Benefits. The Managing Director shall be eligible to participate in the Bank's standard executive benefit plans on terms no less favorable than those generally applicable to other Managing Directors.

4.7 Vacation. The Managing Director shall be entitled to four (4) weeks of paid vacation per calendar year.

4.8 Federal Reserve Incentive Compensation Guidance. The Bank's incentive compensation arrangements with the Managing Director are subject to the Federal Reserve's Guidance on Sound Incentive Compensation Policies (12 CFR Part 252) and shall be structured, operated, and (where required) clawed back consistent with that guidance.

4.9 Sarbanes-Oxley and Dodd-Frank Clawback. Any incentive-based compensation paid to the Managing Director shall be subject to recovery to the extent required by Section 304 of the Sarbanes-Oxley Act, Section 954 of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act, the SEC's clawback rules at 17 CFR § 240.10D-1, and any clawback policy adopted by the Bank.

ARTICLE 5 — TERMINATION AND GARDEN LEAVE

5.1 Termination Events. The Managing Director's employment may be terminated as follows: (a) automatically upon the Managing Director's death; (b) by the Bank on the Managing Director's Disability; (c) by the Bank for Cause; (d) by the Bank without Cause; (e) by the Managing Director for Good Reason; (f) by the Managing Director without Good Reason on at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' prior written notice; or (g) automatically if the Managing Director's FINRA registration is suspended, revoked, or otherwise terminated.

5.2 Garden Leave Period. Following any notice of termination by either Party (other than termination for Cause or for clearance loss), the Managing Director shall enter the Garden Leave Period for ninety (90) days. During the Garden Leave Period:

(a) the Managing Director shall remain an employee of the Bank and shall continue to receive Base Salary and benefits;

(b) the Managing Director shall not be assigned active client responsibilities, shall not pitch new business, and shall transition the Managing Director's existing client relationships in an orderly manner as directed by the Bank;

(c) the Managing Director shall not be present at the Bank's offices except as required for transition activities;

(d) the Managing Director shall not contact any client or prospect of the Bank for any business purpose; and

(e) the Managing Director shall not commence employment with any other financial services firm.

5.3 Accrued Obligations. Upon any termination, the Bank shall pay the Accrued Obligations.

5.4 Severance for Termination Without Cause or for Good Reason. Subject to the Managing Director's execution and non-revocation of a release of claims and continued compliance with the post-termination restrictive covenants in Article 6, the Managing Director shall be entitled to a severance payment equal to twelve (12) months of Base Salary, payable in accordance with the Bank's normal payroll cadence.

5.5 No Severance on Cause or U4 Disqualification. If the Managing Director's employment terminates under Section 5.1(c) (Cause) or Section 5.1(g) (FINRA disqualification), the Managing Director shall not be entitled to severance.

5.6 Section 280G. If any payment to the Managing Director would be subject to the Section 4999 excise tax, the aggregate payments shall be reduced to the extent necessary only if such reduction would result in a greater after-tax amount.

ARTICLE 6 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

6.1 Confidentiality. The Managing Director shall hold in strict confidence all non-public information of the Bank, including client information, deal pipelines, pricing, and financial information.

6.2 Non-Solicitation of Clients. During the Managing Director's employment and for a period of twelve (12) months after the date of termination of the Managing Director's employment for any reason, the Managing Director shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit any equity capital markets client of the Bank with whom the Managing Director had material contact during the twelve (12) months preceding the date of termination, for the purpose of providing competing capital markets services.

6.3 Non-Solicitation of Employees. During the Managing Director's employment and for twelve (12) months after termination, the Managing Director shall not directly solicit for employment any senior banker (Vice President or above) of the Bank with whom the Managing Director worked or had material contact, subject to a general-solicitation carve-out.

6.4 Non-Disparagement. The Managing Director shall not, during or after employment, make any public statement that disparages the Bank or its officers, directors, or employees.

6.5 Two-Year Clawback for Departure to Competitor. Without limiting Section 4.5, if the Managing Director resigns to join a competitor of the Bank within two (2) years after the Managing Director's last bonus payment, the Bank may claw back any portion of that bonus that remains unvested or deferred, in accordance with the Bank's standard deferral and clawback terms.

ARTICLE 7 — REPRESENTATIONS

7.1 Executive Representations. The Managing Director represents that (a) the Managing Director's Form U4 is current and free of any disqualifying disclosures, (b) the Managing Director is not subject to any FINRA, SEC, or other regulatory disciplinary action, and (c) the Managing Director is not subject to any agreement that would prevent the Managing Director from joining the Bank or from servicing the Bank's clients.

ARTICLE 8 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1 FINRA Arbitration. Any dispute or claim covered by FINRA Rule 13200 (Required Arbitration) shall be resolved through FINRA arbitration in accordance with the FINRA Code of Arbitration Procedure for Industry Disputes. All other disputes arising under this Agreement shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

8.2 Equitable Relief. The Bank may seek equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 6 of this Agreement, without bond and without proving actual damages.

8.3 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

ARTICLE 9 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

9.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

9.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

9.3 Assignment. The Managing Director may not assign this Agreement. The Bank may assign this Agreement to any successor.

9.4 Survival. Articles 5, 6, and 8 shall survive the termination of the Managing Director's employment.

9.5 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.
