AMENDED AND RESTATED EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This AMENDED AND RESTATED EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing in the Commonwealth of {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} (the "Executive"). The Company and the Executive are referred to herein individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Company and the Executive previously entered into an Employment Agreement effective as of an earlier date (the "Prior Employment Agreement"), and the Parties now desire to amend and restate the Prior Employment Agreement in its entirety on the terms set forth in this Agreement;

WHEREAS, the Company desires to continue to employ the Executive in the position of {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}, and the Executive desires to accept such continued employment, all on the terms and conditions set forth herein;

WHEREAS, the Executive shall execute, concurrently with this Agreement, the Confidential Information, Intellectual Property and Non-Solicitation Agreement of the Company attached hereto as Attachment A (the "Confidential Information Agreement"), which shall apply to the Executive's employment with the Company in addition to the obligations set forth in this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises, the mutual covenants set forth herein, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — EMPLOYMENT

1.1 Employment. The Company hereby continues to employ the Executive on an at-will basis in the position of {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}. The Executive agrees to comply with all written policies and procedures of the Company in effect from time to time.

ARTICLE 2 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AGREEMENT

2.1 Confidential Information Agreement. The Executive shall sign and deliver to the Company, concurrently with the execution of this Agreement, the Confidential Information Agreement attached as Attachment A. The Confidential Information Agreement is incorporated herein by reference and shall survive the termination of the Executive's employment in accordance with its terms.

ARTICLE 3 — DUTY TO THE COMPANY

(a) Full Working Time and Best Efforts. While employed by the Company, the Executive (i) will devote the Executive's full working time and best efforts to the business and affairs of the Company; and (ii) will not, without the prior express written consent of the Chief Executive Officer of the Company (the "CEO"), engage in any business activity (whether or not for compensation) that interferes with the Executive's work for the Company.

(b) Permitted Outside Activities. Notwithstanding Section 3(a), this Agreement does not prohibit the Executive from (i) managing the Executive's personal investments, (ii) engaging in charitable, civic, or unpaid professional activities (including service on the boards of charitable or professional organizations), or (iii) delivering lectures, fulfilling speaking engagements, or teaching at educational institutions, in each case so long as such activities do not materially interfere with the Executive's duties or violate Section 7 of this Agreement or the Confidential Information Agreement.

ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

(a) Base Salary. The Company shall pay the Executive a base salary at the rate of {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}} per year (the "Base Salary"), payable in accordance with the Company's normal payroll practices for senior executives. The Compensation Committee of the Board of Directors (the "Compensation Committee") may review and adjust the Base Salary from time to time in its sole discretion.

(b) Annual Incentive Bonus. The Executive shall be eligible to participate in the Company's Annual Corporate Management/Key Employee Bonus Plan, as in effect from time to time, with a target annual bonus opportunity of {{EMPLOYEE_TARGET_BONUS_PCT}}% of the Executive's then-current Base Salary. The actual bonus amount, if any, shall be determined by the Compensation Committee in its sole discretion based on Company and individual performance against objectives established by the Compensation Committee for the relevant fiscal year. To be eligible for any annual bonus, the Executive must be employed by the Company on the date the bonus is paid, except as expressly provided in Section 6.

(c) Equity Incentive Plan. The Executive shall be eligible to participate in the Company's stock incentive plan to the extent applicable to the Executive's position. As soon as practicable following the Effective Date, the Company shall grant to the Executive an initial equity award with an aggregate grant-date fair value of {{EMPLOYEE_INITIAL_EQUITY}} (the "Initial Equity Grant"), in such form (restricted stock units, stock appreciation rights, or stock options) and on such terms as the Compensation Committee determines, subject to the terms of the Company's then-effective equity incentive plan and the applicable award agreement. The Initial Equity Grant shall vest in equal annual installments over four years from the Effective Date, subject to the Executive's continued employment through each vesting date and to accelerated vesting under Section 6(g)(iv) below.

(d) Welfare and Retirement Benefits. The Executive shall be eligible to participate in the Company's generally available employee benefit plans, which currently include medical, dental, vision, life, accidental death and dismemberment, short-term disability, long-term disability, a 401(k) savings plan, and an employee stock purchase plan, in each case subject to the terms and conditions of each plan as in effect from time to time.

(e) Paid Time Off. The Executive shall be eligible for twenty-five (25) days of paid vacation per calendar year, plus paid sick time and Company-recognized holidays, all subject to the terms and conditions of the Company's policies in effect from time to time.

(f) Business Expenses. The Company shall reimburse the Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred by the Executive in performing the Executive's duties, in accordance with the Company's standard expense reimbursement policy. Reimbursement of expenses incurred in one tax year shall not affect reimbursement of expenses incurred in any other tax year, in accordance with Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (the "Code").

ARTICLE 5 — TERMINATION

5.1 At-Will Termination. Either the Executive or the Company may end the employment relationship at any time, for any reason, with or without notice or cause, subject to the obligations set forth in this Agreement. The employment relationship will end automatically and immediately upon the Executive's death or upon the Executive becoming entitled to long-term disability benefits under the Company's long-term disability program. The date on which the Executive's employment ends, regardless of how it ends, is referred to in this Agreement as the "Employment End Date."

ARTICLE 6 — SEVERANCE AND TERMINATION BENEFITS

6.1 General Rule. When the employment relationship ends, the Company shall have no obligation to pay or provide the Executive at any time any compensation, payment, or benefit of any kind, except as expressly provided in this Article 6.

(a) Minimum Obligations. When the employment relationship ends, no matter how it ends, the Company shall (i) pay the Executive any unpaid Base Salary earned through the Employment End Date; (ii) provide the Executive with any accrued, vested benefits under the Company's benefit plans and programs in accordance with the terms of those plans; (iii) pay the Executive for any accrued but unused vacation in accordance with applicable Massachusetts law; and (iv) reimburse the Executive for any unreimbursed business expenses incurred through the Employment End Date.

(b) Thirty Days' Base Salary After Certain Resignations. If the Executive provides the Company with at least thirty (30) days' advance written notice of resignation of employment, is an active employee in good standing at the time of such notice, and continues to perform the Executive's duties diligently and professionally to the extent requested thereafter, the Company shall pay the Executive the Executive's Base Salary for at least thirty (30) days after such notice, even if the Company elects to accelerate the Employment End Date during that thirty-day period.

(c) Thirty Days' Base Salary After Certain Terminations. If the Company terminates the Executive's employment other than for Cause, the Company shall provide the Executive with written notice of termination and pay the Executive the Executive's Base Salary for at least thirty (30) days after such notice, even if the Company elects to accelerate the Employment End Date during that thirty-day period.

(d) Eligibility for Ordinary Severance Compensation. If the Company terminates the Executive's employment, the Executive shall be eligible for the Ordinary Severance Compensation described in Section 6(e), provided that all of the following conditions are satisfied: (i) the Company's primary reason for terminating the Executive's employment was a change to the Company's business needs (such as a reduction in force or elimination of position) and not Cause; (ii) the Executive has complied with and continues to comply with all of the Executive's obligations under this Agreement and the Confidential Information Agreement; and (iii) the Executive executes, delivers to the Company within forty-five (45) days after the Employment End Date, and does not thereafter revoke or attempt to revoke, a general release of claims in a form satisfactory to the Company (the "General Release Agreement"). The Company's good-faith determination that one or more of the foregoing conditions has not been satisfied shall be binding and conclusive.

(e) Ordinary Severance Compensation. If the Executive becomes eligible for Ordinary Severance Compensation:

(i) Base Salary. The Company shall pay the Executive, within fourteen (14) days after the General Release Agreement becomes irrevocable, a lump sum equal to twelve (12) months of the Executive's then-current Base Salary.

(ii) Continuation of Health Benefits. For a period of twelve (12) months after the Employment End Date, to the extent the Executive timely elects continuation coverage under the Company's group medical, vision, and dental insurance plans pursuant to the Consolidated Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1985 ("COBRA") and remits the amount of premium charged to similarly situated active employees for comparable coverage, the Company shall pay the Company's usual share of such premiums. Such payments shall terminate to the extent the Executive ceases to be eligible for COBRA coverage. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Company shall not pay the contribution toward COBRA coverage described above to the extent that the Company reasonably determines that doing so would subject the Company to the excise tax under Section 4980D of the Code (as a result of discriminatory coverage under a group health plan).

(f) Eligibility for Enhanced Severance Compensation. The Executive shall become eligible for the Enhanced Severance Compensation described in Section 6(g) (instead of, and not in addition to, the Ordinary Severance Compensation under Section 6(d) and (e)) if and only if all of the following conditions are satisfied: (i) the Company terminates the Executive's employment without Cause, or the Executive resigns for Good Reason; (ii) the Employment End Date occurs within twenty-four (24) months after the effective date of a Change in Control; (iii) the Executive has complied with and continues to comply with all obligations under this Agreement and the Confidential Information Agreement; and (iv) the Executive executes, delivers to the Company within forty-five (45) days after the Employment End Date, and does not thereafter revoke, a General Release Agreement.

(g) Enhanced Severance Compensation. If the Executive becomes eligible for Enhanced Severance Compensation:

(i) Base Salary. The Company shall pay the Executive, within fourteen (14) days after the General Release Agreement becomes irrevocable, a lump sum equal to one and one-half (1.5) times the Executive's annual Base Salary (determined without regard to any reduction in Base Salary giving rise to Good Reason).

(ii) Incentive Compensation. The Company shall pay the Executive, within fourteen (14) days after the General Release Agreement becomes irrevocable, a lump sum equal to one and one-half (1.5) times the annual amount of incentive compensation for which the Executive was eligible under the Company's Annual Corporate Management/Key Employee Bonus Plan for the fiscal year containing the Employment End Date. In addition, the Executive shall receive a payment for the Executive's pro-rated target bonus for the partial year through the Employment End Date.

(iii) Continuation of Health Benefits. For a period of eighteen (18) months after the Employment End Date, to the extent the Executive timely elects COBRA continuation coverage and remits the amount of premium charged to similarly situated active employees for comparable coverage, the Company shall pay the Company's usual share of such premiums. The IRC § 4980D limitation set forth in Section 6(e)(ii) shall apply equally to this Section 6(g)(iii).

(iv) Accelerated Vesting of Equity. The Executive's unvested equity awards as of the Employment End Date, if any, shall be subject to accelerated vesting to the extent provided in the respective equity award agreement issued to the Executive under the then-effective Company equity incentive plan.

(h) No Mitigation; No Offset. The Executive shall not be required to mitigate damages, by seeking other employment or otherwise, as a condition of receiving any portion of the Ordinary Severance Compensation or the Enhanced Severance Compensation. The Severance Compensation shall not be reduced by any compensation earned by the Executive as the result of employment by another employer or otherwise after the Employment End Date.

(i) Section 280G Best-Net Cutback. If any payment or benefit received by the Executive under this Agreement (alone or together with any other payment or benefit) would constitute a "parachute payment" within the meaning of Section 280G of the Code and would be subject to the excise tax imposed by Section 4999 of the Code, then the aggregate amount of such payments and benefits shall be reduced (to the minimum extent necessary) such that no portion of such payments and benefits is subject to the Section 4999 excise tax (the "Best-Net Cutback"); provided, however, that the Best-Net Cutback shall apply only if it would result in the Executive's receiving, on an after-tax basis, a greater amount than the Executive would have received absent the cutback.

(j) Section 409A. This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Code. To the extent any payment or benefit hereunder constitutes deferred compensation subject to Section 409A and the Executive is a "specified employee" within the meaning of Section 409A on the date of termination, any such payment or benefit that would otherwise be paid during the six-month period following the Employment End Date shall be delayed and paid in a single lump sum on the first business day following the six-month anniversary of the Employment End Date.

ARTICLE 7 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

(a) Acknowledgment of Massachusetts Noncompetition Agreement Act. The Executive acknowledges that the Massachusetts Noncompetition Agreement Act (M.G.L. c. 149, § 24L) (the "MNAA") imposes specific requirements on noncompetition agreements entered into in connection with employment in Massachusetts, including (i) a requirement that the agreement be presented to the employee at least ten (10) business days before the commencement of employment, (ii) a requirement that the restricted period not exceed twelve (12) months following the termination of employment, (iii) a requirement of garden leave consideration during the restricted period (or other mutually-agreed consideration set forth in the agreement), (iv) a requirement that the restriction be reasonable in geographic scope and substantive scope, and (v) certain other limitations and notice requirements. The Executive acknowledges that this Agreement, as an Amended and Restated Employment Agreement, was provided to the Executive at least ten (10) business days before the Effective Date and that the Executive had a meaningful opportunity to review it with counsel of the Executive's choosing.

(b) Non-Competition. During the Executive's employment with the Company and for a period of twelve (12) months after the Employment End Date (the "Restricted Period"), the Executive shall not, without the prior written consent of the Company, directly or indirectly, anywhere in the United States in which the Company actively conducts business as of the Employment End Date, engage in, accept employment from, or otherwise provide services to any person or entity that competes with the Company in the design, manufacture, sale, or service of vacuum technology, plasma technology, or related semiconductor process control equipment.

(c) Garden Leave Consideration. As consideration for the Executive's compliance with Section 7(b), and in satisfaction of the MNAA's garden leave requirement, during the Restricted Period the Company shall pay the Executive an amount equal to fifty percent (50%) of the Executive's annualized Base Salary as of the Employment End Date, payable in equal monthly installments over the Restricted Period. For the avoidance of doubt, this garden leave payment is in addition to any Severance Compensation paid under Section 6, except that the Company may, in its sole discretion, offset the garden leave payment against any Severance Compensation under Section 6(e)(i) or Section 6(g)(i) to the extent permitted by the MNAA and applicable law.

(d) Non-Solicitation of Employees. During the Restricted Period, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment, hire, or attempt to solicit for employment or hire any officer or other employee of the Company with whom the Executive worked or had material contact during the twelve (12) months preceding the Employment End Date. The foregoing shall not prohibit (i) general advertising or solicitation not specifically targeted at officers or employees of the Company, or (ii) the hiring of any person who responds to such general advertising on his or her own initiative.

(e) Non-Solicitation of Customers. During the Restricted Period, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit any customer of the Company with whom the Executive had material contact during the twelve (12) months preceding the Employment End Date for the purpose of providing products or services that compete with the Company's products or services.

(f) Non-Disparagement. The Executive shall not, both during the Employment Period and at any time thereafter, make or publish any statement (orally, in writing, on social media, or otherwise) that disparages, defames, or impugns the reputation of the Company or any of its officers, directors, employees, or agents.

(g) Tolling. The Restricted Period shall be tolled day-for-day for any period during which the Executive is in breach of any covenant set forth in this Section 7, until the Executive has fully complied with such covenant.

ARTICLE 9 — DEFEND TRADE SECRETS ACT WHISTLEBLOWER NOTICE

9.1 Defend Trade Secrets Act Whistleblower Notice. Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b) of the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016, the Executive is hereby notified that: (a) an individual shall not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret that is made (i) in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official, either directly or indirectly, or to an attorney, and (ii) solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or that is made in a complaint or other document filed in a lawsuit or other proceeding, if such filing is made under seal; and (b) an individual who files a lawsuit for retaliation by an employer for reporting a suspected violation of law may disclose the trade secret to the attorney of the individual and use the trade secret information in the court proceeding, if the individual (i) files any document containing the trade secret under seal and (ii) does not disclose the trade secret, except pursuant to court order.

The Executive acknowledges that the Executive's incentive compensation is subject to such recovery and agrees to repay any compensation required to be recovered.
