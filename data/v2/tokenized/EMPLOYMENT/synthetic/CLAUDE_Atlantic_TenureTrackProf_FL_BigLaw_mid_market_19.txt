FACULTY APPOINTMENT LETTER (TENURED ASSOCIATE PROFESSOR)

This FACULTY APPOINTMENT LETTER (this "Appointment Letter") is issued by {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "University"), and accepted by {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual (the "Faculty Member").

WHEREAS, the University is a state university and an instrumentality of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, established to advance teaching, research, and service in higher education;

WHEREAS, the University desires to appoint the Faculty Member to a tenured Associate Professor position in the Department of Computer Science, with full faculty privileges including academic freedom, sabbatical eligibility, and tenure protections;

WHEREAS, the appointment is subject to the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, the regulations of the University Board of Trustees, the University Faculty Handbook, and any applicable collective bargaining agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, the University extends and the Faculty Member accepts this appointment on the terms set forth herein.

ARTICLE 1 — APPOINTMENT AND POSITION

1.1 Appointment. The University hereby appoints the Faculty Member as {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}, with tenure, in the Department of Computer Science, College of Engineering and Computer Science.

1.2 Reporting and Department Affiliation. The Faculty Member shall report to the {{EMPLOYEE_REPORTS_TO}}.

1.3 Faculty Duties. The Faculty Member's duties shall consist of (a) teaching graduate and undergraduate courses in computer science as assigned by the Department Chair, (b) conducting and publishing original scholarly research, (c) supervising graduate students and serving on doctoral dissertation committees, (d) performing service to the Department, the College, and the University, including committee work, faculty governance, and professional service, and (e) such other duties as are customary for tenured faculty at a Research-1 university.

1.4 Academic Freedom. The Faculty Member is entitled to academic freedom in teaching, research, and publication, consistent with the AAUP 1940 Statement of Principles on Academic Freedom and Tenure and the University Faculty Handbook.

1.5 Place of Appointment. The Faculty Member's primary place of appointment is the University's main campus at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}}.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM OF APPOINTMENT

2.1 Tenured Appointment. This is a tenured appointment with no fixed term. The Faculty Member's employment shall continue until terminated only as provided in Article 6.

2.2 Annual Reappointment. The Faculty Member's appointment is subject to annual reappointment by the Department Chair, the College Dean, and the Provost, in accordance with the University Faculty Handbook. Annual reappointment is administrative confirmation of continuing tenure status; it is not a renewable contract subject to non-renewal at the discretion of the University.

2.3 Sabbatical Eligibility. The Faculty Member is eligible for sabbatical leave in accordance with the University's sabbatical policy, generally one semester at full pay or two semesters at half pay every seventh year.

ARTICLE 3 — COMPENSATION

3.1 Base Salary. The University shall pay the Faculty Member an annual base salary of {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}}, paid in equal monthly installments over the academic year (or, at the Faculty Member's election, over twelve months in accordance with University payroll policy).

3.2 Summer Salary. The Faculty Member is eligible to earn additional summer salary by serving as Principal Investigator on externally funded research grants during summer months, in accordance with the University's effort reporting and salary buy-out policies.

3.3 Annual Salary Adjustments. The Faculty Member is eligible for annual cost-of-living adjustments and merit-based salary increases as determined by the Department Chair, the Dean, and the Provost in accordance with the University's annual salary adjustment process.

3.4 Welfare and Retirement Benefits. The Faculty Member shall participate in the State University System retirement plan and the University's standard health, dental, vision, and life insurance plans, in accordance with University benefit policies.

3.5 Research Start-Up Package. As an inducement to accept this appointment, the University shall provide a one-time research start-up package valued at One Hundred Twenty-Five Thousand United States Dollars (US$125,000), available for equipment purchases, graduate student support, conference travel, and similar research expenses, to be expended within three (3) academic years.

3.6 Outside Consulting. The Faculty Member is permitted to engage in outside consulting work up to one (1) day per week (for a total of approximately 20% of the Faculty Member's time), subject to (a) prior written disclosure to the Department Chair, (b) compliance with the University's outside activity reporting policy, (c) avoidance of conflicts of interest with the Faculty Member's University duties, and (d) compliance with the Florida public records and ethics laws.

ARTICLE 4 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 Scholarly Publications. The Faculty Member retains copyright in all scholarly publications, including journal articles, conference papers, books, book chapters, monographs, and creative works, consistent with the University's scholarly publication policy and the principles of academic freedom.

4.2 Inventions and Bayh-Dole Compliance. To the extent the Faculty Member conceives or reduces to practice any patentable invention in the course of University-supported research, including any research funded by federal grants subject to the Bayh-Dole Act (35 U.S.C. §§ 200-212), the University retains the right to elect title to such inventions in accordance with the University's intellectual property policy and the requirements of Bayh-Dole. Net proceeds from licensing of such inventions shall be shared with the Faculty Member in accordance with the University's standard inventor royalty schedule.

4.3 Federally Funded Research March-In Rights. The Faculty Member acknowledges that inventions developed in the course of federally funded research are subject to the federal government's march-in rights and royalty-free license rights under 35 U.S.C. § 203.

4.4 Pre-Existing Inventions. Any inventions developed by the Faculty Member before the Effective Date, or developed entirely outside the course of University-supported research and without University resources, are not subject to assignment to the University.

ARTICLE 5 — STUDENT RECORDS, ACCESSIBILITY, AND COMPLIANCE

5.1 FERPA Training. The Faculty Member shall complete FERPA training during onboarding and annually thereafter, and shall handle student educational records in compliance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act and the University's FERPA policy.

5.2 Title IX Compliance. The Faculty Member is a "responsible employee" under Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972 and shall report any disclosure of sexual misconduct in accordance with the University's Title IX policy.

5.3 Florida Public Records Act. The Faculty Member acknowledges that the University is subject to the Florida public records laws and that certain University-held records (including some research records, communications, and personnel records) may be subject to disclosure under the Florida Sunshine Law. The Faculty Member shall cooperate with any lawful public records request directed to the Faculty Member's University records.

ARTICLE 6 — TERMINATION

6.1 For Cause Only. Tenured faculty appointments may be terminated only for "just cause" as defined in the University Faculty Handbook, including without limitation: (a) demonstrably incompetent performance of academic duties; (b) personal conduct that materially impairs the Faculty Member's fitness as a faculty member, such as repeated unprofessional conduct or material violations of University policy; (c) academic misconduct, research misconduct, or plagiarism; or (d) conviction of a felony or any crime involving moral turpitude.

6.2 Due Process. Termination for cause shall be subject to AAUP-style due process, including written specification of charges, a hearing before a faculty committee, the right to be represented by counsel, and the right to a written decision.

6.3 Financial Exigency or Program Discontinuance. The University may also terminate a tenured faculty appointment in cases of bona fide financial exigency or formal program discontinuance, in accordance with the University Faculty Handbook and AAUP guidelines.

6.4 Retirement and Phased Retirement. The Faculty Member is eligible for the University's voluntary phased retirement program, in accordance with University policy.

6.5 Resignation. The Faculty Member may resign from the appointment by giving the Department Chair at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' prior written notice, normally aligned with the end of an academic semester to facilitate course coverage.

ARTICLE 7 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

7.1 Confidentiality of Student Information. The Faculty Member shall maintain the confidentiality of student educational records (including grades, evaluations, and any communications about students) in accordance with FERPA, both during and after the appointment.

7.2 Confidentiality of Research Data. To the extent the Faculty Member has access to non-public research data of other faculty, sponsored research subjects, or third parties, the Faculty Member shall maintain its confidentiality in accordance with applicable IRB protocols, sponsored research agreements, and University policy.

7.3 No Post-Employment Non-Compete. The Faculty Member is not subject to any post-employment non-competition restriction. The Faculty Member retains full freedom of movement to other academic institutions, research labs, or commercial settings after leaving the University.

ARTICLE 8 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1 Internal Grievance. Disputes arising under this Appointment Letter or under the University Faculty Handbook shall first be addressed through the University's internal faculty grievance procedure.

8.2 Governing Law. This Appointment Letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

8.3 Sovereign Immunity. Nothing in this Appointment Letter shall be construed as a waiver of any sovereign or governmental immunity that the University may enjoy under {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}} law or under the Eleventh Amendment to the United States Constitution.

ARTICLE 9 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

9.1 Entire Appointment. This Appointment Letter, together with the University Faculty Handbook, the relevant collective bargaining agreement (if any), and the University's standard terms and conditions of employment, constitutes the entire appointment.

9.2 Amendment. This Appointment Letter may be amended only by a writing signed by the Faculty Member, the Department Chair, the Dean, and the Provost.

9.3 Assignment. This Appointment Letter is personal to the Faculty Member and cannot be assigned.

9.4 Counterparts. This Appointment Letter may be executed in counterparts.
