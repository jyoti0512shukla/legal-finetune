EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement"), dated as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date"), is entered into by and among Pacific Crest Bancorp, a Delaware corporation (the "Bancorp"), {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Bank") (the Bancorp and the Bank are referred to herein collectively as the "Company"), on the one hand, and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing in the State of {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} (the "Executive"), on the other hand. The Bancorp, the Bank, and the Executive are referred to herein collectively as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party."

WHEREAS, the Bancorp is a Delaware bank holding company that owns the Bank, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}}-state-chartered commercial bank insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation;

WHEREAS, the Company desires to employ the Executive in the position of {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}, and the Executive desires to accept such employment, all upon the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement;

WHEREAS, the Company and the Executive acknowledge that this Agreement governs the Executive's employment with both the Bancorp and the Bank, and that the Executive's services for the Bank are an integral part of the Executive's services for the Bancorp;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises, the mutual covenants set forth herein, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Bancorp Board" means the Board of Directors of Pacific Crest Bancorp.

1.2 "Bank Board" means the Board of Directors of the Bank.

1.3 "Boards" means the Bancorp Board and the Bank Board, collectively.

1.4 "Cause" means the Executive's: (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, any felony or any crime involving moral turpitude, fraud, embezzlement, or dishonesty; (b) willful misconduct or gross negligence in the performance of the Executive's duties that has caused, or is reasonably likely to cause, material harm to the Company; (c) willful failure or refusal to perform the lawful and reasonable directives of the Boards or the Executive Chairman, after written notice and a reasonable opportunity to cure; (d) material breach of this Agreement, the Confidential Information Agreement, or any written Company policy, after written notice and a reasonable opportunity to cure; (e) any act or omission constituting a material violation of any law, rule, or regulation governing the Company's operations as a state-chartered banking institution; or (f) any act or omission that results in the Executive being prohibited or suspended from participating in the affairs of the Bank by any banking regulator with jurisdiction over the Company. No act or omission shall be deemed "willful" unless done, or omitted to be done, in bad faith and without reasonable belief that the conduct was in the best interests of the Company.

1.5 "Change in Control" has the meaning set forth in any Change in Control Agreement then in effect between the Parties. For the avoidance of doubt, this Agreement does not itself define a Change in Control event; the Parties have agreed that any Change-in-Control protections applicable to the Executive are governed exclusively by a separate Change in Control Agreement, and the existence and terms of that separate agreement supersede any inconsistent provision herein.

1.6 "Code" means the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.

1.7 "Confidential Information Agreement" means the Confidential Information, Intellectual Property and Non-Solicitation Agreement of the Company, executed by the Executive concurrently with this Agreement and attached hereto as Attachment A.

1.8 "Disability" means the Executive's inability, by reason of physical or mental illness, injury, or other incapacity, to perform the essential functions of the Executive's position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for any 90 consecutive days or 120 days in any 12-month period, as determined in good faith by the Bancorp Board.

1.9 "Employment Period" means the period commencing on the Effective Date and ending on the date the Executive's employment with the Company terminates in accordance with Article 5.

1.10 "Good Reason" means the occurrence, without the Executive's prior written consent, of any of the following: (a) a material and adverse diminution in the Executive's title, authority, duties, or responsibilities; (b) a material reduction in the Executive's Base Salary; (c) a material relocation of the Executive's principal place of employment more than 50 miles from {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}; or (d) a material breach by the Company of this Agreement; provided that the Executive must (i) give the Company written notice of the alleged Good Reason event within 60 days after the Executive becomes aware of it, (ii) afford the Company a 30-day cure period, and (iii) terminate the Executive's employment within 30 days after the expiration of the cure period if the Company has not cured the alleged event.

ARTICLE 2 — POSITION AND DUTIES

2.1 Position. During the Employment Period, the Executive shall serve as {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}, reporting to the {{EMPLOYEE_REPORTS_TO}}. The Executive shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily performed by a chief executive of a state-chartered bank holding company and its subsidiary bank of comparable size and scope, and such other duties as may be reasonably assigned by the Boards or the Executive Chairman of the Bancorp from time to time.

2.2 Exclusive Services. The Executive shall devote substantially all of the Executive's business time, attention, energy, and skill to the performance of the Executive's duties under this Agreement and to advancing the business and interests of the Company. The Executive shall not, without the prior written consent of the Bancorp Board, engage in any other business, profession, or occupation for compensation or otherwise that would interfere with the Executive's duties under this Agreement, provided that the Executive may, subject to the Confidential Information Agreement and the policies of the Company, (a) serve on the boards of directors or advisory boards of charitable, civic, or educational organizations, (b) deliver lectures, fulfill speaking engagements, or teach at educational institutions, and (c) manage the Executive's personal investments, in each case so long as such activities do not materially interfere with the performance of the Executive's duties or violate any policy of the Company.

2.3 Principal Place of Employment. The Executive's principal place of employment shall be the Company's executive offices located in {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}. The Executive acknowledges that the Executive's duties may require travel from time to time on Company business.

2.4 Compliance with Banking Laws and Regulations. The Executive acknowledges that the Company is a regulated banking institution subject to the supervision and examination of the Federal Reserve System, the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, the {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}} Department of Financial Protection and Innovation, and other applicable banking regulators. The Executive shall at all times comply with all laws, rules, regulations, and supervisory directives applicable to the Company and to the Executive in the Executive's capacity as a senior officer of the Company, and shall cooperate fully with any examination, investigation, or supervisory action by any such regulator.

ARTICLE 3 — TERM AND COMMENCEMENT

3.1 Initial Term. The Executive's employment under this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial term of {{TERM_YEARS}} years thereafter (the "Initial Term"), unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 5.

3.2 Renewal Terms. Unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal to the other Party at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} calendar days prior to the expiration of the Initial Term or any then-current Additional Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods (each, an "Additional Term"). For purposes of this Agreement, "Employment Period" includes the Initial Term and any Additional Terms.

3.3 Effect of Non-Renewal. Non-renewal of this Agreement by the Company shall be treated as a termination by the Company without Cause for purposes of Article 6. Non-renewal by the Executive shall be treated as a termination by the Executive without Good Reason.

ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

4.1 Base Salary. The Company shall pay the Executive an annual base salary of {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}} (the "Base Salary"), payable in substantially equal installments in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices for senior executives. The Base Salary shall be reviewed annually by the Compensation Committee of the Bancorp Board (the "Compensation Committee") and may be increased (but not decreased, except in connection with an across-the-board reduction applied to all senior executives of the Company) in the Compensation Committee's sole discretion.

4.2 Annual Bonus. The Executive shall be eligible to participate in the Company's annual cash incentive program for senior executives. The Executive's target annual bonus shall be {{EMPLOYEE_TARGET_BONUS_PCT}}% of the Executive's then-current Base Salary, with the actual bonus amount, if any, determined by the Compensation Committee in its sole discretion based on the achievement of corporate and individual performance objectives established by the Compensation Committee for the relevant fiscal year. To be eligible for any annual bonus, the Executive must be employed by the Company on the date the bonus is paid, except as otherwise expressly provided in Article 6.

4.3 Equity Compensation. As soon as practicable following the Effective Date, the Bancorp shall grant to the Executive an initial equity award with an aggregate grant-date fair value of {{EMPLOYEE_INITIAL_EQUITY}} (the "Initial Equity Grant"), in such form and on such terms as the Compensation Committee determines, subject to the terms of the Bancorp's then-effective equity incentive plan and the applicable award agreement. The Initial Equity Grant shall vest in equal annual installments over a four-year period beginning on the first anniversary of the Effective Date, subject to the Executive's continued employment through each vesting date, and subject to accelerated vesting only as expressly provided in any Change in Control Agreement then in effect.

4.4 Annual Equity Awards. During the Employment Period, the Executive shall be eligible to receive additional equity awards under the Bancorp's equity incentive plan from time to time as determined by the Compensation Committee in its sole discretion, with grant timing, value, vesting schedule, and other terms set forth in the applicable award agreements.

4.5 Welfare and Retirement Benefits. The Executive and the Executive's eligible dependents shall be entitled to participate in all employee welfare and retirement benefit plans, programs, practices, and policies that the Company makes available to its senior executives generally, on terms no less favorable than those generally applicable to other senior executives of the Company. The Company reserves the right to amend, modify, or terminate any such plan, program, practice, or policy at any time in its sole discretion, subject to the terms of the applicable plan and applicable law.

4.6 Vacation. The Executive shall be entitled to four (4) weeks of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued in accordance with the Company's standard vacation policy for senior executives. Unused vacation may be carried over from one year to the next up to a maximum accrual of 1.5 times the Executive's annual entitlement, after which further accrual shall cease until the Executive's accrued balance falls below the cap.

4.7 Business Expenses. The Company shall reimburse the Executive for all reasonable and necessary business expenses incurred by the Executive in the performance of the Executive's duties hereunder, in accordance with the Company's standard expense reimbursement policy. Reimbursement of expenses incurred in one calendar year shall not affect the reimbursement of expenses incurred in any other calendar year, and reimbursement shall be made in accordance with Section 409A of the Code.

4.8 Clawback. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, any incentive-based or other compensation paid or awarded to the Executive under this Agreement or any other arrangement with the Company shall be subject to recovery (clawback) to the extent required by (a) Section 954 of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act and any rules or regulations promulgated thereunder (including 17 CFR § 240.10D-1), (b) any applicable stock exchange listing standard, (c) any banking regulatory clawback or recoupment requirement applicable to the Company, and (d) any clawback or recoupment policy adopted by the Bancorp Board or the Compensation Committee from time to time. The Executive acknowledges that the Executive's incentive compensation is subject to the foregoing and agrees to repay any compensation required to be recovered under any such law, rule, regulation, or policy.

ARTICLE 5 — TERMINATION

5.1 Termination Events. The Executive's employment under this Agreement may be terminated as follows:

(a) Death. The Executive's employment shall terminate automatically upon the Executive's death.

(b) Disability. The Company may terminate the Executive's employment upon the Executive's Disability, on written notice to the Executive.

(c) For Cause. The Company may terminate the Executive's employment for Cause at any time, immediately upon written notice to the Executive, subject to any applicable cure period set forth in the definition of Cause.

(d) Without Cause. The Company may terminate the Executive's employment without Cause at any time, on at least thirty (30) calendar days' prior written notice to the Executive.

(e) For Good Reason. The Executive may terminate the Executive's employment for Good Reason in accordance with the procedure set forth in the definition of Good Reason.

(f) Without Good Reason. The Executive may terminate the Executive's employment without Good Reason at any time, on at least sixty (60) calendar days' prior written notice to the Company.

(g) Non-Renewal. The Employment Period shall end automatically on the expiration of the Initial Term or any Additional Term if either Party has given a timely written notice of non-renewal under Section 3.2.

(h) Regulatory Removal. The Executive's employment shall terminate automatically if any banking regulator with jurisdiction over the Company prohibits or suspends the Executive from participating in the affairs of the Bank.

5.2 Notice of Termination. Any termination of the Executive's employment by either Party (other than termination due to death) shall be communicated by written Notice of Termination. The Notice of Termination shall specify the provision of this Agreement under which termination is effected and, if applicable, the alleged grounds for Cause or Good Reason.

ARTICLE 6 — SEVERANCE AND TERMINATION BENEFITS

6.1 Accrued Obligations. Upon any termination of the Executive's employment, the Company shall pay or provide to the Executive (or, in the event of the Executive's death, to the Executive's estate or designated beneficiary) the following accrued obligations (collectively, the "Accrued Obligations"): (a) any Base Salary earned but unpaid through the date of termination; (b) any annual bonus earned for a completed fiscal year but unpaid as of the date of termination; (c) any unreimbursed business expenses incurred through the date of termination, in accordance with Section 4.7; (d) any accrued but unused vacation, paid out as wages in accordance with applicable law; and (e) any vested benefits under any Company employee benefit plan, payable in accordance with the terms of the applicable plan.

6.2 Severance for Termination Without Cause or for Good Reason. If, during the Employment Period, the Executive's employment is terminated by the Company without Cause (including by reason of the Company's non-renewal under Section 3.3) or by the Executive for Good Reason, then, in addition to the Accrued Obligations and subject to the Executive's execution and non-revocation of a general release of claims in the form attached hereto as Attachment B (the "Release Agreement") within sixty (60) days after the date of termination, the Company shall provide the Executive with the following severance benefits (collectively, the "Severance Benefits"):

(a) a lump-sum cash payment equal to eighteen (18) months of the Executive's then-current Base Salary, payable within fifteen (15) days after the Release Agreement becomes irrevocable;

(b) a lump-sum cash payment equal to one and one-half (1.5) times the Executive's target annual bonus for the fiscal year in which the termination occurs, payable within fifteen (15) days after the Release Agreement becomes irrevocable;

(c) for a period of eighteen (18) months following the date of termination, to the extent the Executive timely elects continuation coverage under the Company's group health plans pursuant to the Consolidated Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1985 ("COBRA") and remits the amount of premium charged to similarly situated active employees for comparable coverage, the Company shall pay to the Executive a monthly cash amount equal to the Company's portion of the premium for such coverage; and

(d) any equity awards that would have vested during the eighteen (18) months following the date of termination shall accelerate and become fully vested as of the date of termination, in each case to the extent permitted by the applicable equity plan and award agreement.

6.3 No-Mitigation; No Offset. The Executive shall not be required to mitigate damages by seeking other employment or otherwise as a condition of receiving the Severance Benefits, and the Severance Benefits shall not be reduced by any compensation or benefits earned by the Executive from any subsequent employer or other source after the date of termination.

6.4 Section 280G. If any payment or benefit received or to be received by the Executive (whether under this Agreement, any other agreement with the Company, or any Company plan) would constitute a "parachute payment" within the meaning of Section 280G of the Code and would be subject to the excise tax imposed by Section 4999 of the Code, then the aggregate amount of such payments and benefits shall be reduced (to the minimum extent necessary) such that no portion of such payments and benefits is subject to the Section 4999 excise tax (the "Best-Net Cutback"); provided, however, that the Best-Net Cutback shall apply only if it would result in the Executive's receiving, on an after-tax basis, a greater amount than the Executive would have received absent the cutback. The determination of whether and to what extent the cutback applies shall be made by an independent nationally recognized public accounting firm selected by the Company.

6.5 Section 409A. This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Code, and shall be interpreted accordingly. To the extent any payment or benefit hereunder constitutes "deferred compensation" subject to Section 409A and the Executive is a "specified employee" within the meaning of Section 409A on the date of termination, any such payment or benefit that would otherwise be paid during the six-month period following the date of termination shall be delayed and paid in a single lump sum on the first business day after the six-month anniversary of the date of termination (or, if earlier, the date of the Executive's death).

6.6 Change in Control Agreement Supersession. Notwithstanding anything in this Article 6 to the contrary, if the Executive is entitled to severance benefits under any separate Change in Control Agreement then in effect between the Parties as a result of a termination occurring within the protected period of such Change in Control Agreement, then (a) the severance benefits under that Change in Control Agreement shall apply in lieu of the Severance Benefits set forth in this Article 6, and (b) the Executive shall not be entitled to duplicate severance under this Agreement and the Change in Control Agreement.

ARTICLE 7 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

7.1 Acknowledgment of California Law. The Executive and the Company acknowledge that California Business and Professions Code Section 16600 generally voids contracts that restrain a person from engaging in a lawful profession, trade, or business of any kind. Accordingly, this Agreement does not contain a post-employment non-competition restriction. The restrictive covenants set forth in this Article 7 are limited to the protection of the Company's confidential information and trade secrets and to the prevention of solicitation activities expressly permitted under California law.

7.2 Non-Solicitation of Employees. During the Employment Period and for a period of twelve (12) months after the date of termination of the Executive's employment for any reason, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, recruit, solicit for employment, hire, or attempt to recruit, solicit for employment, or hire any officer or other employee of the Company with whom the Executive worked or had material contact during the Employment Period, on behalf of the Executive or any other person or entity. The foregoing shall not prohibit (a) general advertising or solicitation not specifically targeted at officers or employees of the Company, or (b) the hiring of any person who responds to such general advertising on his or her own initiative.

7.3 Non-Solicitation of Bank Customers. During the Employment Period and for a period of twelve (12) months after the date of termination of the Executive's employment for any reason, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit any depositor, borrower, or other customer of the Bank with whom the Executive had material contact during the Employment Period for the purpose of providing banking, lending, wealth management, or other financial services that compete with the products or services offered by the Bank. The foregoing is intended to protect the Bank's customer relationships, which constitute trade secrets of the Bank under California law.

7.4 Non-Disparagement. The Executive shall not, both during the Employment Period and at any time thereafter, make or publish any statement (orally, in writing, on social media, or in any other medium) that disparages, defames, or impugns the reputation of the Bancorp, the Bank, any of their subsidiaries or affiliates, or any of their respective officers, directors, employees, or agents, in each case other than truthful statements made (a) in connection with any litigation or governmental investigation, (b) in good faith to any banking regulator with jurisdiction over the Company, or (c) in connection with the Executive's exercise of rights under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016 or any other applicable whistleblower-protection law.

7.5 Tolling. The post-employment restricted period set forth in Sections 7.2 and 7.3 shall be tolled day-for-day for any period during which the Executive is in breach of any such covenant, until the Executive has fully complied with such covenant.

ARTICLE 8 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND INVENTION ASSIGNMENT

8.1 Confidential Information. The Executive shall hold in strict confidence, and shall not use or disclose to any third party (other than as required in the performance of the Executive's duties for the Company), all non-public information, trade secrets, customer data, financial information, business plans, technology, and personnel information of the Company and its subsidiaries and affiliates that the Executive obtains, develops, or has access to during the Employment Period (collectively, the "Confidential Information"). The Executive's obligations under this Section 8.1 shall continue indefinitely after the date of termination of the Executive's employment.

8.2 Defend Trade Secrets Act Whistleblower Notice. Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b) of the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016, the Executive is hereby notified that: (a) an individual shall not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret that is made (i) in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official, either directly or indirectly, or to an attorney, and (ii) solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or that is made in a complaint or other document filed in a lawsuit or other proceeding, if such filing is made under seal; and (b) an individual who files a lawsuit for retaliation by an employer for reporting a suspected violation of law may disclose the trade secret to the attorney of the individual and use the trade secret information in the court proceeding, if the individual (i) files any document containing the trade secret under seal and (ii) does not disclose the trade secret, except pursuant to court order.

8.3 Invention Assignment. The Executive hereby assigns to the Company the Executive's entire right, title, and interest in and to all inventions, discoveries, improvements, works of authorship, and other intellectual property that the Executive conceives, develops, or reduces to practice, alone or jointly with others, during the Employment Period that (a) relate at the time of conception or reduction to practice to the business of the Company, (b) result from any work performed by the Executive for the Company, or (c) are developed using any Company resources or Confidential Information (collectively, the "Work Product"). All copyrightable Work Product shall be deemed a "work made for hire" within the meaning of the United States Copyright Act, with the Company deemed the author thereof, and to the extent any such Work Product does not qualify as a work made for hire, the Executive hereby assigns all right, title, and interest therein to the Company.

8.4 California Labor Code Section 2870 Notice. Notwithstanding Section 8.3, this assignment does not apply to any invention that the Executive developed entirely on the Executive's own time without using Company equipment, supplies, facilities, or trade secret information, except for those inventions that either (a) relate at the time of conception or reduction to practice to the Company's business, or to the Company's actual or demonstrably anticipated research or development, or (b) result from any work performed by the Executive for the Company. This notice is provided pursuant to California Labor Code Section 2870.

8.5 Return of Company Property. Upon the termination of the Executive's employment for any reason, the Executive shall promptly return to the Company all Confidential Information and all other Company property in the Executive's possession, custody, or control, including without limitation all computers, mobile devices, identification cards, keys, files, documents, and electronic records.

ARTICLE 9 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

9.1 Executive Representations. The Executive represents and warrants that: (a) the Executive has the full right and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform all of the Executive's obligations hereunder; (b) the Executive is not subject to any agreement, restriction, or obligation that would prevent or interfere with the Executive's performance of the Executive's duties under this Agreement, including any non-competition, non-solicitation, or confidentiality agreement with any prior employer; (c) the Executive will not bring to the Company or use in the performance of the Executive's duties any confidential information or trade secrets of any prior employer; and (d) the Executive has not been the subject of any final order, agreement, or settlement with any banking regulator that prohibits or restricts the Executive's participation in the affairs of any bank holding company or insured depository institution.

9.2 Company Representations. The Company represents and warrants that the execution and performance of this Agreement by the Company has been duly authorized by the Bancorp Board and the Bank Board.

ARTICLE 10 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GOVERNING LAW

10.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

10.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Agreement, or to the breach, termination, enforcement, interpretation, or validity hereof, shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, and each Party irrevocably submits to the personal jurisdiction of such courts.

10.3 Equitable Relief Carve-Out. Notwithstanding Section 10.2, either Party may seek equitable relief, including specific performance, injunction, or other equitable remedies, in any court of competent jurisdiction, to enforce the provisions of Article 7 or Article 8 of this Agreement, without the requirement of posting bond and without proving actual damages.

10.4 Waiver of Jury Trial. EACH PARTY HEREBY IRREVOCABLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY LEGAL PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

10.5 Attorneys' Fees. In any dispute arising under this Agreement, each Party shall bear its own attorneys' fees and costs, except that the prevailing Party in any litigation to enforce Article 7 or Article 8 of this Agreement shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs from the non-prevailing Party.

ARTICLE 11 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

11.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with the Confidential Information Agreement, the Release Agreement (when executed), and any Change in Control Agreement then in effect, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous oral or written agreements, understandings, representations, or warranties, except as expressly preserved herein.

11.2 Amendment. No amendment, modification, or waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the Executive and a duly authorized officer of the Bancorp.

11.3 Assignment. The Executive may not assign this Agreement or any of the Executive's rights or obligations hereunder. The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor (whether by merger, consolidation, sale of assets, or otherwise) that assumes the obligations of the Company hereunder, and any such successor shall be deemed the "Company" for purposes of this Agreement.

11.4 Notices. All notices and other communications under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, sent by confirmed email, or mailed by certified mail (return receipt requested), to the respective addresses of the Parties set forth above (or to such other address as either Party may designate by written notice to the other).

11.5 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable in any respect, such invalidity, illegality, or unenforceability shall not affect any other provision of this Agreement, and this Agreement shall be construed as if such invalid, illegal, or unenforceable provision had never been contained herein.

11.6 Survival. Articles 6, 7, 8, and 10, and Sections 11.1 through 11.7, shall survive the termination of the Executive's employment and the expiration or termination of this Agreement.

11.7 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in one or more counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Delivery of an executed counterpart by electronic means shall be as effective as delivery of an originally executed counterpart.
