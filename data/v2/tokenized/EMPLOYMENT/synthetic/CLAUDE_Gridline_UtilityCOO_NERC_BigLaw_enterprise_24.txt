EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT — CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing in the State of {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} (the "Executive"). The Company and the Executive are collectively the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Company is an investor-owned electric and gas utility serving residential and commercial customers in upstate New York, regulated by the New York Public Service Commission and the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission;

WHEREAS, the Company desires to employ the Executive as its {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}} to oversee the Company's operations, including generation, transmission, distribution, system reliability, and customer service;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Cause" means the Executive's: (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, any felony or any crime involving fraud or moral turpitude; (b) willful misconduct or gross negligence in the performance of the Executive's duties that has caused or is reasonably likely to cause material harm to the Company; (c) material violation of the Company's code of business conduct; (d) material breach of any NERC Critical Infrastructure Protection (CIP) requirement that compromises bulk-power-system reliability; (e) any final disciplinary action by FERC or the New York Public Service Commission that prohibits the Executive from continuing in the Executive's role; or (f) material breach of this Agreement after written notice and a 30-day cure opportunity.

1.2 "Change in Control" has the meaning customarily given to that term in publicly traded utility holding company executive employment agreements.

1.3 "Code" means the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.

1.4 "Good Reason" means a material adverse change in the Executive's title or duties, a material reduction in Base Salary or target Annual Bonus opportunity, or a material relocation of the Executive's principal place of employment more than 50 miles, in each case subject to a 60-day notice and 30-day cure mechanism.

ARTICLE 2 — POSITION AND DUTIES

2.1 Position. The Company shall employ the Executive as {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}, reporting to the {{EMPLOYEE_REPORTS_TO}}.

2.2 Duties. The Executive shall be responsible for the Company's operations, including (a) electric generation, transmission, and distribution; (b) gas distribution and storage; (c) system reliability and operations control; (d) NERC CIP and physical security compliance; (e) customer service and billing; (f) field operations and emergency response; and (g) such other duties as the Chief Executive Officer may reasonably assign.

2.3 NERC CIP and FERC Compliance. The Executive shall ensure that the Company maintains compliance with all NERC Reliability Standards (including the Critical Infrastructure Protection Reliability Standards), FERC regulations, and New York Public Service Commission orders applicable to the Company. The Executive shall be the senior officer responsible for cybersecurity and physical security under the NERC CIP standards and shall designate appropriate compliance officers reporting to the Executive.

2.4 Wildfire and Catastrophic Event Accountability. The Executive shall be the senior executive responsible for the Company's wildfire mitigation plan, storm response, and other catastrophic event preparedness, in accordance with applicable state and federal regulatory requirements. The Executive shall report at least quarterly to the Board of Directors on the Company's catastrophic event preparedness.

2.5 Place of Employment. The Executive's principal place of employment shall be the Company's offices in {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

ARTICLE 3 — TERM

3.1 Term. The Executive's employment shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated in accordance with Article 5.

ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

4.1 Base Salary. The Company shall pay the Executive an annual base salary of {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}}, reviewed annually by the Compensation Committee.

4.2 Annual Incentive Plan. The Executive shall be eligible to participate in the Company's annual incentive plan with a target of {{EMPLOYEE_TARGET_BONUS_PCT}}% of Base Salary, based on Company financial performance, operational performance (including system reliability metrics), and individual performance.

4.3 Long-Term Incentive Plan. The Executive shall participate in the Company's long-term incentive plan with an annual grant value of approximately {{EMPLOYEE_INITIAL_EQUITY}} (as the initial grant), in the form of restricted stock units, performance share units, or stock options as determined by the Compensation Committee.

4.4 Welfare and Retirement Benefits. The Executive shall participate in the Company's standard executive benefit plans on terms no less favorable than those generally applicable to other senior executives.

4.5 Vacation. The Executive shall be entitled to five (5) weeks of paid vacation per calendar year.

4.6 Indemnification. The Company shall indemnify the Executive to the fullest extent permitted by New York law and shall maintain D&O liability insurance coverage on terms no less favorable than the insurance covering any other senior executive.

4.7 Clawback. Any incentive compensation paid to the Executive shall be subject to recovery to the extent required by Section 304 of the Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002, Section 954 of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act, the SEC's clawback rules at 17 CFR § 240.10D-1, and any clawback policy adopted by the Company.

ARTICLE 5 — TERMINATION AND SEVERANCE

5.1 Termination Events. The Executive's employment may be terminated as follows: (a) automatically upon death; (b) by the Company on the Executive's Disability; (c) by the Company for Cause; (d) by the Company without Cause on at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' notice; (e) by the Executive for Good Reason; (f) by the Executive without Good Reason on at least 90 days' notice.

5.2 Severance for Termination Without Cause or for Good Reason. Subject to the Executive's execution and non-revocation of a release of claims, the Executive shall be entitled to: (a) twenty-four (24) months of Base Salary; (b) two (2) times target Annual Bonus; (c) twenty-four (24) months of COBRA continuation; and (d) accelerated vesting of long-term incentive awards on a pro-rata basis through the date of termination.

5.3 Enhanced Severance on CIC Double-Trigger. If the Executive's employment is terminated by the Company without Cause or by the Executive for Good Reason within twenty-four (24) months after a Change in Control, the Executive shall be entitled to: (a) thirty-six (36) months of Base Salary; (b) three (3) times target Annual Bonus; (c) thirty-six (36) months of COBRA; and (d) full acceleration of all long-term incentive awards.

5.4 Section 280G Best-Net Cutback. If any payment to the Executive would be subject to the Section 4999 excise tax, the aggregate payments shall be reduced to the extent necessary only if such reduction would result in a greater after-tax amount.

5.5 Section 409A. This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Code.

ARTICLE 6 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

6.1 Non-Competition. During the Executive's employment and for a period of twenty-four (24) months after the date of termination of the Executive's employment for any reason, the Executive shall not, within the geographic service territory of the Company as of the date of termination, accept employment or perform services for any other electric or gas utility, energy generator, or energy retailer that competes directly with the Company.

6.2 Non-Solicitation of Employees. During the Executive's employment and for twenty-four (24) months after termination, the Executive shall not directly solicit for employment any senior employee of the Company with whom the Executive worked or had material contact, subject to a general-solicitation carve-out.

6.3 Non-Disparagement. The Executive shall not, during or after employment, make any public statement that disparages the Company or its officers, directors, or employees.

6.4 Confidentiality. The Executive shall hold in strict confidence all non-public information of the Company, including grid topology, system reliability data, NERC CIP-protected information, customer data, and financial information.

6.5 OFCCP Equal Opportunity. The Company is a federal contractor for purposes of Executive Order 11246 and is committed to equal employment opportunity. The Company will not discriminate against the Executive on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, disability, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law.

ARTICLE 7 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 IP Assignment. The Executive assigns to the Company all inventions and works of authorship created during employment that relate to the Company's business or that are developed using Company resources.

7.2 DTSA Whistleblower Notice. Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), the Executive is notified that the Executive cannot be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official or to an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law.

ARTICLE 8 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

8.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

8.3 Equitable Relief. The Company may seek equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 6 or Article 7 of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 9 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

9.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

9.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

9.3 Assignment. The Executive may not assign this Agreement. The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor.

9.4 Survival. Articles 5, 6, 7, and 8 shall survive the termination of the Executive's employment.

9.5 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.
