EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing in the Commonwealth of {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} (the "Executive"). The Company and the Executive are collectively the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Company is a federal contractor that provides cybersecurity, software engineering, and intelligence community technology services to the Department of Defense and the U.S. intelligence community;

WHEREAS, the Executive currently holds an active Top Secret/Sensitive Compartmented Information (TS/SCI) security clearance with full lifestyle polygraph, and the Company desires to employ the Executive in a position that requires such clearance as a continuing condition of employment;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Cause" means the Executive's: (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, any felony or crime involving fraud or moral turpitude; (b) loss, suspension, revocation, or downgrade of any security clearance required by the Executive's role; (c) any final disciplinary action by a federal contracting officer or by the Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency that prohibits the Executive from participating in federal contracts; (d) willful misconduct in the performance of the Executive's duties; or (e) material breach of this Agreement after written notice and a 30-day cure opportunity.

1.2 "CDI" means Covered Defense Information as defined in DFARS 252.204-7012.

1.3 "Clearance" means the Executive's TS/SCI security clearance with all required compartments and accesses, including any required polygraph status.

1.4 "CUI" means Controlled Unclassified Information as defined in 32 CFR Part 2002.

1.5 "Good Reason" has the customary meaning given to that term in DC-area federal contractor executive employment agreements.

ARTICLE 2 — POSITION AND DUTIES

2.1 Position. The Company shall employ the Executive as {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}, reporting to the {{EMPLOYEE_REPORTS_TO}}.

2.2 Clearance Required. The Executive's continued employment is contingent upon the Executive holding and maintaining the Clearance. The Executive shall:

(a) cooperate with all required reinvestigations, Continuous Evaluation processes, and Security Executive Agent Directive requirements;

(b) report immediately to the Company's Facility Security Officer ("FSO") any event that may affect the Clearance, including foreign travel, foreign contacts, financial issues, criminal charges, alcohol or drug treatment, and changes in marital status;

(c) maintain U.S. citizenship and U.S. person status;

(d) refrain from any conduct that could provide grounds for clearance suspension or revocation under the SEAD-4 adjudicative guidelines.

2.3 Personnel Reliability. The Executive shall flow down all required clearance and personnel reliability requirements to the Executive's direct reports holding cleared positions, in accordance with the Company's facility clearance and the National Industrial Security Program Operating Manual ("NISPOM," DoD Manual 5220.22-M).

2.4 Place of Employment. The Executive's principal place of employment shall be the Company's offices in {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, with travel to government customer sites as required.

ARTICLE 3 — TERM

3.1 At-Will Employment. The Executive's employment shall be at-will, commencing on the Effective Date and continuing until terminated in accordance with Article 5.

ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

4.1 Base Salary. The Company shall pay the Executive an annual base salary of {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}}, reviewed annually.

4.2 Annual Bonus. The Executive shall be eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of {{EMPLOYEE_TARGET_BONUS_PCT}}% of Base Salary, based on Company financial and contract performance.

4.3 Initial Equity Grant. The Company shall grant the Executive an initial equity award (in restricted equity units) with a grant-date fair value of {{EMPLOYEE_INITIAL_EQUITY}}, vesting in equal annual installments over four years.

4.4 Welfare and Retirement Benefits. The Executive shall participate in the Company's standard executive benefit plans on terms no less favorable than those generally applicable to other senior executives.

4.5 Vacation. The Executive shall be entitled to four (4) weeks of paid vacation per calendar year.

4.6 Indemnification. The Company shall indemnify the Executive to the fullest extent permitted by law and shall maintain D&O liability insurance.

ARTICLE 5 — TERMINATION

5.1 Termination Events. The Executive's employment may be terminated as follows: (a) automatically upon death; (b) by the Company on the Executive's Disability; (c) by the Company for Cause; (d) by the Company without Cause on at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' notice; (e) by the Executive for Good Reason; (f) by the Executive without Good Reason on at least 60 days' notice; or (g) automatically and immediately upon the loss, suspension, or revocation of the Executive's Clearance, regardless of fault.

5.2 Notice and Effect of Clearance Loss. If the Executive's Clearance is suspended pending further investigation, the Company may, in its discretion, place the Executive on unpaid administrative leave during the suspension period, with the understanding that if the Clearance is restored within ninety (90) days, the Executive shall be reinstated to the Executive's prior position with no loss of benefits accrued during the leave. If the Clearance is not restored within ninety (90) days, the Executive's employment shall terminate automatically under Section 5.1(g).

5.3 Severance for Termination Without Cause or for Good Reason. Subject to the Executive's execution and non-revocation of a release of claims, the Executive shall be entitled to: (a) twelve (12) months of Base Salary; (b) target Annual Bonus for the year of termination, pro-rated; and (c) twelve (12) months of COBRA continuation.

5.4 No Severance on Cause or Clearance Loss. If the Executive's employment terminates under Section 5.1(c) (Cause) or Section 5.1(g) (Clearance loss), the Executive shall not be entitled to severance.

5.5 Section 280G Best-Net Cutback. If any payment to the Executive would be subject to the Section 4999 excise tax, the aggregate payments shall be reduced to the extent necessary only if such reduction would result in a greater after-tax amount.

ARTICLE 6 — CONFIDENTIALITY, CUI, AND CLASSIFIED INFORMATION

6.1 Confidentiality of Company Information. The Executive shall hold in strict confidence all non-public information of the Company.

6.2 CUI and CDI Handling. The Executive shall handle all CUI and CDI in accordance with NIST Special Publication 800-171 and DFARS 252.204-7012, shall maintain the security controls required by those sources, and shall limit access to authorized U.S. persons.

6.3 ITAR and EAR Compliance. The Executive shall comply with the International Traffic in Arms Regulations (22 CFR Parts 120-130) and the Export Administration Regulations (15 CFR Parts 730-774). The Executive shall not export, re-export, or transfer any controlled technical data without the prior approval of the Company's Empowered Official and any required U.S. government authorizations.

6.4 Classified Information. The Executive shall handle all classified information in accordance with the Executive's Clearance, the NISPOM, and the Company's facility security plan.

6.5 Cyber Incident Reporting. The Executive shall promptly report to the Company's FSO any cyber incident, cyber intrusion, or unauthorized access to Company information systems, in accordance with DFARS 252.204-7012's 72-hour reporting requirement.

6.6 Defend Trade Secrets Act Notice. Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), the Executive is notified that the Executive cannot be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official or to an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law.

6.7 Post-Employment Debriefing. Upon any termination of employment, the Executive shall participate in a post-employment debriefing conducted by the Company's FSO, including return of all classified materials, completion of any required certifications, and acknowledgment of continuing classified information non-disclosure obligations under SEAD-4 and the Executive's existing non-disclosure agreements with the U.S. government.

ARTICLE 7 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

7.1 Non-Competition. During the Executive's employment and for a period of twelve (12) months after the date of termination of the Executive's employment for any reason, the Executive shall not, within the geographic area in which the Company performs federal contract work as of the date of termination, accept employment with or perform services for any business that competes directly with the Company on any specific federal contract or program on which the Executive worked or had material involvement during the twelve months preceding termination.

7.2 Non-Solicitation of Employees. During the Executive's employment and for twelve (12) months after termination, the Executive shall not directly solicit for employment any cleared employee of the Company with whom the Executive worked or had material contact, subject to a general-solicitation carve-out.

7.3 Non-Disparagement. The Executive shall not, during or after employment, make any public statement that disparages the Company or its officers, directors, or employees.

7.4 OFCCP Equal Opportunity. The Company is a federal contractor committed to equal employment opportunity and affirmative action under Executive Order 11246. The Company will not discriminate against the Executive on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, disability, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law.

ARTICLE 8 — REPRESENTATIONS

8.1 Clearance Representation. The Executive represents that the Executive (a) holds an active TS/SCI Clearance with all required compartments and accesses, (b) is not subject to any pending Statement of Reasons, clearance suspension, or other adverse clearance action, and (c) is not aware of any condition that would or could result in clearance suspension or revocation.

8.2 OFCCP and Federal Contractor Compliance. The Executive represents that the Executive has not been debarred or excluded from federal contracts.

ARTICLE 9 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Commonwealth of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. To the extent any provision conflicts with any federal statute, regulation, or executive order applicable to federal contractors or to the handling of classified information, federal law shall govern.

9.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

9.3 Equitable Relief. The Company may seek equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 6 or Article 7 of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 10 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

10.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

10.3 Assignment. The Executive may not assign this Agreement. The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor.

10.4 Survival. Articles 5, 6, 7, and 9 shall survive the termination of the Executive's employment.

10.5 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.
