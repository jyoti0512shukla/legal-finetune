EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (ISRAEL)

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, an {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} with its principal office at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Employee"). The Company and the Employee are collectively the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Company is an Israeli high-tech company engaged in the development of cybersecurity products;

WHEREAS, the Company desires to employ the Employee as its {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}} to lead the Company's research and development organization;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — POSITION AND DUTIES

1.1 Position. The Company employs the Employee as {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}, reporting to the {{EMPLOYEE_REPORTS_TO}}.

1.2 Duties. The Employee shall be responsible for the Company's research and development organization, including software architecture, security research, product development, and engineering management.

1.3 Manager Status. The Parties acknowledge that the Employee is a "manager" within the meaning of Section 30 of the Israeli Hours of Work and Rest Law, 5711-1951, due to the senior nature of the Employee's role and the requirement for the Employee to set the Employee's own work hours. Accordingly, the Employee is not entitled to overtime payment under that law, and the Employee's compensation under this Agreement is intended to fully compensate the Employee for any hours worked beyond a standard work week.

1.4 Place of Employment. The Employee's principal place of employment shall be the Company's offices in {{VENUE_CITY}}, Israel, with reasonable travel as required.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM

2.1 Term. The Employee's employment shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue indefinitely until terminated in accordance with Article 5. Israeli law does not recognize "at-will" employment as understood in the United States; accordingly, the Employee's employment may be terminated only as expressly provided in Article 5 below.

ARTICLE 3 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

3.1 Base Salary. The Company shall pay the Employee a monthly gross salary of {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}} (the "Base Salary"), payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices and in compliance with the Israeli Wage Protection Law, 5718-1958.

3.2 Annual Bonus. The Employee shall be eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of {{EMPLOYEE_TARGET_BONUS_PCT}}% of the Employee's annualized Base Salary, determined by the Board of Directors based on Company and individual performance.

3.3 Equity Grant. As soon as practicable after the Effective Date, the Company shall grant the Employee an equity award with a grant-date fair value of {{EMPLOYEE_INITIAL_EQUITY}}, in the form of options to purchase ordinary shares of the Company under the Company's Israeli Section 102 capital gains track equity incentive plan, vesting in equal annual installments over four (4) years subject to the Employee's continued employment.

3.4 Section 102 (Capital Gains Track). The Parties acknowledge that the equity grant is intended to qualify for capital gains tax treatment under Section 102(b)(2) of the Israeli Income Tax Ordinance, subject to a 24-month holding period from the date of the grant deposit with the Section 102 trustee. The Employee shall comply with all applicable holding period and trustee requirements.

3.5 Section 14 Severance Arrangement. Pursuant to Section 14 of the Israeli Severance Pay Law, 5723-1963 ("Section 14"), the Parties hereby elect to apply the Section 14 arrangement to the Employee's employment. Accordingly:

(a) The Company shall make monthly contributions equal to 8.33% of the Employee's monthly Base Salary to a managers insurance policy (or, at the Employee's election, a pension fund) (the "Severance Component"), and an additional 6.5% of the Employee's monthly Base Salary toward the savings or pension component of such policy or fund (the "Pension Component").

(b) The Employee shall contribute 6% of the Employee's monthly Base Salary toward the savings or pension component, deducted from the Employee's gross salary.

(c) The Section 14 contributions shall be made to the managers insurance policy or pension fund of the Employee's choice, in compliance with the General Order under Section 14.

(d) The Section 14 contributions shall be in lieu of the statutory severance payment that would otherwise be due to the Employee under Section 1 of the Severance Pay Law upon termination, and the Employee acknowledges that the Section 14 arrangement constitutes the Employee's full statutory severance entitlement.

(e) The Section 14 contributions shall be released to the Employee at the end of the Employee's employment regardless of the reason for termination, except in cases of termination for "cause" as defined in Section 16 or 17 of the Severance Pay Law.

3.6 Study Fund. The Company shall contribute 7.5% of the Employee's monthly Base Salary to a study fund (Keren Hishtalmut), with the Employee contributing an additional 2.5% deducted from the Employee's gross salary.

3.7 Annual Recreation Pay. The Employee shall be entitled to annual recreation pay (Dmei Havraah) in accordance with Israeli labor law, paid each year in accordance with the relevant general extension order.

3.8 Vacation. The Employee shall be entitled to twenty-two (22) working days of paid annual vacation, accrued in accordance with the Israeli Annual Leave Law, 5711-1951.

3.9 Sick Leave. The Employee shall be entitled to paid sick leave in accordance with the Israeli Sick Pay Law, 5736-1976.

3.10 Welfare and Other Benefits. The Employee shall be eligible to participate in any other employee benefit plans the Company makes available to its senior managers from time to time.

ARTICLE 4 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY (ISRAELI SERVICE INVENTIONS)

4.1 Service Inventions. The Employee acknowledges that any invention conceived or reduced to practice by the Employee in connection with the Employee's employment with the Company is a "service invention" within the meaning of Section 132 of the Israeli Patents Law, 5727-1967, and as such belongs to the Company without any obligation to pay separate consideration to the Employee. The Employee hereby waives any right to royalties or other compensation for any service invention under Sections 134-135 of the Israeli Patents Law.

4.2 Assignment of Other IP. To the extent any work product, invention, copyright, design, or other intellectual property developed by the Employee in the course of employment is not a service invention under Israeli law, the Employee hereby assigns all right, title, and interest in such intellectual property to the Company.

4.3 Pre-Existing IP. Any inventions developed by the Employee before the Effective Date or developed entirely outside the course of the Employee's employment, without using Company resources, are not assigned to the Company.

4.4 Moral Rights Waiver. To the maximum extent permitted by Israeli law, the Employee waives any moral rights in any work created in the course of the Employee's employment.

ARTICLE 5 — TERMINATION AND NOTICE

5.1 Termination Events. The Employee's employment may be terminated as follows:

(a) automatically upon the Employee's death or "Disability" (as defined under the Israeli National Insurance Law);

(b) by the Company for "cause" within the meaning of Sections 16-17 of the Israeli Severance Pay Law;

(c) by the Company without cause, with prior written notice as set forth in Section 5.2 (or pay in lieu of notice);

(d) by the Employee, with prior written notice as set forth in Section 5.2.

5.2 Notice Period. The Employee or the Company shall provide at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' prior written notice of termination of employment (or, in the case of termination by the Company without cause, the Company may pay the Employee in lieu of notice for any unworked portion of the notice period). During the notice period, the Employee shall remain employed and shall continue to receive Base Salary and benefits, but the Company may relieve the Employee of active duties.

5.3 Severance on Termination. Upon termination of employment for any reason other than termination for "cause" under Section 16 or 17 of the Severance Pay Law, the Section 14 Severance Component shall be released to the Employee in accordance with Section 3.5 above.

5.4 Approved Enterprise Acknowledgment. The Employee acknowledges that the Section 102 capital gains tax treatment of the Employee's equity awards may be affected by the timing of termination. The Company makes no representation regarding the tax consequences to the Employee.

ARTICLE 6 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

6.1 Non-Competition. During the Employee's employment and for a period of twelve (12) months after the date of termination of the Employee's employment for any reason, the Employee shall not, anywhere in Israel, the European Union, or the United States, accept employment with or perform services for any business that competes directly with the Company in cybersecurity research and product development. The Parties acknowledge that the enforceability of post-employment non-compete restrictions under Israeli law is limited and that this restriction shall be enforced only to the extent reasonable under the Israeli judicial doctrine of restrictive covenants and only to the extent necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests, including the Company's confidential information, trade secrets, and substantial investment in the Employee's training and access to source code.

6.2 Non-Solicitation of Employees. During the Employee's employment and for twelve (12) months after termination, the Employee shall not directly solicit for employment any employee of the Company with whom the Employee worked or had material contact during the twelve months preceding termination.

6.3 Confidentiality. The Employee shall hold in strict confidence all non-public information of the Company.

6.4 Israeli Privacy Protection Law. The Employee acknowledges that the Company processes the Employee's personal data in accordance with the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981, for purposes of administering the employment relationship. The Employee has the rights of access, correction, and deletion as provided by Israeli law.

ARTICLE 7 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

7.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Israel.

7.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Employee's employment with the Company shall be brought exclusively in the labor courts of {{VENUE_CITY}}, Israel.

7.3 Equitable Relief. The Company may seek equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 4 or Article 6 of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 8 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

8.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

8.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

8.3 Assignment. The Employee may not assign this Agreement. The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor.

8.4 Survival. Articles 4, 5, 6, and 7 shall survive the termination of the Employee's employment.

8.5 Counterparts and Language. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts. This Agreement is executed in the English language; any Hebrew translation prepared for convenience shall not control over the English version in case of conflict.
