1.1 Your role. You'll join us as {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}, reporting to your {{EMPLOYEE_REPORTS_TO}}. ARTICLE 2 — UNION REPRESENTATION (IMPORTANT)

2.1 Union covered. Your position is covered by the collective bargaining agreement between the Hospital and SEIU Healthcare 1199NW (the "CBA"). The CBA controls your wages, benefits, scheduling, seniority, layoff and recall rights, and grievance procedures, and the terms of the CBA prevail over any inconsistent terms in this offer letter. We've attached a copy of the current CBA to this offer letter for your reference.

2.2 Union dues. You may be required to pay union dues or an equivalent representation fee in accordance with the CBA and applicable law.

2.3 Your rights. You have rights under the National Labor Relations Act, including the right to engage in concerted activities for mutual aid or protection. Nothing in this offer letter limits or interferes with those rights.

ARTICLE 3 — TERM AND TERMINATION

3.1 Probationary period. Your first 90 days of employment are a probationary period under the CBA. After successful completion of the probationary period, you'll attain regular employee status with the rights provided by the CBA.

3.2 Termination. After your probationary period, your employment can only be terminated for "just cause" as defined in the CBA, with the grievance and arbitration procedures provided by the CBA.

3.3 Your notice. If you decide to leave, please give us at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' written notice as a courtesy.

ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION

4.1 Base hourly rate. Your starting hourly rate is set by the CBA based on your years of nursing experience. Your annualized base compensation will be approximately {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}}.

4.2 Shift differential. You'll receive shift differentials for evening, night, and weekend shifts as set by the CBA.

4.3 Overtime. As a non-exempt employee under the Fair Labor Standards Act, you're eligible for overtime pay at one and one-half times your regular hourly rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek (or as otherwise required by the CBA, which may provide more generous overtime terms).

4.4 Health insurance. You'll be eligible for the Hospital's comprehensive medical, dental, and vision plans on the first of the month after your start date, in accordance with the CBA's health benefit terms.

4.5 Retirement. You'll be eligible for the Hospital's 403(b) retirement plan on your start date, with employer matching contributions as provided in the plan.

4.6 Paid time off. You'll accrue paid time off in accordance with the CBA. Initial Initiative 1433 paid sick leave under Washington State law accrues from your first hour worked at a rate of one hour for every 40 hours worked.

4.7 Continuing education. The Hospital provides annual continuing education funding to support your nursing license renewal and professional development, as set forth in the CBA.

ARTICLE 5 — LICENSE AND CERTIFICATIONS

5.1 Active nursing license. Your employment is contingent on your maintaining an active, unrestricted Registered Nurse license issued by the Washington State Nursing Care Quality Assurance Commission. Loss, suspension, or revocation of your license, or any disciplinary action by the licensing board, must be reported to the Hospital's HR department immediately and may result in suspension or termination of your employment.

5.2 BLS, ACLS, and other certifications. You will need to maintain current Basic Life Support (BLS) and Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS) certifications, plus any unit-specific certifications required for your assignment. The Hospital will provide reasonable time off and reimbursement for these certifications.

ARTICLE 6 — CONFIDENTIALITY AND PATIENT INFORMATION

6.1 HIPAA compliance. As a healthcare provider, you'll have access to protected health information ("PHI") covered by HIPAA. You agree to comply with all HIPAA Privacy and Security Rule requirements, the Hospital's privacy policies, and the Hospital's information security policies, both during and after your employment. You'll complete the Hospital's HIPAA training program during orientation and annually thereafter.

6.2 Joint Commission patient safety. The Hospital is accredited by The Joint Commission. You'll comply with all Joint Commission patient safety standards and report any patient safety event in accordance with our incident reporting policy.

6.3 Confidential information. Beyond patient information, you'll keep confidential any non-public business or financial information of the Hospital that you obtain in the course of your employment.

ARTICLE 7 — CONDITIONS

7.1 Background check. Your offer is contingent on satisfactory completion of a background check, including verification of your nursing license and prior employment.

7.2 Drug screen. You'll need to complete a pre-employment drug screen.

7.3 Health screen and immunizations. You'll need to complete a pre-employment health screen and be current on all required immunizations (including TB testing, MMR, varicella, hepatitis B, and annual influenza vaccination), in accordance with Joint Commission and Washington State Department of Health requirements.

7.4 I-9 verification. You'll complete Form I-9 within three business days of your start date.

ARTICLE 8 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1 Grievance procedure. Workplace disputes are handled through the grievance and arbitration procedures of the CBA.

8.2 Governing law. Washington State law governs this offer letter, except where preempted by federal labor law applicable to the CBA.
