EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT — CHIEF MEDICAL OFFICER

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Hospital System"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing in the State of {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} (the "CMO"). The Hospital System and the CMO are collectively the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Hospital System is a regional non-profit health system operating multiple acute-care hospitals and ambulatory facilities across the Pacific Northwest;

WHEREAS, the Hospital System desires to employ the CMO to oversee the medical staff, lead clinical quality and patient safety initiatives, ensure compliance with healthcare regulatory requirements, and serve as the medical liaison to the Board of Directors;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Cause" means the CMO's: (a) loss, suspension, restriction, or revocation of the CMO's medical license or board certification; (b) exclusion or debarment from any Medicare, Medicaid, or other federal health care program; (c) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, any felony or any healthcare-related offense; (d) material breach of HIPAA, the Stark Law, the Anti-Kickback Statute, or EMTALA; (e) loss of medical staff privileges at any Hospital System facility for cause; (f) willful misconduct or gross negligence in the performance of the CMO's duties; or (g) material breach of this Agreement after written notice and a 30-day cure opportunity.

1.2 "Code" means the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.

1.3 "Good Reason" means a material adverse change in the CMO's title or duties, a material reduction in Base Salary, or a material relocation of the CMO's principal place of employment more than 50 miles from {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

1.4 "Medical Staff Bylaws" means the Hospital System's medical staff bylaws as in effect from time to time, governing the granting and termination of medical staff privileges at the Hospital System's facilities.

ARTICLE 2 — POSITION AND DUTIES

2.1 Position. The Hospital System shall employ the CMO as {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}, reporting to the {{EMPLOYEE_REPORTS_TO}}.

2.2 Dual Reporting to Medical Executive Committee. The CMO shall also serve as a member of and report to the Hospital System's Medical Executive Committee on matters relating to medical staff governance and clinical quality.

2.3 Duties. The CMO shall be responsible for: (a) overseeing clinical quality and patient safety across all Hospital System facilities; (b) leading the Hospital System's medical staff and credentialing functions; (c) serving as the medical liaison to the Board of Directors and the Quality Committee of the Board; (d) overseeing the Hospital System's compliance with HIPAA, Stark Law, Anti-Kickback Statute, EMTALA, and other healthcare regulatory requirements; (e) representing the Hospital System in interactions with the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, the Joint Commission, and state health departments; and (f) such other duties customarily performed by a Chief Medical Officer of a regional health system.

2.4 License and Board Certification Required. The CMO's continued employment is contingent on the CMO maintaining: (a) an active, unrestricted medical license issued by the {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}} Medical Board; (b) board certification in the CMO's specialty by an American Board of Medical Specialties member board; (c) Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) registration; and (d) eligibility to participate in Medicare, Medicaid, and other federal healthcare programs (i.e., not on the OIG List of Excluded Individuals/Entities).

2.5 Place of Employment. The CMO's principal place of employment shall be the Hospital System's administrative offices in {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

ARTICLE 3 — TERM

3.1 Term. The CMO's employment shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated in accordance with Article 5.

ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

4.1 Base Salary. The Hospital System shall pay the CMO an annual base salary of {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}}, reviewed annually by the Hospital System's Compensation Committee.

4.2 Annual Bonus. The CMO shall be eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of {{EMPLOYEE_TARGET_BONUS_PCT}}% of Base Salary, based on Hospital System and individual performance against objectives that include clinical quality metrics, patient safety metrics, and regulatory compliance.

4.3 IRC § 4958 Compliance. The Hospital System is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization. The CMO acknowledges that the CMO is a "disqualified person" within the meaning of Section 4958 of the Code, and that the CMO's compensation must satisfy the rebuttable presumption of reasonableness procedures of Section 4958, including independent review of comparable compensation data and contemporaneous documentation by the Compensation Committee.

4.4 Welfare and Retirement Benefits. The CMO shall participate in the Hospital System's standard executive benefit plans, including medical, dental, vision, life, disability, and the Hospital System's 403(b) retirement plan, on terms no less favorable than those generally applicable to other senior executives.

4.5 Vacation. The CMO shall be entitled to five (5) weeks of paid vacation per calendar year.

4.6 Continuing Medical Education. The Hospital System shall reimburse the CMO for reasonable continuing medical education expenses, license renewal fees, board certification maintenance, and professional society dues, up to Twenty-Five Thousand United States Dollars (US$25,000) per calendar year.

4.7 Professional Liability Insurance. The Hospital System shall provide the CMO with professional liability (medical malpractice) insurance covering the CMO's services for the Hospital System, with limits no less than the limits provided to other senior physicians of the Hospital System.

4.8 Tail Coverage. Upon termination of the CMO's employment for any reason, the Hospital System shall purchase tail coverage extending the CMO's professional liability insurance for any incidents occurring during the CMO's employment, in perpetuity. This obligation survives termination.

4.9 Indemnification. The Hospital System shall indemnify the CMO to the fullest extent permitted by law and shall maintain D&O liability insurance.

ARTICLE 5 — TERMINATION

5.1 Termination Events. The CMO's employment may be terminated as follows: (a) automatically upon death; (b) by the Hospital System on the CMO's Disability; (c) by the Hospital System for Cause; (d) by the Hospital System without Cause on at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' notice; (e) by the CMO for Good Reason; (f) by the CMO without Good Reason on at least 90 days' notice; or (g) automatically and immediately upon the CMO's loss of medical license, board certification, DEA registration, or eligibility to participate in federal healthcare programs.

5.2 Severance for Termination Without Cause or for Good Reason. Subject to the CMO's execution and non-revocation of a release of claims, the CMO shall be entitled to: (a) eighteen (18) months of Base Salary; (b) target Annual Bonus for the year of termination, pro-rated; (c) eighteen (18) months of COBRA continuation; and (d) tail coverage extending the CMO's professional liability insurance.

5.3 No Severance on Cause or License Loss. If the CMO's employment terminates under Section 5.1(c) or Section 5.1(g), the CMO shall not be entitled to severance, but shall remain entitled to tail coverage as set forth in Section 4.8.

5.4 Section 280G. If any payment to the CMO would be subject to the Section 4999 excise tax, the aggregate payments shall be reduced to the extent necessary only if such reduction would result in a greater after-tax amount.

5.5 Section 409A. This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Code.

ARTICLE 6 — STARK LAW AND ANTI-KICKBACK STATUTE COMPLIANCE

6.1 Compliance Acknowledgment. The CMO acknowledges that the Hospital System is subject to the Physician Self-Referral Law (the "Stark Law," 42 U.S.C. § 1395nn) and the Anti-Kickback Statute (42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7b), and that the CMO's compensation under this Agreement is intended to comply with the bona fide employment relationship exception to the Stark Law and the bona fide employment safe harbor of the Anti-Kickback Statute.

6.2 Compensation Not Tied to Referrals. The CMO's compensation under this Agreement is set at fair market value, is commercially reasonable, and is not determined in any manner that takes into account, directly or indirectly, the volume or value of any referrals or business otherwise generated between the CMO and the Hospital System.

6.3 EMTALA Cooperation. The CMO shall cooperate with the Hospital System in ensuring compliance with the Emergency Medical Treatment and Active Labor Act and shall not direct any patient inappropriately.

ARTICLE 7 — CONFIDENTIALITY AND PROTECTED HEALTH INFORMATION

7.1 HIPAA Compliance. The CMO shall comply with HIPAA and the Hospital System's HIPAA policies, including the HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules and the Hospital System's breach notification protocols.

7.2 Confidential Information. The CMO shall hold in strict confidence all non-public information of the Hospital System (other than PHI, which is governed by HIPAA), including business plans, financial information, strategic initiatives, and personnel matters.

7.3 Defend Trade Secrets Act Notice. Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), the CMO is notified that the CMO cannot be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official or to an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law.

ARTICLE 8 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

8.1 Non-Competition. During the CMO's employment and for a period of twelve (12) months after the date of termination of the CMO's employment for any reason, the CMO shall not, within a fifty (50)-mile radius of any Hospital System facility, accept employment or any administrative role with any other hospital system, ambulatory care provider, or health system that competes directly with the Hospital System for patients in the same service area. This restriction shall not prohibit the CMO from clinical practice as a physician within the restricted area, since such clinical practice is consistent with public policy concerns about access to care.

8.2 Non-Solicitation of Physicians. During the CMO's employment and for twelve (12) months after termination, the CMO shall not directly solicit any physician currently on the Hospital System's medical staff with whom the CMO worked or had material contact during the twelve months preceding termination, for the purpose of recruiting such physician to a competing health system.

8.3 Non-Disparagement. The CMO shall not, during or after employment, make any public statement that disparages the Hospital System or its officers, directors, medical staff, or employees.

ARTICLE 9 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

9.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

9.3 Equitable Relief. The Hospital System may seek equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 7 or Article 8 of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 10 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with the Hospital System's Medical Staff Bylaws, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

10.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

10.3 Assignment. The CMO may not assign this Agreement. The Hospital System may assign this Agreement to any successor.

10.4 Survival. Articles 4.8 (tail coverage), 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9 shall survive the termination of the CMO's employment.

10.5 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.
