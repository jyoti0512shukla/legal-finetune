HEAD COACH EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This HEAD COACH EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Club"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual (the "Coach"). The Club and the Coach are collectively the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Club is a professional sports franchise competing in the league (the "League") and is bound by the League's Constitution, the League collective bargaining agreement (the "League CBA"), and the League personal conduct policy;

WHEREAS, the Club desires to engage the Coach as its {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}} for a fixed term, and the Coach desires to accept such engagement on the terms set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Cause" means the Coach's: (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, any felony or any crime involving moral turpitude; (b) suspension of more than thirty (30) days or permanent ineligibility imposed by the League under the League personal conduct policy; (c) material breach of the Club's code of conduct or the League CBA; (d) material breach of this Agreement after written notice and a 30-day cure opportunity; or (e) the Coach's gross neglect of duties or willful misconduct.

1.2 "Club Buyout Payment" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.4.

1.3 "Coach Buyout Payment" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.5.

1.4 "League Conduct Policy" means the personal conduct policy adopted by the League and applicable to all coaches, players, and Club personnel.

1.5 "NIL Rights" means the Coach's name, image, likeness, signature, voice, photograph, biographical material, and personal endorsements.

ARTICLE 2 — POSITION AND DUTIES

2.1 Position. The Club shall employ the Coach as {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}, reporting to the {{EMPLOYEE_REPORTS_TO}}.

2.2 Duties. The Coach shall be responsible for the Club's playing strategy, player selection (in consultation with the Sporting Director), training program, match-day decision-making, player development, and team discipline. The Coach shall represent the Club in League and media activities as reasonably requested by the Club.

2.3 Full-Time Engagement. This is a full-time engagement. The Coach shall devote the Coach's full professional time and attention to the Club throughout the Term, except as otherwise expressly permitted in this Agreement.

2.4 League CBA Compliance. The Coach acknowledges that this Agreement is subject to the League CBA, the League Constitution, and the League Conduct Policy, each of which is incorporated by reference. To the extent any provision of this Agreement conflicts with the League CBA or the League Constitution, the latter prevails with respect to coaching conduct, player welfare, and competitive integrity matters.

2.5 Place of Engagement. The Coach's principal place of engagement shall be the Club's training facility and home stadium in {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, plus all League away matches.

ARTICLE 3 — TERM

3.1 Fixed Term. The Coach's engagement under this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a fixed term of {{TERM_YEARS}} years from the Effective Date (the "Term"), unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 5.

3.2 Renewal. Any extension or renewal of this Agreement shall be by mutual written agreement.

ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

4.1 Base Compensation. The Club shall pay the Coach an annual base compensation of {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}}, payable in equal monthly installments over the season.

4.2 Performance Bonuses. The Coach shall be eligible to earn additional performance bonuses up to {{EMPLOYEE_TARGET_BONUS_PCT}}% of Base Compensation, based on Club performance against agreed-upon objectives, including (a) playoff qualification, (b) playoff round advancement, (c) championship victory, and (d) individual coach awards from the League. The specific bonus schedule is set forth in Schedule A.

4.3 Welfare and Other Benefits. The Coach shall participate in the Club's standard executive benefit plans on terms no less favorable than those generally applicable to other senior Club personnel.

4.4 Team Travel and Accommodations. The Club shall provide the Coach with first-class travel and accommodations for all League away matches and Club promotional events.

ARTICLE 5 — TERMINATION

5.1 Termination Events. The Coach's engagement may be terminated as follows: (a) automatically upon the Coach's death; (b) by the Club on the Coach's Disability; (c) by the Club for Cause; (d) by the Club without Cause (with the Club Buyout Payment as set forth in Section 5.4); (e) by the Coach for Good Reason; (f) by the Coach without Good Reason (with the Coach Buyout Payment as set forth in Section 5.5); or (g) automatically upon the League's permanent ban or ineligibility decision against the Coach.

5.2 Notice of Termination. Any termination under Section 5.1(c)-(f) shall require at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' prior written notice (or pay in lieu of notice for the unworked period).

5.3 Accrued Obligations. Upon any termination, the Club shall pay the Coach all accrued and unpaid Base Compensation through the date of termination, plus any earned but unpaid performance bonuses for fully completed seasons.

5.4 Club Buyout Payment. If the Club terminates the Coach's engagement without Cause, the Club shall pay the Coach a buyout payment equal to seventy-five percent (75%) of the remaining Base Compensation owed under the Term, payable in equal monthly installments over the remainder of the Term, subject to the Coach's mitigation obligation under Section 5.6.

5.5 Coach Buyout Payment. If the Coach terminates the Coach's engagement without Good Reason during the Term in order to accept a coaching position with another professional sports club, the Coach (or the Coach's new employer) shall pay the Club a buyout payment equal to fifty percent (50%) of the remaining Base Compensation that would have been owed to the Coach under the Term.

5.6 Mitigation. The Coach shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain alternative comparable coaching employment during the period in which the Club Buyout Payment is being paid. Any earnings from such alternative employment shall offset the Club Buyout Payment dollar-for-dollar to the extent the alternative earnings, when combined with the Club Buyout Payment, exceed the Coach's Base Compensation under this Agreement.

5.7 Section 409A. This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code.

ARTICLE 6 — NAME, IMAGE, AND LIKENESS RIGHTS

6.1 Club License of NIL Rights. During the Term, the Coach grants the Club a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use the Coach's NIL Rights in connection with: (a) Club marketing, advertising, and promotional materials; (b) ticket sales and broadcasting rights; (c) Club merchandise and licensed products; (d) Club social media; and (e) Club fundraising and community-engagement activities. This license is limited to the Club's general promotional purposes and does not extend to any third-party endorsement or sponsorship without the Coach's prior written consent.

6.2 Coach-Retained NIL Rights. The Coach retains all NIL Rights for: (a) personal endorsements that have been disclosed to and approved by the Club, (b) media appearances outside the Club's media schedule (subject to the League CBA's media obligations), (c) the Coach's autobiography or biographical works, (d) the Coach's coaching education or speaking engagements (subject to scheduling that does not conflict with Club obligations), and (e) the Coach's personal social media presence. The Coach may continue to monetize personal endorsement deals during the Term, subject to the Club's right of first refusal on any endorsement deal involving a category that competes with the Club's official sponsors.

6.3 No Competing Endorsements. The Coach shall not enter into any endorsement or sponsorship agreement that conflicts with the Club's official sponsors, as identified by the Club in writing from time to time. The Club shall provide the Coach with at least 14 days' notice of any new conflicting category.

6.4 Post-Term NIL Rights. After the Term ends (whether by expiration or earlier termination), all NIL Rights shall revert in full to the Coach. The Club may continue to use existing Club-produced photographs, videos, and broadcast materials referencing the Coach in historical contexts but shall not, without the Coach's written consent, use the Coach's NIL Rights in current marketing or promotional activities.

ARTICLE 7 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

7.1 Confidentiality. The Coach shall hold in strict confidence all non-public information of the Club, including playing strategy, scouting reports, player evaluations, salary cap information, draft strategy, and Club business and financial information.

7.2 Non-Compete (Coaching Other Clubs). During the Term, the Coach shall not accept any coaching, management, or consulting role with any other professional sports club, except as expressly permitted by the Club in writing.

7.3 Non-Disparagement. Both the Coach and the Club shall refrain from publicly disparaging the other Party, the Club's officers, directors, or players, or the League, both during and after the Term, except for truthful statements made in legal proceedings, regulatory inquiries, or as permitted by the League CBA.

7.4 League Conduct Policy. The Coach shall comply with the League Conduct Policy at all times. Any violation may result in League discipline (including suspension or fine) and may also constitute Cause for termination by the Club under Section 1.1.

ARTICLE 8 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, except that disputes specifically governed by the League CBA shall be resolved in accordance with the League CBA's grievance procedures.

8.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute not subject to the League CBA grievance procedure shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

8.3 Right of Publicity. Each Party reserves all rights to enforce its name, image, likeness, and right of publicity under applicable state common law and the Lanham Act § 43(a).

ARTICLE 9 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

9.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with Schedule A and the League CBA, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

9.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

9.3 Assignment. The Coach may not assign this Agreement. The Club may assign this Agreement to any successor that assumes the obligations hereunder, including in connection with a sale of the Club.

9.4 Survival. Articles 5, 6, 7, and 8 shall survive the termination of the Coach's engagement.

9.5 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.
