EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing in the State of {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} (the "Executive"). The Company and the Executive are collectively the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Company is a privately held, late-stage logistics software company that anticipates an initial public offering of its common stock within the next twenty-four (24) months;

WHEREAS, the Company desires to employ the Executive as its {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}} to lead the Company's product strategy through the IPO and post-IPO period;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Cause" means the Executive's: (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony or any crime involving fraud, embezzlement, or moral turpitude; (b) willful misappropriation of Company funds; (c) willful and material breach of this Agreement after written notice and a 30-day cure opportunity; or (d) willful misconduct that causes material harm to the Company.

1.2 "Change in Control" means: (a) the acquisition by any Person of more than 50% of the combined voting power of the Company's then-outstanding voting securities (excluding the IPO itself or any other primary or secondary offering); (b) a merger or consolidation in which the Company's stockholders immediately before do not hold a majority of the surviving entity; or (c) the sale of all or substantially all of the Company's assets.

1.3 "Good Reason" means a material adverse change in the Executive's title or duties, a material reduction in Base Salary or target Annual Bonus opportunity, or a material relocation of the Executive's principal place of employment more than 50 miles, in each case subject to a 60-day notice and 30-day cure mechanism.

1.4 "IPO" means the consummation of the Company's initial public offering of common stock pursuant to a registration statement on Form S-1 (or successor form) filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission.

1.5 "Lock-Up Period" means the standard underwriter lock-up period applicable to officers and directors in connection with the IPO, customarily 180 days from the IPO pricing date, plus any customary "booster" extensions.

ARTICLE 2 — POSITION AND DUTIES

2.1 Position. The Company shall employ the Executive as {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}, reporting to the {{EMPLOYEE_REPORTS_TO}}.

2.2 Duties. The Executive shall be responsible for the Company's product strategy, product management organization, product design and user research, and product analytics functions, plus such other duties as the CEO may reasonably assign.

2.3 Place of Employment. The Executive's principal place of employment shall be the Company's offices in {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

ARTICLE 3 — TERM

3.1 At-Will Employment. The Executive's employment shall be at-will, commencing on the Effective Date and continuing until terminated in accordance with Article 5.

ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

4.1 Base Salary. The Company shall pay the Executive an annual base salary of {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}}, reviewed annually by the Compensation Committee.

4.2 Annual Bonus. The Executive shall be eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of {{EMPLOYEE_TARGET_BONUS_PCT}}% of Base Salary, determined by the Compensation Committee in its discretion based on Company and individual performance.

4.3 Initial Equity Grant with IPO Milestone Vesting. The Company shall grant the Executive an initial equity award with a grant-date fair value of {{EMPLOYEE_INITIAL_EQUITY}} (the "Initial Equity Grant"), structured as follows:

(a) Time-Vesting Portion (75% of the Initial Equity Grant). 75% of the Initial Equity Grant shall vest over four (4) years from the Effective Date, with a one-year cliff (25% vests on the first anniversary of the Effective Date) and quarterly vesting thereafter.

(b) IPO Milestone Vesting Portion (25% of the Initial Equity Grant). The remaining 25% of the Initial Equity Grant shall vest in full upon the consummation of the IPO, subject to the Executive's continued employment through the IPO date. If the IPO is not consummated within thirty-six (36) months after the Effective Date, this 25% portion shall convert to time-vesting on the same schedule as the Time-Vesting Portion, with vesting beginning on the 36-month anniversary of the Effective Date.

4.4 Equity Plan and 83(b) Election. All equity awards shall be granted under the Company's then-effective equity incentive plan and shall be subject to the terms of the applicable award agreement. The Executive acknowledges the right (but not the obligation) to file an election under Section 83(b) of the Internal Revenue Code with respect to any restricted stock granted, and that the Executive bears sole responsibility for filing any such election within the 30-day statutory window. The Company does not provide tax advice.

4.5 Lock-Up Acknowledgment. The Executive acknowledges and agrees that, in connection with the IPO, the Executive will be required to enter into a customary lock-up agreement with the IPO underwriters, prohibiting the Executive from selling or otherwise disposing of any Company equity during the Lock-Up Period (typically 180 days from the IPO pricing date), and from selling at certain times during any "booster" extension. The Executive shall execute the underwriter lock-up agreement promptly upon request.

4.6 Secondary Sale Restrictions. Prior to the IPO, the Executive shall not sell, transfer, or pledge any Company equity except (a) in a Company-organized tender offer, (b) with the prior written consent of the Compensation Committee, or (c) as required by court order. Following the IPO and the expiration of the Lock-Up Period, the Executive's transactions in Company equity shall comply with the Company's insider trading policy and all applicable securities laws.

4.7 Welfare and Retirement Benefits. The Executive shall participate in the Company's standard executive benefit plans on terms no less favorable than those generally applicable to other senior executives.

4.8 Vacation. The Executive shall be entitled to four (4) weeks of paid vacation per calendar year.

4.9 Indemnification and D&O Insurance. The Company shall indemnify the Executive to the fullest extent permitted by Delaware law and shall maintain D&O liability insurance coverage on terms no less favorable than the insurance covering any other senior executive.

ARTICLE 5 — TERMINATION AND SEVERANCE

5.1 Termination Events. The Executive's employment may be terminated as follows: (a) automatically upon death; (b) by the Company on the Executive's Disability; (c) by the Company for Cause; (d) by the Company without Cause; (e) by the Executive for Good Reason; or (f) by the Executive without Good Reason on at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' notice.

5.2 Accrued Obligations. Upon any termination, the Company shall pay the Accrued Obligations.

5.3 Severance for Termination Without Cause or for Good Reason. Subject to the Executive's execution and non-revocation of a release of claims, the Executive shall be entitled to: (a) twelve (12) months of Base Salary, paid as a lump sum; (b) the target Annual Bonus for the fiscal year of termination, pro-rated for the portion of the year worked; (c) twelve (12) months of COBRA continuation; and (d) twelve (12) months of accelerated vesting of the time-vesting portion of all unvested equity awards.

5.4 Enhanced Severance on CIC Double-Trigger. If the Executive's employment is terminated by the Company without Cause or by the Executive for Good Reason within twenty-four (24) months after a Change in Control, the Executive shall be entitled to: (a) eighteen (18) months of Base Salary; (b) 1.5x target Annual Bonus; (c) eighteen (18) months of COBRA; and (d) full acceleration of all outstanding equity awards (including the IPO milestone vesting portion if not yet vested at the time).

5.5 Section 280G Best-Net Cutback. If any payment to the Executive would be subject to the Section 4999 excise tax, the aggregate payments shall be reduced to the extent necessary only if such reduction would result in a greater after-tax amount.

5.6 Section 409A. This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Code.

5.7 Anti-Dilution Ratchet. If the Company completes an equity financing prior to the IPO at a price per share lower than the price per share used to determine the value of the Initial Equity Grant, the Company shall grant the Executive an additional equity award sufficient to preserve the Executive's effective grant-date economic value, on the same vesting terms as the Initial Equity Grant. This anti-dilution adjustment is subject to Compensation Committee approval and applicable equity plan limits.

ARTICLE 6 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

6.1 California Section 16600 Acknowledgment. The Parties acknowledge that California Business and Professions Code Section 16600 generally voids contracts that restrain a person from engaging in a lawful profession, trade, or business of any kind. Accordingly, this Agreement does not contain a post-employment non-competition restriction. The covenants in this Article 6 are limited to the protection of the Company's confidential information and trade secrets and to the prevention of solicitation activities expressly permitted under California law.

6.2 Confidentiality. The Executive shall hold in strict confidence all non-public information of the Company.

6.3 Non-Solicitation of Employees. During the Executive's employment and for twelve (12) months after termination, the Executive shall not directly solicit for employment any senior employee of the Company with whom the Executive worked or had material contact during the twelve months preceding termination, subject to a general-solicitation carve-out.

6.4 Non-Disparagement. The Executive shall not, during or after employment, make any public statement that disparages the Company or its officers, directors, or employees.

ARTICLE 7 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND IP

7.1 IP Assignment. The Executive assigns to the Company all inventions and works of authorship created during employment that relate to the Company's business or that are developed using Company resources.

7.2 California Section 2870 Notice. Notwithstanding Section 7.1, this assignment does not apply to any invention that the Executive developed entirely on the Executive's own time without using Company equipment, supplies, facilities, or trade secret information, except for those inventions that either (a) relate at the time of conception or reduction to practice to the Company's business, or to the Company's actual or demonstrably anticipated research or development, or (b) result from any work performed by the Executive for the Company.

7.3 DTSA Whistleblower Notice. Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), the Executive is notified that the Executive cannot be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official or to an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law.

7.4 Return of Company Property. Upon termination for any reason, the Executive shall return all Company property and confidential information.

ARTICLE 8 — CHANGE IN CONTROL

8.1 Definition. "Change in Control" has the meaning set forth in Section 1.2.

8.2 Equity Acceleration. Upon a Change in Control, the time-vesting portion of the Initial Equity Grant and any subsequent equity awards shall be subject to the double-trigger acceleration set forth in Section 5.4. The IPO milestone vesting portion shall be deemed earned in full upon any Change in Control, regardless of the IPO status.

ARTICLE 9 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

9.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

9.3 Equitable Relief. The Company may seek equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 6 or Article 7 of this Agreement.

9.4 Waiver of Jury Trial. EACH PARTY HEREBY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY.

ARTICLE 10 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

10.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

10.3 Assignment. The Executive may not assign this Agreement. The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor.

10.4 Survival. Articles 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9 shall survive the termination of the Executive's employment.

10.5 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
