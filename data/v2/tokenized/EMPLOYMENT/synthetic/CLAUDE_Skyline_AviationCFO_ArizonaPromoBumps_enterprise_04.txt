EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Employment Agreement") is made and entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing in the State of {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} (the "Executive"). The Company and the Executive are referred to collectively herein as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party."

RECITALS

The Company and the Executive have agreed to enter into this Employment Agreement, which shall supersede any prior agreement or understanding between the Parties with respect to the Executive's employment by the Company.

ARTICLE 1 — EMPLOYMENT AND DUTIES

1.1 Employment. In consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt, adequacy, and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Company agrees to hire the Executive, and the Executive agrees to accept and remain in the employ of the Company, upon the terms set forth in this Employment Agreement.

1.2 Position and Responsibilities.

(a) The Executive shall serve as the {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}} of the Company. The Executive agrees to perform such services, not inconsistent with the Executive's position, as are from time to time assigned to the Executive by the {{EMPLOYEE_REPORTS_TO}}.

(b) During the period of the Executive's employment under this Employment Agreement, the Executive shall devote substantially all of the Executive's business time, attention, skill, and efforts to the faithful performance of the Executive's duties hereunder. The Executive shall have the right to engage in personal business and to participate in charitable and civic activities, during normal business hours and otherwise, so long as such business and activities do not unreasonably interfere with the Executive's duties to the Company.

1.3 Term. The Company may terminate the Executive's employment at any time, subject to providing the Executive with written notice (in accordance with Section 12.4) of not less than {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days. The Executive's employment shall be at-will under the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}} and may be terminated by either Party at any time, for any reason or no reason, subject only to the obligations set forth in this Employment Agreement.

1.4 Location.

(a) During the period of the Executive's employment, the Executive shall not be required, except with the Executive's prior written consent (which may be withheld in the Executive's discretion), to relocate the Executive's principal place of employment outside of the {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}} metropolitan area.

(b) In connection with the Executive's initial relocation to the {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}} metropolitan area (if applicable), the Executive shall be reimbursed for reasonable and customary relocation expenses, subject to receipt by the Company of reasonably detailed supporting receipts and related documentation. In the event the Executive resigns or is terminated for Cause at any time during the twelve (12)-month period following the Effective Date, the Executive shall repay to the Company the full amount of all relocation expenses received by the Executive no later than ninety (90) days following the effective date of such resignation or termination for Cause.

1.5 Required Travel. Required travel on the Company's business shall not be deemed a relocation so long as the Executive is not required to provide services under this Employment Agreement outside of {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, for more than fifty percent (50%) of the Executive's working days during any consecutive six-month period.

ARTICLE 2 — COMPENSATION

For all services rendered by the Executive in any capacity during the Executive's employment under this Employment Agreement, including without limitation services as a director or officer of the Company or any of its subsidiaries, the Company shall compensate the Executive as set forth in this Article 2.

2.1 Base Salary. The Company shall pay the Executive an annual base salary of {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}}, less all applicable taxes and withholdings (the "Base Salary"). The Base Salary shall be paid every other week in equal installments. The Base Salary shall be reviewed annually by the Compensation Committee of the Board of Directors (the "Compensation Committee"), which may, in its discretion, increase the Base Salary. The Company may reduce the Base Salary only under circumstances in which the Company has imposed reductions in salary on other officers on an across-the-board basis, but any such reduction may not be at a greater percentage than the reduction imposed on any other officer (an "Across the Board Reduction").

2.2 Promotion Auto-Bumps. The Base Salary shall be increased to Three Hundred Twenty-Five Thousand United States Dollars (US$325,000) upon the earlier to occur of: (a) the Executive's promotion to Chief Financial Officer of the Company; or (b) the Executive's eighteen (18)-month anniversary of the Effective Date, in each case provided the Executive is then an employee in good standing.

2.3 Annual Bonus. During the period of the Executive's employment, the Executive shall be entitled to participate in the Company's Annual Discretionary Bonus Program, with an annual target bonus opportunity of {{EMPLOYEE_TARGET_BONUS_PCT}}% of the Executive's then-current Base Salary. The annual bonus shall be based on both individual and Company performance. At the discretion of the Chief Executive Officer or Board of Directors, the Company may pay bonuses to the Executive in excess of the annual target bonus amount. The annual target bonus opportunity shall be increased to seventy-five percent (75%) upon the earlier to occur of: (a) the Executive's promotion to Chief Financial Officer; or (b) the Executive's eighteen (18)-month anniversary of the Effective Date, provided the Executive is then an employee in good standing.

2.4 Equity Participation. During the Executive's employment, the Executive shall receive an annual equity award (consisting of restricted stock units, restricted stock, or similar equity instruments) pursuant to the terms of the Company's then-existing equity incentive plan, as determined by the Compensation Committee in its sole discretion. The annual equity grant during the Term of this Employment Agreement shall have a grant-date fair value of not less than {{EMPLOYEE_INITIAL_EQUITY}}, with the first grant occurring on the first anniversary of the Effective Date. This annual equity award amount shall be increased to One Hundred Fifty Thousand United States Dollars (US$150,000) upon the earlier to occur of: (a) the Executive's promotion to Chief Financial Officer; or (b) the Executive's eighteen (18)-month anniversary of the Effective Date, provided the Executive is then an employee in good standing. The agreements governing such equity grants shall provide for vesting over a three-year period.

2.5 Initial Equity Grant. Upon the Effective Date, the Executive shall be granted a one-time restricted stock unit grant of thirty-six thousand (36,000) shares of the Company's common stock (the "Initial RSU Grant"). The agreement governing the Initial RSU Grant shall provide for ratable annual vesting over a five-year period, subject to the Executive's continued employment through each vesting date.

2.6 Welfare and Retirement Benefits. The Executive shall be entitled to participate in all employee benefit and welfare programs, plans, and arrangements (including without limitation pension, profit-sharing, supplemental pension, and other retirement plans, insurance, hospitalization, medical, and group disability benefits, and travel or accident insurance plans) and to receive fringe benefits, in each case to the extent that such programs and benefits are from time to time available to the Company's executive personnel.

2.7 Vacation. The Executive shall be entitled to four (4) weeks of paid vacation per calendar year, plus paid sick time and Company-recognized holidays, all subject to the terms of the Company's standard vacation policy.

2.8 Business Expenses. The Company shall reimburse the Executive for all reasonable and necessary business expenses incurred in performing the Executive's duties, in accordance with the Company's standard expense reimbursement policy.

2.9 Airline Pass Benefits. As an industry-specific perquisite, the Company shall use commercially reasonable efforts to provide for the benefit of the Executive and the Executive's immediate family (the Executive's spouse or domestic partner, the Executive's children, and the spouse and children of any of the Executive's children) the right to fly on a complimentary basis on the Company's aircraft during the Term, subject to availability and the Company's standard travel policies for executives. The Company shall also use commercially reasonable efforts to negotiate, in good faith with other carriers, reciprocal complimentary travel privileges on the aircraft of such other carriers.

2.10 Section 280G Gross-Up. If any payment received by the Executive under this Employment Agreement (or under any other plan or agreement of the Company) following any termination of employment is subject to the excise tax imposed by Section 4999 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (the "Code"), or any successor provision (the "Excise Tax"), the Company shall pay the Executive an additional cash amount (the "Gross-Up Payment") such that the net after-tax amount received by the Executive under this Employment Agreement is the same as if the Excise Tax had not applied to any payments made under this Employment Agreement. For purposes of determining the Gross-Up Payment, the Executive shall be deemed to pay federal income tax at the highest marginal rate of taxation in the calendar year in which the payment to which the Gross-Up Payment relates is to be made. The determination of whether such Excise Tax is payable and the amount of the Excise Tax shall be made upon the opinion of a national accounting firm selected by the Executive and reasonably acceptable to the Company. The Gross-Up Payment shall be subject to the six-month delay of payment described in Section 6.5 below to the extent required by Section 409A of the Code, but in no event shall be paid later than December 31 of the year following the year in which the Executive remits the related taxes.

ARTICLE 3 — TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

3.1 Death or Total Disability. The Executive's employment under this Employment Agreement shall automatically terminate upon the death or Total Disability of the Executive. For purposes of this Employment Agreement, "Total Disability" means the Executive's inability, by reason of physical or mental illness, injury, or other incapacity, to perform the essential functions of the Executive's position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for any 90 consecutive days or 120 days in any 12-month period, as determined in good faith by the Board of Directors.

3.2 Termination by the Executive. The Executive shall be entitled to terminate the Executive's employment under this Employment Agreement by giving Notice of Termination to the Company:

(a) for Good Reason; or

(b) at any time without Good Reason.

3.3 Termination by the Company. The Company shall be entitled to terminate the Executive's employment under this Employment Agreement by giving Notice of Termination to the Executive:

(a) in the event of the Executive's Total Disability;

(b) for Cause; or

(c) at any time without Cause.

3.4 Definitions of Cause and Good Reason. For purposes of this Employment Agreement: (a) "Cause" means the Executive's (i) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, any felony or crime involving moral turpitude or fraud; (ii) willful misconduct or gross negligence in the performance of the Executive's duties; (iii) willful failure to perform the lawful and reasonable directives of the Company after written notice and opportunity to cure; or (iv) material breach of this Employment Agreement after written notice and opportunity to cure; and (b) "Good Reason" means a material adverse diminution in the Executive's title or duties, a material reduction in Base Salary (other than an Across the Board Reduction), or a material relocation of the Executive's principal place of employment more than 50 miles from {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, in each case subject to the Executive's giving the Company prompt written notice and a thirty (30)-day opportunity to cure.

3.5 Notice of Termination. Any termination of the Executive's employment under this Employment Agreement (other than termination due to death) shall be communicated by written Notice of Termination, specifying the provision of this Employment Agreement under which termination is effected.

ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION UPON TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

If the Executive's employment under this Employment Agreement is terminated, then except for any other rights or benefits specifically provided in this Employment Agreement, the Company shall be obligated to provide compensation and benefits to the Executive only as follows and only after the Executive's execution of a general release of claims in favor of the Company and all related parties in a form provided by the Company.

4.1 Termination for Death or Total Disability. If the Executive's employment is terminated by reason of the Executive's death or Total Disability, the Company shall:

(a) pay the Executive (or the Executive's estate) any Base Salary earned but not paid as of the Date of Termination (the "Accrued Base Salary");

(b) pay the Executive any annual bonus earned for a completed fiscal year but unpaid as of the Date of Termination;

(c) reimburse the Executive for any unreimbursed business expenses incurred through the Date of Termination; and

(d) pay any other accrued, vested benefits under any Company benefit plans, in accordance with the terms of those plans.

4.2 Termination for Cause or by the Executive Without Good Reason. If the Executive's employment is terminated by the Company for Cause or by the Executive without Good Reason, the Company shall pay the Executive the items listed in Section 4.1(a), (c), and (d), and the Executive shall not be entitled to any further compensation or benefits except as expressly required by applicable law.

4.3 Severance for Termination Without Cause or for Good Reason. If, during the Executive's employment, the Executive's employment is terminated by the Company without Cause or by the Executive for Good Reason, then, in addition to the items listed in Section 4.1(a)-(d) and subject to the Executive's execution and non-revocation of a general release of claims within forty-five (45) days after the Date of Termination, the Company shall provide the Executive with the following severance benefits (collectively, the "Severance Benefits"):

(a) a lump-sum cash payment equal to twelve (12) months of the Executive's then-current Base Salary, payable within fifteen (15) days after the general release of claims becomes irrevocable;

(b) a lump-sum cash payment equal to one (1) times the Executive's target annual bonus for the fiscal year in which the Date of Termination occurs;

(c) for a period of twelve (12) months following the Date of Termination, to the extent the Executive timely elects continuation coverage under the Company's group health plans pursuant to the Consolidated Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1985 ("COBRA") and remits the amount of premium charged to similarly situated active employees for comparable coverage, the Company shall pay to the Executive a monthly cash amount equal to the Company's portion of the premium for such coverage; and

(d) any equity awards that would have vested during the twelve (12) months following the Date of Termination shall accelerate and become fully vested as of the Date of Termination.

4.4 No Mitigation. The Executive shall not be required to mitigate damages, by seeking other employment or otherwise, as a condition of receiving any portion of the Severance Benefits, and the Severance Benefits shall not be reduced by any compensation earned by the Executive from any subsequent employer.

ARTICLE 5 — CHANGE IN CONTROL

5.1 Change in Control Definition. For purposes of this Employment Agreement, a "Change in Control" means: (a) the acquisition by any person or group of beneficial ownership of more than 50% of the combined voting power of the Company's then-outstanding voting securities; (b) the consummation of a merger, consolidation, reorganization, or similar transaction in which the Company's stockholders immediately before the transaction do not own at least 50% of the voting power of the surviving or resulting entity immediately after the transaction; or (c) the sale of all or substantially all of the Company's assets to an unrelated third party.

5.2 Double-Trigger Acceleration. If the Executive's employment is terminated by the Company without Cause or by the Executive for Good Reason within twenty-four (24) months after a Change in Control, then, in addition to the Severance Benefits set forth in Section 4.3, all of the Executive's outstanding equity awards (including the Initial RSU Grant) shall accelerate and become fully vested as of the Date of Termination.

5.3 Section 280G Gross-Up. If the payments and benefits described in this Article 5, together with any other payments or benefits the Executive is entitled to receive under any other plan or agreement of the Company, would be subject to the excise tax imposed by Section 4999 of the Code, the Company shall pay the Gross-Up Payment described in Section 2.10.

ARTICLE 6 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

6.1 Confidentiality. The Executive agrees that during the course of the Executive's employment with the Company, the Executive will obtain Confidential Information of the Company (including, without limitation, financial information, customer information, route data, fleet maintenance records, fuel-hedging strategies, labor relations matters, and other information that derives independent economic value from not being generally known). The Executive shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence during and after the Executive's employment, shall use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing the Executive's duties, and shall return all Confidential Information and Company property upon the termination of the Executive's employment.

6.2 Non-Competition. During the Executive's employment and for a period of twelve (12) months following the Date of Termination, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, anywhere in the United States in which the Company actively operates scheduled passenger air service as of the Date of Termination, accept employment from, perform services for, or hold any ownership interest greater than 1% in any business that competes with the Company in the operation of scheduled passenger air service.

6.3 Non-Solicitation. During the Executive's employment and for a period of twelve (12) months following the Date of Termination, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or hire any officer or other employee of the Company with whom the Executive worked or had material contact during the twelve (12) months preceding the Date of Termination.

6.4 Non-Disparagement. The Executive shall not, both during the Executive's employment and at any time thereafter, make or publish any statement (orally, in writing, on social media, or in any other medium) that disparages, defames, or impugns the reputation of the Company, its officers, directors, employees, or agents.

6.5 Tolling. The post-termination restricted period set forth in Sections 6.2 and 6.3 shall be tolled day-for-day for any period during which the Executive is in breach of any such covenant, until the Executive has fully complied with such covenant.

6.6 Reasonableness. The Executive acknowledges that the restrictions in this Article 6 are reasonable in scope, duration, and geographic coverage and are necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests, including the Company's confidential information, customer relationships, and substantial investment in the Executive's training and development.

ARTICLE 7 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND INVENTION ASSIGNMENT

7.1 Assignment of Work Product. The Executive hereby assigns to the Company the Executive's entire right, title, and interest in and to all inventions, discoveries, improvements, works of authorship, and other intellectual property that the Executive conceives, develops, or reduces to practice, alone or jointly with others, during the Executive's employment that (a) relate at the time of conception or reduction to practice to the business of the Company, (b) result from any work performed by the Executive for the Company, or (c) are developed using any Company resources or Confidential Information (collectively, the "Work Product").

7.2 Works Made for Hire. All copyrightable Work Product shall be deemed a "work made for hire" within the meaning of the United States Copyright Act, with the Company deemed the author thereof, and to the extent any such Work Product does not qualify as a work made for hire, the Executive hereby assigns all right, title, and interest therein to the Company.

7.3 Defend Trade Secrets Act Whistleblower Notice. Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b) of the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016, the Executive is hereby notified that: (a) an individual shall not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret that is made (i) in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official, either directly or indirectly, or to an attorney, and (ii) solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or that is made in a complaint or other document filed in a lawsuit or other proceeding, if such filing is made under seal; and (b) an individual who files a lawsuit for retaliation by an employer for reporting a suspected violation of law may disclose the trade secret to the attorney of the individual and use the trade secret information in the court proceeding, if the individual (i) files any document containing the trade secret under seal and (ii) does not disclose the trade secret, except pursuant to court order.

ARTICLE 8 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

8.1 Executive Representations. The Executive represents and warrants that (a) the Executive has the full right and authority to enter into this Employment Agreement; (b) the Executive is not subject to any agreement that would prevent or interfere with the Executive's performance of the Executive's duties; and (c) the Executive will not bring to the Company or use any confidential information of any prior employer.

ARTICLE 9 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 Governing Law. This Employment Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

9.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Employment Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, and each Party irrevocably submits to the personal jurisdiction of such courts.

9.3 Equitable Relief Carve-Out. Notwithstanding Section 9.2, the Company may seek equitable relief, including specific performance, injunction, and other equitable remedies, in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 6 or Article 7 of this Employment Agreement, without the requirement of posting bond and without proving actual damages.

9.4 Waiver of Jury Trial. EACH PARTY HEREBY IRREVOCABLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY LEGAL PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT.

9.5 Attorneys' Fees. In any dispute arising under this Employment Agreement, each Party shall bear its own attorneys' fees and costs, except that the prevailing Party in any litigation to enforce Article 6 or Article 7 of this Employment Agreement shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

ARTICLE 10 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 Entire Agreement. This Employment Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous understandings, agreements, representations, and warranties.

10.2 Amendment. No amendment, modification, or waiver of any provision of this Employment Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the Executive and a duly authorized officer of the Company.

10.3 Assignment. The Executive may not assign this Employment Agreement. The Company may assign this Employment Agreement to any successor that assumes the obligations of the Company hereunder.

10.4 Notices. All notices and other communications under this Employment Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, by confirmed email, or by certified mail (return receipt requested), to the respective addresses of the Parties set forth above.

10.5 Severability. If any provision of this Employment Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

10.6 Survival. Articles 4, 5, 6, 7, and 9 shall survive the termination of the Executive's employment and the termination of this Employment Agreement.

10.7 Counterparts. This Employment Agreement may be executed in one or more counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
