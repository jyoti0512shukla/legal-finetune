MASTER EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT WITH PER-EXECUTIVE SCHEDULE A

This MASTER EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Master Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing in the Commonwealth of {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} (the "Executive"). The Company and the Executive are collectively the "Parties."

This Master Agreement establishes the baseline terms applicable to the Executive's employment with the Company in a senior executive capacity. The role-specific details — including the Executive's title, base salary, target bonus opportunity, equity grant, restricted-covenant scope, and CIC severance multiples — are set forth in the Schedule A executed concurrently with this Master Agreement and incorporated herein by reference. The Company uses this Master Agreement as a template across its senior executive cadre; only Schedule A is customized for each individual executive, and Schedule A is the only document amended on promotions, comp adjustments, or scope changes — the Master Agreement itself is not amended on a per-executive basis.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein and other good and valuable consideration, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS AND STRUCTURE

1.1 "Schedule A" means the per-executive schedule executed concurrently with this Master Agreement that specifies, with respect to the Executive: (a) title and reporting line; (b) base salary; (c) target annual bonus opportunity; (d) initial equity grant value; (e) restrictive covenant scope (geographic and substantive); (f) Ordinary Severance multiple; (g) Enhanced Severance (CIC double-trigger) multiple; and (h) any executive-specific terms.

1.2 "Cause" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.2 below.

1.3 "Change in Control" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.5 below.

1.4 "Good Reason" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.3 below.

1.5 "Order of Precedence." In the event of a conflict between this Master Agreement and Schedule A, Schedule A governs solely with respect to the executive-specific terms it addresses.

ARTICLE 2 — POSITION AND DUTIES

2.1 Position. The Company shall employ the Executive in the position set forth in Schedule A, with the duties customarily associated with such position at a federal aerospace and defense contractor of comparable size, plus any additional duties reasonably assigned by the Chief Executive Officer or Board of Directors.

2.2 Reporting. The Executive shall report to the person identified in Schedule A.

2.3 Full-Time Commitment. The Executive shall devote substantially all of the Executive's business time and best efforts to the Company. The Executive may engage in personal investments, charitable activities, and (with the Company's prior written consent) outside board service.

2.4 Eligibility for Federal Contractor Roles. The Executive acknowledges that the Company is a prime federal contractor subject to (a) the Department of Defense Manual 5220.22-M (the National Industrial Security Program Operating Manual, or "NISPOM"), (b) Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement ("DFARS") clauses applicable to the Company's federal contracts, and (c) Executive Order 11246 and the Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs ("OFCCP") affirmative action regulations. The Executive's employment is contingent upon, and shall continue only so long as, the Executive maintains any security clearance required by the Executive's role, as specified in Schedule A.

2.5 Place of Employment. The Executive's principal place of employment shall be the Company's offices in {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

ARTICLE 3 — TERM

3.1 Term. The Executive's employment shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated in accordance with Article 5.

ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

4.1 Base Salary. The Company shall pay the Executive the base salary set forth in Schedule A, subject to annual review by the Compensation Committee of the Board of Directors (the "Compensation Committee").

4.2 Annual Bonus. The Executive shall be eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target opportunity set forth in Schedule A, determined by the Compensation Committee based on Company and individual performance against objectives established at the beginning of each fiscal year.

4.3 Equity Grant. As soon as practicable after the Effective Date, the Compensation Committee shall grant the Executive an initial equity award with the grant-date fair value set forth in Schedule A, in such form and on such vesting terms as the Compensation Committee determines under the Company's then-effective equity incentive plan.

4.4 Welfare and Retirement Benefits. The Executive shall be entitled to participate in the Company's standard executive benefit plans on terms no less favorable than those generally applicable to other senior executives.

4.5 Vacation. The Executive shall be entitled to four (4) weeks of paid vacation per calendar year.

4.6 Business Expenses. The Company shall reimburse the Executive for reasonable, documented business expenses in accordance with the Company's standard policy.

4.7 Indemnification. The Company shall indemnify the Executive to the fullest extent permitted by law and shall maintain D&O liability insurance covering the Executive on terms no less favorable than the insurance covering any other senior executive.

4.8 Section 280G Best-Net Cutback. If any payment to the Executive would constitute a parachute payment subject to the Section 4999 excise tax of the Internal Revenue Code, the aggregate payments shall be reduced (to the minimum extent necessary) only if such reduction would result in the Executive's receiving, on an after-tax basis, a greater amount than the Executive would have received absent the cutback.

4.9 Section 409A. This Master Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code.

ARTICLE 5 — TERMINATION AND SEVERANCE

5.1 Termination Events. The Executive's employment may be terminated as follows: (a) automatically upon the Executive's death; (b) by the Company on the Executive's Disability; (c) by the Company for Cause; (d) by the Company without Cause; (e) by the Executive for Good Reason; (f) by the Executive without Good Reason on at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' prior written notice; or (g) automatically if the Executive loses or fails to maintain any security clearance required by the Executive's role.

5.2 Definition of Cause. "Cause" means the Executive's: (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony or any crime involving fraud or moral turpitude; (b) intentional act of fraud or material misappropriation of Company funds; (c) willful refusal or willful failure to perform the Executive's material duties, after written notice and a thirty-day cure opportunity (where cure is reasonably possible); (d) loss, suspension, or revocation of any security clearance required for the Executive's position; (e) material breach of the Company's code of business conduct, Federal Acquisition Regulation conflict-of-interest rules, or NISPOM personnel security requirements; or (f) any act or omission that prohibits the Executive from being eligible to participate in federal contracts (e.g. debarment).

5.3 Definition of Good Reason. "Good Reason" means the occurrence, without the Executive's consent, of (a) a material adverse change in the Executive's title or duties, (b) a material reduction in Base Salary or target Annual Bonus opportunity, (c) a material relocation of the Executive's principal place of employment more than 50 miles, or (d) a material breach of this Master Agreement by the Company. The Executive must give notice within sixty (60) days, the Company must fail to cure within thirty (30) days, and the Executive must terminate within thirty (30) days after the cure period.

5.4 Severance for Termination Without Cause or for Good Reason. Subject to the Executive's execution and non-revocation of a release of claims (in the form of Schedule B) within sixty (60) days after termination, the Company shall provide the Executive with severance equal to the Ordinary Severance Multiple set forth in Schedule A applied to the Executive's then-current Base Salary plus target Annual Bonus, paid as a lump sum within fifteen (15) days after the release becomes irrevocable, plus continuation of group health benefits (COBRA contribution) for the corresponding period.

5.5 Change in Control Severance. "Change in Control" means: (a) any Person or group acquiring beneficial ownership of 50% or more of the Company's voting securities; (b) the consummation of a merger or sale in which the Company's stockholders immediately before do not hold a majority of the surviving entity; or (c) the sale of all or substantially all of the Company's assets. If, within the period beginning ninety (90) days before and ending twenty-four (24) months after a Change in Control, the Executive's employment is terminated by the Company without Cause or by the Executive for Good Reason, the Executive shall be entitled to enhanced severance equal to the Enhanced Severance Multiple set forth in Schedule A applied to Base Salary plus target Annual Bonus, plus full acceleration of all unvested equity awards.

5.6 No Severance on Clearance Loss. For the avoidance of doubt, if the Executive's employment terminates under Section 5.1(g) due to clearance loss, the Executive shall not be entitled to any severance under Section 5.4 or Section 5.5.

5.7 No Mitigation; No Offset. The Executive shall not be required to mitigate damages, and severance shall not be offset by any compensation earned from any subsequent employer.

ARTICLE 6 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

6.1 Confidentiality. The Executive shall hold in strict confidence all non-public information of the Company, including any Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), Covered Defense Information (CDI), and classified information that the Executive accesses in the course of employment.

6.2 DFARS Cyber Incident Reporting. The Executive shall promptly report to the Company's facility security officer any cyber incident involving Company systems or Company information, in accordance with DFARS 252.204-7012.

6.3 Non-Competition. During the Executive's employment and for a period of twelve (12) months following termination for any reason, the Executive shall not, within the geographic scope set forth in Schedule A, accept employment with any business that competes directly with the Company in the substantive product or service categories set forth in Schedule A.

6.4 Non-Solicitation. During the Executive's employment and for a period of twelve (12) months after termination, the Executive shall not directly solicit for employment any senior employee of the Company with whom the Executive worked or had material contact during the twelve (12) months preceding termination, subject to a general-solicitation carve-out.

6.5 Non-Disparagement. The Executive shall not, during or after employment, make any public statement that disparages the Company or its officers, directors, or employees.

6.6 Tolling. The post-termination restricted period shall be tolled day-for-day for any period during which the Executive is in breach of any covenant in this Article 6.

ARTICLE 7 — CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND IP

7.1 IP Assignment. The Executive assigns to the Company all inventions, works of authorship, and intellectual property created during employment that relate to the Company's business or that are developed using Company resources.

7.2 Defend Trade Secrets Act Notice. Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), the Executive is notified that the Executive cannot be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official or to an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law.

7.3 Post-Employment Debriefing. Upon termination of employment, the Executive shall participate in any post-employment debriefing required by the Company's facility security officer under NISPOM, including return of all classified materials and signing of any required certifications.

ARTICLE 8 — REPRESENTATIONS

8.1 Executive Representations. The Executive represents that the Executive (a) is free to enter into this Master Agreement, (b) holds (or will obtain by the start date) any security clearance required by the Executive's role, and (c) is not subject to any debarment or exclusion under federal procurement regulations.

8.2 OFCCP Equal Opportunity. The Company is a federal contractor committed to equal employment opportunity. The Company will not discriminate against the Executive on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, disability, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law.

ARTICLE 9 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 Governing Law. This Master Agreement and any Schedule A shall be governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to conflict of laws principles. To the extent any provision conflicts with any federal statute, regulation, or executive order applicable to federal contractors, federal law shall govern.

9.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

9.3 Equitable Relief. The Company may seek equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 6 or Article 7 of this Master Agreement.

9.4 Waiver of Jury Trial. EACH PARTY HEREBY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING UNDER THIS MASTER AGREEMENT.

ARTICLE 10 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 Entire Agreement. This Master Agreement, together with Schedule A and any Schedule B (release of claims), constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

10.2 Amendment of Master Agreement. This Master Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by the Executive and a duly authorized officer of the Company.

10.3 Amendment of Schedule A. Schedule A may be amended (without amending this Master Agreement) by a written addendum signed by the Executive and a duly authorized officer of the Company. Promotions, compensation adjustments, and scope changes shall be effected through Schedule A amendments only.

10.4 Assignment. The Executive may not assign this Master Agreement. The Company may assign this Master Agreement to any successor that assumes the obligations hereunder.

10.5 Survival. Articles 5, 6, 7, and 9 shall survive the termination of the Executive's employment.

10.6 Counterparts. This Master Agreement and Schedule A may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Master Agreement as of the Effective Date.
