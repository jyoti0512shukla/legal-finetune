4.1 Base Salary. You will be paid an annual base salary of {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}} (the "Base Salary"), to be paid bi-weekly or in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices, as amended from time to time. 4.2 Annual Discretionary Bonus Program. You will be provided an opportunity to participate in the Company's Annual Discretionary Bonus Program. Bonuses are at the sole and absolute discretion of the Board of Directors of the Company (the "Board") and are determined based on achievement of personal and Company goals, individual and group performance criteria, financial performance of the Company, and any other matters the Board may consider relevant. The target bonus shall be {{EMPLOYEE_TARGET_BONUS_PCT}}% of your annual Base Salary. 4.3 Equity Incentive Plan. You will be eligible to participate in the Company's Equity Incentive Plan, subject at all times to the sole and absolute discretion of the Board. Subject to applicable employment standards legislation, upon the termination of your employment, any outstanding options or awards (or other rights to acquire securities of the Company) shall be governed solely by the terms of the Equity Incentive Plan and the applicable award agreements, and not by this Employment Agreement.

4.4 Initial Stock Option Grant. As soon as practicable following the Start Date, the Board shall grant you an initial stock option grant valued at {{EMPLOYEE_INITIAL_EQUITY}} based on the closing price of the Company's common shares on the Start Date pursuant to the Company's Equity Incentive Plan (the "Initial Grant"). The Initial Grant shall vest in equal annual installments over a four-year period, with the first installment vesting on the first anniversary of the grant date, subject to the terms and conditions of the Equity Incentive Plan and the Executive remaining Actively Employed on each vesting date.

4.5 Benefits. You will be eligible, starting on the first day of your employment, to participate in the standard group benefits program that the Company provides to its executives from time to time in its discretion, subject to the terms and conditions set forth in the various provider benefit plans and insurance policies as amended from time to time (the "Benefits"). You will be entitled to four (4) weeks of paid vacation per calendar year. If you receive a vacation overpayment as a result of taking vacation in advance of it being earned, then upon termination you will be required to repay any vacation overpayment to the Company, except to any extent not permitted by applicable employment standards legislation.

4.8 Business Expenses. The Company shall reimburse you for reasonable, customary, and necessary business expenses incurred in the performance of your duties hereunder, in accordance with the Company's standard expense reimbursement policy.

4.9 Definition of "Actively Employed." For purposes of this Employment Agreement, "Actively Employed" does not include any period following the termination of your employment for any reason, including any period of notice of termination that is given or ought to have been given under this Employment Agreement or under any applicable law (including the common law) in respect of such termination of employment for which pay in lieu of notice is provided, except to the minimum extent (if any) required by applicable employment standards legislation. Subject only to the express minimum requirements of the British Columbia Employment Standards Act and except as expressly set forth in Article 6, no payments will be paid or earned following the Date of Termination, without regard to any notice or severance period to which you may then be entitled (whether pursuant to contract, the common law, or otherwise), and you hereby waive any right that you might otherwise have had to damages or other amounts in lieu thereof.

ARTICLE 5 — TEMPORARY LAYOFF AND SUSPENSION

5.1 Temporary Layoff. The Company may temporarily lay you off in accordance with the requirements of the British Columbia Employment Standards Act, as amended from time to time. Such a temporary layoff shall not constitute a termination of your employment. Further, you will be deemed to have resigned your employment if you decline to accept recall of employment following such temporary layoff, except to any extent not permitted by applicable employment standards legislation, and shall not be entitled to any further compensation except for your minimum statutory entitlements under applicable employment standards legislation.

5.2 Suspension. Subject to applicable employment standards legislation, the Company may suspend your employment with or without pay in circumstances involving progressive discipline, corrective action, or a workplace investigation. Such a suspension shall not constitute a termination of your employment.

ARTICLE 6 — TERMINATION

6.1 Termination by the Company for Cause. The Company may terminate your employment for "just cause" at any time, immediately and without prior notice or pay in lieu of notice or any severance entitlements, except for any minimum statutory entitlements required by the British Columbia Employment Standards Act. For purposes of this Employment Agreement, "just cause" shall have the meaning given to that term under the laws of the Province of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

6.2 Termination by the Company Without Cause. The Company may terminate your employment without "just cause" at any time, by providing you with the greater of: (a) the minimum notice (or pay in lieu of notice) and severance pay required by the British Columbia Employment Standards Act; or (b) the following enhanced severance package, which is provided in lieu of (and not in addition to) any common-law reasonable notice entitlements you may have:

(i) Notice Pay: a lump sum payment equal to twelve (12) months of your then-current Base Salary;

(ii) Bonus: a pro-rated portion of your target Annual Discretionary Bonus for the year in which the Date of Termination occurs, calculated based on the number of days you were Actively Employed during that year;

(iii) Benefits Continuation: continuation of your Benefits (other than disability insurance) for the lesser of twelve (12) months or such shorter period as may be permitted by the applicable benefit plan; and

(iv) Equity: any unvested Initial Grant that would have vested within twelve (12) months following the Date of Termination shall accelerate and become fully vested as of the Date of Termination.

The enhanced severance package set forth in this Section 6.2(b) is conditional upon your signing and not revoking the Full and Final Release attached as Schedule C to this Employment Agreement (the "Release"). For the avoidance of doubt, the enhanced severance package is intended to satisfy and fully discharge any common-law reasonable-notice entitlement you may have, and the Company shall have no further obligation to provide notice or severance beyond the greater of the British Columbia Employment Standards Act minimums and the enhanced package set forth above.

6.3 Termination by You. You may terminate your employment at any time by providing the Company with at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' prior written notice. The Company may, in its sole discretion, accept your resignation effective immediately upon receipt and pay you in lieu of any portion of the notice period you would otherwise have worked.

6.4 Date of Termination. The "Date of Termination" means the last day you are Actively Employed with the Company and does not include any period of notice of termination for which pay in lieu of notice is provided that is given or ought to have been given under this Employment Agreement or under any applicable law (including the common law), except to the minimum extent (if any) required by applicable employment standards legislation.

6.5 Deductions. In the event of the termination of your employment for any reason, the Company shall be entitled to deduct from your final wages any monies due from you to the Company, including without limitation any vacation overpayment, outstanding loans, advances, or any other monies owed by you to the Company, except to any extent not permitted by applicable employment standards legislation.

ARTICLE 7 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

7.1 Application of Confidentiality Agreement. Your obligations of confidentiality, intellectual property assignment, non-competition, non-solicitation, and non-disparagement are set forth in the Confidentiality Agreement attached as Schedule A and incorporated herein by reference. The Confidentiality Agreement shall apply throughout the Term and shall survive the termination of your employment in accordance with its terms.

7.2 Non-Competition. Subject to applicable employment standards and common-law principles of reasonableness, during the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months following the Date of Termination, you shall not, directly or indirectly, anywhere in Canada or the United States, engage in or become employed by any business that competes with the Company in the development, manufacture, sale, or commercialization of pharmaceutical products targeting the same disease indications as those that the Company is actively developing as of the Date of Termination. This restriction is intended to be limited to the minimum geographic and substantive scope reasonably necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests.

7.3 Non-Solicitation of Employees. During the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months following the Date of Termination, you shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment, hire, or attempt to solicit for employment or hire any officer or other employee of the Company with whom you worked or had material contact during the twelve (12) months preceding the Date of Termination.

7.4 Non-Disparagement. You shall not, both during the Term and at any time thereafter, make or publish any statement (orally, in writing, on social media, or otherwise) that disparages, defames, or impugns the reputation of the Company or any of its officers, directors, employees, or agents.

7.5 Tolling. The post-termination restricted period set forth in Sections 7.2 and 7.3 shall be tolled day-for-day for any period during which you are in breach of any such covenant, until you have fully complied with the covenant.
