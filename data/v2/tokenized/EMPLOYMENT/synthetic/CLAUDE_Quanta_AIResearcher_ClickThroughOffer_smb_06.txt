ARTICLE 4 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND PUBLICATION POLICY

4.1 Assignment of Research Work Product. You hereby assign to the Lab all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, model weights, training datasets, prompts, system prompts, model outputs, research papers, software, and other intellectual property that you conceive, develop, or reduce to practice during your employment that relate to the Lab's business or that are developed using Lab resources or Confidential Information (collectively, "Research Work Product").

4.3 Publication Policy.

(a) Right of First Review. You may submit research papers, blog posts, and conference talks describing the Lab's research for external publication, but only after a Lab right-of-first-review window of sixty (60) days during which the Lab may request redactions of confidential information, request a delay for patent filing purposes (up to an additional thirty (30) days), or decline to permit publication if the material would disclose unreleased capabilities or competitive secrets.

(b) Publication Authorship. The Lab is named as the author or co-author of any paper relating to Research Work Product. You may be named as a co-author with attribution.

(c) No Pre-Release Disclosure. Until the Lab publicly releases a model or capability, you may not discuss it on social media, in podcasts, in lectures, or in any external forum.

4.4 No Benchmark Sharing. You may not share evaluation results, benchmark scores, or comparisons of the Lab's models against other models in any external forum until the Lab has officially published the result.

4.5 No Distillation. You may not use Research Work Product or model outputs to train, fine-tune, or distill any model owned by you, by any third party, or by any future employer.
