MASTER AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT WITH PER-HIRE OFFER ADDENDUM

This MASTER AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Master Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing in the State of {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} (the "Employee"). The Company and the Employee are collectively the "Parties."

This Master Agreement is intentionally drafted as a bare framework. It establishes the baseline terms that apply to the Employee's at-will employment with the Company. The role-specific details — including job title, base salary, FLSA classification, work location, reporting line, start date, sign-on bonus, and equity — are set forth in the Employee's Offer Addendum, which is executed concurrently with this Master Agreement and forms an integral part of the Parties' employment relationship. The Company uses this Master Agreement as a reusable template across employment offers; only the Offer Addendum is customized for each individual hire.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS AND STRUCTURE

1.1 "Offer Addendum" means the written addendum executed by the Company and the Employee concurrently with this Master Agreement that incorporates this Master Agreement by reference and specifies: (a) the Employee's job title, (b) the Employee's reporting line, (c) the Employee's base salary, (d) the Employee's FLSA classification (exempt or non-exempt), (e) the Employee's start date, (f) the Employee's primary work location, (g) any sign-on bonus and the conditions for repayment, (h) any equity grant and the vesting terms, (i) any incentive compensation plan eligibility, and (j) any other role-specific terms.

1.2 "Employee Handbook" means the Company's then-current Employee Handbook, as amended from time to time, which is incorporated into this Master Agreement by reference.

1.3 "Order of Precedence." In the event of a conflict between this Master Agreement and an Offer Addendum, the Offer Addendum governs solely with respect to the role-specific terms it addresses. In the event of a conflict between either this Master Agreement or an Offer Addendum and the Employee Handbook, this Master Agreement and the Offer Addendum govern.

ARTICLE 2 — POSITION AND DUTIES

2.1 Position. The Company shall employ the Employee in the position set forth in the Offer Addendum, with the duties customarily associated with that position and any additional duties reasonably assigned by the Company from time to time.

2.2 Reporting. The Employee shall report to the supervisor identified in the Offer Addendum, or to such other person as the Company may designate from time to time.

2.3 Full-Time Commitment. The Employee shall devote substantially all of the Employee's business time and best efforts to the performance of the Employee's duties for the Company.

2.4 Outside Activities. The Employee may engage in personal investments, charitable activities, and unpaid civic activities so long as such activities do not interfere with the Employee's duties or compete with the Company's business.

2.5 Work Location. The Employee's primary work location shall be as set forth in the Offer Addendum. The Company may, in its discretion, modify the Employee's work location upon reasonable notice.

ARTICLE 3 — TERM AND TERMINATION (AT-WILL)

3.1 At-Will Employment. The Employee's employment with the Company is at-will. Either the Employee or the Company may end the employment relationship at any time, for any reason or no reason, with or without notice. Nothing in this Master Agreement, any Offer Addendum, or the Employee Handbook creates a contract of employment for any specific duration or limits the at-will nature of the Employee's employment.

3.2 Termination Notice. While at-will employment does not require notice, the Parties anticipate that the resigning Party will provide at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days' notice as a matter of professional courtesy.

3.3 No Implied Term. The use of phrases like "annual" base salary or "annual" bonus in any Offer Addendum is for compensation calculation purposes only and does not imply any minimum term of employment.

ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

4.1 Base Salary. The Company shall pay the Employee the base salary set forth in the Offer Addendum, less applicable taxes and withholdings, in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices.

4.2 FLSA Classification. The Employee's FLSA classification (exempt or non-exempt) is set forth in the Offer Addendum. Non-exempt Employees are entitled to overtime pay at one and one-half times the regular rate for hours worked over forty (40) in a workweek, in accordance with applicable federal and state wage and hour laws.

4.3 Incentive Compensation. The Employee's eligibility for incentive compensation, bonus plans, and equity grants is set forth in the Offer Addendum. Any incentive compensation, bonus, or equity grant is governed by the terms of the relevant plan document and award agreement, which control over any general statement in this Master Agreement.

4.4 Sign-On Bonus. Any sign-on bonus is set forth in the Offer Addendum, including the conditions under which it must be repaid by the Employee on early departure. Sign-on bonuses are paid net of applicable taxes and withholdings.

4.5 Welfare and Retirement Benefits. The Employee shall be eligible to participate in the Company's standard employee benefit plans (including medical, dental, vision, 401(k), and paid time off), in each case subject to the terms of the applicable plan documents and any waiting periods.

4.6 Vacation. The Employee shall accrue paid time off in accordance with the Company's standard PTO policy as set forth in the Employee Handbook.

4.7 Business Expenses. The Company shall reimburse the Employee for reasonable, documented business expenses incurred in the performance of the Employee's duties, in accordance with the Company's standard expense reimbursement policy.

ARTICLE 5 — CONFIDENTIALITY AND IP

5.1 Confidentiality. The Employee shall hold in strict confidence all non-public information of the Company that the Employee obtains during employment, including trade secrets, customer data, financial information, business plans, technology, and personnel information. The Employee shall not use or disclose such information except as required to perform the Employee's duties.

5.2 Invention Assignment. The Employee assigns to the Company any invention, work of authorship, or other intellectual property the Employee creates during employment that relates to the Company's business or that is developed using Company resources. To the maximum extent permitted by law, copyrightable work shall be deemed a "work made for hire."

5.3 Defend Trade Secrets Act Notice. Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), the Employee is notified that the Employee cannot be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official or to an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law.

5.4 Return of Company Property. Upon termination of employment for any reason, the Employee shall promptly return all Company property and confidential information in the Employee's possession or control.

ARTICLE 6 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

6.1 Confidentiality Survival. The Employee's confidentiality obligations under Section 5.1 survive the termination of employment indefinitely.

6.2 Non-Solicitation of Employees. During the Employee's employment and for a period of twelve (12) months after termination for any reason, the Employee shall not directly solicit for employment any employee of the Company with whom the Employee worked or had material contact during the twelve (12) months preceding termination, subject to a general-solicitation carve-out.

6.3 Non-Disparagement. The Employee shall not, both during and after employment, make any public statement that disparages, defames, or impugns the reputation of the Company, its officers, directors, or employees.

ARTICLE 7 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

7.1 Governing Law. This Master Agreement and any Offer Addendum shall be governed by the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to conflict of laws principles.

7.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute arising under this Master Agreement or any Offer Addendum shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

7.3 Equitable Relief. The Company may seek equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 5 or Article 6 of this Master Agreement, without bond and without proving actual damages.

ARTICLE 8 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

8.1 Entire Agreement. This Master Agreement, the Offer Addendum, and the Employee Handbook constitute the entire agreement between the Parties.

8.2 Amendment. This Master Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by the Employee and a duly authorized officer of the Company. Offer Addenda may be amended through a written addendum without amending this Master Agreement.

8.3 Assignment. The Employee may not assign this Master Agreement. The Company may assign this Master Agreement to any successor.

8.4 Severability. If any provision of this Master Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

8.5 Survival. Articles 5, 6, and 7 survive the termination of employment.

8.6 Counterparts. This Master Agreement and any Offer Addendum may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Master Agreement as of the Effective Date.
