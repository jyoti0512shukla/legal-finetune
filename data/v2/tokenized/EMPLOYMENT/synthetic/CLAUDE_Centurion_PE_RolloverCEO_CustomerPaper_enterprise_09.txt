EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT
[EXECUTIVE PAPER — HEAVILY NEGOTIATED]

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing in the State of {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} (the "Executive"). The Company and the Executive are collectively the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Company is a portfolio company controlled by Northbrook Capital Partners VI, L.P. (the "Sponsor"), and the Sponsor wishes to retain the Executive as the Company's Chief Executive Officer to lead the Company through its anticipated buy-and-build operating thesis;

WHEREAS, the Executive is the former chief executive officer of an unrelated industrial enterprise and is, as of the Effective Date, indirectly vested in equity of the Company through a rollover transaction in which a portion of the Executive's prior consideration was reinvested in the equity of the Company (the "Rollover Equity");

WHEREAS, this Agreement reflects the heavily negotiated terms agreed between the Sponsor, the Company, and the Executive's counsel, and is intended to govern the Executive's employment as Chief Executive Officer until terminated in accordance with Article 6;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises, the mutual covenants set forth herein, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with such Person.

1.2 "Cause" means the Executive's: (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony or any crime involving fraud, embezzlement, or moral turpitude; (b) intentional act of fraud or material misappropriation of Company funds or assets; (c) willful refusal or willful failure to perform the Executive's material duties under this Agreement, after written notice from the Board specifying the alleged failure and providing the Executive at least thirty (30) days to cure; or (d) material breach of this Agreement that is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice from the Board specifying the alleged breach. No act or omission shall constitute Cause unless it is willful, in bad faith, and not reasonably believed by the Executive to be in the best interest of the Company. For the avoidance of doubt, the failure of the Company or the Sponsor to achieve any business or financial objective, by itself, shall not constitute Cause.

1.3 "Change in Control" means: (a) any Person or "group" (as such term is used in Section 13(d)(3) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934) other than the Sponsor and its Affiliates acquiring beneficial ownership of 50% or more of the combined voting power of the Company's then-outstanding voting securities; (b) the consummation of a merger, consolidation, or similar transaction in which the Sponsor and its Affiliates cease to hold a majority of the combined voting power of the surviving entity; or (c) the sale of all or substantially all of the Company's assets to a Person other than the Sponsor or an Affiliate of the Sponsor.

1.4 "Code" means the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.

1.5 "Good Reason" means the occurrence, without the Executive's prior written consent, of any of the following: (a) any material adverse change in the Executive's title, authority, duties, or reporting line (including any failure of the Executive to remain Chief Executive Officer of the ultimate parent entity); (b) any reduction in the Executive's Base Salary or target Annual Bonus opportunity (other than a reduction proportionately applied to all senior executives of the Company on an across-the-board basis, which proportional reduction shall not exceed 10%); (c) any material relocation of the Executive's principal place of employment more than 50 miles from {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}; (d) the Company's failure to obtain the express assumption of this Agreement by any successor following a Change in Control; or (e) any material breach of this Agreement by the Company. The Executive must give written notice within sixty (60) days after the Executive first becomes aware of the Good Reason event, the Company must fail to cure within thirty (30) days of such notice, and the Executive must terminate within thirty (30) days after the cure period expires.

1.6 "MFN Provisions" has the meaning set forth in Section 7.6.

1.7 "Rollover Equity" means the equity interests in the Company beneficially held by the Executive as of the Effective Date as a result of the rollover transaction described in the recitals.

1.8 "Sponsor" has the meaning set forth in the recitals.

ARTICLE 2 — POSITION AND DUTIES

2.1 Title and Responsibilities. The Company shall employ the Executive as {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}, with all the duties, authority, and responsibilities customarily associated with that position at a portfolio company of comparable size and scope. The Executive shall report exclusively to the {{EMPLOYEE_REPORTS_TO}} and to no other person. No other person within the Company or the Sponsor shall have direct supervisory authority over the Executive.

2.2 Full-Time Commitment with Carve-Outs. The Executive shall devote substantially all of the Executive's business time to the affairs of the Company. The Executive may, without the Company's prior written consent and notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, (a) serve on the boards of up to three (3) for-profit entities that are not direct competitors of the Company, (b) serve on charitable, civic, or educational boards, (c) deliver lectures, fulfill speaking engagements, or teach at educational institutions, and (d) manage the Executive's personal investments and family office activities, in each case so long as such activities do not materially interfere with the Executive's duties to the Company.

2.3 Place of Employment. The Executive's principal place of employment shall be the Company's offices in {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}. The Company shall not, without the Executive's prior written consent, relocate the Executive's principal place of employment more than 50 miles from such location. Any such relocation without the Executive's consent shall constitute Good Reason.

ARTICLE 3 — TERM

3.1 Term. The Executive's employment shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated in accordance with Article 6.

ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

4.1 Base Salary. The Company shall pay the Executive an annual base salary of {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}} (the "Base Salary"), subject to annual review by the Compensation Committee of the Board of Directors (the "Compensation Committee"). The Base Salary may be increased (but not decreased, except as part of an across-the-board reduction not exceeding 10% applied to all senior executives) at the discretion of the Compensation Committee.

4.2 Annual Bonus. The Executive shall be eligible to receive an annual cash bonus (the "Annual Bonus") with a target opportunity of {{EMPLOYEE_TARGET_BONUS_PCT}}% of the Executive's then-current Base Salary, with maximum bonus opportunity of 200% of target on overachievement. The Annual Bonus shall be determined by the Compensation Committee based on the achievement of objective Company financial performance metrics established by the Compensation Committee in consultation with the Executive at the beginning of each fiscal year. Earned Annual Bonuses shall be paid no later than March 15 of the year following the year in which they were earned.

4.3 Initial Equity Grant. As soon as practicable following the Effective Date, the Company shall grant the Executive an initial equity award with an aggregate grant-date fair value of {{EMPLOYEE_INITIAL_EQUITY}} (the "Initial Equity Grant") under the Company's then-effective equity incentive plan. The Initial Equity Grant shall consist of (a) 50% performance-vesting equity tied to Sponsor return-on-investment hurdles and (b) 50% time-vesting equity vesting in equal annual installments over five (5) years from the Effective Date. Vesting shall accelerate as set forth in Section 6.5(d) and Section 8.4 below.

4.4 Rollover Equity. The Parties acknowledge that the Executive is, as of the Effective Date, beneficially vested in the Rollover Equity. The Rollover Equity shall be deemed fully vested as of the Effective Date and shall not be subject to any forfeiture, repurchase right, vesting condition, or service requirement under this Agreement, the equity incentive plan, or any other Company arrangement. The Rollover Equity shall remain freely transferable subject to applicable securities laws and customary tag-along and drag-along provisions in the Company's stockholders agreement.

4.5 Sign-On Bonus. Within fifteen (15) days after the Effective Date, the Company shall pay the Executive a one-time sign-on cash bonus of Five Hundred Thousand United States Dollars (US$500,000) (the "Sign-On Bonus"). The Sign-On Bonus shall be fully earned upon payment and is not subject to any clawback or repayment obligation regardless of the Executive's subsequent termination or resignation.

4.6 Retention Bonus. Subject to the Executive's continued employment through the relevant payment date, the Company shall pay the Executive a retention bonus of One Million United States Dollars (US$1,000,000) (the "Retention Bonus") in installments as follows: (a) US$250,000 on the first anniversary of the Effective Date; (b) US$250,000 on the second anniversary of the Effective Date; and (c) US$500,000 on the earlier of (i) an initial public offering of the Company's equity securities, or (ii) a Change in Control. If the Executive's employment is terminated by the Company without Cause or by the Executive for Good Reason before any installment becomes payable, the Executive shall be entitled to that installment as if the Executive had remained employed through the relevant payment date.

4.7 Welfare and Retirement Benefits. The Executive shall be entitled to participate in all employee welfare and retirement benefit plans of the Company on terms no less favorable than those generally applicable to other senior executives.

4.8 Vacation. The Executive shall be entitled to five (5) weeks of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued in accordance with the Company's standard vacation policy.

4.9 Indemnification and D&O Insurance. The Company shall indemnify the Executive to the fullest extent permitted by law and shall maintain directors and officers liability insurance coverage for the Executive on terms no less favorable than those provided to any other officer or director of the Company. The Company's indemnification obligations shall survive the termination of the Executive's employment.

4.10 Legal Fees. The Company shall reimburse the Executive for reasonable legal fees and expenses incurred in connection with the negotiation, drafting, and execution of this Agreement, up to a maximum of Seventy-Five Thousand United States Dollars (US$75,000), upon the Executive's submission of supporting invoices. This payment shall be made within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date.

ARTICLE 5 — CHANGE IN CONTROL

5.1 Definition. "Change in Control" has the meaning set forth in Section 1.3.

5.2 Double-Trigger Severance. If, within the period beginning ninety (90) days before and ending twenty-four (24) months after a Change in Control, the Executive's employment is terminated by the Company without Cause or by the Executive for Good Reason, the Executive shall be entitled to the Enhanced Severance set forth in Section 6.5 in lieu of (and not in addition to) any Ordinary Severance.

5.3 Equity Acceleration on CIC. Upon the occurrence of any Change in Control, all unvested time-vesting equity (including the time-vesting portion of the Initial Equity Grant) shall accelerate and become fully vested as of immediately prior to the Change in Control. Performance-vesting equity shall be deemed earned at target performance levels.

ARTICLE 6 — TERMINATION

6.1 Termination Events. The Executive's employment may be terminated as follows: (a) automatically upon the Executive's death; (b) by the Company on the Executive's Disability; (c) by the Company for Cause; (d) by the Company without Cause; (e) by the Executive for Good Reason; or (f) by the Executive without Good Reason on sixty (60) days' prior written notice.

6.2 Notice and Cure for Cause. Termination by the Company for Cause shall require written notice from the Board specifying the alleged grounds, and the Executive shall have at least thirty (30) days to cure (where cure is reasonably possible). The Executive shall have the right to appear before the full Board with counsel to contest the alleged grounds for Cause prior to any final determination by the Board.

6.3 Accrued Obligations. Upon any termination, the Company shall pay the Executive the following accrued obligations (the "Accrued Obligations"): (a) any Base Salary earned but unpaid through the date of termination; (b) any Annual Bonus earned for a completed fiscal year but unpaid; (c) any unreimbursed business expenses; (d) any accrued but unused vacation; and (e) any vested benefits under any Company benefit plan, payable in accordance with the terms of those plans.

6.4 Ordinary Severance. If the Executive's employment is terminated by the Company without Cause or by the Executive for Good Reason in circumstances not covered by Section 5.2, the Executive shall be entitled, in addition to the Accrued Obligations and subject to the Executive's execution and non-revocation of a release of claims (in the form of Schedule A) within sixty (60) days after the date of termination, to the following Ordinary Severance:

(a) a lump-sum cash payment equal to two (2) times the Executive's then-current Base Salary;

(b) a lump-sum cash payment equal to two (2) times the Executive's target Annual Bonus for the fiscal year in which the termination occurs;

(c) a pro-rata Annual Bonus for the fiscal year in which the termination occurs based on actual performance through the termination date;

(d) for a period of twenty-four (24) months following the date of termination, the Company shall pay the Executive a monthly cash amount equal to the COBRA premium charged for continuation coverage under the Company's group health plans for the Executive and the Executive's covered dependents; and

(e) all unvested time-vesting equity that would have vested within the twenty-four (24) months following the date of termination shall accelerate and vest as of the date of termination.

6.5 Enhanced Severance (CIC Double-Trigger). If the Executive becomes entitled to severance under Section 5.2, the Enhanced Severance shall consist of:

(a) a lump-sum cash payment equal to three (3) times the Executive's then-current Base Salary;

(b) a lump-sum cash payment equal to three (3) times the Executive's target Annual Bonus;

(c) a pro-rata Annual Bonus for the year of termination at target levels;

(d) twenty-four (24) months of Company-paid COBRA continuation coverage; and

(e) full acceleration of all outstanding equity awards (with performance-vesting equity deemed earned at target levels).

6.6 No Mitigation; No Offset. The Executive shall not be required to mitigate damages by seeking other employment or otherwise as a condition of receiving severance, and severance amounts shall not be offset by any compensation earned by the Executive from any subsequent employer.

6.7 Section 280G Best-Net Cutback. If any payment or benefit received by the Executive (whether under this Agreement or otherwise) would constitute a "parachute payment" within the meaning of Section 280G of the Code and would be subject to the excise tax imposed by Section 4999 of the Code, then the aggregate amount of such payments and benefits shall be reduced (to the minimum extent necessary) only if such reduction would result in the Executive's receiving, on an after-tax basis, a greater amount than the Executive would have received absent the cutback. The determination of the cutback (if any) shall be made by an independent nationally recognized public accounting firm selected by the Executive and reasonably acceptable to the Company.

6.8 Section 409A. This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Code, and shall be interpreted accordingly.

6.9 No SOX § 304 Clawback for Predecessor Errors. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement or any clawback policy of the Company, no clawback or recoupment shall be sought against the Executive under Section 304 of the Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002, Section 954 of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act, or any Company clawback policy with respect to any accounting restatement that arises principally from misconduct or errors that occurred before the Executive's appointment as Chief Executive Officer.

ARTICLE 7 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

7.1 Confidentiality. The Executive shall hold in strict confidence all non-public information of the Company and its Affiliates (including the Sponsor and the Sponsor's other portfolio companies) during and after employment.

7.2 Non-Competition. During the Executive's employment and for a period of twelve (12) months following the date of termination of the Executive's employment for any reason, the Executive shall not, anywhere in the United States in which the Company actively conducts business as of the date of termination, engage in or accept employment from any business that competes directly with the Company in industrial manufacturing of the specific product categories the Company manufactures as of the date of termination. For the avoidance of doubt, this Section 7.2 shall not prohibit the Executive from (a) accepting an executive position with any private equity firm or its portfolio companies (other than a competitor of the Company in the specific product categories described above), (b) serving on the board of any company that is not a direct competitor of the Company, or (c) managing the Executive's family office or personal investments.

7.3 Non-Solicitation of Employees. During the Executive's employment and for a period of twelve (12) months following the date of termination, the Executive shall not directly solicit for employment any senior employee of the Company with whom the Executive had material contact during the twelve (12) months preceding the date of termination, subject to a customary general-solicitation carve-out.

7.4 Non-Solicitation of Customers. During the Executive's employment and for a period of twelve (12) months following the date of termination, the Executive shall not directly solicit any customer of the Company with whom the Executive had material contact during the twelve (12) months preceding the date of termination for the purpose of providing goods or services that compete with the Company.

7.5 Non-Disparagement. The Executive shall not publicly disparage the Company, the Sponsor, or any of their respective officers, directors, or employees, and the Company (acting through its officers and directors) shall not publicly disparage the Executive. This mutual non-disparagement covenant survives termination.

7.6 MFN Provisions. The Executive acknowledges that the Company may from time to time enter into employment agreements with other senior executives of the Company. If, during the term of the Executive's employment, the Company grants any other senior executive of the Company (a) a higher severance multiple (in months of base salary or bonus) than the multiple set forth in Section 6.4 or 6.5, (b) a longer COBRA continuation period than the period set forth in Section 6.4 or 6.5, (c) a more favorable Section 280G treatment than the Best-Net Cutback set forth in Section 6.7, or (d) any other materially more favorable severance or change-in-control protection, then the Company shall promptly notify the Executive in writing, and the corresponding term in this Agreement shall automatically be deemed amended to match such more favorable term ("MFN Provisions"). For the avoidance of doubt, the MFN Provisions shall not entitle the Executive to a pure compensation increase but shall apply only to severance, CIC, and termination protections.

ARTICLE 8 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

8.1 IP Assignment. The Executive assigns to the Company all inventions, works of authorship, and intellectual property created during employment that relate to the Company's business or that are developed using Company resources.

8.2 Defend Trade Secrets Act Notice. Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), the Executive is notified that the Executive cannot be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official or to an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law.

8.3 Pre-Existing IP. The Executive's pre-existing IP and any inventions developed entirely outside the scope of the Executive's duties for the Company are excluded from the assignment. A list of such pre-existing IP shall be attached as Schedule B.

8.4 Tail Period for Equity Acceleration. For the avoidance of doubt, any equity acceleration triggered under this Agreement shall apply to all then-outstanding awards held by the Executive, including the Initial Equity Grant and the Rollover Equity (to the extent Rollover Equity is subject to any vesting condition, which the Parties acknowledge it is not).

ARTICLE 9 — REPRESENTATIONS

9.1 Executive Representations. The Executive represents that the Executive is free to enter into this Agreement and that no prior agreement prevents the Executive from performing the Executive's duties hereunder.

9.2 Company Representations. The Company represents that the execution of this Agreement has been duly authorized by the Compensation Committee and the Board of Directors and that this Agreement is a valid and binding obligation of the Company.

10.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

11.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with the Schedules, the equity plan documents, and the Sponsor stockholders agreement, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.

11.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by the Executive and a duly authorized officer of the Company.

11.3 Assignment. The Executive may not assign this Agreement. The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor that assumes the obligations hereunder.

11.4 Survival. Articles 5, 6, 7, 8, and 10 shall survive the termination of the Executive's employment.

11.5 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
