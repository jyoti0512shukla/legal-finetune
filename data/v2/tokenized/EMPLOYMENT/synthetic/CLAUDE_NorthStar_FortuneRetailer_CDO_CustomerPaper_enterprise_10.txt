EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT
[NEGOTIATED EXECUTIVE PAPER]

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing in the State of {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} (the "Executive"). The Company and the Executive are collectively the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Company is a Fortune 500 retail enterprise that has made public commitments to its diversity, equity, and inclusion ("DEI") strategy and seeks to engage the Executive to lead the implementation and oversight of those commitments;

WHEREAS, the Executive is a recognized leader in the field of corporate DEI strategy and brings substantial public reputation, professional networks, and personal brand to the role;

WHEREAS, this Agreement reflects the heavily negotiated terms agreed between the Company and the Executive's counsel and is intended to provide the Executive with appropriate protections for the Executive's reputation and the Company's continued investment in its DEI program;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises, the mutual covenants set forth herein, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Cause" means the Executive's: (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony or any crime involving fraud or moral turpitude; (b) intentional act of fraud or material misappropriation of Company funds; (c) willful and material breach of this Agreement, after written notice from the Board specifying the alleged breach and providing the Executive at least thirty (30) days to cure (where cure is reasonably possible); or (d) willful misconduct that has caused, or is reasonably likely to cause, material reputational harm to the Company. For the avoidance of doubt, the Executive's good-faith DEI program decisions shall not constitute Cause regardless of any backlash from third parties.

1.2 "Code" means the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.

1.3 "DEI Program" means the Company's diversity, equity, and inclusion strategy, programs, initiatives, and budget commitments as in effect on the Effective Date and as may be updated by the Executive in consultation with the Compensation Committee from time to time.

1.4 "Good Reason" means the occurrence, without the Executive's prior written consent, of any of the following: (a) any material adverse change in the Executive's title, authority, duties, or reporting line; (b) any reduction in the Executive's Base Salary or target Annual Bonus opportunity; (c) any material relocation of the Executive's principal place of employment more than 50 miles from {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}; (d) any material reduction in the scope, budget, public visibility, or organizational support of the DEI Program (the "DEI Material Reduction"); or (e) any material breach of this Agreement by the Company. The Executive must give written notice within sixty (60) days, the Company must fail to cure within thirty (30) days, and the Executive must terminate within thirty (30) days after the cure period expires.

ARTICLE 2 — POSITION AND DUTIES

2.1 Title and Reporting. The Company shall employ the Executive as {{EMPLOYEE_TITLE}}, reporting directly to the {{EMPLOYEE_REPORTS_TO}}. The Executive shall not be required to report to any other officer of the Company. The Executive shall have authority over the design, implementation, and oversight of the DEI Program, including budget authority, personnel decisions within the DEI Program team, and policy authority over Company-wide DEI initiatives.

2.2 Board Observer Rights. During the first twelve (12) months following the Effective Date, the Executive shall have the right to attend, as a non-voting observer, all meetings of the Compensation Committee at which DEI Program matters are discussed. After the first twelve (12) months, the Executive shall continue to have the right to be heard by the Compensation Committee at least quarterly on DEI Program progress.

2.3 Full-Time Commitment with Carve-Outs. The Executive shall devote substantially all of the Executive's business time to the affairs of the Company. The Executive may, without the Company's consent, (a) serve on charitable, civic, or educational boards (and the Company encourages such service as consistent with the Executive's DEI mission), (b) deliver keynote speeches, fulfill speaking engagements, write op-eds, and participate in podcasts and panel discussions on DEI topics (subject to the Executive providing the Company with reasonable advance notice), and (c) accept honorary recognitions, fellowships, and academic appointments related to DEI.

2.4 Place of Employment. The Executive's principal place of employment shall be the Company's offices in {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

ARTICLE 3 — TERM AND COMMENCEMENT

3.1 Indefinite Term. The Executive's employment shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated in accordance with Article 6.

ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

4.1 Base Salary. The Company shall pay the Executive an annual base salary of {{EMPLOYEE_BASE_SALARY}} (the "Base Salary"), reviewed annually by the Compensation Committee with potential increases at the Compensation Committee's discretion. The Base Salary may not be decreased.

4.2 Annual Bonus. The Executive shall be eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target opportunity of {{EMPLOYEE_TARGET_BONUS_PCT}}% of the Executive's then-current Base Salary, with maximum bonus opportunity of 200% of target on overachievement. Bonus performance metrics shall be developed jointly by the Executive and the Compensation Committee at the beginning of each fiscal year and shall include a meaningful component tied to DEI Program objectives.

4.3 Initial Equity Grant. As soon as practicable after the Effective Date, the Company shall grant the Executive an initial equity award with an aggregate grant-date fair value of {{EMPLOYEE_INITIAL_EQUITY}}, in the form of restricted stock units, vesting in equal annual installments over four (4) years.

4.4 Two-Year Retention Bonus. The Company shall pay the Executive a retention bonus as follows: (a) Five Hundred Thousand United States Dollars (US$500,000) on the first anniversary of the Effective Date and (b) Five Hundred Thousand United States Dollars (US$500,000) on the second anniversary of the Effective Date, in each case subject to the Executive's continued employment through the relevant payment date or to a qualifying termination under Section 6.4 or Section 5.2.

4.5 Welfare and Retirement Benefits. The Executive shall be entitled to participate in all employee benefit plans of the Company on terms no less favorable than those generally applicable to other senior executives.

4.6 Vacation. The Executive shall be entitled to five (5) weeks of paid vacation per calendar year.

4.7 Reimbursement of Legal Fees. The Company shall reimburse the Executive for reasonable legal fees incurred in connection with the negotiation of this Agreement, up to Fifty Thousand United States Dollars (US$50,000).

4.8 Indemnification. The Company shall indemnify the Executive to the fullest extent permitted by law and shall maintain D&O liability insurance covering the Executive on terms no less favorable than the insurance covering any other senior executive of the Company. This obligation survives termination.

ARTICLE 5 — CHANGE IN CONTROL

5.1 Definition. "Change in Control" means: (a) any Person or "group" (as such term is used in Section 13(d)(3) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934) acquiring beneficial ownership of 50% or more of the combined voting power of the Company's then-outstanding voting securities; (b) the consummation of a merger, consolidation, or similar transaction in which the holders of the Company's voting securities immediately before the transaction do not hold a majority of the voting power of the surviving or resulting entity; or (c) the sale of all or substantially all of the Company's assets.

5.2 Double-Trigger Severance. If, within the period beginning ninety (90) days before and ending twenty-four (24) months after a Change in Control, the Executive's employment is terminated by the Company without Cause or by the Executive for Good Reason, the Executive shall be entitled to the Enhanced Severance set forth in Section 6.5 in lieu of the Ordinary Severance.

5.3 Equity Acceleration on CIC. Upon the occurrence of any Change in Control, all unvested equity awards held by the Executive shall accelerate and become fully vested as of immediately prior to the Change in Control.

ARTICLE 6 — TERMINATION AND SEVERANCE

6.1 Termination Events. The Executive's employment may be terminated as follows: (a) automatically upon the Executive's death; (b) by the Company on the Executive's Disability; (c) by the Company for Cause; (d) by the Company without Cause; (e) by the Executive for Good Reason (including DEI Material Reduction); or (f) by the Executive without Good Reason on sixty (60) days' prior written notice.

6.2 Notice and Cure. Termination by the Company for Cause shall require written notice from the Board specifying the alleged grounds, with at least thirty (30) days to cure (where cure is reasonably possible).

6.3 Accrued Obligations. Upon any termination, the Company shall pay the Accrued Obligations: (a) any Base Salary earned but unpaid; (b) any Annual Bonus earned for a completed fiscal year but unpaid; (c) any unreimbursed business expenses; (d) any accrued but unused vacation; and (e) any vested benefits under any Company benefit plan.

6.4 Ordinary Severance. If the Executive is terminated by the Company without Cause or resigns for Good Reason in circumstances not covered by Section 5.2, the Executive shall be entitled, in addition to the Accrued Obligations and subject to the Executive's execution and non-revocation of a release of claims (in the form of Schedule A) within sixty (60) days after the date of termination, to the following Ordinary Severance:

(a) a lump-sum cash payment equal to twenty-four (24) months of Base Salary;

(b) a lump-sum cash payment equal to two (2) times the Executive's target Annual Bonus;

(c) any unpaid installments of the Retention Bonus, paid on the original schedule;

(d) for a period of twenty-four (24) months following termination, the Company shall pay the Executive a monthly cash amount equal to the COBRA premium charged for continuation coverage for the Executive and the Executive's covered dependents; and

(e) all unvested equity awards that would have vested during the twenty-four (24) months following termination shall accelerate and vest as of the termination date.

6.5 Enhanced Severance (CIC Double-Trigger). If the Executive becomes entitled to severance under Section 5.2, the Enhanced Severance shall consist of: (a) three (3) times Base Salary; (b) three (3) times target Annual Bonus; (c) all unpaid installments of the Retention Bonus paid in a lump sum; (d) thirty-six (36) months of Company-paid COBRA continuation coverage; and (e) full acceleration of all outstanding equity awards.

6.6 No Mitigation; No Offset. The Executive shall not be required to mitigate damages, and severance shall not be offset by any compensation earned from any subsequent employer.

6.7 Section 280G Best-Net Cutback. If any payment to the Executive would constitute a parachute payment subject to the Section 4999 excise tax, the aggregate payments shall be reduced (to the minimum extent necessary) only if such reduction would result in the Executive's receiving, on an after-tax basis, a greater amount than the Executive would have received absent the cutback.

6.8 Section 409A. This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Code.

ARTICLE 7 — POST-EMPLOYMENT NAME, IMAGE, AND REPUTATION

7.1 Mutual Non-Disparagement. The Executive shall not, both during the Executive's employment and at any time thereafter, make any statement that publicly disparages, defames, or impugns the reputation of the Company or any of its officers, directors, employees, or agents. The Company (acting through its officers and directors) shall not, both during the Executive's employment and at any time thereafter, make any statement that publicly disparages, defames, or impugns the reputation of the Executive. This mutual non-disparagement covenant survives termination.

7.2 Right to Discuss DEI Program Achievements. Notwithstanding Section 7.1, the Executive shall retain the right, at any time during or after the Executive's employment, to publicly discuss the Executive's specific contributions to and achievements within the DEI Program, including in books, articles, speeches, podcasts, and academic settings, provided that such discussion (a) does not disclose confidential business information, (b) does not disparage the Company, and (c) is consistent with truthfulness. The Company shall not assert this Agreement to prevent the Executive from describing the Executive's professional accomplishments at the Company.

7.3 Name and Likeness Carve-Out. Without limiting Section 7.2, the Executive retains all rights to use the Executive's name, image, likeness, and personal brand in connection with the Executive's professional career after the Executive's employment with the Company ends, including in academic, advocacy, and consulting roles. The Company may continue to reference the Executive's prior service as Chief Diversity, Equity and Inclusion Officer in historical materials but shall not, without the Executive's written consent, use the Executive's name, image, or likeness in current marketing, advocacy, or recruiting materials after the Executive's employment ends.

7.4 No Restrictive Covenants Beyond Confidentiality and Non-Solicit. The Parties acknowledge that the Executive is not subject to any post-employment non-competition restriction. The Executive's only post-employment restrictions are set forth in this Article 7 and in Article 8 (Confidentiality) and Article 9 (Non-Solicitation).

ARTICLE 8 — CONFIDENTIALITY AND IP

8.1 Confidentiality. The Executive shall hold in strict confidence all non-public information of the Company during and after employment.

8.2 IP Assignment. The Executive assigns to the Company all inventions and works of authorship created during employment that relate to the Company's business or that are developed using Company resources.

8.3 Defend Trade Secrets Act Notice. Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), the Executive is notified that the Executive cannot be held liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official or to an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law.

ARTICLE 9 — NON-SOLICITATION

9.1 Non-Solicitation of Employees. During the Executive's employment and for a period of twelve (12) months following the date of termination of the Executive's employment for any reason, the Executive shall not directly solicit for employment any employee of the Company with whom the Executive had material contact during the twelve (12) months preceding the date of termination, subject to a general-solicitation carve-out. For the avoidance of doubt, this Section 9.1 does not restrict the Executive from continuing professional relationships with current or former Company employees in non-employment contexts (e.g. board service, charitable activities, mentorship).

ARTICLE 10 — REPRESENTATIONS

10.1 Executive Representations. The Executive represents that the Executive is free to enter into this Agreement.

10.2 Company Representations. The Company represents that the execution of this Agreement has been duly authorized by the Compensation Committee.

ARTICLE 11 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

11.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

11.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

11.3 Equitable Relief. Either Party may seek equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 7, Article 8, or Article 9 of this Agreement, without bond and without proving actual damages.

11.4 Attorneys' Fees. In any action arising under this Agreement, the prevailing Party shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs from the non-prevailing Party.

ARTICLE 12 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

12.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

12.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by the Executive and a duly authorized officer of the Company.

12.3 Assignment. The Executive may not assign this Agreement. The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor that assumes the obligations hereunder.

12.4 Survival. Articles 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 11 shall survive the termination of the Executive's employment.

12.5 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
