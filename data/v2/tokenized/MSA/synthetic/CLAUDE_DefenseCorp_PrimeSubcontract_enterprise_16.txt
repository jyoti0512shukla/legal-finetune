FEDERAL PRIME-SUBCONTRACTOR RESEARCH SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Federal Prime-Subcontractor Research Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date"), by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Subcontractor"), having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}}, and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Prime Contractor"), having its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}}. The Subcontractor and the Prime Contractor may each be referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

The Prime Contractor holds one or more prime contracts with United States Government agencies under the Federal Acquisition Regulation ("FAR") and the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement ("DFARS"). The Subcontractor is being engaged to perform research and technology services on a subcontract basis under one or more such prime contracts. The Parties acknowledge that this Agreement is a federal subcontract and is subject to the FAR, the DFARS, and the mandatory flow-down clauses identified in Exhibit A. The mandatory flow-down clauses set forth in Exhibit A are incorporated by reference into this Agreement and shall control over any inconsistent provision of this Agreement. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and any applicable FAR or DFARS clause, federal law, or the terms of the relevant prime contract, the FAR or DFARS clause, federal law, or prime contract term shall control.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Allowable Cost" has the meaning given in FAR Subpart 31.

1.2 "Background IP" means any Intellectual Property Rights owned or licensable by a Party prior to the Effective Date or independently developed outside of any Subcontract Task Order.

1.3 "Controlled Unclassified Information" or "CUI" means information that requires safeguarding pursuant to and consistent with applicable law and government-wide policies.

1.4 "Covered Defense Information" or "CDI" has the meaning given in DFARS 252.204-7012.

1.5 "DCAA" means the Defense Contract Audit Agency.

1.6 "DCMA" means the Defense Contract Management Agency.

1.7 "DFARS" means the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement.

1.8 "Disclosing Party" and "Receiving Party" have the meanings given in Article 9.

1.9 "FAR" means the Federal Acquisition Regulation, 48 C.F.R. Chapter 1.

1.10 "Government Furnished Equipment" or "GFE" means any property furnished by the Government or by the Prime Contractor to the Subcontractor for use in performing the Services.

1.11 "ITAR" means the International Traffic in Arms Regulations.

1.12 "NIST SP 800-171" means the National Institute of Standards and Technology Special Publication 800-171.

1.13 "Prime Contract" means each prime contract between the Prime Contractor and a United States Government customer under which the Subcontractor's Services are required.

1.14 "Services" means the research and technology services to be performed by the Subcontractor for the Prime Contractor under one or more Subcontract Task Orders.

1.15 "Subcontract Task Order" means a written task order issued by the Prime Contractor to the Subcontractor under this Agreement that describes a particular requirement, including the scope, the period of performance, the funding, the contract type (firm-fixed-price, cost-plus-fixed-fee, time-and-materials, or labor-hour), the Key Personnel, the GFE, and any task-specific terms.

1.16 "Work Product" means all deliverables, software, documentation, designs, data, reports, and other tangible work product created by or on behalf of the Subcontractor in the course of performing the Services.

ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 Issuance of Subcontract Task Orders. The Prime Contractor may issue Subcontract Task Orders to the Subcontractor under this Agreement from time to time during the Term, subject to the terms of the relevant Prime Contract.

2.2 Order of Precedence. (a) Applicable federal law, regulations, FAR clauses, and DFARS clauses; (b) the terms of the relevant Prime Contract that flow down to the Subcontractor; (c) the Subcontract Task Order; (d) this Agreement.

2.3 Standard of Performance. The Subcontractor shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the requirements set forth in each Subcontract Task Order, the terms of the relevant Prime Contract that flow down to the Subcontractor, the applicable FAR and DFARS clauses, and the standards of the federal research and technology contracting industry.

2.4 Compliance with Prime Contract. The Subcontractor shall comply with all flow-down obligations imposed by the relevant Prime Contract. The Prime Contractor shall provide the Subcontractor with copies of the flow-down provisions of the Prime Contract upon execution of each Subcontract Task Order.

ARTICLE 3 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Delivery. The Subcontractor shall deliver each Work Product item in accordance with the schedule set forth in the applicable Subcontract Task Order.

3.2 Acceptance. The Prime Contractor shall have the right to inspect and accept or reject each Work Product item in accordance with the procedures set forth in the applicable Subcontract Task Order, FAR Subpart 46.5, and the relevant Prime Contract acceptance procedures.

3.3 Cure of Non-Conformity. If the Prime Contractor rejects a Work Product item, the Subcontractor shall cure the non-conformity at no additional cost within the time period specified in the rejection notice.

3.4 No Deemed Acceptance. There shall be no deemed acceptance of any Work Product item.

ARTICLE 4 — FEES, PAYMENT, AND COST PRINCIPLES

4.1 Fees. The Prime Contractor shall pay the Subcontractor in accordance with the contract type, payment schedule, and ceiling set forth in each Subcontract Task Order, subject to the availability of funding under the relevant Prime Contract.

4.2 Annual Aggregate Reference. The aggregate annual Fees payable by the Prime Contractor across all then-active Subcontract Task Orders as of the Effective Date is approximately {{ANNUAL_FEE_AMOUNT}}, included for reference only.

4.3 FAR Cost Principles. To the extent any Subcontract Task Order is awarded on a cost-reimbursement basis, the Subcontractor shall comply with FAR Subpart 31 (Contract Cost Principles and Procedures) and shall invoice only Allowable Costs. The Subcontractor shall maintain an accounting system adequate to segregate costs by Subcontract Task Order and by cost element, in accordance with the standards of the Defense Contract Audit Agency.

4.4 Indirect Cost Rates. Where the Subcontractor uses indirect cost rates (such as overhead, G&A, fringe), the Subcontractor's indirect cost rates shall be subject to the Prime Contractor's review and to DCAA audit. The Subcontractor shall negotiate provisional billing rates with DCAA or with the cognizant federal agency and shall true-up to actual rates upon settlement of final indirect cost rates.

4.5 DCAA Audit. The Subcontractor's records shall be subject to audit by DCAA, DCMA, the Inspector General, the Government Accountability Office, the Prime Contractor's internal audit personnel, the Government Contracting Officer, and any other federal auditor with jurisdiction over the Prime Contract or this Agreement.

4.6 Limitation of Funds. Where a Subcontract Task Order is incrementally funded, the Subcontractor shall not perform Services in excess of the amount allotted, in accordance with FAR 52.232-22.

4.7 Service Contract Act. Where applicable, the Subcontractor shall comply with the Service Contract Act, 41 U.S.C. §§ 6701-6707, and the Department of Labor wage determinations.

4.8 Prompt Payment. Payment shall be made in accordance with the Prompt Payment Act, 31 U.S.C. §§ 3901-3907, as it applies to subcontractors.

4.9 Taxes. Fees are exclusive of all taxes other than taxes imposed on the Subcontractor's net income.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 Period of Performance. The period of performance of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 13.

5.2 Termination for Convenience. The Prime Contractor may terminate this Agreement, or any Subcontract Task Order, in whole or in part, for convenience in accordance with FAR 52.249-2 (as flowed down by the Prime Contract) or analogous termination clauses. Upon any such termination, the Subcontractor shall be entitled to reimbursement of Allowable Costs incurred prior to termination plus a reasonable profit, in accordance with FAR Subpart 49.

5.3 Termination for Default. The Prime Contractor may terminate this Agreement, or any Subcontract Task Order, for default in accordance with FAR 52.249-8 (as flowed down).

ARTICLE 6 — KEY PERSONNEL AND CLEARANCES

6.1 Key Personnel. Each Subcontract Task Order shall identify the Subcontractor Key Personnel committed to perform the Services.

6.2 Substitution of Key Personnel. The Subcontractor shall not substitute any Key Personnel without the prior written approval of the Prime Contractor (and, where required, the Government Contracting Officer), in accordance with the relevant Prime Contract Key Personnel clauses. Any proposed substitution shall be accompanied by a resume and qualifications for the proposed substitute.

6.3 Security Clearances. Where the Services involve access to classified information or to facilities requiring security clearance, the Subcontractor shall ensure that all personnel performing such Services possess the required security clearance and are eligible to access the relevant classified information at the required level. The Subcontractor shall comply with the National Industrial Security Program Operating Manual (NISPOM).

6.4 Background Investigations. All Subcontractor personnel performing the Services shall undergo background investigations in accordance with the requirements of the applicable Subcontract Task Order, the Prime Contract, and the Prime Contractor's instructions.

6.5 Citizenship. Where the Services involve access to CUI, CDI, ITAR-controlled information, or classified information, only United States citizens may perform such Services, except as expressly permitted by the Prime Contractor and the Government Contracting Officer in writing.

6.6 Removal of Personnel. The Prime Contractor or the Government Contracting Officer may, at any time and for any reason, request that the Subcontractor remove any individual personnel. The Subcontractor shall comply promptly.

ARTICLE 7 — INFORMATION SECURITY, CUI, AND EXPORT CONTROLS

7.1 NIST SP 800-171 Compliance. To the extent the Services involve access to CUI, the Subcontractor shall implement the security requirements of NIST SP 800-171 in its information systems that handle CUI, in accordance with DFARS 252.204-7012 (as flowed down).

7.2 CMMC Compliance. To the extent required by the applicable Subcontract Task Order or by DoD CMMC requirements, the Subcontractor shall maintain the CMMC certification level required by the Subcontract Task Order throughout the period of performance.

7.3 Cyber Incident Reporting. The Subcontractor shall report any "cyber incident" affecting CDI to the Department of Defense at https://dibnet.dod.mil within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery, in accordance with DFARS 252.204-7012(c). The Subcontractor shall simultaneously notify the Prime Contractor of any such cyber incident.

7.4 ITAR and EAR Compliance. The Subcontractor shall comply with the ITAR and the EAR with respect to any defense articles, defense services, technical data, or controlled commodities involved in the performance of this Agreement.

7.5 Government Furnished Equipment. The Subcontractor shall use any GFE solely for the purpose of performing the Services under the applicable Subcontract Task Order. The Subcontractor shall maintain GFE in good condition, shall implement appropriate security controls, and shall return GFE to the Prime Contractor or the Government at the end of the period of performance.

7.6 No Cloud Storage Outside the United States. The Subcontractor shall not store, process, or transmit any CUI, CDI, or other Government information using cloud computing services located outside of the United States.

7.7 Foreign Ownership, Control, or Influence (FOCI). The Subcontractor shall comply with the FOCI requirements of the NISPOM and shall promptly notify the Prime Contractor of any change in foreign ownership, control, or influence.

ARTICLE 8 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

8.1 FAR Data Rights Clauses. The intellectual property rights in any Work Product shall be governed by the FAR data rights clauses (including FAR 52.227-14 (Rights in Data — General), DFARS 252.227-7013 (Rights in Technical Data — Noncommercial Items), DFARS 252.227-7014 (Rights in Noncommercial Computer Software), and any other applicable FAR or DFARS data rights clauses) as flowed down from the Prime Contract.

8.2 Bayh-Dole Act Compliance. To the extent any invention is conceived or first reduced to practice in the performance of the Services using federal funds, the Subcontractor shall comply with the Bayh-Dole Act (35 U.S.C. §§ 200-212) and the implementing regulations at 37 C.F.R. Part 401, including the disclosure, election, and reporting requirements with respect to "subject inventions."

8.3 Government Purpose Rights. Unless the Subcontract Task Order specifies otherwise, the Government shall have the rights in Work Product specified in the applicable FAR or DFARS data rights clauses, which may include "unlimited rights," "limited rights," "Government purpose rights," "restricted rights," or such other rights as the FAR or DFARS specifies.

8.4 Subcontractor's Pre-Existing IP. The Subcontractor shall retain ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property to the extent permitted by the applicable FAR data rights clauses. The Subcontractor shall mark any deliverable containing pre-existing IP with the appropriate restrictive markings.

8.5 No Implied Licenses. Except as expressly granted by the FAR data rights clauses or the applicable Subcontract Task Order, no rights or licenses are granted by either Party to the other Party.

ARTICLE 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Confidentiality Obligations. Each Party (the "Receiving Party") shall (a) hold the other Party's (the "Disclosing Party's") Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use such Confidential Information solely for purposes of this Agreement; (c) not disclose it except to its personnel who have a need to know and are bound by obligations of confidentiality; and (d) protect it with at least reasonable care.

9.2 Government Information. Confidential Information includes any CUI, CDI, classified information, or other Government information received by the Subcontractor from the Prime Contractor or the Government in connection with this Agreement.

9.3 Compelled Disclosure. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by court order, applicable law, or by a Government audit or inspection right.

9.4 Survival. The obligations in this Article 9 shall survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement indefinitely or for as long as required by applicable law and the relevant Prime Contract.

ARTICLE 10 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

10.1 Mutual Warranties. Each Party represents and warrants that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement.

10.2 Subcontractor Warranties. The Subcontractor represents, warrants, and covenants that (a) the Services shall be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner; (b) the Subcontractor shall comply with all applicable federal laws, regulations, FAR clauses, DFARS clauses, and the flow-down obligations of the relevant Prime Contract; (c) the Subcontractor shall maintain all required security clearances, NIST SP 800-171 compliance, and CMMC certifications throughout the period of performance; (d) the Subcontractor is not currently debarred, suspended, or proposed for debarment from federal contracting; (e) the Subcontractor has no organizational conflict of interest that has not been disclosed to and mitigated in coordination with the Prime Contractor and the Government Contracting Officer; (f) the Subcontractor's accounting system is adequate for cost-reimbursement contracting under DCAA standards; and (g) the Subcontractor shall promptly notify the Prime Contractor of any change in its debarment, suspension, or OCI status.

10.3 Prime Contractor Reservation of Rights. Nothing in this Article 10 shall be construed to waive any rights or remedies of the Prime Contractor or the Government under the FAR, the DFARS, or applicable federal law.

10.4 Disclaimer. EXCEPT FOR THE EXPRESS WARRANTIES SET FORTH IN THIS ARTICLE 10 AND IN THE APPLICABLE FAR AND DFARS CLAUSES, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED ON AN "AS IS" BASIS. THE SUBCONTRACTOR DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW.

ARTICLE 11 — INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 Subcontractor Indemnity. The Subcontractor shall hold harmless the Prime Contractor against any third-party claim arising from (a) the Subcontractor's negligent acts or omissions; (b) the Subcontractor's breach of this Agreement, the relevant Prime Contract flow-down obligations, or applicable federal law; (c) the Subcontractor's breach of the security obligations in Article 7; or (d) any infringement claim alleging that the Subcontractor's Work Product infringes any third-party intellectual property right.

11.2 Limitation by Federal Law. The Parties acknowledge that any indemnification obligation under this Article 11 is limited to the extent permitted by applicable federal law and the relevant Prime Contract. Nothing in this Article 11 shall be construed as a waiver of sovereign immunity or as creating any indemnification obligation of the Government.

ARTICLE 12 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 Limitation in Accordance with FAR. The Subcontractor's liability under this Agreement is limited as set forth in the applicable FAR clauses, including FAR 52.246-25 where applicable.

12.2 Aggregate Cap. EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3 OR AS LIMITED BY THE APPLICABLE FAR CLAUSES, IN NO EVENT SHALL THE AGGREGATE LIABILITY OF THE SUBCONTRACTOR ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY THE PRIME CONTRACTOR TO THE SUBCONTRACTOR UNDER THE AFFECTED SUBCONTRACT TASK ORDER DURING THE {{LIABILITY_CAP_MONTHS}} MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY.

12.3 Exceptions to Limitations. The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 shall not apply to (a) the Subcontractor's obligations under Article 11; (b) any breach of Article 9 (Confidentiality); (c) any breach of Article 7 (Information Security, CUI, and Export Controls); (d) any liability arising from gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud; (e) any liability arising from violation of NIST SP 800-171, DFARS 252.204-7012, the ITAR, or the EAR; (f) any debarment-eligible misconduct; or (g) any liability that, under applicable federal law, cannot be limited.

12.4 Indirect Damages. EXCEPT AS PROVIDED BY APPLICABLE FAR CLAUSES OR FEDERAL LAW, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER PARTY FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, EXEMPLARY, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

ARTICLE 13 — TERMINATION

13.1 Termination for Convenience. The Prime Contractor may terminate this Agreement or any Subcontract Task Order for convenience in accordance with FAR 52.249-2 (as flowed down).

13.2 Termination for Default. The Prime Contractor may terminate for default in accordance with FAR 52.249-8 (as flowed down).

13.3 Stop Work Order. The Prime Contractor or the Government Contracting Officer may issue a stop work order in accordance with FAR 52.242-15 (as flowed down).

13.4 Termination of the Prime Contract. If the Prime Contract is terminated by the Government, this Agreement and all Subcontract Task Orders may be terminated by the Prime Contractor without penalty, subject to payment for work performed prior to termination.

13.5 Effects of Termination. Upon termination, (a) the Subcontractor shall promptly cease performance as directed; (b) the Subcontractor shall deliver to the Prime Contractor all Work Product completed or in progress; (c) the Subcontractor shall return GFE; (d) the Subcontractor shall be entitled to reimbursement of Allowable Costs in accordance with the applicable FAR termination clauses; and (e) the provisions that by their nature are intended to survive shall so survive.

ARTICLE 14 — GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

14.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the federal laws of the United States. To the extent any matter is not addressed by federal law, the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}} shall apply.

14.2 Disputes. Disputes between the Parties shall be resolved in accordance with the Disputes clause set forth in the relevant Prime Contract (which generally invokes the Contract Disputes Act, 41 U.S.C. §§ 7101-7109). Where applicable, the Subcontractor shall be entitled to assert claims through the Prime Contractor in accordance with FAR 52.233-1 (Disputes).

14.3 Venue. Any judicial proceeding shall be brought in the United States Court of Federal Claims or the United States district court located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

14.4 Continued Performance. Pending final resolution of any dispute, the Subcontractor shall continue to perform the Services in accordance with the Prime Contractor's direction.

ARTICLE 15 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

15.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with all incorporated FAR and DFARS clauses, the relevant Prime Contract flow-down obligations, and all Subcontract Task Orders, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

15.2 Order of Precedence. As set forth in Section 2.2.

15.3 Amendments. No amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and executed by both Parties.

15.4 Severability. If any provision is held to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

15.5 Waiver. No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

15.6 Notices. Formal notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth in the applicable Subcontract Task Order.

15.7 Independent Contractor. The Subcontractor is an independent contractor of the Prime Contractor.

15.8 Subcontracting. The Subcontractor shall not further subcontract any of the Services without the prior written consent of the Prime Contractor and (where required) the Government Contracting Officer. Any approved sub-subcontract shall include the mandatory flow-down clauses required by the applicable FAR and DFARS clauses.

15.9 Assignment. This Agreement may not be assigned by the Subcontractor except in accordance with FAR Subpart 42.12.

15.10 Anti-Kickback. The Subcontractor shall comply with the Anti-Kickback Act of 1986, 41 U.S.C. §§ 8701-8707.

15.11 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.

15.12 Headings. Headings are for convenience of reference only.
