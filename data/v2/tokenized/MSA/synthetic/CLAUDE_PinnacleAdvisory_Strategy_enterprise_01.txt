This Master Consulting Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date"), by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} ("Consultant"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} ("Client"). ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means, with respect to any entity, any other entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such entity, where "control" means the ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities or the power to direct the management and policies of such entity.

1.2 "Background IP" means any Intellectual Property Rights owned, controlled, or licensable (other than under this Agreement) by a Party prior to the Effective Date or independently developed by such Party outside of any Statement of Work, including without limitation methodologies, frameworks, models, templates, software tools, benchmarks, and proprietary databases.

1.3 "Client Materials" means any data, information, documents, records, materials, software, hardware, facilities, or other resources made available by or on behalf of Client to Consultant in connection with the performance of any Statement of Work.

1.4 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party") that is identified as confidential at the time of disclosure or that, given the nature of the information and the circumstances surrounding its disclosure, would reasonably be understood to be confidential. Confidential Information of Client includes Client Materials, Client business plans and financial information, Client customer information, and the existence and contents of any Statement of Work. Confidential Information of Consultant includes Consultant Background IP, Consultant pricing and rate information, and Consultant proprietary methodologies.

1.5 "Deliverables" means the tangible work product, reports, presentations, models, software code, recommendations, and other items that Consultant is required to deliver to Client under a Statement of Work.

1.6 "Engagement" means a discrete consulting engagement performed by Consultant for Client pursuant to an executed Statement of Work.

1.7 "Intellectual Property Rights" means all intellectual property and proprietary rights of any kind anywhere in the world, including without limitation patents, copyrights, moral rights, trademarks, trade dress, trade secrets, know-how, database rights, and all other rights of a similar nature, whether registered or unregistered.

1.8 "Key Personnel" means the specific individual personnel of Consultant identified as Key Personnel on a Statement of Work, who are committed to perform the relevant Engagement.

1.9 "Services" means the consulting services to be performed by Consultant for Client under one or more Statements of Work.

1.10 "Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a written document executed by both Parties that describes a particular Engagement, including the Services to be performed, the Deliverables to be delivered, the Key Personnel assigned, the Fees, the schedule, the acceptance criteria, and any project-specific terms.

1.11 "Work Product" means all Deliverables and all other work product, materials, ideas, concepts, designs, models, code, documentation, and inventions created, conceived, or first reduced to practice by or on behalf of Consultant in the course of performing any Engagement, in each case excluding Background IP.

ARTICLE 2 — SERVICES AND SCOPE

2.1 Engagement of Services. Consultant shall provide the Services described in each Statement of Work executed by the Parties from time to time during the Term. This Agreement governs all such Statements of Work and the performance of all Services thereunder.

2.2 Statements of Work. Each Statement of Work shall describe in reasonable detail (a) the scope of the Services to be performed; (b) the Deliverables to be produced; (c) the Key Personnel assigned to the Engagement; (d) the Fees and the basis on which they are calculated (whether time and materials, fixed fee, milestone-based, or otherwise); (e) the schedule for performance of the Services and delivery of the Deliverables; (f) the acceptance criteria for the Deliverables; and (g) any other terms specific to the Engagement. Each Statement of Work shall be executed by an authorized representative of each Party and shall be incorporated by reference into this Agreement.

2.3 Order of Precedence. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and a Statement of Work, this Agreement shall control as to the matters expressly addressed in this Agreement, except that the Statement of Work may expressly modify any provision of this Agreement solely as it applies to that specific Engagement, in which case the Statement of Work shall control as to the modified provision and only as it applies to that specific Engagement.

2.4 No Obligation to Engage. Nothing in this Agreement shall obligate Client to engage Consultant for any specific Engagement, and nothing in this Agreement shall obligate Consultant to accept any specific Engagement. Each Engagement requires the mutual execution of a Statement of Work.

2.5 Standard of Performance. Consultant shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the highest standards of the management consulting industry, by individuals possessing the qualifications, skills, and expertise required to perform the Services described in the applicable Statement of Work. Consultant shall comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations in performing the Services.

2.6 Cooperation. Client shall provide Consultant with access to such Client Materials, personnel, facilities, and information as Consultant reasonably requires to perform the Services. Consultant shall not be responsible for delays in performance to the extent caused by Client's failure to provide such access in a timely manner.

ARTICLE 3 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Delivery. Consultant shall deliver each Deliverable in accordance with the schedule and in the format set forth in the applicable Statement of Work.

3.2 Acceptance. Client shall have a period of fifteen (15) business days following delivery of each Deliverable (the "Acceptance Period") to review the Deliverable and notify Consultant in writing of (a) Client's acceptance of the Deliverable, or (b) Client's rejection of the Deliverable, accompanied by a reasonably detailed written description of the alleged non-conformity with the acceptance criteria set forth in the applicable Statement of Work.

3.3 Cure of Non-Conformity. If Client rejects a Deliverable pursuant to Section 3.2, Consultant shall, at no additional charge to Client, use commercially reasonable efforts to correct the alleged non-conformity and resubmit the corrected Deliverable to Client within fifteen (15) business days of receipt of Client's rejection notice. Client shall then have a new Acceptance Period to review the resubmitted Deliverable. If Consultant fails to correct the non-conformity after two (2) cycles of cure, Client may, at its option, (a) accept the Deliverable in its then-current form with an equitable reduction in the Fees attributable to such Deliverable, or (b) terminate the affected Statement of Work and recover any Fees paid for the rejected Deliverable.

3.4 Deemed Acceptance. If Client fails to provide Consultant with written acceptance or rejection of a Deliverable within the applicable Acceptance Period, the Deliverable shall be deemed accepted by Client.

3.5 Payment Tied to Acceptance. Where the applicable Statement of Work provides for milestone-based or Deliverable-based payment, Consultant shall not invoice Client for the Fees attributable to a specific Deliverable until such Deliverable has been accepted (or deemed accepted) by Client.

ARTICLE 4 — FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fees. Client shall pay Consultant the Fees set forth in each Statement of Work. The basis for Fees may include time and materials at the hourly rates set forth in the applicable Statement of Work, fixed fees, milestone-based fees, or any other basis the Parties agree to in the Statement of Work.

4.2 Annual Aggregate Reference. The aggregate annual Fees payable by Client across all then-active Statements of Work as of the Effective Date is approximately {{ANNUAL_FEE_AMOUNT}}, included in this Agreement for reference only and updated by reference to the Statements of Work then in effect from time to time.

4.3 Most Favored Nations Pricing. Consultant represents and warrants that the hourly rates and fixed fees set forth in each Statement of Work are no less favorable than the hourly rates and fixed fees offered by Consultant to any other client of similar size, complexity, and engagement profile during the same calendar year. If Consultant offers more favorable rates to any such other client during the Term, Consultant shall promptly notify Client in writing and shall offer Client such more favorable rates for the remainder of the affected Statement of Work.

4.4 Expenses. Reasonable, pre-approved travel and out-of-pocket expenses incurred by Consultant in performing the Services shall be reimbursed by Client at cost, without markup, subject to Client's then-current travel and expense policy and accompanied by reasonable documentation. No expense in excess of One Thousand United States Dollars ($1,000) shall be reimbursable unless pre-approved in writing by Client.

4.5 Invoicing. Consultant shall invoice Client monthly in arrears for Services performed and expenses incurred during the preceding month. Each invoice shall describe the Services performed, the time spent (where Fees are based on time and materials), the personnel performing the Services, the Deliverables completed, and the expenses incurred.

4.6 Payment Terms. All undisputed invoices shall be due and payable within forty-five (45) days of the date of invoice.

4.7 Disputed Invoices. If Client in good faith disputes any portion of an invoice, Client shall notify Consultant in writing of the dispute within twenty (20) business days, and shall pay all undisputed amounts in accordance with Section 4.6. The Parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve any disputed amount.

4.8 Audit Right. Client shall have the right, upon at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than once per twelve-month period, to audit Consultant's records (including time records, expense reports, and supporting documentation) for any Services billed on a time-and-materials basis or any expenses billed under Section 4.4. If any such audit reveals an overcharge in excess of five percent (5%), Consultant shall reimburse Client for the reasonable costs of the audit in addition to refunding the overcharged amounts.

4.9 Taxes. Fees are exclusive of all taxes other than taxes imposed on Consultant's net income.

5.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 13.

5.2 Renewal. ARTICLE 6 — KEY PERSONNEL AND STAFFING

6.1 Key Personnel. Each Statement of Work shall identify the Key Personnel of Consultant who are committed to perform the Services under that Statement of Work. The Parties acknowledge that the identity, expertise, and availability of the Key Personnel are material considerations in Client's decision to enter into the applicable Statement of Work.

6.2 No Replacement of Key Personnel Without Consent. Consultant shall not remove, reassign, or replace any individual designated as Key Personnel on a Statement of Work without Client's prior written consent, except in the case of (a) the death or disability of the Key Personnel, (b) the resignation of the Key Personnel from Consultant, or (c) the termination of the Key Personnel by Consultant for cause. In any such case, Consultant shall propose a replacement of comparable seniority, expertise, and experience within ten (10) business days, and Client shall have the right to interview the proposed replacement and to reject any proposed replacement on reasonable grounds.

6.3 Removal of Personnel on Client Request. Client may, at any time and for any reason that does not violate applicable employment law, request that Consultant remove any individual personnel from the performance of the Services. Consultant shall, within five (5) business days of such request, remove the requested individual and propose a replacement of comparable seniority, expertise, and experience.

6.4 Background Checks and Training. Consultant shall ensure that all personnel performing the Services have undergone background checks reasonably acceptable to Client and have received training on Client's data security and confidentiality policies before commencing work.

6.5 Non-Solicitation of Personnel. During the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months thereafter, Client shall not solicit or hire any Key Personnel of Consultant who performed Services for Client; provided, however, that this restriction shall not apply to (a) general public employment advertising not directed at Consultant personnel, or (b) any individual whose employment with Consultant has terminated for at least six (6) months prior to the date of solicitation.

ARTICLE 7 — CLIENT MATERIALS AND DATA

7.1 Ownership of Client Materials. As between the Parties, Client shall retain all right, title, and interest in and to Client Materials. Consultant acknowledges that Client Materials are the exclusive property of Client.

7.2 Limited License to Client Materials. Client hereby grants Consultant a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license to use Client Materials solely as necessary to perform the Services in accordance with the applicable Statement of Work. Consultant shall not use Client Materials for any other purpose.

7.3 Return of Client Materials. Upon completion of the relevant Engagement or upon Client's earlier written request, Consultant shall return or destroy all Client Materials in its possession or control, except to the extent that retention is required by applicable law or by Consultant's bona fide internal records retention policies, in which case the retained Client Materials shall remain subject to the obligations of this Agreement.

7.4 Data Security. Consultant shall implement and maintain reasonable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Client Materials against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Such safeguards shall include encryption of Client Materials in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, encryption of Client Materials at rest using AES-256 where stored on Consultant's systems, role-based access controls limiting access to Consultant personnel with a need to know, and a documented information security program reviewed annually.

7.5 Breach Notification. Consultant shall notify Client in writing of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to or disclosure of Client Materials without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery.

ARTICLE 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Confidentiality Obligations. Each Party agrees that it shall (a) hold the other Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use the other Party's Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing its obligations or exercising its rights under this Agreement; (c) not disclose the other Party's Confidential Information to any third party except to its employees, contractors, and advisors who have a legitimate need to know and who are bound by written obligations of confidentiality at least as protective as those set forth in this Article 9; and (d) protect the other Party's Confidential Information using the same degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar importance, but in no event less than reasonable care.

9.2 Exclusions. The obligations in Section 9.1 shall not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate (a) was already known without obligation of confidentiality; (b) was independently developed without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; (c) was rightfully received from a third party without obligation of confidentiality; or (d) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party.

9.3 Compelled Disclosure. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by court order or applicable law, provided that the Receiving Party (a) gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of the requirement, to the extent legally permitted, before making such disclosure; (b) cooperates with the Disclosing Party in any reasonable effort to obtain a protective order; and (c) discloses only that portion of Confidential Information that is legally required.

9.4 Equitable Relief. The Parties acknowledge that any breach of this Article 9 may cause irreparable harm to the Disclosing Party for which monetary damages would be inadequate, and accordingly the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive and other equitable relief without the necessity of proving actual damages or posting bond.

9.5 Survival. The obligations in this Article 9 shall survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years, except that the obligations with respect to trade secrets shall survive for so long as such information continues to qualify as a trade secret under applicable law.

Consultant shall defend Client and its officers, directors, employees, and agents (each a "Client Indemnified Party") against any third-party claim, suit, action, or proceeding (each a "Claim") alleging that (a) any Deliverable, when used by Client in accordance with this Agreement, infringes any Intellectual Property Right of such third party; or (b) Consultant's performance of the Services caused bodily injury or property damage to such third party, in each case to the extent caused by Consultant's negligent acts or omissions or willful misconduct. 11.2 Client Indemnity. Client shall indemnify Consultant for any damages, settlement amounts, and reasonable attorneys' fees finally awarded against Consultant with respect to such Claim.

11.3 Indemnification Procedure. The Party seeking indemnification shall (a) provide the indemnifying Party with prompt written notice of the Claim; (b) grant the indemnifying Party sole control over the defense and settlement of the Claim, provided that the indemnifying Party shall not enter into any settlement that imposes any liability or obligation on the indemnified Party without the indemnified Party's prior written consent (not to be unreasonably withheld); and (c) provide reasonable cooperation and assistance at the indemnifying Party's expense.

If any Deliverable becomes the subject of an infringement claim, Consultant may, at its option, (a) procure for Client the right to continue using the Deliverable; (b) modify the Deliverable to be non-infringing without materially diminishing its functionality or value; (c) replace the Deliverable with a non-infringing equivalent; or (d) accept return of the Deliverable and refund the Fees paid for such Deliverable.

ARTICLE 13 — TERMINATION

13.1 Termination for Material Breach. Either Party may terminate this Agreement, or any Statement of Work, upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party if the other Party commits a material breach of this Agreement or the affected Statement of Work and fails to cure such breach within the thirty (30) day notice period. Termination of an individual Statement of Work for material breach shall not, by itself, terminate this Agreement or any other Statement of Work, unless expressly stated in the notice of termination.

13.2 Termination for Convenience. Client may terminate this Agreement, or any Statement of Work, for convenience at any time upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Consultant. Upon any such termination for convenience, Client shall pay Consultant for (a) all Services performed and Deliverables accepted (or deemed accepted) through the effective date of termination, and (b) any non-cancellable third-party commitments incurred by Consultant in connection with the affected Statement of Work prior to receipt of the termination notice.

13.3 Termination for Insolvency. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party (a) becomes insolvent; (b) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors; (c) files a voluntary petition in bankruptcy; or (d) has an involuntary petition in bankruptcy filed against it that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.4 Effects of Termination. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement or any Statement of Work: (a) Consultant shall promptly cease performance of Services under the affected Statement of Work; (b) Consultant shall deliver to Client all Work Product completed or in progress as of the effective date of termination, in a form reasonably useful to Client; (c) Consultant shall return Client Materials in accordance with Section 7.3; (d) Client shall pay Consultant for accepted Services and Deliverables and for non-cancellable commitments as described in Section 13.2; (e) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information in accordance with Section 9; and (f) the provisions of this Agreement that by their nature are intended to survive termination, including Articles 1, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, and 14, shall so survive.

13.5 Wind-Down Assistance. Following termination of this Agreement or any Statement of Work for any reason, Consultant shall, at Client's request and at Consultant's then-current professional services rates (or, in the case of termination by Client for Consultant's material breach, at no additional charge), provide reasonable wind-down assistance for a period of up to ninety (90) days, including the orderly transfer of in-progress Work Product, the transfer of Client Materials, and reasonable knowledge transfer to Client or its successor consultant.
