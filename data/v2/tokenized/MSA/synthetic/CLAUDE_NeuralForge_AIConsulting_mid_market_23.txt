ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 The "Services" are the AI and ML consulting services we provide under each Statement of Work.

1.2 A "Statement of Work" or "SOW" is a document we both sign that describes a specific engagement — the goals, the data, the deliverables, the team, the fees, and any project-specific terms.

1.3 "Customer Data" is your data we use to train, evaluate, or run a Model — including your training datasets, your validation sets, and any data your applications send to a deployed Model for inference.

1.4 "Customer Models" are the models we train, fine-tune, or build for you under a SOW — including the model weights, the model architecture, the training code, and the inference code.

1.5 "Background IP" is what each of us already owns going in. Our Background IP includes our internal training infrastructure, our evaluation harnesses, our base ML libraries, our methodologies, and any open source software we contribute to.

1.6 "Foundation Models" are third-party large language or vision models we may fine-tune or call on your behalf.

ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 SOW model. We do work for you only under signed SOWs.

2.2 Order of precedence. This Agreement controls except where the SOW expressly modifies as it applies to the relevant engagement.

2.3 What we promise. We do the work in a professional, workmanlike manner using current best practices in machine learning.

2.4 What you do. You give us reasonable access to Customer Data, your existing infrastructure, and your stakeholders. If you do not, we are not responsible for delays.

2.5 No model performance guarantees. ML model performance depends on data quality, problem framing, and many factors outside any consultant's control. The SOW will set expected target metrics; we will use commercially reasonable efforts to meet them but we do not guarantee any specific accuracy, precision, recall, AUC, latency, or other performance metric unless the SOW expressly says so.

ARTICLE 3 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Delivery. We deliver each Customer Model and related Deliverables on the schedule and in the format the SOW says.

3.2 Acceptance criteria. Each SOW lists the acceptance criteria — including any model performance metrics on a holdout dataset.

3.3 Acceptance Period. You have fifteen (15) business days from delivery to test against the acceptance criteria and tell us in writing whether you accept or want changes.

3.4 Cure. If you reject, we use commercially reasonable efforts at no additional charge to address the issue within fifteen (15) business days. After two cycles, you can either accept with a fair fee reduction or terminate the affected SOW.

3.5 Deemed acceptance. If you do not respond within the Acceptance Period, the deliverable is deemed accepted.

3.6 Out-of-scope changes. New use cases, new datasets, or new performance targets need a written change order signed by both of us.

4.6 Disputes. If you think a charge is wrong, tell us within 15 business days. Pay the parts you do not dispute on time.

4.7 Taxes. Fees do not include taxes.

ARTICLE 7 — CUSTOMER DATA AND PRIVACY

7.1 You own Customer Data. You retain all rights to Customer Data.

7.2 Why we touch Customer Data. We use Customer Data only to perform the Services for you under the SOW — training your Customer Model, evaluating it, deploying it, and serving inference requests.

7.3 No training on Customer Data for anyone else. We will not use Customer Data to train any model for any other client, to train our own commercial models, or to contribute to any aggregated training dataset. The only models that touch Customer Data are the Customer Models we build for you.

7.4 No selling. We will not sell Customer Data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any commercial purpose other than the engagement.

7.5 No sharing with Foundation Model providers for training. Where we call a Foundation Model on your behalf, we route the call so the Foundation Model provider does not retain your input or use it to train. We have negotiated this with the providers we use. If a Foundation Model whose terms do not give this protection becomes necessary for your use case, we will tell you in writing first and get your approval.

7.6 Sensitive data. Where Customer Data includes personal information, PHI, payment card data, or other sensitive information, the SOW will list any specific handling requirements (HIPAA, PCI DSS, etc.) and we will follow them.

7.7 Security. We protect Customer Data with TLS 1.2 in transit, AES-256 at rest, role-based access controls in our training infrastructure, multi-factor authentication, an annual SOC 2 Type II audit, and a documented information security program reviewed annually.

7.8 Breach notification. If we discover a security incident affecting Customer Data, we will tell you in writing within 72 hours of discovery.

7.9 Data residency. We process Customer Data in our United States cloud regions by default. EU and APAC regions are available as an extra-cost option.

7.10 Subprocessors. The current subprocessor list is at neuralforge.ai/subprocessors and includes our cloud provider, our experiment tracking provider, our error tracking provider, and the Foundation Model providers you have approved.

7.11 Return on cancellation. When the engagement ends, we return Customer Data and the Customer Models to you, then delete our copies (except for archival in normal records retention).

8.1 Background IP. Each side keeps its Background IP.

8.2 Customer Models are yours. Subject to Section 8.3 and to you paying the fees, we assign to you all rights in the Customer Models we build for you under each SOW — the model weights, the model architecture, the training code, the evaluation code, the inference code, and the documentation. To the maximum extent permitted by copyright law, the Customer Models are "work made for hire" for you. To the extent they are not, we hereby assign them to you.

8.3 License to Background IP. If a Customer Model uses some of our Background IP (for example, our internal training pipeline or our evaluation harness), we grant you a perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use that Background IP solely as embedded in or necessary to operate the Customer Model.

8.4 Foundation Model IP. Foundation Models are owned by their respective providers and are used under those providers' terms. We do not transfer ownership of any Foundation Model. Where you fine-tune a Foundation Model, the resulting fine-tuned weights are yours, subject to the underlying Foundation Model provider's terms of use.

8.5 Output IP. Outputs from a Customer Model that you generate using your inference traffic belong to you, to the extent the law permits ownership of model outputs. We make no warranty that any specific model output is original or non-infringing — you should review outputs before using them in production.

8.6 Customer Data is not training material for our other work. As provided in Section 7.3, Customer Data is not used to train models for anyone else. Improvements to our Background IP that we develop based on general lessons from running engagements (and that do not include any of Customer Data, any Customer Model architecture, or anything Customer-specific) belong to us.

8.7 Feedback. If you tell us how to improve our process or our work, we can use that feedback. We will not use Customer Data in connection with feedback.

8.8 No implied licenses. You only get the rights this Agreement actually grants.
