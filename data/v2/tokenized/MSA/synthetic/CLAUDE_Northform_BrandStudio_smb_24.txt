NORTHFORM — STUDIO AGREEMENT

This is the Northform Studio Agreement (the "Agreement"). It is between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Studio," "we," or "our"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Client," "you"). It starts on {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}.

We are a brand design studio. We make brand systems, identity, websites, packaging, and editorial design. You hire us through Statements of Work for specific projects. We try to keep our paperwork short and human.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 The "Services" are the brand and design services we provide under each Statement of Work.

1.2 A "Statement of Work" or "SOW" is a document we both sign that describes a specific project — the scope, the deliverables, the timeline, the team, the fees, and any project-specific terms.

1.3 "Deliverables" are the things we hand over to you under a SOW — logos, brand systems, design files, websites, packaging artwork, editorial layouts, and so on.

1.4 "Background IP" is what each of us already owns going in. Our Background IP includes our internal templates, our methodologies, our brand-development frameworks, and our design systems we built before this engagement.

1.5 "Client Materials" are things you give us to work with — your existing brand assets, your product information, your photography, your messaging.

1.6 "Third-Party Materials" are things owned by someone else that we license on your behalf for the project — fonts, stock photography, illustrations, sound, software.

ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 SOW model. We do work for you only under signed SOWs.

2.2 Order of precedence. As described in the preamble.

2.3 What we promise. We do the work in a professional, workmanlike manner with the craft and taste you expect from a brand studio.

2.4 What you do. You give us reasonable access to your brand context, your stakeholders, your reference materials, and your timely feedback.

2.5 No exclusivity. We can work with other clients (subject to the conflict rule below). You can work with other studios.

2.6 Conflicts. We will not knowingly take on a direct competitor of yours during an active SOW without telling you first.

ARTICLE 3 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Delivery. We deliver each Deliverable on the schedule and in the format the SOW says — typically as editable design files (Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, etc.) plus exported assets.

3.2 Acceptance Period. You have ten (10) business days from delivery to review and tell us in writing whether you accept or want changes. If you want changes, give us a reasonably specific list.

3.3 Cure. If you ask for changes, we will make them at no additional charge if they are within the original scope of the SOW. The SOW will specify how many rounds of revisions are included; additional rounds may carry additional fees.

3.4 Deemed acceptance. If you do not respond within the Acceptance Period, the Deliverable is deemed accepted.

3.5 Out-of-scope changes. New deliverables, complete redesigns, or scope expansions need a written change order signed by both of us.

3.6 Final files. Once a Deliverable is accepted, we deliver the final source files to you (in the agreed editable format) along with exported assets in the formats the SOW lists.

ARTICLE 4 — FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fees. You pay us the fees in each SOW. Fees are usually fixed-fee per project; some SOWs may use hourly time-and-materials or retainer.

4.2 Annual aggregate. The total fees you pay us across all active SOWs as of the Effective Date is roughly {{ANNUAL_FEE_AMOUNT}}, included for reference only.

4.3 Third-Party Materials. We license fonts, stock photography, and other Third-Party Materials on your behalf when the SOW calls for it. We pass through the cost at cost (no markup) and submit the receipts. We will get your written approval before licensing any Third-Party Material that costs more than Five Hundred United States Dollars ($500).

4.4 Net 30. We invoice on the milestones in the SOW. Late payments accrue 1% per month or the maximum allowed by law, whichever is less.

4.5 Disputes. If you think a charge is wrong, tell us within 15 business days. Pay the parts you do not dispute on time.

4.6 Taxes. Fees do not include taxes.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 Initial Term. This Agreement runs for {{TERM_YEARS}} years from the Effective Date.

5.2 Renewal. After the Initial Term, this Agreement auto-renews for one-year periods. Either of us can stop renewal by giving written notice at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days before the current term ends.

5.3 You can cancel any active SOW for convenience on 30 days notice. We will stop work and bill you for everything completed up to the cancellation date plus any non-cancellable third-party costs.

ARTICLE 6 — TEAM AND STAFFING

6.1 Designated team. Each SOW lists the lead designers — typically the creative director and the project lead.

6.2 No swapping. We will not swap out designated team members without telling you first.

6.3 Removal on your request. If you want us to take someone off your project, ask. We will do it within five business days.

6.4 Background checks. We run background checks on all our employees consistent with our standard practice.

6.5 Non-solicitation. While we are working together and for 12 months after, you will not directly hire or solicit our designers who worked on your project (general public job ads do not count).

ARTICLE 7 — CLIENT MATERIALS AND DATA

7.1 You own Client Materials. Your existing brand assets, your photography, your product info — you own all of it.

7.2 Limited use. We use Client Materials only to do the project for you under the SOWs.

7.3 No selling, no AI training. We will not sell Client Materials or use them to train any artificial intelligence model.

7.4 Security. We protect Client Materials with TLS in transit, encryption at rest in our project management tools, and a documented information security program.

7.5 Breach notification. If we have a security incident affecting Client Materials, we will tell you in writing within 72 hours.

7.6 Return on cancellation. When the engagement ends, we return Client Materials and the Deliverables.

ARTICLE 8 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

8.1 Background IP. Each side keeps its Background IP. We are not transferring our internal templates, our methodologies, or our brand-development frameworks.

8.2 Work Product is yours. Subject to Section 8.3 and to you paying the fees, we assign to you all rights in the Deliverables we create specifically for you under each SOW. To the maximum extent permitted by copyright law, the Deliverables are "work made for hire" for you. To the extent they are not, we hereby assign them to you.

8.3 License to Background IP. If a Deliverable contains some of our Background IP, we grant you a perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use that Background IP solely as embedded in the Deliverable.

8.4 Third-Party Materials. Third-Party Materials in a Deliverable come with the rights granted by the original licensor — those rights pass through to you under the original licensor's terms. If a Third-Party Material has a use limit (medium, term, market), we will tell you in writing before delivery and note it in the file naming.

8.5 Portfolio rights. We can show the work in our portfolio, on our website, in case studies, and in award submissions. If there is anything we should not show (or a confidentiality window we should respect), tell us.

8.6 Feedback. If you tell us how to improve our process or our work, we can use that feedback. We will not use Client Materials in connection with feedback.

8.7 No implied licenses. You only get the rights this Agreement actually grants.

ARTICLE 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Mutual. Each of us holds the other's non-public information confidential, uses it only for the work, and shares it only with our people who need to know.

9.2 Exceptions. The promise does not cover information that is already public, was already known, was independently developed, or was rightfully received from someone else.

9.3 Compelled disclosure. If a court order requires disclosure, we will tell the other side first if we are legally allowed to.

9.4 Survival. Confidentiality obligations last during this Agreement and for two years after.

ARTICLE 10 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

10.1 Authority. Each of us has the authority to enter into this Agreement.

10.2 What we promise. We will do the work professionally and consistent with industry standards. The Deliverables (other than Third-Party Materials and Client Materials) will not knowingly infringe anyone else's IP. We will comply with applicable laws.

10.3 What you promise. You have the right to provide Client Materials. The Client Materials do not infringe anyone else's rights.

10.4 Disclaimer. EXCEPT FOR THE LIMITED WARRANTY ABOVE, THE SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS." WE DISCLAIM ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE. WE DO NOT WARRANT ANY SPECIFIC BRAND, MARKET, OR BUSINESS OUTCOME FROM THE WORK.

ARTICLE 11 — INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 We defend you. We will defend you against any third-party claim that the Deliverables (the parts we built — not Client Materials, not Third-Party Materials beyond their license terms) infringe a third party's U.S. patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret. We will pay the final judgment or agreed settlement, subject to the cap in Article 12.

11.2 You defend us. You will defend us against any third-party claim that arises from (a) Client Materials, or (b) your use of a Deliverable in a way the SOW or licensed scope did not permit.

11.3 How indemnification works. The side seeking indemnity tells the other side promptly, gives the other side control of the defense, and helps out at the indemnifying side's reasonable expense.

11.4 If a Deliverable might infringe. If a Deliverable might be the subject of an IP claim, we may (a) get you a license, (b) modify it to be non-infringing without losing material value, (c) replace it, or (d) take it back and refund the fees you paid for it.

ARTICLE 12 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 Cap. EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, NEITHER OF US WILL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR MORE THAN THE TOTAL FEES YOU HAVE PAID US UNDER THE AFFECTED SOW IN THE {{LIABILITY_CAP_MONTHS}} MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

12.2 No indirect damages. EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, NEITHER OF US WILL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS OR LOSS OF GOODWILL, EVEN IF EITHER OF US HAS BEEN ADVISED THAT THOSE DAMAGES ARE POSSIBLE.

12.3 What is not capped. The cap and the exclusion do not apply to (a) what we owe under Article 11, (b) breach of Article 9 (Confidentiality), (c) your obligation to pay fees, (d) gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud, or (e) anything the law says cannot be limited.

12.4 Why the cap. The cap reflects the price of design services.

ARTICLE 13 — TERMINATION

13.1 Termination for material breach. Either of us can terminate this Agreement, or any SOW, on 30 days written notice if the other side materially breaches and does not cure within those 30 days.

13.2 Termination for insolvency. Either of us can terminate immediately if the other becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy.

13.3 Effects of termination. When this ends: (a) we stop working under the affected SOW, (b) we deliver the Deliverables completed up to the termination date, (c) we return Client Materials, (d) you pay for accepted work and non-cancellable third-party costs, (e) each of us returns or destroys the other's Confidential Information, and (f) Articles 1, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, and 14 keep working.

15.1 Whole agreement. This Agreement plus all signed SOWs make up the whole deal between us.

15.2 Updates. Changes only count if they are in writing signed by both of us.

15.3 Severability. If a court says a part of this Agreement does not work, the rest still does.

15.5 Notices. Formal notices go to the addresses on the SOW.

15.9 Force majeure. Neither of us is liable for delays caused by events outside our reasonable control, except your obligation to pay still applies.

15.10 No third-party beneficiaries. Only you and we have rights under this Agreement.

15.11 Counterparts. We can sign in counterparts.
