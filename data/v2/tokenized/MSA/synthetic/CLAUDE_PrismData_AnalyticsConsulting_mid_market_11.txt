ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with another entity.

1.2 "Background IP" means any Intellectual Property Rights owned or licensable by a Party prior to the Effective Date or independently developed outside of any SOW, including the Consultant's analytics methodologies, dashboard frameworks, modeling code libraries, and benchmarking datasets.

1.3 "Client Data" means data, information, records, and other materials provided by the Client to the Consultant in connection with any SOW, including without limitation customer transaction data, customer demographic data, behavioral data, sales data, marketing data, and any Personal Information contained therein.

1.4 "Deliverables" means the data analytics deliverables prepared for the Client under a SOW, including dashboards, reports, predictive models, segmentation analyses, attribution analyses, and recommendations.

1.5 "Derived Insights" means analytical findings, conclusions, recommendations, models, and other insights generated by the Consultant based on Client Data in the course of performing the Services.

1.6 "Personal Information" has the meaning given in applicable privacy laws (including the California Consumer Privacy Act, Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act, and similar state laws).

1.7 "Services" means the data analytics consulting services to be performed by the Consultant for the Client under one or more SOWs.

1.8 "Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a written document executed by both Parties describing a particular engagement.

1.9 "Work Product" means all Deliverables and other tangible work product created by or on behalf of the Consultant in the course of performing any SOW, in each case excluding Background IP.

ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 Engagement of Services. The Consultant shall provide the Services described in each SOW. Each SOW shall specify (a) the data sources and Client Data scope; (b) the analytical questions to be addressed; (c) the Deliverables; (d) the personnel assigned; (e) the Fees; (f) the schedule; (g) the acceptance criteria; and (h) any project-specific terms.

2.2 Order of Precedence. As described in the preamble.

2.3 Standard of Performance. The Consultant shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with the standards of the data analytics consulting industry.

2.4 Cooperation. The Client shall provide the Consultant with reasonable access to Client Data, data infrastructure, business stakeholders, and reference materials.

ARTICLE 3 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Delivery. The Consultant shall deliver each Deliverable in accordance with the schedule and in the format set forth in the applicable SOW.

3.2 Acceptance Period. The Client shall have a period of fifteen (15) business days following delivery to review the Deliverable and notify the Consultant in writing of acceptance or rejection. Rejection shall be accompanied by a detailed description of the alleged non-conformity.

3.3 Cure of Non-Conformity. The Consultant shall correct rejected Deliverables at no additional charge within fifteen (15) business days. After two (2) cycles of cure, the Client may accept with an equitable Fee reduction or terminate the affected SOW.

3.4 Deemed Acceptance. If the Client fails to provide written acceptance or rejection within the Acceptance Period, the Deliverable shall be deemed accepted.

3.5 Validation of Models. Where a Deliverable is a predictive model, the Consultant shall provide model performance metrics (such as accuracy, precision, recall, AUC, or appropriate metrics for the model class), holdout validation results, and a description of the assumptions and limitations of the model.

ARTICLE 4 — FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fees. The Client shall pay the Consultant the Fees set forth in each SOW. Fees may be calculated on a time-and-materials, fixed-fee, milestone-based, or other basis as set forth in the SOW.

4.2 Annual Aggregate Reference. The aggregate annual Fees payable by the Client across all then-active SOWs as of the Effective Date is approximately {{ANNUAL_FEE_AMOUNT}}, included for reference only.

4.3 Expenses. Reasonable, pre-approved travel and out-of-pocket expenses shall be reimbursed at cost without markup, accompanied by reasonable documentation, subject to the Client's expense policy.

4.4 Payment Terms. All undisputed invoices shall be due and payable within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice.

4.5 Disputed Invoices. The Client shall notify the Consultant of any disputed amount within fifteen (15) business days and shall pay all undisputed amounts on time.

4.6 Audit Right. The Client shall have the right to audit the Consultant's records for any Services billed on a time-and-materials basis, upon at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice.

4.7 Taxes. Fees are exclusive of all taxes other than taxes imposed on the Consultant's net income.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 Initial Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 13.

ARTICLE 6 — KEY PERSONNEL AND STAFFING

6.1 Designated Personnel. Each SOW may identify the data scientists, analysts, and engineers committed to perform the Services.

6.2 Replacement of Designated Personnel. The Consultant shall not replace designated personnel without the Client's prior written consent except in case of death, disability, or departure from the Consultant.

6.3 Removal on Client Request. The Client may request that the Consultant remove any individual personnel from the engagement. The Consultant shall comply with such request promptly.

6.4 Background Checks. The Consultant shall ensure that all personnel performing the Services have undergone background investigations and have completed training on data privacy and Client Data handling.

6.5 Non-Solicitation. During the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months thereafter, the Client shall not solicit or hire any Consultant personnel who performed Services for the Client.

ARTICLE 7 — CLIENT DATA AND PRIVACY

7.1 Ownership of Client Data. As between the Parties, the Client shall retain all right, title, and interest in and to Client Data, including all Personal Information.

7.2 Permitted Uses. The Consultant shall use Client Data solely as necessary to perform the Services in accordance with the applicable SOW. The Consultant shall not (a) use Client Data for any other purpose; (b) sell, lease, or rent Client Data; (c) use Client Data to train any commercial model that the Consultant licenses to other clients; (d) export Client Data outside of the Client's tenant or the Consultant's secure analytics environment; or (e) disclose Client Data to any third party except as expressly permitted by the Client.

7.3 No Exfiltration. The Consultant shall analyze Client Data only within the Consultant's secure analytics environment or within the Client's own data infrastructure. The Consultant shall not download, copy, or transfer Client Data to any unsecured device or location, and shall not allow any individual personnel to take Client Data offline in any form.

7.4 Privacy Law Compliance. The Consultant shall comply with applicable privacy laws (including the CCPA, CPRA, Virginia CDPA, Colorado Privacy Act, Connecticut Data Privacy Act, Utah Consumer Privacy Act, and similar state laws) in handling Client Data. The Consultant shall act as a "service provider" within the meaning of the CCPA/CPRA and shall not "sell" or "share" Personal Information.

7.5 Aggregation Restriction. The Consultant shall not combine Client Data with data from any other client of the Consultant, or with any third-party dataset, without the Client's prior written consent.

7.6 Data Security. The Consultant shall implement and maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Client Data, including encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication, annual third-party penetration testing, and a documented information security program reviewed annually.

7.7 Breach Notification. The Consultant shall notify the Client in writing of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to Client Data without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery.

7.8 Return of Client Data. Upon completion of the relevant engagement or upon the Client's earlier written request, the Consultant shall return or destroy all Client Data in its possession, including any copies in the Consultant's analytics environment, and shall provide written certification of deletion upon request.

ARTICLE 8 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

8.1 Background IP. Each Party retains its respective Background IP. The Consultant's Background IP includes the Consultant's analytics methodologies, generic modeling frameworks, dashboard templates, and proprietary benchmark datasets (which do not contain Client Data).

8.2 Assignment of Work Product and Derived Insights. Subject to Section 8.3 and to the Client's payment of all Fees due in respect of the applicable engagement, the Consultant hereby irrevocably assigns to the Client all right, title, and interest in and to (a) the Work Product, including all dashboards, reports, predictive models, and recommendations created specifically for the Client; and (b) the Derived Insights generated from Client Data. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable copyright law, the Work Product and Derived Insights shall be deemed "work made for hire" for the Client; to the extent they do not so qualify, the Consultant hereby assigns them to the Client.

8.3 License to Background IP. To the extent any Work Product incorporates the Consultant's Background IP, the Consultant hereby grants the Client a perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to use such Background IP solely as embedded in the Work Product.

8.4 Restriction on Reuse. The Consultant shall not reuse any Derived Insights, Client-specific models, Client-specific dashboards, or Client Data for the benefit of any other client of the Consultant. The Consultant may, however, use de-identified, aggregated insights about industry trends (provided such insights do not include any Client Data and do not reveal Client-specific patterns).

8.5 Feedback. The Client may provide the Consultant with feedback. The Client grants the Consultant a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use such feedback to improve its services, provided that the Consultant shall not use any Client Data in connection with such use of feedback.

8.6 No Implied Licenses. Except for the limited rights expressly granted, neither Party grants any rights or licenses to the other Party.

ARTICLE 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Confidentiality Obligations. Each Party shall hold the other Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence, use it solely for purposes of this Agreement, share it only with personnel who have a need to know and are bound by similar confidentiality obligations, and protect it with at least reasonable care. The Client's Confidential Information includes Client Data, Derived Insights, the Client's business and customer strategy, and the existence and contents of this Agreement and any SOW.

9.2 Exclusions. Confidentiality obligations shall not apply to information that is already public, was already known, was independently developed, or was rightfully received from a third party.

9.3 Compelled Disclosure. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by court order or applicable law, with prompt notice to the Disclosing Party where legally permitted.

9.4 Survival. The obligations in this Article 9 shall survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.
