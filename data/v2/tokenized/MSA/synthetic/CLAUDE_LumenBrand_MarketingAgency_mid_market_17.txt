This is the LumenBrand Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement"). We make brand and marketing campaigns. You hire us to do specific projects through Statements of Work. This Agreement covers the rules. If something in a Statement of Work conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement wins on the things it covers, except where the Statement of Work expressly says otherwise.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

We define most terms inline. The terms below come up often.

1.1 "Statement of Work" or "SOW" is a document we both sign that describes a specific project — the scope, the deliverables, the timeline, the team, the fees, and any project-specific terms.

1.2 "Deliverables" are the things we hand over to you under a SOW — campaigns, designs, copy, video, photos, brand guides, and so on.

1.3 "Client Materials" are the things you give us to use on the project — your brand assets, your product information, your customer data, your logos.

1.4 "Background IP" is the stuff each of us already owns going in (or develops on our own outside of any SOW). The Agency's Background IP includes our methodologies, frameworks, internal tools, and templates.

1.5 "Third-Party Materials" are things owned by someone else that we license or buy on your behalf for the project — stock photography, music, talent, fonts, software.

ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 SOW model. We do work for you only under signed SOWs. Each SOW will spell out the scope, deliverables, timeline, team, and fees.

2.2 Order of precedence. As stated in the preamble.

2.3 What we promise. We will do the work in a professional, workmanlike manner, with the talent and craft you expect from a marketing agency.

2.4 What you do. You give us reasonable access to your brand assets, your stakeholders, and the information we need. If you do not, we are not responsible for delays.

2.5 No exclusivity. This Agreement is not exclusive. You can hire other agencies. We can take on other clients (subject to the conflict rule below).

2.6 Conflicts. We will not knowingly take on a direct competitor of yours during an active SOW without telling you first. "Direct competitor" means another company in the same product category and target market — we will check with you before signing on if we are unsure.

ARTICLE 3 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Delivery. We deliver each Deliverable on the schedule and in the format the SOW says.

3.2 Acceptance Period. You have ten (10) business days from delivery to review each Deliverable and tell us in writing whether you accept it or want changes. If you want changes, give us a reasonably specific list.

3.3 Cure. If you ask for changes, we will make them at no additional charge if they are within the original scope of the SOW. We deliver the revised Deliverable within ten (10) business days. If after two rounds of revisions you still are not happy, you can either accept the Deliverable with a fair fee reduction or terminate the affected SOW and get back any fees paid for the rejected Deliverable.

3.4 Deemed acceptance. If you do not respond within the Acceptance Period, the Deliverable is deemed accepted.

3.5 Out-of-scope changes. Changes outside the original scope of a SOW need a written change order signed by both of us, and may carry additional fees.

3.6 Pre-publication review. For any campaign content that names you, your products, or your customers, we will not publish without your written approval.

ARTICLE 4 — FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fees. You pay us the fees in each SOW. Fees can be fixed-fee per project, monthly retainer, hourly time-and-materials, or any other basis the SOW says.

4.2 Annual aggregate. The total fees you pay us across all active SOWs as of the Effective Date is roughly {{ANNUAL_FEE_AMOUNT}}, included for reference only.

4.3 Third-Party Materials. We buy and license Third-Party Materials on your behalf when the SOW calls for it. We pass the cost through to you at cost (no markup) and submit the receipts. We will get your written approval before buying any Third-Party Material that costs more than One Thousand United States Dollars ($1,000).

4.4 Talent and union work. If a project requires union talent (SAG-AFTRA, etc.), we will tell you about union scale rates, residuals, and use rights before we book talent. Residuals and use-fee renewals are passed through at cost.

4.5 Net 30. We invoice monthly and you pay within 30 days. Late payments accrue 1.5% per month or the maximum allowed by law, whichever is less.

4.6 Disputes. If you think a charge is wrong, tell us within 15 business days and we will work it out. Pay the parts you do not dispute on time.

4.7 Taxes. Fees do not include taxes. You are responsible for any taxes that apply.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 Initial Term. This Agreement runs for {{TERM_YEARS}} years from the Effective Date.

5.2 Renewal. After the Initial Term, this Agreement automatically renews for one (1) year periods. Either of us can stop the renewal by giving written notice at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days before the current term ends.

5.3 You can cancel any active SOW for convenience. Cancel any SOW in writing. We will stop work and bill you for everything we have done up to the cancellation date plus any non-cancellable third-party costs we already committed to.

ARTICLE 6 — TEAM AND STAFFING

6.1 Designated team. Each SOW will list the lead Agency people on the project — the account director, the creative lead, and any key creative talent.

6.2 No swapping. We will not swap out designated team members without telling you first, except if someone leaves the Agency, gets sick, or we need to remove them for misconduct.

6.3 Removal on your request. If you want us to take someone off your account, just ask. We will do it within five business days. We will not assign that person back to your account without your okay.

6.4 Background checks. We run background checks on all our employees consistent with our standard practice.

6.5 Non-solicitation. While we are working together and for 12 months after, you will not directly hire or solicit Agency employees who worked on your account (general public job ads do not count).

ARTICLE 7 — CLIENT MATERIALS AND DATA

7.1 You own Client Materials. Your brand assets, your customer data, your product info — you own all of it.

7.2 Limited use. We use Client Materials only to do the work for you under the SOWs. We do not sell them, share them, or use them for any other client.

7.3 Customer data. To the extent you give us access to data about your customers, we treat it as your Confidential Information and we comply with applicable privacy laws (CCPA, CPRA, Virginia CDPA, Colorado CPA, Connecticut DPA, Utah CPA, etc.) as a service provider acting on your behalf.

7.4 Security. We protect Client Materials with TLS 1.2 in transit, AES-256 at rest, role-based access in our project management tools, and an annual review of our security practices.

7.5 Breach notification. If we have a security incident affecting Client Materials, we will tell you in writing within 72 hours of discovering it.

7.6 Return on cancellation. When the engagement ends, we return Client Materials to you and delete the copies we have, except for archival copies in our normal records retention.

ARTICLE 8 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

8.1 Background IP. Each side keeps its Background IP. We are not transferring our methodologies, our internal tools, our brand work for other clients, or our templates.

8.2 Work Product is yours. Subject to Section 8.3 and to you paying the fees, we assign to you all rights in the Deliverables we create specifically for you under each SOW. To the maximum extent permitted by copyright law, the Deliverables are "work made for hire" for you. To the extent they are not, we hereby assign them to you. Our employees and contractors have signed agreements that let us make this assignment.

8.3 License to Background IP. If a Deliverable contains some of our Background IP (like a methodology slide or a generic graphic asset we built), we grant you a perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use that Background IP as embedded in the Deliverable.

8.4 Third-Party Materials. Third-Party Materials in a Deliverable come with the rights granted by the original licensor — those rights pass through to you under the original licensor's terms. If a Third-Party Material has a use limit (a specific medium, a one-year window, a single market), we will tell you in writing before we deliver, and we will note it in the project files.

8.5 Talent rights. Where we hire models, actors, voice talent, or photographers, we will obtain releases assigning to you the rights you need for the campaign use described in the SOW. If you later want to use the work outside that scope (a different market, a longer term, a new medium), you may need to pay additional fees to the talent and/or to us.

8.6 Portfolio rights. We can show the work in our portfolio, on our website, in case studies, and in award submissions, including using your name and logo. If there is anything we should not show, tell us in the SOW or in writing and we will respect it.

8.7 Feedback. If you tell us how to improve our process or our work for other clients, we can use that feedback. We will not use any Client Materials in connection with feedback.

8.8 No implied licenses. You only get the rights this Agreement actually grants.

ARTICLE 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Mutual. Each of us holds the other's non-public information confidential, uses it only for the work, and shares it only with our people who need to know and are bound by similar confidentiality.

9.2 Exceptions. The promise does not cover information that is already public, was already known, was independently developed, or was rightfully received from someone else with no confidentiality duty.

9.3 Compelled disclosure. If a court order requires disclosure, we will tell the other side first if we are legally allowed to.

9.4 Survival. Confidentiality obligations last during this Agreement and for three years after.

10.1 Authority. Each of us has the authority to enter into this Agreement.

10.2 What we promise. We will perform the work professionally and consistent with industry standards. The Deliverables (other than Third-Party Materials and Client Materials) will not knowingly infringe anyone else's IP. We will comply with applicable laws.

10.3 What you promise. You have the right to provide Client Materials. The Client Materials do not infringe anyone else's rights. Your use of the Deliverables (in the medium and term we agreed in the SOW) will comply with applicable advertising laws (FTC endorsement guides, CAN-SPAM, TCPA, ADA web accessibility).

10.4 Disclaimer. EXCEPT FOR THE LIMITED WARRANTY ABOVE, THE SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS." WE DISCLAIM ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE. WE DO NOT WARRANT ANY SPECIFIC SALES, REVENUE, BRAND LIFT, OR MARKET-SHARE OUTCOME FROM A CAMPAIGN.

ARTICLE 11 — INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 We defend you. We will defend you against any third-party claim that the Deliverables (the parts we built — not Client Materials, not Third-Party Materials beyond their license terms) infringe a third party's U.S. patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret. We will pay the final judgment or agreed settlement, subject to the cap in Article 12.

11.2 You defend us. You will defend us against any third-party claim that arises from (a) Client Materials, (b) your use of a Deliverable in a way the SOW or licensed scope did not permit, (c) anything you said about your product that turns out to be untrue, or (d) any FTC, CAN-SPAM, TCPA, or ADA claim arising from your use of a Deliverable.

11.3 How indemnification works. The side seeking indemnity tells the other side promptly, gives the other side control of the defense (subject to a no-bad-settlement-without-consent right), and helps out at the indemnifying side's reasonable expense.

11.4 If a Deliverable might infringe. If a Deliverable might be the subject of an IP claim, we may (a) get you a license, (b) modify it to be non-infringing without losing material value, (c) replace it, or (d) take it back and refund the fees you paid for it.

ARTICLE 13 — TERMINATION

13.1 Termination for material breach. Either of us can terminate this Agreement, or any SOW, on 30 days written notice if the other side materially breaches and does not cure within those 30 days. If you terminate for our material breach, we will refund any prepaid fees that cover the period after termination.

13.2 Termination for insolvency. Either of us can terminate immediately if the other becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, files for bankruptcy, or has bankruptcy filed against it that is not dismissed within 60 days.

13.3 Effects of termination. When this ends: (a) we stop working under the affected SOW, (b) we deliver any work completed up to the termination date, (c) we return Client Materials, (d) you pay us for accepted work and non-cancellable commitments, (e) each of us returns or destroys the other's Confidential Information, and (f) Articles 1, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, and 14 keep working.
