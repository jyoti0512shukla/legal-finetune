ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Statement of Work" or "SOW" is a document we both sign that describes a specific research project.

1.2 "Deliverables" are the things we hand over to you under a SOW — research reports, recordings, transcripts, highlight reels, persona artifacts, and so on.

1.3 "Background IP" is what each of us already owns going in. Our Background IP includes our recruiting databases, our analysis frameworks, and our research methodologies.

1.4 "Client Materials" are things you give us to use on the project — your prototypes, your product information, your existing research, your brand guidelines.

1.5 "Participant" is an end user we recruit to participate in a research session. Participants are not your customers or our customers — they are research subjects.

1.6 "Participant Data" is any information we collect from or about a Participant in the course of a research session, including names, contact information, demographic data, recordings, transcripts, and study responses.

ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 SOW model. We do work for you only under signed SOWs.

2.2 Order of precedence. As described in the preamble.

2.3 What we promise. We will conduct the research in a professional, workmanlike manner, in line with the standards of the user research profession.

2.4 What you do. You give us reasonable access to your prototypes, your product, and any background research we need.

2.5 Recruiting. Unless the SOW says otherwise, we recruit Participants from our own panel, screened against the criteria you and we set in the SOW. We do not recruit from your existing customer list unless the SOW expressly tells us to.

ARTICLE 3 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Delivery. We deliver each Deliverable on the schedule and in the format the SOW says.

3.2 Acceptance Period. You have ten (10) business days from delivery of each Deliverable to review it and tell us in writing whether you accept it or want changes. If you want changes, give us a reasonably specific list.

3.3 Cure. If you ask for changes, we will make them at no additional charge if they are within the original scope. We deliver the revised Deliverable within ten (10) business days. If after two rounds you are still not satisfied, you can either accept the Deliverable with a fair fee reduction or terminate the affected SOW and recover fees paid for that Deliverable.

3.4 Deemed acceptance. If you do not respond within the Acceptance Period, the Deliverable is deemed accepted.

3.5 Out-of-scope changes. New scope, additional rounds of research, or new study designs need a written change order signed by both of us.

ARTICLE 4 — FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fees. You pay us the fees in each SOW. Fees can be fixed-fee per study, time-and-materials at the SOW rates, or any other basis the SOW says.

4.2 Annual aggregate. The total fees you pay us across all active SOWs as of the Effective Date is roughly {{ANNUAL_FEE_AMOUNT}}, included for reference only.

4.3 Participant incentives. We pay participant incentives (gift cards, cash, or in-kind) at the rate set in the SOW. We pass through participant incentive costs to you at cost (no markup) and submit the receipts.

4.4 Recruiting fees. Where we recruit Participants from outside our panel (specialized populations, executive recruiting), we may charge a recruiting fee per recruited Participant. The recruiting fee is set in the SOW.

4.5 Net 30. We invoice monthly and you pay within 30 days. Late payments accrue 1% per month or the maximum allowed by law, whichever is less.

4.6 Disputes. If you think a charge is wrong, tell us within 15 business days. Pay the parts you do not dispute on time.

4.7 Taxes. Fees do not include taxes. You are responsible for any taxes that apply.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 Initial Term. This Agreement runs for {{TERM_YEARS}} years from the Effective Date.

5.2 Renewal. After the Initial Term, this Agreement automatically renews for one (1) year periods. Either of us can stop the renewal by giving written notice at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days before the current term ends.

5.3 You can cancel any active SOW. Cancel any SOW in writing. We will stop work and bill you for everything we have done up to the cancellation date plus any non-cancellable participant incentive commitments.

ARTICLE 6 — RESEARCHERS AND STAFFING

6.1 Designated team. Each SOW lists the lead researchers on the project.

6.2 No swapping. We will not swap out designated researchers without telling you first, except if someone leaves the firm, gets sick, or needs to be removed.

6.3 Removal on your request. If you want us to take someone off your account, ask. We will do it within five business days.

6.4 Background checks. We run background checks on all our researchers consistent with our standard practice.

6.5 Non-solicitation. While we are working together and for 12 months after, you will not directly hire or solicit our researchers who worked on your account (general public job ads do not count).

ARTICLE 7 — PARTICIPANT DATA, CLIENT MATERIALS, AND CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 You own Client Materials. Your prototypes, your existing research, your product information — you own all of it.

7.2 Participant Data ownership. We collect Participant Data on your behalf. As between the Parties, you own the Participant Data, subject to (a) Participant rights under applicable privacy law, and (b) the consent and use limits we agreed with each Participant in the consent form.

7.3 Limited use. We use Participant Data and Client Materials only to perform the research and deliver the Deliverables under the SOW. We will not use Participant Data or Client Materials for any other client, for product marketing, or for any commercial purpose other than the engagement.

7.4 Participant consent. We will obtain written informed consent from each Participant before any session, in a form reviewed and approved by you. The consent will explain who will see the recordings (typically: us, you, and approved subcontractors), how the recordings will be used, how long they will be retained, and the Participant's rights.

7.5 No identifying use without consent. We will not use any Participant's name, image, voice, or likeness in any marketing material, public case study, or external publication without that Participant's specific written consent (separate from the research participation consent).

7.6 Privacy law compliance. We comply with applicable privacy laws (CCPA, CPRA, Virginia CDPA, Colorado CPA, Connecticut DPA, Utah CPA, GDPR where applicable, etc.) in the collection and handling of Participant Data. We act as a service provider on your behalf with respect to Participant Data we collect for you.

7.7 Recording handling. Audio and video recordings of sessions are stored in our secure research platform. Access is limited to the project team and to your designated viewers. Recordings are retained for the period the SOW specifies (typically 12 months after the study ends) and then deleted.

7.8 Data security. We protect Participant Data and Client Materials with TLS 1.2 in transit, AES-256 at rest, role-based access controls, and an annual review of our security practices.

7.9 Breach notification. If we have a security incident affecting Participant Data or Client Materials, we will tell you in writing within 72 hours of discovering it.

7.10 Return on cancellation. When the engagement ends, we return Client Materials to you, deliver the final Deliverables, and follow the Participant Data retention schedule for any recordings.

ARTICLE 8 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

8.1 Background IP. Each side keeps its Background IP. We are not transferring our recruiting database, our research methodologies, or our analysis frameworks.

8.2 Work Product is yours. Subject to Section 8.3 and to you paying the fees, we assign to you all rights in the Deliverables we create specifically for you under each SOW. To the maximum extent permitted by copyright law, the Deliverables are "work made for hire" for you. To the extent they are not, we hereby assign them to you. Our employees and approved subcontractors have signed agreements that let us make this assignment.

8.3 License to Background IP. If a Deliverable contains some of our Background IP (like a methodology slide or our standard report template), we grant you a perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use that Background IP solely as embedded in the Deliverable.

8.4 Aggregate insights. We may use de-identified, aggregated insights from research across multiple clients for industry benchmarking and methodology improvement, provided no Participant Data, no Client Materials, and no Client-identifying information is included.

8.5 Feedback. If you tell us how to improve our process, we can use that feedback. We will not use Client Materials or Participant Data in connection with feedback.

8.6 Portfolio rights. We can mention you as a customer on our website and in case studies, with your prior written approval of the specific case study text. We will not use any Participant identifying information in any portfolio item without separate Participant consent.

8.7 No implied licenses. You only get the rights this Agreement actually grants.

9.3 Exceptions. The promise does not cover information that is already public, was already known, was independently developed, or was rightfully received from someone else with no confidentiality duty.
