HEALTHCARE STAFFING AND LOCUM TENENS MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Healthcare Staffing and Locum Tenens Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date"), by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Staffing Agency"), having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}}, and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Health System"), having its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}}.

This Agreement is a master agreement only. The specific shift requirements, healthcare professional types (physicians, advanced practice providers, nurses, allied health), credentialing requirements, locations, schedules, hourly or shift-based rates, and any project-specific terms shall be set forth in one or more Statements of Work executed under this Agreement (each, a "SOW") and incorporated by reference into this Agreement. The Business Associate Agreement attached as Exhibit A is incorporated by reference into this Agreement.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with another entity.

1.2 "Background IP" means any Intellectual Property Rights owned or licensable by a Party prior to the Effective Date or independently developed outside of any SOW.

1.3 "BAA" means the Business Associate Agreement attached as Exhibit A.

1.4 "Credentialing File" means the complete record of a Healthcare Professional's licensure, board certification, work history, malpractice claims history, DEA registration (where applicable), state controlled substance registration (where applicable), background investigation, drug screening, immunizations, fit-for-duty certifications, and other documentation required to verify that the Healthcare Professional is qualified to practice at the Health System's facilities.

1.5 "Healthcare Professional" or "Provider" means a physician, advanced practice provider, registered nurse, licensed practical nurse, certified registered nurse anesthetist, certified surgical technologist, allied health professional, or other licensed healthcare worker who is placed by the Staffing Agency to provide clinical services at a Health System facility.

1.6 "Health System Facility" means any hospital, clinic, ambulatory surgery center, or other healthcare facility owned, operated, or managed by the Health System or its Affiliates.

1.7 "Intellectual Property Rights" means all intellectual property and proprietary rights of any kind anywhere in the world.

1.8 "Locum Tenens" means temporary clinical staffing in which a Healthcare Professional fills a position on a short-term basis.

1.9 "Services" means the healthcare staffing services to be performed by the Staffing Agency for the Health System under one or more SOWs.

1.10 "Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a written document executed by both Parties describing a particular staffing engagement.

ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 Engagement of Services. The Staffing Agency shall provide qualified Healthcare Professionals to perform clinical services at Health System Facilities as described in each SOW.

2.2 Statements of Work. Each SOW shall specify (a) the type of Healthcare Professional required; (b) the specialty or sub-specialty; (c) the Health System Facility (or facilities); (d) the schedule and shift hours; (e) the duration of the engagement; (f) the hourly or shift-based rate; (g) the credentialing requirements; (h) any required certifications; and (i) any project-specific terms.

2.3 Order of Precedence. As described in the preamble. The BAA controls over both this Agreement and any SOW with respect to PHI.

2.4 No Practice of Medicine. The Staffing Agency does not practice medicine and does not direct the clinical practice of any Healthcare Professional. All clinical decisions are the sole responsibility of the Healthcare Professional and the Health System's clinical leadership. The Staffing Agency's role is limited to recruiting, credentialing, payroll, and administrative support.

2.5 Independent Contractor or Employee. Each SOW shall specify whether each Healthcare Professional is engaged as an employee or as an independent contractor of the Staffing Agency, in accordance with applicable law and the requirements of the Health System.

ARTICLE 3 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Delivery of Services. The Staffing Agency shall provide Healthcare Professionals on the schedule and at the Health System Facility set forth in the applicable SOW.

3.2 Performance-Based Delivery. The Parties acknowledge that the Services consist of ongoing clinical staffing rather than discrete deliverables. The Health System shall provide regular feedback on the performance of placed Healthcare Professionals.

3.3 Removal of Healthcare Professional. The Health System may, at any time and for any reason consistent with applicable law, request that the Staffing Agency remove any Healthcare Professional from the Health System Facility. The Staffing Agency shall comply promptly and shall propose a replacement of comparable qualifications within a reasonable time.

ARTICLE 4 — FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fees. The Health System shall pay the Staffing Agency the Fees set forth in each SOW, calculated on (a) an hourly basis, (b) a per-shift basis, (c) a daily basis, (d) a weekly basis, or (e) any other basis agreed in the SOW. Fees shall include the Healthcare Professional's compensation, the Staffing Agency's overhead and margin, employer taxes, malpractice insurance premium allocation, and any travel or housing expenses pre-approved in the SOW.

4.2 Annual Aggregate Reference. The aggregate annual Fees payable by the Health System across all then-active SOWs as of the Effective Date is approximately {{ANNUAL_FEE_AMOUNT}}, included for reference only.

4.3 Overtime and Holiday Rates. Hours worked by a Healthcare Professional in excess of forty (40) per week, or on holidays designated in the applicable SOW, shall be billed at the overtime or holiday rate set forth in the SOW.

4.4 Travel and Housing. Where the SOW provides for travel and housing for a Locum Tenens engagement, the Staffing Agency shall arrange travel and housing for the placed Healthcare Professional and shall pass through the actual costs at cost, accompanied by reasonable documentation, subject to any cap in the SOW.

4.5 Invoicing. The Staffing Agency shall invoice the Health System weekly for hours worked, expenses incurred, and any other charges.

4.6 Payment Terms. All undisputed invoices shall be due and payable within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice.

4.7 Disputed Invoices. The Health System shall notify the Staffing Agency of any disputed amount within ten (10) business days and shall pay all undisputed amounts on time.

4.8 No Volume- or Value-Based Compensation. Notwithstanding any other provision, the Fees shall not be determined in any manner that takes into account the volume or value of any referrals or other business generated between the Parties or between the Healthcare Professionals and the Health System. The Fees shall be set in advance, shall be commercially reasonable, and shall be consistent with fair market value for the Services, in compliance with the federal Anti-Kickback Statute and the Stark Law.

4.9 Taxes. Fees are exclusive of all taxes other than taxes imposed on the Staffing Agency's net income.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 Initial Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 13. Each SOW shall have a minimum term of one (1) year as required by the AKS personal services safe harbor.

5.2 Renewal. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

ARTICLE 6 — HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONAL CREDENTIALING AND PERSONNEL

6.1 Credentialing Requirements. Before placing any Healthcare Professional at a Health System Facility, the Staffing Agency shall verify and provide to the Health System a complete Credentialing File for the Healthcare Professional. The Credentialing File shall be updated as required to maintain currency throughout the placement.

6.2 Verification of Licensure. The Staffing Agency shall verify that each Healthcare Professional holds a current, unrestricted license to practice in the state where the Health System Facility is located, in the discipline relevant to the engagement. The Staffing Agency shall conduct primary source verification of license status.

6.3 Background Investigations. The Staffing Agency shall conduct a comprehensive background investigation on each Healthcare Professional, including criminal history, OIG/GSA exclusion check, NPDB query, sex offender registry check, and drug screening, in accordance with the credentialing standards of The Joint Commission and applicable state law.

6.4 Malpractice Insurance. The Staffing Agency shall maintain professional liability (medical malpractice) insurance covering the Healthcare Professionals it places, with limits of not less than One Million United States Dollars ($1,000,000) per occurrence and Three Million United States Dollars ($3,000,000) in the aggregate. Coverage shall be on an occurrence basis or, if claims-made, with a tail period of not less than three (3) years following the end of each placement.

6.5 Health System Privileges. The Health System shall remain responsible for granting clinical privileges to placed Healthcare Professionals through the Health System's own medical staff credentialing process, in accordance with the bylaws of the Health System's medical staff and applicable state law.

6.6 Compliance with Health System Policies. Each placed Healthcare Professional shall comply with the policies, procedures, and standards of conduct of the Health System Facility where the Healthcare Professional is placed, including infection control, patient safety, and HIPAA privacy policies.

6.7 Non-Solicitation of Healthcare Professionals. During the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months following the end of any placement, the Health System shall not directly hire or engage any Healthcare Professional placed by the Staffing Agency under this Agreement, without first paying the Staffing Agency a conversion fee equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of the Healthcare Professional's first-year base compensation.

6.8 OIG/GSA Exclusion. The Staffing Agency shall ensure that no Healthcare Professional placed at a Health System Facility is currently excluded from any federal or state healthcare program. The Staffing Agency shall promptly notify the Health System if any placed Healthcare Professional becomes the subject of an exclusion action.

ARTICLE 7 — PHI, PATIENT SAFETY, AND CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Business Associate Status. To the extent the Staffing Agency or any placed Healthcare Professional has access to PHI in performing the Services, the Staffing Agency is a "business associate" of the Health System under HIPAA, and the BAA governs the Staffing Agency's handling of PHI.

7.2 PHI Handling by Healthcare Professionals. Placed Healthcare Professionals shall handle PHI in accordance with the Health System's HIPAA policies and the BAA. Each Healthcare Professional shall complete the Health System's HIPAA training before commencing clinical work at a Health System Facility.

7.3 Confidentiality of Patient Information. The Staffing Agency and each placed Healthcare Professional shall hold all patient information in strict confidence and shall not disclose any patient information except as permitted by HIPAA, the BAA, and applicable law.

7.4 Confidentiality of Health System Information. The Staffing Agency shall hold all non-public information about the Health System's operations, strategy, financial information, payor contracts, and personnel in strict confidence.

7.5 Patient Safety Reporting. The Staffing Agency shall encourage and not discourage placed Healthcare Professionals from reporting patient safety concerns through the Health System's patient safety reporting channels. The Staffing Agency shall not retaliate against any Healthcare Professional for making any such report in good faith.

7.6 Breach Notification. The Staffing Agency shall notify the Health System of any "Breach" of "Unsecured PHI" (as defined under HIPAA) without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery, in accordance with the BAA.

ARTICLE 8 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

8.1 Background IP. As between the Parties, each Party shall retain all right, title, and interest in and to its respective Background IP. The Health System's Background IP includes its medical records, treatment protocols, clinical pathways, and electronic health record system. The Staffing Agency's Background IP includes its credentialing system, scheduling software, and proprietary databases.

8.2 Work Product. The Parties acknowledge that the principal Service is the placement of Healthcare Professionals to render clinical care, and that the medical records and clinical documentation generated in the course of patient care are owned by the Health System as the healthcare provider. Any documentation created by a placed Healthcare Professional in the course of providing clinical care belongs to the Health System.

8.3 No Use of Health System Patient Information. The Staffing Agency shall not use any patient information, medical records, or clinical data from a Health System Facility for any purpose other than supporting the placement and credentialing of Healthcare Professionals, and only as expressly permitted by the BAA.

8.4 No Implied Licenses. Except for the limited rights expressly granted in this Agreement, neither Party grants any rights or licenses to the other Party.

ARTICLE 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Confidentiality Obligations. Each Party shall hold the other Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence, use it solely for purposes of this Agreement, share it only with personnel and Healthcare Professionals who have a need to know and are bound by similar confidentiality obligations, and protect it with at least reasonable care.

9.2 No Confidentiality Provisions Restricting Patient Safety Reporting. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, no provision of this Agreement, any SOW, any non-disclosure agreement signed by a Healthcare Professional, or any other document shall be construed to prohibit a Healthcare Professional from reporting (a) any concern about patient safety to the Health System's patient safety officer, the Joint Commission, or any state or federal regulator with jurisdiction over the Health System; (b) any suspected violation of the federal False Claims Act, the federal Anti-Kickback Statute, the Stark Law, or any other federal or state healthcare law; or (c) any other conduct that the Healthcare Professional reasonably believes to be unlawful. Any such confidentiality provision is hereby disclaimed and shall be unenforceable.

9.3 Compelled Disclosure. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by court order or applicable law, with prompt notice to the Disclosing Party where legally permitted.

9.4 Survival. The obligations in this Article 9 shall survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

ARTICLE 10 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

10.1 Mutual Warranties. Each Party represents and warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, has the corporate authority to enter into this Agreement, and that this Agreement constitutes a binding obligation enforceable against it.

10.2 Staffing Agency Warranties. The Staffing Agency represents, warrants, and covenants that (a) all Healthcare Professionals placed at Health System Facilities shall hold current, unrestricted licenses in the relevant disciplines and states; (b) the Staffing Agency shall conduct background investigations and credentialing in accordance with the standards set forth in Article 6; (c) the Staffing Agency shall maintain malpractice insurance in accordance with Section 6.4; (d) the Staffing Agency shall not place any Healthcare Professional who is currently excluded from any federal or state healthcare program; (e) the Staffing Agency shall comply with HIPAA, the BAA, the AKS, the Stark Law, and all other applicable healthcare laws; and (f) the Staffing Agency shall comply with applicable employment law in connection with its Healthcare Professionals.

10.3 Health System Warranties. The Health System represents and warrants that (a) it has the right to receive the Services at the Health System Facilities and (b) the Health System Facilities are licensed and operating in compliance with applicable healthcare laws.

10.4 Disclaimer. EXCEPT FOR THE EXPRESS WARRANTIES SET FORTH IN THIS ARTICLE 10, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED ON AN "AS IS" BASIS. THE STAFFING AGENCY DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED. THE STAFFING AGENCY DOES NOT WARRANT ANY SPECIFIC CLINICAL OUTCOME.

ARTICLE 11 — INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 Staffing Agency Indemnity. The Staffing Agency shall defend the Health System against any third-party claim arising from (a) the Staffing Agency's failure to credential a Healthcare Professional in accordance with Article 6; (b) any misrepresentation by the Staffing Agency about a Healthcare Professional's qualifications, licensure, or background; (c) the Staffing Agency's breach of HIPAA, the AKS, the Stark Law, or other healthcare laws; or (d) the Staffing Agency's failure to maintain the malpractice insurance required by Section 6.4.

11.2 Healthcare Professional Negligence. The Staffing Agency shall be responsible for the negligent acts or omissions of placed Healthcare Professionals in the course of their clinical work to the extent covered by the Staffing Agency's malpractice insurance, except that the Health System shall be responsible for any negligence in granting clinical privileges, in providing supervision, in maintaining the Health System Facility, or in establishing the standard of care at the Health System Facility.

11.3 Health System Indemnity. The Health System shall defend the Staffing Agency against any third-party claim arising from the Health System's negligent acts or omissions in connection with the Health System Facility, the Health System's policies, or the Health System's supervision of placed Healthcare Professionals.

11.4 Indemnification Procedure. The Party seeking indemnification shall provide prompt written notice, grant the indemnifying Party control over the defense and settlement (subject to a no-bad-settlement-without-consent right), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

ARTICLE 12 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 Aggregate Cap. EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, IN NO EVENT SHALL THE AGGREGATE LIABILITY OF EITHER PARTY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT OR ANY SOW EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY THE HEALTH SYSTEM TO THE STAFFING AGENCY UNDER THE AFFECTED SOW DURING THE {{LIABILITY_CAP_MONTHS}} MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY.

12.2 Exclusion of Indirect Damages. EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER PARTY FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, EXEMPLARY, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT.

12.3 Exceptions to Limitations. The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 shall not apply to (a) the Staffing Agency's obligations under Article 11 and the malpractice insurance coverage in Section 6.4; (b) any breach of Article 9 (Confidentiality); (c) any breach of Article 7 or the BAA; (d) the Health System's payment obligations; (e) any liability arising from gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud; (f) any liability arising from the placement of an excluded Healthcare Professional; or (g) any liability that, under applicable law, cannot be limited.

12.4 Basis of the Bargain. The limitations of liability are an essential element of the bargain between the Parties.

ARTICLE 13 — TERMINATION

13.1 Termination for Material Breach. Either Party may terminate this Agreement, or any SOW, upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure within the notice period.

13.2 Termination for Insolvency. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy.

13.3 Termination for Loss of License or Insurance. The Health System may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if (a) the Staffing Agency loses the malpractice insurance coverage required by Section 6.4; (b) the Staffing Agency or any placed Healthcare Professional becomes excluded from any federal or state healthcare program; or (c) any government regulator with jurisdiction over the Health System directs the Health System to terminate its relationship with the Staffing Agency.

13.4 Effects of Termination. Upon termination: (a) the Staffing Agency shall cease placing new Healthcare Professionals; (b) any placed Healthcare Professionals already on assignment shall complete their current shift or rotation as the Health System directs; (c) the Health System shall pay the Staffing Agency for all undisputed Fees that have accrued; (d) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information; and (e) the provisions that by their nature are intended to survive shall so survive.
