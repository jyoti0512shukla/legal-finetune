This Federal Information Technology Services Master Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date"), by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Contractor"), having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}}, and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Government"), having its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}}. ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "CMMC" means the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification framework adopted by the Department of Defense.

1.2 "Contracting Officer" means the duly authorized contracting officer of the Government with authority to enter into, administer, and modify this Agreement and any Task Order issued hereunder.

1.3 "Controlled Unclassified Information" or "CUI" means information that requires safeguarding or dissemination controls pursuant to and consistent with applicable law, regulations, and government-wide policies.

1.4 "Covered Defense Information" or "CDI" has the meaning given in DFARS 252.204-7012.

1.5 "DFARS" means the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement.

1.6 "Disclosing Party" and "Receiving Party" have the meanings given in Article 9.

1.7 "EAR" means the Export Administration Regulations, 15 C.F.R. Parts 730-774.

1.8 "FAR" means the Federal Acquisition Regulation, 48 C.F.R. Chapter 1.

1.9 "Government Furnished Equipment" or "GFE" means any property furnished by the Government to the Contractor for use in performing the Services under a Task Order.

1.10 "ITAR" means the International Traffic in Arms Regulations, 22 C.F.R. Parts 120-130.

1.11 "Key Personnel" means the specific individual personnel of the Contractor identified as Key Personnel on a Task Order.

1.12 "NIST SP 800-171" means the National Institute of Standards and Technology Special Publication 800-171, "Protecting Controlled Unclassified Information in Nonfederal Systems and Organizations."

1.13 "OCI" means an organizational conflict of interest as defined in FAR Subpart 9.5.

1.14 "Services" means the information technology services to be performed by the Contractor for the Government under one or more Task Orders.

1.15 "Task Order" means a written task order issued by the Contracting Officer under this Agreement that describes a particular requirement, including the scope of work, the period of performance, the funding, the Key Personnel, and any task-specific terms.

1.16 "Work Product" means all deliverables, software code, documentation, designs, data, reports, and other tangible work product created by or on behalf of the Contractor in the course of performing the Services.

ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 Issuance of Task Orders. The Contracting Officer may issue Task Orders to the Contractor under this Agreement from time to time during the Term. Each Task Order shall describe the requirement, the period of performance, the funding (which shall be subject to availability of appropriations), the Key Personnel, the deliverables, and any task-specific terms.

2.2 Order of Precedence. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and a Task Order, the Task Order shall control as to the matters expressly addressed therein. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement (or any Task Order) and any applicable FAR or DFARS clause, the FAR or DFARS clause shall control. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and any other applicable federal law or regulation, the law or regulation shall control.

2.3 Standard of Performance. The Contractor shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the requirements set forth in each Task Order, the applicable FAR and DFARS clauses, and the standards of the federal IT services industry.

2.4 Section 508 Compliance. Any Work Product that constitutes electronic and information technology shall comply with the accessibility standards set forth in Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, 29 U.S.C. § 794d, and the implementing regulations.

2.5 Buy American Act. The Contractor shall comply with the Buy American Act, 41 U.S.C. §§ 8301-8305, and the implementing FAR clauses, with respect to any end products supplied under this Agreement.

ARTICLE 3 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Delivery. The Contractor shall deliver each Work Product item in accordance with the schedule set forth in the applicable Task Order.

3.2 Acceptance Period. The Contracting Officer (or the Contracting Officer's designated representative) shall have a period of thirty (30) calendar days following delivery of each Work Product item to inspect and accept or reject the item, in accordance with FAR Subpart 46.5.

3.3 Cure of Non-Conformity. If the Contracting Officer rejects a Work Product item, the Contractor shall, at no additional cost to the Government, cure the non-conformity within the time period specified in the rejection notice or such longer period as the Contracting Officer may permit.

3.4 Final Acceptance. Final acceptance of each Work Product item shall be in writing by the Contracting Officer. There shall be no deemed acceptance.

ARTICLE 4 — FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fees. The Government shall pay the Contractor in accordance with the funding, payment schedule, and contract type (firm-fixed-price, cost-plus-fixed-fee, time-and-materials, or labor-hour) set forth in each Task Order. All payment shall be subject to the availability of appropriations.

The aggregate annual Fees payable by the Government across all then-active Task Orders as of the Effective Date is approximately {{ANNUAL_FEE_AMOUNT}}, included for reference only and subject to the availability of appropriations and the issuance of Task Orders by the Contracting Officer.

4.3 Service Contract Act Wage Determinations. To the extent the Services are subject to the Service Contract Act, 41 U.S.C. §§ 6701-6707, the Contractor shall comply with the wage determinations issued by the Department of Labor and shall pay covered employees at not less than the wage rates and fringe benefits set forth in the applicable wage determinations.

4.4 Payment in Accordance with FAR. Payment shall be made in accordance with the Prompt Payment Act, 31 U.S.C. §§ 3901-3907, and the applicable FAR clauses.

4.5 Limitation of Funds. Where a Task Order is issued on an incrementally funded basis, the Contractor shall not be obligated to perform Services in excess of the amount allotted, and the Government shall not be obligated to pay any amount in excess of the amount allotted, in accordance with FAR 52.232-22 (Limitation of Funds).

4.6 Audit. The Contractor's records (including time records, expense records, and supporting documentation) shall be subject to audit by the Defense Contract Audit Agency, the Government Accountability Office, the Inspector General, the Contracting Officer, and any other federal auditor with jurisdiction over the Contractor or this Agreement, in accordance with FAR Subpart 4.7.

4.7 Taxes. Fees are exclusive of all taxes other than taxes imposed on the Contractor's net income.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 Period of Performance. The period of performance of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years, subject to the Government's right to extend pursuant to FAR 52.217-9 (Option to Extend the Term of the Contract).

5.2 Termination for Convenience. The Government may terminate this Agreement, or any Task Order, in whole or in part, for the convenience of the Government in accordance with FAR 52.249-2 (Termination for Convenience of the Government). ARTICLE 6 — KEY PERSONNEL AND CLEARANCES

6.1 Key Personnel. Each Task Order shall identify the Contractor Key Personnel committed to perform the Services under that Task Order.

6.2 Substitution of Key Personnel. The Contractor shall not substitute any Key Personnel without the prior written approval of the Contracting Officer, in accordance with FAR 52.222-46 or analogous Key Personnel clauses. Any proposed substitution shall be accompanied by a resume and qualifications for the proposed substitute, and the Contracting Officer shall approve or disapprove the substitution within a reasonable time.

6.3 Security Clearances. Where the Services involve access to classified information or to facilities requiring security clearance, the Contractor shall ensure that all personnel performing such Services possess the required security clearance and are eligible to access the relevant classified information at the required level. The Contractor shall comply with all applicable security clearance requirements, including the National Industrial Security Program Operating Manual (NISPOM).

6.4 Background Investigations. All Contractor personnel performing the Services shall undergo background investigations in accordance with the requirements of the applicable Task Order and the Contracting Officer's instructions.

6.5 Citizenship. Where the Services involve access to CUI, CDI, ITAR-controlled information, or classified information, only United States citizens may perform such Services, except as expressly permitted by the Contracting Officer in writing.

6.6 Removal of Personnel. The Contracting Officer may, at any time and for any reason, request that the Contractor remove any individual personnel from the performance of the Services. The Contractor shall comply with any such request promptly.

6.7 Non-Solicitation. The Government does not impose any restriction on the Contractor's hiring practices except as provided in any applicable post-employment restrictions on former federal employees.

ARTICLE 7 — INFORMATION SECURITY, CUI, AND EXPORT CONTROLS

7.1 NIST SP 800-171 Compliance. To the extent the Services involve access to, processing of, or storage of CUI, the Contractor shall implement the security requirements of NIST SP 800-171 in its information systems that handle CUI, in accordance with DFARS 252.204-7012.

7.2 CMMC Compliance. To the extent required by the applicable Task Order or by DoD CMMC requirements, the Contractor shall maintain the CMMC certification level required by the Task Order throughout the period of performance.

7.3 Cyber Incident Reporting. The Contractor shall report any "cyber incident" affecting CDI or the Contractor's covered information systems to the Department of Defense at https://dibnet.dod.mil within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery, in accordance with DFARS 252.204-7012(c).

7.4 Notification to Government. In addition to the DFARS-required cyber incident reporting, the Contractor shall notify the Contracting Officer in writing of any cyber incident affecting CUI, CDI, or any Government information within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery.

7.5 ITAR and EAR Compliance. The Contractor shall comply with the ITAR and the EAR with respect to any defense articles, defense services, technical data, or controlled commodities involved in the performance of this Agreement. The Contractor shall not export, re-export, or transfer (in-country) any such items to any foreign person or destination without the appropriate export authorization.

7.6 Foreign Ownership, Control, or Influence (FOCI). The Contractor shall comply with the FOCI requirements of the NISPOM and shall promptly notify the Government of any change in foreign ownership, control, or influence over the Contractor.

7.7 Government Furnished Equipment. The Contractor shall use any GFE provided to it by the Government solely for the purpose of performing the Services under the applicable Task Order. The Contractor shall maintain GFE in good condition, shall implement appropriate security controls, and shall return GFE to the Government at the end of the period of performance or upon the Government's earlier request.

7.8 No Cloud Storage Outside the United States. The Contractor shall not store, process, or transmit any CUI, CDI, or other Government information using cloud computing services located outside of the United States, in accordance with DFARS 252.239-7010 and the applicable cloud computing requirements.

ARTICLE 8 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

8.1 Federal Government IP Clauses. The intellectual property rights in any Work Product shall be governed by the applicable FAR data rights clauses (including FAR 52.227-14 (Rights in Data — General), FAR 52.227-17 (Rights in Data — Special Works), and any other applicable FAR or DFARS data rights clauses) and the rights specified in the applicable Task Order. Nothing in this Article 8 shall be construed to grant rights to the Government in excess of, or in derogation of, the rights specified in the applicable FAR or DFARS clauses.

8.2 Government Purpose Rights. Unless the Task Order specifies otherwise, the Government shall have the rights in Work Product specified in the applicable FAR or DFARS data rights clauses, which may include "unlimited rights," "limited rights," "Government purpose rights," "restricted rights," "specifically negotiated license rights," or such other rights as the FAR or DFARS specifies.

8.3 Marking of Restricted Data. The Contractor shall mark any data delivered to the Government with the restrictive markings required by the applicable FAR or DFARS clauses, where such markings are necessary to preserve the Contractor's restricted rights.

8.4 Contractor's Pre-Existing IP. The Contractor shall retain ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property to the extent permitted by the applicable FAR data rights clauses and the applicable Task Order. The Government's rights in any pre-existing Contractor IP delivered to the Government shall be specified in the applicable Task Order.

8.5 No Implied Licenses. Except as expressly granted by the FAR data rights clauses or by the applicable Task Order, no rights or licenses are granted by either Party to the other Party.

ARTICLE 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Confidentiality Obligations. Each Party (the "Receiving Party") shall (a) hold the other Party's (the "Disclosing Party's") Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use such Confidential Information solely for purposes of this Agreement; (c) not disclose it except to its personnel who have a need to know and are bound by obligations of confidentiality; and (d) protect it with at least reasonable care.

9.2 Government's Confidential Information includes any CUI, CDI, or other Government information identified as sensitive, and any Government information that is subject to FOIA exemptions.

9.3 Compelled Disclosure. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, court order, or by a Government audit or inspection right.

9.4 Survival. The obligations in this Article 9 shall survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement indefinitely or for as long as required by applicable law.
