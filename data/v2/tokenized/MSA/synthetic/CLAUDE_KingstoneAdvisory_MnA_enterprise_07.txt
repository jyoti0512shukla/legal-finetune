INVESTMENT BANKING ENGAGEMENT AND ADVISORY SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Investment Banking Engagement and Advisory Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date"), by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Advisor"), having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}}, and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Company"), having its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}}. The Advisor and the Company may each be referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

The Company wishes to engage the Advisor as its non-exclusive financial advisor in connection with potential strategic transactions, including without limitation potential acquisitions, divestitures, mergers, recapitalizations, restructurings, and capital raises (each, a "Transaction"). The Advisor is a registered broker-dealer with the United States Securities and Exchange Commission and a member of the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority ("FINRA"). This Agreement shall govern all engagements between the Parties pursuant to one or more Engagement Letters executed under this Agreement.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Advisor Personnel" means the partners, managing directors, vice presidents, associates, and analysts of the Advisor and its Affiliates assigned to perform the Services.

1.2 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with another entity.

1.3 "Background IP" means any Intellectual Property Rights owned or licensable by a Party prior to the Effective Date or independently developed outside of any Engagement Letter, including the Advisor's financial models, valuation methodologies, deal databases, and proprietary research.

1.4 "Closing" means the consummation of a Transaction.

1.5 "Company Information" means any non-public information about the Company, its Affiliates, its business, its financial condition, its strategic plans, its customers, its suppliers, its employees, its intellectual property, and any potential Transaction, that is provided to the Advisor by the Company in connection with the Services.

1.6 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with the Services, including Company Information.

1.7 "Engagement Letter" means a written letter executed by both Parties in respect of a particular Transaction or class of Transactions, which incorporates this Agreement by reference and which sets forth the scope of the engagement, the fee structure (including any retainer, monthly fees, and Transaction-based success fees), the Transaction parameters, and any matter-specific terms.

1.8 "FINRA" means the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority.

1.9 "Intellectual Property Rights" means all intellectual property and proprietary rights of any kind anywhere in the world.

1.10 "Services" means the financial advisory services to be performed by the Advisor for the Company under one or more Engagement Letters, including identification and evaluation of potential Transactions, valuation analysis, transaction structuring advice, negotiation support, and assistance with diligence, marketing materials, and Closing.

1.11 "Transaction Value" means the aggregate value of consideration paid, received, or implied in a Transaction, calculated as set forth in the applicable Engagement Letter.

1.12 "Work Product" means the financial models, valuation analyses, presentation materials, fairness opinions, and other tangible work product prepared by the Advisor specifically for the Company in connection with the Services.

ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 Engagement Letters. The Advisor shall provide the Services to the Company only pursuant to an Engagement Letter executed by both Parties. Each Engagement Letter shall describe (a) the scope of the Services for the relevant Transaction or class of Transactions; (b) the Transaction parameters (including potential targets, sizing, geographic scope, and timing); (c) the fee structure; (d) the term of the engagement; (e) any retention bonus or expense advance; and (f) any matter-specific terms.

2.2 Order of Precedence. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and an Engagement Letter, this Agreement shall control as to the matters expressly addressed, except that the Engagement Letter may expressly modify any provision solely as it applies to that specific engagement.

2.3 Standard of Performance. The Advisor shall perform the Services with the diligence, skill, and judgment ordinarily exercised by experienced investment banking professionals at major investment banking firms.

2.4 Non-Exclusivity. Unless an Engagement Letter expressly grants the Advisor exclusivity for a specific Transaction or potential Transaction, the engagement of the Advisor shall be on a non-exclusive basis, and the Company may engage other financial advisors in connection with any Transaction.

2.5 No Fiduciary Relationship. The Parties acknowledge that the Advisor is acting solely as an arm's length contractual party and not as a fiduciary or agent of the Company. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as creating a fiduciary relationship between the Advisor and the Company, or between the Advisor and any shareholder, director, officer, employee, or creditor of the Company.

2.6 Reliance on Company Information. The Advisor shall be entitled to rely upon the accuracy and completeness of all Company Information provided to it by the Company without independent verification, and the Advisor shall have no obligation to verify the accuracy or completeness of any such Company Information.

2.7 Cooperation. The Company shall provide the Advisor with such Company Information, personnel access, and management cooperation as the Advisor reasonably requires to perform the Services, including reasonable access to the Company's senior management, accounting records, and data room.

ARTICLE 3 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Work Product. The Advisor shall deliver Work Product to the Company in accordance with the schedule set forth in the applicable Engagement Letter or as otherwise agreed.

3.2 No Acceptance Mechanic for Financial Advice. The Parties acknowledge that financial advice and valuation work are inherently judgmental and that no formal acceptance mechanic applies to the Advisor's Work Product. The Company may, however, request revisions to the Work Product, and the Advisor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to address the Company's reasonable comments at no additional charge.

3.3 Fairness Opinions. Where an Engagement Letter calls for the Advisor to deliver a fairness opinion, the fairness opinion shall be issued in the form of a separate written opinion letter and shall be subject to such customary opinion conditions, qualifications, and limitations as the Advisor's fairness opinion committee determines to be appropriate. The Company shall not represent or distribute the fairness opinion in any manner not expressly authorized by the opinion letter.

ARTICLE 4 — FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fee Structure. The fee structure for each engagement shall be set forth in the applicable Engagement Letter and may include any combination of (a) an upfront retainer fee, (b) monthly retainer fees, (c) progress fees, (d) a success fee contingent upon Closing of a Transaction, and (e) discretionary fees agreed by the Parties.

4.2 Annual Aggregate Reference. The aggregate annual fees payable by the Company across all then-active Engagement Letters as of the Effective Date is approximately {{ANNUAL_FEE_AMOUNT}}, included for reference only and depending heavily on whether any Transactions Close during the year.

4.3 Success Fee. Where an Engagement Letter provides for a success fee contingent upon Closing, the success fee shall be calculated as a percentage of Transaction Value as set forth in the Engagement Letter. The success fee shall be due and payable in cash at Closing, by wire transfer of immediately available funds. The success fee shall be earned and payable regardless of whether the Transaction is consummated with a counterparty introduced by the Advisor or otherwise, provided that the Transaction Closes during the term of the Engagement Letter or during the tail period set forth in the Engagement Letter (typically twelve (12) to twenty-four (24) months following expiration or termination of the Engagement Letter).

4.4 Tail Period. The tail period set forth in the applicable Engagement Letter shall apply to any Transaction that Closes after expiration or termination of the Engagement Letter with a counterparty (a) introduced to the Company by the Advisor during the term of the Engagement Letter, (b) with whom the Advisor had substantive discussions during the term of the Engagement Letter, or (c) included on a list of covered counterparties exchanged at the end of the Engagement Letter. The Advisor's success fee shall be earned and payable on any such tail-period Transaction.

4.5 Retainer Credit. Any monthly retainer fees paid by the Company shall be credited (in whole or in part as set forth in the applicable Engagement Letter) against any success fee payable upon Closing of a Transaction.

4.6 Expenses. The Advisor shall be reimbursed for reasonable and documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with the Services, including travel, due diligence costs, data room subscriptions, and outside counsel fees. The Company shall pay or reimburse such expenses promptly upon receipt of an invoice. Where the Engagement Letter provides for an expense cap, expenses shall not exceed the cap without the Company's prior written consent.

4.7 Payment Terms. Retainer fees and expense reimbursements shall be due and payable within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice. Success fees shall be due and payable at Closing.

4.8 Disputed Invoices. The Company shall notify the Advisor of any disputed amount within fifteen (15) business days of receipt of the invoice and shall pay all undisputed amounts on time.

4.9 Taxes. Fees are exclusive of all taxes other than taxes imposed on the Advisor's net income.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 Term of this Agreement. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 13.

5.2 Term of Engagement Letters. Each Engagement Letter shall continue for the term set forth therein. Termination of an individual Engagement Letter shall not, by itself, terminate this Agreement or any other Engagement Letter.

5.3 Survival of Tail Period. Notwithstanding any termination of this Agreement or any Engagement Letter, the tail period set forth in Section 4.4 shall survive and shall continue to apply to any Transaction that Closes during the tail period with a covered counterparty.

ARTICLE 6 — KEY PERSONNEL

6.1 Engagement Team. Each Engagement Letter shall identify the lead Advisor Personnel responsible for the engagement, including the lead managing director and the deal team.

6.2 Replacement of Lead Personnel. The Advisor shall not replace the lead managing director identified in an Engagement Letter without the Company's prior written consent, except in the case of (a) the death or disability of the lead managing director, (b) the departure of the lead managing director from the Advisor, or (c) the resolution of a conflict of interest. In any such case, the Advisor shall propose a replacement of comparable seniority and experience within fifteen (15) days.

6.3 Conflicts of Interest. The Advisor shall conduct conflict checks in accordance with FINRA rules and the Advisor's compliance policies before accepting any engagement. The Advisor shall promptly notify the Company of any actual or potential conflict of interest identified at the time of engagement or arising during the engagement, and shall not proceed with the engagement without the Company's informed written consent or the resolution of the conflict in accordance with FINRA rules.

6.4 Information Barriers. To the extent the Advisor identifies a potential conflict between the Company's engagement and the Advisor's other clients or the Advisor's own proprietary trading or investment activities, the Advisor shall implement and maintain information barriers (including ethical walls and personnel separation) reasonably designed to protect the Company's Confidential Information.

6.5 Non-Solicitation of Personnel. During the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months thereafter, the Company shall not solicit or hire any Advisor Personnel who performed Services for the Company; provided, however, that this restriction shall not apply to general public employment advertising not directed at Advisor Personnel.

ARTICLE 7 — COMPANY INFORMATION AND CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Use of Company Information. The Advisor shall use Company Information solely for the purposes of performing the Services in accordance with the applicable Engagement Letter. The Advisor shall not use Company Information for the benefit of any other client of the Advisor, for proprietary trading or investment, or for any other purpose.

7.2 Disclosure to Advisor Personnel. The Advisor may disclose Company Information to its personnel and Affiliates who have a need to know in connection with the Services, who are bound by obligations of confidentiality at least as protective as those set forth in this Agreement, and who are subject to applicable information barriers. The Advisor shall remain responsible for any breach of this Agreement by such personnel or Affiliates.

7.3 Disclosure to Counterparties. The Advisor may disclose Company Information to potential Transaction counterparties only after such counterparty has executed a confidentiality agreement reasonably acceptable to the Company, and only as necessary to evaluate the potential Transaction.

7.4 Securities Law Compliance. The Advisor and the Company acknowledge that some Company Information may constitute material non-public information about the Company. The Advisor shall ensure that all Advisor Personnel who receive material non-public information are placed on the Advisor's restricted list and shall comply with applicable insider trading laws and Advisor's compliance policies regarding material non-public information.

7.5 Data Security. The Advisor shall implement and maintain reasonable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Company Information against unauthorized access, including encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and a documented information security program.

7.6 Compelled Disclosure. The Advisor may disclose Company Information to the extent required by court order or applicable law, or to FINRA, the SEC, or other regulators with examination authority over the Advisor, in which case the Advisor shall give the Company prompt notice (where legally permitted).

7.7 Return of Company Information. Upon completion of the Services or upon the Company's earlier written request, the Advisor shall return or destroy all Company Information in its possession or control, except that the Advisor may retain (a) one archival copy of the Work Product as required by FINRA recordkeeping rules, and (b) Company Information embedded in the Advisor's working files for the duration of the Advisor's records retention obligations under FINRA rules and applicable law.

ARTICLE 8 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

8.1 Background IP. As between the Parties, each Party shall retain all right, title, and interest in and to its respective Background IP. The Advisor's Background IP includes the Advisor's financial models, valuation methodologies, deal databases, market intelligence, proprietary research, and presentation templates.

8.2 Ownership of Work Product. Work Product prepared by the Advisor specifically for the Company in connection with the Services shall be owned by the Company, subject to (a) the Advisor's right to retain copies for its own files in accordance with FINRA recordkeeping rules and the Advisor's records retention policies, and (b) the Advisor's right to use Background IP embedded in such Work Product for other clients and purposes. The Advisor hereby assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to such Work Product, to the extent assignable.

8.3 License to Background IP. To the extent any Work Product incorporates the Advisor's Background IP, the Advisor hereby grants to the Company a perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use such Background IP solely as embedded in the Work Product and solely for the Company's internal use in evaluating, structuring, or consummating the relevant Transaction.

8.4 Restrictions on Use of Work Product. The Company shall not represent or distribute any Work Product (including any fairness opinion) in any manner not expressly authorized in writing by the Advisor.

8.5 No Implied Licenses. Except for the limited rights expressly granted, neither Party grants any rights or licenses to the other Party.

ARTICLE 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Confidentiality Obligations. Each Party shall (a) hold the other Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use it solely for the purposes of this Agreement; (c) not disclose it except as permitted by Article 7 (with respect to Company Information) or by this Article 9; and (d) protect it with at least reasonable care.

9.2 Compelled Disclosure. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by court order or applicable law (or by FINRA, the SEC, or other regulators with examination authority over the Advisor), with prompt notice to the Disclosing Party where legally permitted.

9.3 Survival. The obligations in this Article 9 shall survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

ARTICLE 10 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

10.1 Mutual Warranties. Each Party represents and warrants that (a) it is duly organized and in good standing; (b) it has the corporate authority to enter into this Agreement; and (c) this Agreement constitutes a binding obligation enforceable against it.

10.2 Advisor Warranties. The Advisor represents, warrants, and covenants that (a) the Advisor is duly registered as a broker-dealer with the SEC and is a member in good standing of FINRA; (b) the Advisor is not currently the subject of any FINRA or SEC enforcement action that materially affects its ability to perform under this Agreement; (c) the Services shall be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with the standards of the investment banking industry; and (d) the Advisor shall comply with FINRA rules, the Securities Act of 1933, the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, and all other applicable securities laws and regulations in performing the Services.

10.3 Company Warranties. The Company represents and warrants that (a) the Company has the right to engage the Advisor and (b) the Company Information provided to the Advisor is accurate and complete in all material respects to the best of the Company's knowledge.

THE ADVISOR DOES NOT WARRANT THE ACCURACY OR COMPLETENESS OF ANY COMPANY INFORMATION ON WHICH IT RELIES.

ARTICLE 11 — INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 Company Indemnity. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the Company shall indemnify and hold harmless the Advisor and its Affiliates and their respective directors, officers, employees, agents, and shareholders (each an "Indemnified Party") against any losses, claims, damages, liabilities, or expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) incurred by such Indemnified Party arising out of or relating to the Services or this Agreement, except to the extent any such loss, claim, damage, liability, or expense results from the gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud of the Indemnified Party.

11.2 Limitation on Company Indemnity. Notwithstanding Section 11.1, the Company's indemnification obligation shall not extend to any losses arising from (a) the Advisor's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud; (b) the Advisor's violation of FINRA rules or applicable securities laws; (c) the Advisor's breach of this Agreement; or (d) any liability that, under applicable law, the Company is not permitted to indemnify.

11.3 Contribution. If the indemnification provided in this Article 11 is unavailable for any reason, then in lieu of indemnification, the Company and the Advisor shall contribute to the losses in such proportion as is appropriate to reflect the relative benefits received and the relative fault of the Parties.

11.4 Indemnification Procedure. The Indemnified Party shall (a) provide prompt written notice to the Company; (b) cooperate with the Company in the defense and settlement of the claim; and (c) not enter into any settlement without the Company's prior written consent. The Company shall have the right (but not the obligation) to assume the defense of any claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party.

12.1 Aggregate Cap. EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, IN NO EVENT SHALL THE AGGREGATE LIABILITY OF THE ADVISOR ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT OR ANY ENGAGEMENT LETTER, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT (INCLUDING NEGLIGENCE), OR ANY OTHER LEGAL OR EQUITABLE THEORY, EXCEED THE GREATER OF (A) THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY THE COMPANY TO THE ADVISOR UNDER THE AFFECTED ENGAGEMENT LETTER DURING THE {{LIABILITY_CAP_MONTHS}} MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY, OR (B) ANY APPLICABLE INSURANCE COVERAGE PROCEEDS RECEIVED BY THE ADVISOR.

12.2 Exclusion of Indirect Damages. EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER PARTY FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, EXEMPLARY, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION DAMAGES FOR LOST PROFITS, LOST OPPORTUNITY, OR LOST TRANSACTION VALUE, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT.

12.3 Exceptions to Limitations. The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 shall not apply to (a) the Company's payment obligations, including any success fee payable upon Closing; (b) any breach of Article 9 (Confidentiality) or Article 7 (Company Information and Confidentiality); (c) any liability arising from a Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud; (d) any liability arising from the Advisor's violation of FINRA rules or applicable securities laws; or (e) any liability that, under applicable law, cannot be limited.

12.4 Basis of the Bargain. The limitations of liability are an essential element of the bargain between the Parties.

ARTICLE 13 — TERMINATION

13.1 Termination for Material Breach. Either Party may terminate this Agreement, or any Engagement Letter, upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure such breach within the notice period.

13.2 Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate this Agreement, or any Engagement Letter, for convenience at any time upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Notwithstanding such termination, (a) the Company shall remain obligated to pay any retainer fees and expense reimbursements that have accrued through the effective date of termination, and (b) the Advisor's right to a success fee in respect of any Transaction that Closes during the tail period set forth in the applicable Engagement Letter shall survive termination.

13.3 Termination for Insolvency. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, files a voluntary petition in bankruptcy, or has an involuntary petition filed against it that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.4 Effects of Termination. Upon expiration or termination: (a) the Advisor shall promptly cease performance of the Services; (b) the Advisor shall return Company Information in accordance with Section 7.7; (c) the Company shall pay the Advisor for all undisputed Fees and expenses that have accrued; (d) the tail period for success fees shall continue to apply as set forth in the applicable Engagement Letter; (e) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information; and (f) the provisions that by their nature are intended to survive (including Articles 1, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, and 14) shall so survive.
