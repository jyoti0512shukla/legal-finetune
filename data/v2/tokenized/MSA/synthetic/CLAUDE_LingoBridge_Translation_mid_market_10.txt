MASTER TRANSLATION AND LOCALIZATION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Translation and Localization Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date"), by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Language Service Provider" or "LSP"), having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}}, and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Client"), having its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}}.

This Agreement is a master agreement only. The specific source materials, target languages, deliverable formats, schedules, per-word or per-hour rates, and any project-specific terms shall be set forth in one or more Statements of Work executed under this Agreement (each, a "SOW") and incorporated by reference into this Agreement. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and a SOW, this Agreement shall control as to the matters expressly addressed in this Agreement, except where the SOW expressly modifies as it applies to the relevant project.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with another entity.

1.2 "Background IP" means any Intellectual Property Rights owned or licensable by a Party prior to the Effective Date or independently developed outside of any SOW, including the LSP's translation memory system, glossaries, terminology databases, machine translation models, and quality assurance methodologies.

1.3 "Client Materials" means the source materials provided by the Client to the LSP for translation or localization, including without limitation product strings, marketing copy, legal documents, technical documentation, software resource files, audio scripts, and any reference materials.

1.4 "Deliverables" means the translated, localized, or otherwise processed materials prepared by the LSP for the Client under a SOW.

1.5 "Linguist" means an individual translator, editor, proofreader, or localization engineer employed or contracted by the LSP to perform Services.

1.6 "Machine Translation" or "MT" means the use of automated translation systems (whether the LSP's proprietary engines or third-party machine translation services) to produce or assist in producing translations.

1.7 "Services" means the translation, localization, transcreation, post-editing, terminology management, and related language services to be performed by the LSP for the Client under one or more SOWs.

1.8 "Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a written document executed by both Parties describing a particular project.

1.9 "Translation Memory" or "TM" means the bilingual database of source text and target text segments built up through the translation process.

1.10 "Work Product" means all Deliverables and all Translation Memory entries, glossaries, and terminology assets created in the course of performing the Services for the Client.

ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 Engagement. The LSP shall provide the Services described in each SOW. Each SOW shall specify (a) the source language and target language(s); (b) the source materials and word count; (c) the per-word or per-hour rate; (d) the delivery format; (e) the schedule; (f) the quality tier (such as standard translation, premium translation, transcreation, post-edited machine translation, or raw machine translation); (g) any required terminology references; and (h) any project-specific terms.

2.2 Order of Precedence. As described in the preamble.

2.3 Standard of Performance. The LSP shall perform the Services in accordance with the quality standards of the localization industry (including ASTM F2575 and ISO 17100 where applicable) and the quality tier specified in the applicable SOW.

2.4 No MT Without Disclosure. The LSP shall not use Machine Translation in connection with any Service designated as a "human translation" tier in the applicable SOW. Where a SOW expressly permits Machine Translation (post-edited MT or raw MT tiers), the LSP shall disclose to the Client which segments were produced with MT assistance.

2.5 Cooperation. The Client shall provide reference materials, glossaries, style guides, and reasonable access to subject matter experts to assist the LSP in producing accurate translations.

ARTICLE 3 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Delivery. The LSP shall deliver each Deliverable in the format and on the schedule set forth in the applicable SOW.

3.2 Acceptance Period. The Client shall have a period of ten (10) business days following delivery of each Deliverable to review the Deliverable and notify the LSP in writing of acceptance or of any rejection accompanied by a reasonably detailed description of the alleged non-conformity (such as mistranslation, terminology errors, omissions, or formatting issues).

3.3 Cure of Non-Conformity. The LSP shall correct rejected Deliverables at no additional charge within five (5) business days. After two (2) cycles of cure, the Client may accept the Deliverable with an equitable Fee reduction or terminate the affected SOW and recover Fees paid for the rejected Deliverable.

3.4 Deemed Acceptance. If the Client fails to provide written acceptance or rejection within the Acceptance Period, the Deliverable shall be deemed accepted.

3.5 Linguistic Quality Standards. The LSP shall provide a quality assurance score for each Deliverable based on a sample-based linguistic quality review using an industry-standard error typology (such as MQM or LISA QA), as set forth in the applicable SOW.

ARTICLE 4 — FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fees. The Client shall pay the LSP the Fees set forth in each SOW, typically calculated on a per-word basis (with the source word count as the basis), with discounts for repetitions and high-fuzzy-match Translation Memory matches as set forth in the SOW.

4.2 Annual Aggregate Reference. The aggregate annual Fees payable by the Client across all then-active SOWs as of the Effective Date is approximately {{ANNUAL_FEE_AMOUNT}}, included for reference only.

4.3 TM Discount Tiers. Where the LSP uses the Client's Translation Memory in producing the Deliverables, the Fees shall reflect the standard industry discount tiers for high-fuzzy-match (75-99%) and exact-match (100%) segments, as set forth in the applicable SOW.

4.4 Rush Fees. Projects requiring expedited delivery may carry a rush fee as set forth in the applicable SOW, typically calculated as a percentage uplift on the standard per-word rate.

4.5 Payment Terms. All undisputed invoices shall be due and payable within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice.

4.6 Disputed Invoices. The Client shall notify the LSP of any disputed amount within fifteen (15) business days and shall pay all undisputed amounts on time.

4.7 Taxes. Fees are exclusive of all taxes other than taxes imposed on the LSP's net income.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 Initial Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 13.

5.2 Renewal. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

ARTICLE 6 — LINGUISTS AND STAFFING

6.1 Qualifications. The LSP shall ensure that all Linguists assigned to perform the Services possess the qualifications, native-language fluency, and subject matter expertise required for the relevant project.

6.2 Approved Linguist Pool. Where the Client approves a specific pool of Linguists for its account, the LSP shall use only those approved Linguists for the Client's projects, except in case of unavailability and with the Client's prior consent.

6.3 Confidentiality of Linguists. All Linguists shall be bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement.

6.4 Subcontracting. The LSP may use freelance Linguists and other approved subcontractors to perform the Services, subject to the confidentiality and quality obligations of this Agreement. The LSP shall remain primarily responsible for all Services performed by subcontractors.

6.5 Removal of Linguists on Client Request. The Client may request that the LSP remove any Linguist from the Client's account on quality or fit grounds. The LSP shall comply with such request promptly.

6.6 Non-Solicitation. During the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months thereafter, the Client shall not solicit or hire any Linguist who has performed Services for the Client.

ARTICLE 7 — CLIENT MATERIALS AND DATA

7.1 Ownership of Client Materials. As between the Parties, the Client shall retain all right, title, and interest in and to Client Materials.

7.2 Permitted Uses. The LSP shall use Client Materials solely as necessary to perform the Services.

7.3 No Use of Client Materials for MT Training. The LSP shall not use Client Materials to train any machine translation model — whether the LSP's own engines, any third-party MT engine, or any large language model — without the Client's prior written consent. This is a material restriction.

7.4 Confidentiality of Client Source Materials. The LSP shall treat all Client Materials as Confidential Information of the Client and shall not disclose them to any third party except to approved Linguists and subcontractors bound by similar confidentiality obligations.

7.5 Data Security. The LSP shall implement reasonable safeguards to protect Client Materials, including encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and a documented information security program.

7.6 Breach Notification. The LSP shall notify the Client of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to Client Materials within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery.

7.7 Return of Client Materials. Upon completion of the relevant project, the LSP shall return or destroy Client Materials in its possession.

ARTICLE 8 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

8.1 Background IP. Each Party retains its respective Background IP. The LSP's Background IP includes the LSP's translation memory system software, machine translation engines, and quality assurance tools. The Client's Background IP includes the Client's brand, source materials, and pre-existing terminology assets.

8.2 Assignment of Work Product. Subject to Section 8.3 and to the Client's payment of all Fees due in respect of the applicable project, the LSP hereby irrevocably assigns to the Client all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all translated text, all Translation Memory entries created in the course of translating Client Materials, and all glossaries and terminology assets developed for the Client. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable copyright law, the Work Product shall be deemed "work made for hire" for the Client; to the extent it does not so qualify, the LSP hereby assigns it to the Client.

8.3 Translation Memory Ownership. The Translation Memory built from Client Materials and the Deliverables is the exclusive property of the Client. Upon the Client's request, the LSP shall deliver the Client's Translation Memory to the Client in TMX format at no additional charge. The LSP shall not use the Client's Translation Memory for the benefit of any other client, for training any MT model, or for any purpose other than performing Services for the Client.

8.4 License to LSP Background IP. To the extent any Work Product incorporates the LSP's Background IP (such as TM system features), the LSP hereby grants the Client a perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use such Background IP solely as embedded in the Work Product.

8.5 Aggregate Linguistic Insights. The LSP may use de-identified, aggregated linguistic insights derived from work across multiple clients (such as the average word count per document, average turnaround time, or general pricing benchmarks) for industry analysis, provided that such insights do not include any Client Materials or Translation Memory.

8.6 No Implied Licenses. Except for the limited rights expressly granted, neither Party grants any rights or licenses to the other Party.

ARTICLE 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Confidentiality Obligations. Each Party shall hold the other Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence, use it solely for purposes of this Agreement, share it only with personnel and subcontractors who have a need to know and are bound by similar confidentiality obligations, and protect it with at least reasonable care.

9.2 Exclusions. Confidentiality obligations shall not apply to information that is already public, was already known, was independently developed, or was rightfully received from a third party.

9.3 Compelled Disclosure. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by court order or applicable law, with prompt notice to the Disclosing Party where legally permitted.

9.4 Survival. The obligations in this Article 9 shall survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

10.2 LSP Warranties. The LSP warrants that (a) the Services shall be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner; (b) the Deliverables shall conform to the quality tier and specifications set forth in the applicable SOW; (c) all Linguists are duly qualified for the projects assigned; and (d) the LSP shall not use Machine Translation in any Service designated as a human translation tier.

10.3 Client Warranties. The Client warrants that it has the right to provide Client Materials to the LSP for translation and that the Client Materials do not infringe any third-party rights.

10.4 Disclaimer. EXCEPT FOR THE EXPRESS WARRANTIES SET FORTH IN THIS ARTICLE 10, THE SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES ARE PROVIDED ON AN "AS IS" BASIS. THE LSP DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED. THE LSP DOES NOT WARRANT ANY SPECIFIC LEGAL, COMMERCIAL, OR CULTURAL OUTCOME FROM THE CLIENT'S USE OF THE TRANSLATED MATERIALS.

ARTICLE 11 — INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 LSP Indemnity. The LSP shall defend the Client against any third-party claim alleging that the Deliverables, when used by the Client in accordance with this Agreement, infringe any Intellectual Property Right of such third party.

11.2 Client Indemnity. The Client shall defend the LSP against any third-party claim alleging that Client Materials infringe any third-party right.

11.3 Indemnification Procedure. The Party seeking indemnification shall provide prompt written notice, grant the indemnifying Party control over the defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 shall not apply to (a) the LSP's obligations under Article 11; (b) any breach of Article 9 (Confidentiality); (c) any breach of Section 7.3 (No Use of Client Materials for MT Training) or Section 8.3 (Translation Memory Ownership); (d) the Client's payment obligations; (e) any liability arising from gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud; or (f) any liability that, under applicable law, cannot be limited.

12.4 Basis of the Bargain. The limitations of liability are an essential element of the bargain between the Parties.

ARTICLE 13 — TERMINATION

13.1 Termination for Material Breach. Either Party may terminate this Agreement, or any SOW, upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure within the notice period.

13.2 Termination for Convenience. The Client may terminate any SOW for convenience at any time. Upon termination for convenience, the Client shall pay the LSP for all Services performed and Deliverables accepted through the effective date of termination, plus a reasonable termination charge for in-progress work that is no longer needed.

13.3 Termination for Insolvency. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy.

13.4 Effects of Termination. Upon termination: (a) the LSP shall promptly cease performance of Services; (b) the LSP shall deliver to the Client all completed Deliverables and the Client's Translation Memory in TMX format; (c) the LSP shall return Client Materials in accordance with Section 7.7; (d) the Client shall pay the LSP for accepted Services; (e) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information; and (f) the provisions that by their nature are intended to survive shall so survive.
