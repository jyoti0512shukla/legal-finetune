MASTER CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Consulting Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date"), by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Consultant"), having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}}, and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "Client"), having its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}}.

This Agreement establishes the master terms under which the Consultant may, from time to time, perform consulting services for the Client. This Agreement, by itself, does not obligate the Client to engage the Consultant for any specific work or to pay any fees other than as set forth in a duly executed Statement of Work. All specific terms applicable to a particular engagement — including the scope, the deliverables, the personnel, the fees, the schedule, and any project-specific terms — shall be set forth in one or more Statements of Work executed by both Parties under this Agreement (each, a "Statement of Work" or "SOW") and incorporated by reference into this Agreement. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and a Statement of Work, this Agreement shall control as to the matters expressly addressed in this Agreement, except that the Statement of Work may expressly modify any provision of this Agreement solely as it applies to that specific engagement.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with another entity.

1.2 "Background IP" means any Intellectual Property Rights owned or licensable by a Party prior to the Effective Date or independently developed outside of any SOW.

1.3 "Client Materials" means any data, information, documents, records, materials, software, hardware, facilities, or other resources made available by or on behalf of the Client to the Consultant in connection with the performance of any SOW.

1.4 "Deliverables" means the tangible work product, reports, presentations, models, software code, recommendations, and other items that the Consultant is required to deliver to the Client under a SOW.

1.5 "Intellectual Property Rights" means all intellectual property and proprietary rights of any kind anywhere in the world.

1.6 "Services" means the consulting services to be performed by the Consultant for the Client under one or more SOWs.

1.7 "Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a written document executed by both Parties that describes a particular engagement.

1.8 "Work Product" means all Deliverables and other work product, materials, ideas, concepts, designs, and inventions created, conceived, or first reduced to practice by or on behalf of the Consultant in the course of performing any SOW, in each case excluding Background IP.

ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 Engagement of Services. The Consultant shall provide the Services described in each SOW executed by the Parties from time to time during the Term. Each SOW shall describe in reasonable detail (a) the scope of the Services; (b) the Deliverables; (c) the personnel assigned; (d) the Fees and the basis on which they are calculated (whether fixed fee, time and materials, milestone-based, or otherwise); (e) the schedule; (f) the acceptance criteria; and (g) any project-specific terms.

2.2 Order of Precedence. As described in the preamble, this Agreement controls except where the SOW expressly modifies as it applies to the relevant engagement.

2.3 No Obligation to Engage. Nothing in this Agreement shall obligate the Client to engage the Consultant for any specific engagement or to issue any minimum number of SOWs.

2.4 Standard of Performance. The Consultant shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the standards of the consulting industry, by individuals possessing the qualifications, skills, and expertise required.

2.5 Cooperation. The Client shall provide the Consultant with such Client Materials, personnel, facilities, and information as the Consultant reasonably requires to perform the Services. The Consultant shall not be responsible for delays attributable to the Client's failure to provide such cooperation.

ARTICLE 3 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Delivery. The Consultant shall deliver each Deliverable in accordance with the schedule and in the format set forth in the applicable SOW.

3.2 Acceptance Period. Unless otherwise specified in the applicable SOW, the Client shall have a period of fifteen (15) business days following delivery of each Deliverable to review the Deliverable and notify the Consultant in writing of (a) acceptance, or (b) rejection accompanied by a detailed description of the alleged non-conformity with the acceptance criteria set forth in the applicable SOW.

3.3 Cure of Non-Conformity. If the Client rejects a Deliverable, the Consultant shall use commercially reasonable efforts at no additional charge to correct the non-conformity within fifteen (15) business days and resubmit the corrected Deliverable. If the Consultant fails to correct the non-conformity after two (2) cycles of cure, the Client may, at its option, (a) accept the Deliverable in its then-current form with an equitable reduction in the Fees attributable to such Deliverable, or (b) terminate the affected SOW and recover any Fees paid for the rejected Deliverable.

3.4 Deemed Acceptance. If the Client fails to provide the Consultant with written acceptance or rejection within the Acceptance Period, the Deliverable shall be deemed accepted by the Client.

3.5 Change Orders. Any change to the scope, schedule, or Fees set forth in a SOW shall be documented in a written change order signed by both Parties and incorporated into the affected SOW. Change orders may result in adjustments to fees, deliverables, or schedules, as set forth therein.

ARTICLE 4 — FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fees. The Client shall pay the Consultant the Fees set forth in each SOW. The basis for Fees may include time and materials at the hourly rates set forth in the SOW, fixed fees per Deliverable, milestone-based fees, blended-rate fees, or any other basis the Parties agree to in the SOW.

4.2 Annual Aggregate Reference. The aggregate annual Fees payable by the Client across all then-active SOWs as of the Effective Date is approximately {{ANNUAL_FEE_AMOUNT}}, included for reference only and updated by reference to the SOWs then in effect from time to time.

4.3 Expenses. Reasonable, pre-approved travel and out-of-pocket expenses incurred by the Consultant in performing the Services shall be reimbursed by the Client at cost without markup, accompanied by reasonable documentation. No expense in excess of One Thousand United States Dollars ($1,000) shall be reimbursable unless pre-approved in writing by the Client.

4.4 Invoicing. The Consultant shall invoice the Client monthly in arrears for Services performed and expenses incurred during the preceding month.

4.5 Payment Terms. All undisputed invoices shall be due and payable within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice. Late payments shall accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law.

4.6 Disputed Invoices. The Client shall notify the Consultant of any disputed amount within fifteen (15) business days of receipt of the invoice and shall pay all undisputed amounts on time.

4.7 Taxes. Fees are exclusive of all taxes other than taxes imposed on the Consultant's net income.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 Initial Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of {{TERM_YEARS}} years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 13.

5.2 Renewal. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

5.3 Effect of Agreement Termination on SOWs. Termination of this Agreement shall not, by itself, terminate any SOW that is then in effect, except as expressly provided in Article 13.

ARTICLE 6 — KEY PERSONNEL AND STAFFING

6.1 Designated Personnel. Each SOW may identify specific personnel of the Consultant who are committed to perform the Services. The Consultant shall not replace any such designated personnel without the Client's prior written consent except in the case of death, disability, or departure from the Consultant.

6.2 Background Checks. The Consultant shall ensure that all personnel performing the Services have undergone background investigations consistent with the Consultant's standard practice.

6.3 Removal of Personnel on Client Request. The Client may, at any time and for any reason that does not violate applicable employment law, request that the Consultant remove any individual personnel from the performance of the Services. The Consultant shall comply with any such request promptly.

6.4 Non-Solicitation. During the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months thereafter, the Client shall not solicit or hire any personnel of the Consultant who performed Services for the Client, except through general public employment advertising or after a six-month break in the personnel's employment with the Consultant.

ARTICLE 7 — CLIENT MATERIALS AND DATA

7.1 Ownership of Client Materials. As between the Parties, the Client shall retain all right, title, and interest in and to Client Materials.

7.2 Limited License. The Client grants the Consultant a limited license to use Client Materials solely as necessary to perform the Services in accordance with the applicable SOW.

7.3 Return of Client Materials. Upon completion of the relevant engagement or upon the Client's earlier written request, the Consultant shall return or destroy all Client Materials in its possession or control.

7.4 Data Security. The Consultant shall implement and maintain reasonable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Client Materials, including encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and a documented information security program.

7.5 Breach Notification. The Consultant shall notify the Client of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to Client Materials within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery.

ARTICLE 8 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

8.1 Background IP. As between the Parties, each Party shall retain all right, title, and interest in and to its respective Background IP. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to transfer any Background IP from one Party to the other.

8.2 Assignment of Work Product. Subject to Section 8.3 and to the Client's payment of all Fees due in respect of the applicable engagement, the Consultant hereby irrevocably assigns to the Client all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all Intellectual Property Rights therein. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable copyright law, the Work Product shall be deemed "work made for hire" for the Client. To the extent it does not so qualify, the Consultant hereby assigns it to the Client, and shall ensure that all of its personnel have executed agreements sufficient to vest in the Consultant the rights necessary to make the assignment in this Section 8.2.

8.3 License to Background IP. To the extent any Work Product incorporates the Consultant's Background IP, the Consultant hereby grants the Client a perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to use such Background IP solely as embedded in the Work Product.

8.4 Residual Knowledge. The Consultant may use for any purpose the general knowledge, skills, experience, and ideas (other than Confidential Information of the Client and Client Materials) that the Consultant's personnel retain in unaided memory after performing Services for the Client.

8.5 Feedback. The Client may provide the Consultant with feedback regarding the Services. The Client hereby grants the Consultant a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide license to use such feedback to improve its services, provided that the Consultant shall not use any Client Materials in connection with such use of feedback.

8.6 No Implied Licenses. Except for the limited rights expressly granted in this Article 8, neither Party grants any rights or licenses to the other Party.

ARTICLE 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Confidentiality Obligations. Each Party shall (a) hold the other Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use it solely for purposes of this Agreement; (c) not disclose it except to its employees, contractors, and advisors who have a need to know and are bound by similar confidentiality obligations; and (d) protect it with at least reasonable care.

9.2 Exclusions. Confidentiality obligations shall not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate (a) was already known without obligation of confidentiality; (b) was independently developed; (c) was rightfully received from a third party; or (d) is or becomes public through no fault of the Receiving Party.

9.3 Compelled Disclosure. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by court order or applicable law, provided the Receiving Party gives the Disclosing Party prompt notice (where legally permitted).

9.4 Survival. The obligations in this Article 9 shall survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

ARTICLE 10 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

10.1 Mutual Warranties. Each Party represents and warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, has the corporate authority to enter into this Agreement, and that this Agreement constitutes a binding obligation enforceable against it.

10.2 Consultant Warranties. The Consultant warrants that (a) the Services shall be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards; (b) the Deliverables shall conform to the acceptance criteria set forth in the applicable SOW in all material respects; and (c) the Deliverables shall not, when used by the Client in accordance with this Agreement, infringe any Intellectual Property Right of any third party.

10.3 Client Warranties. The Client warrants that it has the right to provide Client Materials to the Consultant.

10.4 Disclaimer. EXCEPT FOR THE EXPRESS WARRANTIES SET FORTH IN THIS ARTICLE 10, THE SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES ARE PROVIDED ON AN "AS IS" BASIS. THE CONSULTANT DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING THE IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

ARTICLE 11 — INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 Consultant Indemnity. The Consultant shall defend the Client against any third-party claim alleging that any Deliverable, when used by the Client in accordance with this Agreement, infringes any Intellectual Property Right of such third party, and shall indemnify the Client for any damages, settlement amounts, and reasonable attorneys' fees finally awarded against the Client.

11.2 Client Indemnity. The Client shall defend the Consultant against any third-party claim alleging that Client Materials infringe any Intellectual Property Right of such third party.

11.3 Indemnification Procedure. The Party seeking indemnification shall (a) provide prompt written notice; (b) grant the indemnifying Party sole control over the defense and settlement (subject to a no-bad-settlement-without-consent right); and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

11.4 Mitigation. If any Deliverable becomes the subject of an infringement claim, the Consultant may, at its option, (a) procure the right to continue using the Deliverable, (b) modify it to be non-infringing without materially diminishing its value, (c) replace it with a non-infringing equivalent, or (d) accept return of the Deliverable and refund the Fees paid.

ARTICLE 12 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 Aggregate Cap. EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, IN NO EVENT SHALL THE AGGREGATE LIABILITY OF EITHER PARTY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT OR ANY STATEMENT OF WORK, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT (INCLUDING NEGLIGENCE), STRICT LIABILITY, OR ANY OTHER LEGAL OR EQUITABLE THEORY, EXCEED THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY THE CLIENT TO THE CONSULTANT UNDER THE AFFECTED STATEMENT OF WORK DURING THE {{LIABILITY_CAP_MONTHS}} MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY.

12.2 Exclusion of Indirect Damages. EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER PARTY FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, EXEMPLARY, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT.

12.3 Exceptions to Limitations. The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 shall not apply to (a) the Consultant's obligations under Article 11 (Indemnification); (b) any breach of Article 9 (Confidentiality); (c) any breach of Section 7.4 (Data Security); (d) the Client's payment obligations under Article 4; (e) any liability arising from a Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud; or (f) any liability that, under applicable law, cannot be limited.

12.4 Basis of the Bargain. The limitations of liability are an essential element of the bargain between the Parties.

ARTICLE 13 — TERMINATION

13.1 Termination for Material Breach. Either Party may terminate this Agreement, or any Statement of Work, upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure such breach within the notice period.

13.2 Termination for Convenience. The Client may terminate this Agreement, or any Statement of Work, for convenience at any time upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Upon any such termination, the Client shall pay the Consultant for (a) all Services performed and Deliverables accepted through the effective date of termination, and (b) any non-cancellable third-party commitments incurred prior to receipt of the termination notice.

13.3 Termination for Insolvency. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, files a voluntary petition in bankruptcy, or has an involuntary petition filed against it that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.4 Effects of Termination. Upon expiration or termination: (a) the Consultant shall promptly cease performance of Services under the affected SOW; (b) the Consultant shall deliver to the Client all Work Product completed or in progress; (c) the Consultant shall return Client Materials in accordance with Section 7.3; (d) the Client shall pay the Consultant for accepted Services and Deliverables; (e) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information; and (f) the provisions of this Agreement that by their nature are intended to survive termination shall so survive.

13.5 Wind-Down Assistance. Following termination, the Consultant shall provide reasonable wind-down assistance for a period of up to ninety (90) days at the Consultant's then-current professional services rates, including the orderly transfer of in-progress Work Product and reasonable knowledge transfer to the Client or its successor consultant.

15.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with all Statements of Work, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

15.2 Order of Precedence. As described in the preamble, in the event of any conflict between this Agreement and a SOW, this Agreement shall control as to the matters expressly addressed in this Agreement, except where the SOW expressly modifies as it applies to the relevant engagement.

15.3 Amendments. No amendment shall be effective unless in writing executed by both Parties.

15.4 Severability. If any provision is held to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

15.5 Waiver. No failure or delay in exercising any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

15.6 Notices. Formal notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth in the applicable Statement of Work.

15.7 Independent Contractors. The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as creating any partnership, joint venture, agency, employment, or other similar relationship.

15.8 Subcontractors. The Consultant shall not subcontract any of the Services without the Client's prior written consent.

15.9 Assignment. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets.

15.10 Force Majeure. Neither Party shall be liable for delays caused by events outside its reasonable control (other than payment obligations).

15.11 No Third-Party Beneficiaries. This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties.

15.12 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.
