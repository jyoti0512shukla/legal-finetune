WHEREAS, the Licensor owns certain patents and patent applications covering a specific antibody platform and methods of use thereof, more particularly identified in Schedule A (the "Licensor Patents");

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to address upfront the financial mechanics of "royalty stacking" — the situation in which the Licensee must pay royalties to multiple upstream patent holders for the same Licensed Product — in a manner that is fair to both Parties and that allows the Licensee to commercialize the Licensed Products without being economically suffocated by stacked royalty obligations;

ARTICLE 4 — FINANCIAL TERMS

4.1 Upfront Payment. Within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date, the Licensee shall pay the Licensor a non-refundable upfront payment of {{LICENSE_UPFRONT_FEE}}.

4.2 Milestone Payments. The Licensee shall pay the Licensor the milestone payments set forth in Schedule D upon the first achievement of each clinical, regulatory, and commercial milestone, on a Licensed Product-by-Licensed Product basis.

4.3 Stated Running Royalty. Subject to the royalty stacking adjustments in Sections 4.4 and 4.5, the Licensee shall pay the Licensor a Stated Royalty Rate equal to {{LICENSE_ROYALTY_RATE}} on Net Sales of Licensed Products in the Territory, on a Licensed Product-by-Licensed Product and country-by-country basis.

4.4 Stacking Deduction Mechanic. If the Licensee pays Third Party Royalties for a Licensed Product in a country, the Licensee may credit fifty percent (50%) of such Third Party Royalties against the running royalties otherwise payable to the Licensor for the same Licensed Product in the same country in the same Calendar Year. The Stacking Deduction shall not exceed the amount necessary to reduce the Licensor's running royalties from the Stated Royalty Rate to the Floor Royalty Rate.

4.5 Floor Royalty Rate. The Floor Royalty Rate is fifty percent (50%) of the Stated Royalty Rate. After application of all Stacking Deductions, the running royalty payable to the Licensor for any Licensed Product in any country in any Calendar Year shall not be reduced below the Floor Royalty Rate.

4.6 Worked Example. For illustration, if the Stated Royalty Rate is 8% of Net Sales and the Licensee pays Third Party Royalties of 6% of Net Sales for the same product, the Stacking Deduction is 50% × 6% = 3%, which would reduce the Licensor's running royalty from 8% to 5%. However, the Floor Royalty Rate (50% × 8% = 4%) means the Licensor's running royalty cannot fall below 4%, so the actual Stacking Deduction is capped at 4 percentage points. This worked example is for illustration only and is not intended to alter the calculations in Sections 4.3, 4.4, and 4.5.

4.7 Minimum Annual Royalty. Beginning in the second Calendar Year after First Commercial Sale, the Licensee shall pay the Licensor a minimum annual royalty of {{LICENSE_MIN_ANNUAL_ROYALTY}} for each Calendar Year, creditable against running royalties (after stacking adjustments) otherwise payable for the same Calendar Year. If running royalties exceed the minimum annual royalty, no additional minimum-royalty payment is due.

4.8 Tiered Royalty Adjustment. The Stated Royalty Rate set forth in Section 4.3 shall be adjusted (after stacking deductions) based on cumulative annual Net Sales of all Licensed Products as set forth in Schedule E (e.g. higher rate for the first $500M of annual Net Sales, lower rate for amounts above).

4.9 Royalty Term. The royalty obligations under this Article 4 shall continue, on a Licensed Product-by-Licensed Product and country-by-country basis, until the later of (a) expiration of the last Valid Claim of the Licensed Patents covering such Licensed Product in such country, (b) expiration of all regulatory exclusivity for such Licensed Product in such country, or (c) ten (10) years after First Commercial Sale of such Licensed Product in such country.

4.10 Disclosure of Third Party Royalties. The Licensee shall disclose to the Licensor, with each royalty report, the existence and rate of any Third Party Royalty for which the Licensee is taking a Stacking Deduction. The Licensee shall provide the Licensor with redacted copies of the relevant Third Party license agreements on request, with sensitive commercial terms reasonably redacted.

4.11 Royalty Reports and Payment. Within sixty (60) days after the end of each Calendar Quarter, the Licensee shall provide the Licensor with a royalty report and shall pay the running royalties due (calculated after Stacking Deductions and subject to the Floor Royalty Rate).

4.12 Currency. All payments in United States Dollars by wire transfer.

ARTICLE 5 — RECORDS AND ROYALTY AUDIT

5.1 Records. The Licensee shall maintain complete and accurate books and records of Net Sales, royalty calculations, Stacking Deductions, and Third Party Royalty payments for at least seven (7) years.

5.2 Audit Right. The Licensor may, on at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than {{LICENSE_AUDIT_FREQUENCY}}, engage an independent certified public accountant reasonably acceptable to the Licensee to audit the Licensee's books and records solely for purposes of verifying the accuracy of the royalty reports and royalty payments, including verification of any Stacking Deductions claimed.

5.3 Audit Costs. The Licensor shall bear the cost of the audit, except that if the audit reveals an underpayment of more than five percent (5%) for any Calendar Year, the Licensee shall reimburse the Licensor for the cost of the audit.

5.4 Audit Findings. The Licensee shall pay any underreported royalties revealed by the audit, plus interest at one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, within thirty (30) days of the audit report.
