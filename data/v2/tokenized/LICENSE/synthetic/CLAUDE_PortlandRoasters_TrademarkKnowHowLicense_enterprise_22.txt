TRADEMARK AND KNOW-HOW LICENSE AGREEMENT

This TRADEMARK AND KNOW-HOW LICENSE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Licensor"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} (the "Licensee"). The Parties wish to enter into a combined trademark and know-how license under which the Licensee receives the right to use the Licensor's trademarks AND the related proprietary process and know-how for manufacturing the Licensed Products.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Licensor owns certain trademarks and trade dress identified on Schedule A (the "Licensed Marks") and certain proprietary process know-how, recipes, formulations, and technical information necessary to manufacture the Licensed Products in a manner consistent with the Licensor's standards (the "Licensed Know-How");

WHEREAS, the Licensee wishes to manufacture and sell the Licensed Products in the Field and Territory under the Licensed Marks and using the Licensed Know-How;

WHEREAS, the Parties acknowledge that the combination of trademark and know-how licensing requires careful coordination of (a) trademark quality control (to maintain the validity of the Licensed Marks), (b) know-how confidentiality (to protect the Licensor's trade secrets), and (c) production standards (to ensure consistency between the Licensee's products and the Licensor's reputation);

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Approved Specifications" means the specifications, recipes, formulations, manufacturing processes, ingredient lists, sourcing requirements, and quality standards for the Licensed Products, as set forth on Schedule B and as updated from time to time by the Licensor.

1.2 "Field" means {{LICENSE_FIELD_OF_USE}}.

1.3 "Licensed Know-How" means the technical information, recipes, formulations, manufacturing processes, equipment specifications, sourcing information, quality control procedures, and operating manuals that are owned or controlled by the Licensor and that are necessary or useful for the Licensee to manufacture the Licensed Products in accordance with the Approved Specifications. The Licensed Know-How includes both information existing as of the Effective Date and any improvements developed by the Licensor during the Term.

1.4 "Licensed Marks" means the trademarks, service marks, logos, and trade dress identified on Schedule A, including any registrations and pending applications listed therein.

1.5 "Licensed Products" means the products manufactured by the Licensee in the Field under the Licensed Marks using the Licensed Know-How and in accordance with the Approved Specifications.

1.6 "Net Sales" is defined in Schedule C.

1.7 "Quality Standards" means the quality standards, batch testing requirements, packaging requirements, and labeling requirements set forth in Schedule D.

1.8 "Territory" means {{LICENSE_TERRITORY}}.

ARTICLE 2 — GRANT OF LICENSE

2.1 Trademark License Grant. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Licensor hereby grants to the Licensee an {{LICENSE_GRANT_TYPE}}, royalty-bearing license to use the Licensed Marks solely in connection with the manufacture, marketing, distribution, advertising, and sale of the Licensed Products in the Field and Territory, and for no other purpose.

2.2 Know-How License Grant. The Licensor hereby grants to the Licensee an {{LICENSE_GRANT_TYPE}}, royalty-bearing license to use the Licensed Know-How solely for the purpose of manufacturing the Licensed Products in accordance with the Approved Specifications. The Licensee shall not use the Licensed Know-How for any other purpose.

2.3 Coordination. The trademark license and the know-how license are coordinated: the Licensee may use the Licensed Marks only on Licensed Products manufactured in accordance with the Licensed Know-How and the Approved Specifications. The Licensee may not use the Licensed Marks on products that do not comply with the Approved Specifications, and may not use the Licensed Know-How to manufacture products that do not bear the Licensed Marks (other than for the Licensee's internal R&D or test batches).

2.4 Reservation of Rights. The Licensor reserves all rights not expressly granted, including the right to use the Licensed Marks and the Licensed Know-How outside the Field or the Territory, the right to license the Licensed Marks and the Licensed Know-How to others outside the Field or the Territory, and the right to sell its own branded products of the Licensor anywhere in the world.

2.5 Sublicensing. {{LICENSE_SUBLICENSE_RIGHTS}}.

2.6 Term and Field. {{LICENSE_TERM}} {{LICENSE_FIELD_OF_USE}}.

2.7 Goodwill. All goodwill arising from the Licensee's use of the Licensed Marks shall inure to the benefit of the Licensor. The Licensee acknowledges that the Licensor is the sole owner of the Licensed Marks and the goodwill associated therewith.

ARTICLE 3 — RESTRICTIONS

3.1 Mark Use Restrictions. The Licensee shall use the Licensed Marks only in the form, color, and manner approved by the Licensor in the Brand Standards Manual. The Licensee shall not modify, alter, or create derivative versions of the Licensed Marks. The Licensee shall not register or attempt to register any trademark, service mark, or domain name confusingly similar to the Licensed Marks.

3.2 Know-How Use Restrictions. The Licensee shall use the Licensed Know-How only for the purpose of manufacturing the Licensed Products. The Licensee shall not (a) disclose the Licensed Know-How to any third party except as expressly permitted, (b) reverse engineer or attempt to derive the underlying chemistry, biology, or engineering of the Licensed Know-How, (c) use the Licensed Know-How to develop competing products under the Licensee's own brand, (d) modify the Licensed Know-How or the Approved Specifications without the Licensor's prior written consent, or (e) export the Licensed Know-How outside the Territory.

3.3 No Generic Equivalent. The Licensee shall not manufacture generic, store-brand, or private-label equivalents of the Licensed Products using the Licensed Know-How. The Licensee may manufacture other products in the Licensee's facilities under the Licensee's own brand, but those products must be developed independently of the Licensed Know-How.

3.4 No Reverse Engineering of Equipment. The Licensee shall not reverse engineer any specialty equipment provided by the Licensor or developed by the Licensor for the manufacture of the Licensed Products.

3.5 No Use of Licensed Know-How After Termination. After termination of this Agreement, the Licensee shall not use the Licensed Know-How for any purpose, including any continued manufacture of similar products under the Licensee's own brand. (This restriction is essential to the trade-secret protection of the Licensed Know-How.)

3.6 Approved Personnel Only. Access to the Licensed Know-How shall be limited to the Licensee's employees who have a need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations consistent with Article 7.

ARTICLE 4 — ROYALTIES, FEES, AND PAYMENT

4.1 Upfront Fee. The Licensee shall pay the Licensor a non-refundable upfront fee of {{LICENSE_UPFRONT_FEE}} within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date, in consideration of the grant of the trademark and know-how licenses, the transfer of the Licensed Know-How package, and the Licensor's training and onboarding services.

4.2 Running Royalty. The Licensee shall pay the Licensor a running royalty equal to {{LICENSE_ROYALTY_RATE}} on Net Sales of Licensed Products. The royalty is structured as a single combined royalty covering both the trademark license and the know-how license.

4.3 Minimum Annual Royalty. The Licensee shall pay the Licensor a minimum annual royalty of {{LICENSE_MIN_ANNUAL_ROYALTY}} for each Calendar Year, creditable against running royalties for the same Calendar Year.

4.4 Marketing Spend. The Licensee shall spend at least three percent (3%) of Net Sales of Licensed Products on local marketing in the Territory.

4.5 Royalty Reports. The Licensee shall provide quarterly royalty reports within thirty (30) days after the end of each quarter, with the running royalties paid concurrently.

4.6 Currency. All payments shall be in United States Dollars by wire transfer.

4.7 Late Payment. Late payments accrue interest at one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum permitted by law.

ARTICLE 5 — RECORDS AND ROYALTY AUDIT

5.1 Records. The Licensee shall maintain complete and accurate books and records of the manufacture, distribution, and sale of Licensed Products, the calculation of Net Sales, batch records, quality control records, and Licensed Know-How access logs for at least four (4) years.

5.2 Audit Right. The Licensor may, on at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than {{LICENSE_AUDIT_FREQUENCY}}, audit the Licensee's books and records (through an independent certified public accountant) and inspect the Licensee's manufacturing facilities (through Licensor representatives) for purposes of (a) verifying the accuracy of the royalty reports, (b) verifying compliance with the Approved Specifications and Quality Standards, and (c) verifying compliance with the Licensed Know-How confidentiality obligations.

5.3 Audit Costs. The Licensor shall bear the cost of the audit, except that if the audit reveals (a) an underpayment of royalties of more than five percent (5%) for any year, (b) material non-compliance with the Approved Specifications, or (c) any breach of the Licensed Know-How confidentiality, the Licensee shall reimburse the Licensor for the cost of the audit and any additional remediation expenses.

5.4 Audit Findings. The Licensee shall pay any underreported royalties plus interest within thirty (30) days of the audit report.

ARTICLE 6 — QUALITY CONTROL

6.1 The Licensee shall manufacture, market, distribute, and sell the Licensed Products in strict accordance with the Approved Specifications and the Quality Standards.

6.2 The Licensor and its representatives shall have the right to inspect the Licensee's manufacturing facilities at reasonable times and on reasonable notice to verify compliance with the Approved Specifications and Quality Standards.

6.3 The Licensee shall submit production samples to the Licensor for approval before commencing commercial production of any new Licensed Product line and after any change in formulation, ingredients, or process.

6.4 If the Licensor identifies any non-compliance, the Licensee shall correct the non-compliance within the time period specified by the Licensor and shall, at the Licensor's option, recall, destroy, or rework any non-conforming products at the Licensee's expense.

6.5 The quality control provisions in this Article 6 are essential to maintain the validity of the Licensed Marks and to protect the Licensor's reputation. The Parties acknowledge that the Licensor's exercise of quality control is necessary to avoid any "naked licensing" claim under trademark law.

ARTICLE 7 — CONFIDENTIALITY AND TRADE SECRET PROTECTION

7.1 Confidentiality of Licensed Know-How. The Licensee shall hold the Licensed Know-How in strict confidence. The Licensee shall not disclose the Licensed Know-How to any third party (other than the Licensee's employees with a need to know who are bound by written confidentiality obligations) and shall use it solely for the purpose of manufacturing Licensed Products under this Agreement.

7.2 Trade Secret Protection. The Parties acknowledge that the Licensed Know-How constitutes trade secrets of the Licensor. The Licensee shall implement and maintain reasonable security measures to protect the Licensed Know-How from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure, including (a) physical security at the Licensee's manufacturing facilities, (b) access controls on documentation containing the Licensed Know-How, (c) employee confidentiality agreements, (d) restricted access to manufacturing areas where the Licensed Know-How is practiced, and (e) audit logs of access to Licensed Know-How documentation.

7.3 Non-Use After Termination. After termination of this Agreement, the Licensee shall (a) return or destroy all documentation containing the Licensed Know-How, (b) cease all use of the Licensed Know-How, and (c) require its employees who had access to the Licensed Know-How to refrain from using it in any subsequent employment, to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law (the Parties acknowledge that some jurisdictions limit non-use covenants on individual employees, and this Section 7.3 shall be enforced only to the extent permitted in the relevant jurisdiction).

7.4 Survival. The confidentiality and trade secret obligations under this Article 7 shall survive termination indefinitely, for as long as the Licensed Know-How remains a trade secret.

ARTICLE 8 — REPRESENTATIONS, WARRANTIES, AND INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Licensor Representations. The Licensor represents and warrants that (a) the Licensor owns the Licensed Marks and the Licensed Know-How, (b) the Licensor has the right to grant the licenses granted herein, and (c) to the Licensor's knowledge, the use of the Licensed Marks within the Field and the Territory does not infringe any third party's trademark rights.

8.2 Trademark Indemnification by Licensor. The Licensor shall defend the Licensee against any third-party claim that the Licensee's use of the Licensed Marks within the scope of this Agreement infringes the third party's trademark rights.

8.3 Licensee Indemnification. The Licensee shall defend the Licensor against any claim arising out of (a) the Licensee's manufacture, marketing, or sale of the Licensed Products (including product liability claims), (b) any consumer protection or labeling violation, (c) the Licensee's breach of the Quality Standards, or (d) the Licensee's misuse of the Licensed Know-How.

8.4 Insurance. The Licensee shall maintain product liability insurance with minimum coverage of Five Million United States Dollars (US$5,000,000) per occurrence, with the Licensor named as additional insured.

8.5 Limitation of Liability. EXCEPT FOR (A) BREACHES OF CONFIDENTIALITY UNDER ARTICLE 7, (B) THE INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, AND (C) WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

ARTICLE 9 — TERM AND TERMINATION OF LICENSE

9.1 Term. The Term commences on the Effective Date and continues as set forth in Section 2.6.

9.2 Termination for Material Breach. Either Party may terminate upon thirty (30) days' written notice for the other Party's material breach.

9.3 Termination for Quality Failures. The Licensor may terminate this Agreement if the Licensee fails the Licensor's quality inspections on more than two (2) occasions within any twelve-month period after notice and opportunity to cure.

9.4 Termination for Trade Secret Breach. The Licensor may terminate immediately, without cure period, if the Licensee breaches the Licensed Know-How confidentiality obligations under Article 7.

9.5 Effect of Termination. Upon termination:

(a) The Licensee shall cease all use of the Licensed Marks and the Licensed Know-How.

(b) The Licensee shall return or destroy all documentation containing the Licensed Know-How.

(c) The Licensee shall remove the Licensed Marks from any signage, packaging, advertising, and marketing materials.

(d) The Licensee shall have a sell-off period of one hundred eighty (180) days for finished inventory, subject to continued royalty payments.

(e) The Licensee shall not manufacture any product (under the Licensed Marks or otherwise) that uses the Licensed Know-How.

(f) The Licensee shall pay all amounts due, including royalties through the end of the sell-off period.

9.6 Survival. Articles 2.7, 5 (post-termination audit), 7 (perpetual), 8 (with respect to claims arising before termination), 9.5, and 10 shall survive termination.
