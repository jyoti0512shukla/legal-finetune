ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means, with respect to a Party, any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such Party, where "control" means the ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities or other equity interests of such entity.

1.2 "Combination Product" means a Licensed Product that incorporates one or more additional active pharmaceutical ingredients in addition to the Compound.

1.3 "Commercially Reasonable Efforts" means the level of effort and resources that a clinical-stage pharmaceutical company of similar size and resources would devote to a product of similar commercial potential and at a similar stage of development, taking into account the patent coverage, regulatory environment, competitive landscape, and the safety and efficacy profile of the Licensed Product.

1.4 "Compound" means the small-molecule compound covered by the Licensed Patents and identified in Schedule A.

1.5 "Field" means the {{LICENSE_FIELD_OF_USE}}.

1.6 "First Commercial Sale" means, on a country-by-country and Licensed Product-by-Licensed Product basis, the first commercial sale of a Licensed Product to a Third Party in such country following Regulatory Approval in such country.

1.7 "Licensed Product" means any pharmaceutical product, the manufacture, use, sale, offer for sale, or importation of which would, but for the license granted in Article 2, infringe a Valid Claim of the Licensed Patents in the country in which such activity occurs.

1.8 "Net Sales" means the gross amounts invoiced by the Licensee, its Affiliates, and its Sublicensees for sales of Licensed Products to Third Parties, less the following deductions to the extent actually incurred and customary in the pharmaceutical industry: (a) trade, quantity, and cash discounts, (b) credits and allowances for returns, recalls, and rejections, (c) freight, postage, shipping, and insurance charges, (d) sales, use, value-added, and excise taxes (excluding income taxes) directly imposed on the sale, (e) charge-backs and rebates to wholesalers, distributors, group purchasing organizations, and government payers (including Medicaid and Medicare rebates), and (f) discounts mandated by applicable law. Sales between the Licensee and its Affiliates or Sublicensees for resale shall not be included in Net Sales; only the resale to a Third Party shall be included. For Combination Products, Net Sales shall be calculated by multiplying actual Net Sales of the Combination Product by the fraction A/(A+B), where A is the average per-unit invoice price of the Licensed Product when sold separately and B is the average per-unit invoice price of the other active ingredients when sold separately, in each case in the same country.

1.9 "Regulatory Approval" means, with respect to a Licensed Product in a country, the approval of the applicable regulatory authority required to market and sell the Licensed Product in such country, including FDA approval of a New Drug Application (NDA) or Biologics License Application (BLA) in the United States and the equivalent approval in other countries.

1.10 "Sublicensee" means any Third Party to which the Licensee grants a sublicense under the Licensed Patents and Licensed Know-How as permitted by Section 2.3.

1.11 "Territory" means {{LICENSE_TERRITORY}}.

1.12 "Third Party" means any person or entity other than the Licensor, the Licensee, and their respective Affiliates.

1.13 "Valid Claim" means a claim of an issued and unexpired Licensed Patent that has not been (a) held invalid or unenforceable by a final, unappealable decision of a court or other governmental body of competent jurisdiction, or (b) admitted to be invalid through reissue, disclaimer, or otherwise.

ARTICLE 4 — FINANCIAL TERMS

4.1 Upfront Payment. In consideration of the rights granted hereunder, the Licensee shall pay the Licensor a one-time, non-refundable, non-creditable upfront payment of {{LICENSE_UPFRONT_FEE}} within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date.

4.2 Milestone Payments. The Licensee shall pay the Licensor the following milestone payments within thirty (30) days after the first achievement of each milestone, on a Licensed Product-by-Licensed Product basis:

(a) Filing of the first Investigational New Drug application (IND) for a Licensed Product: Five Million United States Dollars (US$5,000,000).

(b) First dosing of a patient in a Phase II clinical trial of a Licensed Product: Ten Million United States Dollars (US$10,000,000).

(c) First dosing of a patient in a Phase III clinical trial of a Licensed Product: Twenty Million United States Dollars (US$20,000,000).

(d) Filing of the first New Drug Application (NDA) or Biologics License Application (BLA) for a Licensed Product with the FDA: Thirty Million United States Dollars (US$30,000,000).

(e) First Regulatory Approval of a Licensed Product by the FDA: Fifty Million United States Dollars (US$50,000,000).

(f) First Regulatory Approval of a Licensed Product by the European Medicines Agency: Twenty-Five Million United States Dollars (US$25,000,000).

(g) Cumulative annual Net Sales of any Licensed Product first exceeding US$500,000,000 in a calendar year: Fifty Million United States Dollars (US$50,000,000).

(h) Cumulative annual Net Sales of any Licensed Product first exceeding US$1,000,000,000 in a calendar year: One Hundred Million United States Dollars (US$100,000,000).

Each milestone payment shall be paid only once per Licensed Product, regardless of how many times the milestone is subsequently achieved.

4.3 Running Royalty. The Licensee shall pay the Licensor a running royalty equal to {{LICENSE_ROYALTY_RATE}} on Net Sales of Licensed Products in the Territory, on a Licensed Product-by-Licensed Product and country-by-country basis.

4.4 Royalty Term. The royalty obligations under Section 4.3 shall continue, on a Licensed Product-by-Licensed Product and country-by-country basis, until the later of (a) the expiration of the last Valid Claim of the Licensed Patents covering such Licensed Product in such country, (b) the expiration of all regulatory exclusivity (including data exclusivity, orphan drug exclusivity, and pediatric exclusivity) for such Licensed Product in such country, and (c) ten (10) years after the First Commercial Sale of such Licensed Product in such country (the "Royalty Term").

4.5 Minimum Annual Royalty. Beginning in the first calendar year after First Commercial Sale of any Licensed Product in any country, the Licensee shall pay the Licensor a minimum annual royalty of {{LICENSE_MIN_ANNUAL_ROYALTY}} for each calendar year during the Royalty Term, creditable against running royalties otherwise payable for such calendar year. If the running royalties for a given calendar year exceed the minimum annual royalty, no additional minimum-royalty payment is due.

4.6 Royalty Stacking. If the Licensee is required to pay royalties to a Third Party for a license under intellectual property rights necessary to make, use, or sell a Licensed Product in a country, the Licensee may credit fifty percent (50%) of such Third Party royalties against the running royalties payable to the Licensor under Section 4.3 for such Licensed Product in such country, provided that in no event shall the running royalties payable to the Licensor be reduced below fifty percent (50%) of the rate set forth in Section 4.3.

4.7 Royalty Reports and Payment. Within forty-five (45) days after the end of each calendar quarter, the Licensee shall provide the Licensor with a royalty report setting forth, on a Licensed Product-by-Licensed Product and country-by-country basis: (a) the gross sales and Net Sales of each Licensed Product, (b) the calculation of running royalties due, (c) any deductions, credits, or offsets, and (d) any milestones achieved. The Licensee shall pay the running royalties due with the royalty report.

4.8 Currency. All payments under this Agreement shall be made in United States Dollars by wire transfer to an account designated by the Licensor in writing.

4.9 Late Payment Interest. Any payment not made when due shall bear interest from the date due until paid at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law.

4.10 Withholding Taxes. Any taxes required to be withheld from payments under this Agreement under applicable law shall be deducted from such payments and remitted to the appropriate taxing authority. The Licensee shall promptly furnish the Licensor with documentation evidencing such payment so that the Licensor may claim any available foreign tax credit.

ARTICLE 5 — RECORDS AND ROYALTY AUDIT

5.1 Records. The Licensee shall keep complete and accurate books and records of the development, manufacture, and sale of Licensed Products and the calculation of Net Sales and royalties in sufficient detail to enable the Licensor to verify the royalty payments due under Article 4. Such records shall be maintained for at least five (5) years after the calendar year to which they pertain.

5.2 Audit Right. The Licensor shall have the right, on at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than {{LICENSE_AUDIT_FREQUENCY}}, to engage an independent certified public accountant reasonably acceptable to the Licensee to audit the Licensee's books and records solely for the purpose of verifying the accuracy of the royalty reports and royalty payments. The auditor shall execute a confidentiality agreement reasonably acceptable to the Licensee and shall report to the Licensor only the amount of any underpayment or overpayment.

5.3 Audit Costs. The Licensor shall bear the cost of the audit, except that if the audit reveals an underpayment of more than five percent (5%) for any calendar year, the Licensee shall reimburse the Licensor for the reasonable cost of the audit.

5.4 Audit Findings. The Licensee shall pay any underreported royalties revealed by the audit, plus interest at the rate set forth in Section 4.9, within thirty (30) days after the auditor's report is delivered to both Parties. If the audit reveals an overpayment, the Licensor shall credit such overpayment against future royalty payments or, if the Royalty Term has expired, refund such overpayment within thirty (30) days.

6.1 Licensor Improvements. Any Improvements to the Compound or the Licensed Patents made by the Licensor or its Affiliates during the Term, whether independently or jointly with the Licensee, shall be owned by the Licensor and shall automatically be deemed included in the Licensed Patents and Licensed Know-How licensed to the Licensee under Article 2 on the same terms.

6.2 Licensee Improvements. Any Improvements to the Compound or the Licensed Patents made by the Licensee or its Affiliates during the Term shall be owned by the Licensee. The Licensee hereby grants the Licensor a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual, worldwide, sublicensable license under such Licensee Improvements to research, develop, manufacture, use, and commercialize the Compound outside the Field.

6.3 Patent Prosecution. The Licensor shall control, at its own expense, the preparation, filing, prosecution, and maintenance of the Licensed Patents. The Licensor shall keep the Licensee reasonably informed of the status of the Licensed Patents and shall consider in good faith any comments from the Licensee on prosecution strategy. If the Licensor elects to abandon any Licensed Patent in any country, the Licensor shall provide the Licensee with at least sixty (60) days' written notice and the opportunity to assume prosecution of such patent at the Licensee's expense, in which case the Licensor shall assign such patent to the Licensee and such patent shall cease to be a Licensed Patent under this Agreement.

6.4 Enforcement of Licensed Patents. The Licensor shall have the first right (but not the obligation), at its own expense, to enforce the Licensed Patents against any Third Party infringement in the Field in the Territory. If the Licensor declines to enforce within ninety (90) days of becoming aware of the infringement, the Licensee shall have the right (but not the obligation) to enforce at the Licensee's expense. Any recovery shall be applied first to reimburse the enforcing Party's costs, then to the non-enforcing Party's costs, and the remainder shall be split eighty percent (80%) to the enforcing Party and twenty percent (20%) to the non-enforcing Party. The non-enforcing Party shall reasonably cooperate with the enforcing Party in any such enforcement action.

ARTICLE 9 — TERM AND TERMINATION OF LICENSE

9.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue, on a Licensed Product-by-Licensed Product and country-by-country basis, until the expiration of the Royalty Term for such Licensed Product in such country (the "Term").

9.2 Termination for Material Breach. Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice to the other Party for the other Party's material breach of this Agreement, provided that such termination shall not be effective if the breaching Party cures such breach within the sixty-day notice period. With respect to a payment breach by the Licensee, the cure period shall be thirty (30) days.

9.3 Termination for Insolvency. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice to the other Party if the other Party (a) becomes insolvent, (b) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, (c) files a petition in bankruptcy or has such a petition filed against it that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days, or (d) has a receiver appointed for all or substantially all of its assets. The Parties acknowledge that this Agreement is a license of intellectual property within the meaning of Section 365(n) of the United States Bankruptcy Code, and the Licensee shall retain its rights under this Agreement in the event of the Licensor's bankruptcy in accordance with such section.

9.4 Termination for Failure to Achieve Diligence. The Licensor may terminate this Agreement upon ninety (90) days' written notice if the Licensee fails to use Commercially Reasonable Efforts to develop and commercialize Licensed Products as required by Article 3 and fails to cure such failure within the notice period.

9.5 Effect of Termination. Upon termination of this Agreement (other than expiration of the Term):

(a) The license granted in Article 2 shall terminate, and the Licensee shall cease all use of the Licensed Patents and Licensed Know-How.

(b) The Licensee shall return or, at the Licensor's option, destroy all Confidential Information of the Licensor in its possession.

(c) The Licensee shall pay all amounts due under Article 4 through the effective date of termination, including any milestone payments earned but not yet paid.

(d) The Licensee shall have a sell-off period of six (6) months following the effective date of termination during which the Licensee may continue to sell Licensed Products manufactured prior to termination, subject to continued royalty payments under Article 4.

(e) Sections 4.9, 4.10, 5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 5.4, 7, 8.3, 8.4, 8.5, 8.6, 8.7, 9.5, and 10 shall survive termination.
