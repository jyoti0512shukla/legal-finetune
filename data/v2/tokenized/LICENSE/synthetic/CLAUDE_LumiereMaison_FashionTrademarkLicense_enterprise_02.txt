ARTICLE 4 — ROYALTIES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Upfront Payment. In consideration of the rights granted hereunder, the Licensee shall pay the Licensor a one-time, non-refundable upfront payment of {{LICENSE_UPFRONT_FEE}} within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date.

4.2 Running Royalty. The Licensee shall pay the Licensor a running royalty equal to {{LICENSE_ROYALTY_RATE}} on Net Sales of Licensed Products.

4.3 Minimum Annual Royalty. For each calendar year during the Term, the Licensee shall pay the Licensor a minimum annual royalty of {{LICENSE_MIN_ANNUAL_ROYALTY}}, creditable against running royalties otherwise payable under Section 4.2 for the same calendar year. If the running royalties for a given calendar year exceed the minimum annual royalty, no additional minimum-royalty payment is due. The minimum annual royalty for the first calendar year of the Term shall be prorated based on the number of full calendar months remaining in such calendar year following the Effective Date.

4.4 Marketing Commitment. The Licensee shall spend, in each calendar year during the Term, an amount equal to at least three percent (3%) of Net Sales of Licensed Products in such calendar year on advertising and marketing of Licensed Products. The Licensee shall provide the Licensor with a written report of marketing expenditures with each annual royalty report.

4.5 Royalty Reports. Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, the Licensee shall provide the Licensor with a royalty report setting forth: (a) the Licensed Products sold by category, (b) gross sales and Net Sales by category and country, (c) the calculation of running royalties due, and (d) any deductions taken. The Licensee shall pay the running royalties due with the royalty report.

4.6 Currency. All payments shall be made in United States Dollars by wire transfer to an account designated by the Licensor in writing.

4.7 Late Payment Interest. Any payment not made when due shall bear interest from the due date until paid at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law.

ARTICLE 5 — RECORDS AND ROYALTY AUDIT

5.1 Records. The Licensee shall maintain complete and accurate books and records of the manufacture, distribution, marketing, and sale of Licensed Products and the calculation of Net Sales and royalties for at least three (3) years after the calendar year to which they pertain.

5.2 Audit Right. The Licensor shall have the right, on at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than {{LICENSE_AUDIT_FREQUENCY}}, to engage an independent certified public accountant reasonably acceptable to the Licensee to audit the Licensee's books and records solely for purposes of verifying the accuracy of the royalty reports and royalty payments.

5.3 Audit Costs. The Licensor shall bear the cost of the audit, except that if the audit reveals an underpayment of more than five percent (5%) of the royalties due for any calendar year, the Licensee shall reimburse the Licensor for the reasonable cost of the audit.

5.4 Audit Findings. The Licensee shall pay any underreported royalties revealed by the audit, plus interest at the rate set forth in Section 4.7, within thirty (30) days after the auditor's report.

6.2 Use Restrictions. Each Party shall use the other Party's Confidential Information solely for purposes of exercising its rights and performing its obligations under this Agreement.

6.3 Survival. The confidentiality obligations under this Article 6 shall survive termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years, except that with respect to trade secrets, the obligations shall continue for so long as the information remains a trade secret.

ARTICLE 7 — QUALITY CONTROL AND BRAND STANDARDS

7.1 Quality Standards. The Licensee shall manufacture, distribute, market, and sell Licensed Products in accordance with the Quality Standards set forth in Schedule C. The Licensor may update the Quality Standards from time to time on reasonable notice to the Licensee, provided that any material change shall not apply to Licensed Products already in production or in the Licensee's inventory at the time of the change.

7.2 Sample Approval. Before commencing commercial production of any Licensed Product, the Licensee shall submit at least three (3) production-quality Samples of such Licensed Product to the Licensor for review and approval. The Licensor shall have thirty (30) days from receipt of the Samples to approve or reject (with reasons) the proposed Licensed Product. If the Licensor does not respond within the thirty-day period, the Samples shall be deemed rejected. Approval of a Sample does not waive the Licensor's right to require further Samples or to require modifications to subsequent production runs.

7.3 Pre-Marketing Approval. The Licensee shall submit all proposed advertising, marketing, point-of-sale, packaging, and promotional materials for Licensed Products to the Licensor for approval before use. The Licensor shall have ten (10) business days to approve or reject any such submission.

7.4 Approved Manufacturers. The Licensee may engage third-party manufacturers to manufacture Licensed Products only if such manufacturer has been pre-approved in writing by the Licensor as an Approved Manufacturer. The Licensee shall enter into a written manufacturing agreement with each Approved Manufacturer that includes provisions (a) acknowledging the Licensor's ownership of the Licensed Marks, (b) prohibiting the Approved Manufacturer from using the Licensed Marks for any other purpose, and (c) requiring the Approved Manufacturer to comply with the Quality Standards. The Licensee shall remain primarily liable for the Approved Manufacturer's compliance with this Agreement.

7.5 Inspection Rights. The Licensor and its representatives shall have the right, on reasonable notice and during normal business hours, to inspect the Licensee's facilities and the facilities of any Approved Manufacturer to verify compliance with the Quality Standards.

7.6 Corrective Action. If the Licensor determines that the Licensee or any Approved Manufacturer is not complying with the Quality Standards, the Licensor shall provide written notice to the Licensee specifying the non-compliance, and the Licensee shall have thirty (30) days to cure the non-compliance. If the Licensee fails to cure within thirty (30) days, the Licensor may, in addition to any other remedies, require the Licensee to recall, destroy, or rework any non-conforming Licensed Products at the Licensee's expense.

7.7 Naked License Avoidance. The Parties expressly acknowledge that the quality control provisions in this Article 7 are essential to maintaining the validity and enforceability of the Licensed Marks under United States and foreign trademark law. The Licensee agrees that the Licensor shall exercise the level of control specified in this Article 7 in order to avoid any claim of "naked licensing."

ARTICLE 9 — TERM AND TERMINATION OF LICENSE

9.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial term of {{LICENSE_TERM}}, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Article 9. The Term may be renewed by mutual written agreement of the Parties.

9.2 Termination for Material Breach. Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party for the other Party's material breach, provided that the breaching Party has not cured the breach within the notice period.

9.3 Termination for Failure to Meet Minimum Royalty. The Licensor may terminate this Agreement upon sixty (60) days' written notice if the Licensee fails to pay the minimum annual royalty for any calendar year and fails to cure such failure within the notice period.

9.4 Termination for Repeated Quality Failures. The Licensor may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the Licensee or any Approved Manufacturer fails the Licensor's quality inspection on two (2) or more occasions within any twelve-month period.

9.5 Termination for Insolvency. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or has a receiver appointed.

9.6 Effect of Termination. Upon termination of this Agreement:

(a) The license granted in Article 2 shall terminate, and the Licensee shall immediately cease all use of the Licensed Marks in connection with new production.

(b) The Licensee shall have a sell-off period of one hundred eighty (180) days following the effective date of termination during which the Licensee may continue to sell finished Licensed Products in the Licensee's existing inventory, subject to continued royalty payments under Article 4 and continued compliance with the Quality Standards.

(c) After the sell-off period, the Licensee shall, at the Licensor's option, destroy or deliver to the Licensor all remaining inventory of Licensed Products and all packaging, hangtags, and marketing materials bearing the Licensed Marks.

(d) The Licensee shall pay all amounts due under Article 4 through the end of the sell-off period.

(e) Articles 1, 2.4, 5, 6, 7.7, 8, 9.6, and 10 shall survive termination.
