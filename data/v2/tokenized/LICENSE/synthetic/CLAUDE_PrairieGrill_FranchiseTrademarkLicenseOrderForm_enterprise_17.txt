ARTICLE 1 — GRANT OF LICENSE AND FRANCHISE

1.1 The Franchisor hereby grants the Franchisee a {{LICENSE_GRANT_TYPE}} license to use the Franchisor's trademarks, trade dress, recipes, operations manual, training materials, and proprietary system (collectively, the "Franchised System") to operate one or more franchised business locations in the territory identified in Schedule 1.

1.2 The specific trademarks licensed are identified in Schedule 2 (the "Licensed Marks").

1.3 The Franchisor's broader Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) and System Operating Manual govern the operational details of the Franchisee's use of the Franchised System and are incorporated by reference. The Franchisee acknowledges receipt of the most current FDD at least fourteen (14) calendar days before the Effective Date as required by the FTC Franchise Rule (16 C.F.R. Part 436).

1.4 Field of use: {{LICENSE_FIELD_OF_USE}}.

1.5 Territory: {{LICENSE_TERRITORY}}, with the specific franchised location address(es) identified in Schedule 1.

1.6 Term: {{LICENSE_TERM}}, with renewal options as set forth in Schedule 3.

1.7 Sublicensing. {{LICENSE_SUBLICENSE_RIGHTS}}.

ARTICLE 2 — RESTRICTIONS ON USE

2.1 The Franchisee shall use the Licensed Marks only in connection with the Franchisee's operation of the franchised business at the locations specified in Schedule 1.

2.2 The Franchisee shall use the Licensed Marks only in the form, color, and manner approved by the Franchisor in the Brand Standards Manual (Schedule 4). The Franchisee shall not modify or alter the Licensed Marks.

2.3 The Franchisee shall not (a) sublicense the Licensed Marks, (b) use the Licensed Marks for any business other than the franchised business, (c) register the Licensed Marks (or any marks confusingly similar) anywhere in the world, (d) challenge the validity, ownership, or enforceability of the Licensed Marks, or (e) use the Licensed Marks in a way that creates customer confusion as to the source of the goods or services.

2.4 The Franchisee shall display the Franchisor's trademark notice on all signage, packaging, advertising, and marketing materials in the form set forth in the Brand Standards Manual.

2.5 The Franchisee shall comply with the Franchisor's restrictive covenants set forth in Schedule 5, including in-term and post-term non-competition and non-solicitation provisions.

ARTICLE 3 — ROYALTIES AND FEES

3.1 Initial Franchise Fee. The Franchisee shall pay the Franchisor an initial franchise fee of {{LICENSE_UPFRONT_FEE}} for each franchised location, payable on the Effective Date.

3.2 Continuing Royalty Fee. The Franchisee shall pay the Franchisor a continuing royalty equal to {{LICENSE_ROYALTY_RATE}} of the Franchisee's Gross Sales, payable weekly. "Gross Sales" is defined in Schedule 6.

3.3 Marketing Fund Contribution. The Franchisee shall pay the Franchisor a marketing fund contribution as set forth in Schedule 7, currently equal to two percent (2%) of Gross Sales, paid into the Franchisor's national marketing fund and used for system-wide brand marketing.

3.4 Local Marketing Spend. The Franchisee shall spend at least three percent (3%) of Gross Sales on local marketing of the franchised business, with documentation submitted to the Franchisor on request.

3.5 Minimum Royalty. If the running royalty for any month is less than {{LICENSE_MIN_ANNUAL_ROYALTY}}, the Franchisee shall pay the Franchisor the difference between the running royalty and the minimum.

3.6 Other Fees. Schedule 8 sets forth other fees payable under this Agreement, including transfer fees, training fees, audit fees, and renewal fees.

3.7 Royalty Reports. The Franchisee shall provide the Franchisor with weekly Gross Sales reports (and any other reports required by the Brand Standards Manual) using the Franchisor's point-of-sale system.

3.8 Late Payment. Late payments accrue interest at one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

ARTICLE 4 — RECORDS AND ROYALTY AUDIT

4.1 The Franchisee shall maintain complete and accurate books and records of the franchised business and all Gross Sales for at least four (4) years.

4.2 The Franchisor shall have the right, on at least three (3) days' prior written notice and not more than {{LICENSE_AUDIT_FREQUENCY}}, to audit the Franchisee's books, records, point-of-sale data, and tax returns to verify the accuracy of the Gross Sales reports.

4.3 If the audit reveals an underreporting of Gross Sales of more than two percent (2%) for any month or more than one percent (1%) for any calendar year, the Franchisee shall (a) pay the underreported royalties plus interest, (b) pay a penalty equal to ten percent (10%) of the underreported amount, and (c) pay the cost of the audit.

4.4 The Franchisor's audit right is broader than typical commercial license audits because of the risk of cash skimming in franchised foodservice businesses.

ARTICLE 5 — QUALITY CONTROL AND BRAND STANDARDS

5.1 The Franchisee shall operate the franchised business in strict compliance with the Brand Standards Manual (Schedule 4), the System Operating Manual, and any standards and procedures issued by the Franchisor from time to time.

5.2 The Franchisor and its representatives shall have the right, at any time and without prior notice, to inspect the franchised business location, observe operations, sample food and beverages, and review compliance with the Brand Standards.

5.3 The Franchisee shall use only ingredients, supplies, and equipment from approved suppliers identified in Schedule 9.

5.4 The Franchisor's quality control program is essential to maintaining the validity of the Licensed Marks under trademark law. The Parties acknowledge that the Franchisor's exercise of quality control is necessary to avoid any claim of "naked licensing."

5.5 If the Franchisee fails any quality inspection, the Franchisee shall cure the deficiency within the time period specified in the inspection notice. Repeated quality failures are grounds for termination under Article 9.

ARTICLE 6 — TRAINING AND SUPPORT

6.1 The Franchisee shall complete the Franchisor's initial training program before opening the franchised business.

6.2 The Franchisee shall ensure that designated personnel complete ongoing training programs as required by the Franchisor.

6.3 The Franchisor shall provide the Franchisee with operational support, marketing support, and access to the Franchisor's centralized supply chain on the terms set forth in Schedule 10.

ARTICLE 7 — CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 The Franchisee shall hold all Franchisor Confidential Information (including the System Operating Manual, recipes, supplier agreements, customer data, pricing strategies, and any proprietary system information) in strict confidence during the Term and at all times thereafter.

7.2 The Franchisee shall not use Franchisor Confidential Information for any purpose other than operating the franchised business under this Agreement.

7.3 The Franchisee shall require its employees, officers, and managers to sign confidentiality agreements consistent with this Article 7.

7.4 Survival. The confidentiality obligations under this Article 7 shall survive termination or expiration of this Agreement and shall continue indefinitely with respect to trade secrets.

ARTICLE 8 — REPRESENTATIONS, WARRANTIES, AND INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Franchisor Representations. The Franchisor represents that (a) the Franchisor owns the Licensed Marks and has the right to grant this license, (b) the Franchisor will provide the Franchisee with the FDD as required by applicable law, and (c) the Franchisor will provide the support and training described in Article 6.

8.2 Franchisee Representations. The Franchisee represents that (a) the Franchisee has reviewed the FDD and has had the opportunity to consult with legal counsel and an accountant, (b) the Franchisee has the financial resources to operate the franchised business, (c) the Franchisee will comply with all applicable laws (including health, food safety, employment, labor, and licensing laws), and (d) the Franchisee will operate the franchised business in good faith and in accordance with the Brand Standards.

8.3 Franchisor Trademark Indemnification. The Franchisor shall defend the Franchisee against any third-party claim that the Franchisee's use of the Licensed Marks (within the scope of this Agreement and the Brand Standards) infringes the third party's trademark rights.

8.4 Franchisee Operational Indemnification. The Franchisee shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Franchisor from any claim arising out of (a) the operation of the franchised business, (b) any food safety, premises liability, employment, or labor claim, (c) the Franchisee's failure to comply with applicable law, or (d) the Franchisee's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

8.5 Insurance. The Franchisee shall maintain insurance as set forth in Schedule 11, including general liability, product liability, premises liability, employment practices liability, and workers' compensation, with the Franchisor named as additional insured.

8.6 Limitation of Liability. THE FRANCHISOR'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE INITIAL FRANCHISE FEE PLUS ROYALTIES PAID IN THE PRIOR TWELVE (12) MONTHS, EXCEPT FOR FRAUD AND WILLFUL MISCONDUCT.

ARTICLE 9 — TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 Term. The Term commences on the Effective Date and continues as set forth in Schedule 3.

9.2 Renewal. The Franchisee may renew the Term for additional renewal terms as set forth in Schedule 3, subject to (a) the Franchisee's good standing under this Agreement, (b) execution of the Franchisor's then-current franchise agreement (which may have different terms), (c) compliance with the Brand Standards, (d) payment of a renewal fee, and (e) any required upgrades to the franchised location.

9.3 Termination by Franchisor. The Franchisor may terminate this Agreement immediately for the Franchisee's:

(a) failure to pay any royalty, fee, or other amount due under this Agreement (after a 10-day cure period);

(b) failure to operate the franchised business for more than five (5) consecutive days without the Franchisor's consent;

(c) failure to comply with any health, food safety, or licensing law that results in the closure of the franchised business or the suspension of any required license;

(d) repeated failure to comply with the Brand Standards (more than two failed inspections in any twelve-month period after notice and opportunity to cure);

(e) material misrepresentation in the Franchisee's application or in any report submitted to the Franchisor;

(f) bankruptcy, insolvency, or assignment for the benefit of creditors; or

(g) any other material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice.

9.4 Termination by Franchisee. The Franchisee may terminate this Agreement only for the Franchisor's material, uncured breach of the Franchisor's obligations under Articles 6 and 10 of the System Operating Manual, after sixty (60) days' written notice.

9.5 Effect of Termination. Upon termination:

(a) The Franchisee shall immediately cease operating the franchised business and using the Licensed Marks.

(b) The Franchisee shall remove all signage, advertising, and other materials displaying the Licensed Marks from the franchised location.

(c) The Franchisee shall return or destroy all Franchisor Confidential Information, including the System Operating Manual.

(d) The Franchisee shall comply with the post-term non-competition and non-solicitation provisions in Schedule 5.

(e) The Franchisee shall pay all amounts due through the date of termination, plus any liquidated damages set forth in Schedule 12.

(f) The Franchisor shall have a right of first refusal to purchase the franchised location's tangible assets at fair market value.

9.6 Survival. Articles 4 (post-termination audit of pre-termination period), 7, 8 (with respect to claims that arose before termination), 9.5, and 10 shall survive termination, along with the post-term restrictive covenants in Schedule 5.

ARTICLE 10 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GOVERNING LAW

10.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, except that the Franchisee's rights under any state franchise relationship laws applicable to the Franchisee's location shall not be waived.

10.2 Mediation. The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through mediation administered by a mutually agreed mediator.

10.3 Arbitration. If mediation is unsuccessful, any dispute shall be settled by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules in {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, except that nothing in this Section 10.3 shall waive any claim that, under applicable franchise relationship law, must be brought in a particular forum.

10.4 Equitable Relief. The Franchisor may seek injunctive relief and specific performance in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce the trademark protection, quality control, confidentiality, and post-term restrictive covenants.
