ARTICLE 2 — RESTRICTIONS

2.1 Use within scope. The Licensee shall use the Licensed Catalog only as expressly permitted in Article 1 and Schedule 2. Any unauthorized use is a breach of this Agreement.

2.2 No edits or remixes. The Licensee shall not create edits, remixes, mashups, instrumental versions, karaoke versions, or other derivative works of the Licensed Catalog without the Licensor's prior written approval.

2.3 No sampling for new works. The Licensee shall not permit any third party to sample or use the Licensed Catalog as a basis for new derivative works.

2.4 No use to train AI. The Licensee shall not use the Licensed Catalog (and shall not permit any DSP or third party to use the Licensed Catalog) to train any artificial intelligence model, generative music model, voice cloning system, or AI-generated audio system. The Licensee shall include in its DSP distribution agreements an express AI training prohibition consistent with this Section 2.4.

2.5 Cover art and metadata integrity. The Licensee shall use only the cover art, track titles, artist credits, and other metadata supplied by the Licensor. The Licensee shall not modify cover art or metadata without consent.

2.6 Channel restrictions. The Licensee shall distribute the Licensed Catalog only through the channels in Schedule 2. The Licensee shall not distribute on lyric video sites, fan sites, fan-made compilations, mood and activity playlists assembled by the Licensee, or any non-DSP context not expressly permitted.

2.7 Catalog withdrawals. The Licensor may withdraw individual recordings from the Licensed Catalog (e.g. for legal, contractual, or artist-relationship reasons) on thirty (30) days' notice. The Licensee shall remove withdrawn recordings from distribution within the notice period.

ARTICLE 3 — ROYALTIES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Distribution fee. The Licensor shall pay the Licensee a distribution fee equal to the percentage set forth in Schedule 3 of the Net Receipts collected by the Licensee from the DSPs and other distribution channels. The Licensee retains the distribution fee from the Net Receipts and remits the balance to the Licensor.

3.2 Royalty allocation. After the Licensee retains the distribution fee, the remaining balance is the Licensor's royalty share, which the Licensor allocates to its artists and royalty participants under separate artist agreements (the Licensee has no obligation to track or audit the Licensor's allocation to artists).

3.3 Upfront advance. The Licensee shall pay the Licensor a non-refundable upfront advance of {{LICENSE_UPFRONT_FEE}} on the Effective Date. The advance shall be recoupable from the Licensor's royalty share over the Term but is not refundable if the royalty share is insufficient to recoup the advance.

3.4 Minimum guarantee. The Licensee shall pay the Licensor a minimum annual royalty of {{LICENSE_MIN_ANNUAL_ROYALTY}} for each calendar year during the Term, applied first against the Licensor's running royalty share and then against the unrecouped advance.

3.5 Royalty Reports. The Licensee shall provide the Licensor with monthly royalty reports within forty-five (45) days after the end of each month, in a format reasonably acceptable to the Licensor, showing per-track and per-recording streaming counts, downloads, physical sales, gross receipts from DSPs, the distribution fee, and the Licensor's royalty share.

3.6 Currency and payment. All payments in United States Dollars by wire transfer.

3.7 Mechanical and performance royalties. As stated in Section 1.6(b), mechanical and performance royalties for the underlying compositions are payable separately. The Licensee shall ensure that the DSPs report and pay mechanical royalties to the appropriate publishers and that performance royalties flow through the PROs in the standard manner.

ARTICLE 4 — RECORDS AND ROYALTY AUDIT

4.1 Records. The Licensee shall maintain complete and accurate books and records of (a) gross receipts from each DSP and distribution channel, (b) per-track streaming counts, downloads, and physical sales for the Licensed Catalog, (c) the distribution fee retained, (d) the Licensor's royalty share, and (e) any deductions, returns, or adjustments, for at least four (4) years.

4.2 Audit Right. The Licensor may, on at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than {{LICENSE_AUDIT_FREQUENCY}}, engage an independent certified public accountant reasonably acceptable to the Licensee to audit the Licensee's books and records solely for purposes of verifying the accuracy of the royalty reports and royalty payments.

4.3 Audit Costs. The Licensor shall bear the cost of the audit, except that if the audit reveals an underpayment of more than five percent (5%) for any calendar year, the Licensee shall reimburse the Licensor for the cost of the audit.

4.4 Audit Findings. The Licensee shall pay any underreported royalties revealed by the audit, plus interest at one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, within thirty (30) days of the audit report.

4.5 Audit of DSP reports. The Licensor's audit right includes the right to audit (or to require the Licensee to audit on the Licensor's behalf) the DSPs' reports of streams, downloads, and revenue. The Licensee shall cooperate with the Licensor's audit of DSPs.

ARTICLE 5 — CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 The Licensee shall hold all Licensor Confidential Information (including the Licensor's pricing terms with the Licensee, the Licensor's other distribution arrangements, artist contracts, and pre-release recordings) in strict confidence.

5.2 The Licensor shall hold the Licensee's distribution arrangements, financial terms, and DSP relationships in confidence.

5.3 Survival. The confidentiality obligations under this Article 5 shall survive termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

ARTICLE 6 — REPRESENTATIONS, WARRANTIES, AND INDEMNIFICATION

6.1 Licensor Representations. The Licensor represents that (a) the Licensor is the owner of, or controls the rights granted herein with respect to, the Licensed Catalog, (b) the Licensor has the right to grant the licenses granted herein, (c) the Licensor has obtained all necessary consents from artists, producers, mixers, sample owners, and other parties with rights in the master recordings, (d) the Licensor has paid (or will pay) any required AFM, AFTRA/SAG, or guild re-use payments, and (e) the Licensor has cleared any sample contained in the master recordings.

6.2 Licensee Representations. The Licensee represents that (a) the Licensee has the corporate authority, (b) the Licensee will distribute the Licensed Catalog only through the approved channels, (c) the Licensee will pay the royalties when due, (d) the Licensee will comply with applicable laws, (e) the Licensee will collect mechanical royalties from DSPs and ensure they reach the publishers, and (f) the Licensee will enforce the AI training prohibition in its DSP agreements.

6.3 Licensor Infringement Indemnification. The Licensor shall defend the Licensee against any third-party claim that the Licensed Catalog infringes the third party's rights, provided that the Licensee distributes the Licensed Catalog within the scope of this Agreement.

6.4 Licensee Indemnification. The Licensee shall defend the Licensor against any claim arising out of (a) the Licensee's distribution of the Licensed Catalog outside the licensed scope, (b) the Licensee's failure to enforce the no-AI-training restriction in its DSP agreements, (c) the Licensee's failure to ensure mechanical royalties are paid, or (d) the Licensee's breach of this Agreement.

6.5 Limitation of Liability. EXCEPT FOR (A) BREACHES OF CONFIDENTIALITY, (B) THE INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, AND (C) WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

ARTICLE 7 — TERM AND TERMINATION OF LICENSE

7.1 Term. The Term commences on the Effective Date and continues as set forth in Schedule 4.

7.2 Termination for Material Breach. Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party for the other Party's material breach, provided that the breaching Party has not cured the breach within the notice period.

7.3 Termination for Failure to Pay Minimum Guarantee. The Licensor may terminate this Agreement on sixty (60) days' notice if the Licensee fails to pay the minimum annual royalty.

7.4 Termination for Insolvency. Either Party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or has a receiver appointed.

7.5 Effect of Termination. Upon termination:

(a) The Licensee shall, within thirty (30) days, take down the Licensed Catalog from all DSPs and other distribution channels, except as needed for the wind-down period.

(b) The Licensee shall have a wind-down period of one hundred eighty (180) days during which the Licensee may continue to fulfill physical orders already placed before the termination date and during which existing streaming use shall continue under the standard royalty mechanics.

(c) The Licensee shall pay all amounts due, including all royalties through the end of the wind-down period.

(d) The Licensee shall return or destroy all physical and digital copies of the Licensed Catalog in the Licensee's possession (except for archived copies needed for ongoing royalty accounting).

7.6 Survival. Articles 4 (post-termination audit), 5, 6 (with respect to claims arising before termination), 7.5, and 8 shall survive termination.
