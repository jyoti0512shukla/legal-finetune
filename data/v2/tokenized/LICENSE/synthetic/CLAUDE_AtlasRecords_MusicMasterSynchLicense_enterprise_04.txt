ARTICLE 2 — GRANT OF LICENSE

2.1 Synchronization License. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Licensor (in its capacity as owner of the Composition or as administrator of the publishing rights therein) hereby grants to the Licensee an {{LICENSE_GRANT_TYPE}}, irrevocable license to record the Composition in timed synchronization with visual images in the Production and to reproduce, distribute, publicly perform, and publicly display the Production embodying the Composition through the Media in the Territory during the Term.

2.2 Master Use License. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Licensor (in its capacity as owner of the Master) hereby grants to the Licensee an {{LICENSE_GRANT_TYPE}}, irrevocable license to use the Master in synchronization with visual images in the Production and to reproduce, distribute, publicly perform, and publicly display the Production embodying the Master through the Media in the Territory during the Term.

2.3 Exclusivity in the Field. During the Exclusivity Period, the Licensor shall not license the Master or the Composition for use in any other audiovisual production in the Field of Use anywhere in the Territory. The exclusivity in this Section 2.3 does not restrict (a) the Licensor's right to use, distribute, sell, or license the Master and the Composition for purposes other than synchronization in audiovisual productions in the Field, including phonorecord distribution, streaming services, performance royalty collection, and other ordinary-course exploitation of the Master and the Composition; or (b) the Licensor's right to license the Master and the Composition for use in audiovisual productions outside the Field.

2.4 Reservation of Rights. The Licensor reserves all rights not expressly granted to the Licensee under this Article 2. No rights are granted by implication, estoppel, or otherwise. Without limitation, the Licensor reserves (a) the right to use the Master and the Composition for any purpose other than synchronization in the Production, (b) the right to grant licenses to others for any purpose outside the scope of the rights granted herein, (c) all phonorecord rights, (d) all public performance rights collected through performing rights organizations, and (e) all so-called "neighboring" rights.

2.5 Sublicensing. {{LICENSE_SUBLICENSE_RIGHTS}}. The Licensee's right to sublicense is limited to the right to sublicense to its distributors, exhibitors, broadcasters, streaming services, and other licensees of the Production solely for purposes of distributing, exhibiting, broadcasting, streaming, or otherwise exploiting the Production through the Media. Each such sublicense shall be coterminous with the Term and shall not grant the sublicensee any rights in the Master or the Composition outside the context of the Production.

ARTICLE 3 — RESTRICTIONS

3.1 No Use Outside the Production. The Licensee shall not use the Master or the Composition for any purpose other than as embodied in the Production, except as expressly permitted by Section 2.1 or 2.2.

3.2 No Standalone Distribution. The Licensee shall not, and shall not permit any sublicensee to, distribute the Master or the Composition on a standalone basis (e.g. as a downloadable audio file, a ringtone, a karaoke product, a podcast not part of the Production, or a digital streaming track).

3.3 No Re-Recording. The Licensee shall not re-record the Composition or create a sound-alike recording or cover version. The Licensee's rights are limited to use of the Master in its existing form as delivered by the Licensor.

3.4 No Editing of the Master. The Licensee shall not edit, remix, or alter the Master except for (a) standard audio leveling and dynamic compression as required for the Media, (b) excerpting (so long as the Composition's title and core musical phrase are recognizable), and (c) creating loops or extensions for the Production's runtime, in each case provided that the Licensee does not change the lyrics or the substance of the Composition. Any other modification requires the Licensor's prior written consent.

3.5 No Endorsement. The Licensee shall not represent or imply that the Licensor or any artist embodied in the Master endorses any product, service, or political position other than as expressly required by the context of the Production.

3.6 No Unauthorized Lyrics Display. The Licensee shall not display the lyrics of the Composition in the Production or in any marketing materials for the Production except as part of the natural audio of the Composition, without the Licensor's prior written consent.

ARTICLE 4 — FINANCIAL TERMS

4.1 Synchronization Fee. In consideration of the synchronization license granted in Section 2.1, the Licensee shall pay the Licensor a one-time, non-refundable synchronization fee of {{LICENSE_UPFRONT_FEE}} within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date.

4.2 Master Use Fee. In consideration of the master use license granted in Section 2.2, the Licensee shall pay the Licensor a one-time, non-refundable master use fee equal to the synchronization fee in Section 4.1, payable within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date.

4.3 Performance Royalties. Public performance royalties payable to the writers and publishers of the Composition through performing rights organizations (ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, and their foreign counterparts) are not included in this Agreement and shall be paid by the Licensee, the broadcaster, the exhibitor, the streaming service, or other applicable party in the ordinary course of performing rights administration.

4.4 Step Payments for Theatrical Box Office. If the Production is released theatrically and the worldwide box office receipts of the Production exceed One Hundred Million United States Dollars (US$100,000,000), the Licensee shall pay the Licensor an additional master use and synchronization fee equal to fifty percent (50%) of the original synchronization fee, within sixty (60) days after the Production crosses the box office threshold. If the worldwide box office receipts exceed Two Hundred Fifty Million United States Dollars (US$250,000,000), the Licensee shall pay the Licensor an additional fee equal to one hundred percent (100%) of the original synchronization fee, within sixty (60) days after the Production crosses the second threshold.

4.5 Soundtrack Album Royalty. If the Master is included on a soundtrack album for the Production, the Licensee shall pay the Licensor a royalty equal to {{LICENSE_ROYALTY_RATE}} of the Licensee's net wholesale receipts from sales of the soundtrack album allocable to the Master (calculated pro rata to the total number of recordings on the soundtrack album).

4.6 Most Favored Nations. The Licensor shall be entitled to most favored nations treatment with respect to (a) the synchronization fee, (b) the master use fee, and (c) the credit set forth in Section 5.3, for any other master recording or composition used in the Production. If the Licensee grants any other licensor of any other master or composition used in the Production more favorable terms with respect to (a), (b), or (c), the Licensee shall promptly notify the Licensor and shall increase the corresponding terms for the Licensor to match the more favorable terms.

4.7 Currency. All payments shall be made in United States Dollars by wire transfer to the account designated by the Licensor in writing.

ARTICLE 5 — DELIVERY, CUE SHEETS, AND CREDITS

5.1 Delivery of Master. Within fifteen (15) business days of the Effective Date and the Licensor's receipt of the synchronization fee and the master use fee, the Licensor shall deliver to the Licensee a high-resolution audio file of the Master suitable for use in the Production.

5.2 Cue Sheet. The Licensee shall prepare and deliver to the Licensor an accurate Cue Sheet for the Production within thirty (30) days after the Production is locked, identifying the use of the Master and the Composition (timing, duration, type of use — vocal/instrumental, foreground/background, opening credits, closing credits, etc.).

5.3 On-Screen Credit. The Licensee shall accord the Licensor an on-screen credit in the Production's end credits in substantially the form: "[Composition Title], performed by [Artist], written by [Writers], courtesy of [Licensor]." The credit shall appear in the same size, type, and prominence as the credits accorded to other licensors of master recordings used in the Production. The credit shall be on a single card or in a list with the credits for other master recordings; on a list, the order of credits shall be alphabetical or chronological by appearance in the Production.

ARTICLE 6 — RECORDS AND ROYALTY AUDIT

6.1 Records. The Licensee shall maintain complete and accurate books and records of (a) the soundtrack album sales and the royalty calculation under Section 4.5, and (b) the worldwide box office receipts of the Production, for at least three (3) years after the relevant calendar year.

6.2 Audit Right. The Licensor shall have the right, on at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than {{LICENSE_AUDIT_FREQUENCY}}, to engage an independent certified public accountant reasonably acceptable to the Licensee to audit the Licensee's books and records solely for purposes of verifying the accuracy of any payments due under Sections 4.4 and 4.5.

6.3 Audit Costs and Findings. The Licensor shall bear the cost of the audit, except that if the audit reveals an underpayment of more than five percent (5%) for any calendar year, the Licensee shall reimburse the Licensor for the cost of the audit. The Licensee shall pay any underreported amounts within thirty (30) days of the audit report, plus interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law.
