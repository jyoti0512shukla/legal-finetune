GENOMIC DATASET LICENSE AGREEMENT — RESEARCH AND MODEL TRAINING USE

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Licensor is the curator and licensor of certain de-identified human whole-genome sequencing datasets and associated phenotypic metadata identified on Schedule A (the "Licensed Dataset"), which were collected from research participants under informed consent protocols approved by the relevant Institutional Review Boards;

WHEREAS, the Customer is engaged in research and development of machine learning and statistical models for genomic interpretation, variant prioritization, polygenic risk scoring, and related applications, and the Customer wishes to license the Licensed Dataset for use in training, validating, and evaluating such models;

WHEREAS, the Licensor is willing to grant the Customer the rights set forth in this Agreement, subject to certain confidentiality, security, and consent-honoring obligations that are essential to maintaining the trust of research participants and complying with applicable laws;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Authorized Personnel" means the Customer's employees and individual contractors who have a need to access the Licensed Dataset for the Permitted Use, who have completed the Customer's data-handling training, and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in Article 7.

1.2 "Consented Use" means the categories of research and use that the original research participants consented to when contributing their data to the Licensed Dataset, as further described in Schedule B.

1.3 "Derived Data" means data, models, statistical summaries, embeddings, model weights, and other outputs generated by the Customer's analysis of the Licensed Dataset that do not contain any individually identifiable record from the Licensed Dataset.

1.4 "IRB" means an Institutional Review Board or equivalent ethics committee.

1.5 "Licensed Dataset" means the de-identified whole-genome sequence data, variant call files, phenotypic metadata, and related documentation identified on Schedule A.

1.6 "Permitted Use" means the use of the Licensed Dataset for the Customer's internal research, development, training, validation, and evaluation of machine learning models for genomic interpretation, variant prioritization, polygenic risk scoring, and related research applications, in each case within the Field.

1.7 "Field" means {{LICENSE_FIELD_OF_USE}}.

1.8 "Re-Identification" means any attempt to determine the identity of any individual whose data is included in the Licensed Dataset, including by linking the Licensed Dataset to any external database that could plausibly identify individuals.

1.9 "Secure Computing Environment" means a computing environment that meets the security requirements set forth on Schedule C, including network isolation, access controls, audit logging, encryption at rest and in transit, and physical security.

1.10 "Territory" means {{LICENSE_TERRITORY}}.

(a) access the Licensed Dataset solely within the Customer's Secure Computing Environment;

(b) use the Licensed Dataset for the Permitted Use, by Authorized Personnel only;

(c) generate Derived Data from the Licensed Dataset for the Permitted Use;

(d) publish, in peer-reviewed scientific publications, summary statistics, model performance metrics, and analytical findings derived from the Licensed Dataset, provided that no individual record is published and no information sufficient to enable Re-Identification is disclosed; and

ARTICLE 3 — RESTRICTIONS

3.1 No Re-Identification. The Customer shall not, and shall not permit any Authorized Personnel or third party to, attempt to re-identify any individual whose data is in the Licensed Dataset, link the Licensed Dataset to any external database that could plausibly identify individuals, or contact any individual whose data is in the Licensed Dataset.

3.2 No Distribution of Raw Data. The Customer shall not distribute, publish, or otherwise disclose the Licensed Dataset (or any portion thereof containing individually identifiable records, including individual VCF files, individual sequencing reads, or individual phenotypic records) to any third party, except as expressly permitted in Section 2.1(d) or (e). The Customer shall not post any portion of the Licensed Dataset to any public dataset repository, code repository, cloud storage bucket, or other publicly accessible location.

3.3 No Use Outside Permitted Use. The Customer shall not use the Licensed Dataset for any purpose outside the Permitted Use, including (a) any commercial purpose other than developing the Customer's research models, (b) any purpose inconsistent with the Consented Use as set forth in Schedule B, (c) any clinical decision-making or patient-facing application, (d) any insurance underwriting, employment, or educational placement decision, or (e) any law enforcement purpose.

3.4 No Removal of Privacy Controls. The Customer shall not remove, disable, or circumvent any access controls, encryption, audit logging, or other privacy and security mechanisms applied to the Licensed Dataset.

3.5 Authorized Personnel Only. Access to the Licensed Dataset shall be limited to Authorized Personnel. The Customer shall maintain a current list of Authorized Personnel and shall provide the list to the Licensor on request.

3.6 Honoring Withdrawal of Consent. If the Licensor notifies the Customer that an individual has withdrawn consent to participate in the Licensed Dataset, the Customer shall, within thirty (30) days of such notice, delete the individual's records from the Customer's copy of the Licensed Dataset and from any in-progress analyses. The Customer is NOT required to retrain or modify any models that have already been trained on the Licensed Dataset prior to the withdrawal notice, but the Customer shall not initiate new model training runs that use the withdrawn individual's data.

3.7 No Commercial Use Without Separate License. The Customer shall not use the Licensed Dataset to develop a commercial product (other than research-tool software intended for use by other researchers in non-clinical settings) without first obtaining a separate written commercial license from the Licensor on additional terms.

3.8 No Use to Train Foundation Models for Public Release. The Customer shall not use the Licensed Dataset to train any foundation model that the Customer plans to release publicly (whether open-source, open-weights, or via a public API) without the Licensor's prior written consent. Foundation model training restrictions are intended to address the risk that aggregated training data may inadvertently embed re-identifying information at the model level.

4.3 Annual Maintenance Fee. The Customer shall pay the Licensor an annual maintenance fee of {{LICENSE_MIN_ANNUAL_ROYALTY}} for each year of the Term, in consideration of the Licensor's ongoing data curation, security maintenance, and provision of dataset Updates.

4.4 Payment Terms. All payments are due net sixty (60) days from the Customer's receipt of an undisputed invoice. The Customer may withhold disputed amounts pending resolution.

ARTICLE 5 — RECORDS AND COMPLIANCE AUDIT

5.1 Access Logs. The Customer shall maintain audit logs of all access to the Licensed Dataset, including the Authorized Personnel who accessed the data, the date and time of access, and the actions taken, for at least three (3) years.

5.2 Compliance Audit Right. The Licensor may, on at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice and not more than {{LICENSE_AUDIT_FREQUENCY}}, conduct a compliance audit of the Customer's use of the Licensed Dataset. The audit may include (a) review of the Customer's access logs, (b) review of the Customer's Authorized Personnel list, (c) inspection of the Customer's Secure Computing Environment configuration, (d) review of the Customer's policies and procedures for handling the Licensed Dataset, and (e) interviews with Authorized Personnel. The audit shall be conducted during normal business hours and shall not unreasonably interfere with the Customer's operations. The Licensor shall execute a confidentiality agreement reasonably acceptable to the Customer before the audit and shall comply with the Customer's facility security and policies.

5.3 Audit Findings. If the audit identifies any non-compliance, the Licensor shall provide the Customer with a written report. The Customer shall remediate any non-compliance within a reasonable time. Material non-compliance is grounds for termination under Article 9.

5.4 Cost of Audit. The Licensor shall bear the cost of the audit, except that if the audit reveals material non-compliance, the Customer shall reimburse the Licensor for the reasonable cost of the audit.

ARTICLE 6 — IRB, REGULATORY, AND CONSENT-HONORING OBLIGATIONS

6.1 IRB Approval. The Customer represents that the Customer's use of the Licensed Dataset has received IRB approval (or an IRB exemption determination) under the Customer's institutional research-oversight policies, where required. The Customer shall maintain such approval throughout the Term.

6.2 Compliance with HIPAA, GINA, and Common Rule. The Customer shall comply with all applicable laws relating to the handling of human research data, including (where applicable) the HIPAA Privacy Rule, the Genetic Information Nondiscrimination Act (GINA), the Common Rule (45 C.F.R. Part 46), and any state-level genetic privacy laws.

6.3 No Genetic Discrimination. The Customer shall not use the Licensed Dataset (or any model trained on the Licensed Dataset) to make any decision that would constitute genetic discrimination under GINA or any equivalent law.

6.4 Cooperation with Withdrawal Notices. The Customer shall implement procedures to receive, process, and act on withdrawal notices from the Licensor under Section 3.6 within the required time frame.

ARTICLE 7 — CONFIDENTIALITY AND SECURITY

7.1 Confidentiality. The Licensed Dataset is the Confidential Information of the Licensor and the research participants who contributed to it. The Customer shall hold the Licensed Dataset in strict confidence and shall protect it with at least the same care that the Customer uses to protect its own most sensitive confidential information, but in no event less than the security requirements set forth in Schedule C.

7.2 Secure Computing Environment. The Customer shall store and process the Licensed Dataset only in the Secure Computing Environment described in Schedule C. The Customer shall not copy, transfer, or transmit the Licensed Dataset outside the Secure Computing Environment except as needed for routine backup, disaster recovery, or as expressly authorized by the Licensor.

7.3 Encryption. The Customer shall encrypt the Licensed Dataset at rest using AES-256 (or equivalent) and in transit using TLS 1.3 (or equivalent).

7.4 Incident Notification. The Customer shall notify the Licensor of any actual or suspected breach of the Licensed Dataset's confidentiality or security within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of the incident. The notification shall include the nature of the incident, the data affected, the Customer's response, and the Customer's plan for remediation. The Customer shall reasonably cooperate with the Licensor's investigation and any required notification to research participants, IRBs, or regulatory authorities.

7.5 Survival. The confidentiality and security obligations under this Article 7 shall survive termination or expiration of this Agreement and shall continue indefinitely with respect to the Licensed Dataset.

ARTICLE 8 — REPRESENTATIONS, WARRANTIES, AND INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Licensor Representations. The Licensor represents and warrants that (a) the Licensor has the right to grant the rights granted herein, (b) the Licensed Dataset was collected under informed consent and IRB approval consistent with the Common Rule and applicable laws, (c) the Licensed Dataset has been de-identified in accordance with the HIPAA Safe Harbor method or the HIPAA Expert Determination method, (d) the Licensor has not received any notice from any research participant or IRB challenging the Licensor's right to license the Licensed Dataset, and (e) the Licensed Dataset, as delivered, does not contain any direct identifiers (names, addresses, phone numbers, social security numbers, MRNs, etc.).

8.2 No Warranty of Accuracy. EXCEPT FOR THE EXPRESS REPRESENTATIONS IN SECTION 8.1, THE LICENSED DATASET IS PROVIDED "AS IS." THE LICENSOR DOES NOT WARRANT THE ACCURACY, COMPLETENESS, FITNESS FOR ANY PARTICULAR PURPOSE, OR SUITABILITY FOR ANY PARTICULAR ANALYTICAL OR MACHINE LEARNING APPLICATION.

8.3 Licensor Indemnification. The Licensor shall defend the Customer against any third-party claim arising out of (a) the Licensor's failure to obtain valid informed consent for the Licensed Dataset, (b) the Licensor's misrepresentation of the de-identification status of the Licensed Dataset, or (c) the Licensor's breach of the Licensor's authority warranty under Section 8.1(a). The Licensor's indemnification is capped at three (3) times the License Fee paid.

8.4 Customer Indemnification. The Customer shall defend the Licensor against any third-party claim arising out of (a) the Customer's use of the Licensed Dataset outside the Permitted Use, (b) the Customer's breach of the security or re-identification provisions, (c) the Customer's failure to honor a withdrawal notice, (d) the Customer's failure to maintain IRB approval, or (e) the Customer's gross negligence or willful misconduct in handling the Licensed Dataset. The Customer's indemnification under (a)-(c) is uncapped given the sensitivity of human research data.

8.5 Limitation of Liability. EXCEPT FOR (A) BREACHES OF CONFIDENTIALITY UNDER ARTICLE 7, (B) THE INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTIONS 8.3 AND 8.4, (C) THE CUSTOMER'S BREACH OF THE RE-IDENTIFICATION PROHIBITION, AND (D) WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES. THE CUSTOMER'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IS NOT CAPPED WITH RESPECT TO ITEMS (A) THROUGH (D).

ARTICLE 9 — TERM AND TERMINATION OF LICENSE

9.1 Term. The Term commences on the Effective Date and continues for the period set forth in Section 2.5, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Article 9.

9.2 Termination for Material Breach. The Licensor may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice for the Customer's material breach of (a) Article 3 (Restrictions), (b) Article 6 (IRB / Regulatory), or (c) Article 7 (Confidentiality and Security), without any cure period. The Customer may terminate for the Licensor's material breach upon thirty (30) days' notice and cure opportunity.

9.3 Termination for Convenience by Customer. The Customer may terminate this Agreement on ninety (90) days' written notice for any reason. Upon termination for convenience, the Licensor shall not refund the License Fee, but the Customer's annual maintenance fees shall be prorated.

9.4 Effect of Termination. Upon termination of this Agreement (other than by the Customer for the Licensor's material breach):

(a) The Customer shall, within thirty (30) days, delete or destroy all copies of the Licensed Dataset in the Customer's possession or control, including backup copies, working copies, and intermediate files, and shall certify destruction in writing.

(b) The Customer may retain Derived Data (including trained models) generated prior to termination, subject to continued compliance with Article 3 and Article 7.

(c) The Customer shall not initiate any new analyses, model training runs, or other use of the Licensed Dataset after the date of termination.

(d) The Customer shall pay all amounts due through the date of termination.

(e) The Customer shall cooperate with any post-termination compliance audit reasonably requested by the Licensor under Article 5.

9.5 Survival. Articles 1, 3.1 (re-identification), 3.6 (withdrawal notices that arrive post-termination concerning models trained pre-termination), 5 (audit), 6, 7, 8, 9.4, and 10 shall survive termination.
