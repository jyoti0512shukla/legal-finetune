3.4 Failure to meet milestones. If the Licensee fails to meet a milestone by the deadline, the Licensee may, on written notice to the Licensor, request a one-time extension of up to twelve (12) months. ARTICLE 4 — FINANCIAL TERMS

4.1 Upfront License Fee. The Licensee shall pay the Licensor a one-time upfront license fee of {{LICENSE_UPFRONT_FEE}} within sixty (60) days of the Effective Date.

4.2 Annual Maintenance Fee. The Licensee shall pay the Licensor an annual maintenance fee as set forth in Schedule 3, payable on each anniversary of the Effective Date until the first commercial sale of a Licensed Product.

4.3 Running Royalty. The Licensee shall pay the Licensor a running royalty equal to {{LICENSE_ROYALTY_RATE}} on Net Sales of Licensed Products, on a Licensed Product-by-Licensed Product and country-by-country basis. "Net Sales" is defined in Schedule 4.

4.4 Minimum Annual Royalty. Beginning in the first calendar year after First Commercial Sale, the Licensee shall pay the Licensor a minimum annual royalty of {{LICENSE_MIN_ANNUAL_ROYALTY}} for each calendar year, creditable against running royalties otherwise payable for the same calendar year.

4.5 Sublicense Income. If the Licensee grants any sublicense under the Licensed Patents (where permitted by Section 1.5), the Licensee shall pay the Licensor a percentage of all consideration received by the Licensee from the sublicensee (including upfront fees, milestone payments, royalties, and other consideration), as set forth in Schedule 5. 4.6 Patent Prosecution Cost Reimbursement. The Licensee shall reimburse the Licensor for all out-of-pocket patent prosecution costs (including USPTO fees, foreign filing fees, attorney fees, and translation costs) incurred by the Licensor for the Licensed Patents from the Effective Date forward. The Licensee shall also reimburse a portion of the historical prosecution costs incurred prior to the Effective Date, as set forth in Schedule 6.

4.7 Royalty Reports. The Licensee shall provide the Licensor with semi-annual royalty reports within sixty (60) days after June 30 and December 31 of each calendar year, showing Net Sales and royalty calculations by Licensed Product and country.

4.8 Currency. All payments shall be in United States Dollars by wire transfer or check.

ARTICLE 5 — RECORDS AND ROYALTY AUDIT

5.1 Records. The Licensee shall maintain complete and accurate books and records of the Licensed Products and Net Sales for at least five (5) years.

5.2 Audit Right. The Licensor may, on at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than {{LICENSE_AUDIT_FREQUENCY}}, engage an independent certified public accountant reasonably acceptable to the Licensee to audit the Licensee's books and records solely for purposes of verifying the accuracy of the royalty reports and royalty payments.

5.3 Audit Costs. The Licensor shall bear the cost of the audit, except that if the audit reveals an underpayment of more than five percent (5%) for any calendar year, the Licensee shall reimburse the Licensor for the cost of the audit.

5.4 Audit Findings. The Licensee shall pay any underreported royalties revealed by the audit, plus interest at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, within thirty (30) days of the audit report.

ARTICLE 6 — IMPROVEMENTS, PATENT PROSECUTION, AND ENFORCEMENT

6.1 Patent prosecution. The Licensor shall control the patent prosecution and maintenance of the Licensed Patents at the Licensee's expense (under Section 4.6). The Licensor shall keep the Licensee reasonably informed of the status of the Licensed Patents and shall give the Licensee a reasonable opportunity to comment on prosecution strategy.

6.2 Election to abandon. If the Licensor elects to abandon any Licensed Patent in any country, the Licensor shall give the Licensee at least sixty (60) days' notice and the opportunity to assume prosecution at the Licensee's expense. If the Licensee assumes prosecution, the Licensor may, at its option, assign the patent to the Licensee, in which case the patent shall cease to be a Licensed Patent.

6.3 Enforcement. The Licensor shall have the first right (but not the obligation) to enforce the Licensed Patents against any third-party infringement. If the Licensor does not initiate enforcement within ninety (90) days of becoming aware of the infringement, the Licensee shall have the right (but not the obligation) to enforce, at the Licensee's expense. Recovery shall be allocated as set forth in Schedule 7.

6.4 Improvements. Any Improvements made by the Licensor or the Licensor's faculty during the Term, in the Field, are not automatically included in this license. The Licensee shall have a right of first negotiation to license Improvements separately, on terms to be negotiated in good faith.

ARTICLE 9 — TERM AND TERMINATION OF LICENSE

9.1 Term. As set forth in Schedule 3, the Term commences on the Effective Date and continues until the expiration of the last-to-expire Licensed Patent (subject to earlier termination under this Article 9).

9.2 Termination by the Licensee. The Licensee may terminate this Agreement at any time on ninety (90) days' written notice. The Licensee shall pay all amounts accrued through the date of termination.

9.3 Termination by the Licensor. The Licensor may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice for the Licensee's:

(a) failure to pay any fee, royalty, or reimbursement obligation when due (after a 15-day cure period);

(b) failure to maintain the required insurance under Section 8.4;

(c) failure to meet any milestone after the extension period under Section 3.4;

(d) material breach of any other provision after thirty (30) days' notice and opportunity to cure; or

(e) bankruptcy, insolvency, or assignment for the benefit of creditors.

9.4 Conversion to Non-Exclusive. As an alternative to termination, the Licensor may, at its option, convert an exclusive license to non-exclusive if the Licensee fails to meet diligence requirements.

9.5 Effect of Termination. Upon termination:

(a) The Licensee shall cease all manufacture, use, and sale of Licensed Products, subject to a sell-off period of six (6) months for inventory in the Licensee's possession (with continued royalty payments).

(b) The Licensee shall pay all amounts due through the end of the sell-off period.

(c) The Licensee shall return or destroy any Licensor Confidential Information.

(d) Any sublicenses granted by the Licensee shall, at the Licensor's option, either survive (with the Licensor stepping into the Licensee's shoes for purposes of receiving sublicensee payments) or terminate.

9.6 Survival. Articles 5 (post-termination audit), 7, 8 (with respect to claims arising before termination), 9.5, 10, and 11 shall survive termination.
