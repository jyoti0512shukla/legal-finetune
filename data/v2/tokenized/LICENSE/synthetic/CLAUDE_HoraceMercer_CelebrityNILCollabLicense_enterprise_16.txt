NAME, IMAGE, AND LIKENESS LICENSE AGREEMENT — CELEBRITY BRAND COLLABORATION

This NAME, IMAGE, AND LIKENESS LICENSE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} acting as the loan-out company on behalf of the individual identified on Schedule A (the "Talent"), located at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Licensor"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} located at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} (the "Licensee" or "Brand"). The Brand has insisted on its standard talent-licensing terms.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Talent is a recognized public figure with significant goodwill, brand value, and commercial appeal in the Talent's primary professional field;

WHEREAS, the Licensor is the loan-out company through which the Talent provides services and licenses the Talent's name, image, likeness, voice, signature, biographical information, and personal indicia (collectively, the "NIL Rights") for commercial purposes;

WHEREAS, the Brand wishes to license the NIL Rights for use in connection with the Brand's "Brand Collaboration" product line and related marketing campaign, as further described in Schedule B (the "Campaign");

WHEREAS, the Licensor is willing to grant the license on the terms and conditions set forth herein, which include creative approval, morals clauses, exclusivity within the licensed product category, and standard talent-protection provisions;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Approved Use" means a use of the NIL Rights that has been pre-approved in writing by the Licensor or the Talent's authorized representative in accordance with Article 7.

1.2 "Brand Collaboration Products" means the products in the licensed product category developed and marketed by the Brand as part of the Campaign, as further described in Schedule B.

1.3 "Campaign" means the marketing campaign and product line for which the NIL Rights are licensed, as further described in Schedule B.

1.4 "Excluded Categories" means the product, brand, and political categories identified on Schedule C, in which the Talent expressly declines to be associated.

1.5 "Field" means {{LICENSE_FIELD_OF_USE}}.

1.6 "NIL Rights" means the Talent's name, image, likeness, voice, signature, biographical information, photographs, video footage, and other personal indicia of identity, in each case as identified or approved under this Agreement.

1.7 "Sponsorship Engagements" means the in-person and virtual appearances, photoshoots, video productions, social media activities, and other promotional engagements the Talent is required to participate in under Schedule D.

1.8 "Term" means the term set forth in Section 9.1.

1.9 "Territory" means {{LICENSE_TERRITORY}}.

ARTICLE 2 — GRANT OF LICENSE

2.1 NIL License Grant. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Licensor hereby grants to the Brand an {{LICENSE_GRANT_TYPE}} license to use the Talent's NIL Rights in connection with the Campaign, including:

(a) the manufacture, marketing, advertising, distribution, and sale of the Brand Collaboration Products in the Field;

(b) the production, broadcast, and distribution of marketing campaign materials (television commercials, print ads, digital ads, social media posts, in-store displays, billboards, packaging, point-of-sale materials, public-relations releases) featuring the Talent;

(c) the operation of the Talent's social media presence as part of the Campaign, in coordination with the Talent's representatives; and

(d) the creation of derivative marketing materials based on photoshoots, video shoots, and approved likenesses of the Talent.

2.2 Exclusivity Within Category. During the Term, the Talent shall not endorse, promote, or appear in any commercial association with any direct competitor of the Brand within the licensed product category. The list of restricted competitors is set forth on Schedule E and may be updated by mutual written agreement.

2.3 Exclusivity Outside Category. The exclusivity in Section 2.2 is limited to the licensed product category. The Talent remains free to enter into licensing or endorsement arrangements with brands in any product category outside the licensed product category, including with companies that operate in unrelated industries.

2.4 Reservation of Rights. The Licensor and the Talent retain all rights, title, and interest in and to the NIL Rights not expressly granted to the Brand. The Brand acquires no ownership of the NIL Rights and no right to use the NIL Rights outside the scope of this Agreement.

2.5 Sublicensing. {{LICENSE_SUBLICENSE_RIGHTS}}.

2.6 Field of Use, Territory, and Term. {{LICENSE_FIELD_OF_USE}} {{LICENSE_TERRITORY}} {{LICENSE_TERM}}.

2.7 Goodwill. All goodwill arising from the Brand's use of the NIL Rights inures to the benefit of the Talent and the Licensor, not the Brand.

ARTICLE 3 — RESTRICTIONS

3.1 Approved Uses Only. The Brand shall use the NIL Rights only for Approved Uses. Each photo, video, social post, marketing material, packaging design, and product configuration featuring the NIL Rights must be approved in advance under Article 7.

3.2 No Use After Termination. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, the Brand shall cease all new use of the NIL Rights. The Brand shall have a sell-off period of one hundred eighty (180) days during which the Brand may sell down existing inventory, subject to continued payment of royalties.

3.3 Excluded Categories. The Brand shall not, and shall not permit the Campaign to involve, association with any of the Excluded Categories on Schedule C, including (without limitation) tobacco, e-cigarettes, vaping, firearms, gambling, adult content, prescription pharmaceuticals (other than as expressly approved), and political campaigns.

3.4 No Implied Endorsement. The Brand shall not state or imply that the Talent endorses any product or service of the Brand other than the Brand Collaboration Products. The Brand shall not use the Talent's appearance for the Campaign as a basis for inferring endorsement of the Brand's other product lines.

3.5 No Modifications That Misrepresent. The Brand shall not modify, manipulate, deepfake, or alter any image, video, or audio recording of the Talent in any way that misrepresents the Talent's appearance, voice, statements, or actions.

3.6 No Use to Generate AI Training Data. The Brand shall not use the NIL Rights (or any photograph, video, or audio recording of the Talent) to train any artificial intelligence model, generative AI system, voice cloning tool, or face replacement tool. The Brand shall not provide the NIL Rights to any third party for such purposes.

3.7 Brand Standards. The Brand shall use the NIL Rights in a manner consistent with the Brand's high-quality brand standards and shall not use the NIL Rights in any context that the Talent or the Licensor would reasonably consider degrading, embarrassing, or contrary to the Talent's public persona.

ARTICLE 4 — FINANCIAL TERMS

4.1 Upfront Endorsement Fee. In consideration of the rights granted hereunder and the Talent's participation in the Sponsorship Engagements, the Brand shall pay the Licensor a non-refundable upfront endorsement fee of {{LICENSE_UPFRONT_FEE}}, payable in three installments: (a) one-third within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date, (b) one-third on the first anniversary of the Effective Date, and (c) one-third on the second anniversary of the Effective Date.

4.2 Royalty on Brand Collaboration Products. The Brand shall pay the Licensor a running royalty equal to {{LICENSE_ROYALTY_RATE}} on the Brand's Net Sales of the Brand Collaboration Products in the Territory.

4.3 Minimum Annual Royalty. The Brand shall pay the Licensor a minimum annual royalty of {{LICENSE_MIN_ANNUAL_ROYALTY}} for each calendar year during the Term, creditable against the running royalties under Section 4.2 in the same calendar year.

4.4 Per-Engagement Fees. The Brand shall pay the Licensor an additional fee for each Sponsorship Engagement beyond the included Sponsorship Engagements set forth in Schedule D, at the per-engagement rates specified in Schedule D.

4.5 Travel and Production. The Brand shall reimburse the Licensor and the Talent for all reasonable travel, lodging, hair / makeup, and styling expenses incurred in connection with the Sponsorship Engagements, in accordance with the Talent's standard travel rider attached as Schedule F.

4.6 Royalty Reports. The Brand shall provide the Licensor with quarterly royalty reports within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, showing units sold, Net Sales, and royalty calculations by product. Royalties shall be paid with the report.

4.7 Currency. All payments shall be made in United States Dollars by wire transfer to the account designated by the Licensor.

ARTICLE 5 — RECORDS AND ROYALTY AUDIT

5.1 Records. The Brand shall maintain complete and accurate books and records of unit sales, Net Sales, and royalty calculations for the Brand Collaboration Products for at least four (4) years.

5.2 Audit Right. The Licensor may, on at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice and not more than {{LICENSE_AUDIT_FREQUENCY}}, engage an independent certified public accountant reasonably acceptable to the Brand to audit the Brand's books and records solely for purposes of verifying the accuracy of the royalty reports and royalty payments.

5.3 Audit Costs. The Licensor shall bear the cost of the audit, except that if the audit reveals an underpayment of more than three percent (3%) for any calendar year, the Brand shall reimburse the Licensor for the cost of the audit. (Note: the underpayment threshold is reduced from the customary 5% because of the high commercial sensitivity of celebrity endorsement deals.)

5.4 Audit Findings. The Brand shall pay any underreported royalties revealed by the audit, plus interest at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, within thirty (30) days of the audit report.

ARTICLE 6 — REPRESENTATIONS, WARRANTIES, AND INDEMNIFICATION

6.1 Licensor Representations. The Licensor represents and warrants that (a) the Licensor has the right to grant the NIL license on behalf of the Talent, (b) the Talent has authorized the Licensor to enter into this Agreement, (c) the Talent will reasonably participate in the Sponsorship Engagements, and (d) the Talent is not currently subject to any conflicting endorsement agreement with any direct competitor of the Brand within the licensed product category.

6.2 Brand Representations. The Brand represents and warrants that (a) the Brand will manufacture, market, and sell the Brand Collaboration Products in compliance with applicable laws (including consumer protection, product safety, false advertising, and labeling laws), (b) the Brand will use the NIL Rights only as approved, (c) the Brand has the corporate authority to enter this Agreement, and (d) the Brand carries product liability insurance with minimum coverage of Five Million United States Dollars (US$5,000,000) per occurrence.

6.3 Brand Indemnification of Licensor. The Brand shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Licensor and the Talent from any claim arising out of (a) the Brand Collaboration Products (including product liability, defective product, false advertising, and consumer protection claims), (b) the Brand's marketing materials (other than claims arising solely from the Talent's NIL or pre-approved photoshoot output), (c) the Brand's failure to comply with applicable laws, or (d) the Brand's use of the NIL Rights outside the scope of this Agreement. The Brand's indemnification obligation is uncapped.

6.4 Licensor Indemnification of Brand. The Licensor shall defend the Brand against any claim that the Talent's NIL Rights, as licensed hereunder, infringe the rights of any third party (e.g. a claim by a third party that the Talent has previously licensed the same rights to that third party). The Licensor's indemnification is capped at the upfront endorsement fee paid by the Brand.

6.5 Talent Personal Conduct (Morals Clause). The Talent shall conduct himself or herself in a manner consistent with the goodwill and reputation of the Brand. If the Talent engages in conduct that, in the reasonable judgment of the Brand, brings the Talent or the Brand into significant public disrepute, scandal, or contempt — including conviction of a felony, public conviction of a crime of moral turpitude, or any conduct widely reported and condemned in the press — the Brand may, at its option, (a) suspend the Talent's Sponsorship Engagements pending the Brand's investigation, (b) terminate this Agreement under Section 9.2, or (c) reduce the Brand's marketing use of the NIL Rights pending the Talent's rehabilitation.

6.6 Brand Personal Conduct (Reverse Morals Clause). The Brand shall conduct its business in a manner consistent with the Talent's public persona and stated values. If the Brand engages in conduct that the Talent reasonably determines is harmful to the Talent's public reputation — including any environmental, labor, human rights, or business ethics scandal that is widely reported — the Talent (through the Licensor) may, at its option, (a) suspend the Talent's Sponsorship Engagements pending the Talent's investigation, or (b) terminate this Agreement under Section 9.2 (Talent termination right). In the event of Talent termination under this Section 6.6, the Brand shall continue to pay any earned but unpaid royalties through the date of termination.

6.7 Limitation of Liability. EXCEPT FOR (A) BREACHES OF CONFIDENTIALITY, (B) THE INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, AND (C) WILLFUL MISCONDUCT OR FRAUD, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

ARTICLE 7 — APPROVAL PROCESS AND BRAND STANDARDS

7.1 Submission for Approval. Before using any photograph, video, social media post, marketing material, packaging design, or product configuration that features the NIL Rights, the Brand shall submit it to the Licensor (or the Talent's designated representative) for review and approval.

7.2 Approval Turnaround. The Licensor (or the Talent's representative) shall provide approval, conditional approval, or rejection within ten (10) business days of submission. If the Licensor does not respond within the ten-day window, the submission is deemed approved.

7.3 Talent Pre-Approval of Photoshoot Output. For each photoshoot, video shoot, and similar production session, the Talent shall have the right to review and approve the selection of images and footage that will be used. The Talent shall not unreasonably withhold approval of images that are professional in quality and consistent with the Talent's brand.

7.4 Brand Standards. The Brand shall ensure that all uses of the NIL Rights are consistent with the brand standards, image guidelines, and creative direction set forth in Schedule G, as updated from time to time by the Talent's representatives.

ARTICLE 8 — CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 The Brand and the Licensor each agree to hold the financial terms of this Agreement, the Schedules, and any non-public marketing strategy in strict confidence.

8.2 The Brand shall not announce the Campaign or the Talent's involvement in the Campaign before the agreed launch date set forth in Schedule B, and shall not disclose the Talent's involvement to any third party (other than the Brand's officers, employees, advisors, and necessary production vendors under confidentiality) before the launch date.

8.3 The Talent and the Talent's representatives shall not announce the Talent's involvement in the Campaign before the agreed launch date.

8.4 Survival. The confidentiality obligations under this Article 8 shall survive termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

ARTICLE 9 — TERM AND TERMINATION OF LICENSE

9.1 Term. The Term commences on the Effective Date and continues as set forth in Section 2.6.

9.2 Termination Triggers. This Agreement may be terminated:

(a) By either Party for the other Party's material breach, after thirty (30) days' written notice and opportunity to cure;

(b) By the Brand under the morals clause in Section 6.5, immediately and without refund of any portion of the upfront fee already paid (provided that any future installment payments under Section 4.1 are excused);

(c) By the Licensor (on behalf of the Talent) under the reverse morals clause in Section 6.6, immediately, with the Brand's obligation to pay earned royalties continuing through the date of termination;

(d) By either Party for the other Party's bankruptcy, insolvency, or dissolution; or

(e) By the Brand if the Talent is unable to perform Sponsorship Engagements due to disability, death, or extended unavailability for more than six (6) months, with refund of any portion of the upfront fee that is unearned (calculated proportionally over the Term).

9.3 Effect of Termination. Upon termination:

(a) The Brand shall cease all new use of the NIL Rights, but the Brand shall have the sell-off period under Section 3.2 to dispose of existing inventory of Brand Collaboration Products.

(b) The Brand shall pay all earned but unpaid royalties through the end of the sell-off period.

(c) The Brand shall return or destroy any unpublished photo and video assets featuring the Talent.

(d) The Brand shall not include the Talent in any new marketing campaign or material.

(e) The Brand shall remove the Talent from any continuing social media campaigns.

9.4 Survival. Articles 1, 3 (excluded categories, as a continuing prohibition), 6 (with respect to claims that arose before termination), 8, 9.3, and 10 shall survive termination.
