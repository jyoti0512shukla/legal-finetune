SUB-PROCESSOR DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM (PROCESSOR-TO-PROCESSOR)

This Sub-Processor Data Processing Addendum (this "Sub-Processor DPA") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "DPA Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Sub-Processor"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} (the "Processor"). The Processor acts as a processor for one or more controllers (each a "Controller" and collectively the "Controllers") in connection with the services described in the Agreement, and the Sub-Processor is engaged to assist the Processor in providing those services.

This Sub-Processor DPA forms part of, and is incorporated into, the {{PARENT_AGREEMENT_NAME}} between the Sub-Processor and the Processor dated {{PARENT_AGREEMENT_DATE}} (the "Agreement"). All capitalized terms used but not defined in this Sub-Processor DPA have the meanings given to them in the Agreement, in the EU GDPR, or in Article 1 below.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Applicable Data Protection Law" means the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA / CPRA, and any other privacy law applicable to the Processing under the Agreement.

1.2 "Controller Personal Data" means any Personal Data of a Controller that the Processor receives from the Controller and provides to the Sub-Processor for Processing in connection with the services.

ARTICLE 2 — RELATIONSHIP OF THE PARTIES

2.1 Roles. The Processor is a Processor (with respect to the Controllers' data). The Sub-Processor is a Sub-Processor (within the meaning of Article 28(2) and (4) GDPR), engaged by the Processor on the Controllers' general or specific authorization.

2.2 Processor Documented Instructions. The Sub-Processor shall Process Controller Personal Data only on the Processor's documented instructions, which are: (a) the Agreement, (b) this Sub-Processor DPA, and (c) any further documented instructions from the Processor that are themselves consistent with the Controllers' instructions to the Processor.

2.3 Controller Instructions. The Sub-Processor acknowledges that the Processor's instructions originate from the Controllers and are passed through the Processor. If the Sub-Processor receives an instruction directly from a Controller, the Sub-Processor shall not act on it without first confirming with the Processor.

2.4 Article 28(4) Equivalence. As required by Article 28(4) GDPR, the data protection obligations imposed on the Sub-Processor under this Sub-Processor DPA are equivalent in substance to those imposed on the Processor under the Processor's DPAs with the Controllers.

ARTICLE 3 — SCOPE OF PROCESSING

3.1 Subject Matter and Duration. As set forth in Annex I.

3.2 Nature and Purpose. {{PROCESSING_PURPOSE}}.

3.3 Categories of Personal Data. {{DATA_CATEGORIES}}.

3.4 Categories of Data Subjects. {{DATA_SUBJECT_CATEGORIES}}.

ARTICLE 4 — SUB-PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS

4.1 Compliance. The Sub-Processor shall Process Controller Personal Data in accordance with this Sub-Processor DPA and the Article 28 obligations applicable to a Sub-Processor.

4.2 Personnel Confidentiality. Personnel with access to Controller Personal Data are bound by confidentiality and access is on a need-to-know basis.

4.3 No Independent Use. The Sub-Processor shall not Process Controller Personal Data for the Sub-Processor's own purposes. The Sub-Processor's role is strictly instrumental — Processing only on the Processor's instructions and only to provide the services to the Processor.

4.4 No Sale or Sharing. The Sub-Processor shall not Sell or Share Controller Personal Data within the meaning of any Applicable Data Protection Law.

ARTICLE 5 — TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES

5.1 The Sub-Processor implements the TOMs in Annex II. The TOMs are equivalent in substance to the TOMs the Processor implements under the Processor's DPAs with the Controllers.

5.2 Encryption: TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest.

5.3 Annual SOC 2 Type II audit. Annual ISO 27001 surveillance.

ARTICLE 6 — ONWARD SUB-PROCESSING

6.1 The Sub-Processor shall not engage any further sub-processor (i.e., a sub-sub-processor) without the Processor's prior written consent. The Processor may consult with the Controllers before granting consent.

6.2 The Sub-Processor shall give the Processor at least sixty (60) days' written notice before engaging any new or replacement sub-sub-processor.

6.3 Equivalent Flow-Down. Any sub-sub-processor shall be subject to a written agreement with data protection obligations equivalent to those imposed on the Sub-Processor under this Sub-Processor DPA.

6.4 Primary Liability. The Sub-Processor remains primarily liable to the Processor for any sub-sub-processor's acts and omissions.

ARTICLE 7 — DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

7.1 If the Sub-Processor receives a Data Subject request directly, the Sub-Processor shall promptly forward it to the Processor (within five (5) business days) and shall not respond directly. The Processor shall in turn forward the request to the relevant Controller.

7.2 The Sub-Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to the Processor in responding to Data Subject requests forwarded from the Controllers, on the same terms applicable to the Processor under the Processor's DPAs with the Controllers.

ARTICLE 8 — PERSONAL DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

8.1 The Sub-Processor shall notify the Processor without undue delay and in any event within {{BREACH_NOTIFICATION_HOURS}} hours after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Controller Personal Data. This notification window is materially shorter than the Processor's notification window to the Controllers, in order to give the Processor time to in turn notify the Controllers.

8.2 The notification shall include the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects affected, the categories and approximate number of Personal Data records affected, the contact point at the Sub-Processor, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed.

8.3 Cooperation in investigation, remediation, and the Processor's notification obligations to the Controllers.

ARTICLE 9 — DATA PROTECTION IMPACT ASSESSMENTS

9.1 The Sub-Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to the Processor and (through the Processor) to the Controllers in conducting DPIAs.

ARTICLE 10 — INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

10.1 EU SCCs Module 3 (Processor to Sub-Processor). The Sub-Processor and the Processor incorporate Module 3 of the EU SCCs (Commission Decision 2021/914) for any Restricted Transfer of Controller Personal Data from the Processor to the Sub-Processor, with the parameters set forth in Annex IV.

10.2 UK IDTA. The UK IDTA is incorporated for UK transfers with the same Module 3 parameters.

10.3 The Sub-Processor maintains a current Transfer Impact Assessment.

ARTICLE 11 — AUDIT RIGHTS

11.1 Processor Audit Right. The Processor shall have the right to audit the Sub-Processor's compliance with this Sub-Processor DPA, on at least {{AUDIT_NOTICE_DAYS}} days' notice and no more than once per twelve (12) month period (except after a breach).

11.2 Pass-Through Audit Right. To the extent the Processor's DPAs with the Controllers grant the Controllers an audit right that extends to Sub-Processors, the Sub-Processor shall cooperate with such Controller audits when conducted through the Processor.

11.3 Third-Party Audit Reports. The Sub-Processor shall make available to the Processor (and through the Processor to the Controllers), at no charge, the most recent SOC 2 Type II report and ISO 27001 certificate.

ARTICLE 12 — DELETION OR RETURN OF CONTROLLER PERSONAL DATA

12.1 At the end of the provision of services under the Agreement, the Sub-Processor shall, at the Processor's choice, delete or return all Controller Personal Data and shall delete all existing copies, unless retention is required by law.

12.2 Certification of deletion on request.

12.3 Backup and archival exception per the Sub-Processor's standard retention schedule.

ARTICLE 13 — TERM AND TERMINATION

13.1 Term. This Sub-Processor DPA runs for the term of the Agreement plus the wind-down period.

13.2 Survival. Articles 8, 11, and 12 survive termination.

ARTICLE 14 — INCORPORATION OF THE PARENT AGREEMENT AND ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

14.1 Incorporation. This Sub-Processor DPA is part of the Agreement.

14.2 Order of Precedence. This Sub-Processor DPA controls on personal-data matters; the Agreement controls on commercial matters, IP, liability, governing law, and dispute resolution.

14.3 No Direct Controller Privity. The Sub-Processor's direct contractual relationship is with the Processor, not with the Controllers. The Controllers are intended third-party beneficiaries of certain provisions of this Sub-Processor DPA (specifically the data protection obligations) to the extent expressly provided by Article 28(4) GDPR.

14.4 Counterparts and Execution.
