DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM (PHARMACEUTICAL SPONSOR — CLINICAL TRIAL DATA)

This Data Processing Addendum (this "DPA") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "DPA Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Processor"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} (the "Sponsor" or the "Controller"). This DPA is the Sponsor's standard form of data processing addendum for engagements involving clinical trial Personal Data and shall be governed by the Sponsor's negotiated terms set forth herein.

This DPA forms part of, and is incorporated into, the {{PARENT_AGREEMENT_NAME}} between the Parties dated {{PARENT_AGREEMENT_DATE}} (the "Agreement"). All capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given to them in the Agreement, in the EU GDPR, in 21 C.F.R. Part 11, in HIPAA (where applicable), or in Article 1 below. In the event of any conflict between this DPA and the Agreement, this DPA shall control with respect to the Processing of Personal Data; the Agreement shall control with respect to all other matters not modified by this DPA.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Applicable Privacy Law" means, collectively, the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, HIPAA (where the Sponsor's clinical trial activities involve the use of US PHI), 21 C.F.R. Part 11 (electronic records and signatures), the FDA's guidance on the use of electronic data in clinical investigations, ICH GCP E6(R2), the CCPA / CPRA, and any other privacy law applicable to the Processing under the Agreement.

1.2 "Clinical Trial Data" means Personal Data relating to clinical trial subjects, investigators, study site personnel, and other Data Subjects involved in the Sponsor's clinical trials.

1.3 "Sponsor Personal Data" means any Personal Data Processed by the Processor on behalf of the Sponsor under the Agreement, including Clinical Trial Data.

1.4 "Pseudonymized Data" has the meaning in Article 4(5) of the EU GDPR. Most clinical trial data the Processor handles is pseudonymized; the Sponsor retains the key.

ARTICLE 2 — RELATIONSHIP OF THE PARTIES

2.1 Roles. The Sponsor is the Controller. The Processor is the Processor. Sub-processors are Sub-processors.

2.2 Documented Instructions. The Processor shall Process Sponsor Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Sponsor, set forth in (a) the Agreement, (b) this DPA, (c) the Sponsor's standard operating procedures, and (d) the clinical trial protocol applicable to each study.

2.3 No Secondary Use. The Processor shall not (a) sell or share Sponsor Personal Data, (b) use Sponsor Personal Data for the Processor's own commercial purposes (including product analytics), (c) use Sponsor Personal Data to train AI / ML models, or (d) combine Sponsor Personal Data with personal data from any other source, in each case without the Sponsor's express prior written consent.

2.4 21 C.F.R. Part 11 Compliance. The Processor shall maintain electronic records and electronic signatures in compliance with 21 C.F.R. Part 11. Processor systems used to handle Sponsor Personal Data shall be validated in accordance with the Sponsor's CSV procedures.

ARTICLE 3 — SCOPE OF PROCESSING

3.1 Subject Matter and Duration. The subject matter is the provision of the clinical-trial-related services described in the Agreement. The duration shall be the term of the Agreement plus the records retention period required by 21 C.F.R. § 312.62 (and equivalent foreign regulations).

3.2 Nature and Purpose. The nature and purpose are {{PROCESSING_PURPOSE}}.

3.3 Categories of Personal Data. The categories include {{DATA_CATEGORIES}}.

3.4 Categories of Data Subjects. The categories include {{DATA_SUBJECT_CATEGORIES}}.

3.5 Special Category Data. Clinical Trial Data includes data concerning health within the meaning of Article 9(1) GDPR. The Sponsor represents that it has a valid Article 9 lawful basis for the Processing.

ARTICLE 4 — PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS

4.1 Compliance. The Processor shall Process Sponsor Personal Data in accordance with this DPA, Applicable Privacy Law, ICH GCP E6(R2), and the Sponsor's SOPs.

4.2 Personnel Confidentiality. Personnel with access to Sponsor Personal Data have completed background checks, ICH GCP training, and confidentiality undertakings, and access is limited on a need-to-know basis.

4.3 Validation. The Processor's systems used to process Sponsor Personal Data shall be validated under the Sponsor's CSV process (as set forth in the Agreement) and shall meet 21 C.F.R. Part 11 requirements.

4.4 Cooperation with Regulators. The Processor shall cooperate with the FDA, the EMA, and other regulatory authorities in connection with any inspection or audit relating to the Sponsor's clinical trials.

ARTICLE 5 — TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES

5.1 Implementation. The Processor implements TOMs in Annex II appropriate to the sensitivity of clinical trial data, including 21 C.F.R. Part 11 controls for electronic records and electronic signatures.

5.2 Encryption. TLS 1.2+ in transit. AES-256 at rest. Key separation between the Sponsor's pseudonymized data and any re-identification key (which is held by the Sponsor, not the Processor).

5.3 Audit Trails. The Processor maintains audit trails as required by 21 C.F.R. § 11.10(e), including user, action, and timestamp for any creation, modification, or deletion of Sponsor Personal Data records.

5.4 Annual SOC 2 Type II audit. Annual ISO 27001 certification. Annual third-party penetration testing. Quarterly internal vulnerability scans.

5.5 Documented business continuity and disaster recovery plan with stated RTO/RPO and annual testing.

ARTICLE 6 — SUB-PROCESSORS

6.1 Specific Authorization. The Sponsor's specific written authorization is required for any Sub-processor that Processes Sponsor Personal Data. The currently authorized Sub-processors are listed in Annex III.

6.2 Notice and Consent. The Processor shall give the Sponsor at least sixty (60) days' written notice before engaging any new or replacement Sub-processor and shall obtain the Sponsor's prior written consent.

6.3 Right to Terminate. If the Sponsor withholds consent, the Sponsor may terminate the affected portion of the Agreement on written notice without penalty.

6.4 Sub-processor Obligations. Each Sub-processor is subject to a written agreement with data protection obligations equivalent to those imposed on the Processor under this DPA, including 21 C.F.R. Part 11 compliance for any Sub-processor handling regulated electronic records.

6.5 Primary Liability for Sub-processor Acts and Omissions.

ARTICLE 7 — DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

7.1 The Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to the Sponsor in fulfilling the Sponsor's obligations to respond to Data Subject requests under Applicable Privacy Law, taking into account that re-identification of pseudonymized clinical trial data requires the Sponsor's key (which the Processor does not hold).

7.2 The Processor shall not respond to Data Subjects directly.

ARTICLE 8 — PERSONAL DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

8.1 The Processor shall notify the Sponsor without undue delay and in any event within {{BREACH_NOTIFICATION_HOURS}} hours after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Sponsor Personal Data.

8.2 The notification shall include the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects affected, the categories and approximate number of Personal Data records affected, the contact point at the Processor, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed.

8.3 Cooperation. The Processor shall cooperate with the Sponsor in investigating, remediating, notifying any Supervisory Authority, and notifying any clinical trial site personnel and Data Subjects as required.

8.4 Records of all Personal Data Breaches.

ARTICLE 9 — DATA PROTECTION IMPACT ASSESSMENTS

9.1 The Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to the Sponsor in conducting Data Protection Impact Assessments under Article 35 GDPR. Clinical trial Processing typically requires a DPIA.

9.2 The Processor shall provide reasonable assistance with prior consultations with Supervisory Authorities under Article 36 GDPR.

9.3 The Sponsor is responsible for the DPIA itself.

ARTICLE 10 — INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

10.1 EU SCCs (Commission Decision 2021/914) are incorporated by reference, with Module 2 (Controller to Processor) and Module 3 (Processor to Sub-processor), general sub-processor authorization (Option 2), Irish governing law and venue, and Annexes referencing this DPA's Annexes I, II, and III.

10.2 UK IDTA is incorporated for UK transfers with the same parameters.

10.3 The Processor maintains a current Transfer Impact Assessment.

10.4 Region-specific data residency. Sponsor Personal Data is hosted in: {{DATA_RESIDENCY_REGION}}. The Sponsor may instruct the Processor to use region-locked storage for clinical trial data of subjects from particular countries to comply with country-specific data localization requirements.

ARTICLE 11 — AUDIT RIGHTS

11.1 Sponsor Audit Right. The Sponsor shall have the right to audit the Processor's compliance with this DPA, Applicable Privacy Law, and the Sponsor's SOPs.

11.2 Notice and Frequency. Audits shall be conducted no more than once per twelve (12) month period (except in connection with an incident or a regulatory event). The Sponsor shall provide at least {{AUDIT_NOTICE_DAYS}} days' prior written notice.

11.3 Third-Party Audit Reports. The Processor shall make available to the Sponsor, at no charge, the most recent SOC 2 Type II report, ISO 27001 certificate, and CSV validation summary.

11.4 Regulatory Inspection Cooperation. The Processor shall cooperate with any FDA, EMA, or other regulatory inspection of the Sponsor's clinical trials at no additional charge to the Sponsor.

11.5 Scope Limitations. Audits shall not require disclosure of other customers' data or the Processor's trade secrets, but the Processor shall not assert such limitations to obstruct any regulatory inspection.

ARTICLE 12 — DELETION OR RETURN OF SPONSOR PERSONAL DATA

12.1 End of Provision of Services. Upon the end of the provision of the services, the Processor shall, at the Sponsor's choice, delete or return all Sponsor Personal Data, subject to retention requirements under 21 C.F.R. § 312.62 and equivalent foreign regulations.

12.2 Records Retention. The Processor shall retain Sponsor Personal Data for the period required by Applicable Privacy Law and the Sponsor's records retention SOPs (typically two (2) years following the date of marketing application approval, or two (2) years following the date the Sponsor discontinues development, whichever is later).

12.3 Certification of deletion on request.

ARTICLE 13 — TERM AND TERMINATION

13.1 Term. This DPA runs for the term of the Agreement plus the records retention period in Section 12.2.

13.2 Survival. Articles 8, 11, and 12 (including the records retention requirement) survive termination.

ARTICLE 14 — INCORPORATION OF THE PARENT AGREEMENT AND ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

14.1 Incorporation. This DPA is part of the Agreement.

14.2 Order of Precedence. This DPA controls on personal-data and privacy matters; the Agreement controls on all other matters not modified by this DPA.

14.3 No Limitation of Sponsor Regulatory Obligations. Nothing in this DPA shall limit the Sponsor's obligations or rights under Applicable Privacy Law or applicable clinical trial regulations.

14.4 Counterparts and Execution.
