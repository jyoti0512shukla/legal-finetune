DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM (EU GDPR + UK GDPR)

This Data Processing Addendum (this "DPA") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "DPA Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Processor"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} (the "Controller"). The Controller and the Processor are collectively the "Parties."

This DPA forms part of, and is incorporated into, the {{PARENT_AGREEMENT_NAME}} between the Parties dated {{PARENT_AGREEMENT_DATE}} (the "Agreement"). All capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given to them in the Agreement or in Article 1 below. In the event of any conflict between this DPA and the Agreement, this DPA shall control with respect to the Processing of Personal Data; the Agreement shall control with respect to all other matters (including without limitation commercial terms, intellectual property, limitation of liability, indemnification, governing law, and dispute resolution, which are not modified by this DPA).

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Applicable Data Protection Law" means, collectively, (a) the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) (the "EU GDPR"), (b) the United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation as it forms part of the law of England and Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland by virtue of section 3 of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 and as further amended (the "UK GDPR"), (c) the Data Protection Act 2018 (United Kingdom), and (d) any successor or implementing legislation, in each case as amended from time to time and applicable to the Processing of Controller Personal Data under the Agreement.

1.2 "Controller Personal Data" means any Personal Data Processed by the Processor on behalf of the Controller under the Agreement, including the categories of Personal Data and Data Subjects identified in Annex I.

1.3 "Data Subject," "Personal Data," "Personal Data Breach," "Processing," "Processor," "Sub-processor," "Supervisory Authority," and "Controller" each have the meanings given to those terms in Article 4 of the EU GDPR (and the corresponding provisions of the UK GDPR).

1.4 "EU SCCs" means the standard contractual clauses approved by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021, as amended from time to time.

1.5 "Restricted Transfer" means a transfer of Controller Personal Data from a country whose laws provide an adequate level of protection (within the meaning of Chapter V of the EU GDPR or the UK GDPR) to a country whose laws do not, where such transfer would be unlawful in the absence of a transfer mechanism set forth in Chapter V.

1.6 "UK Addendum" means the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU SCCs issued by the United Kingdom Information Commissioner's Office under section 119A of the UK Data Protection Act 2018, version B1.0, as amended from time to time.

ARTICLE 2 — RELATIONSHIP OF THE PARTIES

2.1 Roles. The Parties acknowledge and agree that, with respect to the Processing of Controller Personal Data under the Agreement: (a) the Controller is the Controller; (b) the Processor is the Processor; and (c) any Sub-processor engaged by the Processor in accordance with Article 6 of this DPA is a Sub-processor.

2.2 Controller's Documented Instructions. The Controller's documented instructions to the Processor with respect to the Processing of Controller Personal Data are set forth in (a) the Agreement, (b) this DPA, and (c) any further documented instructions provided by the Controller from time to time consistent with the foregoing. The Processor shall Process Controller Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller, unless required to do otherwise by Applicable Data Protection Law to which the Processor is subject (in which case the Processor shall inform the Controller of that legal requirement before Processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest).

2.3 Controller's Lawful Basis. The Controller represents and warrants that (a) the Controller has obtained all necessary consents, provided all necessary notices, and otherwise has a lawful basis under Applicable Data Protection Law for the Processor to Process Controller Personal Data on the Controller's behalf in accordance with the Agreement and this DPA; and (b) the Controller's documented instructions comply with Applicable Data Protection Law.

2.4 Notification of Conflicting Instructions. The Processor shall promptly notify the Controller in writing if, in the Processor's reasonable opinion, any Controller instruction would violate Applicable Data Protection Law, in which case the Processor shall not be obligated to perform the instruction until the Controller has confirmed or modified the instruction.

ARTICLE 3 — SCOPE OF PROCESSING

3.1 Subject Matter and Duration. The subject matter and duration of the Processing under this DPA are as set forth in Annex I and shall continue for the term of the Agreement, plus any wind-down period required for the Processor to delete or return Controller Personal Data in accordance with Article 12.

3.2 Nature and Purpose of Processing. The nature and purpose of the Processing under this DPA are {{PROCESSING_PURPOSE}}, as more fully described in Annex I.

3.3 Categories of Personal Data and Data Subjects. The Categories of Personal Data Processed under this DPA include {{DATA_CATEGORIES}}, and the Categories of Data Subjects include {{DATA_SUBJECT_CATEGORIES}}, in each case as more fully described in Annex I.

3.4 Updates to Annex I. The Parties may from time to time update Annex I to reflect changes in the Processing arrangement, by mutual written agreement.

ARTICLE 4 — PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS

4.1 Compliance. The Processor shall Process Controller Personal Data in accordance with this DPA and Applicable Data Protection Law applicable to the Processor's role as a Processor.

4.2 Personnel Confidentiality. The Processor shall ensure that personnel authorized to Process Controller Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality (or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality) and shall limit access to Controller Personal Data to those personnel who require such access for the purpose of providing the services under the Agreement.

4.3 Cooperation with Supervisory Authorities. The Processor shall, to the extent required by Applicable Data Protection Law, cooperate on request with any Supervisory Authority in the performance of its tasks.

4.4 No Sale or Sharing. The Processor shall not (a) sell Controller Personal Data within the meaning of any Applicable Data Protection Law, (b) share Controller Personal Data with any third party for any purpose other than as expressly permitted by the Controller and this DPA, or (c) use Controller Personal Data for the Processor's own commercial purposes.

ARTICLE 5 — TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES

5.1 Implementation. Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of Processing, as well as the risk of varying likelihood and severity for the rights and freedoms of natural persons, the Processor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures (the "Technical and Organizational Measures" or "TOMs") to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk. The TOMs are set forth in Annex II.

5.2 Encryption. The Processor shall apply encryption to Controller Personal Data both in transit (using industry-standard transport layer security) and at rest (using industry-standard encryption such as AES-256), as further described in Annex II.

5.3 Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, and Resilience. The Processor shall ensure the ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of the Processing systems and services used to Process Controller Personal Data, including through redundancy, backup, and incident management procedures.

5.4 Restoration After Incident. The Processor shall maintain the ability to restore the availability of and access to Controller Personal Data in a timely manner in the event of a physical or technical incident, in accordance with the disaster recovery objectives set forth in Annex II.

5.5 Regular Testing. The Processor shall regularly test, assess, and evaluate the effectiveness of the TOMs through internal audits, penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, and (where applicable) third-party assessments such as SOC 2 Type II audits. The Processor shall maintain documentation of such testing and shall make summary results available to the Controller upon reasonable request.

5.6 Updates to Annex II. The Processor may from time to time update Annex II to reflect improvements in the TOMs, provided that no such update shall reduce the overall level of security below the level provided as of the DPA Effective Date.

ARTICLE 6 — SUB-PROCESSORS

6.1 General Authorization. The Controller hereby grants the Processor a general authorization to engage Sub-processors to Process Controller Personal Data, subject to the requirements of this Article 6.

6.2 List of Sub-processors. The Processor shall maintain a current list of its Sub-processors (the "Sub-processors List"), the initial version of which is set forth in Annex III. The Sub-processors List shall identify each Sub-processor by name, the country in which the Sub-processor is established, and a description of the Processing performed by the Sub-processor.

6.3 Notification of Changes. The Processor shall notify the Controller in writing of any intended addition or replacement of a Sub-processor at least thirty (30) days before the change takes effect, by updating the Sub-processors List and notifying the Controller (which notification may be made by email to the Controller's designated DPA contact, by an in-product notification, or by an updated trust center page).

6.4 Right to Object. The Controller may object to the addition or replacement of a Sub-processor on reasonable data protection grounds within thirty (30) days following receipt of the notification under Section 6.3. If the Controller objects, the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the objection, including by the Processor proposing an alternative arrangement. If the Parties cannot resolve the objection within thirty (30) days, the Controller may terminate the affected portion of the Agreement on written notice to the Processor without penalty, in accordance with the termination provisions of the Agreement.

6.5 Sub-processor Obligations. The Processor shall enter into a written agreement with each Sub-processor that imposes data protection obligations on the Sub-processor that are equivalent in substance to those imposed on the Processor under this DPA. The Processor shall, on the Controller's request, make a redacted copy of the relevant Sub-processor agreement available to the Controller, subject to reasonable confidentiality and trade-secret protections.

6.6 Primary Liability. The Processor shall remain primarily liable to the Controller for the acts and omissions of its Sub-processors as if such acts and omissions were the acts and omissions of the Processor itself.

ARTICLE 7 — DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

7.1 Assistance to Controller. Taking into account the nature of the Processing, the Processor shall assist the Controller by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as possible, in fulfilling the Controller's obligation to respond to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights under Applicable Data Protection Law, including the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of Processing, data portability, and objection.

7.2 Forwarding of Requests. If the Processor receives a request from a Data Subject directly, the Processor shall, without undue delay and in any event within five (5) business days, forward the request to the Controller and shall not respond to the Data Subject directly other than to acknowledge receipt and direct the Data Subject to the Controller, except as expressly authorized by the Controller in writing.

7.3 No Direct Response. The Processor shall not respond to or fulfill any Data Subject request without the Controller's prior written authorization, except as required by Applicable Data Protection Law (in which case the Processor shall notify the Controller before responding, unless that law prohibits such notification on important grounds of public interest).

ARTICLE 8 — PERSONAL DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

8.1 Notification Obligation. The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within {{BREACH_NOTIFICATION_HOURS}} hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Controller Personal Data.

8.2 Content of Notification. The Processor's notification shall describe (a) the nature of the Personal Data Breach, including, where possible, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects concerned and the categories and approximate number of Personal Data records concerned; (b) the name and contact details of the Processor's Data Protection Officer or other contact point where more information can be obtained; (c) the likely consequences of the Personal Data Breach; and (d) the measures taken or proposed to be taken by the Processor to address the Personal Data Breach, including, where appropriate, measures to mitigate its possible adverse effects.

8.3 Cooperation. The Processor shall cooperate with the Controller and provide reasonable assistance to the Controller in (a) investigating the Personal Data Breach, (b) remediating the Personal Data Breach, (c) notifying any Supervisory Authority that the Controller is required to notify under Applicable Data Protection Law, and (d) communicating with affected Data Subjects where the Controller is required to do so under Applicable Data Protection Law.

8.4 Records. The Processor shall maintain a record of all Personal Data Breaches affecting Controller Personal Data, including the facts relating to the Personal Data Breach, its effects, and the remedial action taken.

ARTICLE 9 — DATA PROTECTION IMPACT ASSESSMENTS

9.1 Assistance with DPIAs. Taking into account the nature of the Processing and the information available to the Processor, the Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to the Controller in carrying out Data Protection Impact Assessments under Article 35 of the EU GDPR (or the equivalent provision of any other Applicable Data Protection Law).

9.2 Prior Consultation. The Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to the Controller in connection with any prior consultation with a Supervisory Authority under Article 36 of the EU GDPR (or the equivalent provision of any other Applicable Data Protection Law).

9.3 Scope of Assistance. The Processor's assistance under this Article 9 shall include providing documentation, information, and (where reasonably requested) participation in meetings, in each case to the extent necessary for the Controller to fulfill its obligations under Articles 35 and 36 of the EU GDPR. The Controller is responsible for the DPIA itself; the Processor's role is supportive.

ARTICLE 10 — INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

10.1 Restricted Transfers. The Processor shall not engage in any Restricted Transfer of Controller Personal Data without first ensuring that an appropriate transfer mechanism under Chapter V of the EU GDPR (or the equivalent provision of any other Applicable Data Protection Law) is in place. Permitted transfer mechanisms include (a) an adequacy decision, (b) the EU SCCs, (c) the UK Addendum, and (d) such other mechanisms as the Parties may from time to time agree in writing.

10.2 Incorporation of EU SCCs. Where Controller Personal Data is transferred from the European Economic Area to a country that is not the subject of an adequacy decision, the EU SCCs shall apply and are hereby incorporated into this DPA by reference, on the following terms: (a) Module Two (Controller to Processor) shall apply where the Controller is the data exporter and the Processor is the data importer; (b) Module Three (Processor to Sub-processor) shall apply where the Processor is the data exporter and the Sub-processor is the data importer; (c) the optional docking clause (Clause 7) shall apply; (d) Clause 9 (Use of Sub-processors) shall use Option 2 (general authorization) with a 30-day notice period; (e) Clause 11 (Redress) shall not apply to independent dispute resolution body referrals; (f) Clause 17 (Governing Law) shall be the law of Ireland; (g) Clause 18 (Choice of Forum and Jurisdiction) shall be the courts of Ireland; (h) Annexes I, II, and III to the EU SCCs shall be the Annexes I, II, and III of this DPA.

10.3 Incorporation of UK Addendum. Where Controller Personal Data is transferred from the United Kingdom to a country that is not the subject of a UK adequacy regulation, the UK Addendum shall apply and is hereby incorporated into this DPA by reference, with the following completed information: (a) Table 1 (Parties) shall be the Controller and the Processor as set forth in this DPA; (b) Table 2 (Selected SCCs, Modules and Selected Clauses) shall reflect the EU SCCs as incorporated under Section 10.2; (c) Table 3 (Appendix Information) shall reflect Annexes I, II, and III of this DPA; (d) Table 4 (Ending the Addendum when the Approved Addendum Changes) shall be "Importer," meaning the Processor.

10.4 Transfer Impact Assessment. The Processor has conducted, and shall maintain on a current basis, a Transfer Impact Assessment evaluating the risk to Controller Personal Data of any Restricted Transfer in light of the laws of the destination country and the supplementary measures implemented by the Processor. The Processor shall make a summary of the Transfer Impact Assessment available to the Controller on reasonable request.

10.5 Supplementary Measures. The Processor shall implement appropriate supplementary measures to address any risks identified by the Transfer Impact Assessment, including without limitation encryption, pseudonymization, and contractual restrictions on government access requests.

10.6 Government Access Requests. If the Processor receives a binding legally enforceable request from a public authority in a third country to disclose Controller Personal Data, the Processor shall (a) promptly notify the Controller of the request unless legally prohibited from doing so, (b) challenge the request where the Processor reasonably believes the request is unlawful or overbroad, and (c) provide the minimum amount of Controller Personal Data necessary to comply with any request that the Processor is legally required to comply with.

ARTICLE 11 — AUDIT RIGHTS

11.1 Controller Audit Right. The Controller shall have the right to audit the Processor's compliance with this DPA and Applicable Data Protection Law in respect of the Processing of Controller Personal Data, in accordance with this Article 11.

11.2 Notice and Frequency. Audits under this Article 11 shall be conducted no more than once per twelve (12) month period, except where a Personal Data Breach or a documented Supervisory Authority instruction reasonably necessitates an additional audit. The Controller shall provide the Processor with at least {{AUDIT_NOTICE_DAYS}} days' prior written notice of any audit, except where an emergency audit is required in connection with a Personal Data Breach.

11.3 Third-Party Audit Reports. The Processor shall make available to the Controller, on request and subject to reasonable confidentiality protections, the most recent SOC 2 Type II audit report and ISO 27001 certificate covering the Processor's services. The Controller agrees that, where the Processor's third-party audit reports adequately address the scope of a proposed audit, the Controller will accept those reports in lieu of an on-site audit.

11.4 Scope Limitations. Audits under this Article 11 shall (a) be conducted during the Processor's normal business hours, (b) be conducted in a manner that does not unreasonably interfere with the Processor's business operations, (c) not require disclosure of any information that would compromise the confidentiality of other customers' data, (d) not require disclosure of any of the Processor's trade secrets, and (e) be subject to confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in the Agreement.

11.5 Cost. The Processor shall make its third-party audit reports available at no charge. On-site audits requested by the Controller beyond the third-party reports shall be conducted at the Controller's expense, except where the audit reveals material non-compliance with this DPA, in which case the Processor shall reimburse the Controller's reasonable audit costs.

ARTICLE 12 — DELETION OR RETURN OF PERSONAL DATA

12.1 End of Provision of Services. Upon the end of the provision of the services under the Agreement (whether by expiration, termination, or otherwise), the Processor shall, at the Controller's choice, delete or return all Controller Personal Data to the Controller, and shall delete all existing copies thereof, unless retention is required by Applicable Data Protection Law to which the Processor is subject.

12.2 Certification. The Processor shall, on the Controller's written request, certify in writing that it has deleted Controller Personal Data in accordance with this Article 12.

12.3 Backup and Archival Exception. The Processor may retain Controller Personal Data in backup or archival copies in accordance with the Processor's documented backup and retention schedule, provided that (a) any retained copies remain subject to the confidentiality and security obligations of this DPA for as long as they are retained, and (b) the Processor shall not actively Process such retained copies for any purpose other than complying with applicable retention requirements.

12.4 Wind-Down Period. The Processor shall complete the deletion or return of Controller Personal Data within thirty (30) days following the end of the provision of the services, except for backup or archival copies retained under Section 12.3.

ARTICLE 13 — TERM AND TERMINATION

13.1 Term. This DPA shall commence on the DPA Effective Date and shall continue for the term of the Agreement, plus any wind-down period required for the deletion or return of Controller Personal Data under Article 12.

13.2 Survival. Articles 8 (Personal Data Breach Notification — for breaches occurring during the term), 11 (Audit Rights — to the extent of audits commenced during or shortly after termination), and 12 (Deletion or Return) shall survive termination of this DPA.

ARTICLE 14 — INCORPORATION OF THE PARENT AGREEMENT AND ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

14.1 Incorporation. This DPA is incorporated into and forms part of the Agreement. All terms of the Agreement that are not modified or superseded by this DPA continue in full force and effect.

14.2 Order of Precedence. In the event of any conflict between this DPA and the Agreement, this DPA shall control with respect to the Processing of Personal Data and the rights and obligations of the Parties under Applicable Data Protection Law. The Agreement shall control with respect to all other matters, including without limitation commercial terms, intellectual property, limitation of liability, indemnification, governing law, and dispute resolution. For the avoidance of doubt, this DPA does not modify the limitation of liability provisions of the Agreement.

14.3 Parties Bound. This DPA binds the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Each Affiliate of the Controller that has executed a separate order form under the Agreement shall be deemed a Controller under this DPA with respect to its own Personal Data.

14.4 Counterparts and Execution. This DPA may be executed in counterparts. The Parties acknowledge that execution of the Agreement, on or after the DPA Effective Date, shall constitute execution of this DPA.

2. Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, and Resilience. Multi-factor authentication for personnel access to systems Processing Controller Personal Data.
