Joint Controller A and Joint Controller B are collectively the "Joint Controllers." This Arrangement is the joint controller arrangement required by Article 26 of the EU GDPR for the data sharing program described herein.

This Arrangement forms part of, and is incorporated into, the {{PARENT_AGREEMENT_NAME}} between the Parties dated {{PARENT_AGREEMENT_DATE}} (the "Agreement"). Where this Arrangement differs from a typical processor-controller DPA, it reflects the joint controller relationship under Article 26 GDPR.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Applicable Data Protection Law" means the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA / CPRA, and any other privacy law applicable to the Processing under the Agreement.

1.2 "Joint Controller" has the meaning in Article 26(1) GDPR.

1.3 "Shared Personal Data" means the Personal Data that the Joint Controllers jointly determine the purposes and means of Processing.

ARTICLE 2 — RELATIONSHIP OF THE PARTIES (JOINT CONTROLLERS)

2.1 Joint Controller Designation. The Parties acknowledge and agree that, with respect to the Processing of Shared Personal Data under the Agreement, both Joint Controller A and Joint Controller B are joint controllers within the meaning of Article 26 GDPR. The Parties jointly determine the purposes and means of the Processing.

2.2 Allocation of Responsibilities. The respective responsibilities of the Joint Controllers are allocated as follows:

(a) Joint Controller A is responsible for: (i) operating the data sharing infrastructure, (ii) maintaining the technical and organizational measures applicable to the data sharing infrastructure, (iii) responding to data subject access requests received via the data sharing infrastructure (with cooperation from Joint Controller B as set forth in Section 2.4), and (iv) maintaining the records of Processing required by Article 30 GDPR for activities under Joint Controller A's control.

(b) Joint Controller B is responsible for: (i) collecting the Shared Personal Data from data subjects in compliance with Articles 12-14 GDPR (transparency / fair processing notices), (ii) ensuring a valid lawful basis under Article 6 (and Article 9 where applicable) for the Processing, (iii) responding to data subject requests received directly by Joint Controller B (with cooperation from Joint Controller A), and (iv) maintaining the Article 30 records for activities under Joint Controller B's control.

(c) Both Joint Controllers are jointly responsible for: (i) responding to inquiries from Supervisory Authorities, (ii) responding to Personal Data Breaches affecting Shared Personal Data, and (iii) maintaining the Joint Controller Arrangement documentation.

2.3 Article 26(2) Essence. The essence of this Arrangement shall be made available to data subjects through both Joint Controllers' privacy notices, in accordance with Article 26(2) GDPR.

2.4 Single Point of Contact. Joint Controller A shall be the single point of contact for data subjects exercising their rights under Articles 15-22 GDPR with respect to Shared Personal Data, except that data subjects may exercise their rights against either Joint Controller per Article 26(3) GDPR. Joint Controller A and Joint Controller B shall cooperate on responses.

ARTICLE 4 — JOINT CONTROLLER OBLIGATIONS

4.1 Compliance. Each Joint Controller shall comply with the obligations imposed on Controllers under Applicable Data Protection Law, including the obligations of Article 5 (data protection principles), Article 24 (responsibility), Article 30 (records of Processing), and Article 32 (security of Processing).

4.2 Personnel Confidentiality. Each Joint Controller shall ensure that personnel authorized to access Shared Personal Data are bound by confidentiality and access is on a need-to-know basis.

4.3 No Sale or Sharing Beyond Purpose. Neither Joint Controller will sell, share, or disclose Shared Personal Data outside of the agreed purposes set forth in Annex I.

4.4 No AI Training. Neither Joint Controller will use Shared Personal Data to train AI / ML models without the other Joint Controller's express prior written consent.

ARTICLE 5 — TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES

5.1 Each Joint Controller implements TOMs appropriate to the risk of the Processing under its control, as set forth in Annex II.

5.2 Encryption: TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest.

5.3 Annual SOC 2 Type II audit (each Joint Controller, for the systems it operates).

5.4 Documented BCDR plans for each Joint Controller's systems.

ARTICLE 6 — SUB-PROCESSORS (USED BY EITHER JOINT CONTROLLER)

6.1 Each Joint Controller may engage Processors and Sub-processors for activities under its control, subject to the requirements of Articles 28 and 29 GDPR.

6.2 Each Joint Controller shall maintain a list of its Processors and Sub-processors and shall make the list available to the other Joint Controller on request.

6.3 Notification of Material Changes. Each Joint Controller shall notify the other Joint Controller of any material change to its Processor or Sub-processor arrangements that would affect the Shared Personal Data.

6.4 Equivalent Flow-Down. Each Joint Controller shall ensure that its Processors and Sub-processors are subject to data protection obligations equivalent to those imposed on Processors and Sub-processors under Article 28(3) GDPR.

ARTICLE 7 — DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

7.1 Single Point of Contact. Joint Controller A is the single point of contact for data subject requests under Articles 15-22 GDPR with respect to Shared Personal Data, per Section 2.4.

7.2 Either Joint Controller May Be Approached. Notwithstanding Section 7.1, data subjects may exercise their rights against either Joint Controller per Article 26(3) GDPR.

7.3 Cooperation. The Joint Controllers shall cooperate to ensure that all data subject requests are responded to within the timeframes required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

ARTICLE 8 — PERSONAL DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

8.1 Each Joint Controller shall notify the other Joint Controller within {{BREACH_NOTIFICATION_HOURS}} hours after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Shared Personal Data.

8.2 The Joint Controllers shall jointly determine which Joint Controller is responsible for notifying any Supervisory Authority and any affected data subjects, in accordance with Articles 33 and 34 GDPR.

8.3 Cooperation in investigation, remediation, and notification.

8.4 Each Joint Controller maintains records of Personal Data Breaches affecting Shared Personal Data.

ARTICLE 9 — DATA PROTECTION IMPACT ASSESSMENTS

9.1 The Joint Controllers shall jointly conduct any DPIA required under Article 35 GDPR for the Processing of Shared Personal Data.

9.2 The Joint Controllers shall jointly engage in any prior consultation with Supervisory Authorities under Article 36 GDPR.

ARTICLE 10 — INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

10.1 Each Joint Controller shall ensure that any Restricted Transfer of Shared Personal Data is subject to an appropriate transfer mechanism under Chapter V of the EU GDPR (or the equivalent provisions of the UK GDPR).

10.2 Where Joint Controller A acts as data exporter to Joint Controller B (or vice versa), the EU SCCs (Module 1: Controller to Controller) shall apply if the data importer is in a country without adequacy.

10.3 Each Joint Controller maintains a current Transfer Impact Assessment for any Restricted Transfers it makes.

ARTICLE 11 — AUDIT RIGHTS

11.1 Each Joint Controller may audit the other Joint Controller's compliance with this Arrangement, on at least {{AUDIT_NOTICE_DAYS}} days' notice and no more than once per twelve (12) month period.

11.2 Annual SOC 2 Type II reports of each Joint Controller's systems are made available at no charge.

11.3 Audits shall not require disclosure of confidential information beyond what is necessary to verify compliance with this Arrangement.

ARTICLE 12 — DELETION OR RETURN OF SHARED PERSONAL DATA

12.1 At the end of the data sharing program (whether by termination of the Agreement or otherwise), each Joint Controller shall delete or return all Shared Personal Data in its control, in accordance with Annex I and applicable retention requirements.

12.2 Each Joint Controller shall certify the deletion in writing on the other Joint Controller's request.

ARTICLE 13 — TERM AND TERMINATION

13.1 Term. This Arrangement runs for the term of the Agreement plus the wind-down period.

13.2 Survival. Articles 8, 11, and 12 survive termination.

ARTICLE 14 — INCORPORATION OF THE PARENT AGREEMENT AND ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

14.1 Incorporation. This Arrangement is part of the Agreement.

14.2 Order of Precedence. This Arrangement controls on personal-data matters; the Agreement controls on commercial matters not modified by this Arrangement.

14.3 Counterparts and Execution.
