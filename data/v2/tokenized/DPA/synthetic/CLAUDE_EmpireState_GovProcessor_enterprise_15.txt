DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM (STATE GOVERNMENT — RESIDENT DATA)

This Data Processing Addendum (this "DPA") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "DPA Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Contractor" or "Processor"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "State Agency" or "Controller"). This DPA is the State Agency's standard form of data processing addendum for vendor engagements involving resident data and shall be governed by the State Agency's negotiated terms set forth herein.

This DPA forms part of, and is incorporated into, the {{PARENT_AGREEMENT_NAME}} between the Parties dated {{PARENT_AGREEMENT_DATE}} (the "Agreement"). All capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given to them in the Agreement, in applicable state law, or in Article 1 below. In the event of any conflict between this DPA and the Agreement, this DPA shall control with respect to the Processing of Personal Data; the Agreement shall control with respect to all other matters not modified by this DPA.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Applicable Privacy Law" means, collectively, the State Agency's authorizing statutes, the State Public Records Law (FOIL), the State Personal Privacy Protection Law, any applicable state breach notification statute, the State Information Security Breach and Notification Act, and any other state or federal law applicable to the Processing under the Agreement.

1.2 "Resident Personal Data" means any Personal Data of state residents Processed by the Contractor on behalf of the State Agency, including names, addresses, government identification numbers, eligibility records, benefit records, and any other personal information about residents.

ARTICLE 2 — RELATIONSHIP OF THE PARTIES

2.1 Roles. The State Agency is the Controller. The Contractor is the Processor. Sub-processors are Sub-processors.

2.2 Documented Instructions. The Contractor shall Process Resident Personal Data only on documented instructions from the State Agency.

2.3 No Secondary Use. The Contractor shall not (a) sell, share, or disclose Resident Personal Data to any third party, (b) use Resident Personal Data for the Contractor's own commercial purposes, (c) use Resident Personal Data to train AI / ML models, or (d) combine Resident Personal Data with personal data from any other source.

2.4 No Third-Party Marketing. The Contractor shall not use Resident Personal Data for any marketing or commercial purpose, including marketing of the Contractor's own services to state residents.

ARTICLE 3 — SCOPE OF PROCESSING

3.1 Subject Matter and Duration. The subject matter is the provision of the services described in the Agreement. The duration is the term of the Agreement plus the records retention period required by State law.

3.2 Nature and Purpose. {{PROCESSING_PURPOSE}}.

3.3 Categories of Personal Data. {{DATA_CATEGORIES}}.

3.4 Categories of Data Subjects. {{DATA_SUBJECT_CATEGORIES}}.

ARTICLE 4 — CONTRACTOR OBLIGATIONS

4.1 Compliance. The Contractor shall Process Resident Personal Data in accordance with this DPA and Applicable Privacy Law.

4.2 Personnel. Personnel with access to Resident Personal Data have completed background checks (including state-required checks for personnel handling resident data, where applicable), confidentiality undertakings, and annual data protection training.

4.3 US Person Personnel. All Contractor personnel with access to Resident Personal Data shall be physically located in the United States and shall be U.S. persons (within the meaning of relevant federal export control law and the State Agency's vendor security policy).

4.4 Cooperation with State Agencies and Regulators. The Contractor shall cooperate with the State Agency, the State Comptroller, the State Attorney General, the State Inspector General, and any other state oversight body in connection with any audit, examination, or investigation relating to the services.

ARTICLE 5 — TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES

5.1 Information Security Program. The Contractor shall maintain a comprehensive written information security program meeting the State Agency's security policy requirements (Annex II).

5.2 Encryption. TLS 1.2+ in transit. AES-256 at rest. Encryption keys managed under documented key management procedures.

5.3 Annual SOC 2 Type II audit. Annual ISO 27001 certification. Annual third-party penetration testing.

5.4 Documented business continuity and disaster recovery plan with stated RTO/RPO and annual testing. The plan shall include failover capabilities sufficient to support the State Agency's continuity-of-operations requirements.

ARTICLE 6 — SUB-PROCESSORS

6.1 Specific Authorization Required. The State Agency's specific written authorization is required for any Sub-processor that Processes Resident Personal Data.

6.2 Notice and Consent. The Contractor shall give the State Agency at least sixty (60) days' written notice before engaging any new or replacement Sub-processor.

6.3 Sub-processor Obligations. Each Sub-processor shall be subject to a written agreement with data protection obligations equivalent to those imposed on the Contractor under this DPA.

6.4 Primary Liability for Sub-processor Acts and Omissions.

6.5 MWBE Sub-processors. The Contractor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to engage Minority and Women-Owned Business Enterprises (MWBEs) as Sub-processors, consistent with the State Agency's MWBE participation goals.

ARTICLE 7 — DATA SUBJECT / RESIDENT RIGHTS

7.1 Public Records Requests. The Contractor acknowledges that documents shared between the Parties may be subject to disclosure under the State Public Records Law (FOIL). The Contractor shall mark documents containing trade secrets or confidential commercial information accordingly so the State Agency may invoke applicable exemptions, but the State Agency is not obligated to withhold any document that the Public Records Law requires to be disclosed.

7.2 The Contractor shall provide reasonable assistance to the State Agency in responding to resident inquiries about the State Agency's Processing of their Personal Data.

7.3 The Contractor shall not respond to residents directly other than to forward inquiries to the State Agency.

ARTICLE 8 — INCIDENT NOTIFICATION

8.1 The Contractor shall notify the State Agency within {{BREACH_NOTIFICATION_HOURS}} hours after discovering any unauthorized access to, use of, or disclosure of Resident Personal Data.

8.2 The notification shall include the categories and approximate number of state residents affected, the categories and approximate number of Personal Data records affected, the contact point at the Contractor, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed.

8.3 Cooperation. The Contractor shall cooperate with the State Agency in investigating, remediating, and complying with the State Agency's obligations under the State Information Security Breach and Notification Act, including notifying affected residents and the State Attorney General.

8.4 Records of all incidents.

ARTICLE 9 — DATA PROTECTION IMPACT ASSESSMENTS / PRIVACY ASSESSMENTS

9.1 The Contractor shall provide reasonable assistance to the State Agency in conducting privacy impact assessments under the State Personal Privacy Protection Law and any other applicable state law.

9.2 The State Agency is responsible for the privacy impact assessment itself.

ARTICLE 10 — DATA LOCATION

10.1 In-State or US Data Residency. Resident Personal Data shall be Processed and stored exclusively in the United States and, where required by State law or by the State Agency's data residency policy, exclusively in the State of {{CUSTOMER_STATE}}.

10.2 Data Residency Designation. Resident Personal Data is hosted in: {{DATA_RESIDENCY_REGION}}.

10.3 No International Transfers Without Consent.

ARTICLE 11 — AUDIT RIGHTS AND STATE OVERSIGHT

11.1 State Agency Audit Right. The State Agency shall have the right to audit the Contractor's compliance with this DPA, on at least {{AUDIT_NOTICE_DAYS}} days' notice and no more than once per twelve (12) month period (except in connection with an incident or a state oversight directive).

11.2 State Comptroller and Attorney General Audit. The State Comptroller and the State Attorney General shall have audit rights as required by state law. The Contractor shall cooperate fully at no additional charge.

11.3 Third-Party Audit Reports. The Contractor shall make available, at no charge, the most recent SOC 2 Type II report, ISO 27001 certificate, and any state-mandated security assessments.

11.4 Scope Limitations. Audits shall not require disclosure of other customers' data or the Contractor's trade secrets, but the Contractor shall not assert such limitations to obstruct any state oversight examination.

ARTICLE 12 — DELETION OR RETURN OF RESIDENT PERSONAL DATA

12.1 End of Provision of Services. Upon the end of the provision of the services, the Contractor shall, at the State Agency's choice, delete or return all Resident Personal Data, subject to retention requirements under State records retention statutes.

12.2 Records Retention. The Contractor shall retain Resident Personal Data for the period required by the State Agency's records retention schedule.

12.3 Certification of deletion on request.

12.4 Wind-Down Period. Deletion or return is completed within thirty (30) days following the end of services or the expiration of the records retention period.

ARTICLE 13 — TERM AND TERMINATION

13.1 Term. This DPA runs for the term of the Agreement plus the records retention period.

13.2 Survival. Articles 8, 11, and 12 survive termination.

13.3 Funding-Out. The State Agency may terminate this DPA and the Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice for non-appropriation of state funds, without further liability.

ARTICLE 14 — INCORPORATION OF THE PARENT AGREEMENT AND ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

14.1 Incorporation. This DPA is part of the Agreement.

14.2 Order of Precedence. This DPA controls on personal-data matters; the Agreement controls on all other matters not modified by this DPA.

14.3 Sovereign Immunity. Nothing in this DPA shall be construed as a waiver of the State Agency's sovereign immunity or any defenses available to the State Agency under state law.

14.4 No Indemnification of Contractor by State Agency Beyond State Law. The State Agency may not indemnify the Contractor or any third party except to the extent expressly permitted by state law.

14.5 Counterparts allowed.
