ANNEX IV — PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS DETAIL

A. General obligations. Process only on instructions; personnel confidentiality; no sale or sharing under CCPA / CPRA; no use for the Processor's marketing; no use for AI training without consent.

B. Sub-processor requirements. General authorization. 30-day written notice for additions or replacements. Reasonable objection rights within 30 days. Equivalent flow-down. Primary liability for sub-processor acts and omissions.

C. Data subject rights. Self-service tools in the platform for retrieval and deletion. Forwarding of direct requests within 5 business days. No direct response without Controller authorization.

D. Personal data breach notification. Within {{BREACH_NOTIFICATION_HOURS}} hours. Content includes nature, categories, approximate numbers, contact point, likely consequences, measures taken or proposed. Cooperation with investigation, remediation, and notification of any Supervisory Authority and affected Data Subjects.

E. DPIA cooperation. Reasonable assistance with DPIAs under Article 35 GDPR and prior consultations under Article 36 GDPR. Documentation, attendance at meetings, answering reasonable questions.

F. Audit rights. {{AUDIT_NOTICE_DAYS}} days' notice. No more than once per twelve (12) month period (except after a breach). Annual SOC 2 Type II report and PCI DSS AOC available at no charge. Audits won't expose other customers' data or trade secrets. Audit costs beyond the third-party reports at the Controller's expense unless material non-compliance.

G. Deletion or return. At the end of the services, delete or return at the Controller's choice. Certification on request. Backup or archival copies retained per the Processor's retention schedule remain subject to this DPA's confidentiality and security obligations. PCI DSS-mandated retention period applies to cardholder data.
