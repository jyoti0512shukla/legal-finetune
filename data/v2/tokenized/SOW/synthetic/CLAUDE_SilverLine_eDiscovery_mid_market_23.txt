ARTICLE 1 — PROJECT DESCRIPTION AND SCOPE

1.1 Engagement Objectives. The Client (acting at the direction of the Client's outside counsel of record in the Matter) has engaged the Service Provider to provide managed document review services in connection with the Matter. The objectives are to (a) review documents collected from the Client's custodians and produced from third-party productions for responsiveness to the Client's discovery requests, (b) review responsive documents for privilege and confidentiality designations, (c) prepare a privilege log for any documents withheld on privilege grounds, and (d) prepare load files in the format required by the Client's outside counsel for production to opposing counsel.

1.2 In-Scope Activities. The Service Provider shall:

(a) Receive the document collection from the Client's eDiscovery infrastructure provider.

(b) Apply the responsiveness review criteria provided by the Client's outside counsel.

(c) Apply the privilege review criteria provided by the Client's outside counsel.

(d) Conduct first-pass responsiveness review by Service Provider review attorneys.

(e) Conduct second-pass privilege review by senior Service Provider review attorneys for documents flagged as potentially privileged.

(f) Conduct quality control sampling of completed reviewer work.

(g) Prepare a privilege log in the format required by the Client's outside counsel.

(h) Prepare production load files in the format required by the Client's outside counsel.

(i) Apply the redactions identified by the Client's outside counsel.

(j) Cooperate with the Client's outside counsel and the Client's eDiscovery infrastructure provider on any technical or workflow questions.

1.3 Out-of-Scope Activities. The following are out-of-scope. Each requires a Change Order under Article 8:

(a) Document collection from Client custodians or systems. The collection is performed by the Client's eDiscovery infrastructure provider.

(b) Forensic analysis, deduplication, or processing of the document collection. These are performed by the Client's eDiscovery infrastructure provider before the documents reach the Service Provider's review platform.

(c) Legal advice on privilege determinations, responsiveness, or any other legal matter. The Service Provider's review attorneys apply the criteria provided by the Client's outside counsel and do not independently exercise legal judgment.

(d) Trial preparation, deposition preparation, or any in-court support.

(e) Hosting of the documents on the Service Provider's review platform beyond the Engagement Period (post-Engagement hosting is a separate fee).

(f) Document review at custodian or document volumes substantially in excess of the assumptions in Schedule A.

(g) Any work performed without the express direction of the Client's outside counsel of record.

1.4 Engagement Period. The Service Provider shall perform the In-Scope Activities during the period beginning on the Project Start Date and ending on the Project End Date set forth in Article 3.

ARTICLE 2 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

2.1 Deliverables. The Service Provider shall produce and deliver the following deliverables to the Client (or, at the Client's direction, to the Client's outside counsel):

(a) Review Workflow Setup Confirmation — written confirmation of the review workflow configuration in the Service Provider's review platform, including responsiveness criteria, privilege criteria, custodian list, and reviewer assignments. Target: end of Project Week 1.

(b) Daily Review Progress Reports — daily written progress reports during active review weeks, including documents reviewed, responsiveness rate, privilege rate, and quality control sampling results. Target: each business day during the review period.

(c) First-Pass Review Complete Report — written report indicating first-pass responsiveness review is complete. Target: per the Project Schedule.

(d) Privilege Log — written privilege log in the Client's outside counsel's required format. Target: per the Project Schedule.

(e) Production Load Files — production load files in the Client's outside counsel's required format, with the agreed redactions applied. Target: per the Project Schedule.

(f) Quality Control Report — written report covering the quality control sampling results, including reviewer accuracy rates and any identified issues. Target: end of Project Week 8.

(g) Privilege Protection Attestation — a written attestation from the Service Provider's senior privilege reviewer attesting that the privilege review has been performed in accordance with the Client's outside counsel's criteria, that no privileged document has been knowingly produced, and that any inadvertent disclosure shall be promptly notified per Section 9.3.

(h) Final Engagement Report — written report covering the entire engagement, including documents reviewed, productions made, redactions applied, privilege log entries, and quality control results. Target: end of Project Week 9.

2.2 Acceptance Criteria. The Client (or the Client's outside counsel acting on the Client's behalf) reserves the sole right to determine whether each Deliverable conforms to the requirements set forth in Schedule A. The Client's determination is final, subject only to the dispute resolution procedure in the MSA.

2.3 Acceptance Period. The Client shall have a period of ten (10) business days following its receipt of each Deliverable to notify the Service Provider in writing of acceptance or rejection. The Acceptance Period for the Privilege Log and Production Load Files may be extended by the Client to accommodate review by the Client's outside counsel.

2.4 Cure of Non-Conformity. If the Client rejects a Deliverable, the Service Provider shall correct the alleged non-conformity at no additional charge within ten (10) business days. After two (2) cure cycles, the Client may, at its sole option, accept with an equitable Project Fee reduction or terminate this SOW for material breach.

2.5 No Deemed Acceptance. There shall be no deemed acceptance of any Deliverable.

ARTICLE 3 — PROJECT SCHEDULE AND MILESTONES

3.1 Project Start and End Dates. The Project shall commence on {{PROJECT_START_DATE}} and shall be substantially complete by {{PROJECT_END_DATE}}, in each case subject to (a) the Client's Client Responsibilities, (b) the Assumptions in Article 6 remaining valid, (c) any extensions agreed in an executed Change Order, and (d) any extensions necessitated by court-ordered discovery deadlines or other litigation events affecting the Matter.

3.2 Milestones.

(a) Milestone 1 — Review Workflow Setup Confirmation Accepted (Week 1).

(b) Milestone 2 — First Pass Review 25% Complete (Week 3).

(c) Milestone 3 — First Pass Review 50% Complete (Week 5).

(d) Milestone 4 — First Pass Review Complete (Week 7).

(e) Milestone 5 — Privilege Log and Production Load Files Delivered (Week 8).

(f) Milestone 6 — Final Engagement Report Accepted (Week 9).

3.3 Status Reporting. Daily written status reports during active review weeks (per Section 2.1(b)). Weekly status calls with the Client's outside counsel.

3.4 Client Right to Suspend or Re-Direct. The Client (acting through its outside counsel) may at any time direct the Service Provider to (a) pause review pending court rulings or strategic decisions, (b) re-prioritize specific custodians or document sets, (c) modify the responsiveness or privilege criteria, or (d) re-allocate reviewer capacity. The Service Provider shall comply with any such direction within twenty-four (24) hours.

ARTICLE 4 — KEY PERSONNEL

4.1 Review Team. The Service Provider shall assign a managed review team to the Matter, consisting of:

(a) Engagement Manager (the Service Provider's named single point of contact).

(b) Review Attorneys (a team of named first-pass reviewers — Schedule B).

(c) Senior Privilege Reviewers (a team of named senior reviewers for the privilege review pass — Schedule B).

(d) Quality Control Lead (named).

(e) Technology Specialist (the Service Provider's lead on the review platform configuration).

4.2 Bar Admission and Conflict Clearance. All Review Attorneys and Senior Privilege Reviewers shall (a) be admitted to practice in at least one U.S. jurisdiction, (b) be in good standing with all jurisdictions in which they are admitted, (c) have completed the Service Provider's standard conflict clearance against the Matter parties, and (d) have completed the Client's outside counsel's matter-specific conflict clearance procedures.

4.3 No Replacement Without Consent. The Service Provider shall not replace the Engagement Manager, the Senior Privilege Reviewers, or the Quality Control Lead without the Client's prior written consent.

4.4 Background Checks. All Service Provider personnel assigned to the Matter shall have completed (i) the Service Provider's standard background checks, (ii) the Service Provider's confidentiality and conflict-clearance training, and (iii) any matter-specific protective order acknowledgments required by the Court.

4.5 Removal of Personnel. The Client may at any time and without cause request that the Service Provider remove any individual from the Matter. The Service Provider shall remove the named individual within twenty-four (24) hours.

4.6 Non-Solicitation. The Service Provider shall not, during the Project Period and for twelve (12) months thereafter, solicit for employment any employee of the Client's outside counsel with whom the Service Provider had material contact during the Matter.

ARTICLE 5 — CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

5.1 Designated Client Personnel. The Client shall designate (a) the Client's General Counsel or designee as the Client Contact, (b) the Client's outside counsel of record as the day-to-day source of direction, and (c) the Client's eDiscovery infrastructure provider as the source of the document collection.

5.2 Provision of Review Criteria. The Client (through its outside counsel) shall provide the responsiveness review criteria, the privilege review criteria, the custodian list, the redaction criteria, and any other instructions necessary for the Service Provider to conduct the In-Scope Activities. The Client's outside counsel shall make itself reasonably available for clarification questions during the review period.

5.3 Document Collection. The Client (through its eDiscovery infrastructure provider) shall deliver the document collection to the Service Provider's review platform in accordance with the timeline in Schedule A. Material delays in document delivery shall extend the Project Schedule on a day-for-day basis.

5.4 Review and Approval Turnaround. The Client (through its outside counsel) shall use commercially reasonable efforts to respond to the Service Provider's clarification questions and decision requests within two (2) business days for time-sensitive items, five (5) business days otherwise.

ARTICLE 6 — ASSUMPTIONS AND DEPENDENCIES

6.1 Assumptions.

(a) The document collection volume is no more than the volume cap in Schedule A. Document volumes in excess of the cap require a Change Order at the Service Provider's standard per-document rates.

(b) The responsiveness rate, privilege rate, and complexity of the documents are within the ranges identified in Schedule A. Substantially different rates may require a Change Order.

(c) The Client's outside counsel will provide responsiveness and privilege criteria sufficiently clear and stable for the Service Provider to apply them consistently. Frequent or material changes to the criteria during the review period may require a Change Order.

(d) The Service Provider's review platform has sufficient capacity for the document volume.

(e) No court order or protective order in the Matter requires the Service Provider to perform additional steps not contemplated by this SOW. The Service Provider shall comply with any such court orders, but the additional work may require a Change Order.

(f) No additional custodians or third-party productions are added to the Matter during the Engagement Period beyond those identified in Schedule A.

6.2 Third-Party Dependencies. The Engagement depends on (a) the Client's eDiscovery infrastructure provider's continued availability, and (b) the Service Provider's review platform vendor's continued availability.

6.3 Notification of Changed Assumptions. The Client (through its outside counsel) shall promptly notify the Service Provider in writing if it becomes aware that any Assumption has changed.

ARTICLE 7 — FEES AND PAYMENT

7.1 Time-and-Materials with NTE Cap. The Service Provider shall perform the In-Scope Activities on a time-and-materials basis at the rates set forth in Schedule C. The cumulative time-and-materials charges under this SOW shall not exceed {{SOW_FEE_AMOUNT}} (the "Not-to-Exceed Amount") without the Client's prior written approval through a signed Change Order. 7.5 Expense Reimbursement. The Client shall reimburse reasonable, documented expenses subject to the Client's vendor travel policy and pre-approval of any expense over {{EXPENSE_PREAPPROVAL_THRESHOLD}}.

7.6 Disputed Invoices. The Client shall pay the undisputed portion and notify the Service Provider in writing of any dispute.

ARTICLE 8 — CHANGE CONTROL

8.1 Change Requests. Either Party may at any time submit a written Change Request. Material litigation events affecting the Matter (e.g., new custodians, new criteria from the Court, additional document productions) typically require a Change Request from the Client.

8.2 Impact Assessment. The Service Provider shall deliver a written impact assessment within five (5) business days.

8.3 Change Order. Both Parties must sign a written Change Order. No change to this SOW shall be effective unless documented in a signed Change Order.

8.4 No Oral Modifications. Only a signed Change Order modifies this SOW.

8.5 Continuing Performance. The Service Provider continues performing the In-Scope Activities while a Change Request is being negotiated.

ARTICLE 9 — INCORPORATION OF MASTER AGREEMENT AND PRIVILEGE PROTECTION

9.1 Incorporation by Reference. This SOW is issued under and is governed by the MSA. All MSA terms apply, including the MSA's provisions on confidentiality, conflicts of interest, indemnification, limitation of liability, insurance (including the Service Provider's professional liability insurance), governing law, and dispute resolution.

9.2 Order of Precedence. The MSA shall control except that the project-specific commercial terms in this SOW and the privilege protection provisions in this Article 9 shall control over any inconsistent provisions in the MSA, solely as applied to this Engagement.

9.3 Privilege Protection. The Service Provider shall treat all documents reviewed under this SOW as potentially privileged or work product until designated otherwise by the Client's outside counsel. The Service Provider shall implement and maintain controls to prevent the inadvertent disclosure of privileged documents to opposing counsel or any third party. In the event of any inadvertent disclosure of a privileged or work product document, the Service Provider shall (a) immediately notify the Client and the Client's outside counsel, (b) cooperate with the Client's outside counsel to invoke any applicable claw-back procedures or Federal Rule of Evidence 502(b) protections, and (c) preserve all records relating to the inadvertent disclosure.

9.4 Attorney Work Product. All work product produced by the Service Provider in connection with the Matter, including the privilege log, the responsiveness designations, the redactions, and the quality control sampling results, is intended to be protected attorney work product prepared at the direction of the Client's outside counsel. The Service Provider shall preserve the work product designation in all communications and shall not disclose any work product to any third party without the express written authorization of the Client's outside counsel.

9.5 Common Interest. To the extent the Service Provider's role brings it within the Client's common interest privilege with the Client's outside counsel, the Parties intend for the Service Provider's communications and work product to be covered by that common interest privilege.

9.6 Termination. The Client may terminate this SOW at any time, with or without cause, on thirty (30) days' written notice (or shorter notice if the Court enters an order requiring termination). Upon termination, the Service Provider shall deliver to the Client (or to the Client's outside counsel) all in-progress work product, including the documents already reviewed, the privilege log entries, and any quality control records.
