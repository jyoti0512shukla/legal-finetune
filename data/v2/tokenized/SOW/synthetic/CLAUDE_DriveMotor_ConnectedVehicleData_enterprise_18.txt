ARTICLE 1 — PROJECT DESCRIPTION AND SCOPE

1.1 Order Form Structure. This SOW Order Form is intentionally minimal. The substantive project terms are set forth in the Schedules. Where the Schedules conflict with the body of this Order Form, the Schedules control. Where this Order Form (including Schedules) conflicts with the MSA, the Order Form's project-specific commercial terms control as to this Engagement only.

1.2 Project Description and Scope. The Vendor shall design, build, and deploy a connected-vehicle telemetry data platform that ingests, validates, and aggregates sensor data, diagnostic codes, and usage telemetry from the Customer's connected vehicle fleet, in accordance with Schedule 1 (Project Description and Scope). In-scope and out-of-scope activities are listed in Schedule 1.A and Schedule 1.B respectively.

1.3 Vehicle Data Ownership Matrix. The data ownership rules for the connected-vehicle data ingested under this Project are set forth in the Vehicle Data Ownership Matrix attached as Schedule 1.C. The Matrix establishes which categories of data are owned by the Customer (the Vendor having no rights of use), which are jointly held (with usage rights as specified), and which (if any) the Vendor may use for its own platform development purposes. The Matrix shall be construed strictly in the Customer's favor; any data not expressly classified as joint or Vendor-usable shall be deemed Customer-owned with no Vendor usage rights.

1.4 Order of Precedence Within Order Form. Schedule 1 (including the Vehicle Data Ownership Matrix in 1.C) controls over the other Schedules. Within the remaining Schedules, the order is: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

ARTICLE 2 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

2.1 Deliverables. The Deliverables are listed in Schedule 2 with target completion dates and acceptance criteria.

2.2 Acceptance Period. Each Deliverable is subject to a fifteen (15) business day Acceptance Period.

2.3 Cure of Non-Conformity. The Vendor shall correct any non-conformity within fifteen (15) business days at no additional charge. After two (2) cure cycles, the Customer may accept with an equitable Project Fee reduction or terminate this SOW for material breach.

2.4 No Deemed Acceptance. There shall be no deemed acceptance.

ARTICLE 3 — PROJECT SCHEDULE AND MILESTONES

3.1 Project Window. The Project shall commence on {{PROJECT_START_DATE}} and shall be substantially complete by {{PROJECT_END_DATE}}.

3.2 Milestones. Named milestones with target completion dates and inter-milestone dependencies are set forth in Schedule 3.

3.3 Status Reporting and Schedule Slippage. The Vendor shall deliver weekly written status reports. The Vendor shall promptly notify the Customer of any actual or anticipated milestone slippage exceeding five (5) business days, together with a remediation plan.

ARTICLE 4 — KEY PERSONNEL

4.1 Named Key Personnel. The Vendor shall assign the Key Personnel named in Schedule 4. The Vendor shall not replace any Key Personnel without the Customer's prior written consent.

4.2 Background Checks. All Vendor personnel shall complete the background checks required by the MSA prior to receiving any access to Customer systems or vehicle telemetry data.

4.3 Removal of Personnel. The Customer may at any time and without cause request that the Vendor remove any individual; the Vendor shall comply within ten (10) business days.

ARTICLE 5 — CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

5.1 Customer Responsibilities. The Customer's responsibilities — designated personnel, access to vehicle telemetry sources, access to vehicle data dictionaries, review and approval cadence — are set forth in Schedule 5.

5.2 Customer Delays. If the Customer fails to satisfy its responsibilities, the Vendor shall be entitled to schedule extensions and reasonable cost recovery as set forth in Schedule 5.A.

ARTICLE 6 — ASSUMPTIONS AND DEPENDENCIES

6.1 Assumptions. The In-Scope Activities, the Project Schedule, and the Project Fee are based on the Assumptions in Schedule 6, including assumptions about the volume of vehicle telemetry data, the stability of the in-vehicle data interfaces, and the Customer's existing data infrastructure.

6.2 Third-Party Dependencies. Third-party dependencies are listed in Schedule 6.A, including the Customer's vehicle telemetry communication providers and the Customer's cloud platform provider.

6.3 Notification of Changed Assumptions. Each Party shall promptly notify the other in writing if it becomes aware that any Assumption has changed.

9.3 Connected Vehicle Data Privacy. The Vendor acknowledges that connected vehicle data may include personal information about vehicle owners and operators and shall be handled in accordance with the data privacy requirements of the MSA, the Customer's privacy policy, and applicable law (including the California Consumer Privacy Act and any state-specific connected vehicle laws).
