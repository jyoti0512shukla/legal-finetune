ARTICLE 1 — PROJECT DESCRIPTION AND SCOPE

1.1 Project Objectives. The Sponsor has engaged the Vendor to design, build, validate, and deploy a clinical trial data analytics platform that aggregates clinical trial data from the Sponsor's electronic data capture (EDC) systems, the Sponsor's clinical trial management system (CTMS), and the Sponsor's central laboratory systems, and provides validated dashboards, statistical analysis outputs, and submission-ready datasets for the Sponsor's regulatory affairs and biostatistics teams. The platform must comply with 21 C.F.R. Part 11 for electronic records, support audit-trail requirements applicable to clinical trial data, and maintain GCP-compliant data integrity controls.

1.2 In-Scope Activities. The Vendor shall:

(a) Conduct user requirements workshops with the Sponsor's clinical operations, biostatistics, data management, and regulatory affairs teams.

(b) Produce the User Requirements Specification (URS) and the Functional Requirements Specification (FRS).

(c) Produce the System Design Specification (SDS) and the Architecture Design Document (ADD).

(d) Build the platform in accordance with the SDS, including the data ingestion pipeline from EDC, CTMS, and central laboratory sources, the data warehouse, the validated calculations engine, the dashboards, and the submission dataset export module.

(e) Implement the 21 C.F.R. Part 11 controls: audit trails, electronic signatures, access controls, system validation documentation, and data integrity controls.

(f) Conduct Installation Qualification (IQ), Operational Qualification (OQ), and Performance Qualification (PQ) for the platform in coordination with the Sponsor's computer systems validation (CSV) team.

(g) Produce the Validation Master Plan (VMP), the Validation Protocols, and the Validation Reports.

(h) Conduct user acceptance testing in coordination with the Sponsor's UAT team.

(i) Conduct end-user training for the Sponsor's clinical operations, biostatistics, and regulatory affairs teams.

(j) Provide a 60-calendar-day post-go-live stabilization period.

(k) Cooperate with any Sponsor or regulator audit of the platform during or after the Project Period.

1.3 Out-of-Scope Activities. The following are out-of-scope. Each requires a Change Order:

(a) Modifications to the Sponsor's existing EDC, CTMS, or central laboratory systems.

(b) Modifications to the Sponsor's existing data warehouse beyond the integration interfaces.

(c) Statistical analysis plans, statistical analysis outputs beyond the validated calculations in the SDS, or any submission preparation work beyond producing submission-ready datasets in the SDTM/ADaM format.

(d) Migration of trial data from clinical trials closed prior to the Project Start Date.

(e) Validation of any system other than the platform built under this SOW.

(f) Pharmacovigilance reporting, adverse event tracking, or any safety database integration.

(g) Patient-facing applications, e-consent platforms, or recruitment tools.

(h) Any work product subject to U.S. export-control restrictions other than EAR99 commercial software.

(i) Tax, legal, regulatory, or pharmacovigilance advice.

1.4 Engagement Period. The Vendor shall perform the In-Scope Activities during the period beginning on the Project Start Date and ending on the Project End Date set forth in Article 3.

The Sponsor shall provide the Vendor with access to the Sponsor's EDC, CTMS, central laboratory systems, and the Sponsor's data warehouse, in coordination with the Sponsor's standard access provisioning procedures. ARTICLE 6 — ASSUMPTIONS AND DEPENDENCIES

6.1 Assumptions.

(a) The Sponsor's existing EDC, CTMS, and central laboratory systems support standard data export interfaces.

(b) The Sponsor's CSV process and SOPs are stable and remain unchanged during the Project. Material changes to the Sponsor's CSV SOPs may trigger a Change Order to update validation documents.

(c) FDA and EMA regulations governing electronic records (21 C.F.R. Part 11 and equivalents) remain materially unchanged during the Project.

(d) The Sponsor's data warehouse has sufficient capacity for the platform's data ingestion volumes.

(e) The Sponsor's regulatory affairs team has aligned the platform's submission dataset format with the Sponsor's intended submission format (SDTM/ADaM).

(f) No regulatory inspection or data integrity audit during the Project Period requires the Sponsor to suspend the Project for more than ten (10) business days.

6.2 Third-Party Dependencies. The Project depends on (a) the Sponsor's EDC, CTMS, and central laboratory vendors' continued support, (b) the FDA's electronic records guidance remaining materially stable, and (c) any third-party libraries or components used by the Vendor (subject to the MSA's open-source compliance and Part 11 compatibility requirements).

6.3 Notification of Changed Assumptions. The Sponsor shall promptly notify the Vendor in writing if it becomes aware that any Assumption has changed.
