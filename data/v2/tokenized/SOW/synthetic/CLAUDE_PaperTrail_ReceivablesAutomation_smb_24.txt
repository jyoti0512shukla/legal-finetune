ARTICLE 1 — PROJECT DESCRIPTION AND SCOPE

1.1 What you're getting

The Provider will help the Customer get up and running on the Provider's receivables automation product. The Provider's onboarding team will (a) configure the Customer's instance, (b) integrate the product with the Customer's accounting system, (c) import the Customer's open invoices, (d) train the Customer's team on day-to-day use, and (e) provide 14 days of post-launch support.

This is a fixed-fee, self-serve onboarding. It is NOT custom consulting. The Provider's standard onboarding playbook applies.

1.2 What's in scope

(a) Standard configuration of the Customer's instance using the Customer's selections in the Provider's onboarding wizard.

(b) Integration with one (1) supported accounting system (QuickBooks Online, Xero, or NetSuite — see the supported list in Schedule A).

(c) Import of the Customer's open invoices via the standard CSV importer.

(d) Configuration of the standard collection cadences (gentle reminder, firm reminder, escalation).

(e) Standard email template setup using the Provider's templates.

(f) One (1) live training session (60 minutes, remote).

(g) Recorded training videos accessible via the Customer portal.

(h) 14 days of post-launch support via the Customer portal.

(i) Per-user account provisioning for up to ten (10) Customer users.

1.3 What's NOT in scope

(a) Anything beyond the standard onboarding playbook in Section 1.2.

(b) Custom integration with any accounting system not on the supported list.

(c) Import of historical paid invoices, customer master data beyond the open-invoice records, or any other historical data.

(d) Custom email templates, custom collection cadences, or custom escalation rules. Customer can configure these themselves after onboarding.

(e) Migration from another receivables automation product.

(f) Multi-entity, multi-currency, or multi-subsidiary configuration. Single-entity setup only.

(g) Provisioning for more than ten (10) Customer users (additional users are billable per the Provider's standard pricing).

(h) Any tax, accounting, or financial advice.

(i) Any work performed outside the engagement period.

1.4 Engagement period

Start: {{PROJECT_START_DATE}}. End: {{PROJECT_END_DATE}}. The Engagement Period is approximately 4 weeks.

ARTICLE 2 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

2.1 Deliverables

(a) Configuration Confirmation — written confirmation that the Customer's instance is configured per the onboarding wizard. Delivered: end of Project Week 1.

(b) Integration Test Confirmation — confirmation that the integration with the Customer's accounting system is functioning, with a test invoice imported successfully. Delivered: end of Project Week 2.

(c) Invoice Import Confirmation — confirmation that the Customer's open invoices have been imported and reconciled against the source. Delivered: end of Project Week 3.

(d) Live Training Session — 60-minute remote training session conducted with up to ten (10) Customer attendees. Delivered: end of Project Week 3.

(e) Go-Live Confirmation — confirmation that the Customer is live and the standard collection cadences are running. Delivered: end of Project Week 4.

(f) Post-Launch Support Closure — at the end of the 14-day post-launch support period, summary of any support tickets opened and resolved.

2.2 Acceptance

Each Deliverable is automatically accepted upon delivery via the Customer portal unless the Customer raises a written objection within three (3) business days. The objection must specifically identify why the Deliverable does not match what is described in Article 2.1.

2.3 Cure

If the Customer raises a valid objection, the Provider's onboarding team will fix the issue within three (3) business days at no additional charge. The fix is bounded by what is described in Article 2.1 — out-of-scope requests are out-of-scope.

2.4 No deemed acceptance windows beyond Section 2.2

The 3-business-day objection window in Section 2.2 is the entire acceptance mechanism. After 3 business days with no objection, the Deliverable is deemed accepted.

ARTICLE 4 — KEY PERSONNEL

4.1 Onboarding team

The Provider assigns the next available onboarding specialist from its standard onboarding team rotation. The Customer cannot request specific individuals or require any specific named onboarding specialist to remain on the engagement throughout. The Provider's onboarding team is fungible and trained to deliver the standard onboarding consistently.

4.2 No background checks beyond standard

The Provider's onboarding team has completed the Provider's standard background checks. No additional background checks beyond the Provider's standard program are required for this self-serve onboarding.

4.3 Removal of personnel

If the Customer is unhappy with a specific onboarding specialist, the Customer may request a different specialist via the Customer portal. The Provider will assign a different specialist within five (5) business days.

ARTICLE 5 — CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

5.1 What the Provider needs from the Customer

(a) Designate one Customer point of contact who will work with the onboarding team.

(b) Provide accounting system credentials with the permissions needed for the integration (see the integration setup guide in the Customer portal).

(c) Provide the open-invoice export from the Customer's accounting system in the standard CSV format (template in the Customer portal).

(d) Confirm the Customer's collection cadences and escalation preferences via the onboarding wizard.

(e) Show up to the live training session.

(f) Respond to onboarding-team messages in the Customer portal within 2 business days.

5.2 Accuracy

The Customer is responsible for the accuracy of the open-invoice data and the accounting system credentials. Errors caused by inaccurate Customer data are not the Provider's responsibility.

5.3 Customer delays

If the Customer is more than 5 business days late on the onboarding wizard or the open-invoice export, the Project End Date moves out by the same number of days. The 14-day post-launch support window starts on actual go-live, not on the originally scheduled go-live date.

ARTICLE 6 — ASSUMPTIONS AND DEPENDENCIES

6.1 Assumptions

(a) The Customer uses one of the supported accounting systems (Schedule A) and is on a current/supported version.

(b) The Customer has fewer than 2,000 open invoices at the time of import. Higher volumes may require additional onboarding fee — the Provider will flag this during the onboarding wizard.

(c) The Customer has fewer than 10 users.

(d) The Customer has no multi-entity, multi-currency, or multi-subsidiary configuration needs.

(e) The Customer is not migrating from another receivables automation product.

(f) The Customer has the legal right to use the customer-contact email addresses in its accounting system for receivables follow-up communications.

6.2 Third-party dependencies

The Project depends on (a) the Customer's accounting system being accessible to the integration, and (b) the Provider's product platform being operational.

6.3 If an assumption is wrong

The Provider notifies the Customer through the Customer portal. The Customer can either bring the engagement back into the standard onboarding scope or proceed via a Change Order.

8.1 Out-of-scope requests

If the Customer wants something that's out-of-scope under Section 1.3, the Customer can submit a Change Request through the Customer portal. The Provider has no obligation to accept any out-of-scope request — most out-of-scope requests are better handled by the Provider's custom professional services team under a separate SOW.

8.2 Standard Change Order pricing

If the Provider does accept an out-of-scope request, the Provider's standard professional services pricing applies. The Customer agrees to the Provider's standard rates by submitting a Change Request that the Provider accepts.

8.3 De minimis

8.4 No oral changes

9.2 Self-serve nature

This SOW is the Provider's standard self-serve professional services agreement. The Customer acknowledges that the engagement is structured for low-touch, standard onboarding and that the Provider is not providing custom consulting services.

9.3 Termination

Termination is governed by the MSA. The Customer may terminate the engagement at any time, but the onboarding fee is non-refundable per Section 7.4. The Provider may terminate at any time if the Customer is in material breach.
