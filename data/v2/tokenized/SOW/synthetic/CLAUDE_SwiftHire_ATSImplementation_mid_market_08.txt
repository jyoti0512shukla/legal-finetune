ARTICLE 1 — PROJECT DESCRIPTION AND SCOPE

1.1 What we're building

The Provider is implementing a modern Applicant Tracking System (ATS) for the Client's talent team. The goal is to get the Client off its current spreadsheets-and-email recruiting workflow and onto a structured platform that supports req creation, candidate sourcing, interview scheduling, scorecards, offer letters, and reporting. The Project covers configuration, data migration, integrations with the Client's HRIS and email/calendar, training, and a 30-day go-live hypercare period.

1.2 In-scope

The Provider will:

(a) Configure the ATS instance for the Client (req templates, scorecards, interview kits, offer letter templates, EEO/OFCCP fields, custom fields per Schedule A).

(b) Build the org structure (departments, teams, hiring managers, recruiters, interviewers) per the Client-supplied org map.

(c) Implement the role-based access controls per the access matrix in Schedule B.

(d) Migrate the active req pipeline (open reqs and in-progress candidates) from the Client's current system into the ATS.

(e) Integrate the ATS with the Client's HRIS (BambooHR or Workday — Schedule C identifies which) for new-hire push.

(f) Integrate the ATS with the Client's calendar system (Google Workspace or Microsoft 365) for interview scheduling.

(g) Implement the candidate-sourcing channel integrations: LinkedIn Recruiter System Connect, Indeed, the Client's careers page, and the Client's employee referral form.

(h) Configure standard reports (req status, time-to-fill, source effectiveness, pass-through rates by stage, EEO reporting).

(i) Run two training sessions: one for recruiters (90 minutes) and one for hiring managers (45 minutes).

(j) Provide 30 calendar days of go-live hypercare following cutover, with daily standups during the first week and weekly office hours during weeks 2-4.

1.3 Out-of-scope

These are NOT included. Each of these, if requested, requires a Change Order.

(a) Migration of historical (closed) reqs and candidates older than 90 days. Only active reqs and candidates currently in-flight are migrated.

(b) Custom-built integrations beyond the standard integrations in Section 1.2. Custom API work is billable.

(c) Background-check vendor integration. The Client's background-check vendor integration is set up by the background-check vendor, not by the Provider.

(d) Compensation benchmarking, leveling, or comp-band setup.

(e) Diversity sourcing services or DE&I consulting.

(f) Recruitment marketing content (career page copy, employer branding).

(g) Performance management, learning management, or any non-recruiting HR module.

(h) Multi-tenant or multi-region instance setup beyond the single instance described in Schedule A.

ARTICLE 2 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

2.1 Deliverables

(a) Configuration Workbook — written workbook documenting the as-configured ATS, including req templates, scorecards, custom fields, and role-based access. Target: end of Project Week 4.

(b) Integration Test Report — report covering HRIS, calendar, and sourcing channel integration test results. Target: end of Project Week 6.

(c) Migration Validation Report — report comparing the migrated active pipeline against the source system. Target: end of Project Week 7.

(d) UAT Sign-off — signed UAT acceptance from the Client's recruiting team confirming that the ATS is ready for go-live. Target: end of Project Week 8.

(e) Go-Live and Cutover Confirmation — confirmation that the ATS is in production and the legacy workflow is retired. Target: end of Project Week 9.

(f) Hypercare Closure Report — report summarizing the 30-day hypercare period, including issues raised and resolved. Target: end of Project Week 13.

2.2 Acceptance window

10 business days from receipt of each Deliverable. Client either accepts in writing or sends a single consolidated rejection notice with the alleged non-conformities. No deemed acceptance — the Client must affirmatively accept or reject each Deliverable within the acceptance window. The "no deemed acceptance" rule is intentional given the regulatory implications of mis-handled candidate data.

2.3 Cure

If the Client rejects, the Provider has 10 business days to fix and re-deliver. The Client gets a fresh acceptance window for the resubmitted Deliverable. After two cure cycles, the Client may either accept with an equitable Project Fee reduction or terminate this SOW for material breach.

2.4 UAT criteria

UAT sign-off (Deliverable (d)) is conditioned on the Client's recruiting team running through the test scripts in Schedule D and confirming that each test script passes. Test scripts cover req creation, candidate intake, interview scheduling, scorecard submission, offer letter generation, and HRIS push.

ARTICLE 5 — CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

5.1 What we need from the Client

(a) Designate one Client Implementation Owner with authority to approve Deliverables and make decisions on configuration choices.

(b) Provide the org map, role-based access matrix, and custom field list (Schedules A and B) within the first 5 business days.

(c) Provide HRIS API credentials for the integration described in Section 1.2(e).

(d) Provide calendar admin access for the integration described in Section 1.2(f).

(e) Provide the active req pipeline export from the current system within the first 10 business days.

(f) Make the Client's recruiting team available for the UAT test scripts in Section 2.4.

(g) Make hiring managers available for the 45-minute training session in Week 8.

(h) Respond to deliverables, decision requests, and integration questions within 3 business days for time-sensitive items, 5 business days otherwise.

5.2 Accuracy

The Client is responsible for the accuracy of the org map, the access matrix, and the active req pipeline data. The Provider relies on the Client-provided data without independent verification.

5.3 Client delays

If the Client misses a 3-or-5-business-day response window and the delay impacts the Project Schedule, the Project End Date moves out by the same number of days. If a Client delay forces the Provider to re-mobilize personnel, the Provider may charge re-mobilization at the rates in the MSA after notice.

ARTICLE 6 — ASSUMPTIONS AND DEPENDENCIES

6.1 Assumptions

(a) The Client has no more than 50 active reqs and no more than 800 candidates in flight as of the migration cutoff. Higher volumes are billable.

(b) The Client uses one of the supported HRIS systems (BambooHR or Workday). Other HRIS systems are out of scope.

(c) The Client uses Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 for calendar. Other calendar systems are out of scope.

(d) The Client has an active LinkedIn Recruiter seat that supports the LinkedIn Recruiter System Connect (RSC) integration.

(e) The Client's careers page is hostable on the standard ATS-provided careers site OR the Client's existing careers page can accept the standard ATS embed snippet. Custom careers page work beyond the embed is out of scope.

(f) The Client is OFCCP-covered or, if not, doesn't require OFCCP-specific compliance reporting beyond the standard EEO fields. OFCCP-specific scope additions require a Change Order.

(g) The Client doesn't have any legacy data residency or data export requirements that would prevent the use of the standard SaaS ATS.

(h) The Client has the budget allocated for the ATS subscription itself (the Provider invoices for implementation services, not for the ATS subscription).

6.2 Third-party dependencies

The Project depends on (a) the ATS vendor's SaaS platform being operational, (b) the Client's HRIS API being available and stable, (c) LinkedIn Recruiter System Connect being available for the Client's seat tier, and (d) the Client's calendar admin granting integration access on schedule.

6.3 If an assumption is wrong

The Provider notifies the Client promptly upon discovering any incorrect assumption. The Parties work out a Change Order.
