ARTICLE 1 — PROJECT DESCRIPTION AND SCOPE

1.1 What we're doing

The Provider is a SOC 2 readiness consultancy. The Client is a B2B SaaS startup that needs SOC 2 Type I readiness in time for a strategic enterprise customer's procurement process. The Provider will assess the Client's current security posture against the AICPA Trust Services Criteria for Security (and Availability + Confidentiality if requested), identify gaps, prescribe remediation, and produce a readiness report the Client can hand to its eventual SOC 2 auditor.

This is a readiness ENGAGEMENT, not an audit. The Provider is not a CPA firm and does NOT issue any SOC 2 attestation or auditor's opinion. The Client must engage a separate licensed CPA firm to perform the actual SOC 2 audit.

1.2 In-scope

The Provider will:

(a) Conduct a kickoff and scoping session to confirm the Trust Services Criteria in scope (Security at minimum; Availability and Confidentiality if the Client elects).

(b) Document the Client's current control environment via interviews, evidence review, and walkthroughs.

(c) Assess the Client's controls against the criteria for the elected categories.

(d) Identify gaps between the Client's current state and the criteria.

(e) Recommend remediation for each gap, with prioritization (must-fix before audit / nice-to-have).

(f) Help the Client draft policies and procedures for the categories where the Client has none (the Provider provides templates; the Client tailors them to its environment).

(g) Recommend tooling for evidence collection and ongoing compliance monitoring (e.g., Vanta, Drata, Secureframe — Provider is vendor-neutral).

(h) Produce the Readiness Report described in Section 2.1(d).

(i) Conduct a 90-minute readout session with the Client's leadership.

1.3 Out-of-scope

(a) Performing the SOC 2 audit. The Provider is not a CPA firm and does not issue audit opinions or SOC 2 reports. The Client engages a separate CPA firm for the audit.

(b) Implementing remediation. The Provider identifies gaps and prescribes remediation; the Client implements. The Provider can be re-engaged for implementation support under a separate SOW.

(c) Penetration testing. Penetration testing is a separate engagement under a separate SOW.

(d) Trust Services Criteria categories the Client does not elect (Processing Integrity, Privacy). Adding categories mid-engagement requires a Change Order.

(e) ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, FedRAMP, or any framework other than SOC 2.

(f) Implementing or operating any compliance tooling (Vanta, Drata, Secureframe). The Provider recommends; the Client procures and operates.

(g) Tax, legal, accounting, or financial advice.

ARTICLE 2 — DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

2.1 Deliverables

(a) Scoping Memo — written memo confirming the Trust Services Criteria in scope, the in-scope systems and environments, the in-scope personnel, and the audit period scope. Target: end of Project Week 1.

(b) Current-State Documentation — written documentation of the Client's current control environment, including organizational structure, system inventory, data flows, and existing controls. Target: end of Project Week 3.

(c) Gap Assessment — written assessment of the Client's controls against the elected Trust Services Criteria, with each criterion rated as "Met," "Partially Met," or "Not Met." Target: end of Project Week 5.

(d) Readiness Report — comprehensive written report covering scope, methodology, current state, gap assessment, prioritized remediation plan, and a target audit-readiness date. The Report is intended for the Client's internal use and to share with the Client's eventual SOC 2 auditor. Target: end of Project Week 7.

(e) Policy Pack — template policies and procedures for the categories where the Client has none, customized to the Client's environment. Includes Information Security Policy, Access Control Policy, Incident Response Plan, Business Continuity Plan, Vendor Management Policy, and Acceptable Use Policy. Target: end of Project Week 6.

(f) Readout Session — 90-minute session with the Client's leadership covering the Readiness Report findings and recommendations. Target: end of Project Week 8.

2.2 Report-only deliverable

The Readiness Report (Deliverable (d)) is an INFORMATIONAL deliverable for the Client's internal use and for the Client to share with its eventual auditor. The Report is NOT an audit opinion, NOT a SOC 2 report, and NOT a representation that the Client meets the Trust Services Criteria. The Client is responsible for its own representations to third parties about its compliance posture. The Provider's name and the Report may not be used in any external marketing or compliance claim by the Client without the Provider's prior written consent.

2.3 Acceptance window

10 business days from receipt of each Deliverable. Client either accepts in writing or sends a single consolidated rejection notice. If the Client doesn't respond within 10 business days, the Provider sends a written reminder; if still no response 5 business days after the reminder, the Deliverable is deemed accepted.

2.4 Cure

Provider has 10 business days to fix and re-deliver. After two cure cycles, the Client may either accept with an equitable Project Fee reduction or terminate this SOW for material breach.

ARTICLE 6 — ASSUMPTIONS AND DEPENDENCIES

6.1 Assumptions

(a) The Client has fewer than 75 employees and a single primary product.

(b) The Client's production environment is a single-region cloud deployment on AWS, GCP, or Azure (not on-prem, not multi-cloud, not hybrid).

(c) The Client's customer-facing application is a single SaaS product, not multiple products under one company.

(d) The Client elects the Security category at minimum. Adding Availability and Confidentiality is included if elected at kickoff. Adding Processing Integrity or Privacy is a Change Order.

(e) The Client has no prior SOC 2 audit, qualified opinion, or SOC 2 control failures that need to be remediated as part of this readiness engagement.

(f) The Client has the budget for the eventual auditor engagement and is committed to the SOC 2 process beyond this readiness engagement.

(g) The Client has not engaged another SOC 2 readiness consultant in parallel.

6.2 Third-party dependencies

The Project depends on (a) the Client's identity provider, code repo, cloud accounts, and MDM being accessible to the Provider during the engagement, and (b) the Client's leadership being available for interviews and the readout session.

6.3 If an assumption is wrong

The Provider notifies the Client promptly upon discovering any incorrect assumption. The Parties work out a Change Order.
