ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "21 CFR Part 820" means Title 21, Part 820 of the United States Code of Federal Regulations (the FDA Quality System Regulation for medical device manufacturers).

1.2 "Authorized User" means a physician, advanced practice provider, nurse, technician, researcher, administrator, or other workforce member of Customer or its Affiliates who is authorized by Customer to access and use the Platform in connection with Patient care, clinical operations, or research activities.

1.3 "BAA" means the Business Associate Agreement attached hereto as Exhibit A.

1.4 "Common Rule" means the Federal Policy for the Protection of Human Subjects, 45 C.F.R. Part 46.

1.5 "Customer Data" means all electronic data, content, records, and information uploaded to, transmitted through, processed by, or stored within the Platform by or on behalf of Customer or any Authorized User, including without limitation Protected Health Information, Research Data, Patient-Reported Outcomes, and Wearable Device Data.

1.6 "FDA" means the United States Food and Drug Administration.

1.7 "HIPAA" means the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, as amended by the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act ("HITECH"), and the regulations promulgated thereunder at 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164.

1.8 "NIH" means the National Institutes of Health.

1.9 "NIH DMS Policy" means the NIH Data Management and Sharing Policy (NOT-OD-21-013), as the same may be supplemented or replaced from time to time.

1.10 "Patient" means an individual receiving clinical care from Customer or participating in a research study conducted by Customer.

1.11 "Platform" means Provider's hosted remote patient monitoring and telemedicine software-as-a-service platform, including the patient-facing mobile app, the clinician-facing dashboards, the wearable device integration layer, the video visit module, the asynchronous messaging module, the clinical decision support module, the population health Module, and any updates released by Provider during the Term. The Platform incorporates one or more components that are or may be regulated as Software as a Medical Device ("SaMD") by the FDA.

1.12 "Protected Health Information" or "PHI" has the meaning set forth in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103.

1.13 "Research Data" means data generated through clinical research conducted by Customer, including data subject to the NIH DMS Policy and Common Rule.

1.14 "SaMD" means Software as a Medical Device, as defined by the International Medical Device Regulators Forum and as the term is used by the FDA.

1.15 "Subprocessor" means any third party engaged by Provider to process Customer Data on Provider's behalf.

1.16 "Wearable Device Data" means physiological measurements (heart rate, blood pressure, blood glucose, oxygen saturation, weight, activity level, sleep, electrocardiogram tracings, and similar measurements) collected from Patient wearable devices integrated with the Platform.

3.3 Implementation Fee. The implementation services shall be provided for a fee of {{IMPLEMENTATION_FEE}}, invoiced upon the milestones set forth in the implementation plan.

ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Uptime Commitment. Provider shall make the Platform available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.95% during all hours, twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week, recognizing that Patient monitoring and telemedicine services may be required at any time.

If Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below 99.95% in any calendar month, Customer shall be entitled to receive service credits, the amounts of which shall be set forth in the Service Level Exhibit. Service credits shall be in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other rights or remedies of Customer.

6.3 Patient-Critical Incident Response. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to acknowledge any Severity 1 incident affecting Patient care within fifteen (15) minutes and to provide a workaround within thirty (30) minutes. The Parties acknowledge that the failure of certain Platform components could pose a risk to Patient safety, and that such incidents shall be treated with the utmost urgency.

6.4 SaMD-Specific Incidents. Any incident affecting an SaMD component of the Platform shall be reported by Provider to Customer's Chief Medical Information Officer and Chief Quality Officer within two (2) hours of Provider's discovery, regardless of severity. Provider shall cooperate with Customer's evaluation of whether the incident constitutes a reportable event under 21 C.F.R. Part 803 (Medical Device Reporting), and Provider shall make any required Medical Device Reports to FDA in its capacity as the manufacturer of the SaMD components.

6.5 Maintenance Windows. Provider shall not perform scheduled maintenance during regular Patient care hours, defined as 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. local time at Customer's primary location, except with Customer's prior written consent.

ARTICLE 7 — CUSTOMER DATA, PHI, AND RESEARCH DATA

7.1 Ownership of Customer Data. As between the Parties, Customer shall retain all right, title, and interest in and to Customer Data, including all PHI, Research Data, Patient-Reported Outcomes, and Wearable Device Data.

7.2 Business Associate Status. Provider is a "business associate" of Customer within the meaning of HIPAA. The BAA attached as Exhibit A governs Provider's handling of PHI. In the event of conflict between this Article 7 and the BAA with respect to PHI, the BAA shall control.

7.3 Permitted Uses. Provider shall use and disclose Customer Data only as permitted by HIPAA, the BAA, this Agreement, the Data Use Agreement, and applicable law. Provider shall not use Customer Data for any other purpose, including without limitation (a) to train any artificial intelligence or machine learning model, (b) to develop any commercial product, (c) to contribute to any aggregated dataset for sale or licensing, (d) to support any clinical research conducted by Provider or any third party, or (e) to market any product or service to Patients. Customer Data shall not be de-identified by Provider for any purpose except as expressly permitted by Customer in writing.

7.4 Research Data and the NIH DMS Policy. The Parties acknowledge that some Customer Data constitutes Research Data subject to the NIH DMS Policy, federal grant terms and conditions, and the Common Rule. Provider shall (a) cooperate with Customer in any data management and sharing plan submitted by Customer to NIH, (b) provide Customer with the technical means to extract and share Research Data in standard formats required by NIH-designated repositories, (c) not share Research Data with any third party other than as instructed by Customer in writing, and (d) maintain Research Data for the period required by the applicable federal grant or by the Common Rule, whichever is longer.

7.5 SaMD Algorithm Transparency. Provider shall make available to Customer such documentation regarding the SaMD components of the Platform — including the intended use, the validation data set, the performance characteristics, and the limitations — as Customer reasonably requires for clinical decision-making, for IRB review of any research involving the SaMD components, and for response to any FDA inquiry. Provider shall not make any material change to an SaMD component (including any change that requires a new 510(k) submission under FDA's guidance on software changes to existing medical devices) without prior written notice to Customer and an opportunity for Customer to validate the change.

7.6 Security Safeguards. Provider shall implement and maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to comply with the HIPAA Security Rule (45 C.F.R. Part 164, Subpart C), the BAA, and the heightened expectations applicable to academic medical centers. Such safeguards shall include without limitation (a) encryption of Customer Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher; (b) encryption of Customer Data at rest using AES-256; (c) role-based access controls aligned with Customer's clinical workflow structure; (d) multi-factor authentication for all Authorized User access; (e) annual third-party penetration testing; (f) maintenance of HITRUST CSF certification or equivalent throughout the Term; and (g) a documented information security program reviewed annually.

7.7 Breach Notification. Provider shall notify Customer's Privacy Officer in writing of any "Breach" of "Unsecured PHI" (as both terms are defined under 45 C.F.R. § 164.402) without unreasonable delay and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours of Provider's discovery. The notification shall include all information required by the BAA, and Provider shall provide regular updates at intervals no less frequent than daily until the incident is resolved. Provider shall reimburse Customer for the reasonable costs of HIPAA-required breach notifications, credit monitoring, and forensic investigation to the extent the breach was caused by Provider's breach of Article 7 or the BAA.

7.8 Subprocessors. Provider shall not engage any Subprocessor to process PHI or Research Data without Customer's prior written approval. Provider shall maintain a list of approved Subprocessors at a Customer-accessible Customer Trust Portal. Customer may withhold approval of any Subprocessor on any reasonable ground.

7.9 Data Residency. Provider shall store and process all Customer Data within data centers physically located in the contiguous United States, and shall not transfer Customer Data outside of the United States without Customer's prior written consent.

7.10 Right to Audit. Once per calendar year (and additionally following any Security Incident or upon the request of any Regulator with jurisdiction over Customer), Customer or a third-party auditor engaged by Customer (under reasonable confidentiality obligations) shall have the right to audit Provider's compliance with this Article 7, the BAA, the Data Use Agreement, and applicable laws, upon at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice (or shorter notice as required by the circumstances).

7.11 Regulator Cooperation. Provider shall cooperate fully with any audit, inquiry, or investigation by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights, the FDA, the NIH Office of Extramural Research, the Office for Human Research Protections, any state attorney general, or any other governmental authority with jurisdiction over Customer or its activities, in each case as such audit, inquiry, or investigation relates to Customer's use of the Platform.

7.12 Data Return and Deletion. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, Provider shall, at Customer's election, (a) make Customer Data available for export by Customer in standard healthcare interoperability formats (HL7, FHIR, CCDA, DICOM as applicable) for a period of one hundred eighty (180) days at no additional charge; or (b) permanently delete Customer Data from Provider's production systems within thirty (30) days, with backup deletion to follow within ninety (90) days, in each case subject to Provider's right to retain Customer Data to the extent required by applicable law or by the FDA's recordkeeping requirements for SaMD.
