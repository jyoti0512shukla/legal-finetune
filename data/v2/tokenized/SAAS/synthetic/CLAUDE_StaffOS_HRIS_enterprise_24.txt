STAFFOS MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date"), by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} ("Provider"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} {{CUSTOMER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} ("Customer").

This Agreement establishes the master terms under which Provider shall make its StaffOS human resources information system available to Customer. This Agreement, by itself, does not obligate Customer to purchase any Subscription, Module, or Professional Services. The specific Modules, employee headcount limits, payroll integration scope, applicable countries, Subscription Fees, and Subscription Term dates shall be set forth in one or more Order Forms executed by both Parties (each, an "Order Form") and incorporated by reference into this Agreement. In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and an Order Form, the Order Form shall control as to the matters expressly addressed therein.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.

1.2 "Authorized User" means an employee, contractor, or other workforce member of Customer or its Affiliates who is authorized by Customer to access and use the Platform pursuant to the rights granted on an Order Form. Authorized Users include both administrative users (HR business partners, payroll operators, recruiters) and self-service users (employees managing their own profiles).

1.3 "Covered Country" means a country in which Customer's Employee Population is located and for which Customer has subscribed to the StaffOS country-specific module on an Order Form.

1.4 "Customer Data" means all electronic data, content, records, and other information uploaded to, transmitted through, processed by, or stored within the Platform by or on behalf of Customer or any Authorized User, including without limitation Employee Data and Payroll Data.

1.5 "Employee Data" means information about employees, contractors, and other workers of Customer or its Affiliates, including names, contact information, identification numbers (where lawful), compensation, performance reviews, employment history, dependents, beneficiaries, and any other information processed by the Platform in the course of human resources administration.

1.6 "Employee Population" means the total number of active employees, contractors, and other workers of Customer and its Affiliates whose records are stored in the Platform on the last day of any given month.

1.7 "Module" means a discrete functional component of the Platform (such as Core HR, Recruiting, Onboarding, Time and Attendance, Performance Management, Compensation Planning, Learning Management, or any country-specific Payroll Module) as identified on an Order Form.

1.8 "Order Form" has the meaning set forth in the preamble.

1.9 "Payroll Data" means employee compensation, deductions, taxes, payroll period calculations, and any related information processed by a Payroll Module or transmitted to a third-party payroll provider through the Platform.

1.10 "Platform" means Provider's hosted human resources information system software-as-a-service platform, marketed as StaffOS, including all Modules made available to Customer under one or more Order Forms.

1.11 "Subscription" means Customer's right to access and use a specified set of Modules for a specified Employee Population in specified Covered Countries for a specified Subscription Term, as set forth on an Order Form.

1.12 "Subscription Fees" means the fees payable by Customer for a Subscription, as set forth on the applicable Order Form, typically billed on a per-Employee per-month or per-Employee per-year basis.

1.13 "Third-Party Payroll Provider" means a third-party payroll processing service to which the Platform transmits Payroll Data on Customer's behalf in connection with a country-specific Payroll Module, in each case under a separate agreement between Customer and such Third-Party Payroll Provider.

ARTICLE 2 — SUBSCRIPTION AND LICENSE

2.1 Provision of Services. Subject to the terms of this Agreement and the applicable Order Form, Provider shall make the Modules identified on each Order Form available to Customer and the Authorized Users specified on such Order Form during the applicable Subscription Term.

2.2 License Grant. Provider hereby grants to Customer, during each Subscription Term, a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable right and license to access and use the Modules identified on the applicable Order Form for the internal human resources operations of Customer and its Affiliates, for the Employee Population and Covered Countries specified on such Order Form.

2.3 Restrictions. Customer shall not (a) modify or create derivative works based on the Platform; (b) sublicense, sell, or otherwise commercially exploit access to the Platform; (c) reverse engineer the Platform; (d) use the Platform to build a competing HRIS product; (e) use the Platform to process the data of any individual who is not an employee, contractor, or other worker of Customer or its Affiliates without specific written approval from Provider; or (f) use the Platform in any country that is not a Covered Country on the applicable Order Form for any operations regulated by the laws of that country.

2.4 Affiliates. Customer's Affiliates may use the Platform under the rights granted to Customer, provided that Customer shall remain responsible for the acts and omissions of its Affiliates and shall ensure that its Affiliates comply with this Agreement.

2.5 Suspension. Provider may suspend Customer's access to the Platform, in whole or in part, upon prior written notice if Provider reasonably determines that (a) Customer's use poses an immediate security risk; (b) Customer has materially breached the license restrictions in Section 2.3 and failed to cure within ten (10) business days of written notice; or (c) suspension is required by law. Provider shall not suspend access during a payroll run window in a manner that would foreseeably prevent timely payroll processing for Authorized Users, except where required by law or to prevent imminent harm.

ARTICLE 3 — IMPLEMENTATION AND PROFESSIONAL SERVICES

3.1 Statements of Work. All implementation, data migration, integration with Customer's existing systems, country-specific Payroll Module configuration, training, and other professional services ("Professional Services") shall be performed pursuant to one or more Statements of Work ("SOW"), each signed by both Parties and incorporated by reference into this Agreement.

3.2 No Implementation by Default. This Agreement does not, by itself, obligate Provider to perform any Professional Services. Implementation work shall be undertaken only pursuant to an executed SOW.

3.3 Country-Specific Implementation. Each country-specific Payroll Module added to a Subscription shall require its own implementation phase under a separate SOW, reflecting the country's specific regulatory requirements, third-party integrations, and statutory reporting obligations.

3.4 Change Orders. Any change to the scope of Professional Services described in an SOW shall be documented in a written change order signed by both Parties.

3.5 Customer Cooperation. Customer acknowledges that timely completion of Professional Services depends on Customer's cooperation, including timely access to systems, data, and personnel. Provider shall not be responsible for delays attributable to Customer.

ARTICLE 4 — SUBSCRIPTION FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fees. Customer shall pay Provider the Subscription Fees, Professional Services fees, and any other amounts set forth on the applicable Order Form or SOW.

4.2 Per-Employee Pricing. Subscription Fees are typically billed on a per-Employee per-month or per-Employee per-year basis as specified on the Order Form. The Order Form shall specify the included Employee Population and the price per additional employee above the included headcount.

4.3 Annual Aggregate. The annual aggregate Subscription Fees payable by Customer across all then-active Order Forms as of the Effective Date is {{ANNUAL_FEE_AMOUNT}}, included for reference only and updated by reference to the Order Forms then in effect from time to time.

4.4 Payment Terms. Unless otherwise set forth on an Order Form, all undisputed invoices shall be due and payable within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice. Late payments shall accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law.

4.5 True-Ups. Provider shall conduct a true-up of Customer's Employee Population at the end of each Subscription Year, and Customer shall pay any incremental fees for headcount above the included Employee Population at the per-employee rate set forth on the Order Form. True-up fees shall be invoiced within thirty (30) days of the end of the Subscription Year.

4.6 Disputed Invoices. If Customer in good faith disputes any portion of an invoice, Customer shall notify Provider in writing within fifteen (15) business days. Customer shall pay all undisputed amounts on time.

4.7 Taxes. Subscription Fees and Professional Services fees are exclusive of all taxes other than taxes imposed on Provider's net income. Customer shall be responsible for all applicable taxes in any country where the Platform is used.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND RENEWAL

5.1 Agreement Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in effect so long as any Order Form executed under it remains in effect.

5.2 Subscription Term. The Subscription Term for each Subscription shall be as set forth on the applicable Order Form. Each Subscription shall automatically renew for successive renewal terms equal in length to the initial Subscription Term unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days prior to the expiration of the then-current Subscription Term.

5.3 Effect of Order Form Expiration. Expiration or termination of an individual Order Form shall not, by itself, terminate this Agreement or any other Order Form, unless expressly stated otherwise.

5.4 Fee Adjustments at Renewal. Provider may adjust Subscription Fees for any renewal Subscription Term by providing written notice at least one hundred twenty (120) days prior to the start of the renewal Subscription Term. Any adjustment shall not exceed seven percent (7%) above the Subscription Fees in effect during the immediately preceding Subscription Year.

ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Service Levels. The Service Level commitments applicable to each Module shall be set forth in the Service Level Exhibit to the applicable Order Form.

6.2 Default Uptime. Notwithstanding Section 6.1, the Platform (including all subscribed Modules) shall be subject to a default uptime commitment of at least 99.8% measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance and force majeure events, regardless of whether such commitment is restated on the Order Form.

6.3 Payroll-Critical Windows. During the seventy-two (72) hours immediately preceding a scheduled payroll run for any Covered Country, Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain the Platform with no scheduled maintenance and shall escalate any issue affecting Payroll Data processing to a Severity 1 incident.

6.4 Support Tiers. Provider shall provide the support tier specified on the applicable Order Form. Standard support includes (a) twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week access to Provider's online help center; (b) email and telephone support during defined business hours covering each Covered Country; and (c) twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week emergency support for Severity 1 incidents.

6.5 Service Credits. Service credits for failure to meet the Service Levels shall be Customer's sole and exclusive monetary remedy for such failure, except that two (2) or more consecutive months of failure to meet the default uptime commitment shall constitute a material breach entitling Customer to terminate the affected Order Form for cause.

ARTICLE 7 — CUSTOMER DATA, EMPLOYEE PRIVACY, AND PAYROLL

7.1 Ownership of Customer Data. As between the Parties, Customer shall retain all right, title, and interest in and to Customer Data, including all Employee Data and Payroll Data. Provider acknowledges that Customer Data is the exclusive property of Customer.

7.2 Permitted Uses. Provider shall use and disclose Customer Data only as necessary to provide the Services to Customer in accordance with this Agreement and the applicable Order Form. Provider shall not use Customer Data for any other purpose, including without limitation product improvement, machine learning model training, marketing, or sale to third parties, except as expressly permitted by Customer in writing.

7.3 Controller / Processor Roles. With respect to Employee Data and Payroll Data that constitute personal data under applicable privacy laws, Customer shall be the controller and Provider shall be the processor. The Data Processing Addendum attached as Exhibit B is incorporated by reference and governs Provider's processing of personal data on Customer's behalf.

7.4 Sensitive Categories of Employee Data. The Parties acknowledge that Employee Data may include sensitive categories of personal data (such as medical information for benefits administration, immigration status, demographic data for EEO-1 reporting, and protected characteristics under federal and state employment discrimination laws). Provider shall handle such sensitive categories with the heightened safeguards set forth in the DPA and shall not access them except as necessary to operate the Module that processes them.

7.5 EEOC Recordkeeping. Provider shall configure the Platform to enable Customer to comply with applicable federal and state recordkeeping requirements, including without limitation the recordkeeping requirements imposed by Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, the Age Discrimination in Employment Act, the Americans with Disabilities Act, the Fair Labor Standards Act, and the EEOC's reporting requirements (EEO-1, EEO-3, EEO-4, EEO-5 as applicable). Customer remains responsible for the underlying compliance.

7.6 State Employment Privacy. Provider acknowledges that several states impose specific obligations on the handling of employee personal data, including Illinois (Biometric Information Privacy Act, where applicable), California (the California Consumer Privacy Act as it applies to employee personal information under the CPRA), New York (the SHIELD Act), and others. Provider shall configure the Platform to enable Customer to comply with the obligations of any state in which Customer has employees, to the extent such obligations are addressable through the configuration of the Platform.

7.7 Third-Party Payroll Providers. The Platform may transmit Payroll Data to one or more Third-Party Payroll Providers in connection with country-specific Payroll Modules. Customer shall be responsible for entering into any necessary agreement with each Third-Party Payroll Provider and for any obligations to such Third-Party Payroll Provider. Provider shall transmit Payroll Data to Third-Party Payroll Providers only as instructed by Customer through the Platform.

7.8 Cross-Border Transfers. To the extent Customer Data is transferred across borders in connection with a Covered Country, the cross-border transfer mechanism set forth in the DPA (including Standard Contractual Clauses, where applicable) shall apply.

7.9 Security. Provider shall implement and maintain reasonable and appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data, including (a) encryption in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher; (b) encryption at rest using AES-256; (c) role-based access controls aligned with the Module structure on each Order Form; (d) annual third-party penetration testing; (e) annual SOC 2 Type II audits; and (f) a documented information security program reviewed and updated at least annually.

7.10 Breach Notification. Provider shall notify Customer in writing of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to or disclosure of Customer Data without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of Provider's discovery.

7.11 Data Return and Deletion. Upon expiration or termination of an Order Form, Provider shall, at Customer's election, make Customer Data attributable to such Order Form available for export by Customer in a commercially reasonable, machine-readable format for a period of {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days, or permanently delete such Customer Data from production systems within thirty (30) days. EEO and statutory recordkeeping obligations applicable to Customer may require Customer to retain certain Employee Data on Customer's own systems following deletion.
