1.6 "HIPAA" means the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, as amended by the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act ("HITECH") and the regulations promulgated thereunder at 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164.

1.9 "Platform" means Provider's hosted electronic health record and clinical workflow software-as-a-service platform, including all Modules made available to Customer under one or more Order Forms.

1.11 "Protected Health Information" or "PHI" has the meaning set forth in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103 and includes any individually identifiable health information transmitted, maintained, or processed through the Platform on Customer's behalf.

1.12 "Service Level Agreement" or "SLA" means the service level commitments set forth in the applicable Order Form or the Service Level Exhibit attached thereto.

Service credits for failure to meet the Service Levels shall be Customer's sole and exclusive monetary remedy for such failure, except that two (2) or more consecutive months of failure to meet the clinical Module uptime commitment under Section 6.2 shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement entitling Customer to terminate the affected Order Form for cause.

ARTICLE 7 — CUSTOMER DATA, PRIVACY, AND HIPAA

7.1 Ownership of Customer Data. As between the Parties, Customer shall retain all right, title, and interest in and to Customer Data, including all PHI. Provider acknowledges that Customer Data is the exclusive property of Customer.

7.2 Business Associate Status. The Parties acknowledge that, in providing the Services, Provider shall be acting as a "business associate" of Customer, as that term is defined under HIPAA. The BAA attached hereto as Exhibit A is incorporated by reference and constitutes the binding Business Associate Agreement between the Parties. 7.3 Permitted Uses. Provider shall use and disclose Customer Data, including PHI, only as permitted by HIPAA, the BAA, this Agreement, and the applicable Order Form. Provider shall not use Customer Data for any other purpose, including without limitation product improvement, machine learning model training, marketing, or sale to third parties, except as expressly permitted by Customer in writing.

7.4 Security. Provider shall implement and maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to comply with the HIPAA Security Rule (45 C.F.R. Part 164, Subpart C), including without limitation (a) encryption of PHI in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher; (b) encryption of PHI at rest using AES-256; (c) role-based access controls aligned with the Authorized Site and Authorized User structure on each Order Form; (d) annual third-party penetration testing; (e) maintenance of HITRUST CSF certification or equivalent; and (f) a documented information security program reviewed and updated at least annually.

7.5 Breach Notification. Provider shall notify Customer in writing of any "Breach" of "Unsecured Protected Health Information" (as both terms are defined under 45 C.F.R. § 164.402) without unreasonable delay and in any event within the timeframe set forth in the BAA, which shall not exceed seventy-two (72) hours of Provider's discovery of such Breach.

Customer shall conduct any audit during normal business hours, in a manner that does not unreasonably interfere with Provider's operations, and in compliance with Provider's reasonable security protocols. The frequency of audits may be increased without limitation following a Security Incident or upon the request of any regulator with jurisdiction over Customer.

7.7 Subprocessors. Provider shall maintain a list of subprocessors that have access to PHI on Provider's Customer Trust Portal, which list is incorporated by reference into the BAA. Provider shall provide Customer with at least thirty (30) days prior written notice of any addition or change to a subprocessor that has access to PHI. Customer may object to any new or changed subprocessor on reasonable security or compliance grounds. If the Parties cannot resolve Customer's objection in good faith, Customer may terminate the affected Order Form for cause and receive a prorated refund of any prepaid Subscription Fees.

7.8 Regulator Cooperation. Provider shall cooperate with any audit, inquiry, or investigation by the Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights, any state attorney general, or any other regulator with jurisdiction over Customer's handling of PHI. Provider shall make available to Customer such information as Customer may reasonably require to respond to any such regulatory inquiry.

7.9 Data Residency. Provider shall store and process Customer Data within data centers physically located in the contiguous United States. Provider shall not transfer Customer Data outside of the United States without Customer's prior written consent.

7.10 Data Return and Deletion. Upon expiration or termination of an Order Form for any reason, Provider shall, at Customer's election: (a) make Customer Data attributable to such Order Form available for export by Customer in a commercially reasonable, machine-readable format for a period of {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days following expiration or termination; or (b) permanently delete such Customer Data from Provider's production systems within thirty (30) days of expiration or termination, with backup deletion to follow in accordance with Provider's standard backup retention schedule.
