1.1 The "Service" is the TastePilot hosted online ordering platform — your branded ordering web page, the mobile app, the third-party delivery integrations, the loyalty program engine, the customer database, the analytics dashboards, the marketing campaign tools, and any updates we ship.

1.5 "Locations" are the restaurant locations you operate that use the Service. The number of Locations covered by your subscription is set on the order confirmation email or in the dashboard.

1.6 "Third-Party Delivery Marketplace" is a third-party food delivery service (such as DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub) that we integrate with so that orders placed on the marketplace flow into your TastePilot dashboard.
