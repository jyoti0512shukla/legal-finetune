ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

We tried to keep defined terms to a minimum. Most terms are defined inline where they first appear. The terms below show up often enough that we collected them here.

1.1 "BrightStep Platform" means our cloud-based reading and math practice software, including the teacher dashboard, the student app, the parent portal, and any updates we release during the Term.

1.2 "Order Form" means the document that lists the schools, the number of students, the price, and the Start Date for your subscription. The first Order Form is attached as Exhibit A. Future Order Forms can add schools or students.

1.3 "Student Data" means any information about a student that you upload to the BrightStep Platform or that the BrightStep Platform collects from a student while they are using it. Student Data includes student names, grade levels, login IDs, reading scores, math scores, time-on-task data, and any other information that is "education records" under FERPA or "personal information" of a child under COPPA.

1.4 "Authorized User" means a teacher, administrator, or student that you authorize to use the BrightStep Platform.

1.5 "FERPA" means the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act, 20 U.S.C. § 1232g.

1.6 "COPPA" means the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, 15 U.S.C. § 6501 et seq.

1.7 "{{CUSTOMER_STATE}} Student Data Laws" means the {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 22584, "SOPIPA") and Assembly Bill 1584 (Cal. Educ. Code § 49073.1).

ARTICLE 2 — SUBSCRIPTION AND LICENSE

2.1 What you get. We give you a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the BrightStep Platform during the Term for the schools, students, and teachers listed on the Order Form. You can use the BrightStep Platform for any educational purpose tied to your curriculum.

2.2 Who can use it. You can let teachers, administrators, students, and (if a parent has given the necessary consent) parents use the BrightStep Platform. You are responsible for making sure they only use it for school purposes and follow your acceptable use policies.

2.3 What you cannot do. You agree not to (a) sell, sublicense, or rent the BrightStep Platform to anyone else; (b) try to copy or reverse engineer the software; (c) use it to build something that competes with us; or (d) remove our copyright or trademark notices.

2.4 If we need to suspend access. If we have a serious security concern or if you have not paid an undisputed invoice for more than thirty (30) days after we send a written reminder, we can suspend access. We will give you as much notice as we reasonably can. We will turn access back on once the issue is resolved. We will not suspend access during the last two weeks of any school grading period unless there is a real security emergency.

ARTICLE 3 — IMPLEMENTATION AND ONBOARDING

3.1 Setup. We will set up the BrightStep Platform for you within thirty (30) days of the Start Date. Setup includes creating accounts for your teachers, importing your school roster from your student information system, configuring single sign-on, and running a 90-minute training session for each school's lead teacher.

3.2 Setup fee. The one-time setup fee is {{IMPLEMENTATION_FEE}}, invoiced when this Agreement is signed and due within thirty (30) days.

3.3 Your part. You agree to give us a clean roster file in the format we ask for, designate a project lead at each school, and respond to onboarding questions within five (5) business days. If onboarding gets delayed because we are waiting on you, we are not responsible for that delay.

3.4 Done. Onboarding is "done" when each school's lead teacher confirms in writing (email is fine) that they can log in, see their roster, and run a sample assignment.

ARTICLE 4 — FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Subscription fee. You pay an annual subscription fee of {{ANNUAL_FEE_AMOUNT}} for the right to enroll up to four thousand five hundred (4,500) students across the schools listed on the Order Form. We invoice the subscription fee once per year, payable thirty (30) days after the invoice date.

4.2 Net 30 with school district timing. We understand that school districts pay on warrant cycles. If your warrant cycle is longer than thirty (30) days, tell us at signature and we will adjust the payment terms in writing.

4.3 Adding students mid-year. If you go above 4,500 enrolled students, we charge thirty United States Dollars ($30) per extra student per year. We do not unenroll students automatically — we send you a notice at the end of each quarter showing your enrollment count.

4.4 Renewal pricing. We can adjust the annual fee at renewal. We will not raise the per-student price by more than five percent (5%) above the prior year, and we will give you written notice at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the renewal starts so you have time to budget.

4.5 Taxes. Public school districts in {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} are generally tax-exempt. You will give us your exemption certificate when this Agreement is signed. If a tax later applies, we will invoice it separately and you will pay it.

4.6 Disputed invoices. If you think an invoice is wrong, tell us in writing within thirty (30) days. We will work it out together. You should pay the parts you do not dispute on time.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND RENEWAL

5.1 Initial Term. This Agreement runs for {{TERM_YEARS}} years from the Start Date.

5.2 Renewal. After the Initial Term, this Agreement automatically renews for one (1) year terms unless either side gives written notice that it does not want to renew at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days before the current term ends.

5.3 Termination for convenience by you. You can terminate this Agreement at the end of any school year for any reason by giving us written notice at least {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days before the end of the school year. We will refund any prepaid fees for the time after the termination date.

5.4 No early termination by us for convenience. We cannot terminate this Agreement for convenience during the Initial Term or any renewal term. We can only terminate for the reasons listed in Article 13.

ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Uptime. We aim for the BrightStep Platform to be available at least 99.5% of the time during the school year (defined as August 15 through June 15). Uptime is measured by the minute. We do not promise uptime during the summer break period; we use that time for upgrades.

6.2 What does not count as downtime. Scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance, downtime caused by your network or your single sign-on provider, downtime caused by a force majeure event, and downtime caused by you using the BrightStep Platform in a way we did not approve.

6.3 If we miss the SLA. If our uptime drops below 99.5% in any school month, you get a service credit equal to ten percent (10%) of one twelfth (1/12) of the annual subscription fee. The credit is applied to your next invoice. This is the only money remedy you get for an SLA miss. If we miss the SLA for two months in a row during the school year, you can terminate this Agreement for cause without any further notice and we will refund the prepaid portion.

6.4 Support hours. We answer email support questions Monday through Friday, 6:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Pacific Time, excluding federal holidays. We aim to give a substantive first response within four (4) business hours. For Severity 1 issues (BrightStep Platform completely down for everyone), we have an after-hours pager with a one (1) hour response target.

6.5 Maintenance. We do routine maintenance on Sundays from 11:00 p.m. to 3:00 a.m. Pacific Time. We will give 72 hours notice for any other planned maintenance.

ARTICLE 7 — STUDENT DATA AND PRIVACY

This is the most important article in this Agreement, so we kept the language plain. If anything in this Article 7 conflicts with anything elsewhere in the Agreement, this Article 7 wins.

7.1 You own Student Data. The District owns all Student Data. We do not own it, we do not have any rights to it beyond the limited permission you give us in Section 7.2, and we will not claim a right to it later.

7.2 Why we touch Student Data. You give us permission to host, store, process, and display Student Data only to provide the BrightStep Platform to you. We will not use Student Data for any other purpose. Specifically, we will not (a) use Student Data to advertise or market to students or parents, (b) sell Student Data, (c) use Student Data to build user profiles for non-educational purposes, or (d) use Student Data to train any artificial intelligence model unless you give us specific written permission for a specific project.

7.3 FERPA. We are acting as a "school official" with "legitimate educational interests" under FERPA, 34 C.F.R. § 99.31(a)(1)(i)(B). That means we are subject to all the same restrictions on use and re-disclosure of education records that apply to your own employees. We will not re-disclose Student Data to anyone except the limited subprocessors we list at brightstep.com/subprocessors, and even then only as needed to run the BrightStep Platform.

7.4 COPPA. Many of your students are under 13. We rely on you to provide the parental consent that COPPA requires for school use, and we limit our collection and use of personal information from students under 13 to what is reasonably necessary for the educational service. We do not require students to disclose more personal information than is reasonably necessary to participate in an activity.

7.5 {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} Student Data Laws. We comply with SOPIPA and AB 1584. We do not engage in targeted advertising, do not use covered information to amass a profile, and do not sell covered information. We will delete your students' covered information within forty-five (45) days of your written request.

7.6 Security. We protect Student Data with what we believe are reasonable security measures, including:
(a) encryption of Student Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher;
(b) encryption of Student Data at rest using AES-256;
(c) role-based access controls so only employees who need to see Student Data can see it;
(d) annual third-party penetration tests; and
(e) annual SOC 2 Type II audits, the report of which we will share with you under NDA.

7.7 Breach. If we discover a security incident that compromises Student Data, we will tell you in writing within seventy-two (72) hours, even if we are still investigating. The notice will tell you what happened, who we think was affected, what data was involved, and what we are doing about it. We will pay the reasonable costs of any breach notifications you have to send out under {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} law if the incident was caused by something we did wrong. We will help your team investigate.

7.8 Where Student Data lives. We store Student Data in data centers inside the contiguous United States. We do not move it outside the United States. If we ever need to change that, we will get your written approval first.

7.9 Subprocessors. We use a small list of service providers (like our cloud hosting provider and our error tracking provider) that touch Student Data. The current list is at brightstep.com/subprocessors. We give you thirty (30) days notice before we add or change a subprocessor that handles Student Data, and you can object on reasonable security grounds. If we cannot resolve your objection, you can terminate this Agreement and get a prorated refund.

7.10 Audit. Once per year, with at least thirty (30) days notice, you (or a third party you choose, under NDA) can review our security practices. We will respond to a reasonable security questionnaire. For a deeper audit (like an on-site visit), we will charge you our reasonable costs and you will follow our protocols.

7.11 Data return and deletion. When this Agreement ends, you have {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days to export Student Data using our self-service export tool at no charge. After that, we delete Student Data from our production systems within thirty (30) days. Backups are deleted within {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days as part of our normal retention cycle. We will give you a written certification of deletion if you ask.

ARTICLE 8 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

We will not use your name or your teachers' names in our marketing without asking first. We will not use any Student Data when we work on those ideas.

8.4 No implied licenses. You only get the rights this Agreement actually gives you. We are not granting you anything else by implication.

ARTICLE 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 What is confidential. Information one of us shares with the other that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential is "Confidential Information." Our Confidential Information includes our pricing, our technical architecture, and our security details. Your Confidential Information includes Student Data, your security setup, and your strategic plans for technology in your district.

9.2 What we both promise. Each of us will (a) keep the other's Confidential Information secret, (b) only use it as needed to do this Agreement, (c) only share it with employees and contractors who need to know and who are bound by similar confidentiality duties, and (d) protect it with at least reasonable care.

9.3 Exceptions. The promise in Section 9.2 does not cover information that is already public, information one of us already knew before the other shared it, information independently developed without using the other's Confidential Information, or information rightfully received from someone else with no duty of confidence.

9.4 If a court asks. If a court order or law requires one of us to disclose Confidential Information, we will tell the other side first (if we are legally allowed to) so they have a chance to object or get a protective order. We will only disclose what is legally required.

9.5 Public records. We understand that you, as a {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} public school district, are subject to the {{CUSTOMER_STATE}} Public Records Act. If you receive a public records request for our Confidential Information, you will (to the extent the law allows) tell us about the request and give us a reasonable chance to seek protective relief.

9.6 How long this lasts. The confidentiality promise lasts during this Agreement and for three (3) years after it ends. For trade secrets and Student Data, it lasts as long as the information is still a trade secret or still protected under FERPA and the other student privacy laws.

11.3 How indemnification works. The side seeking indemnification will (a) tell the other side promptly in writing, (b) let the other side run the defense (but the indemnifying side cannot agree to anything that puts an obligation on the indemnified side without the indemnified side's written consent), and (c) help out as needed at the indemnifying side's expense.

11.4 If a claim is brewing. If we think the BrightStep Platform might soon be the subject of an IP claim, we can (a) get you a license to keep using it, (b) modify it so it does not infringe but still works for you, (c) replace it with something equivalent, or (d) terminate this Agreement and refund a prorated portion of your prepaid fees.

11.5 Sole remedy. This Article 11 is the only remedy you get from us for IP infringement claims about the BrightStep Platform.
