ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Uptime Commitment. Provider shall make the Platform available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.99% during all hours, twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week. Monthly Uptime Percentage shall be calculated as the percentage of total minutes in the calendar month during which the Platform was available to process payment transactions, expressed as a percentage.

6.2 Service Credits. If Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below 99.99% in any calendar month, Bank shall be entitled to receive service credits as follows: (a) below 99.99% but at or above 99.95%: a service credit equal to ten percent (10%) of one twelfth (1/12) of the annual Subscription Fee; (b) below 99.95% but at or above 99.9%: a service credit equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of one twelfth (1/12) of the annual Subscription Fee; (c) below 99.9% but at or above 99.5%: a service credit equal to fifty percent (50%) of one twelfth (1/12) of the annual Subscription Fee; (d) below 99.5%: a service credit equal to one hundred percent (100%) of one twelfth (1/12) of the annual Subscription Fee.

6.3 Service Credits Are Not Sole Remedy. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, the service credits set forth in Section 6.2 shall be in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other rights or remedies available to Bank under this Agreement or applicable law, including without limitation Bank's right to terminate this Agreement for material breach.

6.4 Severity 1 Incident Response. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to acknowledge any Severity 1 incident (defined as any event affecting Bank's ability to process payment transactions) within fifteen (15) minutes and to provide a workaround within thirty (30) minutes.

6.5 Maintenance Windows. Provider shall not perform scheduled maintenance during the four (4) hours preceding or following the close of the major U.S. equity markets, during U.S. federal holidays, or during quarter-end or year-end financial reporting windows, except with Bank's prior written consent.

6.6 Annual Disaster Recovery Test. Provider shall conduct a full disaster recovery test of the Platform at least annually and shall provide Bank with a written summary of the results of each test, including the recovery time objective achieved, the recovery point objective achieved, and any deficiencies identified, within thirty (30) days of the test.
