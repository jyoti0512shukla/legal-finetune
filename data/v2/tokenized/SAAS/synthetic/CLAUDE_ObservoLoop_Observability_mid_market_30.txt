ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 The "Service" is the ObservoLoop hosted observability platform — log ingestion, metric ingestion, distributed trace collection, the dashboards, the query engine, the alerting engine, the SDKs and agents, and any updates we ship.

1.2 "Customer Data" is the telemetry you send us — log events, metric series, distributed traces, span attributes, alert events, dashboard configurations, query history, and any source-attribution data your agents include.

1.3 "Authorized User" is anyone you authorize to log in to the Service.

1.4 "Ingest Volume" is the metered volume of telemetry your account ingests, measured in gigabytes per month for logs and traces and in active metric series for metrics.

1.5 "Sampling" is the practice of selecting a subset of telemetry events for ingestion, configurable in the agents and the Service dashboard.

2.3 What you cannot do. You cannot reverse engineer the Service, build a competing observability platform with it, send us telemetry that contains personal data without the right to do so, or use the Service to monitor systems you do not have permission to monitor.

2.4 Suspension. We will give you a heads-up before suspending you, except in the case of a security emergency. Reasons we might suspend: you are abusing the Service, you owe us money for more than 30 days, or a court tells us to.

ARTICLE 4 — FEES AND PAYMENT

4.2 Volume metering. Your tier includes a baseline Ingest Volume. If you exceed the baseline in any month, the overage is billed at the rates on observoloop.io/pricing. Overages are billed monthly in arrears.

4.3 Sampling controls. You can configure sampling at the agent level and at the Service ingest endpoint to control your Ingest Volume. We offer guides at observoloop.io/sampling. We bill based on what you ingest, not what you generate, so good sampling can reduce your bill.

4.4 Volume alerts. We send a dashboard alert at 80% and 100% of your monthly baseline so you can adjust sampling or upgrade your tier before incurring overage.

4.5 Net 30. Annual fees are due 30 days after the invoice date. Late payments accrue 1% per month or the most the law allows, whichever is less.

ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Ingestion uptime. We aim for 99.95% monthly ingestion uptime — meaning your agents can successfully send telemetry to our ingest endpoint.

6.2 Query uptime. We aim for 99.9% monthly query uptime — meaning you can run dashboards and alerts against your ingested data. Query and ingestion are separate because we sometimes pause query temporarily during platform upgrades while ingestion continues normally.

6.3 Excluded downtime. Scheduled maintenance with 48 hours notice, your code (including misconfigured agents), third-party services we depend on, force majeure events, and your tier limits being exceeded do not count as downtime.

6.4 Service credits. If we miss the ingestion or query SLA, you get a service credit equal to (a) 5% of one twelfth of the annual subscription fee for any month between 99.9% and 99.95%, (b) 10% for any month between 99.5% and 99.9%, or (c) 25% for any month below 99.5%. Credits apply to your next invoice. They are the only money remedy for an SLA miss.

6.5 Support. Standard support is included: 24/7 access to the help center and ticketing portal, email support with a 4-business-hour first response target during business hours (Pacific Time), and 24/7 emergency on-call support for Severity 1 incidents (a complete production outage of your account) with a 1-hour first response target.

6.6 Maintenance. We do routine maintenance on Sundays from 11:00 p.m. to 2:00 a.m. Pacific Time. We give 72 hours notice for any other planned maintenance.

ARTICLE 7 — YOUR DATA AND PRIVACY

7.1 You own Customer Data. You retain all rights to Customer Data. We do not own it.

7.2 Why we touch Customer Data. We host, store, index, query, and serve Customer Data only to provide the Service to you — running your dashboards, evaluating your alert rules, and serving your queries. We will not use Customer Data for any other purpose.

7.3 No training. We will not use Customer Data — including your logs, metrics, traces, span attributes, alert payloads, or query history — to train any artificial intelligence or machine learning model. If we ever want to do anything else with your data, we will ask first.

7.4 No selling. We will not sell Customer Data, share it with advertisers, or include it in any commercial dataset.

7.5 Aggregate stats. We may compute and use de-identified, aggregated statistics about platform-wide usage (such as the average query latency across all customers, or the average ingest volume per host) to operate and improve the Service. Aggregate statistics do not identify you, your hosts, your applications, or any individual.

7.6 Personal data in telemetry. The Parties acknowledge that logs, traces, and span attributes may inadvertently contain personal data (user IDs, IP addresses, request headers, error messages with personal data in them). You are responsible for configuring your agents and your application to scrub or sample personal data before it leaves your environment. We provide configurable scrubbing rules at the agent and ingest level. The Data Processing Addendum at observoloop.io/dpa applies to any personal data we end up processing on your behalf.

7.7 Tenant isolation. Your Customer Data is logically isolated from every other customer's Customer Data.

7.8 Security. We protect Customer Data with TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest, role-based access controls, annual third-party penetration testing, and an annual SOC 2 Type II audit. We share the SOC 2 report under NDA on request.

7.9 Breach notification. If we discover a security incident that compromises Customer Data, we will tell you in the dashboard and by email within 72 hours of discovery.

7.10 Data residency. We host Customer Data in our United States region by default. EU and APAC regions are available on the Pro tier and above.

7.11 Subprocessors. The current subprocessor list is at observoloop.io/subprocessors. We give Pro tier customers and above 30 days notice before adding or changing a subprocessor that handles Customer Data.

7.12 Retention. By default, we retain logs and traces for 30 days and metrics for 13 months. You can change retention in your account settings, subject to the limits of your tier.

7.13 Data export and deletion. You can export Customer Data using our API at any time at no charge. After your account is canceled, we keep Customer Data available for 60 days for export. After that, we delete Customer Data within 30 days, with backup deletion completed within 90 days.
