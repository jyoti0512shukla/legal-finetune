INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT — INTERIM CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER

This INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} (the "Interim CFO"). The Company and the Interim CFO are collectively the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Company is a late-stage venture-backed technology company preparing for a potential initial public offering and seeking interim Chief Financial Officer leadership while it conducts a search for a permanent Chief Financial Officer; and

WHEREAS, the Interim CFO is a financial executive with relevant late-stage and IPO-readiness experience;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT

1.1 Engagement of Interim CFO. The Company hereby engages the Interim CFO, and the Interim CFO hereby accepts the engagement, to provide interim Chief Financial Officer services on a non-exclusive basis as set forth in this Agreement.

1.2 Scope of Services. The Interim CFO shall provide the following services (the "Services"): (a) lead the Company's finance organization on an interim basis, including oversight of accounting, financial planning and analysis, treasury, tax, and investor relations; (b) lead the Company's IPO-readiness workstream, including audit committee preparation, internal control over financial reporting (ICFR) gap assessment, S-1 financial statement preparation, and underwriter relationship management; (c) participate in board of directors and audit committee meetings; (d) support the Company's permanent CFO search process and onboarding of the permanent CFO upon hire; and (e) such other interim CFO duties as the Company's Chief Executive Officer and Board of Directors may reasonably request.

1.3 Title and Designation. The Interim CFO shall hold the title "Interim Chief Financial Officer" during the Term, but, for the avoidance of doubt, the Interim CFO shall not be a corporate officer or director of the Company and shall not be authorized to sign filings with the Securities and Exchange Commission, the Company's tax returns, or any other binding legal documents on behalf of the Company except as expressly authorized in writing by the Company's Chief Executive Officer or Board of Directors. The Interim CFO is engaged in an advisory and operational leadership capacity, not as a corporate officer.

1.4 Personal Services. The Interim CFO shall personally perform the Services and shall not delegate or subcontract any portion of the Services to any other person without the Company's prior written consent.

1.5 Best Efforts. The Interim CFO shall use the Interim CFO's best efforts to perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, consistent with the standards expected of a senior public-company-ready CFO.

1.6 Reporting. The Interim CFO shall report to {{IC_REPORT_TO}} and shall participate in regular leadership team meetings as reasonably requested by the Company.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 Term. The Term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for {{IC_TERM_MONTHS}} months thereafter, or until the Company hires a permanent Chief Financial Officer and the Interim CFO completes a reasonable handover (typically thirty (30) days), whichever occurs first.

2.2 Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate this Agreement for any reason or no reason on {{IC_NOTICE_DAYS_CONVENIENCE}} days' prior written notice.

2.3 Termination for Cause. The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice for: (a) the Interim CFO's material breach, (b) willful misconduct or gross negligence, (c) conviction of any felony or any crime involving fraud or moral turpitude, (d) any final disciplinary action by the Securities and Exchange Commission or the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board that prohibits the Interim CFO from continuing in a CFO-equivalent role, or (e) any conflict of interest that materially impairs the Interim CFO's ability to perform the Services.

2.4 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, the Interim CFO shall (a) deliver to the Company any work product, files, and materials in the Interim CFO's possession, (b) submit a final invoice for fees and expenses through the termination date, and (c) cooperate in transferring open matters to the Company's permanent CFO or other designated personnel.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES, EXPENSES, AND COMPENSATION

3.1 Compensation Structure. The Company shall compensate the Interim CFO under a hybrid structure consisting of a monthly retainer for base interim CFO services plus an additional hourly rate for time worked above a defined cap and a contingent IPO-completion bonus.

3.2 Monthly Retainer. The Company shall pay the Interim CFO a flat monthly retainer of {{IC_MONTHLY_RETAINER}}, payable in arrears on the first business day of the following calendar month, prorated for any partial month.

3.3 Hours Cap and Excess-Hour Hourly Rate. The monthly retainer entitles the Company to up to {{IC_HOURS_PER_MONTH_CAP}} hours of the Interim CFO's time per calendar month. For any time worked in excess of the monthly cap, the Company shall pay the Interim CFO at the hourly rate of {{IC_HOURLY_RATE}} per hour, invoiced monthly with the regular monthly retainer invoice. Unused hours within the monthly cap shall not carry forward.

3.4 IPO Completion Bonus. If the Company completes an initial public offering or a direct listing during the Term or within ninety (90) days following the termination of this Agreement (other than termination by the Company for Cause), the Company shall pay the Interim CFO a one-time IPO completion bonus of two (2) times the monthly retainer amount, payable within thirty (30) days following the closing of the offering or direct listing. For the avoidance of doubt, this Section 3.4 does not entitle the Interim CFO to any equity in the Company; the IPO completion bonus is a cash bonus only.

3.5 Invoicing. The Interim CFO shall submit monthly invoices identifying the monthly retainer, any excess-hour fees, and any reimbursable expenses. Invoices shall be due and payable within thirty (30) days of receipt.

3.6 Expense Reimbursement. The Company shall reimburse the Interim CFO for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses, subject to the Company's standard expense policy. Any individual expense in excess of One Thousand United States Dollars (US$1,000) shall require pre-approval in writing.

3.7 No Additional Compensation; No Equity. The fees set forth in this Article 3 constitute the Interim CFO's entire compensation. The Interim CFO shall not be entitled to any equity grants, restricted stock units, stock options, severance, or other compensation beyond what is expressly set forth in this Article 3. The Parties expressly agree that the Interim CFO is not eligible to participate in the Company's equity incentive plan during this engagement.

ARTICLE 4 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

4.1 No Employment Relationship. The Parties expressly agree that they are creating an independent contractor relationship and that the Interim CFO is not an employee of the Company. This Agreement does not create any employer-employee, partnership, joint venture, or principal-agent relationship.

4.2 No Withholding. The Company shall not withhold any federal, state, or local income taxes, Social Security or Medicare contributions (FICA), unemployment insurance contributions, or any other employment-related withholdings from the Interim CFO's compensation.

4.3 No Employee Benefits. The Interim CFO is not entitled to any benefits provided to the Company's employees, including without limitation medical, dental, vision, life, or disability insurance; retirement plans; paid or unpaid leave; workers' compensation coverage; unemployment insurance; or any other welfare or fringe benefits. The Interim CFO is not eligible to participate in the Company's 401(k) plan, equity incentive plan, or any other Company-sponsored employee benefit plan.

4.4 Form W-9; Form 1099-NEC. The Interim CFO shall, promptly upon execution, deliver to the Company a completed and signed IRS Form W-9. The Company shall report all compensation paid under this Agreement on IRS Form 1099-NEC.

4.5 Self-Employment Taxes. The Interim CFO is solely responsible for the payment of all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, Social Security and Medicare contributions, and any other tax obligations attributable to the Interim CFO's compensation under this Agreement.

4.6 Tax Indemnification. The Interim CFO shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Company and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, demands, losses, damages, taxes, penalties, interest, fees, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of (a) any failure by the Interim CFO to pay any tax obligation under this Agreement, or (b) any claim by any taxing authority or other person that the Interim CFO is or should have been classified as an employee of the Company.

4.7 Other Engagements. Subject to Article 6, the Interim CFO is permitted to provide CFO services or financial consulting services to other companies during the Term, provided that such other engagements do not interfere with the Interim CFO's ability to perform the Services and do not create a conflict of interest.

ARTICLE 5 — CONFIDENTIALITY AND SECURITIES LAW COMPLIANCE

5.1 Confidential Information. As used in this Agreement, "Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information of the Company, including without limitation financial information, business plans, customer lists, pricing, supplier relationships, IPO planning materials, audit committee materials, internal control documentation, financial projections, draft S-1 filings, draft press releases, board materials, and any other information identified as confidential or that would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

5.2 Confidentiality Obligation. The Interim CFO shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence during the Term and at all times thereafter. The Interim CFO shall not directly or indirectly use, disclose, copy, transfer, or otherwise make available any Confidential Information to any third party other than as required to perform the Services.

5.3 Material Non-Public Information; Insider Trading. The Interim CFO acknowledges that, in the course of providing the Services, the Interim CFO will have access to material non-public information about the Company. The Interim CFO acknowledges and agrees that the Interim CFO is subject to the Company's Insider Trading Policy (as in effect from time to time) and to the federal securities laws, including the prohibitions on insider trading and tipping under Section 10(b) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 and Rule 10b-5 thereunder. The Interim CFO shall not trade in the Company's securities while in possession of material non-public information about the Company, and shall not communicate any material non-public information to any other person who might trade on the basis of such information.

5.4 Regulation FD Awareness. The Interim CFO acknowledges that the Company may become subject to Regulation FD upon completion of an initial public offering, and agrees to participate in the Company's Reg FD training and to comply with the Company's Reg FD policies in any communications with securities analysts, institutional investors, or holders of the Company's securities.

5.5 Return of Materials. Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Interim CFO shall promptly return all Confidential Information and materials to the Company.

5.6 Injunctive Relief. The Interim CFO acknowledges that any breach of this Article 5 would cause the Company irreparable harm, and that the Company shall be entitled to injunctive relief without the necessity of posting bond.

ARTICLE 6 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS AND CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

6.1 Non-Competition During Engagement. During the Term, the Interim CFO shall not, directly or indirectly, provide CFO services or interim CFO services to any person or entity that competes directly with the Company in the Company's primary line of business, except as expressly permitted by the Company in writing.

6.2 Non-Solicitation. During the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months following the termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Interim CFO shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any senior employee of the Company with whom the Interim CFO had material contact, except for general solicitations not directed at the Company.

6.3 Conflicts of Interest. The Interim CFO shall promptly notify the Company in writing of any actual or potential conflict of interest, including any proposed engagement with a competitor, customer, vendor, or other party with which the Company has a material business relationship.

6.4 Auditor Independence. The Interim CFO acknowledges that the Company will engage an independent audit firm in connection with the Company's IPO-readiness work, and that the Interim CFO's service to the Company must not impair the auditor's independence under SEC and PCAOB independence rules. The Interim CFO shall not provide services to the Company's audit firm, and shall promptly disclose any prior or current relationship with the Company's audit firm to the Company's audit committee.

ARTICLE 7 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

7.1 Authority. The Interim CFO represents and warrants that the Interim CFO has full right, power, and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform the Services.

7.2 No Conflicts. The Interim CFO represents and warrants that the Interim CFO is not party to any agreement that would prevent the Interim CFO from entering into this Agreement or performing the Services.

7.3 Professional Qualifications. The Interim CFO represents and warrants that the Interim CFO has the professional qualifications and experience necessary to serve as an Interim Chief Financial Officer for a late-stage IPO-track technology company.

7.4 Compliance with Law. The Interim CFO will, in performing the Services, comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, including without limitation the federal securities laws, the Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002, and the Public Company Accounting Reform and Investor Protection Act of 2002.

7.5 No Disqualification Events. The Interim CFO represents and warrants that no "disqualifying event" within the meaning of Rule 506(d) of Regulation D under the Securities Act of 1933 has occurred with respect to the Interim CFO. The Interim CFO shall promptly notify the Company in writing if any such disqualifying event occurs during the Term.

ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION AND INSURANCE

8.1 Indemnification by Interim CFO. In addition to the tax indemnification in Section 4.6, the Interim CFO shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Company and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, demands, losses, damages, liabilities, penalties, fees, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of (a) any breach by the Interim CFO of any representation, warranty, or covenant in this Agreement, or (b) any willful misconduct or gross negligence by the Interim CFO.

8.2 D&O Coverage. To the extent the Interim CFO performs services that are typically performed by a director or officer of the Company and that fall within the scope of the Company's directors and officers liability insurance policy, the Company shall, in its discretion, name the Interim CFO as an "insured person" or "additional insured" under the D&O policy for the duration of the Term, subject to the policy's terms, conditions, and exclusions. The Company makes no representation as to whether the D&O policy will cover any particular claim.

8.3 Professional Liability Insurance. The Interim CFO shall maintain professional liability / errors and omissions insurance with coverage limits of at least Two Million United States Dollars (US$2,000,000) per claim and in the aggregate, and shall provide the Company with a certificate of insurance upon request.

8.4 Limitation. The Interim CFO's aggregate liability under Article 4 (tax indemnification) and this Article 8 shall not exceed three (3) times the total compensation paid to the Interim CFO under this Agreement, except for willful misconduct, fraud, or any indemnification obligation arising under Section 4.6 (which shall be uncapped).

9.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

9.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

9.3 Equitable Relief. The Company may seek equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 5 (Confidentiality and Securities Law) or Article 6 (Restrictive Covenants).

9.4 Waiver of Jury Trial. EACH PARTY HEREBY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY.

ARTICLE 10 — MISCELLANEOUS

10.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.

10.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

10.3 Assignment. The Interim CFO may not assign this Agreement or delegate any of the Services. The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor.

10.4 Survival. Articles 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10 shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

10.5 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.
