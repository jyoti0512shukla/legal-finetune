The Company is hiring the Virtual Assistant as an independent contractor to provide remote administrative support. Plain English terms below.

ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT

1.1 The Company is engaging the Virtual Assistant to provide remote administrative support, including: email triage, calendar management, travel booking, document formatting, expense reporting, customer follow-ups, social media scheduling, and other administrative tasks as the Company reasonably requests.

1.2 The Virtual Assistant will work approximately {{IC_HOURS_PER_MONTH_CAP}} hours per month during the Term.

1.3 The Virtual Assistant will work remotely using the Virtual Assistant's own computer and internet connection.

1.4 The Virtual Assistant will personally perform the work and will not delegate or subcontract.

1.5 The Virtual Assistant will use best efforts to perform the work in a professional, reliable manner.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 The Term begins on the Effective Date and continues until either party terminates it.

2.2 Either party can terminate for convenience on {{IC_NOTICE_DAYS_CONVENIENCE}} days' written notice.

2.3 The Company can terminate immediately for material breach, fraud, dishonesty, or any breach of confidentiality.

2.4 On termination, the Virtual Assistant returns any Company materials, credentials, or data and submits a final invoice.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES, EXPENSES, AND COMPENSATION

3.1 Hourly rate. The Company will pay the Virtual Assistant at the hourly rate of {{IC_HOURLY_RATE}} per hour for time worked.

3.2 Time tracking. The Virtual Assistant will track time using the Company's preferred time-tracking tool (Toggl, Harvest, or similar) or by submitting weekly time logs.

3.3 Invoicing. The Virtual Assistant will invoice the Company monthly. Invoices are due net 15.

3.4 Expense reimbursement. The Company will reimburse the Virtual Assistant for pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses (paid software the Company asks the Virtual Assistant to use, courier fees, etc.). Any expense over One Hundred United States Dollars (US$100) requires written pre-approval.

3.5 No equity, no bonuses, no benefits.

ARTICLE 4 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

4.1 The Virtual Assistant is an independent contractor, not an employee. No employer-employee, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship is created by this Agreement.

4.2 No tax withholding. The Company will not withhold any taxes. The Virtual Assistant is responsible for all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, Social Security, and Medicare contributions.

4.3 No employee benefits. The Virtual Assistant is not entitled to any Company employee benefits — no medical, dental, retirement, paid leave, workers' compensation, or unemployment insurance.

4.4 W-9 and 1099. The Virtual Assistant will provide a Form W-9. The Company will report compensation on Form 1099-NEC.

4.5 Virtual Assistant's control. The Virtual Assistant determines how, when, and where to do the work. The Virtual Assistant is free to provide virtual assistance to other clients during this engagement. The Virtual Assistant uses the Virtual Assistant's own equipment and software (except for any Company-provided software the Company licenses for the Virtual Assistant's use during the engagement).

4.6 Tax indemnification. The Virtual Assistant indemnifies the Company against any tax authority claim or reclassification claim.

ARTICLE 5 — WORK PRODUCT AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 Any work product the Virtual Assistant creates for the Company under this Agreement (including drafted emails, formatted documents, spreadsheets, scheduled posts) belongs to the Company.

5.2 The Virtual Assistant assigns all such Work Product to the Company.

5.3 The Virtual Assistant retains rights in the Virtual Assistant's pre-existing templates and methods, with a non-exclusive perpetual license to the Company for any such materials incorporated into the Work Product.

ARTICLE 6 — CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 The Virtual Assistant will hold all Company Confidential Information in strict confidence during this engagement and after.

6.2 Confidential Information includes the Company's customer lists, financial information, internal communications, calendar data, vendor relationships, and anything else the Virtual Assistant learns about the Company in the course of the work.

6.3 The Virtual Assistant will not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Company's work.

6.4 On termination, the Virtual Assistant deletes all Confidential Information and returns or surrenders Company credentials.

6.5 Particular care for personal data. Because the Virtual Assistant has access to the Company's personnel calendars, email, and contact lists, the Virtual Assistant agrees to handle this personal data with strict confidence and to comply with any Company privacy policies the Company shares.

6.6 The Virtual Assistant agrees that breach of this Article 6 would cause irreparable harm and that the Company can seek injunctive relief.

ARTICLE 7 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

7.1 The Virtual Assistant is free to provide virtual assistance to other clients during this engagement.

7.2 During the Term and for six (6) months after, the Virtual Assistant will not solicit any Company employee or contractor to leave the Company.

7.3 The Virtual Assistant will not use any Company Confidential Information when working for other clients.

ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION

The Virtual Assistant indemnifies the Company for breach of confidentiality, gross negligence, or willful misconduct, in addition to the tax indemnification in Article 4.

The Virtual Assistant's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the total fees paid in the prior twelve (12) months, except for tax indemnification, breach of confidentiality, willful misconduct, and fraud (uncapped).
