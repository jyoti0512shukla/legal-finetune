INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR ORDER FORM — BOARD ADVISOR

This Independent Contractor Order Form (this "Order Form" or "Agreement") is dated {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} and incorporates the terms set forth in the Schedules attached hereto. This Order Form is the Company's standard form for board advisor engagements. The Company is {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} ({{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}}), and the Advisor is {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}}.

This Order Form is structured as a modular form: the substantive terms are in the attached Schedules. Where the Schedules conflict with the body of this Order Form, the Schedules control.

ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT

1.1 The Company is engaging the Advisor to serve in an advisory capacity as a member of the Company's Advisory Board. The Advisor's role is set forth in Schedule 1.

1.2 The Advisor will personally perform the advisory services and will not delegate or subcontract.

1.3 The Advisor is not a director or officer of the Company. The Advisor is engaged solely as an Advisor and shall not represent that the Advisor has any authority to bind the Company.

1.4 Best efforts. The Advisor will use the Advisor's best efforts to provide thoughtful, candid advice to the Company in the Advisor's areas of expertise.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 Term. The Advisor's term as a board advisor commences on the Effective Date and continues for {{IC_TERM_MONTHS}} months thereafter, unless extended by mutual written agreement.

2.2 Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate at any time on {{IC_NOTICE_DAYS_CONVENIENCE}} days' written notice.

2.3 Termination for Cause. The Company may terminate immediately for material breach, willful misconduct, conflict of interest, or any disqualification from serving on a board.

2.4 Effect of Termination. The Advisor's role as a board advisor ends on the termination date. Unvested equity grants are forfeited per Schedule 3.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES, EXPENSES, AND COMPENSATION

3.1 Compensation Structure. The Advisor's compensation under this Agreement consists of (a) cash compensation as set forth in Schedule 2 and (b) equity compensation as set forth in Schedule 3 (under a separate Restricted Stock Unit grant agreement or Option grant agreement).

3.2 Cash Component. Cash compensation, if any, is set forth in Schedule 2.

3.3 Equity Component. The equity component is granted under the Company's equity incentive plan and is governed by a separate equity grant agreement. The equity grant typically vests over a period of years and is forfeited on termination, subject to the terms of the equity grant agreement.

3.4 Expense Reimbursement. The Company reimburses pre-approved travel and out-of-pocket expenses for advisory board meetings and Company events the Advisor attends, with any individual expense above Five Hundred United States Dollars (US$500) requiring written pre-approval.

3.5 No Hourly Rate. There is no hourly rate or invoicing under this Agreement. The Advisor's compensation is the cash and equity components in Sections 3.1-3.3.

ARTICLE 4 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

4.1 No Employment Relationship. The Advisor is an independent contractor, not an employee of the Company. No employer-employee, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship is created by this Agreement.

4.2 No Withholding. Cash compensation paid to the Advisor is paid without tax withholding.

4.3 No Employee Benefits. The Advisor is not entitled to any Company employee benefits.

4.4 Form W-9; Form 1099-NEC. The Advisor shall provide a Form W-9. The Company shall report cash compensation on Form 1099-NEC. Equity compensation is reported separately when income is recognized for tax purposes (typically on RSU vesting or option exercise) and may be subject to Form 1099-MISC, Form 1099-B, or other applicable tax forms.

4.5 Self-Employment Taxes. The Advisor is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, Social Security and Medicare contributions, and any tax obligations arising out of the Advisor's compensation.

4.6 Tax Indemnification. The Advisor indemnifies the Company against any reclassification claim or tax authority claim relating to the Advisor's compensation.

ARTICLE 5 — CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 The Advisor shall hold all Company Confidential Information in strict confidence during the Term and at all times thereafter.

5.2 Confidential Information includes business plans, financial information, customer lists, product roadmap, financial projections, board materials, and any non-public information shared with the Advisor in connection with the advisory role.

5.3 The Advisor shall not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than serving as an Advisor.

5.4 Material Non-Public Information. To the extent the Company is or becomes a publicly traded company, the Advisor acknowledges that the Advisor will be subject to the federal securities laws, including the prohibitions on insider trading, and shall not trade in the Company's securities while in possession of material non-public information.

5.5 Return of Materials. On termination, the Advisor returns all Confidential Information and surrenders any Company materials.

5.6 Injunctive Relief. The Company may seek injunctive relief without bond.

ARTICLE 6 — CONFLICTS OF INTEREST AND RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

6.1 Conflict Disclosure. The Advisor shall disclose to the Company any actual or potential conflict of interest, including any board position, advisory role, equity holding, or consulting engagement with another company in the Company's industry.

6.2 No Direct Competitors. During the Term, the Advisor shall not serve as a board advisor or board member of any direct competitor of the Company in the Company's primary line of business, except as expressly permitted by the Company in writing.

6.3 Non-Solicitation. During the Term and for twelve (12) months after, the Advisor shall not solicit any Company employee or contractor.

6.4 Other Engagements. Subject to the foregoing, the Advisor is free to serve as a board advisor, consultant, or board member for non-competing companies during the Term.

ARTICLE 7 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

7.1 Authority. The Advisor has full authority to enter this Agreement.

7.2 No Conflicts. The Advisor is not subject to any agreement that would prevent the Services.

7.3 No Disqualification. The Advisor has not been disqualified from serving on a board of directors or as a board advisor by any regulatory agency or court order.

ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION AND D&O COVERAGE

8.1 D&O Coverage. To the extent the Company maintains directors and officers liability insurance covering the Company's board advisors, the Company shall name the Advisor as an "insured person" under the D&O policy for the duration of the Term, subject to the policy's terms and conditions.

8.2 Indemnification by Company. The Company shall indemnify the Advisor for any third-party claim arising out of the Advisor's advisory services, to the same extent the Company indemnifies its directors under the Company's bylaws and indemnification agreements, except for claims arising out of the Advisor's gross negligence, willful misconduct, fraud, or breach of this Agreement.

8.3 Indemnification by Advisor. The Advisor indemnifies the Company against any breach of representations, willful misconduct, gross negligence, and breach of confidentiality (in addition to the tax indemnification in Article 4).

8.4 Limitation. The Advisor's aggregate liability under Article 4 and Article 8 shall not exceed the total cash compensation paid to the Advisor under this Agreement, except for tax indemnification, breach of confidentiality, willful misconduct, and fraud (uncapped).

ARTICLE 9 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GOVERNING LAW

9.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

9.2 Exclusive Venue. Disputes shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

9.3 Equitable Relief. The Company may seek equitable relief to enforce confidentiality and conflict-of-interest provisions.

10.2 Amendment in writing.
