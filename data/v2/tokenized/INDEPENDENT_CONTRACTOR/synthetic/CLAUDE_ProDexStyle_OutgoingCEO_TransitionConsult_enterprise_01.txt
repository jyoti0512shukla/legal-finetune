INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT — POST-RESIGNATION TRANSITION CONSULTING

This INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} (the "Independent Contractor"). The Company and the Independent Contractor are collectively the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Independent Contractor served as the Company's Chief Executive Officer and President through the date immediately preceding the Effective Date and therefore possesses knowledge and experience of value to the Company; and

WHEREAS, the Company desires to engage the services of the Independent Contractor on a non-exclusive, short-term basis to assist with transitioning a new executive into the Chief Executive Officer position;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT

1.1 Engagement of Contractor. The Company hereby engages the Independent Contractor, and the Independent Contractor hereby accepts the engagement, to provide the transition consulting services described in Section 1.2 (the "Services") for the term and on the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

1.2 Scope of Services. The Independent Contractor shall provide professional transition consulting services, including: (a) advising the incoming Chief Executive Officer on the Company's strategic plan, customer relationships, and key vendor and supplier relationships; (b) responding to questions from the Company's Board of Directors and senior management as reasonably requested; (c) supporting the Company's investor relations function during the transition period, including participation in up to two (2) earnings calls if the Company so requests; (d) facilitating introductions to the Company's key customers, regulators, and strategic partners; and (e) such other transition-related advisory activities as the Company's Board may reasonably request and the Independent Contractor agrees to perform.

1.3 Best Efforts. The Independent Contractor agrees to use the Independent Contractor's best efforts in providing the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner.

1.4 No Subcontracting. The Independent Contractor is being engaged to perform personal services within the Independent Contractor's areas of professional expertise and shall not delegate or subcontract any portion of the Services to any other person without the Company's prior written consent. This is a personal services engagement, and the Independent Contractor's individual involvement is essential to the engagement.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 Term. The term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for {{IC_TERM_MONTHS}} months thereafter (the "Term"), unless earlier terminated as provided herein, or unless extended by mutual written agreement signed by both Parties prior to the expiration of the Term.

2.2 Termination for Convenience. The Term may be terminated for any reason or no reason by either Party upon {{IC_NOTICE_DAYS_CONVENIENCE}} days' prior written notice to the other Party. The shortened convenience-termination notice period reflects the transitional nature of this engagement.

2.3 Termination for Cause. Notwithstanding Section 2.2, the Company may terminate this Agreement immediately and without any advance notice in the event of (a) the Independent Contractor's material breach of any obligation under this Agreement, (b) the Independent Contractor's willful misconduct or gross negligence in connection with the performance of the Services, (c) the Independent Contractor's conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, any felony or any crime involving moral turpitude, or (d) the Independent Contractor's accepting an engagement with a competitor of the Company in violation of Section 6.1.

2.4 Effect of Termination. Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement for any reason, the Independent Contractor shall (a) deliver to the Company any work product, files, documents, or other materials in the Independent Contractor's possession that were created during the engagement, (b) submit a final invoice for the Services performed and reimbursable expenses incurred through the termination date, and (c) cooperate with the Company in transferring any open matters to the Company's designated personnel.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES, EXPENSES, AND COMPENSATION

3.1 Compensation Structure. The Company shall compensate the Independent Contractor under a hybrid fee structure consisting of a flat monthly retainer and an hourly rate for time worked above a defined cap.

3.2 Monthly Retainer. The Company shall pay the Independent Contractor a flat monthly retainer of {{IC_MONTHLY_RETAINER}}, payable in arrears on the first business day of the following calendar month. The monthly retainer shall be prorated for any partial month at the beginning or end of the Term.

3.3 Hours Cap and Excess-Hour Hourly Rate. The monthly retainer entitles the Company to up to {{IC_HOURS_PER_MONTH_CAP}} hours of the Independent Contractor's time per calendar month (the "Monthly Hours Cap"). For any time worked in excess of the Monthly Hours Cap during any calendar month, the Company shall pay the Independent Contractor at the hourly rate of {{IC_HOURLY_RATE}} per hour. The Company's failure to require the Independent Contractor to work the full Monthly Hours Cap in any month shall not relieve the Company of its obligation to pay the monthly retainer for that month, and unused hours within the Monthly Hours Cap shall not carry forward to any subsequent month.

3.4 Invoicing. The Independent Contractor shall submit invoices to the Company on a monthly basis, identifying (a) the calendar month covered by the invoice, (b) the monthly retainer fee due, (c) the hours worked above the Monthly Hours Cap and the resulting excess-hour fees, and (d) any reimbursable expenses incurred during the month. Invoices shall be due and payable within thirty (30) days of receipt by the Company.

3.5 Expense Reimbursement. The Company shall reimburse the Independent Contractor for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred by the Independent Contractor in performing the Services, subject to the Company's then-current vendor expense policy. Any individual expense in excess of Five Hundred United States Dollars (US$500) shall require pre-approval in writing by the Company's Chief Financial Officer.

3.6 No Additional Compensation. The fees and expense reimbursement set forth in this Article 3 constitute the Independent Contractor's entire compensation under this Agreement. The Independent Contractor shall not be entitled to any equity grants, bonus, severance, or other compensation beyond what is expressly set forth in this Article 3.

ARTICLE 4 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

4.1 No Employment Relationship. The Company and the Independent Contractor each expressly agree and understand that they are creating an independent contractor relationship, and that the Independent Contractor shall not be considered an employee, partner, agent, or joint venturer of the Company for any purpose. This Agreement does not create, and shall not be construed to create, any employer-employee relationship, partnership, joint venture, or principal-agent relationship between the Parties.

4.2 No Withholding; Self-Employment Taxes. The Company shall not withhold any federal, state, or local income taxes, Social Security or Medicare contributions (FICA), unemployment insurance contributions (FUTA), or any other employment-related withholdings from the Independent Contractor's compensation under this Agreement. The Independent Contractor is solely and exclusively responsible for the payment of all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, Social Security and Medicare contributions, and any other tax obligations attributable to the Independent Contractor's compensation under this Agreement.

4.3 No Employee Benefits. The Independent Contractor is not an employee of the Company and is therefore not eligible to participate in or receive any benefits provided to the Company's employees, including without limitation medical, dental, vision, life, or disability insurance; retirement plans (including the Company's 401(k) plan); paid or unpaid vacation, sick leave, personal leave, holiday pay, or other leave; workers' compensation coverage; unemployment insurance; or any other welfare or fringe benefits.

4.4 Form W-9; Form 1099-NEC. The Independent Contractor shall, promptly upon execution of this Agreement, deliver to the Company a completed and signed IRS Form W-9 (Request for Taxpayer Identification Number and Certification). The Company shall report all compensation paid to the Independent Contractor under this Agreement on IRS Form 1099-NEC for the applicable calendar year.

4.5 Tax Indemnification. The Independent Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Company and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, demands, losses, damages, taxes, penalties, interest, fees, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to (a) any failure by the Independent Contractor to pay any tax obligation arising out of compensation paid under this Agreement, or (b) any claim by any taxing authority or any other person that the Independent Contractor is or should have been classified as an employee of the Company rather than an independent contractor.

ARTICLE 5 — CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE

5.1 Definition of Confidential Information. As used in this Agreement, "Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information of the Company, including without limitation trade secrets, business plans, financial information, customer lists, pricing data, marketing strategies, supplier and vendor relationships, the Company's product roadmap, the Company's strategic plans, and any other information that is identified as confidential by the Company at the time of disclosure or that, given the nature of the information and the circumstances surrounding its disclosure, would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

5.2 Confidentiality Obligation. The Independent Contractor shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence during the Term of this Agreement and at all times thereafter, and shall not directly or indirectly use, disclose, copy, transfer, or otherwise make available any Confidential Information to any third party other than as required to perform the Services or as required by applicable law (in which case the Independent Contractor shall promptly notify the Company and cooperate with the Company in seeking a protective order or similar remedy).

5.3 Use Restriction. The Independent Contractor shall use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing the Services under this Agreement and for no other purpose. The Independent Contractor shall not use any Confidential Information to compete with the Company, to provide services to any other person, or for the Independent Contractor's own commercial benefit.

5.4 Return of Materials. Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement for any reason, or upon the Company's request at any time, the Independent Contractor shall promptly return to the Company all Confidential Information and all materials in any form (including any copies, notes, or derivatives) in the Independent Contractor's possession or control that contain or reflect any Confidential Information.

5.5 Injunctive Relief. The Independent Contractor acknowledges that any breach of this Article 5 would cause the Company irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate, and that the Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief and specific performance to enforce this Article 5 in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, without the necessity of posting bond or proving actual damages.

ARTICLE 6 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS AND CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

6.1 Non-Competition During Engagement. During the Term of this Agreement, the Independent Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly, accept any employment, independent contractor engagement, consulting engagement, board position, or other compensated relationship with any person or entity that competes directly with the Company in the Company's line of business, except as expressly permitted by the Company in writing. The determination of which businesses constitute direct competitors of the Company shall be made by the Company in good faith.

6.2 Non-Solicitation. During the Term of this Agreement and for a period of twelve (12) months following the termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Independent Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or independent contractor engagement any employee or independent contractor of the Company with whom the Independent Contractor had material contact during the Term, except for general solicitations not directed at the Company's personnel.

6.3 Conflicts of Interest. The Independent Contractor shall promptly notify the Company in writing of any actual or potential conflict of interest that arises during the Term, including any proposed engagement with a vendor of, customer of, or competitor of the Company. The Company shall have the right to disapprove any such engagement on conflict-of-interest grounds, and the Independent Contractor's continuation of any such engagement after the Company's disapproval shall constitute Cause for termination under Section 2.3.

6.4 Customer-Imposed Conditions Flow-Down. The Company's customers and vendors may from time to time impose restrictions or conditions, including conditions of confidentiality and security clearance, on the Company and on personnel working with the Company. The Independent Contractor agrees that, upon written notice from the Company of any such customer-imposed condition that applies to the Services, the Independent Contractor shall accept and comply with the condition as if it were a term of this Agreement.

6.5 Other Engagements. Subject to Sections 6.1, 6.2, and 6.3, the Independent Contractor is free to perform services for other clients during the Term, provided that the Independent Contractor's other engagements do not interfere with the Independent Contractor's ability to perform the Services under this Agreement.

ARTICLE 7 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

7.1 Authority. The Independent Contractor represents and warrants that the Independent Contractor has the full right, power, and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform the Services contemplated hereunder.

7.2 No Conflicts. The Independent Contractor represents and warrants that the Independent Contractor is not, and during the Term will not be, party to any agreement or under any obligation that would prevent or restrict the Independent Contractor from entering into this Agreement or performing the Services.

7.3 Compliance with Law. The Independent Contractor represents and warrants that the Independent Contractor will, in performing the Services, comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, rules, and regulations, including without limitation any laws applicable to the Independent Contractor's prior service as the Company's Chief Executive Officer (such as continuing trading restrictions under the Company's insider trading policy and any applicable Section 16 reporting obligations).

7.4 Insider Trading Acknowledgment. The Independent Contractor acknowledges that the Independent Contractor remains subject to the Company's Insider Trading Policy with respect to the Independent Contractor's prior service as the Company's Chief Executive Officer and with respect to any material non-public information the Independent Contractor learns or has learned about the Company. The Independent Contractor shall not trade in the Company's securities while in possession of material non-public information.

ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Indemnification by Independent Contractor. In addition to the tax indemnification in Section 4.5, the Independent Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Company and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, demands, losses, damages, liabilities, penalties, fines, fees, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to (a) any breach by the Independent Contractor of any representation, warranty, or covenant in this Agreement, or (b) any willful misconduct or gross negligence by the Independent Contractor in performing the Services.

8.2 Limitation. The Independent Contractor's aggregate liability under Article 4 (tax indemnification) and this Article 8 shall not exceed three (3) times the total compensation paid to the Independent Contractor under this Agreement, except in the case of willful misconduct, fraud, or any indemnification obligation arising under Section 4.5 (which shall be uncapped).

8.3 No Indemnification by Company. The Company shall have no indemnification obligation to the Independent Contractor under this Agreement, except as may be required by the Company's certificate of incorporation, bylaws, or any director and officer indemnification agreement that survives the Independent Contractor's prior service as the Company's Chief Executive Officer (which obligation, if any, shall continue in accordance with its terms).

ARTICLE 9 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GOVERNING LAW

9.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

9.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Services shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, and each Party hereby consents to the personal jurisdiction of such courts.

9.3 Equitable Relief. Notwithstanding Section 9.2, the Company may seek equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 5 (Confidentiality), Article 6 (Restrictive Covenants), or Article 7 (Representations and Warranties) of this Agreement.

9.4 Waiver of Jury Trial. EACH PARTY HEREBY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

ARTICLE 10 — MISCELLANEOUS

10.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous oral or written agreements, understandings, and representations between the Parties relating to the subject matter hereof, except for any director and officer indemnification agreement and any equity award agreements that survived the Independent Contractor's prior service as the Company's Chief Executive Officer (which shall continue in accordance with their terms).

10.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

10.3 Assignment. The Independent Contractor may not assign this Agreement or delegate any of the Services. The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor that assumes the obligations hereunder.

10.4 Survival. Articles 4 (Independent Contractor Relationship), 5 (Confidentiality), 6 (Restrictive Covenants), 7 (Representations and Warranties), 8 (Indemnification), 9 (Dispute Resolution and Governing Law), and 10 (Miscellaneous) shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

10.5 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect, and the invalid or unenforceable provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

10.6 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.
