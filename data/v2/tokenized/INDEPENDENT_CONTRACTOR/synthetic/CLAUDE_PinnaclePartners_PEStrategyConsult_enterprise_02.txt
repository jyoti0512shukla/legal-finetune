INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT — STRATEGY CONSULTING (PE PORTFOLIO COMPANY)

This INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} (the "Consultant"). The Company and the Consultant are collectively the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Company is a private equity-backed portfolio company seeking strategic advisory support during a value-creation initiative; and

WHEREAS, the Consultant is an experienced strategy professional with relevant industry expertise;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT

1.1 Engagement of Consultant. The Company hereby engages the Consultant, and the Consultant hereby accepts the engagement, to provide the strategy consulting services described in Section 1.2 (the "Services") for the term and on the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

1.2 Scope of Services. The Consultant shall provide professional strategy consulting services, including: (a) leading the Company's value-creation playbook development, including identification of revenue growth, margin expansion, and operational efficiency initiatives; (b) facilitating quarterly strategic planning workshops with the Company's executive leadership team; (c) preparing materials for the Company's quarterly board of directors meetings on initiative progress; (d) coordinating with the Company's private equity sponsor on strategic initiatives; and (e) such other strategy advisory activities as the Company's Chief Executive Officer and the Consultant may agree from time to time.

1.3 Project Plan. The Services shall be performed against a written project plan that the Consultant shall develop and submit to the Company within fifteen (15) business days of the Effective Date. The project plan shall identify the specific value-creation initiatives, milestones, and target completion dates. The project plan shall be subject to the Company's reasonable approval and may be updated from time to time by mutual agreement.

1.4 Best Efforts. The Consultant agrees to use the Consultant's best efforts in providing the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with the standards of senior strategy consultants serving comparable engagements.

1.5 Personal Services; No Subcontracting. The Consultant is being engaged to perform personal services within the Consultant's specific areas of professional expertise, and the Consultant shall not delegate or subcontract any portion of the Services to any other person without the Company's prior written consent. The Consultant's individual involvement is essential to this engagement.

1.6 Reporting. The Consultant shall report directly to {{IC_REPORT_TO}} and shall provide the Company with monthly written status updates summarizing the Services performed during the prior month, the milestones achieved, and the activities planned for the following month.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 Term. The term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for {{IC_TERM_MONTHS}} months thereafter (the "Term"), unless earlier terminated as provided herein, or unless extended by mutual written agreement.

2.2 Termination for Convenience. The Term may be terminated for any reason or no reason by either Party upon {{IC_NOTICE_DAYS_CONVENIENCE}} days' prior written notice to the other Party. The Company may, at its option, pay the Consultant in lieu of notice for any unworked portion of the notice period.

2.3 Termination for Cause. Notwithstanding Section 2.2, the Company may terminate this Agreement immediately and without any advance notice in the event of (a) the Consultant's material breach of any obligation under this Agreement, (b) the Consultant's willful misconduct or gross negligence, (c) the Consultant's conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, any felony or any crime involving moral turpitude, or (d) the Consultant's accepting an engagement that violates Section 6.1 or that creates an unwaivable conflict of interest.

2.4 Effect of Termination. Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Consultant shall (a) deliver to the Company any work product, files, documents, or materials in the Consultant's possession that were created during the engagement, (b) submit a final invoice for fees and expenses through the termination date, and (c) cooperate with the Company in transferring any open initiatives to the Company's designated personnel.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES, EXPENSES, AND COMPENSATION

3.1 Fixed Fee with Milestone Payments. The Company shall compensate the Consultant under a fixed-fee structure with milestone-based payments. The total fixed fee for the Services performed during the Term shall be {{IC_FIXED_FEE}} (the "Total Fee"), payable in accordance with the milestone schedule in Section 3.2.

3.2 Milestone Payment Schedule. The Total Fee shall be invoiced and payable as follows:

(a) Twenty percent (20%) of the Total Fee shall be invoiced upon execution of this Agreement and payable within thirty (30) days of the date of the invoice.

(b) Twenty percent (20%) shall be invoiced upon the Company's acceptance of the project plan under Section 1.3 and payable within thirty (30) days.

(c) Twenty percent (20%) shall be invoiced upon completion of the first quarterly strategic planning workshop under Section 1.2(b), payable within thirty (30) days.

(d) Twenty percent (20%) shall be invoiced upon completion of the mid-Term board materials presentation under Section 1.2(c), payable within thirty (30) days.

(e) The remaining twenty percent (20%) shall be invoiced upon expiration or termination of the Term, payable within thirty (30) days.

3.3 Expense Reimbursement. The Company shall reimburse the Consultant for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred in performing the Services, subject to the Company's standard expense policy. Any individual expense in excess of One Thousand United States Dollars (US$1,000) shall require pre-approval in writing by the Company.

3.4 No Additional Compensation. The Total Fee and expense reimbursement constitute the Consultant's entire compensation under this Agreement. The Consultant shall not be entitled to any equity, bonus, severance, or other compensation beyond what is expressly set forth in this Article 3.

ARTICLE 4 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

4.1 No Employment Relationship. The Parties expressly agree and acknowledge that they are creating an independent contractor relationship, and that the Consultant shall not be considered an employee, partner, agent, or joint venturer of the Company for any purpose. This Agreement does not create any employer-employee relationship, partnership, joint venture, or principal-agent relationship.

4.2 Consultant's Control. The Consultant shall determine the means and methods by which the Services are performed. The Consultant is not subject to the Company's day-to-day supervision in the performance of the Services, although the Consultant shall reasonably accommodate the Company's scheduling requests for meetings, workshops, and board presentations.

4.3 No Withholding. The Company shall not withhold any federal, state, or local income taxes, Social Security or Medicare contributions, unemployment insurance contributions, or any other employment-related withholdings from the Consultant's compensation under this Agreement. The Consultant is solely responsible for the payment of all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, Social Security and Medicare contributions, and any other tax obligations attributable to the Consultant's compensation.

4.4 No Employee Benefits. The Consultant is not entitled to any benefits provided to the Company's employees, including without limitation medical, dental, vision, life, or disability insurance; retirement plans; paid or unpaid leave; workers' compensation coverage; unemployment insurance; or any other welfare or fringe benefits.

4.5 Form W-9; Form 1099-NEC. The Consultant shall, promptly upon execution of this Agreement, deliver to the Company a completed and signed IRS Form W-9. The Company shall report all compensation paid to the Consultant under this Agreement on IRS Form 1099-NEC.

4.6 Tax Indemnification. The Consultant shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Company and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, demands, losses, damages, taxes, penalties, interest, fees, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of (a) any failure by the Consultant to pay any tax obligation arising out of compensation under this Agreement, or (b) any claim by any taxing authority or other person that the Consultant is or should have been classified as an employee of the Company.

4.7 Other Engagements. The Consultant is free to perform consulting services for other clients during the Term, subject to the restrictions in Article 6, provided that the Consultant's other engagements do not interfere with the Consultant's ability to perform the Services.

ARTICLE 5 — WORK PRODUCT AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 Definitions. As used in this Article 5:

(a) "Work Product" means all reports, presentations, analyses, models, methodologies, frameworks, recommendations, and other deliverables created by the Consultant in the course of performing the Services for the Company under this Agreement.

(b) "Background IP" means any intellectual property owned or controlled by the Consultant prior to the Effective Date, or independently developed by the Consultant outside of and without reference to the Services.

5.2 Assignment of Work Product. The Consultant hereby assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all copyrights, patent rights, trademark rights, trade secret rights, and other intellectual property rights therein. The Work Product shall be deemed "work made for hire" within the meaning of Section 101 of the United States Copyright Act to the maximum extent permitted by law; to the extent any Work Product does not qualify as "work made for hire," the Consultant hereby assigns all such Work Product to the Company under the foregoing assignment.

5.3 Background IP Retained by Consultant. The Consultant retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Consultant's Background IP. The Consultant grants the Company a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide license to use any Background IP that the Consultant incorporates into the Work Product, solely to the extent necessary for the Company to use the Work Product for its intended purpose.

5.4 Moral Rights Waiver. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the Consultant waives any moral rights the Consultant may have in the Work Product.

5.5 Representations and Warranties. The Consultant represents and warrants that (a) the Consultant has full authority to assign the Work Product to the Company, (b) the Work Product will be the original work of the Consultant, and (c) the Work Product will not infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party.

5.6 Further Assurances. The Consultant agrees to execute any additional documents and take any additional actions reasonably requested by the Company to perfect or evidence the Company's ownership of the Work Product.

ARTICLE 6 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS AND CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

6.1 Non-Competition During Engagement. During the Term, the Consultant shall not, directly or indirectly, accept any consulting engagement, employment, board position, or other compensated relationship with any person or entity that competes directly with the Company in the Company's primary line of business or with the Company's private equity sponsor's portfolio in the same industry vertical, except as expressly permitted by the Company in writing.

6.2 Non-Solicitation. During the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months following the termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Consultant shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or independent contractor engagement any employee or contractor of the Company with whom the Consultant had material contact during the Term, except for general solicitations not directed at the Company's personnel.

6.3 Conflicts of Interest. The Consultant shall promptly notify the Company in writing of any actual or potential conflict of interest that arises during the Term, including any proposed engagement with a vendor of, customer of, or competitor of the Company. The Company shall have the right to disapprove any such engagement on conflict-of-interest grounds.

6.4 Customer-Imposed Conditions Flow-Down. The Company's customers, vendors, and the Company's private equity sponsor may from time to time impose restrictions or conditions on the Company and on personnel working with the Company. Upon written notice from the Company of any such condition that applies to the Services, the Consultant agrees to accept and comply with the condition.

ARTICLE 7 — CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE

7.1 Definition. "Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information of the Company, including without limitation trade secrets, business plans, financial information, customer lists, pricing data, marketing strategies, supplier and vendor relationships, the Company's product roadmap, the Company's strategic plans, information about the Company's private equity sponsor, and any other information identified as confidential or that would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

7.2 Confidentiality Obligation. The Consultant shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence during the Term and at all times thereafter.

7.3 Use Restriction. The Consultant shall use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing the Services and for no other purpose.

7.4 Return of Materials. Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, or upon the Company's request, the Consultant shall promptly return all Confidential Information and all materials in the Consultant's possession that contain or reflect Confidential Information.

7.5 Injunctive Relief. The Consultant acknowledges that any breach of this Article 7 would cause the Company irreparable harm, and that the Company shall be entitled to injunctive relief to enforce this Article 7 without the necessity of posting bond.

ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Indemnification by Consultant. In addition to the tax indemnification in Section 4.6, the Consultant shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Company and its officers, directors, employees, agents, and the Company's private equity sponsor from and against any and all claims, demands, losses, damages, liabilities, penalties, fines, fees, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of (a) any breach by the Consultant of any representation, warranty, or covenant in this Agreement, or (b) any willful misconduct or gross negligence by the Consultant.

8.2 Limitation. The Consultant's aggregate liability under Article 4 (tax indemnification) and this Article 8 shall not exceed two (2) times the Total Fee, except in the case of willful misconduct, fraud, or any indemnification obligation arising under Section 4.6 (which shall be uncapped).

ARTICLE 9 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GOVERNING LAW

9.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

9.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

9.3 Equitable Relief. The Company may seek equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 5 (Work Product), Article 6 (Restrictive Covenants), or Article 7 (Confidentiality).

9.4 Waiver of Jury Trial. EACH PARTY HEREBY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

ARTICLE 10 — MISCELLANEOUS

10.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.

10.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

10.3 Assignment. The Consultant may not assign this Agreement or delegate any of the Services. The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor that assumes the obligations hereunder, including in connection with a sale of the Company by the Company's private equity sponsor.

10.4 Survival. Articles 4 (Independent Contractor Relationship), 5 (Work Product), 6 (Restrictive Covenants), 7 (Confidentiality), 8 (Indemnification), 9 (Dispute Resolution), and 10 (Miscellaneous) shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

10.5 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.
