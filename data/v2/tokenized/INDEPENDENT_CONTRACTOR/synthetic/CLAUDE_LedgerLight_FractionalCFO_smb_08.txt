This is a standalone independent contractor agreement for fractional CFO services. No parent agreement.

ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT

The Company is engaging the Fractional CFO to provide part-time CFO services to support the Company's finance and accounting function. Specifically:

- Monthly close oversight and review
- Financial reporting to the Company's leadership and investors
- Cash flow management and forecasting
- Accounts receivable / accounts payable oversight
- Budget development and variance analysis
- Banking and lender relationship management
- Investor reporting and equity capital coordination
- Vendor and contract review for financial implications

The Fractional CFO is in an advisory and oversight capacity, not a hands-on bookkeeping role. The Company maintains its own bookkeeper or controller for day-to-day transactions.

The Fractional CFO will personally perform the services and will not delegate or subcontract without Company written consent.

The Fractional CFO will use best efforts to perform the services in a professional manner consistent with the standards expected of a fractional CFO serving venture-backed startups.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND TERMINATION

The Term begins on the Effective Date and continues until either party terminates it.

Either party can terminate for convenience on {{IC_NOTICE_DAYS_CONVENIENCE}} days' written notice.

The Company can terminate immediately for material breach, fraud, dishonesty, conviction of a felony, or any disciplinary action by a state board of accountancy.

On termination, the Fractional CFO will (a) deliver the most recent financial reports and any in-progress work, (b) submit a final invoice, (c) return any Company materials, and (d) cooperate with the transition to the next CFO (interim or permanent) for up to fifteen (15) business days.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES, EXPENSES, AND COMPENSATION

Monthly retainer. The Company will pay the Fractional CFO a monthly retainer of {{IC_MONTHLY_RETAINER}}, payable on the first business day of each month for that month's services.

Fee structure. The retainer covers up to {{IC_HOURS_PER_MONTH_CAP}} hours per month. For time worked above the cap, the Fractional CFO may invoice at the hourly rate of {{IC_HOURLY_RATE}} per hour, subject to Company prior written approval before exceeding the cap.

Expense reimbursement. The Company will reimburse pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses (travel for board meetings, paid software subscriptions used exclusively for Company work). Any expense over Five Hundred United States Dollars (US$500) requires written pre-approval.

No equity. The Fractional CFO is not eligible to receive any equity, stock options, RSUs, or warrants in the Company under this engagement. (If the Company wishes to grant equity to the Fractional CFO, it must do so under a separate equity grant agreement, not under this Agreement.)

No bonus. The Fractional CFO is not eligible for any bonus tied to Company performance.

Late payment. Interest at the lower of 1.5% per month or the maximum legal rate applies to invoices unpaid after thirty (30) days.

ARTICLE 4 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

The Fractional CFO is an independent contractor, not an employee. No employer-employee, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship is created.

No tax withholding. The Company will not withhold any taxes. The Fractional CFO is responsible for all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, Social Security, and Medicare contributions.

No benefits. The Fractional CFO is not entitled to any Company employee benefits.

W-9 and 1099. The Fractional CFO will provide a Form W-9. The Company will report compensation on Form 1099-NEC.

The Fractional CFO determines how, when, and where to do the work, uses the Fractional CFO's own equipment (laptop, accounting software access), and is free to serve other Company clients during this engagement.

Tax indemnification. The Fractional CFO indemnifies the Company against any tax authority claim or reclassification claim relating to the Fractional CFO's compensation.

The Parties expressly intend the engagement to qualify as an independent contractor relationship under the IRS 20-factor test, and the parties' arrangements (Fractional CFO using own equipment, serving other clients, paid by deliverable rather than by employee-style salary structure, with no benefits) are consistent with that intent.

ARTICLE 5 — WORK PRODUCT AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 The Fractional CFO assigns to the Company all financial reports, models, forecasts, dashboards, vendor analyses, and other deliverables created by the Fractional CFO for the Company under this Agreement (the "Work Product").

5.2 The Work Product is "work made for hire" to the maximum extent allowed; to the extent it doesn't qualify, the Fractional CFO assigns it to the Company.

5.3 Background IP. The Fractional CFO retains all rights in the Fractional CFO's pre-existing financial models, templates, methodologies, and tools. The Fractional CFO grants the Company a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use any Background IP incorporated into the Work Product, solely as part of the Work Product.

5.4 Original work. The Fractional CFO represents that the Work Product will be original.

ARTICLE 6 — CONFIDENTIALITY AND PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT

6.1 The Fractional CFO will hold all Company financial information and other Confidential Information in strict confidence during this engagement and after.

6.2 Confidential Information includes the Company's financials, cap table, customer lists, vendor relationships, payroll, and any non-public information that comes to the Fractional CFO's attention in the course of the work.

6.3 The Fractional CFO will not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the work for the Company.

6.4 On termination, the Fractional CFO will return or delete all Confidential Information.

6.5 Material non-public information. If the Company is or becomes a publicly traded company, the Fractional CFO acknowledges that the Fractional CFO will be subject to the federal securities laws regarding insider trading and will not trade in the Company's securities while in possession of material non-public information.

6.6 The Fractional CFO agrees that breach of this Article 6 would cause irreparable harm and that the Company can seek injunctive relief without bond.

ARTICLE 7 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

7.1 During the Term, the Fractional CFO will not serve as Fractional CFO or financial advisor for any direct competitor of the Company in the same product category.

7.2 During the Term and for twelve (12) months after, the Fractional CFO will not solicit any Company employee or contractor to leave the Company.

7.3 The Fractional CFO is free to serve as Fractional CFO for non-competing companies during this engagement.

7.4 The Fractional CFO will disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest in writing.

ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION

In addition to the tax indemnification in Article 4, the Fractional CFO indemnifies the Company for any breach of this Agreement, gross negligence, or willful misconduct.

The Fractional CFO's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the total fees paid in the prior twelve (12) months, except for tax indemnification, willful misconduct, and fraud (uncapped).

The Fractional CFO will maintain professional liability insurance with coverage of at least One Million United States Dollars (US$1,000,000) per claim.
