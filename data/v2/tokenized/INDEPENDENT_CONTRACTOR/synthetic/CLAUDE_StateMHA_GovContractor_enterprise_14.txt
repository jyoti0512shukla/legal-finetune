INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT — STATE GOVERNMENT VENDOR

This INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} (the "State Agency"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} (the "Contractor"). The State Agency and the Contractor are collectively the "Parties." This Agreement is the State Agency's standard form of independent contractor engagement and shall be governed by the State Agency's negotiated terms set forth herein.

ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT

1.1 Engagement of Contractor. The State Agency hereby engages the Contractor to provide the consulting services described in Section 1.2 (the "Services") on the terms set forth in this Agreement.

1.2 Scope of Services. The Contractor shall provide professional consulting services to the State Agency's Behavioral Health Programs Division, including: (a) advising on best practices in evidence-based mental health programming, (b) supporting the State Agency's grant application development for federal SAMHSA funding opportunities, (c) facilitating stakeholder workshops with the State Agency's contracted community mental health providers, (d) drafting policy and procedure recommendations, and (e) such other related advisory services as the State Agency's Division Director may reasonably request.

1.3 Personal Services. The Contractor shall personally perform the Services and shall not delegate or subcontract without the State Agency's prior written consent.

1.4 Best Efforts. The Contractor shall use the Contractor's best efforts to perform the Services in a professional manner.

1.5 Reporting. The Contractor shall report to {{IC_REPORT_TO}} and shall provide written status reports on a monthly basis.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 Term. The Term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for {{IC_TERM_MONTHS}} months thereafter, unless earlier terminated as provided herein or extended by mutual written agreement.

2.2 Termination for Convenience. The State Agency may terminate this Agreement at any time and for any reason on {{IC_NOTICE_DAYS_CONVENIENCE}} days' prior written notice to the Contractor. The Contractor may terminate on thirty (30) days' notice.

2.3 Termination for Cause. The State Agency may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice for: (a) the Contractor's material breach, (b) willful misconduct or gross negligence, (c) conviction of any felony or any crime involving moral turpitude, (d) any conflict of interest the Contractor fails to disclose, or (e) any disqualification from State or federal contracting (including any debarment).

2.4 Termination for Non-Appropriation. The State Agency may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice if appropriations are not made available by the State legislature for the Services in any fiscal year. Termination for non-appropriation shall not give rise to any claim by the Contractor for damages or compensation beyond fees earned through the termination date.

2.5 Effect of Termination. On termination, the Contractor shall (a) deliver any work product, files, documents, or materials, (b) submit a final invoice through the termination date, (c) cooperate with the transition to State Agency personnel or another contractor, and (d) surrender any State Agency credentials.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES, EXPENSES, AND COMPENSATION

3.1 Hourly Rate. The State Agency shall pay the Contractor at the hourly rate of {{IC_HOURLY_RATE}} per hour for time spent performing the Services, subject to the not-to-exceed cap in Section 3.2.

3.2 Not-to-Exceed Cap. The cumulative compensation under this Agreement shall not exceed {{IC_FIXED_FEE}} (the "NTE Cap") without a written amendment signed by both Parties and approved by the State Agency's procurement office.

3.3 Time Tracking. The Contractor shall maintain detailed time records and shall provide them to the State Agency on request.

3.4 Invoicing. Monthly, with detail consistent with State Agency requirements, supported by State Agency vendor portal submission. Invoices shall be due and payable within forty-five (45) days, in accordance with the State's prompt payment statute.

3.5 Expense Reimbursement. The State Agency shall reimburse pre-approved travel and out-of-pocket expenses, subject to State per diem rates. Any individual expense above Three Hundred United States Dollars (US$300) requires written pre-approval.

3.6 No Additional Compensation. The fees and expenses constitute the Contractor's entire compensation. No equity, no bonus, no severance.

ARTICLE 4 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

4.1 No Employment Relationship. The Parties expressly agree that they are creating an independent contractor relationship and that the Contractor is not an employee of the State Agency, the State, or any other governmental entity for any purpose. The Contractor is not a State employee and is not entitled to State employee benefits, civil service protections, or pension contributions.

4.2 No Withholding. The State Agency shall not withhold any federal, state, or local income taxes, FICA contributions, or other employment-related withholdings from the Contractor's compensation.

4.3 No Employee Benefits. The Contractor is not entitled to any benefits provided to State Agency employees, including without limitation State health insurance, State retirement plan participation, paid leave, or workers' compensation coverage.

4.4 Form W-9; Form 1099-NEC. The Contractor shall provide a completed Form W-9. The State Agency shall report compensation on Form 1099-NEC.

4.5 Self-Employment Taxes. The Contractor is solely responsible for the payment of all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, Social Security and Medicare contributions, and any other tax obligations.

4.6 Tax Indemnification. The Contractor shall indemnify the State Agency for any tax liability arising from the Contractor's failure to pay required taxes and for any reclassification claim by any taxing authority.

4.7 No State Authority. The Contractor shall not represent that the Contractor has any authority to bind the State Agency or to commit State funds. The Contractor's role is advisory only.

ARTICLE 5 — WORK PRODUCT, INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, AND PUBLIC RECORDS

5.1 Work Product. The Contractor assigns to the State Agency all reports, recommendations, analyses, drafts of policies and procedures, presentation materials, and other deliverables created by the Contractor for the State Agency under this Agreement (the "Work Product").

5.2 Work Made for Hire. The Work Product is "work made for hire" to the maximum extent allowed; to the extent it doesn't qualify, the Contractor assigns it to the State Agency.

5.3 Background IP. The Contractor retains all rights in the Contractor's pre-existing methodologies, frameworks, and tools, with a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to the State Agency for any Background IP incorporated into the Work Product.

5.4 Public Records. The Contractor acknowledges that the State Agency is subject to the State's public records statute, and that any document or communication shared with the State Agency may be subject to public disclosure. The Contractor may mark documents containing trade secrets or confidential commercial information with a confidentiality designation, but the State Agency is not obligated to withhold any document that the Public Records Law requires to be disclosed.

5.5 Original work. The Contractor represents that the Work Product will be original.

ARTICLE 6 — CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 The Contractor shall hold all State Agency Confidential Information in strict confidence during the Term and at all times thereafter, subject to the public records exception in Section 5.4.

6.2 Confidential Information includes the State Agency's grant strategy, program operations, contracted provider performance data, and any non-public state information.

6.3 The Contractor will not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the State Agency's work.

6.4 Personal Health Information. To the extent the Contractor has access to any personal health information, the Contractor shall handle it in accordance with applicable State and federal health privacy laws (including HIPAA where applicable).

6.5 Return of Materials. On termination, the Contractor returns all Confidential Information.

6.6 Injunctive relief available for breach.

ARTICLE 7 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS AND CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

7.1 Conflicts of Interest. The Contractor shall promptly disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest, including any current or proposed engagement with a State Agency contractor, grantee, or vendor.

7.2 No Lobbying. The Contractor shall not lobby the State legislature or any other state or federal government body on behalf of the State Agency or any related party during the Term.

7.3 Non-Solicitation. The Contractor shall not solicit any State Agency employee for employment or engagement during the Term and for twelve (12) months after.

7.4 No Use of Confidential Information for Other Engagements. The Contractor shall not use State Agency Confidential Information when performing services for other clients.

ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION, INSURANCE, AND SOVEREIGN IMMUNITY

8.1 Indemnification by Contractor. In addition to the tax indemnification in Section 4.6, the Contractor shall indemnify the State Agency for any breach of representations and warranties, gross negligence, or willful misconduct.

8.2 Limitation. The Contractor's aggregate liability under Article 4 and this Article 8 shall not exceed two (2) times the cumulative compensation under this Agreement, except for tax indemnification, willful misconduct, and fraud (uncapped).

8.3 Professional Liability Insurance. The Contractor shall maintain professional liability insurance with coverage of at least One Million United States Dollars (US$1,000,000) per claim and provide a certificate of insurance.

8.4 Sovereign Immunity Preserved. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as a waiver of the State Agency's sovereign immunity or any defenses available to the State Agency under State law. The State Agency does not waive its sovereign immunity by entering this Agreement.

8.5 No Indemnification by State Agency. The State Agency may not indemnify the Contractor or any third party except to the extent expressly permitted by State law.

ARTICLE 9 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GOVERNING LAW

9.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to conflict of laws principles.

9.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute shall be brought exclusively in the state courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

9.3 Equitable Relief. The State Agency may seek equitable relief to enforce Article 5 (Confidentiality), Article 6 (Confidentiality), or Article 7 (Conflicts).

10.1 Entire Agreement.

10.2 Amendment in writing signed by both Parties (and approved by State procurement where required).

10.3 No assignment by Contractor. State Agency may assign on a State reorganization.

10.4 Survival of Articles 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10.

10.5 Counterparts.

10.6 Funding Out. The State Agency may terminate this Agreement at any time on thirty (30) days' written notice for non-appropriation of State or federal funds.
