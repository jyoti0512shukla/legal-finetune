INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT — RETAINED EXPERT WITNESS

This INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Firm"), acting on behalf of its client {{CUSTOMER_NAME}} (the "Client") in the matter described in Schedule A, and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} (the "Expert"). The Firm and the Expert are collectively the "Parties." For purposes of this engagement, the Firm is acting as outside litigation counsel for the Client, and the Expert is being retained by the Firm pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 26 and the corresponding state procedural rules.

ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT

1.1 Engagement of Expert. The Firm hereby retains the Expert, and the Expert hereby accepts the retention, to provide expert witness consulting services in connection with the litigation matter described in Schedule A (the "Matter") on the terms set forth in this Agreement.

1.2 Scope of Services. The Expert shall provide the following expert witness services (the "Services"): (a) review documents, depositions, exhibits, expert reports, and other case materials provided by the Firm; (b) form opinions within the Expert's areas of professional expertise as relevant to the issues in the Matter; (c) prepare a written expert report compliant with Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 26(a)(2)(B) (or the equivalent state rule); (d) sit for one or more depositions taken by opposing counsel in the Matter; (e) testify at trial, hearings, or evidentiary proceedings in the Matter as requested by the Firm; and (f) consult with the Firm and the Client's other expert witnesses on technical and substantive issues in the Matter.

1.3 Personal Services. The Expert shall personally perform the Services. The Expert shall not delegate or subcontract the substantive opinion-formation work to any other person without the Firm's prior written consent. The Expert may, however, retain support personnel (research assistants, technicians, etc.) to assist with non-opinion-related tasks (such as data analysis or document review), provided that any opinions or conclusions rendered in the Matter are the Expert's own.

1.4 Best Efforts. The Expert shall use the Expert's best efforts in performing the Services in a professional manner consistent with the standards of the Expert's profession.

1.5 Retaining Counsel Direction. The Expert shall report to and take direction from the Firm. The Firm is the Expert's point of contact for the Matter. The Expert shall not communicate directly with the Client or any other party in the Matter without the Firm's prior written consent.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 Term. The Term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until the conclusion of the Matter (including any post-trial motions, appeals, and remand proceedings) or until terminated earlier as provided herein.

2.2 Termination for Convenience. The Firm may terminate this Agreement at any time and for any reason on {{IC_NOTICE_DAYS_CONVENIENCE}} days' prior written notice to the Expert. The Expert may not terminate the engagement during the period beginning thirty (30) days before any scheduled deposition, evidentiary hearing, or trial date in the Matter, except for cause attributable to the Firm.

2.3 Termination for Cause. The Firm may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice for: (a) the Expert's material breach of any obligation under this Agreement, (b) the Expert's willful misconduct or gross negligence, (c) any conflict of interest that materially impairs the Expert's ability to serve as an expert witness, (d) the Expert's loss of any professional license or certification material to the Expert's qualification to opine in the Matter, or (e) the Expert's testimony, opinions, or conduct that the Firm reasonably determines is materially harmful to the Client's case.

2.4 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, the Expert shall (a) deliver to the Firm any work product, files, notes, drafts, and materials in the Expert's possession that relate to the Matter, (b) submit a final invoice for fees and reimbursable expenses through the termination date, and (c) cooperate with the Firm in transferring open work to a successor expert if one is engaged.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES, EXPENSES, AND COMPENSATION

3.1 Hourly Rate. The Firm shall pay the Expert at the hourly rate of {{IC_HOURLY_RATE}} per hour for all time spent on the Matter, including document review, opinion formation, report drafting, deposition preparation, deposition testimony, trial preparation, trial testimony, and meetings with the Firm.

3.2 Travel Time. Travel time required for the Services shall be billed at fifty percent (50%) of the hourly rate.

3.3 Retainer. The Expert acknowledges receipt of an initial retainer of {{IC_FIXED_FEE}}, to be applied against the Expert's fees as incurred. The Expert shall replenish the retainer to the initial amount upon the Firm's written request.

3.4 Invoicing. The Expert shall submit invoices to the Firm on a monthly basis, identifying the time worked, the activities performed, and any reimbursable expenses. Invoices shall be due and payable within thirty (30) days of receipt.

3.5 Expense Reimbursement. The Firm shall reimburse the Expert for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred in performing the Services, including travel, lodging, and per diem. Travel expenses for any trip in excess of Two Thousand Five Hundred United States Dollars (US$2,500) shall require pre-approval in writing by the Firm.

3.6 Fee for Testimony. The Expert's fee for time spent in deposition and trial testimony is the same hourly rate set forth in Section 3.1. The Expert shall not charge any premium fee for testimony, and the Expert shall not be paid on a contingent basis. The Expert's compensation under this Agreement is in no way contingent on the outcome of the Matter or on the substance of the Expert's opinions.

3.7 No Contingent Compensation. The Parties expressly agree that the Expert's compensation under this Agreement is not in any way contingent on the outcome of the Matter, on the substance of the Expert's opinions, or on the verdict or settlement of the Matter. Contingent fee arrangements with expert witnesses are professionally improper and may render the Expert's testimony inadmissible.

ARTICLE 4 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

4.1 No Employment Relationship. The Parties expressly agree that they are creating an independent contractor relationship and that the Expert is not an employee, partner, agent, or joint venturer of the Firm or the Client.

4.2 No Withholding. The Firm shall not withhold any federal, state, or local income taxes, Social Security or Medicare contributions, unemployment insurance contributions, or any other employment-related withholdings from the Expert's compensation.

4.3 No Employee Benefits. The Expert is not entitled to any benefits provided to the Firm's employees.

4.4 Form W-9; Form 1099-NEC. The Expert shall, promptly upon execution, deliver to the Firm a completed and signed IRS Form W-9. The Firm shall report all compensation paid to the Expert on IRS Form 1099-NEC.

4.5 Self-Employment Taxes. The Expert is solely responsible for the payment of all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, Social Security and Medicare contributions, and any other tax obligations attributable to compensation under this Agreement.

4.6 Tax Indemnification. The Expert shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Firm and the Client from any claim that the Expert is or should have been classified as an employee, and from any tax liability resulting from the Expert's failure to pay required taxes.

4.7 No Restriction on Other Engagements. Subject to Article 6, the Expert is free to serve as an expert witness in other matters and to maintain the Expert's primary professional practice during the Term.

ARTICLE 5 — ATTORNEY WORK PRODUCT AND CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Attorney Work Product Doctrine. The Expert acknowledges that the Expert is being retained by the Firm in anticipation of and for use in the Matter, and that materials and communications between the Expert and the Firm are intended to be protected by the attorney work product doctrine under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 26(b)(3) (or the equivalent state rule). The Expert shall treat all materials, drafts, notes, and communications as protected work product unless and until the Firm directs otherwise (or unless the materials become discoverable under Rule 26(b)(4) or applicable state law as part of the Expert's report or opinions).

5.2 Drafts and Notes. The Expert shall not preserve drafts of the expert report or any prior versions thereof, except to the extent required by applicable law. The Expert shall maintain only the final version of the report and the materials the Expert relied upon in forming the Expert's opinions, as required to be disclosed under Rule 26(a)(2)(B). All earlier drafts shall be deleted in the ordinary course.

5.3 Confidentiality of Case Materials. The Expert shall hold all case materials, documents, and information provided by the Firm in strict confidence. The Expert shall not directly or indirectly use, disclose, copy, transfer, or otherwise make available any such materials to any third party other than as required to perform the Services and as expressly authorized by the Firm.

5.4 Confidentiality of Opinions. Until the Expert's report is disclosed in accordance with Rule 26(a)(2)(B) (or the equivalent state rule), the Expert shall not disclose the Expert's opinions in the Matter to any third party.

5.5 Return of Materials. Upon conclusion of the Expert's work in the Matter (or earlier termination), the Expert shall return to the Firm all case materials, documents, and information provided by the Firm.

5.6 Protective Orders. The Expert shall comply with any protective order entered by the court in the Matter, and shall execute any protective order acknowledgment form requested by the Firm.

ARTICLE 6 — CONFLICTS OF INTEREST AND OTHER ENGAGEMENTS

6.1 Conflict Check. The Expert represents and warrants that the Expert has conducted a conflict check against the parties identified by the Firm and has not identified any conflict of interest that would prevent the Expert from serving in the Matter.

6.2 Continuing Conflict Disclosure. During the Term, the Expert shall promptly notify the Firm in writing of any new engagement (existing or proposed) that may create a conflict of interest with the Firm's representation of the Client in the Matter. The Firm shall have the right to direct the Expert to decline any such engagement on conflict-of-interest grounds.

6.3 Other Expert Witness Engagements. Subject to Section 6.2, the Expert is free to serve as an expert witness in other matters during the Term, provided that the Expert maintains the confidentiality of the Matter and does not develop opinions in other matters that would contradict the Expert's opinions in the Matter (which could be used by opposing counsel to impeach the Expert's testimony).

6.4 No Restrictive Covenants. For the avoidance of doubt, the Expert is not subject to any non-compete, non-solicit, or other restrictive covenant under this Agreement other than the conflict-of-interest provisions in this Article 6 and the confidentiality provisions in Article 5. Restrictive covenants are inappropriate for expert witness engagements.

ARTICLE 7 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

7.1 Qualifications. The Expert represents and warrants that the Expert has the professional qualifications, training, experience, and expertise to opine on the issues in the Matter as a qualified expert under Federal Rule of Evidence 702 (or the equivalent state rule).

7.2 No Disqualification. The Expert represents and warrants that the Expert has not been disqualified, sanctioned, or otherwise prohibited from serving as an expert witness in any prior matter, and that no professional disciplinary action is pending against the Expert.

7.3 Independent Opinions. The Expert represents and warrants that the Expert's opinions in the Matter will be the Expert's own, formed independently based on the Expert's review of the case materials and the Expert's professional expertise. The Expert's opinions will not be ghostwritten or unduly influenced by the Firm or the Client.

7.4 No Conflicting Engagements. The Expert is not currently engaged as an expert witness in any matter that would create a conflict of interest with the Matter.

ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY

8.1 No Indemnification of Expert. Consistent with the professional norms applicable to expert witness engagements, the Firm and the Client shall not indemnify the Expert against any liability arising out of the Expert's testimony or opinions in the Matter. Expert witnesses are generally afforded testimonial immunity for in-court testimony but not for pre-trial conduct or out-of-court statements.

8.2 Indemnification by Expert. The Expert shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Firm and the Client from any claim arising out of (a) the Expert's gross negligence or willful misconduct, or (b) the Expert's breach of the confidentiality obligations in Article 5 or the conflict-of-interest obligations in Article 6.

8.3 Professional Liability Insurance. The Expert shall maintain professional liability insurance with coverage limits of at least One Million United States Dollars (US$1,000,000) per claim and in the aggregate, covering the Expert's professional services as an expert witness, and shall provide the Firm with a certificate of insurance upon request.

ARTICLE 9 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GOVERNING LAW

9.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

9.2 Exclusive Venue; Arbitration. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement (other than disputes within the jurisdiction of the court hearing the Matter) shall be settled by binding arbitration in {{VENUE_CITY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}, in accordance with the Commercial Arbitration Rules of the American Arbitration Association. Arbitration is appropriate for fee and engagement disputes between expert witnesses and retaining counsel because such disputes typically involve confidential information that should not be litigated in open court.

9.3 Equitable Relief. The Firm may seek equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 5 (Confidentiality) or Article 6 (Conflicts of Interest).

ARTICLE 10 — MISCELLANEOUS

10.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.

10.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

10.3 Assignment. The Expert may not assign this Agreement or delegate the Services. The Firm may not assign this Agreement without the Expert's consent, except in connection with a merger or sale of the Firm.

10.4 Survival. Articles 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10 shall survive termination of this Agreement.

10.5 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.
