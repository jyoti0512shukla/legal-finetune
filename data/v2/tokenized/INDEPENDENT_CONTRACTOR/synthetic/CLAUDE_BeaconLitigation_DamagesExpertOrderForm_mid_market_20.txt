INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR ORDER FORM — DAMAGES EXPERT

This Independent Contractor Order Form (this "Order Form" or "Agreement") is dated {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} and incorporates the terms set forth in the Schedules attached hereto. The Firm is {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} ({{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}}), and the Damages Expert is {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}}. The Firm is acting as outside litigation counsel for its client (the "Client") in the matter described in Schedule 1 (the "Matter").

This Order Form is structured as a modular form. Substantive engagement terms are in the Schedules. Where the Schedules conflict with the body of this Order Form, the Schedules control.

ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT

1.1 The Firm hereby retains the Damages Expert to provide damages analysis and expert testimony in the Matter described in Schedule 1, in accordance with Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 26 (or the equivalent state rule).

1.2 The Damages Expert's role and the scope of damages analysis are described in Schedule 2.

1.3 The Damages Expert shall personally perform the substantive damages analysis. The Damages Expert may use research analysts and modeling assistants for non-opinion-related tasks, with the Damages Expert remaining responsible for all opinions.

1.4 Best efforts. The Damages Expert shall use the Damages Expert's best efforts in a professional manner consistent with the standards of the Damages Expert's profession.

1.5 Direction by Firm. The Damages Expert shall report to and take direction from the Firm. The Firm is the Damages Expert's point of contact. The Damages Expert shall not communicate directly with the Client or any other party in the Matter without the Firm's prior written consent.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 Term. The Term commences on the Effective Date and continues until the conclusion of the Damages Expert's role in the Matter (typically through trial, post-trial motions, and any appeals).

2.2 Termination for Convenience. The Firm may terminate at any time on {{IC_NOTICE_DAYS_CONVENIENCE}} days' written notice. The Damages Expert may not terminate within thirty (30) days of any scheduled deposition, hearing, or trial date in the Matter, except for cause attributable to the Firm.

2.3 Termination for Cause. The Firm may terminate immediately for material breach, willful misconduct, conflict of interest, loss of professional credentials, or testimony / opinions the Firm reasonably determines are materially harmful to the Client's case.

2.4 Effect of Termination. The Damages Expert delivers any work product, files, notes, drafts, and materials, submits a final invoice, and cooperates with the transition to a successor expert (if any).

ARTICLE 3 — FEES, EXPENSES, AND COMPENSATION

3.1 Hourly Rate. The Firm shall pay the Damages Expert at the hourly rate of {{IC_HOURLY_RATE}} per hour for time spent on the Matter.

3.2 Travel Time. Travel time is billed at fifty percent (50%) of the hourly rate.

3.3 Retainer. The Damages Expert acknowledges receipt of an initial retainer of {{IC_FIXED_FEE}}, to be applied against fees as incurred. The Damages Expert shall replenish the retainer to the initial amount on the Firm's written request.

3.4 Invoicing. Monthly, with detailed time records, due net 30.

3.5 Expense Reimbursement. The Firm reimburses pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses, with travel expenses for any single trip above Two Thousand Five Hundred United States Dollars (US$2,500) requiring written pre-approval.

3.6 Fee for Testimony. The Damages Expert's fee for time in deposition and trial testimony is the same hourly rate as in Section 3.1. No premium fee.

3.7 No Contingent Compensation. The Damages Expert's compensation under this Agreement is in no way contingent on the outcome of the Matter, on the substance of the Damages Expert's opinions, or on the verdict or settlement. Contingent fee arrangements with damages experts are professionally improper and may render the testimony inadmissible.

ARTICLE 4 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

4.1 No Employment Relationship.

4.2 No Withholding.

4.3 No Employee Benefits.

4.4 Form W-9; Form 1099-NEC. The Damages Expert shall provide a Form W-9. The Firm shall report compensation on Form 1099-NEC.

4.5 Self-Employment Taxes. The Damages Expert is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, Social Security and Medicare contributions.

4.6 Tax Indemnification. The Damages Expert indemnifies the Firm against any reclassification claim or tax authority claim relating to the Damages Expert's compensation.

ARTICLE 5 — ATTORNEY WORK PRODUCT, OPINIONS, AND CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Work Product Doctrine. The Damages Expert's analysis, drafts, notes, and communications with the Firm are intended to be protected by the attorney work product doctrine under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 26(b)(3) (or the equivalent state rule), to the extent permitted by Rule 26(b)(4).

5.2 Final Report. The Damages Expert's final report (and the materials the Damages Expert relied upon) are subject to the disclosure requirements of Rule 26(a)(2)(B) (or the equivalent state rule).

5.3 Drafts and Notes. The Damages Expert shall not preserve drafts of the report, except to the extent required by applicable law. The Damages Expert shall maintain only the final version of the report and the materials the Damages Expert relied upon.

5.4 Confidentiality. The Damages Expert shall hold all case materials and information in strict confidence during the engagement and at all times thereafter.

5.5 Pre-Disclosure Confidentiality of Opinions. Until the Damages Expert's report is disclosed in accordance with Rule 26(a)(2)(B), the Damages Expert shall not disclose the Damages Expert's opinions in the Matter to any third party.

5.6 Return of Materials. On termination, the Damages Expert returns all case materials.

5.7 Protective Orders. The Damages Expert shall comply with any protective order in the Matter.

ARTICLE 6 — CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

6.1 Conflict Check. The Damages Expert has conducted a conflict check against the parties identified by the Firm and has not identified any conflict that would prevent the Damages Expert from serving in the Matter.

6.2 Continuing Conflict Disclosure. The Damages Expert shall promptly notify the Firm in writing of any new engagement that may create a conflict of interest with the Matter.

6.3 Other Damages Expert Engagements. The Damages Expert is free to serve as a damages expert in other matters during the Term, subject to the conflict-of-interest provisions and the Damages Expert's confidentiality obligations.

6.4 No Restrictive Covenants. The Damages Expert is not subject to any non-compete, non-solicit, or other restrictive covenant other than the conflict-of-interest provisions in this Article 6 and the confidentiality provisions in Article 5.

ARTICLE 7 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

7.1 Qualifications. The Damages Expert has the professional qualifications, training, and experience to opine on the damages issues in the Matter as a qualified expert under Federal Rule of Evidence 702 (or the equivalent state rule).

7.2 No Disqualification. The Damages Expert has not been disqualified, sanctioned, or otherwise prohibited from serving as an expert witness in any prior matter, and no professional disciplinary action is pending against the Damages Expert.

7.3 Independent Opinions. The Damages Expert's opinions in the Matter will be the Damages Expert's own, formed independently based on the Damages Expert's review of case materials and professional expertise.

ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 No Indemnification of Damages Expert. Consistent with professional norms, the Firm and the Client shall not indemnify the Damages Expert against any liability arising out of the Damages Expert's testimony or opinions in the Matter. Damages experts are afforded testimonial immunity for in-court testimony but not for pre-trial conduct or out-of-court statements.

8.2 Indemnification by Damages Expert. The Damages Expert indemnifies the Firm and the Client for breach of confidentiality and for willful misconduct or fraud.

8.3 Professional Liability Insurance. The Damages Expert shall maintain professional liability insurance with coverage of at least One Million United States Dollars (US$1,000,000) per claim.
