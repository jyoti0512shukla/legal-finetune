INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT — REGULATED BANK VENDOR CONSULTANT

This INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Bank"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} (the "Consultant"). The Bank and the Consultant are collectively the "Parties." This Agreement is the Bank's standard form of independent contractor engagement for individual consultants providing services to the Bank's regulated business and shall be governed by the Bank's negotiated terms set forth herein.

ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT

1.1 Engagement of Consultant. The Bank hereby engages the Consultant, and the Consultant hereby accepts the engagement, to provide the consulting services described in Section 1.2 (the "Services") on the terms set forth in this Agreement.

1.2 Scope of Services. The Consultant shall provide professional consulting services in connection with the Bank's third-party risk management program, including: (a) advising the Bank's Vendor Risk Management organization on industry best practices for third-party oversight; (b) reviewing the Bank's existing third-party risk assessment methodology and recommending enhancements; (c) participating in the Bank's vendor risk committee meetings; (d) advising on regulatory expectations under OCC Bulletin 2013-29, OCC Bulletin 2020-10, and the Interagency Guidelines (12 C.F.R. Part 30, Appendix B); (e) supporting the Bank's preparation for any regulatory examination of its third-party risk management program; and (f) such other related advisory services as the Bank's Chief Risk Officer may reasonably request.

1.3 Personal Services. The Consultant is being engaged to perform personal services within the Consultant's specific areas of expertise and shall not delegate or subcontract any portion of the Services to any other person without the Bank's prior written consent.

1.4 Best Efforts. The Consultant shall use the Consultant's best efforts in performing the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner.

1.5 Reporting. The Consultant shall report to {{IC_REPORT_TO}} and shall provide written status reports on a monthly basis.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 Term. The Term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for {{IC_TERM_MONTHS}} months thereafter, unless earlier terminated as provided herein or extended by mutual written agreement.

2.2 Termination for Convenience. The Bank may terminate this Agreement at any time and for any reason on {{IC_NOTICE_DAYS_CONVENIENCE}} days' prior written notice to the Consultant. The Consultant may terminate on thirty (30) days' prior written notice.

2.3 Termination for Cause. The Bank may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice for: (a) the Consultant's material breach, (b) willful misconduct or gross negligence, (c) conviction of any felony or any crime involving dishonesty, (d) any disqualification from providing services to a federally regulated financial institution (including any 12 U.S.C. § 1818(e) prohibition order), (e) any conflict of interest the Consultant fails to disclose, or (f) any breach of the onshore-only or US person requirements in Article 4.

2.4 Bank's Right to Suspend. The Bank may, in its sole discretion, suspend the Services at any time on twenty-four (24) hours' written notice in connection with (a) any regulatory examination or supervisory action affecting the Bank, (b) any actual or suspected security incident, or (c) any other reason the Bank determines is necessary. During any suspension, the Bank shall pay for time charged through the suspension date but shall not pay for time during the suspension period.

2.5 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, the Consultant shall (a) deliver any work product, files, documents, or materials in the Consultant's possession that relate to the Services, (b) submit a final invoice for time worked and reimbursable expenses, (c) cooperate in transferring open matters to the Bank's designated personnel, and (d) immediately surrender any Bank credentials or facility access.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES, EXPENSES, AND COMPENSATION

3.1 Hourly Rate. The Bank shall pay the Consultant at the hourly rate of {{IC_HOURLY_RATE}} per hour for time spent performing the Services, subject to the not-to-exceed cap in Section 3.2.

3.2 Not-to-Exceed Cap. The cumulative compensation under this Agreement shall not exceed {{IC_FIXED_FEE}} (the "NTE Cap") without the Bank's prior written approval, which approval shall be implemented through a written amendment signed by both Parties. If the Consultant determines that the cumulative time charges will reach seventy-five percent (75%) of the NTE Cap, the Consultant shall promptly notify the Bank.

3.3 Time Tracking. The Consultant shall maintain detailed time records identifying the date, hours worked, and a description of the activities performed. The Bank shall have the right to audit the Consultant's time records.

3.4 Invoicing. The Consultant shall submit invoices to the Bank monthly, identifying the time worked, the activities performed, the cumulative spend against the NTE Cap, and any reimbursable expenses with supporting documentation. Invoices shall be due and payable within forty-five (45) days of receipt by the Bank.

3.5 Expense Reimbursement. The Bank shall reimburse the Consultant for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses, subject to the Bank's standard vendor expense policy. Any individual expense in excess of Seven Hundred Fifty United States Dollars (US$750) shall require written pre-approval by the Bank.

3.6 No Additional Compensation. The fees and expenses set forth in this Article 3 constitute the Consultant's entire compensation. The Consultant shall not be entitled to any equity, bonus, severance, or other compensation.

ARTICLE 4 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP AND BANK REQUIREMENTS

4.1 No Employment Relationship. The Parties expressly agree that they are creating an independent contractor relationship and that the Consultant shall not be considered an employee, partner, agent, or joint venturer of the Bank for any purpose.

4.2 No Withholding. The Bank shall not withhold any federal, state, or local income taxes, Social Security or Medicare contributions, or any other employment-related withholdings from the Consultant's compensation.

4.3 No Employee Benefits. The Consultant is not entitled to any benefits provided to the Bank's employees.

4.4 Form W-9; Form 1099-NEC. The Consultant shall, promptly upon execution, deliver to the Bank a completed Form W-9. The Bank shall report compensation on Form 1099-NEC.

4.5 Self-Employment Taxes. The Consultant is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, Social Security and Medicare contributions, and any other tax obligations.

4.6 Tax Indemnification. The Consultant shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Bank and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, demands, losses, damages, taxes, penalties, interest, fees, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of (a) any failure by the Consultant to pay any tax obligation, or (b) any claim by any taxing authority that the Consultant is or should have been classified as an employee of the Bank.

4.7 Onshore-Only and US Person Requirement. The Consultant shall perform all Services from facilities physically located in the contiguous United States. The Consultant shall be a U.S. person (within the meaning of relevant federal banking and export control law) and shall not delegate any Services to any personnel located outside the United States. This requirement is material to the Bank's compliance with the Interagency Guidelines and OCC third-party risk management guidance.

4.8 Background Check; Personnel Security. The Consultant has completed and shall maintain in good standing the Bank's standard vendor personnel background check, which includes criminal history, credit history (consistent with the Bank's vendor security policy), prior litigation involving fraud or financial misconduct, and screening against the OFAC Specially Designated Nationals list. The Consultant has not been the subject of any 12 U.S.C. § 1818(e) prohibition order or other order disqualifying the Consultant from providing services to federally regulated financial institutions.

ARTICLE 5 — CONFIDENTIALITY AND BANK CUSTOMER DATA

5.1 Confidential Information. All non-public information of the Bank — including without limitation customer information, customer lists, account information, transaction data, financial information, business plans, regulatory examination materials, supervisory communications, internal audit reports, vendor risk assessments, and any non-public information about the Bank's regulators or supervisory relationships — constitutes Confidential Information of the Bank.

5.2 Confidentiality Obligation. The Consultant shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence during the Term and at all times thereafter.

5.3 No Use of Customer NPI. The Consultant shall not access, view, copy, or otherwise use any Nonpublic Personal Information ("NPI") of the Bank's customers within the meaning of the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, except to the minimum extent necessary to perform the Services and only with the express prior written authorization of the Bank's Vendor Risk Management organization. Any access to NPI shall be logged.

5.4 Return of Materials. Upon termination, the Consultant shall return all Confidential Information and materials to the Bank.

5.5 Injunctive Relief. The Consultant agrees that any breach of this Article 5 would cause the Bank irreparable harm, and the Bank shall be entitled to injunctive relief without bond.

5.6 Cooperation with Regulators. The Consultant shall cooperate with the Bank's regulators (including the OCC, the Federal Reserve, the FDIC, and the CFPB) in any examination of the Bank or the Bank's third-party risk management program, on the same terms applicable to the Bank itself, in accordance with the Bank Service Company Act (12 U.S.C. § 1867).

ARTICLE 6 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS AND CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

6.1 Non-Competition During Engagement. During the Term, the Consultant shall not, directly or indirectly, provide consulting or advisory services on third-party risk management or vendor oversight to any other federally regulated banking institution that competes directly with the Bank in the Bank's primary geographic market, except as expressly permitted by the Bank in writing.

6.2 Non-Solicitation. During the Term and for twelve (12) months following termination, the Consultant shall not solicit for employment or contractor engagement any senior employee of the Bank with whom the Consultant had material contact, except for general solicitations.

6.3 Conflicts of Interest. The Consultant shall promptly notify the Bank in writing of any actual or potential conflict of interest, including any proposed engagement with a vendor of the Bank, a customer of the Bank, or any party with a material business relationship with the Bank.

6.4 Customer-Imposed Conditions. To the extent the Bank's customers or vendors impose conditions on personnel working with the Bank, those conditions flow through to the Consultant upon written notice from the Bank.

ARTICLE 7 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

7.1 Authority. The Consultant has the full right and authority to enter this Agreement.

7.2 No Conflicts. The Consultant is not party to any agreement that would prevent the Consultant from performing the Services.

7.3 Professional Qualifications. The Consultant has the professional qualifications, training, and experience to perform the Services.

7.4 No Disqualification Orders. The Consultant has not been the subject of any prohibition order under 12 U.S.C. § 1818(e), the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, or any other federal or state regulatory action that would disqualify the Consultant from providing services to a regulated financial institution.

7.5 Compliance with Laws. The Consultant will comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws in performing the Services.

ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION AND INSURANCE

8.1 Indemnification by Consultant. In addition to the tax indemnification in Section 4.6, the Consultant shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Bank and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from any claims arising out of (a) the Consultant's breach of any representation or covenant in this Agreement, or (b) the Consultant's willful misconduct or gross negligence in performing the Services.

8.2 Limitation. The Consultant's aggregate liability under Article 4 (tax indemnification) and this Article 8 shall not exceed three (3) times the cumulative compensation paid to the Consultant under this Agreement, except in the case of willful misconduct, fraud, or any indemnification obligation arising under Section 4.6 (which shall be uncapped).

8.3 Professional Liability Insurance. The Consultant shall maintain professional liability insurance with coverage limits of at least Two Million United States Dollars (US$2,000,000) per claim and in the aggregate, and shall provide the Bank with a certificate of insurance.

ARTICLE 9 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GOVERNING LAW

9.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

9.2 Exclusive Venue. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

9.3 Equitable Relief. The Bank may seek equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce Article 5 (Confidentiality), Article 6 (Restrictive Covenants), or Article 7 (Representations and Warranties).

9.4 Waiver of Jury Trial. EACH PARTY HEREBY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY.

ARTICLE 10 — MISCELLANEOUS

10.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

10.2 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

10.3 Assignment. The Consultant may not assign this Agreement. The Bank may assign to any successor.

10.4 Survival. Articles 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10 shall survive termination.

10.5 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.
