ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT

1.1 Engagement of Contractor. The Company hereby engages the Contractor to provide the services described in Section 1.2 (the "Services") as an independent contractor.

1.2 Scope of Services. The Contractor shall provide professional photography services for the Company's marketing department, on a per-shoot basis as agreed in writing for each engagement. Each shoot will be specified in a Shoot Brief identifying the date, location, deliverables, and per-shoot fee.

1.3 Personal Services. The Contractor shall personally perform the Services. The Contractor may use the Contractor's own assistants for non-creative tasks (lighting setup, equipment management).

1.4 Best Efforts. The Contractor shall use best efforts to perform the Services in a professional manner consistent with industry standards.

1.5 Contractor's Control. The Contractor shall determine the means and methods of performing the Services, including the equipment, software, lighting, post-production techniques, and creative approach. The Contractor is not subject to the Company's day-to-day supervision in the performance of the Services. The Company specifies the deliverables (subject matter, intended use, target dates) but does not direct the Contractor on how to capture or process the images.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 Term. This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues until either party terminates it.

2.2 Termination for Convenience. Either party may terminate at any time on {{IC_NOTICE_DAYS_CONVENIENCE}} days' written notice.

2.4 Effect of Termination. The Contractor delivers any in-progress work, submits a final invoice, and returns any Company materials.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES, EXPENSES, AND COMPENSATION

3.1 Per-shoot fee. The Contractor is paid a per-shoot fee specified in each Shoot Brief, OR (alternatively) at the hourly rate of {{IC_HOURLY_RATE}} per hour as specified in the Shoot Brief.

The Contractor uses the Contractor's own camera equipment, lenses, lighting, and editing software.

3.4 No additional compensation. The fees in this Article 3 are the Contractor's entire compensation. No equity, bonuses, severance, or benefits.

ARTICLE 4 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP AND ABC TEST COMPLIANCE

4.1 No Employment Relationship. The Parties expressly agree that they are creating an independent contractor relationship and that the Contractor is not an employee of the Company for any purpose under California Labor Code, the Industrial Welfare Commission Wage Orders, or the federal Fair Labor Standards Act.

4.2 ABC Test Compliance. The Parties intend this engagement to satisfy each of the three prongs of the ABC test under California Labor Code § 2775:

(a) A — Free from Control. The Contractor is and shall remain free from the Company's control and direction in connection with the performance of the Services, both under this Agreement and in fact. The Contractor decides how, when, and where to perform the Services, what equipment to use, and what creative and technical methods to apply. The Contractor is not subject to the Company's day-to-day supervision.

(b) B — Outside the Usual Course of the Hiring Entity's Business. The Services performed by the Contractor (professional photography) are outside the usual course of the Company's business. The Company is in the business of selling sustainable consumer products, not in the business of providing photography services. The Contractor's photography services are an outside, specialized service that the Company purchases from independent professionals rather than employing in-house photographers.

(c) C — Customarily Engaged in Independently Established Trade. The Contractor is customarily engaged in an independently established trade, occupation, or business of the same nature as the Services provided to the Company. Specifically, the Contractor (i) operates the Contractor's own professional photography business, (ii) maintains the Contractor's own business location, business cards, professional liability insurance, and a public-facing portfolio website, (iii) holds the Contractor's own business licenses, fictitious business name (if any), and tax registrations, (iv) provides photography services to multiple clients other than the Company, and (v) markets the Contractor's photography services to the general public.

4.3 Contractor's Representations Supporting ABC Test. The Contractor represents and warrants that, as of the Effective Date and throughout the Term, the Contractor (a) maintains the Contractor's own photography business, (b) provides photography services to other clients (not just the Company), (c) holds professional liability insurance for the Contractor's photography business, (d) uses the Contractor's own equipment, (e) markets the Contractor's services to the general public via a website, social media, or other means, and (f) maintains all required business registrations, licenses, and tax filings to operate the Contractor's photography business.

4.4 No Withholding. The Company shall not withhold any federal, state, or local income taxes, FICA contributions, SDI contributions, or any other employment-related withholdings from the Contractor's compensation.

4.5 No Employee Benefits. The Contractor is not entitled to any benefits provided to Company employees, including without limitation the Company's medical, dental, vision, or retirement plans; paid sick leave under California Labor Code § 246 (which applies only to employees); workers' compensation coverage; or unemployment insurance.

4.6 Form W-9 and Form 1099-NEC. The Contractor shall provide a Form W-9. The Company shall report compensation on Form 1099-NEC.

4.7 Self-Employment Taxes. The Contractor is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, Social Security and Medicare contributions, and any other tax obligations.

4.8 Tax Indemnification. The Contractor indemnifies the Company against any reclassification claim by the California Employment Development Department, the IRS, or any other taxing authority, and against any claim that the Contractor is or should be classified as an employee under the ABC test or any other test.

4.9 Workers' Compensation. The Contractor acknowledges that the Contractor is not covered by the Company's workers' compensation insurance and that the Contractor is responsible for obtaining the Contractor's own injury coverage.

ARTICLE 5 — WORK PRODUCT AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 Photographs and Deliverables. The Company owns the photographs and other deliverables produced under each Shoot Brief once the Contractor has been paid in full for that shoot.

5.2 Work Made for Hire. The deliverables are "work made for hire" to the maximum extent allowed under U.S. copyright law; to the extent any deliverables don't qualify as work made for hire, the Contractor assigns them to the Company.

5.3 Background IP. The Contractor retains all rights in the Contractor's pre-existing presets, lighting setups, retouching techniques, and other photographic methods, with a non-exclusive perpetual license to the Company for any such Background IP incorporated into the deliverables.

5.4 Portfolio Rights. The Contractor may include the deliverables in the Contractor's professional portfolio (online and physical) and may credit the Contractor's role on the Company's marketing materials, unless the Company expressly designates a shoot as confidential at the start of the shoot.

5.5 Original work. The Contractor represents that the deliverables will be original or properly licensed.

ARTICLE 6 — CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 The Contractor will hold all Company Confidential Information in strict confidence during this engagement and after.

6.2 Confidential Information includes the Company's business plans, marketing strategy, customer lists, product roadmap, and any pre-launch product images shared with the Contractor.

6.3 The Contractor will not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Contractor's work for the Company.

6.4 On termination, the Contractor returns or deletes Confidential Information.

ARTICLE 7 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS AND CONFLICTS

7.1 Free to Work for Other Clients. The Contractor is and remains free to provide photography services to other clients during this engagement and after. This is a foundational element of the ABC test and the parties' intent that the Contractor maintain independent contractor status.

7.2 Section 16600 Acknowledgment. The Contractor and the Company expressly acknowledge that California Business and Professions Code § 16600 voids any contract that restrains a person from engaging in a lawful profession, trade, or business. Accordingly, this Agreement does not impose any post-engagement non-compete on the Contractor. The Contractor is free to provide photography services to any other client (including the Company's competitors) at any time, both during and after this engagement.

7.3 Non-Solicitation. The Contractor shall not, during the Term and for six (6) months after, solicit any Company employee. (This narrow non-solicit is permitted by California law because it protects the Company's relationship with its employees, not its trade-or-business activities.)

7.4 Conflict Disclosure. The Contractor shall promptly disclose any actual conflict of interest in writing.

ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION

The Contractor indemnifies the Company for breach of representations and warranties, IP infringement of any deliverables, breach of confidentiality, or willful misconduct, in addition to the tax indemnification in Article 4.

The Contractor's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the total fees paid to the Contractor under this Agreement, except for tax indemnification, IP infringement, willful misconduct, and fraud (uncapped).
