INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT — ACADEMIC RESEARCHER (CONSULTING ENGAGEMENT)

This INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the "Effective Date") by and between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} having its principal place of business at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual residing at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} (the "Researcher"). The Company and the Researcher are collectively the "Parties." This Agreement is the Company's standard form for individual academic researcher consulting engagements and shall be governed by the Company's negotiated terms.

The Researcher is a tenured faculty member at the academic institution identified in Schedule A (the "University") and is entering into this Agreement in the Researcher's individual capacity, subject to the University's outside-consulting policy. The Company acknowledges that the Researcher's primary obligations are to the University and that this consulting engagement is subject to the limits and conditions of the University's outside-consulting policy and any applicable conflict-of-interest rules.

ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT

1.1 Engagement of Researcher. The Company hereby engages the Researcher to provide the consulting services described in Section 1.2 (the "Services") on the terms set forth in this Agreement.

1.2 Scope of Services. The Researcher shall provide professional consulting services to the Company's Research and Development organization, including: (a) advising the Company on the state of the art in the Researcher's area of academic expertise, (b) reviewing the Company's research and development plans and providing technical feedback, (c) participating in the Company's R&D advisory meetings (in person or remotely), (d) reviewing pre-publication drafts of the Company's scientific papers (where the Company elects to publish), and (e) such other related advisory services as the Company's Chief Scientific Officer may reasonably request.

1.3 Personal Services. The Researcher shall personally perform the Services and shall not delegate or subcontract.

1.4 Best Efforts. The Researcher shall use the Researcher's best efforts in a professional manner.

1.5 Time Commitment. The Researcher's time commitment under this Agreement shall not exceed forty (40) hours per calendar month or the limit imposed by the University's outside-consulting policy, whichever is less. The Researcher shall not perform Services that would interfere with the Researcher's University obligations.

1.6 University Notification. The Researcher has notified the University of this consulting engagement and (where required by the University) has obtained the University's approval. The Researcher shall comply with the University's reporting requirements regarding outside consulting.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 Term. The Term shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for {{IC_TERM_MONTHS}} months thereafter.

2.2 Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate on {{IC_NOTICE_DAYS_CONVENIENCE}} days' written notice.

2.3 Termination for Cause. The Company may terminate immediately for: (a) material breach, (b) willful misconduct, (c) loss of the Researcher's University position, (d) any conflict of interest the Researcher fails to disclose, or (e) any breach of Article 5 (Confidentiality) or Article 6 (IP).

2.4 Effect of Termination. The Researcher returns work product, submits a final invoice, and surrenders any Company materials.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES, EXPENSES, AND COMPENSATION

3.1 Hourly Rate. The Company shall pay the Researcher at the hourly rate of {{IC_HOURLY_RATE}} per hour for time spent performing the Services.

3.2 Cap. The cumulative compensation under this Agreement shall not exceed {{IC_FIXED_FEE}} (the "Cap") without the Company's prior written approval.

3.3 Time Tracking. The Researcher shall maintain detailed time records and shall provide them to the Company on request.

3.4 Invoicing. Monthly, due net 45.

3.5 Expense Reimbursement. The Company will reimburse pre-approved travel and out-of-pocket expenses, with any individual expense above Five Hundred United States Dollars (US$500) requiring written pre-approval.

3.6 No Equity. No equity, stock options, RSUs, or warrants under this engagement. (If the Company wishes to grant equity to the Researcher, it must do so under a separate equity grant agreement and only after the Researcher confirms that the equity grant is permitted under the University's outside-consulting policy and conflict-of-interest rules.)

ARTICLE 4 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

4.1 No Employment Relationship. The Researcher is an independent contractor, not an employee of the Company.

4.2 No Withholding.

4.3 No Employee Benefits.

4.4 Form W-9; Form 1099-NEC. The Researcher shall provide a Form W-9. The Company shall report compensation on Form 1099-NEC.

4.5 Self-Employment Taxes. The Researcher is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, Social Security and Medicare contributions.

4.6 Tax Indemnification. The Researcher indemnifies the Company against any reclassification claim or tax authority claim relating to the Researcher's compensation.

4.7 No Effect on University Employment. This Agreement does not in any way affect the Researcher's employment relationship with the University.

ARTICLE 5 — CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Confidential Information. All non-public information of the Company, including business plans, research and development strategies, product roadmaps, pre-publication drafts of Company papers, financial information, and any other non-public information.

5.2 Confidentiality Obligation. The Researcher shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence during the Term and at all times thereafter.

5.3 Use Restriction. The Researcher shall not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Services. In particular, the Researcher shall not use Company Confidential Information in the Researcher's University research, in the Researcher's academic publications, or in advising any other party.

5.4 Carve-Out for Academic Freedom. The Researcher's general background knowledge in the Researcher's field, including knowledge developed independently of this engagement, is not Confidential Information and may be used by the Researcher in the Researcher's University research and academic publications.

5.5 Return of Materials. On termination, the Researcher returns all Confidential Information.

5.6 Injunctive Relief. The Company may seek injunctive relief without bond.

ARTICLE 6 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND ACADEMIC PUBLICATION RIGHTS

6.1 Work Product. Any work product the Researcher creates specifically for the Company under this Agreement (e.g., written reports advising the Company on the state of the art, technical reviews of Company R&D plans, written feedback on pre-publication papers) is "Work Product" and belongs to the Company. The Researcher assigns all right, title, and interest in the Work Product to the Company.

6.2 Background IP and Independent Research Carve-Out. The Researcher retains all rights in (a) the Researcher's pre-existing intellectual property created prior to or outside of this engagement, (b) the Researcher's University research conducted independently of this engagement, and (c) the Researcher's academic publications. None of the foregoing constitutes Work Product.

6.3 University IP Policy Acknowledgment. The Researcher's intellectual property created in the course of the Researcher's University duties is owned by the University in accordance with the University's IP policy. Nothing in this Agreement is intended to assign University-owned IP to the Company. To the extent any University-owned IP would otherwise be deemed Work Product under Section 6.1, the Researcher's assignment is limited to the Researcher's own personal interests and does not affect the University's interests.

6.4 Academic Publication Rights. The Researcher is free to publish academic papers in the Researcher's field of expertise during and after the Term, subject to (a) the Researcher's confidentiality obligations under Article 5, and (b) the Company's right to review pre-publication drafts of any paper that references or discusses information learned through this engagement (such review limited to forty-five (45) days, after which the Researcher may proceed to publish, with the Researcher reasonably accommodating any Company requests to remove Confidential Information).

6.5 Disclosure. The Researcher shall disclose this consulting engagement on any academic paper or grant application where disclosure is required by the relevant journal or grant agency's conflict-of-interest rules.

6.6 Original Work. The Researcher represents that any Work Product will be original.

ARTICLE 7 — CONFLICTS OF INTEREST AND RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

7.1 University Conflict-of-Interest Compliance. The Researcher shall comply with the University's conflict-of-interest rules and shall report this consulting engagement to the University as required.

7.2 Conflict Disclosure. The Researcher shall promptly disclose to the Company any actual or potential conflict of interest, including any consulting engagement with another company in the same industry, any equity or financial interest in another company in the same industry, or any grant funding from a Company competitor.

7.3 Other Engagements. The Researcher is free to provide consulting services to other companies during the Term, subject to (a) the Researcher's confidentiality obligations under Article 5, (b) the University's outside-consulting policy, and (c) the Researcher's obligation to disclose any actual or potential conflict.

7.4 No Non-Compete. The Researcher is NOT subject to any post-engagement non-compete under this Agreement. The Researcher is free to consult for any company after this engagement ends, subject only to the surviving confidentiality obligations.

7.5 Non-Solicitation. The Researcher shall not solicit any Company employee for twelve (12) months after termination.

ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Indemnification by Researcher. The Researcher indemnifies the Company against breach of representations and warranties, breach of confidentiality, willful misconduct, or gross negligence.

8.2 Limitation. The Researcher's aggregate liability under Article 4 (tax) and this Article 8 shall not exceed two (2) times the cumulative compensation under this Agreement, except for breach of confidentiality, willful misconduct, and tax indemnification (uncapped).

8.3 No Indemnification of Researcher by Company. The Company does not indemnify the Researcher.

ARTICLE 9 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GOVERNING LAW

9.2 Exclusive Venue. Disputes shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in {{VENUE_COUNTY}}, {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}.

9.3 Equitable Relief. The Company may seek equitable relief to enforce Article 5 (Confidentiality) or Article 6 (IP).
