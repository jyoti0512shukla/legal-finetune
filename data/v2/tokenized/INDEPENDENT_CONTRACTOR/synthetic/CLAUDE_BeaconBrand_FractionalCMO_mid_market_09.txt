This is a standalone independent contractor agreement engaging the Fractional CMO to provide part-time CMO services to the Company.

ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT

The Company is engaging the Fractional CMO to lead the Company's marketing function on a fractional basis. Specifically: brand strategy, demand generation, marketing org leadership, marketing technology stack, customer marketing, and marketing measurement.

The Fractional CMO will work approximately twenty (20) hours per week during the Term, with flexibility for the Fractional CMO to determine the schedule and means of work.

The Fractional CMO will personally perform the services and will not delegate or subcontract substantive marketing leadership work.

The Fractional CMO will use best efforts to perform the services in a professional manner consistent with the standards expected of a fractional CMO.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND TERMINATION

The Term begins on the Effective Date and continues for {{IC_TERM_MONTHS}} months unless extended by mutual written agreement or terminated earlier.

Either party can terminate for convenience on {{IC_NOTICE_DAYS_CONVENIENCE}} days' written notice.

The Company can terminate immediately for material breach, willful misconduct, or any conflict of interest the Fractional CMO fails to disclose.

On termination, the Fractional CMO will deliver any in-progress work, submit a final invoice, and cooperate with the transition for up to ten (10) business days.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES, EXPENSES, AND COMPENSATION

Hybrid retainer plus project bonuses.

Monthly retainer. The Company will pay the Fractional CMO a monthly retainer of {{IC_MONTHLY_RETAINER}}, payable on the first business day of each month.

Project bonuses. For specific marketing initiatives that the parties agree in writing in advance, the Company may agree to pay an additional project bonus tied to project completion. Each project bonus will be documented in a written addendum signed by both parties identifying the project, the deliverable, and the bonus amount.

Hours cap. The retainer covers up to twenty (20) hours per week. For time worked above the cap, the Fractional CMO may invoice the Company at the hourly rate of {{IC_HOURLY_RATE}} per hour, subject to Company prior written approval.

Expense reimbursement. The Company will reimburse the Fractional CMO for pre-approved expenses (paid analytics tools, premium content subscriptions, conference travel where the Fractional CMO is representing the Company). Any expense over Five Hundred United States Dollars (US$500) requires written pre-approval.

No equity. No equity, RSUs, options, or warrants under this engagement.

ARTICLE 4 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

The Fractional CMO is an independent contractor, not an employee. No employer-employee, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship is created.

No tax withholding. The Company will not withhold any taxes. The Fractional CMO is responsible for all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, Social Security, and Medicare contributions.

No benefits. The Fractional CMO is not entitled to any Company employee benefits.

W-9 and 1099. The Fractional CMO will provide a Form W-9. The Company will report compensation on Form 1099-NEC.

The Fractional CMO determines how, when, and where to do the work, uses the Fractional CMO's own equipment, and is free to serve other clients during this engagement subject to Article 7.

Tax indemnification. The Fractional CMO indemnifies the Company against any tax authority claim or reclassification claim relating to the Fractional CMO's compensation.

ARTICLE 5 — WORK PRODUCT AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 The Fractional CMO assigns to the Company all marketing strategies, plans, content, brand frameworks, campaign briefs, and other deliverables created for the Company under this Agreement (the "Work Product").

5.2 The Work Product is "work made for hire" to the maximum extent allowed; to the extent it doesn't qualify, the Fractional CMO assigns it to the Company.

5.3 Background IP. The Fractional CMO retains all rights in pre-existing methodologies, frameworks, templates, and customer journey models. The Fractional CMO grants the Company a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use any Background IP incorporated into the Work Product, solely as part of the Work Product.

5.4 Original work. The Fractional CMO represents that the Work Product will be original.

5.5 Speaking and writing rights. The Fractional CMO retains the right to speak publicly and write about general marketing topics, frameworks, and case studies, provided that any case studies referencing the Company are de-identified or have the Company's written approval.

ARTICLE 6 — CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 The Fractional CMO will hold all Company Confidential Information in strict confidence during this engagement and after.

6.2 Confidential Information includes the Company's customer lists, pricing, marketing strategy, financials, product roadmap, and anything else marked confidential.

6.3 The Fractional CMO will not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Company's marketing.

6.4 On termination, the Fractional CMO returns all Confidential Information.

6.5 The Fractional CMO agrees that breach of this Article 6 would cause irreparable harm and that the Company can seek injunctive relief without bond.

ARTICLE 7 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

7.1 During the Term, the Fractional CMO will not serve as Fractional CMO or marketing advisor for any direct competitor of the Company in the same product category.

7.2 During the Term and for twelve (12) months after, the Fractional CMO will not solicit any Company employee or contractor.

7.3 The Fractional CMO is free to serve as Fractional CMO for non-competing companies during this engagement.

7.4 The Fractional CMO will disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest in writing.

ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION

In addition to the tax indemnification in Article 4, the Fractional CMO indemnifies the Company for any breach of this Agreement, IP infringement of any Work Product, gross negligence, or willful misconduct.

The Fractional CMO's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the total fees paid in the prior twelve (12) months, except for tax indemnification, IP infringement, willful misconduct, and fraud (uncapped).
