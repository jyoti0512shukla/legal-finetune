INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT — MARKETING CONSULTANT

This Agreement is between {{PROVIDER_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_STATE}} {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} located at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} (the "Company"), and {{CUSTOMER_NAME}}, an individual at {{CUSTOMER_ADDRESS}} (the "Consultant"). Effective Date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}.

The Company is hiring the Consultant as an independent contractor to provide marketing consulting services. Here are the terms.

ARTICLE 1 — ENGAGEMENT

1.1 The Consultant is engaged to provide marketing consulting services, including: marketing strategy development, brand positioning, content marketing planning, paid media oversight (in coordination with the Company's existing agency partners), and quarterly marketing performance reviews.

1.2 The Consultant will work approximately fifteen (15) hours per week during the Term, with flexibility for the Consultant to determine the schedule and the means of work.

1.3 The Consultant is responsible for the marketing strategy and recommendations; the Company's internal marketing team is responsible for implementation. The Consultant is in an advisory and oversight role, not a hands-on implementation role.

1.4 The Consultant will perform the services personally and will not delegate or subcontract without Company written consent.

1.5 The Consultant will use the Consultant's best efforts to perform the services in a professional manner.

ARTICLE 2 — TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 The Term starts on the Effective Date and continues for {{IC_TERM_MONTHS}} months unless terminated earlier or extended by mutual written agreement.

2.2 Either party can terminate for convenience on {{IC_NOTICE_DAYS_CONVENIENCE}} days' written notice.

2.3 The Company can terminate immediately for material breach, willful misconduct, or any conflict of interest the Consultant fails to disclose.

2.4 On termination, the Consultant delivers any in-progress work, submits a final invoice, and returns any Company materials.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES, EXPENSES, AND COMPENSATION

3.1 Monthly retainer. The Company pays the Consultant a flat monthly retainer of {{IC_MONTHLY_RETAINER}}, payable within fifteen (15) days of receipt of the Consultant's monthly invoice.

3.2 Fee structure. The retainer covers up to fifteen (15) hours per week (approximately sixty (60) hours per calendar month). For time worked above sixty hours in any month, the Consultant may invoice the Company at the hourly rate of {{IC_HOURLY_RATE}} per hour, subject to the Company's prior written approval before exceeding the cap.

3.3 Expense reimbursement. The Company will reimburse pre-approved expenses (travel, conference fees, paid research subscriptions). Any expense over Three Hundred United States Dollars (US$300) requires written pre-approval.

3.4 Late payment. Invoices not paid within thirty (30) days accrue interest at the lower of 1.5% per month or the maximum legal rate.

3.5 No equity, no bonuses, no benefits.

ARTICLE 4 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

4.1 The Consultant is an independent contractor, not an employee. No employer-employee, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship is created by this Agreement.

4.2 No withholding. The Company will not withhold any taxes from the Consultant's compensation. The Consultant is responsible for all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, Social Security, and Medicare contributions.

4.3 No employee benefits. The Consultant is not entitled to any benefits provided to Company employees: no medical, dental, retirement, paid leave, workers' compensation, or unemployment insurance.

4.4 W-9 and 1099. The Consultant will provide a Form W-9. The Company will report compensation on Form 1099-NEC.

4.5 Consultant's control. The Consultant determines how, when, and where to do the work, and uses the Consultant's own equipment.

4.6 Tax indemnification. The Consultant indemnifies the Company against any reclassification claim or tax authority claim relating to the Consultant's compensation under this Agreement.

4.7 Other clients. The Consultant is free to provide marketing consulting services to other clients during the Term, subject to the conflicts and restrictive covenants in Article 7.

ARTICLE 5 — WORK PRODUCT AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 The Consultant assigns to the Company all marketing strategies, plans, content drafts, brand positioning frameworks, and other deliverables created by the Consultant for the Company under this Agreement (the "Work Product").

5.2 The Work Product is "work made for hire" to the maximum extent allowed by U.S. copyright law; to the extent it doesn't qualify, the Consultant assigns it to the Company.

5.3 Background IP. The Consultant retains all rights in the Consultant's pre-existing methodologies, frameworks, templates, and tools. The Consultant grants the Company a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use any Background IP incorporated into the Work Product, solely as part of the Work Product.

5.4 Original work. The Consultant represents that the Work Product will be original and will not infringe third-party IP rights.

ARTICLE 6 — CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 The Consultant will hold all Company Confidential Information in strict confidence during this engagement and after.

6.2 Confidential Information includes the Company's customer lists, pricing, marketing strategy, financial information, and anything else marked confidential or that would reasonably be understood as confidential.

6.3 The Consultant will not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the work for the Company.

6.4 On termination, the Consultant returns all Confidential Information to the Company.

6.5 The Consultant agrees that breach of this Article 6 would cause irreparable harm and that the Company can seek injunctive relief without posting bond.

ARTICLE 7 — RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

7.1 During the Term, the Consultant will not provide marketing consulting services to any direct competitor of the Company in the same product category.

7.2 During the Term and for six (6) months after, the Consultant will not solicit any Company employee or contractor.

7.3 The Consultant will not use any Company Confidential Information when working for other clients.

7.4 The Consultant will disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest in writing as soon as it arises.

ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY

8.1 The Consultant indemnifies the Company for breach of representations and warranties, IP infringement, and willful misconduct, in addition to the tax indemnification in Article 4.

8.2 The Consultant's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the total fees paid by the Company in the prior twelve (12) months, except for tax indemnification, IP infringement, and willful misconduct (uncapped).
