SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Software as a Service Subscription Agreement (hereinafter referred to as the "Agreement") is entered into as of the 1st day of November, 2023 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Apex Workflow Platform, Inc., a Delaware corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of the State of California, with its principal place of business at 1234 Innovation Drive, San Jose, California 75201 (hereinafter referred to as the "Provider"), and Liberty Pharmaceuticals Inc., a Delaware corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of the State of California, with its principal place of business at 5678 Logistics Lane, San Jose, California 75202 (hereinafter referred to as the "Customer").

WHEREAS, Provider is engaged in the business of offering software solutions for logistics and fleet management, including a platform that optimizes route planning and fleet operations;

WHEREAS, Customer desires to obtain access to Provider's software platform to enhance its fleet management capabilities across its enterprise operations;

WHEREAS, Provider agrees to grant such access to the Customer under the terms and conditions set forth herein, including the provision of necessary support and ancillary services;

WHEREAS, the parties acknowledge that the hardware necessary for the utilization of the software is to be acquired separately by the Customer under distinct purchase orders;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties hereby agree as follows:

The Services shall be provided in accordance with the specifications set forth in the applicable Order Form and the Service Level Agreement.

2.2 License Grant. Provider hereby grants to Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, and non-sublicensable right to access and use the Services solely for Customer's internal business operations. This license is limited to the number of vehicle licenses specified in the applicable Order Form.

Provider reserves the right to suspend Customer’s access to the Services in the event of a breach of this Agreement by Customer, including failure to pay any undisputed amounts due. ARTICLE 3 — IMPLEMENTATION AND PROFESSIONAL SERVICES

3.1 Scope. Provider shall provide Implementation Services to assist Customer in deploying and configuring the Services as set forth in the applicable Order Form. The scope of such services shall be detailed in a mutually agreed-upon statement of work.

3.2 Fees. Customer shall pay Provider the fees for Implementation Services as specified in the Order Form. Such fees shall be invoiced separately from Subscription Fees and shall be payable in accordance with the payment terms set forth herein.

3.3 Acceptance Criteria. Implementation Services shall be deemed accepted upon the successful completion of the agreed-upon acceptance criteria as specified in the statement of work. Customer shall notify Provider in writing of any deficiencies within ten (10) Business Days of completion.

Any delays caused by Customer’s failure to provide such cooperation may result in adjustments to the project timeline.

ARTICLE 4 — SUBSCRIPTION FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fees. Customer shall pay Provider the Subscription Fees as specified in the applicable Order Form. Subscription Fees are based on a per-vehicle subscription tier and are due annually in advance.

4.2 Billing Cycle. Provider shall invoice Customer for the Subscription Fees on an annual basis, with the first invoice issued on the Effective Date. Subsequent invoices shall be issued on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

4.3 Late Payment Interest. Any undisputed amounts not paid when due shall accrue interest at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, or the highest rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is lower, from the due date until paid.

4.4 Taxes. All fees are exclusive of taxes, levies, or duties imposed by taxing authorities, and Customer shall be responsible for payment of all such taxes, excluding taxes based solely on Provider's income.

4.5 Fee Changes. Provider reserves the right to modify the Subscription Fees upon renewal of the Subscription Term, provided that Provider gives Customer at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice of any such changes.

4.6 Audit Rights. Provider shall have the right to audit Customer’s use of the Services to ensure compliance with the terms of this Agreement. Such audits shall be conducted during normal business hours and upon reasonable notice.

4.7 Disputed Invoices. In the event Customer disputes any portion of an invoice, Customer shall notify Provider in writing within thirty (30) days of receipt of the invoice, specifying the nature of the dispute. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve any such disputes promptly.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND RENEWAL

5.1 Initial Term. The initial Subscription Term shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of twenty-four (24) months, as specified in the applicable Order Form.

5.2 Automatic Renewal. Upon expiration of the initial Subscription Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current Subscription Term.

5.3 Notice of Non-Renewal. Either party may elect not to renew this Agreement by providing written notice to the other party at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current Subscription Term.

5.4 Fee Escalation Upon Renewal. Provider may increase the Subscription Fees for any renewal term by providing Customer with at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice of such increase.

5.5 Effects of Expiration. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, Customer's right to access and use the Services shall immediately cease. Provider shall have no obligation to retain any Customer Data beyond the retention period specified herein.

ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Uptime Commitment. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that the Services achieve an uptime percentage of at least 99.5% during each calendar month, excluding any downtime resulting from a Force Majeure Event or scheduled maintenance.

6.2 Exclusions. The uptime commitment shall not apply to any unavailability caused by factors outside Provider’s reasonable control, including but not limited to satellite or cellular outages, Customer's equipment or third-party services.

6.3 Measurement Methodology. Uptime shall be measured using Provider's monitoring tools, which shall be the sole basis for determining compliance with the uptime commitment.

6.4 Service Credit Calculation. In the event Provider fails to meet the uptime commitment, Customer shall be entitled to a service credit, calculated as a percentage of the monthly Subscription Fee, as specified in the Service Level Agreement.

6.5 Support Tiers. Provider shall offer support services in accordance with the support tiers specified in the Service Level Agreement. Each support tier shall include specific response times and escalation procedures.

6.6 Response Times. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to respond to support requests within the response times specified in the Service Level Agreement. Response times shall vary based on the severity level of the issue.

6.7 Escalation Procedures. Provider shall maintain escalation procedures to address unresolved support issues. Customer may escalate issues in accordance with the procedures set forth in the Service Level Agreement.

6.8 Maintenance Windows. Provider shall schedule regular maintenance windows to perform necessary updates and maintenance on the Services. Provider shall provide Customer with reasonable advance notice of any scheduled maintenance.
