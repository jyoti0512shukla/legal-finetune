RESTAURANT POINT-OF-SALE PLATFORM SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Restaurant Point-of-Sale Platform Subscription Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of May 22, 2024 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Acuity Software Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at 4799 Summit Street, 10th Floor, Boston, Massachusetts 02810 ("Provider"), and Summit Retail Partners LLC, a Massachusetts limited liability company having its principal place of business at 5110 Innovation Street, 8th Floor, Boston, Massachusetts 02209 ("Customer"). Provider and Customer may each be referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Provider develops, hosts, and operates a cloud-based restaurant point-of-sale and management platform designed to support order entry, payment processing, kitchen display, inventory management, employee scheduling, and reporting for multi-unit restaurant operators (the "MenuMaster Platform");

WHEREAS, Customer owns and operates a portfolio of eight (8) full-service and quick-service restaurants located in the metropolitan Boston area and desires to subscribe to the MenuMaster Platform to standardize point-of-sale operations across its locations;

WHEREAS, the use of the Platform requires the deployment of certain hardware components at Customer's restaurant locations, including terminals, kitchen display systems, receipt printers, and payment card readers, and the Parties acknowledge that such hardware will be supplied through a separate Hardware Purchase Order under terms set forth in this Agreement;

WHEREAS, the Parties further acknowledge that restaurants operate during evenings, weekends, and holidays when many service providers are unavailable, and that the Platform must accordingly be supported by 24x7 technical assistance to minimize operational disruption; and

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to establish a multi-year commercial relationship to support Customer's restaurant operations;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE 4 — SUBSCRIPTION FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Subscription Fees. In consideration of the Services, Customer shall pay an annual Subscription Fee of One Hundred Fifty Thousand United States Dollars ($150,000), payable in monthly installments of One Thousand United States Dollars ($1,000) in advance. The Subscription Fee covers all eight (8) Authorized Locations identified on Schedule A as of the Effective Date.

4.2 Payment Terms. All Subscription Fees shall be due and payable in United States Dollars within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice (the "Due Date"). Customer shall make all payments by automatic credit card charge or electronic funds transfer to the account designated by Provider in writing. Hardware Purchase Orders shall be invoiced separately upon shipment of the Hardware.

4.3 Additional Authorized Locations. Customer may add additional Authorized Locations to the Subscription by providing Provider with written notice. Each additional Authorized Location shall be billed at One Hundred Twenty-Five United States Dollars ($125) per Authorized Location per month, prorated for any partial month. Provider shall provision the additional Authorized Location within five (5) business days of receipt of Customer's notice.

4.4 Disputed Invoices. If Customer in good faith disputes any portion of an invoice, Customer shall notify Provider in writing of the dispute within fifteen (15) business days of receipt of the invoice. Customer shall pay all undisputed amounts in accordance with Section 4.2, and the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve the disputed amount.

4.5 Late Payments. Any undisputed amounts not paid by the Due Date shall accrue interest at a rate equal to the lesser of (a) one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or (b) the maximum rate permitted by applicable Massachusetts law.

4.6 Taxes. The Subscription Fees and Hardware prices are exclusive of all taxes, levies, and duties, except taxes imposed on Provider's net income. Customer shall be responsible for payment of all such taxes, including without limitation Massachusetts state and local sales taxes on Hardware purchases.

4.7 Fee Adjustments at Renewal. Provider may adjust the Subscription Fees for any Renewal Term by providing written notice to Customer at least sixty (60) days prior to the start of the Renewal Term. Any such adjustment shall not exceed five percent (5%) above the Subscription Fees in effect during the immediately preceding Subscription Year.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND RENEWAL

5.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Agreement (the "Initial Term") shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of two (2) year thereafter, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 13.

5.2 Renewal Terms. Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive two (2) year renewal terms (each a "Renewal Term") unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal to the other Party at least thirty (30) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

5.3 Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party at any time after the first six (6) months of the Initial Term. Upon termination by Customer for convenience, no refund of prepaid Subscription Fees shall be provided. Upon termination by Provider for convenience, Provider shall refund to Customer the prepaid Subscription Fees corresponding to the period after the effective date of termination.

ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Uptime Commitment. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Platform available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.5%, calculated as: (Total Minutes in Month - Excluded Minutes - Downtime Minutes) / (Total Minutes in Month - Excluded Minutes), expressed as a percentage. The Parties acknowledge that the Platform is designed to operate in offline mode at the terminal level for short periods of time, and that brief cloud connectivity interruptions affecting reporting and synchronization shall not generally affect order entry and payment processing functionality at the Authorized Locations.

6.2 Excluded Downtime. The following shall not be counted as Downtime: (a) scheduled maintenance windows, which shall be conducted between 3:00 a.m. and 5:00 a.m. Eastern Time and shall not exceed four (4) hours per calendar month; (b) emergency maintenance with as much advance notice as is reasonably practicable; (c) downtime caused by Customer's acts or omissions, including loss of internet connectivity at any Authorized Location; (d) downtime caused by Force Majeure Events; (e) downtime caused by failures of third-party services, including without limitation Payment Processors; and (f) downtime caused by Customer's use of third-party hardware that does not meet Provider's specifications.

6.3 Service Credits. If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below 99.5%, Customer shall be entitled to receive service credits as follows: (a) below 99.5% but at or above 99.0%: a service credit equal to five percent (5%) of one-twelfth (1/12) of the annual Subscription Fee; (b) below 99.0% but at or above 98.0%: ten percent (10%); (c) below 98.0%: twenty-five percent (25%). To claim a service credit, Customer shall submit a written request within thirty (30) days following the end of the affected month. Service credits shall not be paid in cash and shall not exceed in the aggregate twenty-five percent (25%) of the annual Subscription Fee in any Subscription Year.

6.4 Support Services. Provider shall provide the following Support Services: (a) twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week ("24x7") telephone support, with a target initial response time of fifteen (15) minutes for Severity 1 issues (defined as any condition preventing order entry or payment processing at one or more Authorized Locations during business hours); (b) email and live chat support during Provider's business hours of 9:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. Eastern Time, seven days per week; (c) twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week ("24x7") access to a self-service knowledge base and online help center; and (d) on-site technician dispatch as a separately-priced service available upon Customer's request.

6.5 Severity Levels. Provider shall categorize support issues using the following severity levels: Severity 1 (any condition preventing order entry or payment processing at any Authorized Location during business hours), Severity 2 (significant degradation affecting reporting, inventory, or other non-critical functions), Severity 3 (limited functionality issue with workaround), and Severity 4 (general inquiry or feature request). Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve Severity 1 issues within two (2) hours, Severity 2 issues within two (2) business day, Severity 3 issues within five (5) business days, and Severity 4 issues per Provider's standard product roadmap. The Parties acknowledge that the two-hour resolution target for Severity 1 issues reflects the operational criticality of point-of-sale functionality during restaurant service hours.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, Provider may use aggregated and anonymized data derived from Customer's use of the Platform for statistical, analytical, and benchmarking purposes, provided that such aggregated data does not identify Customer, any Authorized Location, or any individual.

7.3 Guest Data. Customer Data may include personally identifiable information of Customer's restaurant guests, including without limitation names, contact information, and dining preferences (collectively, "Guest Data"). Provider shall treat Guest Data as Personal Data and shall comply with applicable privacy laws.

7.4 Data Security. Provider shall implement and maintain commercially reasonable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction, including without limitation (a) encryption of Customer Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher; (b) encryption of Customer Data at rest using AES-256 or equivalent; (c) role-based access controls; (d) annual third-party penetration testing; and (e) a documented information security program reviewed and updated at least annually.

7.5 PCI-DSS Compliance and Cardholder Data. The Platform is designed to facilitate payment processing through Payment Processors integrated with the Platform. Provider represents and warrants that the Platform shall be PCI-DSS compliant as a Payment Application (PA-DSS) or equivalent successor standard, and that Provider's hosting infrastructure shall maintain PCI-DSS Level 1 service provider compliance throughout the Subscription Term. Customer acknowledges that (a) Cardholder Data is processed by Customer's Payment Processor, not by Provider, and that Provider does not store Cardholder Data; (b) Customer is responsible for entering into a separate agreement with a PCI-DSS compliant Payment Processor; and (c) Customer is ultimately responsible for the PCI-DSS compliance of its own payment acceptance environment, including the secure handling of Cardholder Data at the Authorized Locations.

7.6 Breach Notification. Provider shall notify Customer in writing of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, acquisition of, or disclosure of Customer Data (a "Security Incident") without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of Provider's discovery of such Security Incident. Provider shall cooperate with Customer's investigation and shall reimburse Customer for the reasonable costs of mandatory breach notifications to affected individuals to the extent the Security Incident was caused by Provider's breach of its obligations under this Article 7.

7.7 Data Retention and Deletion. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, Provider shall, at Customer's election, (a) make Customer Data available for export by Customer in a commercially reasonable, machine-readable format for a period of sixty (60) days following expiration or termination; or (b) permanently delete Customer Data from Provider's production systems within thirty (30) days of expiration or termination, with deletion from backup systems to follow within ninety (90) days. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Provider shall retain Customer Data for any longer period required by applicable law.
