RECITALS

WHEREAS, Provider develops, hosts, and operates a cloud-based learning management platform that provides course delivery, learner analytics, assessment management, and credential issuance functionality for higher education institutions (the "LearnPath Platform");

WHEREAS, Customer operates one or more institutions of higher education within the Pennsylvania State University System and desires to subscribe to the LearnPath Platform to support online and blended course delivery to its enrolled students, faculty, and staff;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE 4 — SUBSCRIPTION FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Subscription Fees. In consideration of the Services to be provided by Provider, Customer shall pay an annual Subscription Fee of Seven Hundred Twenty Thousand United States Dollars ($720,000), which entitles Customer to provision the Platform to up to five thousand (5,000) Authorized Users who are enrolled students of Customer's constituent institutions. The Subscription Fee shall be invoiced annually in advance, with the first invoice due upon execution of this Agreement and subsequent annual invoices due thirty (30) days prior to the start of each Subscription Year.

4.2 Payment Terms. All Subscription Fees shall be due and payable in United States Dollars within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice (the "Due Date"). Customer shall make all payments by electronic funds transfer to the account designated by Provider in writing. The Parties acknowledge that, as Customer is a state agency, payment is subject to applicable Pennsylvania state appropriations law and the Pennsylvania Prompt Payment Act, Chapter 2251 of the Pennsylvania Government Code.

4.3 Excess Usage. If Customer's usage of the Platform exceeds five thousand (5,000) Authorized Users in any Subscription Year, Customer shall pay an additional fee of Thirty United States Dollars ($30) per Authorized User per Subscription Year for each Authorized User in excess of the included allotment, calculated based on the peak number of concurrent provisioned Authorized Users during the Subscription Year. Such excess usage fees shall be invoiced at the end of the applicable Subscription Year and shall be due within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice.

4.4 Late Payments. Any undisputed Subscription Fees not paid by the Due Date shall accrue interest at a rate equal to the lesser of (a) one percent (1.0%) per month or (b) the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, in each case calculated from the original Due Date until the date of actual payment. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Provider acknowledges that Customer's payment obligations are subject to the Pennsylvania Prompt Payment Act and that interest shall accrue only as permitted thereunder.

4.5 Disputed Invoices. If Customer in good faith disputes any portion of an invoice, Customer shall notify Provider in writing of the dispute within fifteen (15) business days of receipt of the invoice, identifying with reasonable particularity the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute. Customer shall pay all undisputed amounts in accordance with Section 4.2, and the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve the disputed amount. Late payment interest shall not accrue on amounts that are the subject of a good-faith dispute during the period of dispute resolution.

4.6 Taxes. The Subscription Fees are exclusive of all taxes, levies, and duties, except taxes imposed on Provider's net income. Customer represents that, as a state agency of the State of Pennsylvania, Customer is exempt from Pennsylvania sales and use tax pursuant to Section 151.309 of the Pennsylvania Tax Code, and Customer shall provide Provider with a valid Pennsylvania sales and use tax exemption certificate upon request.

Notwithstanding Section 5.2, Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience by providing Provider with at least one hundred eighty (180) days' prior written notice, with such termination to take effect at the end of an Academic Term to minimize disruption to Authorized Users.

ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Uptime Commitment. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Platform available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.9% during Academic Terms, calculated as: (Total Minutes in Month - Excluded Minutes - Downtime Minutes) / (Total Minutes in Month - Excluded Minutes), expressed as a percentage. During academic break periods (defined as the period between Academic Terms when fewer than ten percent (10%) of provisioned Authorized Users are actively using the Platform), Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain availability but shall not be subject to the 99.9% commitment.

6.2 Excluded Downtime. The following shall not be counted as Downtime for purposes of calculating Monthly Uptime Percentage: (a) scheduled maintenance windows announced at least seventy-two (72) hours in advance, which shall not exceed eight (8) hours per calendar month and shall be scheduled outside of peak usage hours; (b) emergency maintenance required to address security vulnerabilities, with as much advance notice as is reasonably practicable; (c) downtime caused by Customer's acts or omissions, including misconfiguration of Customer's identity provider; (d) downtime caused by force majeure events; and (e) downtime caused by failures of third-party services or networks outside Provider's reasonable control.

6.3 Service Credits. If the Monthly Uptime Percentage during an Academic Term falls below 99.9%, Customer shall be entitled to receive service credits as follows: (a) below 99.9% but at or above 99.5%: a service credit equal to five percent (5%) of one-twelfth (1/12) of the annual Subscription Fee; (b) below 99.5% but at or above 99.0%: a service credit equal to ten percent (10%) of one-twelfth (1/12) of the annual Subscription Fee; (c) below 99.0%: a service credit equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of one-twelfth (1/12) of the annual Subscription Fee. Service credits shall be Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for failures to meet the Service Level Agreement, except that two (2) or more consecutive months of SLA failures during Academic Terms shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement entitling Customer to terminate for cause pursuant to Section 13.2 without further notice.

6.4 Service Credit Procedure. To claim a service credit, Customer shall submit a written request to Provider within thirty (30) days following the end of the affected month, specifying the basis for the claim. Provider shall apply approved service credits to the next subsequent invoice. Service credits shall not be paid in cash and shall not exceed in the aggregate twenty-five percent (25%) of the annual Subscription Fee for any Subscription Year.

6.5 Support Services. Provider shall provide the following Support Services to Customer's designated administrators during the Subscription Term: (a) twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week ("24x7") access to a knowledge base, online help center, and self-service ticketing portal; (b) email support with a target initial response time of four (4) business hours for issues affecting the production environment; (c) telephone support during Provider's standard business hours of 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Pacific Time, Monday through Friday, excluding Provider's published holidays; and (d) emergency on-call support for Severity 1 incidents (defined as a complete outage of the Platform affecting all Authorized Users) with a target initial response time of one (1) hour, twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week.

6.6 Severity Levels. Provider shall categorize support issues using the following severity levels: Severity 1 (complete production outage), Severity 2 (significant degradation affecting a substantial portion of users), Severity 3 (limited functionality issue with a workaround), and Severity 4 (general inquiry or feature request). Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve Severity 1 issues within four (4) hours, Severity 2 issues within one (1) business day, Severity 3 issues within five (5) business days, and Severity 4 issues in accordance with Provider's standard product roadmap.

6.7 Maintenance Windows. Provider shall conduct routine maintenance of the Platform during a standard maintenance window of Sunday from 10:00 p.m. to 2:00 a.m. Pacific Time. Provider shall provide Customer with at least seventy-two (72) hours' advance notice of any maintenance that is expected to result in service interruption during the standard maintenance window. Maintenance scheduled outside the standard window shall require at least five (5) business days' advance notice except in the case of emergency maintenance.
