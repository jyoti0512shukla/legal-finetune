ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND RENEWAL

5.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of three (3) years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

5.2 Automatic Renewal. Upon the expiration of the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms, unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

5.3 Notice of Non-Renewal. Either party may elect not to renew this Agreement by providing written notice to the other party at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

5.4 Fee Escalation Upon Renewal. The Subscription Fee for any renewal term shall increase by five percent (5%) over the Subscription Fee for the immediately preceding term, unless otherwise agreed by the parties in writing.

5.5 Effects of Expiration. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, Customer's right to access and use the Services shall immediately cease. Provider shall have no obligation to retain Customer Data beyond the retention period specified in this Agreement, and Customer shall promptly return or destroy all copies of the Documentation in its possession.

ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Uptime Commitment. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that the Services are available with an uptime of 99.99% during each calendar month, excluding any scheduled maintenance or Force Majeure Events.

6.2 Exclusions. The uptime commitment shall not apply to any unavailability of the Services caused by (i) Customer's use of the Services in violation of this Agreement; (ii) issues with Customer's network or internet connectivity; or (iii) Third-Party Applications.

6.3 Measurement Methodology. Uptime shall be measured based on the total number of minutes in a calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance windows and Force Majeure Events, compared to the total number of minutes the Services were available during that month.

6.4 Service Credit Calculation. In the event that Provider fails to meet the uptime commitment, Customer shall be entitled to a service credit, calculated as a percentage of the monthly Subscription Fee, as specified in the SLA.

6.5 Support Tiers. Provider shall provide support services in accordance with the support tiers set forth in the SLA, which shall specify the levels of support available, including response times and escalation procedures.

6.6 Response Times. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to respond to support requests from Customer within the response times specified in the SLA, based on the severity level of the issue.

6.7 Escalation Procedures. In the event that an issue is not resolved within the initial response time, Provider shall escalate the issue in accordance with the escalation procedures set forth in the SLA, which may include involving senior technical personnel.

6.8 Maintenance Windows. Provider shall conduct scheduled maintenance during maintenance windows as specified in the SLA. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to minimize the impact of maintenance on the availability of the Services and shall provide advance notice to Customer of any scheduled maintenance.
