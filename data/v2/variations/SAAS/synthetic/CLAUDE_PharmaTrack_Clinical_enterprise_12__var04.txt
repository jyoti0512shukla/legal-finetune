CLINICAL TRIAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM SOFTWARE-AS-A-SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Clinical Trial Management System Software-as-a-Service Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of January 15, 2024 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Pinnacle Digital Systems, Inc., a Delaware Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at 6498 Highland Boulevard, Columbus, Ohio 43533 ("Provider"), and Highland Capital Group, Inc., a Delaware Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at 6543 Liberty Boulevard, Columbus, Ohio 43974 ("Customer"). Provider and Customer may each be referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Provider develops, validates, hosts, and supports a clinical trial management system designed for use by pharmaceutical and biotechnology sponsors in the planning, conduct, monitoring, and reporting of human clinical trials, which system has been validated for use in regulated environments and qualifies for use under the United States Food and Drug Administration's regulations governing electronic records and electronic signatures (the "PharmaTrack Platform");

WHEREAS, Customer is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company that conducts clinical trials in the United States and other jurisdictions for the purpose of obtaining regulatory approval for its therapeutic products and desires to subscribe to the PharmaTrack Platform to support such clinical trials;

WHEREAS, the Parties acknowledge that the use of the PharmaTrack Platform in regulated clinical research is subject to applicable laws and regulations, including without limitation 21 C.F.R. Part 11 (Electronic Records; Electronic Signatures), the principles of Good Clinical Practice (GCP) as set forth by the International Council for Harmonisation (ICH E6), the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA), and applicable data protection laws of jurisdictions in which clinical trials are conducted;

WHEREAS, the Parties have negotiated this Agreement to allocate the rights, responsibilities, risks, and remedies arising out of Customer's subscription to and use of the PharmaTrack Platform, with particular attention to the heightened regulatory environment in which the Platform is used; and

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to establish a long-term commercial relationship to support multiple clinical trials over the Subscription Term;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "21 CFR Part 11" means Title 21, Part 11 of the United States Code of Federal Regulations, governing the criteria under which the United States Food and Drug Administration considers electronic records, electronic signatures, and handwritten signatures executed to electronic records to be trustworthy, reliable, and generally equivalent to paper records and handwritten signatures executed on paper.

1.2 "Affiliate" means, with respect to any entity, any other entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such entity, where "control" means the ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of such entity or the power to direct the management and policies of such entity, whether through the ownership of voting securities, by contract, or otherwise.

1.3 "Authorized User" means any individual employee, contractor, clinical investigator, study coordinator, monitor, statistician, or other individual authorized by Customer to access and use the Platform in the performance of Customer's clinical trial activities. Each Authorized User shall be uniquely identified within the Platform and shall be assigned access rights commensurate with such Authorized User's role and responsibilities under applicable Standard Operating Procedures.

1.4 "Clinical Trial" means a research investigation involving human subjects designed to evaluate the effects of one or more biomedical or behavioral interventions on health outcomes, including without limitation Phase I, Phase II, Phase III, and Phase IV interventional clinical trials sponsored by Customer or its Affiliates and conducted in accordance with applicable Good Clinical Practice principles.

1.5 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other Party that is identified as confidential at the time of disclosure or that, given the nature of the information and the circumstances surrounding its disclosure, would reasonably be understood to be confidential. Confidential Information of Customer includes Trial Data, Customer's clinical trial protocols, research strategies, drug development plans, regulatory filings, and patient identifying information. Confidential Information of Provider includes the source code, validation documentation, security architecture, and pricing of the Platform.

1.6 "Customer Data" means all electronic data, content, and information uploaded to, transmitted through, or stored within the Platform by or on behalf of Customer or any Authorized User, including without limitation Trial Data and any metadata related thereto. Customer Data does not include Provider's aggregated, de-identified usage telemetry concerning the Platform itself.

1.7 "Documentation" means the user manuals, validation documentation, installation qualification (IQ) protocols, operational qualification (OQ) protocols, performance qualification (PQ) protocols, system requirements specifications, technical architecture documentation, and other documentation made available by Provider in respect of the Platform, as updated from time to time.

1.8 "Effective Date" has the meaning set forth in the preamble.

1.9 "FDA" means the United States Food and Drug Administration, and any successor agency thereto.

1.10 "Force Majeure Event" means any event or circumstance beyond the reasonable control of a Party, including without limitation acts of God, natural disasters, fire, flood, earthquake, war, terrorism, civil unrest, governmental action, pandemic, epidemic, labor disputes, internet or telecommunications failures, or denial of service attacks, but excluding Customer's payment obligations.

1.11 "GxP Audit Trail" means the system-generated record maintained by the Platform that captures, in a manner compliant with 21 CFR Part 11, all user actions affecting Customer Data, including the identity of the user, the date and time of the action, the action taken, the prior value (if applicable), and the new value, in each case in a tamper-evident and non-modifiable form.

1.12 "Implementation Services" means the professional services described in Article 3 to be provided by Provider in connection with the initial deployment, validation, configuration, and acceptance of the Platform for Customer's use.

1.13 "Intellectual Property Rights" means all intellectual property and proprietary rights of any kind anywhere in the world, including without limitation patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, know-how, database rights, moral rights, and all other rights of a similar nature, whether registered or unregistered.

1.14 "IQ/OQ/PQ" means, collectively, Installation Qualification, Operational Qualification, and Performance Qualification, being the three formal validation phases required to demonstrate that the Platform is installed correctly, operates as designed, and performs reliably under intended conditions of use, in each case in accordance with applicable industry standards including those set forth in the International Society for Pharmaceutical Engineering (ISPE) Good Automated Manufacturing Practice (GAMP) 5 guide.

1.15 "Monthly Uptime Percentage" means the percentage calculation defined in Section 6.1 of this Agreement.

1.16 "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, including without limitation patient identifying information collected in the course of a Clinical Trial, in each case as defined under applicable privacy laws including HIPAA, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (where applicable), and applicable state privacy laws.

1.17 "Platform" means the PharmaTrack Clinical Trial Management System software-as-a-service platform, including all features, modules (including but not limited to study management, monitoring, randomization, electronic data capture, and reporting), application programming interfaces, web interfaces, mobile applications, and updates thereto, in each case as made available by Provider during the Subscription Term.

1.18 "Quality Management System" or "QMS" means Provider's documented quality management system applicable to the design, development, validation, deployment, and ongoing maintenance of the Platform, which QMS is designed to comply with applicable industry standards for software used in regulated clinical research environments.

1.19 "Regulatory Authority" means the FDA, the European Medicines Agency, and any other governmental authority responsible for the regulation of pharmaceutical development, clinical research, and product approval in any jurisdiction in which Customer conducts a Clinical Trial.

1.20 "Services" means, collectively, the Subscription, the Implementation Services, the Support Services, and any other services expressly provided for in this Agreement or any Statement of Work.

1.21 "Source Code Escrow Agreement" means the source code escrow agreement to be entered into by Provider, Customer, and a mutually agreed independent escrow agent in accordance with Section 8.5.

1.22 "Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a written document executed by the Parties that references this Agreement and describes specific services to be performed by Provider for Customer.

1.23 "Subscription" means Customer's right of access to and use of the Platform as set forth in this Agreement during the Subscription Term.

1.24 "Subscription Fees" means the fees payable by Customer to Provider for the Subscription as set forth in Section 4.1.

1.25 "Subscription Term" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1.

1.26 "Support Services" means the technical support services to be provided by Provider as more fully described in Article 6.

1.27 "Trial Data" means all data, information, records, and reports generated, collected, processed, or stored by or through the Platform in connection with a Clinical Trial, including without limitation case report forms, source data, monitoring reports, statistical analyses, randomization records, drug accountability records, adverse event reports, and Personal Data of Clinical Trial participants. Trial Data is a subset of Customer Data.

ARTICLE 3 — IMPLEMENTATION AND VALIDATION SERVICES

3.1 Scope of Implementation Services. Provider shall provide Implementation Services to Customer in accordance with a written Implementation Plan to be developed by the Parties within forty-five (45) days of the Effective Date. The Implementation Services shall include, at a minimum, (a) installation and configuration of the Platform within Provider's validated cloud environment to reflect Customer's organizational structure, study designs, and data standards; (b) execution of Installation Qualification (IQ), Operational Qualification (OQ), and Performance Qualification (PQ) protocols sufficient to demonstrate the Platform's fitness for Customer's intended use under 21 CFR Part 11 and applicable GxP standards; (c) provision of validation documentation, including IQ/OQ/PQ protocols and reports, traceability matrices, and risk assessments; (d) integration with Customer's existing systems via standard application programming interfaces; (e) training of Customer's designated administrators, study managers, and Authorized Users; and (f) user acceptance testing.

3.2 Implementation Fee. The Implementation Services shall be provided for a one-time fee of Ten Thousand United States Dollars ($10,000), payable in three installments: (a) one-third upon execution of the Implementation Plan; (b) one-third upon successful completion of OQ; and (c) one-third upon Customer's written acceptance of the Implementation Services.

3.3 Validation Documentation Ownership. The validation documentation produced as part of the Implementation Services, including the IQ/OQ/PQ protocols and reports specific to Customer's instance of the Platform, shall be deliverable to Customer and may be used by Customer in support of Customer's regulatory submissions and inspections, provided that the validation documentation shall remain Confidential Information of both Parties to the extent it discloses Provider's proprietary technical information.

3.4 Customer Cooperation. Customer acknowledges that the timely completion of the Implementation Services depends on Customer's cooperation, including the timely provision of organizational information, study designs, data standards, and personnel for training and acceptance testing. Provider shall not be responsible for delays caused by Customer's failure to provide such cooperation, and any associated rescheduling shall not constitute a breach of this Agreement.

3.5 Acceptance. Implementation shall be deemed complete upon Customer's written acceptance, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed beyond fifteen (15) business days following Provider's notification of completion of Performance Qualification. If Customer identifies material non-conformities, Customer shall provide Provider with a written description, and Provider shall have thirty (30) days to remediate and resubmit for acceptance.

ARTICLE 4 — SUBSCRIPTION FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Subscription Fees. In consideration of the Subscription and Services, Customer shall pay an annual Subscription Fee of Eighteen Thousand United States Dollars ($18,000), payable in equal quarterly installments of Two Hundred Forty Thousand United States Dollars ($240,000) in advance. The first quarterly installment shall be due upon execution of this Agreement, and subsequent quarterly installments shall be due thirty (30) days prior to the start of each calendar quarter.

4.2 Payment Terms. All Subscription Fees shall be due and payable in United States Dollars within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice (the "Due Date"). Customer shall make all payments by wire transfer to the account designated by Provider in writing.

4.3 Disputed Invoices. If Customer in good faith disputes any portion of an invoice, Customer shall notify Provider in writing of the dispute within fifteen (15) business days of receipt of the invoice, identifying with reasonable particularity the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute. Customer shall pay all undisputed amounts in accordance with Section 4.2, and the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve the disputed amount within thirty (30) days.

4.4 Late Payments. Any undisputed Subscription Fees not paid by the Due Date shall accrue interest at a rate equal to the lesser of (a) one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or (b) the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, in each case calculated from the Due Date until the date of actual payment.

4.5 Taxes. The Subscription Fees are exclusive of all taxes, levies, and duties, except taxes imposed on Provider's net income. Customer shall be responsible for payment of all such taxes.

4.6 Fee Escalation. Subscription Fees may be adjusted at the start of each Renewal Term by Provider's written notice given at least thirty (30) days prior to the start of the Renewal Term. Any such adjustment shall not exceed the lesser of (a) four percent (4%) above the Subscription Fees in effect during the immediately preceding Subscription Year or (b) the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) published by the United States Bureau of Labor Statistics for the twelve-month period ending three months prior to the start of the Renewal Term.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND RENEWAL

5.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Agreement (the "Initial Term") shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of two (2) years thereafter, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 13.

5.2 Renewal Terms. Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms (each a "Renewal Term") unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal to the other Party at least thirty (30) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

5.3 Continuation Through End of Trial. Notwithstanding any expiration or termination of this Agreement, in the event a Clinical Trial supported by the Platform remains in progress as of the effective date of expiration or termination (other than termination for material breach by Customer), Customer may elect to continue the Subscription, on a Clinical-Trial-by-Clinical-Trial basis, until the database lock for the affected Clinical Trial, and Customer shall pay a pro-rata Subscription Fee for the duration of such continuation.

ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Uptime Commitment. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Platform available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.99%, calculated as: (Total Minutes in Month - Excluded Minutes - Downtime Minutes) / (Total Minutes in Month - Excluded Minutes), expressed as a percentage. The 99.99% commitment reflects the mission-critical nature of the Platform's use in active Clinical Trials and the regulatory consequences of data unavailability during dosing visits, monitoring, or adverse event reporting.

6.2 Excluded Downtime. The following shall not be counted as Downtime for purposes of calculating Monthly Uptime Percentage: (a) scheduled maintenance windows announced at least seventy-two (72) hours in advance, which shall not exceed two (2) hours per calendar month and shall be scheduled during off-peak hours; (b) emergency maintenance required to address security vulnerabilities or system failures, with as much advance notice as is reasonably practicable; (c) downtime caused by Customer's acts or omissions; (d) downtime caused by Force Majeure Events; and (e) downtime caused by failures of third-party services or networks outside Provider's reasonable control.

6.3 Service Credits. If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below 99.99%, Customer shall be entitled to receive service credits as follows: (a) below 99.99% but at or above 99.9%: a service credit equal to ten percent (10%) of one-twelfth (1/12) of the annual Subscription Fee; (b) below 99.9% but at or above 99.5%: a service credit equal to twenty percent (20%) of one-twelfth (1/12) of the annual Subscription Fee; (c) below 99.5%: a service credit equal to fifty percent (50%) of one-twelfth (1/12) of the annual Subscription Fee. To claim a service credit, Customer shall submit a written request within thirty (30) days following the end of the affected month. Service credits shall not exceed in the aggregate fifty percent (50%) of the annual Subscription Fee in any Subscription Year.

6.4 Support Services. Provider shall provide the following Support Services to Customer's designated administrators during the Subscription Term: (a) twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week ("24x7") access to a knowledge base and self-service ticketing portal; (b) email support with a target initial response time of one (1) hour for Severity 1 issues and two (2) business hours for Severity 2 issues; (c) telephone support during Provider's business hours of 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday through Friday, excluding Provider's published holidays; and (d) emergency on-call support for Severity 1 incidents (defined as a complete outage of the Platform or loss of access to Trial Data) with a target initial response time of fifteen (15) minutes, twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week.

6.5 Severity Levels. Provider shall categorize support issues using the following severity levels: Severity 1 (complete outage or loss of data access), Severity 2 (significant degradation affecting Clinical Trial operations), Severity 3 (limited functionality issue with workaround), and Severity 4 (general inquiry or feature request). Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve Severity 1 issues within two (2) hours, Severity 2 issues within one (1) business day, Severity 3 issues within five (5) business days, and Severity 4 issues in accordance with Provider's standard product roadmap.

6.6 Quality Management System. Provider shall maintain its Quality Management System throughout the Subscription Term and shall make available to Customer, upon reasonable request, summary documentation of the QMS sufficient to demonstrate the validated state of the Platform.

ARTICLE 7 — CUSTOMER DATA, PRIVACY, AND REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

7.1 Ownership of Customer Data. As between the Parties, Customer shall retain all right, title, and interest in and to Customer Data, including all Trial Data and all Personal Data contained therein. Provider acknowledges that Customer Data is the exclusive property of Customer and that Provider has no right, title, or interest in Customer Data except for the limited license set forth in Section 7.2.

7.2 License to Process. Customer hereby grants to Provider a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license to access, store, process, transmit, and display Customer Data solely as necessary to provide the Services to Customer in accordance with this Agreement. Provider shall not use Customer Data for any other purpose, including without limitation product improvement, machine learning model training, marketing, or sale to third parties. The Parties acknowledge that this restriction is essential to the protection of Trial Data and patient privacy.

7.3 21 CFR Part 11 Compliance. Provider represents and warrants that the Platform has been designed, developed, and validated to comply with the technical requirements of 21 CFR Part 11, including without limitation the maintenance of GxP Audit Trails, electronic signature functionality, system access controls, time-stamped operational checks, and authority checks.

7.4 GxP Audit Trail. The Platform shall automatically generate and maintain a GxP Audit Trail capturing all user actions affecting Customer Data. The GxP Audit Trail shall be tamper-evident, time-stamped, and exportable in a standard format suitable for inspection by Regulatory Authorities.

7.5 HIPAA and Privacy Compliance. To the extent that Customer Data includes Protected Health Information as defined under HIPAA, Provider shall act as a Business Associate of Customer with respect to such Protected Health Information, and the Parties shall enter into a Business Associate Agreement substantially in the form attached as Exhibit A. Provider shall comply with applicable privacy laws of jurisdictions in which Customer conducts Clinical Trials.

7.6 Regulatory Inspections. Provider shall reasonably cooperate with Customer in connection with any inspection of the Platform or related records by a Regulatory Authority, including by making available, at Customer's request, validation documentation, audit trail records, and personnel for interview. Provider shall promptly notify Customer of any inspection or audit by a Regulatory Authority that involves or relates to the Platform's use by Customer.

7.7 Data Security. Provider shall implement and maintain commercially reasonable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction, including without limitation (a) encryption of Customer Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher; (b) encryption of Customer Data at rest using AES-256 or equivalent; (c) role-based access controls; (d) annual third-party penetration testing; (e) maintenance of SOC 2 Type II certification; and (f) a documented information security program reviewed and updated at least annually.

7.8 Breach Notification. Provider shall notify Customer in writing of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, acquisition of, or disclosure of Customer Data (a "Security Incident") without undue delay and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours of Provider's discovery of such Security Incident. Provider shall cooperate with Customer's investigation and shall reimburse Customer for the reasonable costs of mandatory breach notifications to affected individuals to the extent the Security Incident was caused by Provider's breach of this Article 7.

7.9 Customer Audit Rights. Customer shall have the right, no more than once per calendar year (and additionally as reasonably required following a Security Incident), to audit Provider's Quality Management System, validation documentation, security controls, and compliance with this Agreement. Such audits shall be conducted upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice, during normal business hours, by Customer or by an independent third-party auditor reasonably acceptable to Provider, and shall be conducted in a manner that does not unreasonably interfere with Provider's operations. Customer shall bear its own costs of audit, and the audit shall be subject to confidentiality obligations.

7.10 Data Return and Deletion. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement (or earlier upon Customer's request following database lock for a particular Clinical Trial), Provider shall, at Customer's election, (a) make Customer Data available for export in a commercially reasonable, machine-readable format, including the GxP Audit Trail, for a period of one hundred twenty (120) days; or (b) permanently delete Customer Data from Provider's production systems within sixty (60) days of expiration or termination, with deletion from backup systems to follow in accordance with Provider's standard backup retention schedule. Upon Customer's written request, Provider shall provide written certification of deletion. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Provider shall retain Customer Data for any longer period required by applicable law or by Customer for purposes of regulatory inspection.
