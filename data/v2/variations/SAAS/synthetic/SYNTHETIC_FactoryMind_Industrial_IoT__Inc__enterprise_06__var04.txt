1.19 "Subscription Fee" shall mean the fees payable by Customer to Provider for the use of the Services during the Subscription Term, as specified in the applicable Order Form.

The Services shall be made available to Customer in accordance with the specifications set forth in the applicable Order Form and Documentation.

2.2 License Grant. Provider hereby grants to Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right to access and use the Services solely for Customer's internal business purposes during the Subscription Term. This license shall extend to Authorized Users as designated by Customer, subject to the terms herein.

Customer shall not use the Services for any purpose other than as expressly permitted by this Agreement. Any unauthorized use of the Services shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement.

Customer agrees to use the Services in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations. Customer shall promptly notify Provider of any unauthorized use of its account or any other breach of security.

2.5 Suspension Rights. Provider reserves the right to suspend Customer's access to the Services in the event of any breach of this Agreement by Customer, including but not limited to non-payment of fees. Provider shall provide Customer with notice of any such suspension and an opportunity to cure the breach, if applicable, prior to suspension.

ARTICLE 3 — IMPLEMENTATION AND PROFESSIONAL SERVICES

3.1 Scope of Implementation Services. Provider shall perform the Implementation Services as described in the applicable Order Form. Such services shall include the initial site survey and configuration of the Platform for Customer's operational environment.

3.2 Fees for Implementation Services. Customer agrees to pay Provider a one-time fee of $100,000 for the Implementation Services. This fee is non-refundable and shall be invoiced upon completion of the site survey.

3.3 Acceptance Criteria. The Implementation Services shall be deemed accepted upon successful completion of the tasks outlined in the Order Form, unless Customer provides written notice of any deficiencies within ten (10) Business Days following completion.

3.4 Change Requests. Any changes to the scope of the Implementation Services shall be documented in a written change order, which shall be subject to mutual agreement by the parties. Fees for additional services shall be agreed upon in writing prior to commencement.

3.5 Customer Cooperation. Customer shall provide all necessary access to its facilities, personnel, and information required by Provider to perform the Implementation Services. Customer's failure to provide such cooperation may result in delays for which Provider shall not be liable.

ARTICLE 4 — SUBSCRIPTION FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Fees. Customer shall pay to Provider the Subscription Fee of Ninety-Six Thousand United States Dollars ($96,000) annually, payable in monthly installments of USD 60,000. The fees are due in advance for each month of the Subscription Term.

4.2 Billing Cycle. Provider shall invoice Customer on a monthly basis, with each invoice due and payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. All payments shall be made in U.S. dollars.

4.3 Late Payment Interest. Any amounts not paid when due shall accrue interest at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less, from the due date until paid in full.

4.4 Taxes. All fees are exclusive of applicable taxes, levies, or duties imposed by taxing authorities. Customer shall be responsible for the payment of all such taxes, excluding taxes based solely on Provider's income.

4.5 Fee Changes. Provider reserves the right to adjust the Subscription Fees upon renewal of the Subscription Term, provided that Provider gives Customer at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice of any such changes.

4.6 Audit Rights. Provider shall have the right to audit Customer's use of the Services to ensure compliance with the terms of this Agreement. Any such audit shall be conducted during normal business hours and shall not unreasonably interfere with Customer's business operations.

4.7 Disputed Invoices. In the event of a dispute regarding any invoice, Customer shall notify Provider in writing of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute within fifteen (15) Business Days of the invoice date. The parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND RENEWAL

5.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of three (3) years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with the terms herein.

5.2 Automatic Renewal. Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

5.3 Notice of Non-Renewal. Either party may elect not to renew this Agreement by providing written notice to the other party at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

5.4 Fee Escalation Upon Renewal. Provider reserves the right to increase the Subscription Fees upon renewal, provided that such increase does not exceed five percent (5%) of the fees for the preceding term, unless otherwise agreed in writing by the parties.

5.5 Effects of Expiration. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, Customer's rights to access and use the Services shall immediately terminate. Provider shall have no obligation to retain Customer Data beyond the termination date, except as required by applicable law.

ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Uptime Commitment. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that the Services achieve an uptime availability of 99.9%, excluding scheduled maintenance and any Force Majeure Events.

6.2 Exclusions. The uptime commitment shall not apply to any unavailability caused by Customer's use of the Services in violation of this Agreement, Third-Party Applications, or any other factors beyond Provider's reasonable control.

6.3 Measurement Methodology. Uptime shall be measured on a monthly basis, using Provider's monitoring tools and methodologies. Provider shall provide Customer with access to uptime reports upon request.

6.4 Service Credit Calculation. In the event that Provider fails to meet the uptime commitment, Customer shall be entitled to a service credit calculated as a percentage of the monthly Subscription Fee, as specified in the SLA.

6.5 Support Tiers. Provider shall offer support services in accordance with the support tiers outlined in the Documentation. Each tier shall specify the level of support, response times, and escalation procedures.

6.6 Response Times. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to respond to support requests within the timeframes specified in the applicable support tier. Response times shall be measured from the time a support request is received by Provider.

6.7 Escalation Procedures. In the event that a support issue is not resolved within the specified response time, Customer may escalate the issue in accordance with the escalation procedures set forth in the Documentation.

6.8 Maintenance Windows. Provider shall schedule regular maintenance windows for the Services, during which time the Services may be unavailable. Provider shall provide Customer with at least seventy-two (72) hours' notice prior to any scheduled maintenance.
