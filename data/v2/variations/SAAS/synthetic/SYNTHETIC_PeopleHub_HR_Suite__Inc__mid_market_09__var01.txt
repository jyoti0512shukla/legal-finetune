4.1 Fees. Customer shall pay Provider the Subscription Fee as specified in the Order Form. The Subscription Fee shall be payable in monthly installments of USD 5,000, commencing on the Effective Date.

4.3 Late Payment Interest. Any amounts not paid when due shall accrue interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, from the due date until paid.

4.6 Audit Rights. Provider shall have the right to audit Customer's use of the Services to ensure compliance with the terms of this Agreement. Any such audit shall be conducted during regular business hours and in a manner that does not unreasonably interfere with Customer's business operations.

4.7 Disputed Invoices. If Customer disputes any portion of an invoice, Customer shall notify Provider in writing within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date, specifying the nature of the dispute. ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND RENEWAL

5.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of two (2) years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

5.2 Automatic Renewal. Upon expiration of the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

5.3 Notice of Non-Renewal. Either party may elect not to renew this Agreement by providing written notice to the other party at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

5.4 Fee Escalation Upon Renewal. Provider may increase the Subscription Fee for any renewal term by providing Customer with written notice of such increase at least sixty (60) days prior to the commencement of the renewal term.

5.5 Effects of Expiration. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, Customer's right to access and use the Services shall immediately terminate. Customer shall cease all use of the Services and return or destroy all copies of the Documentation in its possession.

ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Uptime Commitment. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that the Services are available at least 99.9% of the time, measured on a monthly basis, excluding any scheduled maintenance or Force Majeure Events.

6.2 Exclusions. The uptime commitment shall not apply to any unavailability caused by Customer's use of the Services in violation of the terms of this Agreement, or any issues arising from Third-Party Applications.

6.3 Measurement Methodology. Provider shall measure the uptime of the Services using industry-standard monitoring tools and shall provide Customer with access to uptime reports upon request.

6.4 Service Credit Calculation. In the event that Provider fails to meet the uptime commitment, Customer shall be entitled to a service credit equal to a prorated portion of the Subscription Fee for the period of downtime, subject to the terms set forth in the SLA.

6.5 Support Tiers. Provider shall offer support services in accordance with the support tiers described in the Documentation, which shall include different levels of support based on the severity of the issue reported by Customer.

6.6 Response Times. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to respond to support requests within the response times specified in the SLA, based on the severity of the issue.

6.7 Escalation Procedures. In the event that an issue is not resolved within the specified response time, Customer may escalate the issue in accordance with the escalation procedures outlined in the SLA.

6.8 Maintenance Windows. Provider shall conduct routine maintenance during scheduled maintenance windows, as specified in the SLA. Provider shall provide Customer with at least forty-eight (48) hours' notice of any scheduled maintenance that may impact the availability of the Services.
