4.1 Fees. The Customer shall pay the Subscription Fee to the Provider as specified in the applicable Order Form. The Subscription Fee shall include access for up to fifty (50) Authorized Users, with additional fees applicable for any additional Authorized Users.

4.2 Billing Cycle. The Provider shall invoice the Customer annually in advance for the Subscription Fee and any applicable fees for additional Authorized Users. Payment shall be due within thirty (30) days from the date of the invoice.

4.3 Late Payment Interest. Any amounts not paid when due shall accrue interest at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less, from the due date until paid in full.

4.5 Fee Changes. The Provider reserves the right to change the Subscription Fee upon renewal of the Subscription Term, provided that the Provider notifies the Customer of such change at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current Subscription Term.

4.6 Audit Rights. The Customer shall have the right to conduct an audit of the Provider's records related to the calculation of fees under this Agreement once annually, upon reasonable notice and during normal business hours, to ensure compliance with the terms herein.

4.7 Disputed Invoices. In the event the Customer disputes any portion of an invoice, the Customer shall provide written notice to the Provider within thirty (30) days of receipt of the invoice, specifying the nature of the dispute. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve any such disputes promptly.

5.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of one (1) years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with the terms herein.

5.2 Automatic Renewal. Upon the expiration of the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms, unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

5.3 Notice of Non-Renewal. Either Party may elect not to renew this Agreement by providing written notice to the other Party at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current Subscription Term.

5.4 Fee Escalation Upon Renewal. The Provider may increase the Subscription Fee for any renewal term by providing written notice to the Customer at least sixty (60) days prior to the commencement of such renewal term.

5.5 Effects of Expiration. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, the Customer's right to access and use the Services shall immediately cease, and the Customer shall promptly return or destroy all copies of the Provider's Confidential Information in its possession.

6.3 Measurement Methodology. The Provider shall measure the uptime availability of the Services using industry-standard monitoring tools. The Provider shall provide reports of such measurements to the Customer upon request.

6.4 Service Credit Calculation. If the Provider fails to meet the uptime commitment, the Customer shall be entitled to a service credit as specified in the Service Level Agreement. Such service credit shall be the Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for any failure to meet the uptime commitment.

6.5 Support Tiers. The Provider shall provide Support Services in accordance with the support tiers described in the Service Level Agreement. The Customer shall have access to support during the Provider's normal business hours.

6.6 Response Times. The Provider shall respond to critical support requests within thirty (30) minutes and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve such issues within four (4) hours, as further described in the Service Level Agreement.

6.7 Escalation Procedures. In the event that a support issue is not resolved within the target resolution time, the Customer may escalate the issue to the Provider's senior management in accordance with the escalation procedures set forth in the Service Level Agreement.

6.8 Maintenance Windows. The Provider shall conduct scheduled maintenance during designated maintenance windows, as specified in the Service Level Agreement. The Provider shall provide the Customer with reasonable advance notice of any scheduled maintenance.
