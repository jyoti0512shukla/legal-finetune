CLOUD SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Cloud Software Subscription Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of August 5, 2024 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Cascade Digital Solutions Inc., a corporation incorporated under the Delaware Business Corporations Act having its registered office at 8576 North Maple Avenue, Suite 1000, Chicago, Illinois 60689 ("Provider"), and Compass Insurance Group Inc., a Delaware corporation governed by the Bank Act (Delaware) having its head office at 253 North Commerce Avenue, Suite 500, Chicago, Illinois 60857 ("Customer"). Provider and Customer may each be referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Provider develops, hosts, and operates a cloud-based regulatory compliance and reporting platform designed for use by Canadian financial institutions in meeting their reporting obligations to the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI), the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Delaware (FINTRAC), and provincial securities regulators (the "NorthernEdge Platform");

WHEREAS, Customer is a federally regulated financial institution licensed to carry on banking business in Delaware and desires to subscribe to the NorthernEdge Platform to support its regulatory reporting and anti-money-laundering compliance functions;

WHEREAS, the Parties acknowledge that Customer is subject to extensive supervision by Canadian financial regulators, including OSFI Guideline B-10 (Outsourcing of Business Activities, Functions and Processes), Guideline B-13 (Technology and Cyber Risk Management), and the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA);

WHEREAS, the Parties further acknowledge that Customer's use of the Platform constitutes "outsourcing of a material business activity" within the meaning of OSFI Guideline B-10, and the terms of this Agreement have been negotiated to comply with the requirements applicable to such outsourcing arrangements;

WHEREAS, the Parties intend that this Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Province of Illinois and the federal laws of Delaware applicable therein; and

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to establish a multi-year commercial relationship under terms that satisfy Customer's regulatory obligations and provide Provider with reasonable commercial certainty;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE 2 — SUBSCRIPTION AND LICENCE

2.1 Provision of the Platform. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement and Customer's timely payment of the Subscription Fees, Provider shall make the Platform available to Customer and its Authorized Users during the Subscription Term in accordance with this Agreement, the Documentation, and the Service Level Agreement set forth in Article 6.

2.2 Licence Grant. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, Provider hereby grants to Customer a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right and licence during the Subscription Term to access and use the Platform solely for Customer's internal business purposes in connection with Customer's regulatory compliance and reporting functions.

2.3 Authorized Users. The Subscription includes the right for up to seventy-five (75) Authorized Users to access the Platform concurrently. Customer may add additional Authorized Users by providing Provider with written notice and paying additional Subscription Fees of Three Hundred Canadian Dollars (CAD $300) per additional Authorized User per month, prorated for any partial month.

2.4 Restrictions on Use. Customer shall not, and shall not permit any Authorized User to, (a) modify, adapt, alter, translate, or create derivative works based upon the Platform; (b) sublicence, lease, sell, resell, or transfer access to the Platform to any third party; (c) reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or otherwise attempt to derive the source code of the Platform, except to the extent such restriction is prohibited by applicable Canadian copyright law; (d) use the Platform to develop a competing product or service; (e) remove or alter any proprietary notices appearing on the Platform; or (f) use the Platform in violation of applicable laws or regulations.

ARTICLE 3 — IMPLEMENTATION SERVICES

3.1 Scope of Implementation. Provider shall provide Implementation Services to Customer in accordance with a written Implementation Plan to be developed by the Parties within forty-five (45) days of the Effective Date. The Implementation Services shall include configuration of the Platform to reflect Customer's compliance workflows, integration with Customer's core banking system, training of Customer's compliance personnel, and user acceptance testing.

3.2 Implementation Fee. The Implementation Services shall be provided for a one-time fee of Twenty-Five Thousand United States Dollars ($25,000), payable in two installments: one-half upon execution of the Implementation Plan and one-half upon Customer's written acceptance of the Implementation Services.

3.3 Implementation Schedule. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to complete the Implementation Services within one hundred fifty (150) days of the Effective Date, subject to Customer's timely cooperation.

3.4 Acceptance. Implementation shall be deemed complete upon Customer's written acceptance, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed.

ARTICLE 4 — SUBSCRIPTION FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Subscription Fees. In consideration of the Services, Customer shall pay an annual Subscription Fee of Seventy-Two Thousand United States Dollars ($72,000), payable in equal quarterly installments of One Hundred Fifty Thousand Canadian Dollars (CAD $150,000) in advance. The first quarterly installment shall be due upon execution of this Agreement.

4.2 Payment Terms. All Subscription Fees shall be due and payable in Canadian Dollars within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice (the "Due Date"). Customer shall make all payments by wire transfer to the account designated by Provider in writing.

4.3 Disputed Invoices. If Customer in good faith disputes any portion of an invoice, Customer shall notify Provider in writing of the dispute within twenty (20) business days of receipt of the invoice. Customer shall pay all undisputed amounts in accordance with Section 4.2, and the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve the disputed amount within thirty (30) days.

4.4 Late Payments. Any undisputed amounts not paid by the Due Date shall accrue interest at a rate equal to the lesser of (a) one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or (b) the maximum rate permitted by applicable Illinois law.

4.5 Taxes. The Subscription Fees are exclusive of all taxes, levies, and duties, except taxes imposed on Provider's net income. Customer shall be responsible for payment of all such taxes, including without limitation any applicable Goods and Services Tax / Harmonized Sales Tax (GST/HST) imposed under the Excise Tax Act (Delaware), provincial sales taxes, and Quebec Sales Tax (where applicable).

4.6 Fee Escalation. Subscription Fees may be adjusted at the start of each Renewal Term by Provider's written notice given at least one hundred twenty (120) days prior to the start of the Renewal Term. Any such adjustment shall not exceed the lesser of (a) four percent (4%) above the Subscription Fees in effect during the immediately preceding Subscription Year or (b) the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for Delaware (all items) published by Statistics Delaware for the twelve-month period ending three months prior to the start of the Renewal Term.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND RENEWAL

5.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Agreement (the "Initial Term") shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of two (2) years thereafter, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 13.

5.2 Renewal Terms. Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms (each a "Renewal Term") unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal to the other Party at least one hundred eighty (180) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. The one hundred eighty (180) day non-renewal notice period reflects Customer's regulatory obligation to provide its regulators with adequate notice of changes to material outsourcing arrangements.

5.3 Customer Termination Rights. In addition to Customer's rights under Article 13, Customer may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if (a) Provider loses or fails to maintain SOC 2 Type II certification or ISO/IEC 27001 certification; or (b) OSFI or another regulator having jurisdiction over Customer directs Customer to terminate the outsourcing arrangement contemplated by this Agreement.

ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Uptime Commitment. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Platform available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.95%, calculated as: (Total Minutes in Month - Excluded Minutes - Downtime Minutes) / (Total Minutes in Month - Excluded Minutes), expressed as a percentage.

6.2 Excluded Downtime. The following shall not be counted as Downtime: (a) scheduled maintenance windows announced at least seventy-two (72) hours in advance, which shall not exceed six (6) hours per calendar month and shall be scheduled outside of peak business hours; (b) emergency maintenance with as much advance notice as is reasonably practicable; (c) downtime caused by Customer's acts or omissions; (d) downtime caused by Force Majeure Events; and (e) downtime caused by failures of third-party services or networks outside Provider's reasonable control.

6.3 Service Credits. If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below 99.95%, Customer shall be entitled to receive service credits as follows: (a) below 99.95% but at or above 99.9%: a service credit equal to ten percent (10%) of one-twelfth (1/12) of the annual Subscription Fee; (b) below 99.9% but at or above 99.5%: twenty percent (20%); (c) below 99.5%: forty percent (40%). To claim a service credit, Customer shall submit a written request within thirty (30) days following the end of the affected month. Service credits shall not exceed in the aggregate fifty percent (50%) of the annual Subscription Fee in any Subscription Year.

6.4 Support Services. Provider shall provide the following Support Services: (a) twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week ("24x7") access to a self-service knowledge base and online help centre; (b) email support with a target initial response time of one (1) hour for Severity 1 issues and four (4) business hours for Severity 2 issues; (c) telephone support during Provider's business hours of 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday through Friday; and (d) emergency on-call support for Severity 1 incidents (defined as a complete outage of the Platform) with a target initial response time of thirty (30) minutes, twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week.

6.5 Severity Levels. Provider shall categorize support issues using the following severity levels: Severity 1 (complete outage), Severity 2 (significant degradation affecting compliance operations), Severity 3 (limited functionality issue with workaround), and Severity 4 (general inquiry). Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve Severity 1 issues within four (4) hours, Severity 2 issues within one (1) business day, Severity 3 issues within five (5) business days, and Severity 4 issues per Provider's standard product roadmap.

ARTICLE 7 — CUSTOMER DATA, PRIVACY, AND REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

7.1 Ownership of Customer Data. As between the Parties, Customer shall retain all right, title, and interest in and to Customer Data, including all Personal Information. Provider acknowledges that Customer Data is the exclusive property of Customer.

7.2 Licence to Process. Customer hereby grants to Provider a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free licence to access, store, process, transmit, and display Customer Data solely as necessary to provide the Services to Customer in accordance with this Agreement. Provider shall not use Customer Data for any other purpose, including without limitation product improvement, machine learning model training, marketing, or sale to third parties.

7.3 PIPEDA Compliance. Provider shall handle all Personal Information in accordance with PIPEDA and the principles set out in Schedule 1 thereto, and shall implement and maintain physical, organizational, and technological safeguards consistent with the sensitivity of the Personal Information processed.

7.4 Data Residency. Provider shall store and process all Customer Data in data centres physically located within Delaware. Provider shall not transfer Customer Data outside of Delaware without Customer's prior written consent. Provider acknowledges that this data residency requirement is essential to Customer's compliance with OSFI guidelines and provincial privacy legislation, and that any transfer of Customer Data outside of Delaware without Customer's prior written consent shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement entitling Customer to terminate immediately.

7.5 OSFI B-10 and B-13 Compliance. The Parties acknowledge that Customer's use of the Platform constitutes a material outsourcing arrangement within the meaning of OSFI Guideline B-10 and that the Platform constitutes a technology service subject to OSFI Guideline B-13. Provider shall (a) cooperate with Customer in fulfilling Customer's obligations under such guidelines; (b) provide Customer with such information about the Platform, Provider's controls, and Provider's subcontractors as Customer may reasonably require to satisfy its regulatory obligations; (c) notify Customer promptly of any material change to the Platform, the Services, Provider's subcontractors, or Provider's controls that could affect the outsourcing arrangement; and (d) permit Customer and its regulators to exercise the audit rights set forth in Section 7.9.

7.6 Data Security. Provider shall implement and maintain commercially reasonable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction, including without limitation (a) encryption of Customer Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher; (b) encryption of Customer Data at rest using AES-256 or equivalent; (c) role-based access controls; (d) annual third-party penetration testing; (e) maintenance of SOC 2 Type II certification and ISO/IEC 27001 certification; and (f) a documented information security program reviewed and updated at least annually.

7.7 Breach Notification. Provider shall notify Customer in writing of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, acquisition of, or disclosure of Customer Data (a "Security Incident") without undue delay and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours of Provider's discovery of such Security Incident. Provider shall cooperate with Customer's investigation and shall reimburse Customer for the reasonable costs of mandatory breach notifications to affected individuals, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Delaware, and applicable provincial privacy commissioners, to the extent the Security Incident was caused by Provider's breach of its obligations under this Article 7.

7.8 Subcontractors. Provider may use subcontractors to perform the Services, provided that (a) Provider shall remain fully responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors; (b) Provider shall ensure that each subcontractor is bound by written obligations of confidentiality and data protection at least as protective as those set forth in this Agreement; (c) Provider shall provide Customer with written notice of any new subcontractor at least sixty (60) days before such subcontractor begins providing services in connection with this Agreement; and (d) Customer shall have the right, upon reasonable grounds, to object to the use of any new subcontractor and to require Provider to engage an alternative subcontractor or to terminate the Agreement without penalty.

7.9 Audit Rights. Customer and its regulators (including OSFI) shall have the right to audit Provider's facilities, records, and information security controls in order to verify Provider's compliance with this Agreement and applicable regulatory requirements. Such audits shall be conducted upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice (or such shorter notice as may be required by Customer's regulators), during normal business hours, by Customer or by an independent third-party auditor reasonably acceptable to Provider, and shall be conducted in a manner that does not unreasonably interfere with Provider's operations. The costs of such audits shall be borne as follows: (a) Customer shall bear the costs of audits initiated as routine examinations; (b) Provider shall bear the costs of audits initiated as a result of a Security Incident or other failure attributable to Provider; and (c) the costs of audits required by Customer's regulators shall be borne in accordance with the directions of such regulators.

7.10 Data Return and Deletion. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, Provider shall, at Customer's election, (a) make Customer Data available for export by Customer in a commercially reasonable, machine-readable format for a period of ninety (90) days following expiration or termination; or (b) permanently delete Customer Data from Provider's production systems within thirty (30) days of expiration or termination, with deletion from backup systems to follow within ninety (90) days. Provider shall provide written certification of deletion upon Customer's request. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Provider shall retain Customer Data for any longer period required by applicable law.
