6.1 Uptime Commitment. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that the Services are available with an uptime of at least 99.5% during each calendar month, excluding any scheduled maintenance or Force Majeure Events.

6.2 Exclusions. 6.3 Measurement Methodology. Uptime shall be calculated on a monthly basis as the total number of minutes in the month minus the total number of minutes of unavailability, divided by the total number of minutes in the month, and multiplied by 100.

6.4 Service Credit Calculation. In the event that Provider fails to meet the uptime commitment, Customer shall be entitled to a service credit equal to one (1) day’s worth of the monthly Subscription Fee for each one-tenth of a percentage point (0.1%) by which Provider fails to meet the uptime commitment, up to a maximum of the total monthly Subscription Fee.

6.5 Support Tiers. Provider shall provide support services to Customer in accordance with the support tiers described in the Documentation. Support shall include email and chat support during business hours, with response times as specified in the Documentation.

6.6 Response Times. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to respond to support requests within the timeframes specified in the Documentation, based on the severity level of the issue.
