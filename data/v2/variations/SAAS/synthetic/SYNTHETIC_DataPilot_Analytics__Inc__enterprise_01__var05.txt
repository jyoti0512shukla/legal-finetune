ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Uptime Commitment. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that the Services are available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.9%, as measured over each calendar month during the Subscription Term.

6.2 Exclusions. The uptime commitment does not apply to any unavailability caused by (i) planned maintenance, (ii) Customer’s use of Third-Party Applications, (iii) Customer’s failure to comply with the Documentation, or (iv) Force Majeure Events.

6.3 Measurement Methodology. The Monthly Uptime Percentage shall be calculated by subtracting from 100% the percentage of minutes during the month in which the Services were unavailable due to Provider-controlled factors.

6.4 Service Credit Calculation. If Provider fails to meet the uptime commitment, Customer shall be eligible for service credits as follows: (i) 5% of the monthly Subscription Fee for uptime between 99.0% and 99.9%; (ii) 10% for uptime between 95.0% and 99.0%; and (iii) 25% for uptime below 95.0%.

6.5 Support Tiers. Provider shall provide Support Services in accordance with the support tiers described in Exhibit B, which outlines the levels of support and response times applicable to each tier.

6.6 Response Times. Provider shall respond to critical support requests within one (1) hour of receipt, twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve such issues in a timely manner.

6.7 Escalation Procedures. Provider shall maintain escalation procedures to address unresolved support issues, including the involvement of senior technical personnel to facilitate resolution.

6.8 Maintenance Windows. Provider shall schedule regular maintenance windows during which the Services may be unavailable. Provider shall provide Customer with at least seventy-two (72) hours' notice prior to any planned maintenance that is expected to result in downtime.
