INSURANCE CLAIMS PROCESSING PLATFORM SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Insurance Claims Processing Platform Subscription Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of March 12, 2024 (the "Effective Date"), by and between ClaimsFlow Insurance Platform, Inc., a Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at 350 North Orleans Street, Suite 950, Chicago, Illinois 60654 ("Provider"), and Heartland Mutual Insurance Company, an Illinois mutual insurance company having its principal administrative office at 200 East Randolph Street, Suite 4400, Chicago, Illinois 60601 ("Customer"). Provider and Customer may each be referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Provider develops, hosts, and operates a cloud-based claims processing and management platform designed for use by property and casualty insurers in the intake, triage, adjustment, payment, and reporting of insurance claims (the "ClaimsFlow Platform");

WHEREAS, Customer is a property and casualty insurance carrier licensed by the Illinois Department of Insurance and other state insurance regulators and desires to subscribe to the ClaimsFlow Platform to support its claims operations across multiple lines of business, including personal auto, homeowners, and commercial property;

WHEREAS, the Parties acknowledge that Customer is a regulated insurance carrier subject to the supervision of state insurance commissioners and to the requirements of state insurance laws and regulations, the National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC) Model Laws, and applicable cybersecurity regulations including the New York Department of Financial Services Cybersecurity Regulation (23 NYCRR Part 500) where applicable;

WHEREAS, the Parties further acknowledge that the loss or unauthorized disclosure of policyholder information could subject Customer to regulatory penalties and reputational harm, and that the Platform must accordingly meet heightened security and operational reliability standards; and

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to establish a multi-year commercial relationship to support Customer's claims operations;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Affiliate" means, with respect to any entity, any other entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such entity, where "control" means the ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities or membership interests of such entity.

1.2 "Authorized User" means each individual claims adjuster, supervisor, manager, IT administrator, or other employee, contractor, or agent of Customer who is authorized by Customer to access and use the Platform.

1.3 "Claims Data" means all electronic data and information related to insurance claims that is uploaded to, generated by, or stored within the Platform, including without limitation policyholder information, claimant information, loss descriptions, photographs, estimates, payment records, fraud indicators, and any other information relating to a specific claim or claimant.

1.4 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other Party that is identified as confidential at the time of disclosure or that, given the nature of the information and the circumstances surrounding its disclosure, would reasonably be understood to be confidential. Confidential Information of Customer includes Claims Data, NPI, Customer's underwriting strategies, and Customer's pricing and reserving methodologies.

1.5 "Customer Data" means all electronic data, content, and information uploaded to, transmitted through, or stored within the Platform by or on behalf of Customer, including without limitation Claims Data and NPI.

1.6 "Documentation" means the user manuals, technical documentation, security white papers, integration guides, and other materials describing the functionality and operation of the Platform.

1.7 "Effective Date" has the meaning set forth in the preamble.

1.8 "Force Majeure Event" means any event or circumstance beyond the reasonable control of a Party, including without limitation acts of God, natural disasters, fire, flood, war, terrorism, civil unrest, governmental action, pandemic, epidemic, labor disputes, internet or telecommunications failures, denial of service attacks, or material failures of third-party cloud infrastructure providers, but excluding Customer's payment obligations.

1.9 "Implementation Services" means the professional services to be provided by Provider in connection with the initial deployment, configuration, integration, training, and acceptance testing of the Platform.

1.10 "Insurance Regulator" means any state insurance commissioner, department of insurance, or other governmental authority having regulatory authority over Customer's insurance business.

1.11 "Intellectual Property Rights" means all intellectual property and proprietary rights of any kind anywhere in the world, including without limitation patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, know-how, database rights, and all other rights of a similar nature.

1.12 "NPI" or "Nonpublic Personal Information" means information about an individual that is collected in connection with providing an insurance product or service and that is not publicly available, as defined in the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (15 U.S.C. § 6801 et seq.) and applicable state insurance privacy laws.

1.13 "PCI-DSS" means the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard, as published by the PCI Security Standards Council, as the same may be amended from time to time.

1.14 "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, including without limitation NPI and any data protected under applicable state and federal privacy laws.

1.15 "Platform" means the ClaimsFlow Insurance Claims Processing Platform, including all features, functionality, modules (including but not limited to claim intake, adjuster workflow, fraud detection, payment processing, and reporting), application programming interfaces, web interfaces, mobile applications, and updates thereto.

1.16 "Services" means, collectively, Customer's right of access to the Platform during the Subscription Term, the Implementation Services, the Support Services, and any other services expressly provided for in this Agreement.

1.17 "Subscription Fees" means the fees payable by Customer to Provider for the Services as set forth in Section 4.1.

1.18 "Subscription Term" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1.

1.19 "Support Services" means the technical support services to be provided by Provider as more fully described in Article 6.

ARTICLE 2 — SUBSCRIPTION AND LICENSE

2.1 Provision of the Platform. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement and Customer's timely payment of the Subscription Fees, Provider shall make the Platform available to Customer and its Authorized Users during the Subscription Term in accordance with this Agreement, the Documentation, and the Service Level Agreement set forth in Article 6.

2.2 License Grant. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, Provider hereby grants to Customer a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right and license during the Subscription Term to access and use the Platform solely for Customer's internal business purposes in connection with Customer's claims operations.

2.3 Authorized Users. The Subscription includes the right for up to one hundred twenty (120) Authorized Users to access the Platform concurrently. Customer may add additional Authorized Users by providing Provider with written notice and paying additional Subscription Fees of One Hundred Eighty United States Dollars ($180) per additional Authorized User per month, prorated for any partial month.

2.4 Restrictions on Use. Customer shall not, and shall not permit any Authorized User to, (a) modify, adapt, alter, translate, or create derivative works based upon the Platform; (b) sublicense, lease, sell, resell, or transfer access to the Platform to any third party; (c) reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Platform; (d) use the Platform to develop a competing product or service; (e) remove or alter any proprietary notices appearing on the Platform; or (f) use the Platform in violation of applicable laws or regulations.

2.5 Customer Responsibilities. Customer shall be responsible for (a) the acts and omissions of its Authorized Users in connection with their use of the Platform; (b) the secure management of Authorized User credentials, including immediate deactivation of credentials upon termination of an Authorized User's relationship with Customer; (c) ensuring that all Authorized Users comply with applicable laws and regulations and with Customer's internal policies regarding the handling of Claims Data and NPI; and (d) promptly notifying Provider of any unauthorized access to or use of the Platform.

ARTICLE 3 — IMPLEMENTATION SERVICES

3.1 Scope of Implementation. Provider shall provide Implementation Services to Customer in accordance with a written Implementation Plan to be developed by the Parties within forty-five (45) days of the Effective Date. The Implementation Services shall include (a) configuration of the Platform to reflect Customer's lines of business, claim types, adjuster roles, and approval workflows; (b) integration with Customer's existing policy administration system and general ledger system via standard application programming interfaces; (c) migration of historical claims data, subject to a data migration scope of work to be mutually agreed; (d) training of Customer's administrators, supervisors, and adjusters; and (e) user acceptance testing.

3.2 Implementation Fee. The Implementation Services shall be provided for a one-time fee of Sixty Thousand United States Dollars ($60,000), payable in two installments: (a) one-half upon execution of the Implementation Plan and (b) one-half upon Customer's written acceptance of the Implementation Services.

3.3 Implementation Schedule. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to complete the Implementation Services within one hundred twenty (120) days of the Effective Date, subject to Customer's timely cooperation.

3.4 Acceptance. Implementation shall be deemed complete upon Customer's written acceptance, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. If Customer identifies material deficiencies, Customer shall provide Provider with a written description, and Provider shall have thirty (30) days to remediate and resubmit for acceptance.

ARTICLE 4 — SUBSCRIPTION FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Subscription Fees. In consideration of the Services, Customer shall pay an annual Subscription Fee of One Hundred Forty-Four Thousand United States Dollars ($144,000), payable in equal monthly installments of Twelve Thousand United States Dollars ($12,000) in advance. The first monthly installment shall be due upon execution of this Agreement, and subsequent monthly installments shall be due on the first day of each calendar month thereafter.

4.2 Payment Terms. All Subscription Fees shall be due and payable in United States Dollars within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice (the "Due Date"). Customer shall make all payments by electronic funds transfer to the account designated by Provider in writing.

4.3 Disputed Invoices. If Customer in good faith disputes any portion of an invoice, Customer shall notify Provider in writing of the dispute within twenty (20) business days of receipt of the invoice, identifying the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute. Customer shall pay all undisputed amounts in accordance with Section 4.2, and the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve the disputed amount within thirty (30) days.

4.4 Late Payments. Any undisputed Subscription Fees not paid by the Due Date shall accrue interest at a rate equal to the lesser of (a) one percent (1.0%) per month or (b) the maximum rate permitted by applicable Illinois law.

4.5 Taxes. The Subscription Fees are exclusive of all taxes, levies, and duties, except taxes imposed on Provider's net income. Customer shall be responsible for payment of all such taxes.

4.6 Fee Escalation. Subscription Fees may be adjusted at the start of each Renewal Term by Provider's written notice given at least one hundred twenty (120) days prior to the start of the Renewal Term. Any such adjustment shall not exceed the lesser of (a) four percent (4%) above the Subscription Fees in effect during the immediately preceding Subscription Year or (b) the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) for the twelve-month period ending three months prior to the start of the Renewal Term.

ARTICLE 5 — TERM AND RENEWAL

5.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Agreement (the "Initial Term") shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of two (2) years thereafter, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 13.

5.2 Renewal Terms. Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year renewal terms (each a "Renewal Term") unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal to the other Party at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

5.3 Customer Termination Rights. In addition to Customer's rights under Article 13, Customer may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if (a) Provider loses or fails to maintain SOC 2 Type II certification; (b) Provider loses or fails to maintain PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance; or (c) Provider becomes subject to any regulatory enforcement action or consent decree relating to the protection of personal information that, in Customer's reasonable judgment, materially impairs Provider's ability to perform its obligations under this Agreement.

ARTICLE 6 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND SUPPORT

6.1 Uptime Commitment. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Platform available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.95%, calculated as: (Total Minutes in Month - Excluded Minutes - Downtime Minutes) / (Total Minutes in Month - Excluded Minutes), expressed as a percentage. The 99.95% commitment reflects the operational criticality of the Platform to Customer's claims operations.

6.2 Excluded Downtime. The following shall not be counted as Downtime: (a) scheduled maintenance windows announced at least seventy-two (72) hours in advance, which shall not exceed six (6) hours per calendar month and shall be scheduled outside of peak business hours (peak business hours being defined as Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Central Time); (b) emergency maintenance with as much advance notice as is reasonably practicable; (c) downtime caused by Customer's acts or omissions; (d) downtime caused by Force Majeure Events; and (e) downtime caused by failures of third-party services or networks outside Provider's reasonable control.

6.3 Service Credits. If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below 99.95%, Customer shall be entitled to receive service credits as follows: (a) below 99.95% but at or above 99.9%: a service credit equal to ten percent (10%) of one-twelfth (1/12) of the annual Subscription Fee; (b) below 99.9% but at or above 99.5%: twenty percent (20%); (c) below 99.5% but at or above 99.0%: thirty percent (30%); (d) below 99.0%: fifty percent (50%). Service credits shall not exceed in the aggregate fifty percent (50%) of the annual Subscription Fee in any Subscription Year.

6.4 Support Services. Provider shall provide the following Support Services: (a) twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week ("24x7") access to a self-service knowledge base and online help center; (b) email support with a target initial response time of two (2) business hours for Severity 1 issues and four (4) business hours for Severity 2 issues; (c) telephone support during Provider's business hours of 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Central Time, Monday through Friday; and (d) emergency on-call support for Severity 1 incidents (defined as a complete outage of the Platform or loss of access to Claims Data) with a target initial response time of thirty (30) minutes, twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week.

6.5 Severity Levels. Provider shall categorize support issues using the following severity levels: Severity 1 (complete outage), Severity 2 (significant degradation), Severity 3 (limited functionality issue with workaround), and Severity 4 (general inquiry). Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve Severity 1 issues within four (4) hours, Severity 2 issues within one (1) business day, Severity 3 issues within five (5) business days, and Severity 4 issues per Provider's standard product roadmap.

ARTICLE 7 — CUSTOMER DATA, PRIVACY, AND REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

7.1 Ownership of Customer Data. As between the Parties, Customer shall retain all right, title, and interest in and to Customer Data, including all Claims Data and NPI. Provider acknowledges that Customer Data is the exclusive property of Customer and that Provider has no right, title, or interest in Customer Data except for the limited license set forth in Section 7.2.

7.2 License to Process. Customer hereby grants to Provider a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license to access, store, process, transmit, and display Customer Data solely as necessary to provide the Services to Customer in accordance with this Agreement. Provider shall not use Customer Data for any other purpose, including without limitation product improvement, machine learning model training, marketing, or sale to third parties.

7.3 Data Residency. Provider shall store and process all Customer Data in data centers physically located in the contiguous United States, with primary processing facilities located in the United States Midwest region. Provider shall not transfer Customer Data outside the United States without Customer's prior written consent.

7.4 Compliance with Insurance Privacy Laws. Provider acknowledges that Customer is subject to applicable insurance privacy laws and regulations, including without limitation the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, the Illinois Insurance Information and Privacy Protection Act (215 ILCS 5/1001 et seq.), and the NAIC Insurance Information and Privacy Protection Model Act. Provider shall implement and maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards consistent with the requirements applicable to "service providers" under such laws.

7.5 Data Security. Provider shall implement and maintain commercially reasonable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction, including without limitation (a) encryption of Customer Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher; (b) encryption of Customer Data at rest using AES-256 or equivalent; (c) role-based access controls; (d) annual third-party penetration testing; (e) maintenance of SOC 2 Type II certification and PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance; and (f) a documented information security program reviewed and updated at least annually.

7.6 Breach Notification. Provider shall notify Customer in writing of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, acquisition of, or disclosure of Customer Data (a "Security Incident") without undue delay and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours of Provider's discovery of such Security Incident. Such notification shall include a description of the nature of the Security Incident, the categories and approximate number of individuals affected, and Provider's response measures. Provider shall cooperate with Customer's investigation and shall reimburse Customer for the reasonable costs of mandatory breach notifications to affected individuals and to Insurance Regulators to the extent the Security Incident was caused by Provider's breach of its obligations under this Article 7.

7.7 Regulator Audit Rights. Customer's Insurance Regulators shall have the right to audit Provider's facilities, records, and information security controls to the same extent and on the same terms that they may audit Customer, and Provider shall reasonably cooperate with any such audit. Provider shall make available to Customer's Insurance Regulators, upon reasonable request and through Customer, copies of Provider's most recent SOC 2 Type II report, PCI-DSS Attestation of Compliance, and any other relevant compliance documentation. The costs of such regulator audits shall be borne as follows: (a) if the audit is initiated as a routine examination of Customer, Customer shall bear its own costs and Provider shall bear its own costs of cooperation; (b) if the audit is initiated as a result of a Security Incident or other failure attributable to Provider, Provider shall bear all reasonable costs of cooperation, including any costs incurred by Customer.

7.8 Customer Audit Rights. Customer shall have the right, no more than once per calendar year (and additionally as reasonably required following a Security Incident), to audit Provider's compliance with its obligations under this Article 7. Such audits shall be conducted upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice, during normal business hours, by Customer or by an independent third-party auditor reasonably acceptable to Provider, and shall be conducted in a manner that does not unreasonably interfere with Provider's operations.

7.9 PCI-DSS Compliance. Because the Platform may process payment card data in connection with claim payments, Provider shall maintain PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance throughout the Subscription Term and shall provide Customer with annual evidence of such compliance in the form of a current PCI-DSS Attestation of Compliance.

7.10 Data Retention and Deletion. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, Provider shall, at Customer's election, (a) make Customer Data available for export by Customer in a commercially reasonable, machine-readable format for a period of ninety (90) days following expiration or termination; or (b) permanently delete Customer Data from Provider's production systems within thirty (30) days of expiration or termination, with deletion from backup systems to follow within ninety (90) days. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Provider shall retain Customer Data for any longer period required by applicable law or by Customer for purposes of regulatory examination.
