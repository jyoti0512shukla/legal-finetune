**ARTICLE 6 — ENGAGEMENT TEAM AND PARTNER ROTATION**

6.1 **Engagement Partner.** The Auditor shall designate a lead engagement partner responsible for the overall management of the Audit Engagements, in accordance with PCAOB standards. The name of the lead engagement partner shall be set forth in each Engagement Letter.

6.2 **Concurring Partner.** For each Audit Engagement, the Auditor shall assign a concurring partner to perform the engagement quality review required by PCAOB AS 1220. The Auditor shall provide the Audit Committee with the name of the concurring partner upon request.

6.3 **Partner Rotation.** The lead engagement partner and the concurring partner shall rotate in accordance with PCAOB Rule 3526 and 3527, as amended from time to time. The Auditor shall notify the Audit Committee in writing of any scheduled partner rotation at least sixty (60) days in advance.

6.4 **Audit Committee Oversight.** The Audit Committee shall have the authority to approve the selection, appointment, and removal of the lead engagement partner and the concurring partner. The Audit Committee may consult with the lead engagement partner and the concurring partner without the presence of Company management.

6.5 **Replacement of Engagement Team.** The Audit Committee may request the replacement of any member of the Auditor's engagement team, including the lead engagement partner or the concurring partner, for cause. The Auditor shall cooperate with such request in accordance with PCAOB standards and the Auditor's internal quality control procedures.

6.6 **Personnel Continuity.** The Auditor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain continuity of the engagement team throughout the duration of each Audit Engagement, subject to the partner rotation requirements in Section 6.3.

6.7 **Conflict of Interest.** The Auditor shall notify the Audit Committee promptly of any actual or potential conflict of interest involving any member of the engagement team that may affect the Auditor's independence with respect to the Company.
