(a)

The responsibilities of Provider to each applicable BFA Recipient for deposits accepted on Provider’s books shall be that of a U.S. bank for a similar deposit held at a domestic branch office. The State of New York is the “bank’s jurisdiction” (as defined in UCC Section 9-304(b)) of Provider and any Affiliate of Provider with respect to each On-book Account and all funds credited thereto. Provider will use best efforts to ensure that such On-Book Accounts are insured to the maximum extent possible by all applicable deposit insurers including, without limitation, the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.
