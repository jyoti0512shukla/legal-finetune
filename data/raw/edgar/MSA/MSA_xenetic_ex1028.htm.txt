EXHIBIT A

PROGRAM SUMMARY

 

 

Introduction 

 

Xenetic Biosciences is developing a CAR T platform
(termed ‘XCART’) for the treatment of certain non-Hodgkin Lymphoma (NHL) subtypes, by targeting a patient- and tumor- specific
lymphoma neoantigen, namely the unique B-cell receptor (BCR) displayed by a given malignant B-cell clone.

 

XCART will utilize a universal ‘CAR cassette’,
into which neoantigen-specific, antigen-binding domains (ABDs) can be inserted to create a patient-specific CAR. The resulting CAR construct
can then be engineered into an autologous CAR T product for treatment of the patient’s lymphoma.
