Preamble
VIEWRAY has experience and know-how about the combination of Magnetic Resonance Imaging ("MRI") and Radiotherapy ("RT") to provide real-time beam-on imaging and targeting of tumors. In order to achieve a fast time to market for ViewRay’s next generation MR guided Radiotherapy ("MRgRT") device, VIEWRAY has an interest to find an experienced partner in the field of MRI.
SIEMENS has over 40 years of experience, know-how and comprehensive intellectual property in MRI systems, solutions and services. Today, SIEMENS is the market leader in the MRI industry due to its long-standing technology and innovation leadership. To further expand this leadership position, SIEMENS is interested to support the field of Image guided Therapy.
The PARTIES intend to combine their know-how and experience for the purpose of continuing a long-term business relationship for the supply of Magnetic Resonance Imaging ("MRI") subsystems ("COMPONENTS") for MRgRT systems to provide real-time beam-on imaging and targeting of tumors.
